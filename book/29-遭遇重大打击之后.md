# 29. 遭遇重大打击之后·加州版

亲人去世、被告知重病、失业、离婚。这几件事在研究里有个共同点：出事的不只是心情，还有身体。风险最高的那一段，集中在头几周到头一年。这一节不讲怎么「走出来」，只讲这段时间里哪些事必须有人盯住，哪些决定必须往后放，什么时候该去挂号。本章的法律与制度内容按加州和美国联邦现行规定写：心理危机打 988（电话或短信都行），失业保险走加州 EDD。健康科学类条目的证据和数字沿用原书。多数条目算的是死亡概率。谈花钱和待遇的四条算的是钱：别一上来就花钱做哀伤咨询（第 9 条）、打 988 和看心理医生（第 11 条）、不可逆的大决定往后推（第 12 条）、别拿死当还债的办法（第 13 条）。两类数字不互相换算。没有亲人也没有朋友可找的，「找个人盯住」这句话怎么落地见第 6 条。

*The death of a loved one, a serious diagnosis, job loss, divorce. Research finds these share something: it's not just mood that takes the hit, but the body too. The riskiest stretch runs from the first weeks through the first year. This section isn't about "moving on" — it's about what must be watched during that window, which decisions must wait, and when to see a doctor. The legal and institutional content follows current California and U.S. federal rules: for a mental-health crisis call or text 988; unemployment insurance runs through California's EDD. Health-science items keep the original book's evidence and numbers. Most items are judged by mortality. Four items about money are judged by money: don't pay for grief counseling right away (item 9), calling 988 and seeing a mental-health professional (item 11), postponing irreversible big decisions (item 12), and not using death to dodge debt (item 13). The two kinds of numbers are never converted into each other. If you have no family or friends to turn to, item 6 covers how "find someone to watch over you" works in practice.*

亲人去世当时的手续、遗体和该取的钱见第 25 节（原书第 25 节）。老人生前的监护与遗嘱见第 17 节（原书第 17 节）。失业能领什么见第 7 节（原书第 7 节）。情绪低落时的运动与光照见第 3 节和第 22 节（原书第 3 节、原书第 22 节）。有自杀念头时先打 988，见第 1 节第 25 条（原书第 1 节）、念头的时间尺度见第 1 节第 32 条（原书第 1 节）、救回来之后的后遗症见第 1 节第 33 条（原书第 1 节）。这些本节都不重复。

### 1. 亲人刚走的那几天别一个人待着，有心脏病、高血压、糖尿病的照常吃药，胸口压着疼就打 911 / In the first days after a death, don't be alone; keep taking heart, blood pressure, and diabetes meds — chest pressure means calling 911
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。这几天别一个人待着，药照常吃。
  Cost: Free. Don't be alone these days; keep taking your meds.
- 说人话：亲人去世后头一天，心梗发作的概率是平时的 21.1 倍，之后一天比一天低。60 到 89 岁的人在伴侣去世后 30 天内，心梗或中风的比例大约翻倍，一个月后回落。本来就有心血管病的人，这几天别停药。胸口压着疼、喘不上气、一侧手脚没力，立刻打 911。
  In plain terms: In the first 24 hours after losing someone close, heart-attack risk runs 21.1 times the usual, declining day by day after. For ages 60–89, the rate of heart attack or stroke roughly doubles in the 30 days after losing a partner, then falls back. If you already have cardiovascular disease, don't stop your meds these days. Pressure in the chest, shortness of breath, weakness on one side — call 911 immediately.
- 收益：1985 名急性心梗患者，拿同一个人发病前后的不同时间段自己跟自己比。其中 270 人（13.6%）在发病前 6 个月内失去过重要的人，19 人是在心梗前 1 天内失去的。重要的人去世后 24 小时内，急性心梗发作率升高 21.1 倍（95% CI 13.1–34.1）。此后逐日下降。换成实际人数：十年心梗风险 5% 的人里，每 1394 个刚失去亲人的多出 1 例。风险 20% 的人里，每 320 个多出 1 例。英国另一项研究，比了 30447 名 60 至 89 岁丧偶者和 83588 名条件相当的对照。丧偶后 30 天内发生心梗或中风的，丧偶的一边 50 人（0.16%），对照的一边 67 人（0.08%），IRR 2.20（1.52–3.15），即约 2.2 倍。其中心梗 2.14（1.20–3.81），中风 2.40（1.22–4.71）。30 天以后减弱
  Benefit: 1,985 acute-MI patients in a case-crossover design: 270 (13.6%) had lost a significant person in the prior 6 months, 19 within a day before the MI. MI incidence rose 21.1-fold (95% CI 13.1–34.1) in the 24 hours after the death, declining daily thereafter. In absolute terms: among people with 5% ten-year MI risk, 1 extra MI per 1,394 newly bereaved; with 20% risk, 1 per 320. A UK matched cohort of 30,447 widowed people aged 60–89 vs 83,588 controls: MI or stroke within 30 days of bereavement, 50 (0.16%) vs 67 (0.08%), IRR 2.20 (1.52–3.15) — about 2.2×; MI 2.14 (1.20–3.81), stroke 2.40 (1.22–4.71). Attenuated after 30 days.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Mostofsky E, et al. (2012). Risk of acute myocardial infarction after the death of a significant person in one's life: the Determinants of Myocardial Infarction Onset Study. Circulation. <https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.111.061770>；Carey IM, et al. (2014). Increased risk of acute cardiovascular events after partner bereavement: a matched cohort study. JAMA Internal Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2013.14558>
- 备注：药盒交给别人管，别靠自己记。这几天别开长途。别靠喝酒扛过去，酒的账见第 2 节（原书第 2 节）。心梗、中风怎么认、怎么叫车见第 13 节（原书第 13 节）。两项都是回头翻人群数据的研究，分不清里面有多少人本来就要发病。但两项指的方向和时间段一致。没有人可以把药盒交出去、这几天注定一个人在屋里的，见本节第 6 条。/ Note: Hand the pillbox to someone else; don't rely on your own memory. No long drives these days. Don't drink your way through it (see Section 2 — original book, Section 2). Recognizing MI/stroke and calling for help: Section 13 (original book, Section 13). Both studies look backward at population data and can't say how many would have fallen ill anyway — but they agree on direction and timing. If there's no one to hand the pillbox to and you'll be alone, see item 6.

### 2. 被告知重病诊断的那一周，别一个人去拿报告，治疗以外的决定往后放 / The week of a serious diagnosis: don't pick up the report alone; postpone every non-treatment decision
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。找个人陪你去拿报告。把治疗以外的大决定推后几天。
  Cost: Free. Bring someone to get the report with you. Push big non-treatment decisions back a few days.
- 说人话：拿到癌症诊断的第一周，自杀的概率是没确诊的人的 12.6 倍，死于心血管病的概率是 5.6 倍。到第一年，自杀风险降到 3.1 倍，越难治的癌越明显。所以拿报告那天带个人去，回家别一个人待着。辞职、卖房这类事，留到下次门诊再说。
  In plain terms: In the first week after a cancer diagnosis, suicide risk runs 12.6× that of the undiagnosed, cardiovascular death 5.6×. By year one, suicide risk falls to 3.1× — sharper for harder-to-treat cancers. So bring someone on report day, and don't go home to an empty house. Quitting, selling the house — leave all of that for the next appointment.
- 收益：瑞典全国档案跟踪 607 万多人（6,073,240 人），时间是 1991 至 2006 年。和没确诊癌症的人相比，确诊后第一周自杀相对风险 12.6（95% CI 8.6–17.8）。这一周的发病率是 2.50/1000 人年。到第一年降到 3.1（2.7–3.5，0.60/1000 人年）。心血管死亡第一周 5.6（5.2–5.9，116.80/1000 人年）。前 4 周 3.3（3.1–3.4，65.81/1000 人年）。风险升高在确诊后第一年内迅速回落。越是治起来难、活下来概率低的癌种，升得越明显
  Benefit: Swedish national registers, 6,073,240 people, 1991–2006. Vs the undiagnosed, suicide relative risk in the first week after a cancer diagnosis: 12.6 (95% CI 8.6–17.8), incidence 2.50/1,000 person-years that week; falling to 3.1 (2.7–3.5, 0.60/1,000 person-years) by year one. Cardiovascular death: 5.6 (5.2–5.9, 116.80/1,000 person-years) in week one; 3.3 (3.1–3.4, 65.81/1,000 person-years) over the first 4 weeks. The elevation recedes fast within the first year, and is steepest for the least curable cancers.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Fang F, et al. (2012). Suicide and cardiovascular death after a cancer diagnosis. New England Journal of Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1110307>
- 备注：这是癌症病人的数据。换到别的重大诊断，没有同等分量的原文。出事的人其实很少：第一周自杀率 2.50/1000 人年，即 1000 人跟一年出 2.5 例。写出来不是吓人，是说明「刚知道的那几天」要有人在旁边。确诊后的长期管理、保险和复查见第 16 节和第 24 节（原书第 24 节）。找不到人陪着去的见本节第 6 条。/ Note: These are cancer-patient data; other major diagnoses lack equally strong sources. Absolute numbers are small: 2.50/1,000 person-years in week one — 2.5 cases per 1,000 people followed a year. It's written here not to scare but to say: have someone beside you in those first days. Long-term management, insurance, and follow-ups after diagnosis: Sections 16 and 24 (original book, Section 24). If no one can accompany you, see item 6.

### 3. 失业之后先把作息、医保和找工作的节奏固定下来：去 EDD 申领失业保险，别整天待在家里 / After job loss, fix your routine, health coverage, and job-search rhythm: file for unemployment with the EDD
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。先去 EDD 申领失业保险（UI），再把找工作排成固定日程。难在没人催你的时候还要按点起床。
  Cost: Free. File for UI with the EDD first, then put job hunting on a fixed schedule. The hard part is getting up on time with no one pushing you.
- 说人话：失业的人之后死亡的概率比在职的人高约六成，男性和年纪不大的人更明显，头十年最高。把抽烟喝酒的影响扣掉，升幅少了约四分之一，这部分自己能管住。先去 EDD 申领失业保险金：myEDD/UI Online 网上申请，或打 1-800-300-5616；最高 $450/周，最长 26 周；有一周无薪等待期，之后每两周认证一次。失业丢了雇主医保，去 Covered California 走 60 天特殊参保期（coveredca.com），或选 COBRA 续保。
  In plain terms: The unemployed die at about 1.6× the rate of the employed — sharper for men and younger workers, highest in the first ten years. Adjusting for smoking and drinking shaves about a quarter off the excess; that part you can control. File for UI with the EDD first: apply through myEDD/UI Online or call 1-800-300-5616; up to $450/week for up to 26 weeks, with a one-week unpaid waiting period and biweekly certification. Lost your employer health plan with the job? Use Covered California's 60-day special enrollment (coveredca.com) or COBRA continuation.
- 收益：42 项研究、235 个死亡风险估计、逾 2000 万人合起来算。把年龄等条件的影响扣平之后，失业者各种原因加起来的死亡风险平均 HR 为 1.63，比在职的人高约六成。男性高于女性。对处在职业生涯早期和中期的人影响明显，晚期较小。跟踪的头 10 年风险最高，之后下降。在把抽烟喝酒这类健康行为也算进去的那部分研究里，平均 HR 低 24%
  Benefit: 42 studies, 235 mortality risk estimates, 20M+ people pooled. Adjusted for age and other factors, all-cause mortality for the unemployed averaged HR 1.63 — about 60% above the employed. Higher for men than women; sharper in early and mid-career than late. Highest in the first 10 years of follow-up, then declining. In studies that also adjusted for health behaviors like smoking and drinking, the average HR was 24% lower.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Roelfs DJ, et al. (2011). Losing life and livelihood: a systematic review and meta-analysis of unemployment and all-cause mortality. Social Science & Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socscimed.2011.01.005>；California EDD – Unemployment Insurance. <https://edd.ca.gov/en/unemployment>
- 备注：这是回头翻人群数据的研究，分不清因果方向。本来身体差的人也更容易失业。但「把生活习惯那部分影响扣掉后，效应低 24%」给了可着手的方向。失业能领什么、社保怎么续见第 7 节（原书第 7 节）。被辞退该拿多少补偿、别签主动辞职见第 19 节（原书第 19 节）。/ Note: These look-back studies can't sort out which way causation runs — sicker people are also more likely to lose jobs. But "24% lower after adjusting for health behaviors" points at something actionable. What unemployment entitles you to and how to keep coverage: Section 7 (original book, Section 7). Severance amounts and not signing a "voluntary resignation": Section 19 (original book, Section 19).

### 4. 亲人死于自杀、意外或凶案的，别指望硬扛，主动去找专业帮助 / After a suicide, accident, or homicide death in the family: don't white-knuckle it — seek professional help
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：打 988 不花钱。要看医生，走保险看心理医生是普通门诊的自付额。难在自己开口找人。
  Cost: Calling 988 is free. Seeing a doctor costs a regular visit copay with insurance. The hard part is reaching out.
- 说人话：配偶死于自杀的人，五年内被查出精神障碍的概率比一般人高约八成（男性）、七成（女性）。和配偶死于其他原因的人比，也高七成到一倍。这类丧亲的人更容易觉得被人疏远、觉得羞耻。这正是要主动去找人的理由，别等自己好起来。
  In plain terms: People widowed by a partner's suicide are diagnosed with a mental disorder within five years at about 1.8× (men) and 1.7× (women) the general rate — and 1.7–2.0× vs those widowed by other causes. Suicide-bereaved people more often feel shunned and ashamed. That's exactly why you go find help instead of waiting to feel better.
- 收益：丹麦全国档案跟踪 670 万成年人，1980 至 2014 年，合计逾 1.36 亿人年。因配偶自杀而丧偶的人，5 年内新查出精神障碍的风险和一般人比，IRR 男性 1.8（95% CI 1.6–2.0），即高约八成；女性 1.7（1.6–1.8），即高约七成。和因其他死因丧偶的人相比，IRR 男性 1.7（1.5–1.9）、女性 2.0（1.9–2.2）。另有 57 项研究逐项归纳：因自杀丧亲，和伴侣自杀风险升高、子女自杀后父母住进精神科风险升高、成年子女自杀后母亲自杀风险升高、父母自杀后子女抑郁风险升高相关；和因其他暴力死亡丧亲的人相比，因自杀丧亲的人更常说自己被人疏远、觉得羞耻
  Benefit: Danish national registers, 6.7M adults, 1980–2014, 136M+ person-years. Suicide-widowed people: 5-year risk of a newly diagnosed mental disorder vs the general population, IRR 1.8 (95% CI 1.6–2.0) for men (~80% higher), 1.7 (1.6–1.8) for women (~70% higher); vs those widowed by other causes, IRR 1.7 (1.5–1.9) men, 2.0 (1.9–2.2) women. A 57-study review adds: suicide bereavement is linked to higher partner suicide risk, higher psychiatric admission for parents after a child's suicide, higher maternal suicide risk after an adult child's suicide, and higher depression risk for children after a parent's suicide; vs other violent-death bereavement, the suicide-bereaved more often report feeling shunned and ashamed.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Erlangsen A, et al. (2017). Association Between Spousal Suicide and Mental, Physical, and Social Health Outcomes: A Longitudinal and Nationwide Register-Based Study. JAMA Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapsychiatry.2017.0226>；Pitman A, et al. (2014). Effects of suicide bereavement on mental health and suicide risk. The Lancet Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2215-0366(14)70224-X>
- 备注：突然的、暴力性的死亡，指的是事故、凶案、灾害。这类死亡之后，创伤后应激障碍、抑郁和哀伤卡住的比例都比病亡后高，恢复也更慢。自己有自杀念头时的处置见第 1 节第 25 条（原书第 1 节）。/ Note: Sudden violent death means accidents, homicides, disasters. After these, PTSD, depression, and stuck grief run higher than after illness deaths, and recovery is slower. If suicidal thoughts appear, see Section 1, item 25 (original book, Section 1).

### 5. 丧偶后的头半年，把三餐、睡觉、慢性病的药和复查交给一个固定的人盯着 / For the first six months of widowhood, put meals, sleep, meds, and checkups under one specific person's watch
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。找一个具体的人，子女、兄弟姐妹或者朋友都行，让他定期上门或者打电话。难在要开口把这件事托付出去。
  Cost: Free. Name one specific person — a child, sibling, or friend — to visit or call regularly. The hard part is asking.
- 说人话：丧偶后不到半年，同期死亡的概率比没丧偶的人高约四成。半年以后降到高约一成半。男性高约两成三，女性那一档小到可能只是巧合。这个差不是靠想开点能扛过去的。该做的是把药、饭和复查落到一个具体的人身上，别指望自己记得住。
  In plain terms: In the first six months of widowhood, mortality runs about 40% above the non-widowed; after six months it drops to about 15% above. Men run ~23% higher; the women's figure is small enough to be chance. No amount of positive thinking closes that gap. Put the pills, the meals, and the checkups on one specific person — don't count on remembering yourself.
- 收益：15 项先登记再往后跟踪的研究、2,263,888 人合起来算。丧偶不足 6 个月时死亡 RR 1.41（95% CI 1.26–1.57），即高约四成。6 个月以后是 1.14（1.10–1.18），降到高约一成半。男性 RR 1.23（1.18–1.28），高约两成三。女性 1.04（1.00–1.08，无统计学意义）。另一项分析合并了 123 篇文献、1377 个风险估计、逾 5 亿人。把条件差异扣平后 HR 1.23（1.19–1.28），即整体高约两成三；男性 1.27（1.19–1.35），女性 1.15（1.08–1.22）
  Benefit: 15 prospective studies, 2,263,888 people pooled: mortality RR 1.41 (95% CI 1.26–1.57) in the first 6 months of widowhood (~40% higher), 1.14 (1.10–1.18) afterward (~15% higher). Men RR 1.23 (1.18–1.28); women 1.04 (1.00–1.08, not significant). A second analysis of 123 papers, 1,377 estimates, 500M+ people: adjusted HR 1.23 (1.19–1.28) overall (~23% higher); men 1.27 (1.19–1.35), women 1.15 (1.08–1.22).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Moon JR, et al. (2011). Widowhood and mortality: a meta-analysis. PLoS ONE. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0023465>；Shor E, et al. (2012). Widowhood and mortality: a meta-analysis and meta-regression. Demography. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s13524-012-0096-x>
- 备注：这是回头翻人群数据的研究，别的因素掺进来去不掉。夫妻的生活方式和疾病本来就相似。但「头半年比之后高得多」这个规律，在两份各自独立的汇总分析里都一样，值得当成一段照看窗口。男性风险更高，也更少开口求助。家里有丧偶老人的，按第 17 节把账户、监护和遗嘱一并理清（原书第 17 节）。找不到这样一个人的（独居、失独、子女也不在了），把这件事交给社区和手机，见本节第 6 条。/ Note: These look-back studies can't fully strip out confounding — spouses share lifestyles and illnesses. But "the first six months are much worse than later" shows up in two independent meta-analyses, so treat it as a watch window. Men face higher risk and ask for help less. For a widowed elder in the family, sort out accounts, guardianship, and wills per Section 17 (original book, Section 17). If no such person exists (living alone, no children left), hand this to the community and your phone — see item 6.

### 8. 哀伤过了半年还在原地、日子过不下去，去看精神科医生或心理医生 / If grief is still stuck after six months and life is unlivable, see a psychiatrist or psychologist
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：走保险看一次是普通门诊的自付额。这类治疗按疗程做，试验里平均 19 周做 16 次。难在要自己迈出去挂这个号。
  Cost: With insurance, one visit costs a regular copay. This kind of therapy runs in courses — 16 sessions over 19 weeks on average in the trial. The hard part is making the appointment.
- 说人话：大约每十个丧亲的成年人里有一个会卡住：半年以上还在持续想念到吃不下饭、上不了班、带不了孩子，人麻木，觉得生活没有意义。这不是不够坚强，它有诊断标准（延长哀伤障碍），也有专门的治疗。针对性治疗的有效率 51%，普通心理治疗是 28%，起效也更快。
  In plain terms: About one in ten bereaved adults gets stuck: six-plus months of yearning so intense they can't eat, work, or parent — numb, life feeling meaningless. - 收益：14 项研究合起来算，对象是亲人不是死于暴力、本人也没在精神科就诊的成年人。延长哀伤障碍患病率 9.8%（95% CI 6.8–14.0），即每 100 个丧亲的成年人里约 10 个。年龄越大比例越高。诊断的核心表现是对逝者的强烈想念，再加上九项里的至少五项（情感麻木、震惊、觉得生活没有意义、不信任他人、因这次失去而痛苦、难以接受、认不清自己该是谁、回避现实、无法继续生活），每天都出现或严重到干不了事，在亲人去世至少 6 个月后仍然存在，还影响日常生活。95 人抽签分两组的试验，做 16 次治疗、平均 19 周：针对复杂哀伤的治疗有效率 51%，普通的人际心理治疗 28%（P=0.02），前者起效也更快，需治疗人数 4.3

- 来源：Lundorff M, et al. (2017). Prevalence of prolonged grief disorder in adult bereavement: A systematic review and meta-analysis. ### 9. 别一上来就花钱做哀伤咨询，先看自己的哀伤是不是真的卡住了（第 8 条那几条症状） / Don't pay for grief counseling right away — first check whether your grief is actually stuck (the symptoms in item 8)
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱，反过来省下一笔咨询费。
  Cost: Free — it saves a counseling bill instead.
- 说人话：给所有丧亲的人都安排心理咨询，好处很小，过一阵就看不出来了，因为多数人本来会自己缓过来。但对已经明显适应不了的人，治疗和治别的心理问题一样有效。所以先对照第 8 条，对上了再花这笔钱。
  In plain terms: Counseling for every bereaved person helps little, and the effect fades — because most people recover on their own. - 成本：不花钱，只是把决定往后推，可能因此错过某些机会。难在当时会觉得机会不等人。
  Cost: Free — it just delays decisions, possibly missing some opportunities. The hard part is feeling that opportunity won't wait.
- 说人话：这段时间你对各种「机会」的判断力最差。说能联系逝者的、说能帮你把钱翻回来的、劝你辞职去外地重新开始的、拉你合伙的，都会找上来。金额超过一个月收入、或者反悔代价很大的事，一律推后三个月。先讲给一个跟这件事没利益关系的人听，别当天拍板。
  In plain terms: Right now your judgment about "opportunities" is at its worst. The mediums who can contact the dead, the schemes that'll win your money back, the voices urging you to quit and start over elsewhere, the partnership pitches — they all show up now. Anything over a month's income, or anything hard to undo, waits three months. First tell it to someone with no stake in it; never decide the same day.
- 收益：避免在刚受打击、判断力最差的那段时间，做出反悔不了的财产处置。这类事包括卖房、辞职、把抚恤金或赔偿款一次性投出去、仓促再婚、替人担保。这类决定的金额通常在数万美元到一套房子这个量级，收益因此定为中
  Benefit: Avoids irreversible financial moves made at your worst judgment. That includes selling the house, quitting, investing a death benefit or settlement in one shot, hasty remarriage, and co-signing for someone. These decisions typically run from tens of thousands of dollars to a whole house — hence the medium grade.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验，无直接文献 / Author's experience; no direct literature
- 备注：身边没有这样一个没利益关系的人，就打 988 讲一遍（见本节第 11 条），或者自己写下来隔三天再读一遍。「三个月」是作者自己划的线，没有文献支持这个具体时长，所以记 C 级。针对丧亲者的常见骗局，留神「投资养老」「以房养老」类话术（见第 17 节，原书第 17 节）。有几笔钱要主动去办，不在「往后推」之列：雇主的人寿保险、401(k)/IRA 受益人手续、社保遗属福利，见第 25 节（原书第 25 节）。/ Note: With no disinterested person around, talk it through on 988 (see item 11) or write it down and re-read it three days later. "Three months" is the author's own line — no literature backs that exact duration, hence grade C. Watch for bereavement-targeted scams, especially "invest for retirement" and "home-equity for retirement" pitches (see Section 17 — original book, Section 17). ### 13. 别拿死当还债的办法：寿险头两年自杀不赔，工伤不认自伤，债先从遗产里扣 / Don't use death to dodge debt: life insurance won't pay for suicide in the first two years, workers' comp doesn't cover self-harm, and debts come out of the estate first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱，只是把这笔账算清。
  Cost: Free — just doing the math.
- 说人话：美国寿险几乎都有两年自杀免责条款：保单生效两年内自杀，保险公司不赔保额，只退已交保费；两年后一般照赔。加州 Ins. Code §10113.5 还规定，保单生效满两年后不可 contest（不可再以投保时的陈述问题拒赔）。工伤保险一般不赔故意自伤或自杀（具体法条待核实）。欠的债先从遗产里还，走 probate（遗嘱认证）程序按顺序清偿（具体法条待核实），继承人以继承所得为限，不用自己再掏。他们还要承受本节第 4 条说的那份健康代价。
  In plain terms: Nearly all U.S. life insurance has a two-year suicide exclusion: suicide within two years of issue means no death benefit, only premiums returned; after two years it generally pays. California's Ins. Code §10113.5 also makes a policy incontestable after two years in force. Workers' comp generally doesn't cover intentional self-injury or suicide (exact statute to be verified). Debts are paid out of the estate first through probate, in statutory order (exact statute to be verified); heirs are liable only up to what they inherit — no reaching into their own pockets. And the family still pays the health price described in item 4.
- 收益：两年自杀条款是行业通行做法；加州 Ins. Code §10113.5(a)：在加州签发的人寿保单，在被保险人生存的两年之后不可 contest（除欠缴保费等少数例外）。寿险保额通常在数十万美元级，收益因此定为大
  Benefit: The two-year suicide clause is industry standard; Cal. Ins. Code §10113.5(a): an individual life policy issued in California becomes incontestable after two years in force during the insured's lifetime (with narrow exceptions like nonpayment). Life insurance face amounts typically run in the hundreds of thousands of dollars — hence the large grade.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Cal. Ins. Code §10113.5. <https://www.casemine.com/act/us/643920acd0293739de1c0d70>；寿险两年自杀条款与 contestability 的行业说明. <https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/life-insurance-contestability%3A-7-things-to-know-2013-09-06>
- 备注：这里只算钱这一笔账。本节和第 1 节的其他条目说的是别走到这一步。§10113.5 管的是两年后的不可 contest，自杀免责本身写在保单条款里，两年是行业通行线（具体以保单为准）。念头冒出来时打 988，见第 1 节第 25 条（原书第 1 节）。家人的健康代价见本节第 4 条。/ Note: This is only the money math.
