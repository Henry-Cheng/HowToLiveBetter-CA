# 20. 刚出生的孩子怎么带·加州版

口径：婴儿死亡率与金钱。这一节只收关系到孩子能不能活下来、或者你要花多少钱的事，而且只收差别足够大、又有硬证据的。怎么哄、怎么带这些育儿细节，本书不写。健康科学类条目的证据和数字沿用原书；疫苗条目按美国 CDC 2026 年儿童免疫时间表本地化，出生证明和社安号按加州流程写。

*Scope: infant mortality and money. This section covers only what affects whether the baby survives or how much you spend — and only where the difference is large enough and backed by hard evidence. Soothing and parenting details aren't in this book. Health-science items keep the original book's evidence and numbers; vaccine items follow the U.S. CDC 2026 childhood immunization schedule, and birth-certificate/SSN items follow California procedures.*

出生证明和社安号（原书放在第 27 节，原书第 27 节）：在医院填出生登记表时勾选申请社安号（Enumeration at Birth），社安局（SSA）会把卡寄到家里；错过的话去当地 SSA 办公室填 SS-5 表申请。出生证明复印件去孩子出生所在县的 county recorder（县书记官）或加州公共卫生部 Vital Records 申请，2026 年每份 $31。

*Birth certificate and Social Security number (covered in original section 27): check the SSN box on the birth registration worksheet at the hospital (Enumeration at Birth) and SSA mails the card to your home; if missed, apply at a local SSA office with Form SS-5. For certified copies of the birth certificate, apply to the county recorder in the county where the child was born or to CDPH Vital Records — $31 per copy in 2026.*

### 1. 让孩子仰着睡，睡硬的平面，同房不同床，床上不放任何软东西 / Put the baby to sleep on their back, on a firm flat surface, in your room but not in your bed, with nothing soft in the sleep area
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。床垫要换成硬的，$50–$150。难在要顶住长辈的说法，一直按这个来。
  Cost: Free. A firm mattress costs $50–$150. The hard part is holding the line against grandparents' objections.
- 说人话：让孩子脸朝上躺着睡，睡在硬的平面上。大人和孩子睡同一间房，但不睡同一张床。床上不放枕头、被子、床围、毛绒玩具和定型枕。斜着的摇篮和婴儿椅只能坐，不能当床睡。光美国每年就有约 3500 个婴儿死在睡眠里。
  In plain terms: Lay the baby face-up on a firm, flat surface. Share the room with the baby, but not the bed. No pillows, blankets, bumpers, stuffed animals, or positioners in the sleep area. Inclined sleepers and baby chairs are for sitting, not sleeping. About 3,500 U.S. infants still die of sleep-related causes each year.
- 收益：美国每年约 3500 名婴儿死于睡眠相关原因。这里面包括婴儿猝死综合征、死因不明和在床上意外憋住。美国儿科学会给的安全睡眠环境是"仰卧位；使用坚实、不倾斜的睡眠表面；同房不同床；避免柔软的寝具和过热"。还有几件事也能降低风险：母乳喂养、别让孩子接触尼古丁和酒精、按时接种疫苗、使用安抚奶嘴。
  Benefit: About 3,500 U.S. infants die of sleep-related causes each year, including SIDS, unknown causes, and accidental suffocation in bed. The American Academy of Pediatrics' safe-sleep environment: "back to sleep; a firm, flat sleep surface; room-sharing without bed-sharing; avoiding soft bedding and overheating." Other risk reducers: breastfeeding, no nicotine or alcohol exposure, on-time vaccination, and pacifier use.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Moon RY, Carlin RF, Hand I, Task Force on Sudden Infant Death Syndrome and the Committee on Fetus and Newborn (2022). Sleep-Related Infant Deaths: Updated 2022 Recommendations for Reducing Infant Deaths in the Sleep Environment. Pediatrics, 150(1), e2022057990. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2022-057990>
- 备注："床上不放任何软东西"是指枕头、被子、床围、毛绒玩具、定型枕全都不放。看起来很安全的哺乳枕也不放。斜着的摇篮和婴儿椅不能当睡觉的地方。这一节里，这件事的差别最大，也最需要顶住长辈的压力。/ Note: "Nothing soft" means no pillows, blankets, bumpers, stuffed animals, or positioners. Not even a nursing pillow that looks safe. Inclined sleepers and baby chairs are not sleep spaces. Of everything in this section, this one makes the biggest difference — and takes the most resolve against grandparents.

### 2. 出生后 24 小时内打上乙肝疫苗第一针 / Give the first hepatitis B shot within 24 hours of birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。医院出生当天就打，费用含在住院账单里，大多数保险覆盖。
  Cost: Free at the point of care. Given at the hospital on the day of birth, billed with the delivery; most insurance covers it.
- 说人话：孩子出生后 24 小时之内要打上第一针乙肝疫苗。在婴儿期和幼儿期感染乙肝的人里，约有 95% 会转成慢性肝炎。成年以后才感染的，绝大多数能自己好。所以这一针越早打越有用。妈妈乙肝表面抗原是阳性的，孩子还要同时打乙肝免疫球蛋白（HBIG）。
  In plain terms: The baby should get the first hepatitis B shot within 24 hours of birth. About 95% of people infected with hepatitis B as infants or toddlers develop chronic hepatitis; adults infected later mostly clear it on their own. So the earlier this shot, the more it matters. If the mother is hepatitis B surface-antigen positive, the baby also needs hepatitis B immune globulin (HBIG).
- 收益：世界卫生组织的说法是："所有婴儿都应在出生后尽早（24 小时内）接种乙肝疫苗。"原因是"婴儿期和幼儿期感染约有 95% 会发展为慢性肝炎"。成年后感染的绝大多数能自愈（不用治也会好）。美国儿科学会同样建议所有新生儿在出生 24 小时内打第一针。
  Benefit: The World Health Organization states: "All infants should receive the hepatitis B vaccine as soon as possible after birth (within 24 hours)," because "about 95% of infections in infancy and early childhood become chronic." Most adult infections clear on their own. The American Academy of Pediatrics likewise recommends the first dose within 24 hours of birth for all newborns.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：World Health Organization. Hepatitis B fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/hepatitis-b>
- 备注：2026 年 1 月 CDC 修订的儿童免疫时间表把出生乙肝第一针改为"医患共同决策"（母亲乙肝阴性且状态明确时，建议第一针不早于 2 月龄），该修订争议较大，儿科医生普遍仍建议出生 24 小时内打——打之前直接问你的儿科医生。妈妈的乙肝表面抗原是阳性的，孩子出生后还要同时打一针乙肝免疫球蛋白。这件事产检的时候就要查清楚，别等生完再问。/ Note: The CDC's January 2026 schedule revision moved the birth HepB dose to "shared clinical decision-making" (for infants of mothers confirmed HepB-negative, the first dose is suggested no earlier than 2 months). That change is contested, and most pediatricians still recommend the birth dose — ask your pediatrician directly. If the mother is HepB-positive, the baby also needs HBIG at birth. Confirm the mother's HepB status during prenatal care, not after delivery.

### 3. 按 CDC 儿童免疫时间表把该打的疫苗打齐；没保险的孩子有联邦免费疫苗计划 / Follow the CDC childhood immunization schedule; uninsured children get free vaccines through the federal VFC program
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：疫苗本身对符合条件的孩子免费。保险覆盖的按常规儿科门诊走；没保险、低收入或保险不覆盖疫苗的孩子，走联邦 Vaccines for Children（VFC）计划免费打。要按月龄跑几趟儿科诊所。免疫记录收好，别弄丢。
  Cost: The vaccines themselves are free for eligible children. With insurance, they're part of routine pediatric visits; uninsured, low-income, or vaccine-uncovered children get them free through the federal Vaccines for Children (VFC) program. It takes several clinic trips by age. Keep the immunization record safe.
- 说人话：2026 年版 CDC 时间表里，所有儿童常规推荐的疫苗防 11 种病：百白破（DTaP）、b 型流感嗜血杆菌（Hib）、肺炎球菌（PCV）、脊灰（IPV）、麻腮风（MMR）、水痘、HPV。另有乙肝、轮状病毒、甲肝、流感、新冠、脑膜炎球菌按"医患共同决策"或高危儿童推荐，具体听儿科医生的。孩子入托、入学都要查免疫记录（加州入学免疫要求见 CDPH），漏打的会被查出来补上。错过了只补没打完的那几剂，不用从头再打。
  In plain terms: The 2026 CDC schedule routinely recommends vaccines against 11 diseases for all children: DTaP, Hib, pneumococcal (PCV), polio (IPV), MMR, varicella, and HPV. Hepatitis B, rotavirus, hepatitis A, flu, COVID-19, and meningococcal vaccines are recommended via "shared clinical decision-making" or for high-risk children — follow your pediatrician. Childcare and school entry require immunization records (California school requirements via CDPH); missed doses get caught and must be made up. If you fall behind, you only make up the missed doses — never restart the series.
- 收益：CDC 2026 年 1 月修订的时间表（常规推荐 11 种疫苗防 11 种病：白喉、破伤风、百日咳、Hib、肺炎球菌、脊灰、麻疹、腮腺炎、风疹、HPV、水痘）。联邦 VFC 计划为 18 岁及以下、没保险、保险不覆盖疫苗、符合 Medicaid 资格、或美洲原住民/阿拉斯加原住民的孩子免费提供疫苗。加州入学时学校查验免疫记录。
  Benefit: The CDC's January 2026 schedule (routine: 11 vaccines against 11 diseases — diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis, Hib, pneumococcal, polio, measles, mumps, rubella, HPV, varicella). The federal VFC program provides free vaccines to children 18 and younger who are uninsured, underinsured, Medicaid-eligible, or American Indian/Alaska Native. California schools check immunization records at entry.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：CDC 2026 儿童免疫时间表（按推荐组别）. <https://web.archive.org/web/20260611001550/https://www.hhs.gov/childhood-immunization-schedule/index.html>；CDC. Vaccines for Children (VFC) Program Eligibility. <https://cdc.gov/vaccines-for-children/hcp/program-eligibility/>；CDC. VFC Information for Parents. <https://cdc.gov/vaccines-for-children/vfc-information-for-parents/>
- 备注：最常见的误会是"孩子体质不好，先别打"。真正不能打的只有：以前打同一种疫苗出现过严重的急性过敏反应，或对疫苗里某种已知成分严重过敏。有免疫缺陷、正在化疗放疗或用免疫抑制剂的孩子，要单独评估，去诊所说明情况再定。湿疹、食物过敏、过敏性鼻炎、哮喘病情稳定时，新生儿黄疸也一样，都能正常打。加州学校入学具体要求见 CDPH 学校免疫页面（链接待核实）。乙肝第一针见第 2 条。/ Note: The most common misunderstanding is "the child is fragile, let's wait." True contraindications are only: a severe acute allergic reaction to a previous dose of the same vaccine, or severe allergy to a known vaccine component. Children with immune deficiency, on chemo/radiation, or on immunosuppressants need individual evaluation — tell the clinic first. Eczema, food allergies, allergic rhinitis, stable asthma, and newborn jaundice are all fine for vaccination. For California school-entry specifics see CDPH's school immunization page (link to be verified). Birth HepB dose: see item 2.

### 4. 前 6 个月只喂母乳，连水都不用喂，6 个月起加辅食并继续母乳 / Exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months — not even water — then solids at 6 months while continuing to breastfeed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，还省下奶粉钱。费的是时间，喂奶每天都要占掉不少。难在要一直喂下去，前 6 个月不能断。
  Cost: Free, and saves formula money. It costs time — nursing takes up a big chunk of every day. The hard part is keeping it up without interruption for the first 6 months.
- 说人话：孩子出生后第一个小时里就开始喂奶。前 6 个月只喂母乳，连水都不用喂。满 6 个月起加辅食，每天要有含铁多的食物，母乳接着喂到 2 岁或更久。母乳不够或者不能喂，就用配方奶，不用自责。喂什么对孩子安危的影响，远没有睡姿大。
  In plain terms: Start nursing within the first hour after birth. Exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months — not even water. From 6 months, add solids including iron-rich foods daily, and keep breastfeeding to age 2 or beyond. If there's not enough milk or nursing isn't possible, use formula without guilt. What you feed matters far less for safety than sleep position.
- 收益：世界卫生组织的建议是"在出生后第一个小时内开始母乳喂养"。前 6 个月"纯母乳喂养，即不提供其他任何食物或液体，包括水"。然后"从 6 月龄起开始添加安全充足的辅食，同时继续母乳喂养到 2 岁或以上"。美国儿科学会同样建议纯母乳喂养约 6 个月。
  Benefit: The World Health Organization recommends "initiation of breastfeeding within the first hour of birth," exclusive breastfeeding for the first 6 months — "no other food or liquid, including water" — then "safe and adequate complementary foods from 6 months while continuing breastfeeding up to 2 years or beyond." The American Academy of Pediatrics likewise recommends exclusive breastfeeding for about 6 months.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：World Health Organization. Breastfeeding. <https://www.who.int/health-topics/breastfeeding>
- 备注：母乳不够，或者身体不允许喂，就用配方奶。喂母乳还是喂配方奶，差距远小于安全睡眠那条。加辅食后每天吃的东西要多样，含铁丰富的食物（肉泥、强化铁的米粉）优先。孩子有重度湿疹或鸡蛋过敏时，花生要不要躲着，见本节第 12 条（别躲着花生）。/ Note: If there's not enough milk or nursing isn't medically possible, use formula. Breast vs. formula matters far less than safe sleep. Once solids start, offer variety with iron-rich foods (meat purees, iron-fortified cereals) first. For infants with severe eczema or egg allergy, whether to avoid peanut — see item 12 (don't avoid peanut).

### 5. 冲奶粉用 70 ℃ 以上的水，冲好放凉再喂，喝剩的倒掉 / Mix formula with water at 70 °C or hotter, cool before feeding, discard leftovers
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。每次多等几分钟把奶放凉。难在孩子饿得哭的时候，也要等水温降下来再冲。
  Cost: Free. A few extra minutes to cool the bottle each time. The hard part is waiting for the water to cool even when the baby is screaming with hunger.
- 说人话：奶粉不是无菌的，可能带一种叫阪崎克罗诺杆菌的细菌。新生儿一旦感染，报告的病死率在四成到八成之间。冲的时候先把热水倒进奶瓶，等水温降到约 73.8 ℃ 再放奶粉。这时奶液还在 70 ℃ 以上，能把这种菌杀到原来的十万分之一以下。冲好放凉再喂，喝剩的倒掉。
  In plain terms: Powdered formula isn't sterile and can carry Cronobacter sakazakii. Infected newborns face a reported 40–80% fatality rate. Pour hot water into the bottle first and let it cool to about 73.8 °C (165 °F) before adding powder — the mixture stays above 70 °C (158 °F), killing the bacteria down to less than one hundred-thousandth of the original load. Cool before feeding; throw away leftovers.
- 收益：奶粉不是无菌的，可能带阪崎克罗诺杆菌。新生儿感染后的病死率，报告为 40% 到 80%。实验研究显示：先把热水倒进奶瓶，等水温降到约 73.8 ℃ 再加奶粉，绝大多数情况下奶液温度还在 70 ℃ 以上。这样可以让这种菌减少 5 个对数级以上，降到原来的十万分之一以下。
  Benefit: Powdered formula isn't sterile and may carry Cronobacter sakazakii, with reported neonatal fatality of 40–80%. Experimental work shows pouring hot water into the bottle first and adding powder once it cools to about 73.8 °C keeps the mixture above 70 °C in the vast majority of cases, cutting the bacteria by more than 5 log units — to under one hundred-thousandth of the original load.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Beary MA, Daly SE, Baker J, Snyder AB (2025). Assessing Hot Water Reconstitution Instructions and Labeling of Powdered Infant Formula to Ensure Cronobacter spp. Reduction. Journal of Food Protection, 88(9), 100571. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfp.2025.100571>；World Health Organization & FAO (2007). Safe preparation, storage and handling of powdered infant formula: guidelines. <https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789241595414>
- 备注：70 ℃ 这个门槛来自公共卫生指南。Beary 等人的原话是"针对高危婴儿照护者的公共卫生指南建议用加热到至少 70 ℃（158 ℉）的水冲调配方奶粉以灭活微生物"。早产儿、体重偏低的孩子和不满 2 个月的孩子风险最高。别用保温瓶里放了一天的水来冲。也别把冲好的奶搁在室温下，等着下一顿再喂。CDC 的配方奶冲调指引说法一致。/ Note: The 70 °C threshold comes from public health guidance; Beary et al. note that guidelines for caregivers of high-risk infants recommend water heated to at least 70 °C (158 °F) to inactivate microbes. Preterm, low-birth-weight, and under-2-month infants are at highest risk. Don't use water that's been sitting in a thermos all day. Don't leave mixed formula at room temperature for the next feed. CDC's formula preparation guidance agrees.

### 6. 不满 1 岁不喂蜂蜜 / No honey before age 1
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。
  Cost: Free.
- 说人话：孩子不满 1 岁，不要喂蜂蜜。蜂蜜水、拌进辅食里的蜂蜜、含蜂蜜的糕点，都算。蜂蜜里可能带有引起肉毒中毒的细菌。大人肚子里的菌群能压住它，婴儿压不住。自家做的腌菜和发酵食品，也是同样的道理。
  In plain terms: No honey for children under 1 — not honey water, not honey mixed into solids, not honey-containing pastries. Honey can carry the bacteria that cause botulism. Adult gut flora keeps it in check; infants' can't. Home-fermented pickles and ferments are the same story.
- 收益：美国疾控中心的说法是"不要给 1 岁以下的儿童喂蜂蜜"，因为"蜂蜜可能含有引起肉毒中毒的细菌"。成年人的肠道菌群能压住这种芽孢（这种细菌的休眠状态），婴儿压不住。
  Benefit: The U.S. CDC says "do not give honey to children younger than 12 months" because "honey can contain the bacteria that cause infant botulism." Adult gut flora suppresses the spores; infants' can't.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：US CDC. Preventing Botulism. <https://www.cdc.gov/botulism/prevention/index.html>
- 备注：不能喂的包括蜂蜜水、拌了蜂蜜的辅食、含蜂蜜的糕点。同样的道理，自家做的腌菜和发酵食品，也不要给婴儿吃。/ Note: Off-limits includes honey water, honey-mixed solids, and honey pastries. Same logic for home pickles and fermented foods — keep them away from infants.

### 7. 出生时的维生素 K 针一定要打 / Make sure the newborn gets the vitamin K shot at birth
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：医院常规操作，费用含在住院账单里，不用另外交。
  Cost: Routine hospital procedure, bundled into the delivery bill — nothing extra to pay.
- 说人话：新生儿身体里存的维生素 K 很少，不补可能出血，晚出现的那种常常是脑子里出血。在没普遍打这一针的地方，每 10 万个新生儿有 478 个出这种血。打了这一针，估计能少掉七成八以上。美国医院会常规打，在家生或在不正规机构生的，要主动问一句打了没有。
  In plain terms: Newborns have very little vitamin K stored; without supplementation they can bleed, and the late-onset form is often bleeding in the brain. Where the shot isn't routine, 478 per 100,000 newborns develop this bleeding. The shot cuts it by an estimated 78.5% or more. U.S. hospitals give it routinely; for home births or non-hospital births, ask explicitly whether it was given.
- 收益：新生儿体内存的维生素 K 很少。不补充可能出现维生素 K 缺乏性出血，晚出现的那一种常常表现为颅内出血（脑子里出血）。比利时的共识建议是"出生时肌肉注射 1 或 2 毫克维生素 K 一次"。在没有系统提供预防的地区，维生素 K 缺乏性出血的发生率是每 10 万人 478 例，这个数字来自塔什干的 4.2 万名新生儿。作者估计，做了预防可以使发生率下降"高于 78.5%"。
  Benefit: Newborns store very little vitamin K; deficiency can cause vitamin K deficiency bleeding, with the late-onset form often presenting as intracranial hemorrhage. Belgian consensus recommends "1 or 2 mg of vitamin K intramuscularly once at birth." Where prophylaxis isn't systematic, incidence is 478 per 100,000 — from 42,000 newborns in Tashkent — and the authors estimate prophylaxis cuts it by "more than 78.5%."
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B

Don't substitute sunshine at home: the AAP says it's hard to do safely without sunburn, so it's not recommended as treatment. Don't give water or glucose water for jaundice either — the guideline explicitly advises against it. Eyeballing yellowness can't estimate bilirubin levels; when in doubt, get it measured. Babies born before 38 weeks, with siblings who needed phototherapy, or with inadequate breastfeeding intake are more prone to severe jaundice. Jaundice itself doesn't delay vaccination (see item 3). California newborn screening (heel-stick blood test) is run by CDPH's Genetic Disease Screening Program and done routinely at hospital birth; stool color cards are not routine in the U.S. — ask the pediatrician directly if biliary atresia is a concern (this detail to be verified).
