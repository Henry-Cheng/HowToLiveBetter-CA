# 34. 家里的常备药别吃出事 / Don't Let Your Home Medicine Cabinet Hurt You

退烧药、止痛药、感冒药、胃药、止泻药，很多在药店就能自己买。不用处方能买，不等于怎么吃都行。这一节不讲每种病该吃什么药，只挑一个动作就能避开重后果的几件事。

Fever reducers, painkillers, cold medicines, stomach medicines, anti-diarrheals — many can be bought at the pharmacy without a prescription. Available without a prescription doesn't mean safe however you take them. This section doesn't cover what to take for each illness — only things where one action avoids a serious outcome.

所有条目按死亡率口径算，包括肝衰竭、胃出血、新生儿肾衰竭这类健康终点。收益大多是「少出一次严重事故」，研究给不出「做了能少几成」的数字，收益量级是按后果轻重判断的，每条备注里写了凭什么定。

All items are measured in mortality terms, including endpoints like liver failure, stomach bleeding, and neonatal kidney failure. Benefits are mostly "one fewer serious accident"; studies can't give "how many percent fewer" numbers, so benefit size is judged by severity of outcome, with the reasoning in each item's note.

已经写在别处的，这里只指路。误服了药或清洁剂先做什么，见第 13 节第 20 条（误服）。慢性病的药按医嘱吃满，见第 16 节第 1 条（药按医嘱吃满）。网上买处方药要先过处方审核，见第 28 节第 6 条（减肥药就去医院拿处方）。

What's covered elsewhere is just signposted here. For what to do first after accidentally swallowing medicine or cleaners, see section 13, item 20 (accidental ingestion). For taking chronic-disease medicines as prescribed, see section 16, item 1. For prescription review before buying prescription drugs online, see section 28, item 6.

本节条目的受益人是你自己和家人，属于最高的两档。孩子或家人误服了药，先打美国中毒控制中心 Poison Control 1-800-222-1222（24 小时）。

The beneficiaries of this section's items are you and your family — the top two tiers. If a child or family member swallows medicine by accident, first call the U.S. Poison Control Center at 1-800-222-1222 (24 hours).

### 1. 同时吃两种感冒药或止痛药之前，先看成分表，对乙酰氨基酚只能占一种 / Before taking two cold or pain medicines at once, check the ingredient labels — acetaminophen should appear in only one
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。看一眼每盒药的成分表，一两分钟。
  Cost: Free. One look at each box's ingredient label — a minute or two.
- 说人话：很多感冒药里都掺了对乙酰氨基酚（acetaminophen，泰诺 Tylenol 的主要成分），两盒一起吃就是吃了双份，过量会伤肝。在美国，它造成的急性肝衰竭病人里，近三成没等到换肝就死了。吃药期间也别喝酒，别超过标签上的一天最大量。
  In plain terms: Many cold medicines contain acetaminophen (the main ingredient in Tylenol) — take two boxes together and you've taken a double dose, and overdose injures the liver. In the U.S., of acute liver failure patients harmed by it, nearly 30% died before getting a transplant. Don't drink while taking it, and don't exceed the label's maximum daily amount.
- 收益：美国 22 家医院用 6 年时间连续登记了 662 例急性肝衰竭。其中 275 例（42%）是对乙酰氨基酚造成的，吞下的剂量中位数是 24 克。这 275 人里，131 人（48%）是不小心吃多的，不是故意。不小心吃多的人里，38% 同时吃了两种以上含这个成分的药。这 275 人里，27% 没等到换肝就死了，8% 做了肝移植。美国法规要求这类非处方药在警示第一条写明三种伤肝情形：超过一天最大量，和其他含对乙酰氨基酚的药同吃，用药期间每天喝 3 杯以上的酒。
  Benefit: 22 U.S. hospitals prospectively registered 662 cases of acute liver failure over 6 years. 275 (42%) were caused by acetaminophen, with a median ingested dose of 24 g. Of these 275, 131 (48%) were unintentional overdoses. Among unintentional overdoses, 38% had taken two or more products containing it. Of the 275, 27% died before transplant and 8% received a liver transplant. U.S. regulations require these OTC products to list three liver-injury scenarios first in their warnings: exceeding the maximum daily amount, taking it with other acetaminophen-containing drugs, and drinking 3 or more alcoholic drinks daily while using it.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Larson AM, Polson J, Fontana RJ, et al.; Acute Liver Failure Study Group (2005). Acetaminophen-induced acute liver failure: results of a United States multicenter, prospective study. Hepatology, 42(6):1364-1372. <https://doi.org/10.1002/hep.20948>；21 CFR 201.326(a)(1)(iii)(A) Liver warning. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-201/subpart-G/section-201.326>
- 备注：对乙酰氨基酚也叫扑热息痛（中国大陆的叫法）。复方感冒药的成分表里写着 "acetaminophen" 的，都含这个成分。拿不准就把几盒药都拿给药店的药师看。定 B 是因为美国那组数字是病例登记，没有对照组，算不出看了成分表能少出多少事。收益定「大」是按后果定的：急性肝衰竭里有近三成直接死亡。
  Note: Acetaminophen is also called paracetamol (the name used in mainland China). Any combination cold medicine whose label lists "acetaminophen" contains it. When in doubt, take all the boxes to the pharmacy and show the pharmacist. Grade B because the U.S. numbers are a case registry with no control group — you can't compute how many incidents checking labels prevents. Benefit is rated "large" by consequence: nearly 30% of these acute liver failure cases die outright.

### 2. 孩子发烧不用阿司匹林、尼美舒利和安乃近，含安乃近的感冒药也不给未成年人吃 / For a child's fever, don't use aspirin, nimesulide, or metamizole — and don't give minors cold medicines containing metamizole
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。买退烧药时看一眼通用名和成分表。
  Cost: Free. Check the generic name and ingredient label when buying fever reducers.
- 说人话：孩子退烧，这三种药都别用。阿司匹林（aspirin）和瑞氏综合征有关，这种病伤脑伤肝，美国统计里约三成病孩死亡，警告发出后病例少了九成多。尼美舒利（nimesulide）和安乃近（metamizole/dipyrone）在美国根本没上市，FDA 没批准过（待核实）。从国内带来的感冒药里可能含安乃近，同样别给孩子吃。
  In plain terms: For a child's fever, don't use any of these three. Aspirin is linked to Reye's syndrome, which injures the brain and liver — about 30% of affected children died in U.S. statistics, and cases dropped by over 90% after warnings went out. Nimesulide and metamizole (dipyrone) aren't even on the U.S. market — never FDA-approved (to be verified). Cold medicines brought from China may contain metamizole; don't give those to children either.
- 收益：瑞氏综合征会引起脑病和肝脏脂肪变性，通常出现在流感或水痘之后。美国 1981 到 1997 年的全国监测里，18 岁以下报告了 1207 例。82% 的病孩血里测得到水杨酸，这是阿司匹林这类药的成分。总病死率 31%。1980 年开始发布水杨酸类药的警告。那一年报告了 555 例，是高峰，1987 年起每年不超过 36 例。
  Benefit: Reye's syndrome causes brain disease and fatty degeneration of the liver, usually after flu or chickenpox. In U.S. national surveillance from 1981 to 1997, 1,207 cases were reported in people under 18. 82% of the children had detectable salicylate in their blood — the ingredient in aspirin-type drugs. Overall case fatality was 31%. Warnings about salicylate drugs began in 1980. That year saw 555 reported cases, the peak; from 1987 on, no more than 36 per year.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Belay ED, Bresee JS, Holman RC, Khan AS, Shahriari A, Schonberger LB (1999). Reye's syndrome in the United States from 1981 through 1997. New England Journal of Medicine, 340(18):1377-1382. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM199905063401801>；FDA 未批准 dipyrone（安乃近）上市（待核实）
- 备注：安乃近对成年人也不是首选药。它可能引起粒细胞缺乏、再生障碍性贫血和过敏性休克，只在病情急重、又没有别的有效药时短期用。从国内带来的感冒药（如重感灵，中国产品）里可能含安乃近。孩子该用什么退烧药、按体重吃多少，问儿科医生或药师。不满 3 个月的孩子发烧直接去医院，见第 20 节第 8 条（38 ℃ 就直接去医院）。定 B 是因为瑞氏综合征的数字来自监测报告，病例减少和警告发布是前后关系，不是对照试验。收益定「大」，是因为警告之后病例从每年 555 例降到 36 例以下。
  Note: Metamizole isn't a first choice for adults either. It can cause agranulocytosis, aplastic anemia, and anaphylactic shock — used short-term only when the illness is urgent and severe and no other effective drug exists. Cold medicines brought from China (such as 重感灵, a Chinese product) may contain metamizole. Ask a pediatrician or pharmacist which fever reducer a child should use and how much by weight. For a fever in a baby under 3 months, go to the hospital directly — see section 20, item 8. Grade B because the Reye's numbers come from surveillance reports — the drop in cases followed the warnings but wasn't a controlled trial. Benefit is rated "large" because cases fell from 555 a year to under 36 after the warnings.

### 3. 60 岁以上、胃出过血、在吃抗凝药或激素的人，吃布洛芬这类止痛药之前先问医生 / If you're over 60, have had stomach bleeding, or take blood thinners or steroids, ask a doctor before taking ibuprofen-type painkillers
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。问一次医生或药师。
  Cost: Free. Ask a doctor or pharmacist once.
- 说人话：布洛芬（ibuprofen，Advil 的主要成分）、萘普生（naproxen）、阿司匹林这类止痛药会伤胃。在大剂量的试验里，吃布洛芬的人胃出血、穿孔的风险是不吃的约 4 倍。年纪大、胃出过血、同时吃抗凝药或激素、每天喝酒的人，风险更高。
  In plain terms: Painkillers like ibuprofen (the main ingredient in Advil), naproxen, and aspirin injure the stomach. In high-dose trials, people on ibuprofen had about 4 times the risk of stomach bleeding or perforation versus non-users. The risk is higher if you're older, have had stomach bleeding, take blood thinners or steroids, or drink daily.
- 收益：这类药叫非甾体抗炎药（NSAIDs），常见的有布洛芬、萘普生、双氯芬酸。一项荟萃分析汇总了 280 项这类药对安慰剂的随机试验，共 124513 人。和安慰剂比，上消化道穿孔、梗阻或出血的风险：布洛芬 RR 3.97（95% CI 2.22–7.10，约 4 倍），萘普生 4.22（2.71–6.56），双氯芬酸 1.89（1.16–3.09）。所有这类药都让心力衰竭的风险大约翻倍。美国法规要求这类非处方药写明胃出血警示。警示列出风险更高的六种人：60 岁以上，有过胃溃疡或出血，在吃抗凝药或激素，同时吃别的这类药，用药期间每天喝 3 杯以上的酒，吃得比说明书多或久。
  Benefit: These are non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs (NSAIDs); common ones include ibuprofen, naproxen, and diclofenac. A meta-analysis pooled 280 randomized trials of these drugs versus placebo, 124,513 people total. Versus placebo, the risk of upper-GI perforation, obstruction, or bleeding: ibuprofen RR 3.97 (95% CI 2.22–7.10, about 4x), naproxen 4.22 (2.71–6.56), diclofenac 1.89 (1.16–3.09). All of them roughly doubled the risk of heart failure. U.S. regulations require these OTC products to carry a stomach-bleeding warning listing six higher-risk groups: age 60+, history of stomach ulcers or bleeding, taking blood thinners or steroids, taking other NSAIDs at the same time, drinking 3 or more alcoholic drinks daily while using, and taking more or longer than the label says.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Coxib and traditional NSAID Trialists' (CNT) Collaboration; Bhala N, Emberson J, Merhi A, et al. (2013). Vascular and upper gastrointestinal effects of non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs: meta-analyses of individual participant data from randomised trials. Lancet, 382(9894):769-779. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(13)60900-9>；21 CFR 201.326(a)(2)(iii)(A) Stomach bleeding warning. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-201/subpart-G/section-201.326>
- 备注：论文结论讲的是大剂量用法。偶尔吃一两天、按说明书剂量吃的风险，这篇没有单独给数字。很多复方感冒药里也有这类成分，和止痛药一起吃就重复了，先看成分表。医生让你长期吃小剂量阿司匹林防血栓的，别自己停，也别自己再加一种止痛药。
  Note: The paper's conclusions concern high-dose use. It gives no separate numbers for the risk of taking it occasionally for a day or two at label doses. Many combination cold medicines also contain these ingredients — taking them with a painkiller duplicates the dose, so check the label first. If your doctor has you on long-term low-dose aspirin to prevent clots, don't stop it yourself — and don't add another painkiller on your own.

### 4. 2 岁以下的孩子感冒，别自己喂复方感冒药和止咳药 / For children under 2 with a cold, don't give combination cold or cough medicines on your own
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，还能省下买药的钱。
  Cost: Free, and you save the money you'd spend on the medicine.
- 说人话：美国 FDA 早在 2008 年就发出过警示：2 岁以下儿童不要用非处方感冒咳嗽药（待核实），要在医生或药师指导下用。在孩子身上做的试验里，止咳药、抗过敏药效果都不比安慰剂好。
  In plain terms: The U.S. FDA warned as early as 2008 that OTC cough and cold medicines should not be used in children under 2 (to be verified) — use them only under a doctor's or pharmacist's guidance. In trials done in children, cough suppressants and anti-allergy ingredients worked no better than placebo.
- 收益：一篇 Cochrane 系统综述纳入 29 项随机对照试验，其中 10 项在儿童身上做。儿童试验里，这几类药都不比安慰剂好：止咳药，抗组胺药（抗过敏成分），抗组胺药加减充血剂（通鼻子的成分），止咳药加支气管扩张剂。21 项研究报告了不良反应，含抗组胺药和右美沙芬的制剂不良反应更多。
  Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review included 29 randomized controlled trials, 10 of them in children. In the child trials, none of these drug classes beat placebo: cough suppressants, antihistamines (anti-allergy ingredients), antihistamines plus decongestants (nose-clearing ingredients), and cough suppressants plus bronchodilators. 21 trials reported adverse effects; products with antihistamines and dextromethorphan had more of them.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Smith SM, Schroeder K, Fahey T (2014). Over-the-counter (OTC) medications for acute cough in children and adults in community settings. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (11):CD001831. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD001831.pub5>；U.S. FDA 2008 年儿童感冒咳嗽药警示（具体链接待核实）
- 备注：很多儿童感冒药也含对乙酰氨基酚，再和退烧药一起吃就重复了，见本节第 1 条（对乙酰氨基酚）。那篇综述里有一项试验发现蜂蜜比安慰剂好，但不满 1 岁不能喂蜂蜜，见第 20 节第 6 条（蜂蜜）。定 B 是因为综述里每类药的试验都少，作者没有合并出数字。收益定「中」是判断：这类药对小孩没有证实的好处，风险主要是过量和成分重复。
  Note: Many children's cold medicines also contain acetaminophen — combined with a fever reducer, that's a duplicate dose; see item 1 of this section (acetaminophen). One trial in that review found honey beat placebo, but babies under 1 must not be given honey — see section 20, item 6. Grade B because each drug class had few trials and the authors pooled no numbers. Benefit is rated "medium" by judgment: no proven benefit for young children, with risks mainly from overdose and duplicated ingredients.

### 5. 怀孕 20 周以后，别自己吃布洛芬这类止痛药 / After 20 weeks of pregnancy, don't take ibuprofen-type painkillers on your own
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。
  Cost: Free.
- 说人话：怀孕 20 周以后，羊水大部分是胎儿的肾做出来的。这时吃布洛芬这类药，可能伤到胎儿的肾、让羊水变少。美国收到的这类报告里，有新生儿死亡的病例。医生开的小剂量阿司匹林不在此列。
  In plain terms: After about 20 weeks of pregnancy, most amniotic fluid is made by the fetus's kidneys. Taking ibuprofen-type drugs at this stage can injure the fetal kidneys and reduce amniotic fluid. U.S. reports of this include newborn deaths. Low-dose aspirin prescribed by a doctor is not covered here.
- 收益：美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）2020 年 10 月发布安全警示。怀孕约 20 周以后使用非甾体抗炎药，可能引起胎儿肾脏问题，导致羊水过少。警示同时管处方药和非处方药，包括阿司匹林、布洛芬、萘普生、双氯芬酸、塞来昔布。FDA 查了截至 2017 年 7 月的不良事件报告，找到 35 例羊水过少或新生儿肾功能不全，全是严重结局。其中 5 例新生儿死亡，都伴有新生儿肾衰竭。文献里，多数羊水过少在停药后 72 小时到 6 天内恢复。FDA 建议孕妇 20 周以后不要用这类药，除非医生明确这么建议。81 毫克的小剂量阿司匹林由医生开来治孕期特定问题的，不受这条限制。需要止痛退烧时，FDA 提到可以用对乙酰氨基酚等其他药，先问药师或医生。
  Benefit: The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) issued a safety communication in October 2020: using NSAIDs at about 20 weeks of pregnancy or later can cause fetal kidney problems, leading to low amniotic fluid. The warning covers both prescription and OTC drugs, including aspirin, ibuprofen, naproxen, diclofenac, and celecoxib. The FDA reviewed adverse event reports through July 2017 and found 35 cases of low amniotic fluid or newborn kidney impairment, all serious outcomes. 5 involved newborn deaths, all with newborn kidney failure. In the literature, most low-amniotic-fluid cases resolved within 72 hours to 6 days after stopping the drug. The FDA advises pregnant people not to use these drugs after 20 weeks unless a doctor specifically recommends it. 81 mg low-dose aspirin prescribed for specific pregnancy conditions is not restricted here. When pain relief or fever reduction is needed, the FDA mentions alternatives such as acetaminophen — ask a pharmacist or doctor first.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：U.S. Food and Drug Administration (2020-10-15). FDA recommends avoiding use of NSAIDs in pregnancy at 20 weeks or later because they can result in low amniotic fluid. Drug Safety Communication. <https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/fda-recommends-avoiding-use-nsaids-pregnancy-20-weeks-or-later-because-they-can-result-low-amniotic>
- 备注：FDA 提醒，很多治感冒、流感、失眠的非处方药里也含这类成分，要看成分表。小剂量阿司匹林防子痫前期，见第 27 节第 5 条（小剂量阿司匹林）。定 B 是因为数字来自不良事件报告，有多少人吃了药却没出事不知道。收益定「中」是判断：结局严重，但发生得少。
  Note: The FDA reminds that many OTC cold, flu, and sleep medicines also contain these ingredients — check the label. For low-dose aspirin to prevent preeclampsia, see section 27, item 5. Grade B because the numbers come from adverse event reports — how many people took the drugs without harm is unknown. Benefit is rated "medium" by judgment: severe outcomes, but rare.

### 6. 反酸烧心自己买奥美拉唑，按标签疗程吃；吞咽困难、呕血、黑便就直接去医院 / For heartburn, buy omeprazole OTC and follow the label's course; trouble swallowing, vomiting blood, or black stools mean go to the hospital
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。按说明书的疗程吃。
  Cost: Free. Take it for the course the label specifies.
- 说人话：奥美拉唑（omeprazole）在美国本来就是非处方药（Prilosec OTC）。按包装标签的疗程吃，一个疗程一般是 14 天，症状没缓解就去看医生（待核实）。吞咽困难或疼痛、呕血、便血或黑便时不要自己吃，这些可能是严重情况的信号。这药还可能盖住胃癌的症状，耽误诊断。
  In plain terms: Omeprazole is already over-the-counter in the U.S. (Prilosec OTC). Take it for the course on the package label — typically a 14-day course, and see a doctor if symptoms don't improve (to be verified). Don't take it on your own if you have difficulty or pain swallowing, vomit blood, or have bloody or black stools — these can signal serious conditions. The drug can also mask stomach cancer symptoms and delay diagnosis.
- 收益：这类药的适应症是「胃酸过多引起的烧心和反酸症状的短期缓解」。按标签的疗程吃，症状没缓解要咨询医师或药师，症状反复要就医（具体天数以包装标签为准）。吞咽困难或疼痛、呕血、便血或黑便时请勿使用，这些可能是严重情况的征兆。出现明显消瘦、反复呕吐、吞咽困难、呕血或黑便这类报警症状时，要先排除恶性肿瘤，因为治疗可能掩盖症状、延误诊断。55 岁以上出现新的症状或症状有变化，要咨询医生。在吃氯吡格雷（clopidogrel）的人要避免同时吃奥美拉唑。
  Benefit: The indication for these drugs is "short-term relief of heartburn and acid reflux from excess stomach acid." Take the course on the label; if symptoms don't improve, consult a doctor or pharmacist, and see a doctor if symptoms return (exact days per the package label). Don't use with difficulty or pain swallowing, vomiting blood, or bloody or black stools — these can be signs of serious conditions. With alarm symptoms like notable weight loss, repeated vomiting, difficulty swallowing, vomiting blood, or black stools, malignancy must be ruled out first, because treatment can mask symptoms and delay diagnosis. New or changed symptoms at age 55+ warrant a doctor visit. People taking clopidogrel should avoid omeprazole at the same time.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Prilosec OTC 药品标签 / U.S. FDA（具体链接待核实）
- 备注：氯吡格雷是放过支架、得过心梗的人常吃的防血栓药。奥美拉唑会让它变弱，在吃的人先问医生。定 B 是因为这些是说明书的规定，没有研究算过照着做能少出多少事。收益定「中」是判断：它防的是把胃出血、胃癌拖晚了，但多数反酸烧心并不严重。
  Note: Clopidogrel is a common anti-clot drug for people with stents or prior heart attacks. Omeprazole weakens it — ask a doctor first if you take it. Grade B because these are label rules; no study has quantified how much harm following them avoids. Benefit is rated "medium" by judgment: it guards against delaying stomach bleeding or stomach cancer, but most heartburn isn't serious.

### 7. 普通感冒别找医生要抗生素 / Don't ask a doctor for antibiotics for a common cold
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，还能省下药钱。
  Cost: Free, and you save the drug money.
- 说人话：感冒吃抗生素，好得不比不吃快。成年人吃了，副作用的风险是不吃的两倍多。流脓鼻涕不到 10 天的，吃抗生素也没有确定的好处。
  In plain terms: Antibiotics don't make a cold get better any faster. In adults, they more than double the risk of side effects. For runny noses with pus under 10 days, antibiotics show no definite benefit either.
- 收益：一篇 Cochrane 系统综述汇总了 6 项随机试验、1147 人。感冒吃抗生素和吃安慰剂比，没好或症状持续的比例没有明显差别（RR 0.83，95% CI 0.60–1.14，差别不显著）。不良反应的风险是安慰剂组的 1.8 倍（95% CI 1.01–3.21）。其中成人 2.62 倍（1.32–5.18），儿童没有增加（0.91，0.51–1.63）。流脓鼻涕不到 10 天的急性脓性鼻炎，吃抗生素也没有明确好处（RR 0.73，0.47–1.13）。不良反应却多了（RR 1.46，1.10–1.94，高约 46%）。作者结论是：这两种情况都不推荐常规用抗生素。
  Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review pooled 6 randomized trials, 1,147 people. Antibiotics versus placebo for colds: no significant difference in the proportion not improved or still symptomatic (RR 0.83, 95% CI 0.60–1.14, not significant). Adverse-effect risk was 1.8 times the placebo group's (95% CI 1.01–3.21): 2.62 times in adults (1.32–5.18), no increase in children (0.91, 0.51–1.63). For acute purulent rhinitis with runny pus under 10 days, antibiotics showed no clear benefit (RR 0.73, 0.47–1.13) but more adverse effects (RR 1.46, 1.10–1.94, about 46% more). The authors conclude routine antibiotics are not recommended for either.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Kenealy T, Arroll B (2025). Antibiotics for the common cold and acute purulent rhinitis. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 11:CD000247. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000247.pub4>
- 备注：这一版是 2025 年重新发布的，摘要写明文献检索截至 2013 年。抗生素在美国同样是处方药，家里剩的抗生素也别自己拿来吃。收益定「中」是判断：它防的是不良反应，不是死亡。
  Note: This edition was republished in 2025; the abstract notes the literature search ran through 2013. Antibiotics are prescription-only in the U.S. too — don't take leftover antibiotics at home on your own. Benefit is rated "medium" by judgment: it prevents adverse effects, not death.

### 8. 拉肚子先喝口服补液盐补水，5 岁以下的孩子不给止泻药 / For diarrhea, rehydrate with oral rehydration salts first; don't give anti-diarrheal drugs to children under 5
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一盒口服补液盐几美元，家里备一盒。
  Cost: A box of oral rehydration salts costs a few dollars — keep one at home.
- 说人话：拉肚子最要紧的是补水，不是止泻。按说明书兑水喝口服补液盐（比如 Pedialyte），别拿果汁饮料代替，太甜反而让人更缺水。5 岁以下的孩子不给洛哌丁胺（loperamide）这类止泻药，严重的会肠梗阻，可能致命。
  In plain terms: With diarrhea, rehydration matters most — not stopping it. Mix oral rehydration salts with water per the instructions (e.g., Pedialyte); don't substitute juice or soda — too much sugar dehydrates further. Don't give children under 5 anti-diarrheal drugs like loperamide; severe cases can cause intestinal blockage and can be fatal.
- 收益：世界卫生组织的腹泻治疗手册写明，「止泻」药和止吐药对儿童急性或迁延性腹泻没有实际益处。它们不能预防脱水，也不能改善营养。有些有危险的、有时致命的副作用，绝不能给 5 岁以下儿童用。洛哌丁胺这类减慢肠蠕动的药，可能引起严重的麻痹性肠梗阻，可以致命，还可能拖长感染。手册还写明，糖太多的饮料会把身体里的水吸进肠子，造成高钠性脱水，比如软饮料和市售果汁饮料。世卫手册推荐的是低渗配方，总渗透压 245 mOsm/L。和旧配方比，它让计划外的静脉输液少了 33%。一篇 Cochrane 综述汇总 8 项试验，结果相近（OR 0.59，95% CI 0.45–0.79，少约四成）。
  Benefit: The WHO's diarrhoea treatment manual states that "anti-diarrhoeal" and anti-vomiting drugs have no practical benefit for acute or persistent diarrhoea in children. They don't prevent dehydration or improve nutrition. Some have dangerous, sometimes fatal side effects and must never be given to children under 5. Drugs like loperamide that slow intestinal movement can cause severe paralytic ileus, which can be fatal and can prolong infection. The manual also notes that overly sugary drinks draw water from the body into the intestines, causing hypernatremic dehydration — e.g., soft drinks and commercial fruit juices. The WHO manual recommends the reduced-osmolarity formula, total osmolarity 245 mOsm/L. Compared with the old formula, it cut unscheduled IV infusions by 33%. A Cochrane review pooling 8 trials found similar results (OR 0.59, 95% CI 0.45–0.79, about 40% fewer).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：World Health Organization (2005). The treatment of diarrhoea: a manual for physicians and other senior health workers, 4th revision. <https://iris.who.int/handle/10665/43209>；Hahn S, Kim S, Garner P (2002). Reduced osmolarity oral rehydration solution for treating dehydration caused by acute diarrhoea in children. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (1):CD002847. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD002847>
- 备注：买的时候看说明书上的渗透压，选低渗配方。手册写明，孩子大便带血多半是细菌性痢疾，要去看医生用抗生素，不能只在家补水。收益定「中」是判断：手里的数字比的是两种补液盐，不是喝和不喝。
  Note: When buying, check the osmolarity on the label and choose the reduced-osmolarity formula. The manual notes that bloody stools in a child usually mean bacterial dysentery — see a doctor for antibiotics; home rehydration alone isn't enough. Benefit is rated "medium" by judgment: the available numbers compare two rehydration formulas, not drinking versus not drinking.

### 9. 止痛药一个月别吃超过 15 天，复方止痛药别超过 10 天，不然头痛可能就是药吃出来的 / Don't take painkillers more than 15 days a month (10 for combination products) — the headache may be caused by the pills themselves
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。要记吃药的天数，还要忍住不随手吃。
  Cost: Free. You have to track the days you take pills, and resist popping them casually.
- 说人话：头痛老犯、就天天吃止痛药，可能越吃越痛。国际头痛分类把这叫药物过度使用性头痛。普通止痛药一个月吃 15 天以上，复方止痛药 10 天以上，连着超过 3 个月，就够得上。多数人停掉吃多的药以后会好转。
  In plain terms: If headaches keep coming and you take painkillers daily, they can make the pain worse. The international headache classification calls this medication-overuse headache. Plain painkillers 15+ days a month, combination painkillers 10+ days a month, for more than 3 months straight, meets the definition. Most people improve after stopping the overused drug.
- 收益：国际头痛学会的《国际头痛疾病分类》第 3 版给了诊断标准。原本有头痛病的人，每月头痛 15 天以上，同时规律过量使用治头痛的药超过 3 个月。对乙酰氨基酚、阿司匹林、布洛芬这类非阿片止痛药，过量的门槛是每月 15 天及以上。复方止痛药的门槛是每月 10 天及以上。复方止痛药指两类以上有止痛作用的药合在一起，或者加了咖啡因这类辅助成分，比如对乙酰氨基酚加可待因。几种非阿片止痛药轮着吃，天数合起来算。分类说明写明，多个国家的流行病学证据显示，每月头痛 15 天以上的人里，超过一半属于这种头痛。多数病人停掉过量的药后会好转，预防头痛的治疗也更起作用。
  Benefit: The International Headache Society's International Classification of Headache Disorders, 3rd edition, gives the diagnostic criteria. People with a pre-existing headache disorder, headaches 15+ days a month, plus regular overuse of headache medication for over 3 months. For non-opioid painkillers like acetaminophen, aspirin, and ibuprofen, the overuse threshold is 15+ days a month. For combination painkillers it's 10+ days a month. Combination painkillers means two or more pain-relieving drugs combined, or with added helpers like caffeine — e.g., acetaminophen plus codeine. Rotating several non-opioid painkillers counts the days together. The classification notes epidemiological evidence from multiple countries: over half of people with headaches 15+ days a month fall into this category. Most patients improve after stopping the overused drug, and preventive headache treatment works better.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Headache Classification Committee of the International Headache Society (2018). The International Classification of Headache Disorders, 3rd edition. Cephalalgia, 38(1):1-211. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0333102417738202>；ICHD-3 在线版 8.2 Medication-overuse headache（含 8.2.3、8.2.5）. <https://ichd-3.org/8-headache-attributed-to-a-substance-or-its-withdrawal/8-2-medication-overuse-headache-moh/>
- 备注：在美国，标签上写着含咖啡因（caffeine）的止痛片按复方算 10 天。一个月里头痛的天数越来越多，就去神经内科看，别只靠加药。定 B 是因为分类标准给的是诊断门槛，不是对照试验。收益定「中」是判断：它换回的是少头痛的日子，不涉及死亡。
  Note: In the U.S., painkillers whose labels list caffeine count as combination products at the 10-day threshold. If headache days keep increasing within a month, see a neurologist — don't just take more pills. Grade B because the classification gives diagnostic thresholds, not a controlled trial. Benefit is rated "medium" by judgment: it buys back headache-free days, not mortality.

### 10. 过期药和用不上的剩药定期清出来，送到回收点，别留在家里 / Regularly clear out expired and unused medicines and take them to a drug take-back site — don't keep them at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。每年翻一次药箱，十几分钟。
  Cost: Free. Go through the medicine cabinet once a year — about fifteen minutes.
- 说人话：小孩背着大人误吃的药，近一半是处方药片。误吃的处方药里，约三成是爷爷奶奶的。用不上的药清出来送到回收点，就少一样能被孩子翻到的东西。孩子误服了药，先打美国中毒控制中心 Poison Control 1-800-222-1222（24 小时）。
  In plain terms: Of medicines children swallow without adults knowing, nearly half are prescription tablets. Of those prescription drugs, about 30% belong to grandparents. Clearing out unneeded medicines and taking them to a take-back site removes one more thing a child can find. If a child swallows medicine, first call the U.S. Poison Control Center at 1-800-222-1222 (24 hours).
- 收益：美国急诊监测数据估计，2004 到 2013 年，不满 6 岁的孩子背着大人误吃药品，看了约 64 万次急诊（估计值 640161 次）。2013 年一年约 5.9 万次（59092 次）。2010 到 2013 年，最常见的是口服处方药片剂，占 45.9%。处方药里最常见的是阿片类止痛药（13.8%）和苯二氮䓬类安眠镇静药（12.7%）。美国 5 家中毒控制中心 2017 年问了打电话求助的家长。孩子误吃处方药的，30.7% 吃的是祖父母的药。一半以上（51.5%）的处方药，在孩子拿到之前已经被大人从原包装里取了出来。美国 FDA 说，处理过期和用不上的药，最好的办法是交给回收点（药店的回收箱、定期的药品回收日）。
  Benefit: U.S. emergency-department surveillance estimates that from 2004 to 2013, children under 6 swallowing medicines unsupervised led to about 640,000 ED visits (estimate 640,161). In 2013 alone, about 59,000 (59,092). From 2010 to 2013, the most common were oral prescription tablets, 45.9%. Among prescription drugs, the most common were opioid painkillers (13.8%) and benzodiazepine sleep/sedative drugs (12.7%). Five U.S. poison control centers surveyed parents who called for help in 2017: of children who swallowed prescription drugs, 30.7% took grandparents' medicines. Over half (51.5%) of the prescription drugs had already been taken out of their original packaging by an adult before the child got them. The U.S. FDA says the best way to dispose of expired and unused medicines is a take-back site (pharmacy drop boxes, periodic drug take-back days).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Lovegrove MC, Weidle NJ, Budnitz DS (2015). Trends in Emergency Department Visits for Unsupervised Pediatric Medication Exposures, 2004-2013. Pediatrics, 136(4):e821-e829. <https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2015-2092>；Agarwal M, Lovegrove MC, Geller RJ, et al. (2020). Circumstances Involved in Unsupervised Solid Dose Medication Exposures among Young Children. Journal of Pediatrics, 219:188-195.e6. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpeds.2019.12.027>；U.S. Food and Drug Administration (2025). Where and How to Dispose of Unused Medicines. <https://www.fda.gov/consumers/consumer-updates/where-and-how-dispose-unused-medicines>
- 备注：以当地回收点和市政有害垃圾收集的规定为准。留下来的药放在原盒里，别倒进分药盒或别的瓶子，放到孩子够不着的地方，见第 13 节第 20 条（误服）。家里剩的抗生素也别自己拿来吃，见本节第 7 条（抗生素）。美国在没有回收点时，让人把药混进咖啡渣、猫砂再封袋扔进普通垃圾，少数强效止痛药允许冲马桶。孩子误服先打 Poison Control 1-800-222-1222。定 B 是因为美国那几组数字只是登记，没有研究算过清理药箱能少多少次误吃。收益定「小」：手里的数字是急诊次数，不是死亡，也算不出清理这个动作的效果。
  Note: Follow your local take-back sites and municipal hazardous-waste collection rules. Keep remaining medicines in their original boxes — don't pour them into pill organizers or other bottles — and store them where children can't reach; see section 13, item 20 (accidental ingestion). Don't take leftover antibiotics at home on your own; see item 7 of this section (antibiotics). Where no take-back site exists, U.S. guidance is to mix medicines into coffee grounds or cat litter, seal in a bag, and put in regular trash; a few potent painkillers may be flushed. For a child's accidental ingestion, call Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 first. Grade B because the U.S. numbers are registries — no study has quantified how many ingestions clearing the cabinet prevents. Benefit is rated "small": the available numbers are ED visits, not deaths, and the effect of the clearing action itself can't be computed.

### 11. 用头孢、甲硝唑这类抗菌药期间和停药后 7 天内不喝酒，含酒精的药（如某些止咳糖浆）也不碰 / While taking cephalosporins or metronidazole, and for 7 days after stopping, don't drink alcohol — and avoid alcohol-containing medicines (like some cough syrups)
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。一周多不喝酒，吃别的药前看一眼成分里有没有乙醇（alcohol）。
  Cost: Free. Skip alcohol for a bit over a week, and check other medicines' labels for alcohol before taking them.
- 说人话：部分头孢、甲硝唑（metronidazole）这类药碰上酒精，会让人脸红、心慌、胸闷、呕吐，重的血压往下掉。不光是啤酒葡萄酒，含酒精的止咳糖浆、酊剂也会引起。吃药期间到停药后 7 天都别碰。
  In plain terms: Some cephalosporins and drugs like metronidazole react with alcohol — flushing, sweating, palpitations, chest tightness, vomiting, and in severe cases dropping blood pressure. It's not just beer and wine; alcohol-containing cough syrups and tinctures can trigger it too. Avoid all of it during treatment and for 7 days after stopping.
- 收益：这叫双硫仑样反应（disulfiram-like reaction）。部分头孢类药物服药前后饮酒可能引发这种反应，会严重损害健康甚至导致生命危险。症状有面部潮红、出汗、心悸，甚至呼吸困难、血压下降、过敏性休克。常见涉及的药有头孢唑林、头孢曲松、头孢哌酮、甲硝唑、替硝唑、呋喃唑酮等（中国药典临床用药须知所列，中国来源）。广州一家医院整理了 2 年里的 82 例病例（中国病例整理）：由头孢类引起的 57 例（69.51%），硝基咪唑类（甲硝唑、替硝唑这类）10 例（12.20%）。酒精来自酒类饮料的 40 例（48.78%），来自含乙醇药物的 31 例（37.80%）。口服的有藿香正气水（中国成药）、止咳糖浆等。82 例里轻度 68 例、中度 9 例、重度 5 例，1 到 3 小时内缓解，没有死亡。美国甲硝唑（Flagyl）说明书要求，用药期间和停药后至少 3 天不喝酒，也不用含丙二醇的产品。
  Benefit: This is called a disulfiram-like reaction. Drinking around the time of taking certain cephalosporins can trigger it, seriously harming health and even endangering life. Symptoms include facial flushing, sweating, palpitations, and even breathing difficulty, blood pressure drop, and anaphylactic shock. Commonly implicated drugs include cefazolin, ceftriaxone, cefoperazone, metronidazole, tinidazole, and furazolidone (listed in China's pharmacopeia clinical guidance — Chinese source). One Guangzhou hospital tallied 82 cases over 2 years (Chinese case series): 57 (69.51%) from cephalosporins, 10 (12.20%) from nitroimidazoles (metronidazole, tinidazole, etc.). Alcohol came from alcoholic drinks in 40 cases (48.78%) and from alcohol-containing medicines in 31 (37.80%). Oral sources included 藿香正气水 (a Chinese patent medicine) and cough syrups. Of the 82, 68 were mild, 9 moderate, 5 severe; all resolved within 1–3 hours, no deaths. The U.S. metronidazole (Flagyl) label requires no alcohol during treatment and for at least 3 days after stopping, and no propylene-glycol-containing products.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：房财富, 涂星, 刘韬, 黄红兵, 邱孟, 梁蔚婷 (2015). 我院双硫仑样反应82例分析. 中国药房, 26(29):4089-4091. <https://doi.org/10.6039/j.issn.1001-0408.2015.29.19>；Pfizer (2026).   Note: Not every cephalosporin does this, but ordinary people can't tell them apart — following this rule for all of them is simplest. When a doctor prescribes a cephalosporin or metronidazole, volunteer whether you've drunk alcohol in recent days. If flushing and palpitations start, stop all alcohol-containing things immediately. Mild cases resolve on their own; severe ones need 911 and a hospital. Don't drink while taking acetaminophen either — see item 1 of this section (acetaminophen). Grade B because the evidence is case series plus drug labels, with no controls. Benefit is rated "small": all 82 cases resolved within hours, no deaths, only 5 severe.
