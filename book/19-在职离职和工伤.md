# 19. 在职、离职和工伤 / On the Job, Leaving, and Work Injuries

口径：金钱。/ Scope: Money.

这一节算的是钱：钱怎么算、字怎么签、期限怎么算。前三条讲你还在岗时就该拿到手的钱，中间几条讲离职（注意：加州是 at-will，没有中国的 N、2N 和代通知金），接着讲工伤。工伤的钱比被裁大一个量级，期限也更硬。职业病造成的损伤换不回来。节末还有：在单位被欺负怎么办、离职时公司必须给你什么、遣散费的税怎么算。

This section is about money: how it's calculated, what you sign, and what the deadlines are. The first items cover pay you should already be getting while employed; the middle ones cover leaving (note: California is at-will—there is no statutory "N," "2N," or pay-in-lieu-of-notice); then work injuries, where the money is an order of magnitude bigger than a layoff and the deadlines are harder. Occupational-disease damage can't be undone. At the end: what to do about workplace bullying, what your employer must hand you at separation, and how severance is taxed.

本节条目按主题分成下面几块，括号里是条号。第 5 条（代通知金）为中国大陆专属概念，加州版略去。

Items are grouped by theme below (item numbers in parentheses). Item 5 (pay in lieu of notice) is a mainland-China-only concept and is omitted in the California edition.

**在岗的钱 / Money while employed**：加班费三档（第 1 条）/ Overtime tiers (Item 1)，年假离职折现（第 2 条）/ Vacation payout at separation (Item 2)，试用期与 at-will（第 3 条）/ Probation and at-will (Item 3)。

**离职 / Leaving**：没有法定遣散费（第 4 条）/ No statutory severance (Item 4)，违法解雇走 FEHA 和报复保护（第 6 条）/ Wrongful firing: FEHA and anti-retaliation (Item 6)，别签"主动辞职"（第 7 条）/ Don't sign "voluntary resignation" (Item 7)，离职前存证据（第 8 条）/ Save evidence before leaving (Item 8)，离职时公司必须给什么（第 17 条）/ What you're owed at separation (Item 17)，遣散费的税（第 18 条）/ Tax on severance (Item 18)。

**职业病和工伤 / Occupational disease and work injuries**：危害告知和体检（第 9 条）/ Hazard disclosure and exams (Item 9)，防护用品和拒绝权（第 10 条）/ PPE and the right to refuse (Item 10)，受伤先报、填 DWC-1（第 11 条）/ Report and file the DWC-1 (Item 11)，没有 48 小时线（第 12 条）/ No 48-hour rule (Item 12)，公司没买工伤保险（第 13 条）/ Uninsured employer (Item 13)，伤残评级换算成钱（第 14 条）/ Disability rating into money (Item 14)，工亡的钱（第 15 条）/ Death benefits (Item 15)。

**被欺负 / Bullied**：先记录再走渠道（第 16 条）/ Document, then use the channels (Item 16)。

### 1. 加班费按 1.5 倍、2 倍两档算：8 小时/天、40 小时/周是线，不给就去 Labor Commissioner 投诉 / Overtime at 1.5x and 2x: the lines are 8 hours/day and 40 hours/week — complain to the Labor Commissioner if it's unpaid
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。平时顺手把打卡记录、排班表、加班审批单存一份，工作群里领导派活的消息也截图留着。难在每个月都要记得存。
  Cost: Free. Save your time-clock records, schedules, and overtime approvals as you go; screenshot work assignments from your boss in chat. The hard part is remembering every month.
- 说人话：加州按天算加班，比联邦狠。一天超 8 小时按 1.5 倍，超 12 小时按 2 倍；一周超 40 小时 1.5 倍；连上 7 天的第 7 天，前 8 小时 1.5 倍、之后 2 倍。"公司规定加班要审批""我是自愿留下的"不等于可以不给钱，但会让你更难举证，所以派活和审批记录要留着。不给就去 Labor Commissioner 投诉，还能要回利息和律师费。
  In plain terms: California counts overtime daily—stricter than federal law. Over 8 hours in a day: 1.5×; over 12: 2×; over 40 in a week: 1.5×; on the 7th consecutive day, the first 8 hours at 1.5× and beyond at 2×. "Overtime needs approval" or "you stayed voluntarily" doesn't erase the obligation—but it makes proof harder, so keep assignment and approval records. Unpaid? File with the Labor Commissioner; you can also recover interest and attorney's fees.
- 收益：Cal. Labor Code §510 定了上面的档位；§1194 给了员工直接追讨欠薪的诉权，约定"不要加班费"无效，加班费按 regular rate（含奖金、津贴，不只看基本时薪）算。2026 年加州最低工资 $16.90/小时（DIR 公告：2026 年 1 月 1 日起从 $16.50 上调）。
  Benefit: Cal. Labor Code §510 sets the tiers above; §1194 gives employees a direct right to recover unpaid overtime—agreements to waive it are void—and overtime is calculated on the regular rate (including bonuses and differentials, not just base pay). California's 2026 minimum wage is $16.90/hour (per the DIR: raised from $16.50 effective January 1, 2026).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Labor Code §510 (overtime); Cal. Labor Code §1194 (private right of action, attorney's fees). DIR, state minimum wage $16.90 effective Jan 1, 2026. <https://www.dir.ca.gov/DIRNews/2025/2025-118.html>
- 备注：替代工时制（alternative workweek，如 4×10）需受影响员工 2/3 无记名投票通过才合法（Labor Code §511，已核实）。被归为 exempt（豁免）才不用付加班费，门槛是月薪 ≥ 2 倍最低工资全职标准且主要干豁免类工作，公司说了不算。
  Note: Alternative workweeks (e.g., 4×10) need a two-thirds secret-ballot vote of affected employees to be lawful (Labor Code §511, verified). Only genuinely exempt workers are outside overtime—requiring salary of at least twice the full-time minimum wage plus primarily exempt duties; the employer's label doesn't decide.

### 2. 加州不强制带薪年假，但攒下的假离职必须折钱，"年底清零"违法 / California doesn't mandate paid vacation — but accrued vacation must be paid out at separation; "use it or lose it" is illegal
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。入职时把公司的 vacation/PTO 政策看一遍，弄清是 accrual 制还是入职一次性给。难在公司递"自愿放弃年假"的表时要能不签。
  Cost: Free. Read the company's vacation/PTO policy at hire—figure out whether it accrues or is granted lump-sum. The hard part is not signing "voluntary forfeiture" forms.
- 说人话：加州法律不要求公司给带薪年假；但给了就是工资，攒下多少，离职时按最后工资一分不少折现，"年底清零"违法（§227.3）。公司可以设合理的累积上限（accrual cap，攒满就暂停累积），但不能把你已攒的作废。注意：病假（sick leave）离职不折现，别和年假搞混。
  In plain terms: California law doesn't require paid vacation—but once granted, it's wages: every accrued hour must be cashed out at your final rate when you leave, and "use it or lose it" is illegal (§227.3). Employers may set a reasonable accrual cap (pausing further accrual at the cap) but can't wipe out what you've earned. Note: sick leave is *not* paid out at separation—don't confuse the two.
- 收益：§227.3：年假是 deferred compensation，vest 了就不能 forfeit。离职折现按最后工资算。floating holiday 如果按年假管理（随时可用、不 привязана к特定节日），离职也要折现。
  Benefit: §227.3: vacation is deferred compensation—once vested, it can't be forfeited. Payout is at the final rate of pay. Floating holidays managed like vacation (usable anytime, not tied to a specific holiday) must be paid out too.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Labor Code §227.3; DIR, Vacation pay rules. <http://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/VacationWorksheet.pdf>
- 备注：只有一种例外：集体谈判协议（CBA）里明确放弃的，普通员工碰不上。公司逼你"年底前必须休完"，可以休，但休不完的不能作废。
  Note: The only exception is a collective bargaining agreement that clearly and unmistakably waives the right—ordinary employees won't encounter it. An employer can pressure you to use time before year-end, but unused time can't be erased.

### 3. 试用期没有法定上限：加州默认 at-will，关键看 offer 和工资单 / No statutory probation cap: California defaults to at-will — what matters is the offer letter and the pay stub
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。签字前花几分钟，把 offer 里的 at-will 条款、仲裁条款看一遍。
  Cost: Free. Spend a few minutes before signing: read the offer's at-will clause and any arbitration clause.
- 说人话：加州默认 at-will employment：公司和你都可以随时、无理由结束雇佣（歧视、报复除外，见第 6 条）。"试用期 3 个月"是公司政策，不是法律；法律没规定试用期多长、试用期工资打几折。真正的法定保护是：最后工资必须按时付（见第 17 条），每个 payday 必须给明细工资条（§226），入职必须给书面工资通知（§2810.5）。
  In plain terms: California defaults to at-will employment: either side can end it anytime, for no reason (except discrimination and retaliation—see Item 6). A "3-month probation" is company policy, not law; no statute caps its length or discounts its pay. The real statutory protections: final wages on time (see Item 17), an itemized pay stub every payday (§226), and a written wage notice at hire (§2810.5).
- 收益：at-will 不等于公司可以为所欲为：最后工资、工资条、加班费、病假都是硬法。试用期被开也没有补偿，但要看理由是不是歧视或报复。
  Benefit: At-will doesn't mean the employer can do anything: final pay, pay stubs, overtime, and sick leave are hard law. Being let go during probation carries no severance—but the reason still can't be discrimination or retaliation.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Labor Code §§ 201–203 (final pay); Cal. Labor Code §226 (itemized wage statements); Cal. Labor Code §2810.5 (hire notice).
- 备注：at-will 的例外（歧视、报复、违反公共政策）见第 6 条。offer 里让你签的仲裁条款，签了之后劳动纠纷多半走仲裁而非法庭，签字前看清。
  Note: Exceptions to at-will (discrimination, retaliation, public-policy violations) are in Item 6. Arbitration clauses in offer letters route most disputes to arbitration instead of court—read before signing.

### 4. 被裁没有法定 N：加州 at-will 下遣散费全靠谈 / No statutory "N" when laid off: in at-will California, severance is 100% negotiation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。谈判前算好三笔：未休年假折现、最后工资、COBRA 几个月的费用。十分钟。
  Cost: Free. Before negotiating, tally three numbers: unused vacation payout, final wages, and a few months of COBRA. Ten minutes.
- 说人话：加州没有 N、N+1、2N，被裁公司一分钱遣散费不给也合法（除非合同、offer 或员工手册里写了）。能谈的是：多给几周工资换你签 release（放弃起诉的协议）、延长几个月医保、要一封推荐信。公司要你签 release，40 岁以上的有 21 天考虑期 + 签完 7 天反悔期（联邦 OWBPA），别被"今天必须签"唬住。
  In plain terms: California has no statutory severance—no "N," no "N+1," no "2N." A layoff with zero severance is legal (unless your contract, offer, or handbook promises it). What's negotiable: extra weeks of pay in exchange for signing a release, extended health coverage, a reference letter. If you're 40+, federal law (OWBPA) gives you 21 days to consider a release and 7 days to revoke after signing—don't be rushed by "sign today."
- 收益：release 的本质是交易：你放弃起诉，公司多给钱。签之前算清你手里有没有牌：未付的加班费、没折现的年假、可能的歧视/报复事实，都是筹码。牌越多，谈下来的越多。
  Benefit: A release is a trade: you waive claims, the company pays more. Before signing, count your cards—unpaid overtime, uncashed vacation, possible discrimination or retaliation facts are all leverage. More cards, better deal.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：29 U.S.C. §626(f) (Older Workers Benefit Protection Act, release requirements).
- 备注：OWBPA 细则已核实：个人裁员 21 天考虑期，群体裁员（同一决定同一批）45 天；签完都有 7 天反悔期，反悔期不可放弃。release 通常还包含"不说坏话"和"保密"条款，谈的时候可以要求对等。签了再反悔极难。
  Note: OWBPA details verified: 21 days to consider for individual terminations, 45 days for group terminations (same decision, same decisional unit); a 7-day revocation period after signing applies in both cases and cannot be waived. Releases often include non-disparagement and confidentiality clauses—ask for mutuality. Undoing a signed release is extremely hard.

### 5. 公司辞退你没提前 30 天通知，还要多付一个月工资 / If the company fires you without 30 days' notice, it must pay an extra month's wages
- （本条为中国大陆专属概念，加州版略去。加州 at-will 下无法定代通知金；大规模裁员的 60 天通知见第 17 条备注。）
  (China-specific concept, omitted in the California edition. At-will California has no statutory pay in lieu of notice; the 60-day mass-layoff notice is noted under Item 17.)

### 6. 违法解雇没有 2N，但赔偿可能更大：走 FEHA 反歧视和反报复 / No "2N" for unlawful firing — but damages can be bigger: FEHA anti-discrimination and anti-retaliation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：向 California Civil Rights Department（民权局）投诉不花钱；打官司找 employment 律师，很多按 contingency（赢了才收费）。难在要跟几个月到几年。
  Cost: Filing with the California Civil Rights Department is free; employment lawyers often work on contingency (paid only if you win). The hard part is the months-to-years timeline.
- 说人话：加州 at-will 不等于随便开。因种族、性别、年龄（40 岁以上）、怀孕、残疾、宗教、性取向开人，违法（FEHA，Government Code §12940）。因你投诉欠薪、报工伤、请病假、举报违法而开你，算报复（§1102.5、§6310），也违法。没有固定的"2 倍"公式，判的是：欠的工资补发 + 精神损害 + 惩罚性赔偿，金额可能远超 2N。先找 employment 律师评估，再决定是谈还是去民权局投诉（3 年时效）。
  In plain terms: At-will doesn't mean fire-at-whim. Firing over race, sex, age (40+), pregnancy, disability, religion, or sexual orientation is illegal (FEHA, Government Code §12940). Firing you for complaining about wages, filing workers' comp, taking sick leave, or reporting violations is retaliation (§1102.5, §6310)—also illegal. There's no fixed "2×" formula; awards cover back pay plus emotional-distress and punitive damages, potentially far exceeding 2N. Get an employment lawyer's assessment first, then decide: negotiate or file with the Civil Rights Department (3-year deadline).
- 收益：FEHA 覆盖 5 人以上的雇主（骚扰条款 1 人以上就管）。报复保护的范围很宽：向 Labor Commissioner 投诉、报工伤、请法定病假、拒绝违法指令，都算受保护行为。时效 3 年（Government Code §12960）。
  Benefit: FEHA covers employers with 5+ employees (harassment provisions apply at 1+). Retaliation protection is broad: Labor Commissioner complaints, workers' comp filings, statutory sick leave, and refusing illegal orders are all protected. The filing deadline is 3 years (Government Code §12960).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Government Code §§ 12940, 12960 (FEHA); Cal. Labor Code §§ 1102.5, 6310 (anti-retaliation); California Civil Rights Department. <https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/>
- 备注：是不是"违法解雇"要看具体情形，先咨询再行动，路径见第 7 节（第 2 条 Labor Commissioner、第 3 条法律援助）。单纯"老板不喜欢你"不违法。
  Note: Whether a firing is unlawful turns on the facts—consult first, then act (see Chapter 7, Items 2–3 for the Labor Commissioner and legal aid). "The boss just doesn't like you" isn't illegal by itself.

### 7. 别签"主动辞职"：被裁写成自愿走，失业金可能一起落空 / Don't sign "voluntary resignation": a layoff papered as quitting can kill your unemployment benefits too
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难在要顶住当场签字的压力。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is resisting the pressure to sign on the spot.
- 说人话：EDD 只给"非本人过错失业"发钱（每周 $40–$450，最多 26 周）。公司裁你却让你签"主动辞职"，EDD 可能直接拒发。真自己走的，要证明"好理由"（被欠薪、长期不给加班费、被骚扰、通勤剧变）才可能拿到。签字前想清楚：到底是谁先提出的走人。不必当天签，冷静一晚再说。
  In plain terms: The EDD only pays the "not your fault" unemployed ($40–$450/week, up to 26 weeks). If the company lays you off but has you sign "voluntary resignation," the EDD may deny benefits outright. If you truly quit, you need "good cause" (unpaid wages, chronic unpaid overtime, harassment, a drastic commute change) to qualify. Before signing, get clear on whose idea the departure was. You don't have to sign today—sleep on it.
- 收益：EDD 判定看的是事实，不是纸面标题：公司先找你谈"走人"，就是解雇。但纸上签了"主动辞职"，举证难度大增。公司常用的话术是"先签了好走流程，补偿另外给"——要就当场写进协议，不写就不签。
  Benefit: The EDD judges facts, not paper titles: if the company came to you first, it's a termination. But a signed "voluntary resignation" makes proof much harder. The classic line is "sign now for smooth processing, severance separately"—if it's real, get it in the agreement on the spot; otherwise don't sign.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：EDD, unemployment eligibility ("unemployed through no fault of your own"). <https://edd.ca.gov/en/unemployment/eligibility/>
- 备注：失业金怎么领，见第 7 节第 1 条。当天不签没有任何法律后果。
  Note: For how UI works, see Chapter 7, Item 1. Not signing on the spot has zero legal consequences.

### 8. 离职前把工资条、工时记录和邮件先存下来：加州法律站在你这边 / Before you leave, save your pay stubs, time records, and emails — California law is on your side here
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。花半小时导出/截图。难在走之前要记得存。
  Cost: Free. Half an hour to export and screenshot. The hard part is remembering before you go.
- 说人话：加州是全美对工资记录要求最严的州之一：每个 payday 必须给明细工资条（§226：总工资、总工时、各时薪及对应小时数、扣款、起止日期），公司必须存 3 年（§226(a)、§1174），员工自己记工时公司不许拦（§1174(d)）。入职时公司必须给书面工资通知（§2810.5：时薪、发薪日、公司地址电话、工伤保险公司）。电脑和账号一交回去就拿不到了，离职前把工资条、考勤、合同、工资通知、关键邮件都存一份。只存自己的劳动材料，别带走源码和客户名单。
  In plain terms: California has among the strictest pay-record laws in the US: an itemized stub every payday (§226—gross wages, total hours, each rate with hours at that rate, deductions, pay-period dates), 3-year retention by the employer (§226(a), §1174), and the employer may not stop you from keeping your own time records (§1174(d)). At hire you must get a written wage notice (§2810.5—pay rates, payday, company address/phone, workers' comp carrier). Once you surrender the laptop and accounts, it's all gone—save pay stubs, attendance, the contract, the wage notice, and key emails before you leave. Keep only your own employment records; don't take source code or client lists.
- 收益：仲裁和诉讼里，工资多少、有没有加班，举证责任虽偏向公司，但你自己手上的材料是底线。工资条本身不合规，公司还可能被另罚。
  Benefit: In hearings and lawsuits, how much you were paid and whether overtime happened still needs your evidence—the employer's records help, but yours are the floor. Non-compliant pay stubs can draw separate penalties against the employer.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Labor Code §226 (itemized wage statements; text via leginfo). <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/billNavClient.xhtml?bill_id=201720180SB1252>；Cal. Labor Code §1174 (payroll records, 3 years); Cal. Labor Code §2810.5 (wage notice at hire).
- 备注：§226(e) 罚则已核实：员工因雇主明知故犯遭受损失的，可获实际损失，或首个违规发薪期每人 $50、之后每个发薪期每人 $100（每人上限 $4,000），另加诉讼费和合理律师费。云端邮箱、Slack 消息离职后即失访问权，提前导出。
  Note: §226(e) penalties verified: an employee injured by a knowing and intentional violation recovers the greater of actual damages or $50 per employee for the initial pay period and $100 per employee for each subsequent pay period, capped at $4,000 per employee, plus costs and reasonable attorney's fees. Cloud email and Slack access die at separation—export early.

### 9. 进有粉尘、噪声、化学品的岗位之前，先看危害告知；职业体检由单位安排并掏钱 / Before taking a dusty, noisy, or chemical job, read the hazard disclosure — occupational exams are on the employer's dime
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。入职培训时把 hazard communication 那部分听完，Safety Data Sheet（SDS）知道去哪查。每次体检的报告自己留一份。难在要当面开口问、当面把材料要到手。

- 说人话：Cal/OSHA 要求雇主书面告知化学品危害、提供 SDS、做培训（Hazard Communication, 8 CCR §5194）。铅、石棉、硅尘等岗位，法律要求定期职业体检，由单位安排并掏钱；体检发现你不适合的，单位必须调岗。没做该做的体检就让你上岗，本身就是违规。
  In plain terms: Cal/OSHA requires employers to disclose chemical hazards in writing, provide SDSs, and train you (Hazard Communication, 8 CCR §5194). Putting you on the job without the required exams is itself a violation.
- 收益：体检是日后职业病索赔的关键证据链起点。Cal/OSHA 投诉可匿名，报复投诉人违法（§6310）。
  Benefit: These exams are the start of the evidence chain for any future occupational-disease claim. Cal/OSHA complaints can be anonymous, and retaliating against complainants is illegal (§6310).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：8 CCR §5194 (Hazard Communication); Cal. Labor Code §§ 6401, 6403 (employer safety duties); Cal. Labor Code §6310 (anti-retaliation).

  Note: Exam frequencies differ by substance (lead: 8 CCR §1532.1; silica: §5204; asbestos: §1529)—details 待核实/unverified. Get copies of all past exam reports before you leave.

### 10. 粉尘、噪声、化学毒物造成的损伤不可逆：防护用品单位必须免费给，没有防护的活可以拒绝 / Dust, noise, and chemical damage is irreversible: PPE must be free from the employer, and you can refuse unprotected work
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难在要一直戴防颗粒物口罩、耳塞、护目镜，还要拒绝没有防护的活，代价是可能被说"事多"。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is wearing the respirator, earplugs, and goggles every time—and refusing unprotected work at the cost of being called "difficult."
- 说人话：尘肺、噪声聋、化学性眼灼伤都是不可逆的，肺和听力坏了治不回来。加州雇主必须提供合格的防护用品，而且免费（§§6401、6403）。工作存在"现实明显的危险"（real and apparent hazard），你有权拒绝干并立即报告，雇主不得因此报复你（§6311）；被报复的去 Cal/OSHA 投诉（§6310）。
  In plain terms: Pneumoconiosis, noise-induced hearing loss, and chemical eye burns are irreversible—lungs and hearing don't grow back. California employers must provide adequate PPE, free (§§6401, 6403). Where work poses a real and apparent hazard, you may refuse it and must report it at once; the employer may not retaliate (§6311). If it does, complain to Cal/OSHA (§6310).
- 收益：拒绝权是 Cal/OSHA 给的一线保命工具：先保命，再走投诉。报复（降薪、调岗、开除）本身另构成违法，可单独追责。
  Benefit: The right to refuse is Cal/OSHA's frontline life-saving tool: protect yourself first, complain after. Retaliation (pay cuts, demotions, firing) is separately illegal and separately actionable.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Labor Code §§ 6401, 6403 (employer duty, PPE); Cal. Labor Code §6311 (right to refuse imminently hazardous work); Cal. Labor Code §6310 (retaliation complaints to Cal/OSHA).
- 备注：§6311 的措辞已按现行法条核对：'real and apparent hazard'（现实明显的危险）。口罩要认对：防颗粒物口罩（N95 等）才挡粉尘，普通外科口罩不挡。化学品溅到身上的现场处理见第 13 节。
  Note: §6311's wording checked against the current statute: 'real and apparent hazard.' Get the right mask: only particulate respirators (N95 and the like) stop dust—surgical masks don't. For on-the-spot chemical splash response, see Chapter 13.

### 11. 上班受伤：先报告、填 DWC-1，雇主 1 个工作日内必须把表给你 / Hurt at work: report it and file the DWC-1 — the employer must hand you the form within one working day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。30 天内必须通知雇主（§5400），越快越好，最好书面留证。
  Cost: Free. You must notify the employer within 30 days (§5400)—sooner is better, in writing for proof.
- 说人话：受伤第一时间告诉雇主（邮件、短信留证）。雇主知道后 1 个工作日内必须给你 DWC-1 索赔表（§5401）；你填好交回，索赔即立案。立案后保险公司先批最高 $10,000 的治疗费（§5402(c)），90 天内不拒赔就推定成立。看病去雇主 MPN（医疗网络）名单内的医生；入职时书面指定过自己医生的（predesignation），可以看自己的医生。上下班路上出车祸：加州一般不算工伤（commuting 除外情形极少，如开公司车、出差途中）。
  In plain terms: Tell the employer right away (email or text, for proof). Within one working day of learning about the injury, the employer must give you the DWC-1 claim form (§5401); once you complete and return it, the claim is filed. The insurer must then authorize up to $10,000 in treatment (§5402(c)), and a claim not denied within 90 days is presumed compensable. Treat with a doctor in the employer's MPN (medical provider network)—unless you predesignated your own doctor in writing at hire, in which case you can see yours. Car crashes on the regular commute: generally *not* workers' comp in California (rare exceptions like company cars or business travel).
- 收益：不立案，医药费、养伤期间的工资（temporary disability）、伤残赔偿全部落空。立案后雇主必须 1 天内安排治疗授权，拖延另有 25% 罚则（§5814）。
  Benefit: Without a filed claim, medical bills, wage replacement during recovery (temporary disability), and disability awards are all lost. After filing, the employer must authorize treatment within 1 day—unreasonable delay draws a 25% penalty (§5814).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Labor Code §§ 5400 (30-day notice), 5401 (DWC-1 within one working day), 5402(b)–(c) ($10,000 treatment, 90-day presumption), 5405 (1-year deadline), 5814 (25% delay penalty).
- 备注：正式立案（Application for Adjudication）要在 1 年内（§5405），别拖。雇主不给表、不报保险的，直接去当地 DWC 办公室找 Information & Assistance officer，或自己从 dir.ca.gov 下载 DWC-1。
  Note: The formal Application for Adjudication has a 1-year deadline (§5405)—don't sit on it. If the employer won't hand over the form or report to insurance, go to your local DWC office's Information & Assistance officer, or download the DWC-1 from dir.ca.gov yourself.

### 12. 没有"48 小时"这条线：不舒服先打 911，别算时间 / There's no 48-hour rule: feeling ill? Call 911 first — don't do math
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。
  Cost: Free.
- 说人话：加州工伤只看"因工作引起、在工作过程中"（arising out of and in the course of employment, §3600），没有"发病 48 小时内死亡才算"这种线。工作时心脏病、脑卒中发作，能证明和工作相关就可能赔；但为了"凑条件"耽误叫救护车，赌的是自己的命。先打 911，再谈索赔。
  In plain terms: California workers' comp asks only whether the injury arose out of and in the course of employment (§3600)—there's no "die within 48 hours" tripwire. A heart attack or stroke at work can be compensable if work-relatedness is shown. But delaying the ambulance to "meet the criteria" gambles your life. Call 911 first; argue the claim later.
- 收益："工作相关"要医生意见支撑，发病当天的就医记录、目击者、同事联系方式都是证据。短视频把这类事讲成"撑到公司就有钱"，方向正好反了：能改变结局的是发病后那几十分钟有没有去医院。
  Benefit: "Work-relatedness" needs a doctor's opinion—same-day medical records, witnesses, and coworkers' contacts are the evidence. Viral videos framing this as "make it to the office and get paid" have it backwards: what changes the outcome is whether you got to a hospital in those first minutes.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Labor Code §3600 (AOE/COE requirement).
- 备注：累积伤（cumulative trauma，如长期重复劳损）的起算点是"你知道或应当知道是工作引起"之日，别等。
  Note: For cumulative trauma (e.g., repetitive-strain injuries), the clock starts when you knew or should have known it was work-related—don't wait.

### 13. 公司没买工伤保险：待遇一分不少，州基金先垫付 / No workers' comp insurance? Your benefits don't shrink a cent — the state fund pays first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。公司不认的，去当地 DWC 办公室找 Information & Assistance officer 帮忙。难在可能要走听证程序。
  Cost: Free. If the company won't cooperate, get help from the Information & Assistance officer at your local DWC office. The hard part is possibly going through hearings.
- 说人话：加州雇主必须买工伤保险（§3700），不买是轻罪，最高可判县监狱 1 年 + 罚款（§3700.5）。公司没买，你的待遇一分不少：先找公司赔，公司不赔，州的 Uninsured Employers Benefits Trust Fund（UEBTF）先垫付（§3716），再去找公司追。而且没保险的雇主面前，"工伤保险是唯一救济"不适用——你还可以额外去法院告他。
  In plain terms: California employers must carry workers' comp insurance (§3700); going without is a misdemeanor punishable by up to a year in county jail plus fines (§3700.5). If yours didn't, your benefits don't shrink: claim against the company first, and if it won't pay, the state's Uninsured Employers Benefits Trust Fund (UEBTF) pays out (§3716) and chases the company later. Plus, against an uninsured employer the "workers' comp is your exclusive remedy" rule doesn't apply—you can also sue in civil court.
- 收益："公司没买工伤险所以赔不了"是这类事里最常见的一句假话。没参保只会让公司被加罚，你的待遇不减。
  Benefit: "No insurance, no payout" is the most common lie in these cases. Lack of coverage only adds penalties for the company; your benefits stay whole.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Labor Code §3700 (insurance required), §3700.5 (misdemeanor), §§ 3715–3716 (UEBTF).
- 备注：UEBTF 对职业病/累积伤一般不赔——例外仅当整个暴露期间没有任何雇主有工伤保险、合法自保或合法未参保（§3716(b)，已核实）。雇主收到 stop order（停工令）还继续用工的，另构成轻罪。
  Note: The UEBTF generally doesn't cover occupational-disease/cumulative-injury claims — the only exception is when no employer during the entire exposure period was insured, permissibly self-insured, or legally uninsured (§3716(b), verified). An employer that keeps using labor after a stop order commits a separate misdemeanor.

### 14. 伤情稳定后做伤残评级：等级直接换算成钱 / After maximum medical improvement: the disability rating converts directly to money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。等医生写 P&S（permanent and stationary）报告；评级按 AMA 指南打分，再按年龄、职业调整。前后要等一段时间。
  Cost: Free. Wait for the doctor's P&S (permanent and stationary) report; ratings follow the AMA Guides, adjusted for age and occupation. It takes a while.
- 说人话：加州叫 permanent disability（PD）评级。2026 年标准：PD 每周 $160–$290，按评级折成"周数"发——评级越高，周数越多。70% 以上评级，PD 发完还有终身年金（life pension）。评级出来之前，别签"一次性了结"协议。另外，回不去原岗位的，还有 $6,000 的培训代金券（Supplemental Job Displacement Benefit voucher, §4658.7）。
  In plain terms: California calls it a permanent disability (PD) rating. For 2026: PD pays $160–$290 per week, converted by your rating into a number of weeks—higher rating, more weeks. Ratings of 70%+ add a lifetime pension after the PD award runs out. Don't sign any "full and final settlement" before the rating is done. And if you can't return to the old job, there's a $6,000 retraining voucher (Supplemental Job Displacement Benefit, §4658.7).
- 收益：养伤期间先拿 temporary disability（TD）：工资的 2/3，每两周发，2026 年最高每周 $1,764.11、最低 $264.61，免税。TD 和 PD 是两笔钱，别混。
  Benefit: During recovery you first get temporary disability (TD): two-thirds of wages, paid biweekly—2026 max $1,764.11/week, min $264.61/week, tax-free. TD and PD are separate payments; don't confuse them.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Labor Code §§ 4658 (PD weeks schedule), 4658.7 (SJDB voucher $6,000); DWC workers' compensation benefits chart (2026 TD/PD rates). <https://www.dir.ca.gov/dwc/workerscompensationbenefits.htm>
- 备注：具体"评级→周数"查 DWC 评级表。PD 按受伤日期档算金额，以当年 DWC 表为准。
  Note: Look up the exact rating-to-weeks conversion in the DWC schedule. PD amounts follow the date-of-injury bracket—use the current DWC chart.

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱，办一套手续。
  Cost: Free—one round of paperwork.
- 说人话：第一笔丧葬费，最高 $10,000（§4701）。第二笔死亡抚恤金，按完全抚养家属人数：1 人 $250,000，2 人 $290,000，3 人及以上 $320,000（§4702），按周发放；有未成年子女的，发到最小的满 18 岁（每周不低于 $224）。谈赔偿时别只谈一笔。
  In plain terms: First, burial expenses up to $10,000 (§4701). Second, the death benefit by number of total dependents: $250,000 for one, $290,000 for two, $320,000 for three or more (§4702), paid weekly; with minor children, payments run until the youngest turns 18 (no less than $224/week). Don't settle for just one part.

WCAB, 145 Cal.App.4th 91 (2006)).

- 来源：Cal. ### 16. 在单位长期被欺负、辱骂、威胁：别硬扛，先记下来，再按性质走渠道 / Bullied, berated, or threatened at work? Don't tough it out — document first, then use the right channel
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。要花时间记录：日期、在场的人、对方原话，聊天截图和邮件留原件。走投诉、打官司要花几个月。换工作的代价最高，走不走由你自己算。
  Cost: Free. It takes time to log dates, witnesses, and exact words, and to keep original screenshots and emails. Complaints and lawsuits take months. Quitting costs the most—that math is yours.
- 说人话：在单位被欺负的人，得心脏病、中风的风险高约六成（跟踪 12 年的研究），几年后冒出自杀念头的几率约是别人的两倍。挨骂受气不是忍一忍就过去的小事。法律上：加州没有专门管"职场霸凌"的法律，单纯的冷落、排挤、刁难很难立案；但 SB 553（2024 年 7 月生效，§6401.9）要求几乎所有雇主要求书面防暴力计划 + 全员培训 + 暴力事件日志，你可以要求走公司的报告渠道。涉及种族、性别等受保护类别的辱骂、骚扰 → FEHA 投诉；被打、被威胁 → 报警 911；因报工伤、投诉欠薪被穿小鞋 → 反报复保护（§6310、§1102.5）。已经撑不住了，先打 988（心理危机热线）。
  In plain terms: Bullied workers face roughly 60% higher cardiovascular risk (a 12-year follow-up study) and about double the odds of later suicidal ideation. It's not something you just shrug off. Legally: California has no standalone "workplace bullying" law—pure cold-shouldering and nitpicking are hard to action. But SB 553 (effective July 2024, §6401.9) requires nearly every employer to keep a written workplace-violence prevention plan with training and an incident log—you can demand the company's reporting channel. Abuse tied to a protected class (race, sex, etc.) → FEHA complaint; assault or threats → call 911; punished for filing workers' comp or wage complaints → anti-retaliation protection (§6310, §1102.5). If you're at breaking point, call 988 (the crisis lifeline) first.
- 收益：SB 553 下雇主必须有"接受和回应暴力报告、禁止报复"的程序，报告了公司不处理，公司先违法。FEHA 的骚扰条款 1 人以上的雇主就适用。精神类工伤索赔一般要求受雇满 6 个月（§3208.3，突发极端事件除外）。
  Benefit: Under SB 553, employers must maintain procedures to receive and respond to violence reports and to prohibit retaliation—ignoring your report puts the company in violation first. FEHA's harassment provisions apply to employers with even one employee. Psychiatric workers' comp claims generally require 6 months of employment (§3208.3, except for sudden extraordinary events).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Xu T et al. (2019). Workplace bullying and workplace violence as risk factors for cardiovascular disease: a multi-cohort study. European Heart Journal. <https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/ehy683>；Nielsen MB et al. (2015). Workplace bullying and suicidal ideation: a 3-wave longitudinal Norwegian study. - 备注：定 B 的理由：健康数字来自跟踪记录，不是分组实验，"被欺负"是本人自己报的。§3208.3(d) 的 6 个月规则已核实：精神类工伤一般要求受雇满 6 个月（不必连续）；突发极端工作事件（sudden and extraordinary employment condition）除外。不主张一受气就辞职，换工作的代价由你自己算。
  Note: Graded B because the health figures come from observational follow-ups, not experiments, and "bullying" was self-reported. §3208.3(d)'s 6-month rule verified: psychiatric-injury claims generally require 6 months of employment (need not be continuous), except for a sudden and extraordinary employment condition. Quitting at the first insult isn't the advice—weigh the cost of leaving yourself.

### 17. 离职时公司必须给你：最后工资、EDD 变更通知、医保延续通知 / At separation the company must hand you: final pay, the EDD change notice, and health-continuation notice

- 成本：不花钱。走之前当面要，或者发一条书面消息要，留下要过的记录。难在关系闹僵了还得开这个口。
  Cost: Free. Ask in person before you go, or send a written request and keep the record. The hard part is asking when the relationship is already broken.
- 说人话：加州没有中国式的"离职证明"，但公司必须给三样。① 最后工资：被裁、被开当天结清；主动辞职 72 小时内结清（提前 72 小时通知的，最后一天结清）；含未休年假折现。拖欠的每天罚一天工资、最多 30 天（§§201–203）。② EDD 的《雇佣关系变更通知》（DE 2320），写清离职原因和日期，申领失业金要用。③ 医保延续通知：20 人以上公司走联邦 COBRA（18 个月），20 人以下走 Cal-COBRA（最长 36 个月），60 天内决定。另外，大规模裁员（75 人以上企业、30 天内裁 50 人以上）公司要提前 60 天书面通知（CalWARN，§§1400–1408），没通知的按缺的天数赔工资。
  In plain terms: California has no China-style separation certificate, but the employer must give you three things. (1) Final pay: due immediately if you're fired or laid off; within 72 hours if you quit (on your last day if you gave 72 hours' notice)—including accrued vacation payout. Late payment draws a day's wages per day, up to 30 days (§§201–203). (2) The EDD "Notice to Employee as to Change in Relationship" (DE 2320), stating the reason and date—needed for your UI claim. (3) Health-continuation notice: federal COBRA (18 months) at 20+ employee firms, Cal-COBRA (up to 36 months) below 20—decide within 60 days. Separately, mass layoffs (75+ employee firms cutting 50+ people in 30 days) require 60 days' written advance notice (CalWARN, §§1400–1408); miss it and the company owes back pay for the shortfall days.
- 收益：最后工资含加班费、未休年假、已挣未付的佣金。DE 2320 是 EDD 审核失业金资格的依据。CalWARN 赔的是"应通知未通知"天数的工资和福利。
  Benefit: Final pay includes overtime, unused vacation, and earned commissions. The DE 2320 is what the EDD uses to judge UI eligibility. CalWARN pays back wages and benefits for the days of notice you should have gotten.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Labor Code §§ 201–203 (final pay timing and penalties), §227.3 (vacation payout); Cal. Labor Code §§ 1400–1408 (CalWARN); DIR, Cal-WARN Act. <http://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/Cal-WARNAct.html>；California DMHC, COBRA/Cal-COBRA. <https://www.dmhc.ca.gov/HealthCareinCalifornia/TypesofPlans/KeepYourHealthCoverage(COBRA).aspx>
- 备注：DE 2320 的法条依据已核实：UI Code §1089(c) 要求雇主在雇佣关系变更时立即书面通知员工；实施细则见 22 CCR §1089-1。公司拖着不给最后工资的，走第 7 节第 2 条（Labor Commissioner）。新雇主做背景调查时，前雇主通常只确认职位和日期。
  Note: The DE 2320's statutory basis verified: UI Code §1089(c) requires employers to immediately notify employees in writing of any change in the employment relationship; implementing details in 22 CCR §1089-1. If the company stalls on final pay, see Chapter 7, Item 2 (Labor Commissioner). In reference checks, former employers typically confirm only title and dates.

### 18. 遣散费没有免税额：每一美元都算工资交税；失业金联邦要税、加州免税 / No tax break on severance: every dollar is taxable wages; UI is federally taxable but California-exempt
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。核对 W-2 和最后一张工资条。
  Cost: Free. Check your W-2 and final pay stub.
- 说人话：美国没有"3 倍平均工资内免税"：遣散费每一美元都算工资，联邦和加州都要交税（按补充工资预扣，到手比想象的少）。失业金：联邦算收入（可自愿预扣 10%，不然次年 4 月补税），加州不收州税。401(k) 别直接取现：59 岁半之前取要交 10% 罚金，先 rollover 到 IRA。
  In plain terms: There's no "tax-free up to 3× average wage" in the US: every severance dollar is wages, taxable federally and by California (withheld as supplemental wages—expect a smaller check than you imagined). Unemployment benefits: federally taxable (you can elect 10% withholding, or pay up the next April), California-exempt. Don't cash out your 401(k): withdrawals before 59½ draw a 10% penalty—roll it into an IRA instead.
- 收益：遣散费谈判时按"税后"算，别被税前数字忽悠。失业金的 10% 预扣在 EDD 申请时就能选，省得次年补税肉疼。
  Benefit: Negotiate severance in after-tax terms—don't be dazzled by the pre-tax number. The 10% UI withholding can be elected right in the EDD application, sparing next April's pain.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS, supplemental wages (severance taxable as wages); EDD, unemployment benefits and taxes (federal taxable, California-exempt). EDD DE 231PS (supplemental wage withholding rates). <https://edd.ca.gov/siteassets/files/pdf_pub_ctr/de231ps.pdf>
- 备注：加州补充工资预扣率已核实（EDD DE 231PS）：奖金和股票期权按 10.23% 预扣，其他补充工资（含遣散费）按 6.6% 预扣。具体税单问 CPA 或用 IRS 预扣计算器。遣散费分期付 vs 一次付清的税差，金额大时值得算一算。
  Note: California supplemental-wage withholding rates verified (EDD DE 231PS): bonuses and stock options at 10.23%; other supplemental wages (including severance) at 6.6%. For your actual bill, ask a CPA or use the IRS withholding estimator. For large severance, lump-sum vs. installments can change the tax bite—worth modeling.
