# 11. 程序员和技术人容易踩的红线 / Red Lines for Programmers and Technologists

口径：人身自由与金钱。/ Scope: Personal liberty and money.

本节口径是人身自由与金钱，换回的是「别被关」和「别赔钱」。写代码、做技术的人，常把下面这些事当成「只是技术问题」，可它们对应的是联邦刑法或加州法律里的具体罪名，或者民事上要赔的具体一笔钱。每条都给出法条里的数字。条目顺序按「技术人最容易以为没事 × 后果最重」从高到低排。第 1 条是通用的一条，讲动手前怎么判断，以及出事之后先做什么。

This section is about personal liberty and money — what buys you "don't get locked up" and "don't get sued into debt." People who write code often treat the items below as "just technical problems," but each maps to a specific federal or California criminal offense, or a specific sum of civil damages. Every entry gives the numbers straight from the statute. Entries are ordered by "how likely a technologist thinks it's harmless × how bad the consequences are," highest first. Item 1 is the general one: how to judge before you act, and what to do first if you're in trouble.

### 1. 动手前先问三句：损害了谁的利益、对方多有能力追究、我留了多少证据；被盯上后先找刑事律师、再开口前先闭嘴 / Before You Act, Ask Three Questions: Whose Interests You Harm, How Hard They Can Come After You, What Evidence You Left; If Investigated, Get a Criminal Defense Lawyer First and Keep Quiet Until Then
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。动手前把「技术上能不能做」换成另一个问题：厂商、平台、公司会不会去报案。难在忍住「这点小事没人管」的念头，因为服务器日志、支付记录、群聊记录，办案时默认都会被调出来
  Cost: Costs nothing. Before acting, swap the question "can I technically do this" for a different one: will the vendor, platform, or company report it to law enforcement? The hard part is resisting the thought "nobody cares about something this small" — server logs, payment records, and chat histories are routinely pulled in investigations.
- 收益：在美国，你有权保持沉默、有权见律师再说话（Miranda v. Arizona, 1966）。对联邦探员撒谎本身就是犯罪（18 U.S.C. §1001），最高判 5 年——"我没干"这句话说错了，比原事更贵。出事后删日志、删聊天记录，按妨碍司法办（18 U.S.C. §1519），最高 20 年。所以顺序只有一种：先找刑事辩护律师，再决定说不说话，而不是先解释、先"配合"。
  Benefit: In the U.S., you have the right to remain silent and the right to talk to a lawyer before saying anything (Miranda v. Arizona, 1966). Lying to federal agents is itself a crime (18 U.S.C. §1001), punishable by up to 5 years — a wrongly worded "I didn't do it" can cost more than the original act. Deleting logs or chat records after the fact is obstruction of justice (18 U.S.C. §1519), up to 20 years. So there is only one correct order: get a criminal defense lawyer first, then decide whether to talk — not explain first or "cooperate" first.
- 说人话：动手之前先问一句：对方会不会报警、找 FBI。FBI 上门"随便聊聊"，你有权一句话不说、先找律师。对探员撒谎单独立罪，最高 5 年；删证据最高 20 年。律师越早介入越便宜，等起诉书下来再找就晚了。
  In plain terms: Before you act, ask: will the other side call the police or the FBI? If the FBI shows up for a "casual chat," you have the right to say nothing and get a lawyer first. Lying to agents is a standalone crime (up to 5 years); destroying evidence is up to 20 years. The earlier a lawyer gets involved, the cheaper it is — waiting until the indictment lands is too late.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：U.S. Supreme Court (1966). Miranda v. Arizona, 384 U.S. 436. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/supremecourt/text/384/436>; 18 U.S.C. §1001 (false statements, up to 5 years). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1001>; 18 U.S.C. §1519 (destruction of records in federal investigations, up to 20 years). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1519>
- 备注：联邦和州是两套系统：FBI、Secret Service 查联邦案，州警察、地方检察官查州案，两边可以同时办。本节其余条目讲的都是同一件事：对方有没有追究你的理由和能力。
  Note: The federal and state systems are separate: the FBI and Secret Service handle federal cases, state police and district attorneys handle state cases, and both can pursue you at the same time. Every other entry in this section is about the same thing: whether the other side has a reason and the ability to come after you.

### 2. 不做、不卖游戏外挂和辅助，包括"只读屏幕不碰内存"的 AI 辅助 / Don't Make or Sell Game Cheats and Assist Tools — Including "Screen-Reading Only" AI Assistants
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。代价是放弃这类接单和"小工具"的收入。难在这笔钱来得快，又看不出谁会来追究
  Cost: Costs nothing. The price is giving up this kind of gig income and "little tool" revenue. The hard part is that the money comes fast and it's unclear who would come after you.
- 收益：绕过或破解游戏的技术保护措施（反作弊、登录验证、加密协议）再卖工具，撞上 DMCA 反规避条款（17 U.S.C. §1201）。故意为商业利益这么干，刑事最高 5 年、罚 50 万美元（§1204）；民事按每次规避行为判 200 到 2500 美元法定赔偿，游戏公司还可以告你违反用户协议、索赔实际损失。历史上游戏公司（暴雪等）靠这套组合拳让外挂作者赔到破产、和解金额动辄百万美元。只卖"不碰内存"的按键模拟，一样可能被认定为规避访问控制。
  Benefit: Bypassing or cracking a game's technical protection measures (anti-cheat, login checks, encrypted protocols) and then selling the tool runs into the DMCA's anti-circumvention rules (17 U.S.C. §1201). Done willfully for commercial gain, it's a crime: up to 5 years and a $500,000 fine (§1204); civilly, statutory damages of $200 to $2,500 per act of circumvention, plus the game company can sue for breach of its terms of service and actual losses. Game companies (Blizzard and others) have historically used this combination to bankrupt cheat developers, with settlements routinely in the millions. Even selling "memory untouched" key-simulation tools can be found to circumvent access controls.
- 说人话：卖外挂绕过反作弊，刑事最高 5 年加 50 万美元罚款；民事按每份工具、每次规避行为累计赔，游戏公司告你违约另算。"只读屏、只模拟按键"不是护身符，法院看的是你有没有绕过它的技术保护措施。
  In plain terms: Selling cheats that bypass anti-cheat is up to 5 years plus a $500,000 fine criminally; civil damages stack per tool sold and per act of circumvention, and the game company can separately sue you for breach of contract. "Screen-reading only, key simulation only" is no shield — courts look at whether you circumvented its technical protection measures.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：17 U.S.C. §1201 (circumvention of copyright protection systems). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/1201>; 17 U.S.C. §1204 (criminal offenses: up to 5 years / $500,000 first offense). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/1204>; 17 U.S.C. §1203(c)(3) (statutory damages $200–$2,500 per act of circumvention). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/1203>

### 3. 不写、不卖抢票、秒杀、刷单、薅羊毛脚本，哪怕只是"自动化点按钮" / Don't Write or Sell Ticket Bots, Flash-Sale Scripts, or Fake-Order Tools — Even "Just Automating Clicks"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不占时间
  Cost: Costs nothing and takes no time.
- 收益：联邦《BOTS 法案》（Better Online Ticket Sales Act, 2016）明文禁止绕过票务网站的购票限制、安全措施（验证码、限购、下单频率控制），也禁止转卖用这种手段抢到的票。归 FTC 执法，按违反 FTC 法第 5 条办。FTC 2021 年第一次出手，一次就罚了三家黄牛公司共 3160 万美元（实缴 370 万美元）。民事罚款按每次违规累计（随通胀调整，单次最高数万美元）。卖抢票软件本身也可能构成对票务平台的侵权（协助违约、trespass to chattels）。
  Benefit: The federal BOTS Act (Better Online Ticket Sales Act, 2016) expressly prohibits circumventing a ticket seller's purchase limits and security measures (CAPTCHAs, per-person limits, order-rate controls), and prohibits reselling tickets obtained that way. The FTC enforces it as a violation of Section 5 of the FTC Act. In the FTC's first cases (2021), three scalper companies were hit with $31.6 million in judgments ($3.7 million actually paid). Civil penalties stack per violation (inflation-adjusted, tens of thousands of dollars each). Selling the bot software itself can also expose you to claims from the ticketing platform (inducing breach of contract, trespass to chattels).
- 说人话：写脚本绕过验证码、限购去抢票，联邦法律明文禁止，FTC 罚起来一次就是几千万美元量级。刷单、薅羊毛脚本同理：平台按违约和欺诈追究，金额大了还可能撞上电汇欺诈（wire fraud，最高 20 年）。
  In plain terms: Scripting around CAPTCHAs and purchase limits to grab tickets is expressly banned by federal law, and FTC fines run into the tens of millions per case. Flash-sale and fake-order scripts are the same story: platforms pursue breach-of-contract and fraud claims, and at scale you can run into wire fraud (up to 20 years).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Better Online Ticket Sales Act of 2016, Pub. L. 114-274. <https://www.congress.gov/114/plaws/publ274/PLAW-114publ274.htm>; FTC (2021). FTC Brings First-Ever Cases Under the BOTS Act ($31.6M judgments). <https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2021/01/ftc-brings-first-ever-cases-under-bots-act>; 18 U.S.C. §1343 (wire fraud, up to 20 years). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1343>
- 备注：2025 年 3 月白宫行政令要求 FTC 严格执法 BOTS 法案，执法只会更严，不会松。本条只讲抢票；自己手动买一张自用不在此列。
  Note: A March 2025 White House executive order directed the FTC to enforce the BOTS Act rigorously — enforcement will only get stricter. This entry covers bots; manually buying one ticket for yourself is not covered.

### 4. 爬虫只爬无需登录的公开页面，不绕反爬、不碰个人信息，爬到的数据不卖 / Scrape Only Public Pages That Need No Login; Don't Bypass Anti-Scraping, Don't Touch Personal Data, Don't Sell What You Scrape
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。爬之前先看一眼 robots.txt 和网站的服务条款。要登录、要 API key、要破解签名或绕过验证码才拿得到的数据，不爬。难在这些数据技术上一抓就到手，忍住全靠自觉
  Cost: Costs nothing. Before scraping, check robots.txt and the site's terms of service. Data that requires a login, an API key, or defeating signatures or CAPTCHAs is off limits. The hard part is that this data is technically trivial to grab — restraint is entirely on you.
- 收益：hiQ v. LinkedIn（第九巡回上诉法院，2022）在 Van Buren v. United States（最高法院，2021）之后确立：在第九巡回区，爬取完全公开的页面不构成 CFAA 下的"未经授权访问"——公开网站默认对所有人开放，不存在"门"。但三条红线还在：第一，绕过登录、付费墙、验证码、技术封禁去拿数据，仍可能构成 CFAA 违法（18 U.S.C. §1030(a)(2)，轻则 1 年、为牟利或信息价值超 5000 美元则最高 5 年）；第二，违反服务条款会被告违约，网站还可以告 trespass、copyright；第三，爬到加州居民的个人信息再卖，撞上 CCPA/CPRA（见第 15 条），按每条违规 2,663 到 7,988 美元累计。
  Benefit: hiQ v. LinkedIn (9th Cir., 2022), following Van Buren v. United States (Supreme Court, 2021), established that in the Ninth Circuit, scraping fully public pages is not "without authorization" under the CFAA — a public website is open to everyone by default; there is no "gate" to break. But three red lines remain: first, defeating logins, paywalls, CAPTCHAs, or technical blocks to get data can still violate the CFAA (18 U.S.C. §1030(a)(2): up to 1 year, or up to 5 years if for gain or the information is worth over $5,000); second, violating terms of service invites breach-of-contract suits, plus trespass and copyright claims; third, scraping Californians' personal information and selling it runs into the CCPA/CPRA (see item 15), at $2,663 to $7,988 per violation.
- 说人话：公开页面随便看、随便爬（在加州所在的第九巡回区），但要登录、要破解、要绕验证码的数据别碰，碰了就是联邦计算机犯罪，牟利最高 5 年。爬到的个人信息拿去卖，按加州隐私法一条一条累计罚款（每次违规最高 7,988 美元）。
  In plain terms: Public pages are fine to view and scrape (in the Ninth Circuit, which covers California), but data behind logins, cracks, or CAPTCHA-bypasses is off limits — that's a federal computer crime, up to 5 years if done for gain. Selling scraped personal information stacks fines per record under California privacy law (up to $7,988 per violation).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：hiQ Labs, Inc. v. LinkedIn Corp., 31 F.4th 1180 (9th Cir. 2022). <https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca9/17-16783/17-16783-2022-04-18.html>; Van Buren v. United States, 593 U.S. 374 (2021). <https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/20pdf/19-783_k53l.pdf>; 18 U.S.C. §1030(a)(2), (c)(2) (penalties). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1030>
- 备注：hiQ 案只解决了 CFAA 这一条，LinkedIn 的合同等主张还在打；而且这是第九巡回区的判例，其他巡回区口径可能更严。收到 cease-and-desist（停止函）后继续爬，风险陡增。
  Note: hiQ resolved only the CFAA question; LinkedIn's contract and other claims continued separately — and it's a Ninth Circuit decision; other circuits may read the law more strictly. Continuing to scrape after receiving a cease-and-desist letter sharply increases risk.

### 5. 不接赌博、诈骗、色情 App 的开发单，不替"客户"搭支付通道、解封账号 / Don't Take Development Gigs for Gambling, Fraud, or Porn Apps — and Don't Build Payment Channels or Unban Accounts for "Clients"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。代价是放弃这类报价高、需求又说不清的外包。难在对方给的钱常常比正常单子高一大截
  Cost: Costs nothing. The price is turning down high-paying, vaguely-specified outsourcing work. The hard part is that these clients often pay far above market rates.
- 收益：在美国，给赌博网站写代码、搭支付、做运维，按"帮助犯"（aiding and abetting, 18 U.S.C. §2）与主犯同罪。非法赌博业务（18 U.S.C. §1955）最高 5 年；为非法网络赌博提供资金通道（UIGEA, 31 U.S.C. §5363/5366）最高 5 年；跨州传赌博信息（Wire Act, 18 U.S.C. §1084）最高 2 年；诈骗类 App 直接撞电汇欺诈（18 U.S.C. §1343），最高 20 年。洗钱（18 U.S.C. §1956）再加 20 年。"我只是写代码的"不是辩护理由——帮助犯罚得和主犯一样重。
  Benefit: In the U.S., writing code, building payments, or doing ops for a gambling site makes you an aider and abettor (18 U.S.C. §2), punished the same as the principal. Illegal gambling business (18 U.S.C. §1955): up to 5 years; providing funding channels for unlawful internet gambling (UIGEA, 31 U.S.C. §§5363, 5366): up to 5 years; transmitting wagering information interstate (Wire Act, 18 U.S.C. §1084): up to 2 years; fraud apps run straight into wire fraud (18 U.S.C. §1343): up to 20 years; money laundering (18 U.S.C. §1956) adds another 20. "I just wrote the code" is not a defense — aiders face the same punishment as principals.
- 说人话：这类报价高、需求含糊的单子，接了就和赌博团伙、诈骗团伙同罪：赌博 5 年起步，诈骗类最高 20 年，洗钱再加 20 年。帮诈骗团伙解封账号、搭支付通道，按共犯办，不按"帮忙"打折。
  In plain terms: Taking these high-paying, vague gigs puts you in the same boat as the gambling or fraud ring: gambling starts at 5 years, fraud goes up to 20, money laundering adds 20 more. Unbanning accounts or building payment rails for fraudsters is charged as complicity — no "just helping" discount.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：18 U.S.C. §2 (aiding and abetting — punished as principal). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2>; 18 U.S.C. §1955 (illegal gambling business, up to 5 years). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1955>; 31 U.S.C. §5366 (UIGEA criminal penalties, up to 5 years). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/31/5366>; 18 U.S.C. §1343 (wire fraud, up to 20 years). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1343>; 18 U.S.C. §1956 (money laundering, up to 20 years). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1956>
- 备注："明知"不需要对方把用途说出口：报价明显高于市价、功能是充值提现加赔率、客户不肯说用途，都会被拿来推断你心里清楚。加州自己也有赌博法（Penal Code §330 起），州和联邦可以同时追究。
  Note: "Knowledge" doesn't require the client to state the purpose outright: above-market pay, deposit/withdrawal-plus-odds features, and a client who won't explain the use case are all used to infer you knew. California has its own gambling laws (Penal Code §330 et seq.), and state and federal authorities can pursue you simultaneously.

### 6. 离职时交清账号权限，不删库、不留后门、不改密码锁系统，哪怕公司欠你钱 / When You Leave, Hand Back All Access: Don't Delete Databases, Plant Backdoors, or Lock Systems — Even If the Company Owes You Money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。公司欠你工资，走劳工局投诉（Labor Commissioner）或请律师要回来，别自己动手。难在气头上要忍住不去碰系统
  Cost: Costs nothing. If the company owes you wages, file with the Labor Commissioner or get a lawyer — don't take it into your own hands. The hard part is keeping your hands off the systems when you're furious.
- 收益：离职后用原账号登录、删数据、改密码、留后门，撞两条：联邦 CFAA（18 U.S.C. §1030(a)(5)）"故意破坏受保护的计算机"，最高 10 年；加州 Penal Code §502(c)(4)（未经许可增删改数据）、(c)(5)（干扰计算机服务），wobbler 罪名，轻则县监 1 年、罚 5000 美元，重则 16 个月到 3 年、罚 1 万美元。公司还可以民事索赔恢复费用和业务损失。欠薪纠纷和删库是两码事，法官不会因为公司欠你钱就原谅你删库。
  Benefit: Logging in with old credentials after leaving, deleting data, changing passwords, or planting backdoors hits two statutes: the federal CFAA (18 U.S.C. §1030(a)(5)) for intentionally damaging a protected computer — up to 10 years; and California Penal Code §502(c)(4) (unauthorized altering/deleting of data) and (c)(5) (disrupting computer services) — wobblers carrying up to 1 year in county jail and a $5,000 fine as misdemeanors, or 16 months to 3 years and a $10,000 fine as felonies. The company can also sue civilly for recovery costs and business losses. A wage dispute and deleting a database are two separate matters — no judge excuses the deletion because the company owed you money.
- 说人话：账号密码是公司给的，不等于离职后还能用，登进去、删数据、改密码都算犯罪：联邦最高 10 年，加州最高 3 年。欠薪走劳工局，别自己动手。
  In plain terms: Credentials the company gave you don't stay valid after you leave — logging in, deleting data, or changing passwords is a crime: up to 10 years federally, up to 3 years in California. Take wage disputes to the Labor Commissioner, not into your own hands.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：18 U.S.C. §1030(a)(5), (c)(4) (intentional damage, up to 10 years). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1030>; Cal. Penal Code §502(c)(4), (c)(5), (d)(1) (wobbler: up to 1 yr / $5,000 misdemeanor; 16 mo–3 yrs / $10,000 felony). <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=502.&lawCode=PEN>

### 7. 离职不带走源码、客户名单和技术文档，不上传个人网盘，不在下家复用 / Don't Take Source Code, Client Lists, or Technical Docs When You Leave — Not to Personal Cloud Drives, Not to Reuse at Your Next Job
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。离职前把个人电脑和网盘里的公司资料删干净。难在有些文件是自己一行行写出来的，舍不得
  Cost: Costs nothing. Before leaving, wipe company materials from your personal computer and cloud drives. The hard part is that some of those files you wrote line by line yourself.
- 收益：商业秘密在美国有两套武器。民事上：联邦《保护商业秘密法》(DTSA, 18 U.S.C. §1836(b))，公司可以直接到联邦法院起诉，要禁令、实际损失加不当得利、恶意侵权的 2 倍惩罚性赔偿和律师费，紧急情况下法院可以不经通知直接扣押你的设备和文件；加州《统一商业秘密法》(CUTSA, Civil Code §3426 起）同样有禁令、实际损失、恶意时 2 倍赔偿（§3426.3）和律师费（§3426.4）。民事追诉时效 3 年。刑事上：盗窃商业秘密（18 U.S.C. §1832）个人最高 10 年、罚 25 万美元，公司最高罚 500 万美元。客户名单、源码、技术文档都算商业秘密，"自己写的代码"著作权也归公司（见第 13 条）。

Criminally: theft of trade secrets (18 U.S.C. §1832) carries up to 10 years and a $250,000 fine for individuals, up to $5 million for companies. Client lists, source code, and technical docs all count as trade secrets, and code you wrote yourself belongs to the company too (see item 13).
- 说人话：自己写的代码著作权也归公司，客户名单也算商业秘密，带走就是拿公司的东西。公司可以不经通知让法院直接扣你的电脑和硬盘；刑事盗窃商业秘密个人最高 10 年。下家用了你带去的代码，下家一起被告。
  In plain terms: Code you wrote belongs to the company, client lists are trade secrets — taking them is taking company property. The company can get a court to seize your computers and drives without warning; criminal trade-secret theft is up to 10 years for individuals. If your next employer uses the code you brought, they get sued too.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：18 U.S.C. §1836(b) (DTSA civil action: injunction, damages, double exemplary damages, ex parte seizure; 3-year limitations). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1836>; 18 U.S.C. §1832 (theft of trade secrets: up to 10 years / $250,000 individual; $5M organization). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1832>; Cal. Civil Code §§3426–3426.11 (CUTSA). <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?division=4.&part=1.&lawCode=CIV&title=5>
- 备注：DTSA 要求公司在保密协议里书面告知员工"举报违法可披露商业秘密"的豁免权，否则拿不到惩罚性赔偿和律师费——这是打工人反向可用的细节。离职前删干净个人设备里的公司资料，删不干净就别带走设备。
  Note: The DTSA requires companies to give employees written notice of the whistleblower immunity for disclosing trade secrets to report violations — without it, the company can't get exemplary damages or attorney's fees. That's a detail that works in employees' favor. Before leaving, wipe company data from personal devices; if you can't wipe it clean, don't take the device.

- 成本：不花钱，也不占时间
  Cost: Costs nothing and takes no time.
- 收益：未经许可控制别人的电脑、服务器、摄像头（哪怕只是跑个挖矿程序、扫个端口、放个木马"学习"），都撞 CFAA（18 U.S.C. §1030）：故意破坏、植入恶意代码按 (a)(5) 办，最高 10 年；加州 Penal Code §502(c)(7) 规定，纯粹未经授权的访问，初犯、无损害也是 infraction（违章），最高罚 1000 美元，带损害或再犯就升级成轻罪/重罪。公司机器上挖矿，公司可以按商业秘密和忠实义务（fiduciary duty）民事索赔，电费、算力损失一笔一笔算。僵尸网络（botnet）案在美国是 FBI 和司法部刑事起诉的重灾区。

### 9. 没有书面授权不测别人的系统，安全研究只走公开的漏洞赏金计划 / Don't Test Other People's Systems Without Written Authorization; Do Security Research Only Through Public Bug-Bounty Programs
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。公司内部的授权要落在纸上：邮件或工单里写清测试范围、时间窗口、谁签字。没有授权就不碰。难在"顺手看一眼"最容易忍不住
  Cost: Costs nothing. Internal authorization must be in writing: an email or ticket stating the test scope, time window, and who signed off. No authorization, no touching. The hard part is resisting "just taking a quick look."
- 收益：CFAA 对"未经授权访问"的刑事打击，按 (a)(2) 算：初犯 1 年，为牟利或信息价值超 5000 美元则最高 5 年。最高法院 Van Buren 案（2021）把"超出授权范围"收窄了：有权限但用了不该用的目的，不再算 CFAA 犯罪——但这不等于没事，违反员工手册一样可以被开除、被告违约。司法部 2022 年的政策是：对善意安全研究（good-faith security research）和仅违反服务条款的访问，不再刑事起诉；但民事诉讼和州法风险还在。所以安全路线只有一条：走厂商的漏洞赏金计划（HackerOne、Bugcrowd），或者拿到书面授权再测。
  Benefit: Criminal CFAA liability for "unauthorized access" falls under (a)(2): 1 year for a first offense, up to 5 years if for gain or the information is worth over $5,000. The Supreme Court's Van Buren (2021) narrowed "exceeds authorized access": authorized users who misuse access for the wrong purpose are no longer committing a CFAA crime — but that doesn't mean it's safe; violating the employee handbook can still get you fired and sued for breach. The DOJ's 2022 policy is to decline criminal prosecution for good-faith security research and for access that merely violates terms of service; civil suits and state-law risk remain. So there is only one safe route: go through vendors' bug-bounty programs (HackerOne, Bugcrowd) or get written authorization before testing.
- 说人话：没拿到书面授权，别人系统再脆弱也别碰：联邦最高 5 年。"有权限但目的不对"不再算联邦犯罪，可公司照样能开除你、告你违约。想练手，走漏洞赏金平台，那是唯一合法的靶场。
  In plain terms: Without written authorization, don't touch anyone else's system no matter how vulnerable it is: up to 5 years federally. "Authorized but wrong purpose" is no longer a federal crime, but the company can still fire you and sue for breach. Want to practice? Use bug-bounty platforms — that's the only legal firing range.

### 10. 漏洞按厂商的 coordinated disclosure 走，不先公开利用代码、不勒索 / Disclose Vulnerabilities Through the Vendor's Coordinated Disclosure Process; Don't Publish Exploits First or Extort
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。发现漏洞先私下报厂商，走 coordinated disclosure（先报、给修复期、再公开）。美国没有中国式"强制上报、强制备案"制度，报给厂商或 CISA（网络安全和基础设施安全局）就行。难在发现高危漏洞想炫技、想先发论文、想拿赏金的心
  Cost: Costs nothing. Report vulnerabilities privately to the vendor first, following coordinated disclosure (report, give a fix window, then publish). The U.S. has no China-style mandatory reporting-and-filing regime — reporting to the vendor or to CISA (the Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency) is enough. The hard part is the urge to show off, publish a paper first, or chase the bounty.
- 收益：先公开利用代码，厂商可以告你 DMCA 反规避（§1201，传播规避工具民事违法），受害者可以告你促成入侵；拿漏洞勒索厂商"不给钱就公开"，撞 CFAA 勒索条款（§1030(a)(7)），最高 5 年。发现漏洞本身不违法，但怎么用漏洞决定你是研究者还是罪犯。把漏洞卖给境外买家的，另有出口管制和经济间谍方面的风险（见本章第 7 条）。
  Benefit: Publishing exploit code before a fix lets the vendor sue you under DMCA anti-circumvention (sec. 1201 — trafficking in circumvention tools is civilly actionable), and victims can sue you for facilitating intrusions; extorting a vendor with "pay or I publish" hits the CFAA's extortion provision (§1030(a)(7)), up to 5 years. Finding a vulnerability isn't a crime — how you use it decides whether you're a researcher or a criminal. Selling vulnerabilities to foreign buyers carries additional export-control and economic-espionage risk (see item 7).
- 说人话：发现漏洞不犯法，拿漏洞勒索犯法：联邦最高 5 年。先公开利用代码，厂商和受害者会民事追你。正确姿势：私下报厂商，给修复期，再公开。
  In plain terms: Finding a vulnerability isn't a crime; extorting with one is: up to 5 years federally. Publishing exploits first invites civil suits from the vendor and victims. The correct posture: report privately to the vendor, allow a fix window, then publish.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：18 U.S.C. §1030(a)(7), (c)(3) (extortion involving computers, up to 5 years). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1030>; CISA. Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure Process. <https://www.cisa.gov/coordinated-vulnerability-disclosure-process>
- 备注：待核实：向境外提供未公开漏洞具体触发出口管制的哪一条款——例如 EAR §740.17（网络安全物项/入侵软件）的许可要求、ITAR 下 USML 的适用类别，或 18 U.S.C. §1831（经济间谍罪）的构成要件——公开资料中未找到直接对应的判例或执法实例，动手前请咨询出口管制律师。本条证据等级为 B，因为"先公开利用代码"的民事责任依赖具体案情，没有一条万能法条。
  Note: To be verified: exactly which export-control provision is triggered by providing undisclosed vulnerabilities to foreign buyers — e.g., licensing requirements under EAR §740.17 (cybersecurity items / intrusion software), the applicable ITAR USML category, or the elements of 18 U.S.C. §1831 (economic espionage) — no directly on-point case or enforcement example was found in public sources; consult an export-control attorney before acting. This entry is grade B because civil liability for pre-disclosure publication of exploit code is fact-dependent; there is no one-size-fits-all statute.
