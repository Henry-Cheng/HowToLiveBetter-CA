# 8. 别把自己搭进去 / Don't Get Yourself in Trouble: Legal and Property Safety

口径：这一节算的是钱和人身自由，不换算成寿命。讲的是三件事：法律规定你要担什么责任、照着做能少赔多少钱、出了事你在程序上有哪些权利。法条编号都和官方原文核对过。排序的办法是：一次出事损失最大、做起来又最省事的放最前面；需要提前安排的放中间；只有经验、没有官方文件撑着的放最后。
Scope: This section counts money and personal liberty, not converted into lifespan. It covers three things: what liability the law imposes on you, how much money you can save by doing things right, and what procedural rights you have when something goes wrong. Every statute number cited has been checked against the official text. Items are ordered so that the costliest mistakes that are also easiest to avoid come first, items needing advance planning sit in the middle, and experience-only items without official backing come last.

**肇事逃逸的代价最大，先说**：出了交通事故先停车、救人、报警，不要跑（第 1 条）。
**The costliest mistake is hit-and-run, so it comes first**: after a crash, stop, help, and call 911 — never flee (Item 1).

**被骗了怎么办**：发现被骗，立刻联系银行要求止付，并向 FTC、FBI IC3 和警方报案（第 2 条）；反诈的四句硬规则（第 3 条）；视频电话里看见脸、听见声音都不算核实（第 4 条）。
**What to do if you're scammed**: contact your bank immediately to stop the transfer, then report to the FTC, FBI IC3, and police (Item 2); four hard anti-scam rules (Item 3); seeing a face or hearing a voice on a video call is not verification (Item 4).

**和警察打交道**：被指控或逮捕时保持沉默、要求律师（第 5 条）；出了事别想着"去说清楚"（第 6 条）；被警察拦下时配合但别放弃权利（第 9 节第 17 条）。
**Dealing with police**: if accused or arrested, stay silent and demand a lawyer (Item 5); don't try to "clear things up" yourself (Item 6); comply when stopped but don't waive your rights (Chapter 9, Item 17).

**债务、合同和诉讼时效**：签字前把纸看完，不替人签字（第 17 条）；借钱写清借条，替人担保先想清楚（第 18 条）；维权有期限，书面合同 4 年、口头协议 2 年、人身伤害 2 年（第 19 条）；被起诉、被执行了，如实申报财产，别转移财产（第 20 条）。
**Debts, contracts, and deadlines**: read before you sign; never sign for others (Item 17); put loans in writing; think hard before guaranteeing (Item 18); know the deadlines — 4 years for written contracts, 2 for oral agreements and personal injury (Item 19); if sued or facing collection, disclose assets honestly (Item 20).

**婚姻和家庭**：恋爱和婚内的大额赠与，交付之前想清楚（第 23 条）；订婚赠与婚没结成时可以要回（第 24 条）；婚前财产写清楚，加州是夫妻共同财产州（第 25 条）；结婚前确认对方婚姻状况，重婚是犯罪（第 26 条）。
**Marriage and family**: think before giving big gifts (Item 23); engagement gifts are recoverable if the wedding is called off (Item 24); put premarital property in writing — California is a community property state (Item 25); confirm your partner's marital status — bigamy is a crime (Item 26).

**其余按场景**：喝了酒就不碰方向盘（第 7 条）；不把银行账户借给任何人（第 8 条）；每年查一次信用报告（第 9 条）；起了冲突先报警不动手（第 10 条）；躲不掉的侵害可以还手但只用合理武力（第 11 条）；被欠薪被坑钱走投诉和起诉（第 12 条）；自杀杀人念头当急症处理（第 14、15 条）；养狗必须牵绳（第 30 条）；家暴先打 911 再去法院申请保护令（第 43 条）。
**The rest by scenario**: don't drive after drinking (Item 7); never lend your bank account (Item 8); check your credit reports yearly (Item 9); call 911 before raising your fists (Item 10); fight back only with reasonable force when there's no escape (Item 11); owed wages or cheated — file complaints and lawsuits (Item 12); treat homicidal or suicidal thoughts as emergencies (Items 14–15); leash your dog (Item 30); for domestic violence, call 911 first, then seek a court order (Item 43).

### 1. 出了交通事故先停车、救人、报警，不要跑 / Stop, Help, and Call 911 After a Crash — Never Flee
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。撞了之后停车、开双闪、看有没有人受伤、打 911 报警，等警察来做记录。有人受伤先救人、叫救护车。小事故没人受伤、双方对事实没争议，交换姓名、电话、车牌、驾照和保险信息，拍照留证。
  Cost: Free. After a crash, stop, turn on hazard lights, check for injuries, and call 911 so police can take a report. If someone is hurt, help them and call an ambulance first. For a minor crash with no injuries and no dispute about what happened, exchange names, phone numbers, license plates, driver's license and insurance info, and take photos.
- 说人话：跑了，性质就变了。只撞坏东西跑掉，是轻罪，最多坐 6 个月牢、罚 1,000 美元。撞伤人跑掉，是"可轻可重"的罪：按轻罪最多 1 年牢加 1,000 到 10,000 美元罚款，按重罪 16 个月、2 年或 3 年监禁，撞死人或造成永久重伤是 2、3、4 年。留在现场，最多耽误一两个小时。
  In plain terms: Fleeing changes everything. Fleeing after only property damage is a misdemeanor — up to 6 months in jail and a $1,000 fine. Fleeing after injuring someone is a "wobbler": as a misdemeanor up to 1 year in jail plus $1,000–$10,000 in fines; as a felony 16 months, 2, or 3 years in prison — 2, 3, or 4 years if someone dies or suffers permanent serious injury. Staying at the scene costs you an hour or two at most.
- 收益：加州车辆法要求，发生事故必须停车、交换信息；撞伤人还必须救助伤者并报警。Vehicle Code §20002：只涉及财产损失的肇事逃逸是轻罪，最高 6 个月县监狱监禁和 1,000 美元罚款。Vehicle Code §20001：造成他人受伤或死亡后逃逸是 wobbler——按轻罪判最高 1 年监禁、1,000 到 10,000 美元罚款；按重罪判 16 个月、2 年或 3 年监禁，造成死亡或永久性重伤的判 2、3、4 年。另外，逃逸还可能让你的车险拒赔或大幅涨保费（加州）。
  Benefit: California law requires you to stop and exchange information after any crash; if someone is injured you must also render aid and call police. Vehicle Code §20002: hit-and-run involving only property damage is a misdemeanor — up to 6 months in county jail and a $1,000 fine. Vehicle Code §20001: hit-and-run causing injury or death is a wobbler — as a misdemeanor up to 1 year in jail plus $1,000–$10,000 in fines; as a felony 16 months, 2, or 3 years in prison, or 2, 3, or 4 years if the crash caused death or permanent serious injury. Fleeing can also get your claim denied or your premiums hiked (California).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Vehicle Code §§20001, 20002
- 备注："撞了一下没感觉"就开走，事后也可能被算成逃逸。拿不准就报警。
  Note: Even a bump you "barely felt" can later count as hit-and-run. When in doubt, call police.

### 2. 发现被骗，立刻联系银行要求止付，并向 FTC、FBI IC3 和警方报案 / If You're Scammed, Call Your Bank Immediately, Then Report to the FTC, FBI IC3, and Police
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。发现被骗，立刻打银行客服电话要求止付、冻结；同时把转账截图、聊天记录留好。
  Cost: Free. The moment you realize you've been scammed, call your bank to stop the transfer and freeze the account; save screenshots of the transfer and chat records.
- 说人话：钱刚转出去的那一小段时间是唯一的窗口。银行转账、借记卡、电汇还有可能拦，Zelle 一旦到账基本追不回来。所以先打银行电话，再向 FTC（reportfraud.ftc.gov）、FBI 网络犯罪中心 IC3（ic3.gov）和当地警察报案。凡是自称 IRS（国税局）、社保局、警察，让你把钱转到"安全账户"的，都是骗局。
  In plain terms: The short window right after the money leaves is the only one you get. Bank transfers, debit cards, and wire transfers can sometimes be stopped; once Zelle money lands, it's basically gone for good. So call your bank first, then report to the FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov), the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3, ic3.gov), and your local police. Anyone claiming to be the IRS, the Social Security Administration, or the police telling you to move money to a "safe account" is a scammer.
- 收益：FTC 负责受理消费欺诈举报（reportfraud.ftc.gov），FBI IC3（ic3.gov）受理网络犯罪报案，加州总检察长办公室也接受消费者投诉。银行在欺诈案件中有义务配合调查。Zelle 官方也明确：一旦钱转给已注册的收款人，通常无法撤销。所以止付越早越有效，报案材料留得越全越好（美国）。
  Benefit: The FTC takes consumer fraud reports (reportfraud.ftc.gov), FBI IC3 (ic3.gov) takes cybercrime complaints, and the California Attorney General's office accepts consumer complaints too. Banks are obligated to cooperate with fraud investigations. Zelle itself states that once money reaches a registered recipient, the payment generally cannot be reversed. So the sooner you stop the transfer the better, and the more complete your evidence the better (United States).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Federal Trade Commission, ReportFraud.ftc.gov；FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center, ic3.gov；加州总检察长办公室 oag.ca.gov
- 备注：Zelle 诈骗追回率极低，预防远比追回重要。银行客服电话印在卡背面，别在短信里找号码。
  Note: Zelle scam recovery rates are extremely low — prevention matters far more than recovery. Find your bank's customer service number on the back of your card, never in a text message.

### 3. 记住反诈硬规则：陌生来电不轻信、个人信息不透露、可疑链接不点击、转账先核实 / Anti-Scam Rules: Don't Trust Unknown Callers, Don't Share Personal Info, Don't Click Suspicious Links, Verify Before You Transfer
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是顶回去的那一秒钟：凡是电话、短信里让你"马上""立刻""不说就抓你"的人，先挂断。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is the one second of pushback: whenever a call or text pressures you to act "immediately" or threatens arrest, hang up first.
- 说人话：美国常见的骗局有几类：冒充 IRS（国税局）说你欠税要抓人；冒充社保局说你的号码涉案；"你孙子出车祸了快汇钱"的祖父母骗局；电脑弹窗说你中毒了要远程修；婚恋交友聊几个月带你"投资"（杀猪盘）；支票多汇了让你退差价。口诀是四句：来电不轻信、信息不透露、链接不点击、转账先核实。政府机构不会用电话或短信让你转账、买礼品卡。
  In plain terms: The common scams in the US come in a few flavors: fake IRS agents saying you owe taxes and will be arrested; fake Social Security Administration agents saying your number is involved in a crime; the "your grandchild was in an accident, wire money now" grandparent scam; pop-ups saying your computer is infected and offering remote repair; romance scammers who chat for months then invite you to "invest" (pig-butchering); and overpayment checks asking you to return the difference. The rules are four lines: don't trust unknown callers, don't share personal info, don't click suspicious links, verify before you transfer. Government agencies will never ask you to transfer money or buy gift cards by phone or text.
- 收益：报案渠道：FTC（reportfraud.ftc.gov）、FBI IC3（ic3.gov）、当地警察局。凡是电话里自称执法人员并要求转账的，都是骗局：真正的 IRS 和社保局只通过纸质信件联系你（美国）。
  Benefit: Report channels: the FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov), FBI IC3 (ic3.gov), and your local police department. Anyone on the phone claiming to be law enforcement and demanding a transfer is a scammer: the real IRS and Social Security Administration contact you by postal mail only (United States).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Federal Trade Commission（反诈骗指引）；FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center
- 备注：礼品卡、加密货币、Zelle 是骗子最爱的三样支付方式，因为基本追不回。
  Note: Gift cards, cryptocurrency, and Zelle are scammers' three favorite payment methods because they are basically untraceable.

### 4. 视频里看见脸、电话里听见声音都不算核实，涉及转账先挂断、用自己通讯录里的旧号码打回去 / Seeing a Face or Hearing a Voice Is Not Verification — Hang Up and Call Back on a Number You Already Have
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。多花两分钟，用自己存的旧号码打回去确认。
  Cost: Free. Spend two extra minutes calling back on a number you already had saved.
- 说人话：AI 换脸和声音克隆已经很便宜了，"老板"在视频里让你打款、"家人"在电话里哭着要钱，都不算核实。标准动作是：挂断，用自己通讯录里存的旧号码打回去，亲口跟本人确认。凡是不让你挂电话、催你"快点，马上"，基本就是骗局。转账一律走官方渠道，不在聊天软件里直接转。
  In plain terms: AI face-swapping and voice cloning are cheap now. Your "boss" asking for a payment on video, your "family member" crying for money on the phone — none of that counts as verification. The standard move is: hang up, call back on the old number in your own contacts, and confirm with the real person directly. Anyone who won't let you hang up and rushes you to "hurry, right now" is almost certainly a scammer. Always transfer through official channels, never directly inside a chat app.
- 收益：换脸和声音克隆诈骗在 FBI IC3 的年度报告中持续增长。挂断回拨是目前唯一可靠的核实手段：对方给的"核实号码"本身也可能是假的（美国）。
  Benefit: Face-swap and voice-clone scams keep growing in the FBI IC3's annual reports. Hanging up and calling back is currently the only reliable verification method: the "verification number" the other party gives you may itself be fake (United States).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center（IC3 年度报告）；Federal Trade Commission
- 备注：和家人提前约定一个只有家里人知道的"暗号"，专治 AI 仿冒。
  Note: Agree on a secret code word with your family in advance that only family members know — it defeats AI impersonation.

### 5. 被指控或被逮捕：保持沉默，要求律师，在没有律师在场时不跟警察谈话 / If You're Accused or Arrested: Stay Silent, Demand a Lawyer, Don't Talk to Police Without One
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：请刑事律师很贵，几千到几万美元；请不起的，法院会指派公设辩护人（public defender），不花钱。被问话时只说一句话："I am exercising my right to remain silent. I want a lawyer."
  Cost: A criminal defense lawyer is expensive — thousands to tens of thousands of dollars; if you can't afford one, the court will appoint a public defender for free. When questioned, say one sentence only: "I am exercising my right to remain silent. I want a lawyer."
- 说人话：被警察拦下、带走或逮捕，第一件事是闭嘴，第二件事是要律师。你有权保持沉默，你说的每句话都可能在法庭上用来对付你。你有权在问话时有律师在场，请不起律师，法院会免费指派。在律师到之前，一个字都不要解释、不要"把事情说清楚"。明确说出"我要保持沉默，我要见律师"，警察就必须停止问话。
  In plain terms: If police stop, detain, or arrest you, the first thing to do is stay quiet and the second is to demand a lawyer. You have the right to remain silent, and anything you say can be used against you in court. You have the right to have a lawyer present during questioning; if you can't afford one, the court will appoint one for free. Before your lawyer arrives, don't explain anything — don't try to "clear things up." Once you clearly state "I want to remain silent, I want a lawyer," police must stop questioning you.
- 收益：美国宪法第五修正案保障不得自证其罪，第六修正案保障获得律师帮助的权利。Miranda v. Arizona（1966）要求警察在羁押讯问前告知四项权利：有权保持沉默；所说的话可被用作法庭证据；有权请律师；请不起律师可获指派。明确主张权利后，警察必须停止讯问（美国）。
  Benefit: The Fifth Amendment protects you from self-incrimination and the Sixth Amendment guarantees the right to counsel. Miranda v. Arizona (1966) requires police to inform you of four rights before custodial interrogation: the right to remain silent; that anything you say can be used as evidence in court; the right to an attorney; and that one will be appointed if you can't afford one. Once you clearly invoke these rights, police must stop questioning (United States).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：U.S. Supreme Court, Miranda v. Arizona, 384 U.S. 436 (1966). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_warning
- 备注：沉默不会让你显得有罪，乱说话才会。这条同样适用于移民执法的问话（具体情形问律师）。
  Note: Silence doesn't make you look guilty — talking without a lawyer does. This applies to immigration enforcement questioning too (ask a lawyer about specifics).

### 6. 出了事别想着"去说清楚"：你说的每句话都可能成为呈堂证供，先找律师 / Don't Try to "Clear Things Up" Yourself — Anything You Say Can Be Used Against You; Get a Lawyer First
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是顶住"去解释一下就没事了"的念头。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is resisting the urge to "just go explain."
- 说人话：在美国，"主动去跟警察说清楚"是最危险的动作之一。警察可以合法地对你撒谎、套话，你说的每句话都会被记下来用在法庭上。正确做法：什么都不说，直接找刑事律师，让律师替你说话。追诉时效按罪行轻重有不同期限，具体问律师，不要自己估算。
  In plain terms: In the US, "going to the police to clear things up yourself" is one of the most dangerous moves you can make. Police are legally allowed to lie to you and trick you into talking, and everything you say will be written down and used against you in court. The right move: say nothing, get a criminal lawyer, and let the lawyer speak for you. Statutes of limitations vary by offense — ask a lawyer, don't estimate them yourself.
- 收益：宪法第五修正案的沉默权就是用来防这一手的。警察没有义务告诉你全部实情，但你有权利一句话不说。找律师不是"心虚的表现"，而是制度给你的标准动作（美国）。
  Benefit: The Fifth Amendment's right to remain silent exists precisely for this. Police have no obligation to tell you the whole truth, but you have the right to say nothing at all. Getting a lawyer isn't a sign of guilt — it's the standard move the system gives you (United States).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：U.S. Supreme Court, Miranda v. Arizona, 384 U.S. 436 (1966). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda_warning
- 备注：追诉时效（statute of limitations）的具体期限因罪名而异，待核实；一律先问律师。
  Note: Statute-of-limitations periods vary by offense — pending verification; always ask a lawyer first.

### 7. 喝了酒就不碰方向盘 / Don't Drive After Drinking — Not Even "Just Moving the Car"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：打车、代驾几十美元。难在被劝酒时顶回去。
  Cost: A taxi or rideshare costs tens of dollars. The hard part is saying no when people push drinks on you.
- 说人话：血液酒精浓度 0.08% 就是酒驾（Vehicle Code §23152）。第一次被抓：48 小时到 6 个月坐牢，390 到 1,000 美元基础罚款（加上各种附加费实际要几千美元），驾照吊销 6 个月，还要上 3 个月的 DUI 学校、装点火联锁装置。"就挪一下车"也算开车。
  In plain terms: A blood alcohol concentration of 0.08% means DUI (Vehicle Code §23152). First offense: 48 hours to 6 months in jail, a base fine of $390–$1,000 (with surcharges the real total runs into the thousands), a 6-month license suspension, 3 months of DUI school, and an ignition interlock device. "Just moving the car" counts as driving.
- 收益：Vehicle Code §23152(b) 规定血液酒精浓度 0.08% 即违法。初犯：48 小时至 6 个月监禁、390 至 1,000 美元罚款（基础罚金，附加费另计）、驾照暂扣 6 个月、3 个月 DUI 教育课程、点火联锁装置（IID）。酒驾出事故还可能面临重罪指控和民事巨额赔偿（加州）。
  Benefit: Vehicle Code §23152(b) makes 0.08% BAC illegal. First offense: 48 hours to 6 months in jail, $390–$1,000 in base fines (surcharges extra), a 6-month license suspension, 3 months of DUI school, and an ignition interlock device (IID). A DUI crash can also bring felony charges and massive civil liability (California).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Vehicle Code §23152
- 备注：服用大麻或药物后开车同样可能被控 DUI，具体条号待核实。出事故见第 1 条。
  Note: Driving under the influence of marijuana or drugs can also bring DUI charges — statute number pending verification. For crashes, see Item 1.
