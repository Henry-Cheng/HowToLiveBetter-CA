# 27. 怀孕和生产：从发现怀孕到出院办证·加州版

这一节前半段算的是孕妇和胎儿的死亡风险。后半段算的是钱和跑腿时间。两种账不放在一起换算。内容只讲「什么时候做什么」。孕期营养、胎教这类没有硬证据、或者作用很小的事，这里不写。本章法律和制度内容按加州及美国联邦现行规定写——加州的产假和工资替代走 SDI / PFL / PDL / CFRA，联邦走 FMLA；健康科学类条目的证据与数字沿用原书。生育津贴、产假和养孩子的钱账见第 18 节（本章配套加州版），孩子出生之后怎么带见第 20 节（原书第 20 节），急症见第 13 节（原书第 13 节）。

The first half of this chapter counts mortality risks for the pregnant person and the fetus; the second half counts money and errand time. The two ledgers don't convert into each other. It covers only "what to do when." Things with no hard evidence or tiny effects — pregnancy nutrition fads, prenatal "education" — aren't here. Legal and institutional content follows current California and U.S. federal rules: in California, leave and wage replacement run through SDI / PFL / PDL / CFRA, and federally through FMLA. Health-science items carry over their evidence and numbers from the original book. For wage replacement, leave, and the money ledger of raising kids, see Chapter 18 (California edition of this book); for caring for the baby after birth, see Chapter 20 (original book, Chapter 20); for emergencies, see Chapter 13 (original book, Chapter 13).

世界卫生组织给的数字：2023 年，全球约有 26 万名女性死于怀孕和生产。其中约 75% 死于这五类原因：产后大出血、感染、妊娠期高血压疾病（怀孕后血压升高的一类病，包括子痫前期和子痫）、分娩时的并发症、不安全的人工流产。世界卫生组织同时说，「大多数孕产妇死亡是可以预防的，因为预防和处理并发症的医疗手段都是已知的」。这一节里作用最大的几条，就是把这几类问题提前拦下来。

WHO's numbers: in 2023, about 260,000 women worldwide died from pregnancy and childbirth. About 75% died from five causes: postpartum hemorrhage, infections, hypertensive disorders of pregnancy (including preeclampsia and eclampsia), childbirth complications, and unsafe abortions. WHO also notes that "most maternal deaths are preventable, because the medical means to prevent and manage complications are known." The highest-impact items in this chapter are the ones that intercept these problems early.

### 1. 准备怀孕就开始每天补 0.4 毫克叶酸，一直吃到孕早期满 3 个月 / Starting before conception, take 0.4 mg folic acid daily through the first 3 months
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：十几美元一瓶。每天吃一粒，难在天天记得住。
  Cost: A bottle costs around ten dollars. One pill a day — the hard part is remembering every day.
- 说人话：怀孕前后每天吃一粒叶酸，孩子出现神经管缺陷的概率大约能降七成。神经管缺陷就是无脑儿、脊柱裂这一类的出生缺陷。已经生过一个这样的孩子的人，再生一个还出问题的概率，大约降到原来的三分之一。美国 CDC 建议所有育龄女性每天补 400 微克（0.4 毫克）；药店的 prenatal vitamin 一般都含这个量。
  In plain terms: Taking folic acid daily around conception cuts the chance of neural tube defects — anencephaly, spina bifida and the like — by about 70%. For someone who already had a child with one, the chance of it happening again drops to about a third. The U.S. CDC recommends 400 mcg (0.4 mg) daily for all people who could become pregnant; drugstore prenatal vitamins typically contain that amount.
- 收益：Cochrane 系统综述把 5 项试验、6708 例分娩合起来一起算，证据质量评为高。怀孕前后每天补叶酸，胎儿神经管缺陷的风险 RR 0.31（95% CI 0.17 至 0.58，可信范围），约降低七成。已经生过神经管缺陷孩子的人，再生一个又出问题的风险 RR 0.34（95% CI 0.18 至 0.64），降到原来的三分之一左右。按人群分组来看，0.4 毫克和更高的剂量效果没有差别。单独吃叶酸和吃复合维生素，效果也没有差别。
  Benefit: A Cochrane systematic review pooled 5 trials and 6,708 births, rating the evidence quality as high. Periconceptional folic acid: neural tube defect risk RR 0.31 (95% CI 0.17–0.58) — about a 70% reduction. For those with a prior affected child, recurrence risk RR 0.34 (95% CI 0.18–0.64) — about a third of the original risk. By subgroup, 0.4 mg worked no differently from higher doses, and folic acid alone worked no differently from multivitamins.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：De-Regil LM, Peña-Rosas JP, Fernández-Gaxiola AC, Rayco-Solon P (2015). Effects and safety of periconceptional oral folate supplementation for preventing birth defects. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (12), CD007950. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007950.pub3>
- 备注：关键是「怀孕前就开始吃」。胎儿的神经管在受孕后 28 天内就长合了。等你测出怀孕再吃，这段时间已经过去大半。所以打算要孩子，就提前 3 个月开始吃。同一篇汇总研究里，叶酸对唇腭裂、先天性心脏病、流产都没有明确效果，别指望它治别的。
  Note: The key is starting BEFORE pregnancy. The fetal neural tube closes within 28 days of conception — by the time a pregnancy test turns positive, most of that window is gone. So start 3 months before trying. In the same review, folic acid showed no clear effect on cleft lip/palate, congenital heart disease, or miscarriage — don't expect it to fix those.

### 2. 确认怀孕后尽快约第一次产检（通常孕 8–12 周），别拖到孕中期 / Book the first prenatal visit soon after confirmation (usually weeks 8–12) — don't wait until the second trimester
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：有保险走保险（ACA 把产检列为预防性服务，多数计划全包）；无保险可申请 Medi-Cal 或 Covered California。要按孕周跑几趟。
  Cost: With insurance it's preventive care under the ACA (most plans cover it fully); without insurance, apply for Medi-Cal or Covered California. Takes several visits across the pregnancy.
- 说人话：加州没有「母子健康手册」和免费产检额度这套制度，对应做法就是：验孕棒两道杠之后尽快约产科医生（OB/GYN）或助产士，第一次产检通常在孕 8–12 周。去得早，建档、风险分级、该做的筛查都排得上。
  In plain terms: California has no maternal-health handbook or free-visit quota system; the counterpart is simply this: soon after the positive test, book an OB/GYN or midwife — the first prenatal visit usually happens at weeks 8–12. Going early gets the chart started, risk stratification done, and due screenings scheduled.
- 收益：CDC 建议所有孕妇在第一次产检时筛查 HIV、梅毒、乙肝（见第 3 条）；产检越早，高危因素（高血压、糖尿病史、多胎等）越早被发现，越早转到接得住的医院和医生。
  Benefit: The CDC recommends all pregnant patients be screened for HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B at the first prenatal visit (see item 3); the earlier care starts, the earlier high-risk factors (hypertension, diabetes history, multiples) are caught and routed to the right hospital and doctor.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：CDC, Screening and Testing for HIV, Viral Hepatitis, STD & Tuberculosis in Pregnancy（clinician timeline: first prenatal visit）. <http://cdc.gov/pregnancy-hiv-std-tb-hepatitis/php/screening/>
- 备注：搬家换州、换保险的，产检记录记得带走。第一次产检通常会抽血、量血压、定预产期，把病史一次说清楚。
  Note: If you move states or switch insurance, take your prenatal records with you. The first visit usually includes bloodwork, blood pressure, dating the pregnancy — get the full history on the table at once.

### 3. 第一次产检就把艾滋病、梅毒、乙肝查掉，查出来也有成熟的母婴阻断方案 / Get HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B screened at the first prenatal visit — positives have proven prevention protocols
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：筛查列为预防性服务，保险覆盖；无保险走 Medi-Cal 或诊所 sliding scale。
  Cost: Screening is preventive care, covered by insurance; without insurance, Medi-Cal or clinic sliding-scale fees apply.
- 说人话：第一次产检就把艾滋病、梅毒、乙肝这三样查掉。真查出来也不是绝路：抗病毒治疗、规范产检、分娩时用药，新生儿打乙肝疫苗加免疫球蛋白——挡住病从母亲传给孩子的那条路，这叫母婴阻断，方案都是成熟的。
  In plain terms: Get HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B screened at the first prenatal visit. A positive isn't a dead end: antiretroviral treatment, managed prenatal care, medication during labor, and for hepatitis B the newborn vaccine plus immune globulin — the protocols for blocking mother-to-child transmission are well established.
- 收益：CDC 建议所有孕妇每次怀孕都在第一次产检时筛查 HIV、梅毒、乙肝（梅毒、HIV、乙肝是 USPSTF A 级推荐，保险必须覆盖）；丙肝建议所有孕妇筛查（B 级）。越早查，阻断和治疗的时间越充裕。
  Benefit: The CDC recommends all pregnant patients be screened for HIV, syphilis, and hepatitis B at the first prenatal visit of each pregnancy (USPSTF grade A for all three — insurers must cover them); hepatitis C screening is recommended for all pregnant patients (grade B). The earlier the screen, the more time for prevention and treatment.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：CDC, STI Screening Recommendations. <https://www.cdc.gov/std/treatment-guidelines/screening-recommendations.htm>；CDC, Screening and Testing for HIV, Viral Hepatitis, STD & Tuberculosis in Pregnancy. <http://cdc.gov/pregnancy-hiv-std-tb-hepatitis/php/screening/>
- 备注：日常的预防和检测见第 1 节（原书第 1 节）。发生高危行为之后的暴露后阻断见第 13 节（原书第 13 节）。
  Note: For routine prevention and testing, see Section 1 (original book, Section 1). For post-exposure prophylaxis after high-risk exposure, see Section 13 (original book, Section 13).

### 4. 整个孕期一支烟一口酒都不要，家里人也别在屋里抽 / No cigarettes, no alcohol for the whole pregnancy — and no smoking indoors by anyone at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，还能省下烟酒钱。难在让同住的人跟着一起戒。
  Cost: No money — it saves cigarette and alcohol money. The hard part is getting everyone at home to quit along with you.
- 说人话：孕期抽烟，孩子早产、生下来太小、唇腭裂的概率都往上走。孕期和生产时异常出血的风险直接翻一倍。婴儿猝死的风险也更高。家里人在屋里抽，一样算。什么时候戒都还来得及。
  In plain terms: Smoking in pregnancy raises the chances of preterm birth, low birth weight, and cleft lip/palate. It doubles the risk of abnormal bleeding during pregnancy and delivery. It raises the risk of sudden infant death. Secondhand smoke at home counts the same. It's never too late to quit.
- 收益：美国疾病控制与预防中心的口径：孕期吸烟会让孩子「足月出生也可能过小」「可能早产」。它会「使孕期和分娩期异常出血的风险翻倍」。它会提高唇裂、腭裂等出生缺陷的风险。它还会「损害胎儿正在发育的肺和大脑，损害可持续到儿童期」。孕期吸烟的母亲生的婴儿，以及出生后接触烟雾的婴儿，婴儿猝死综合征的风险更高。官方建议是「最好在怀孕前就戒烟，但如果已经怀孕，戒了仍然有用」「什么时候戒都不晚」。
  Benefit: Per the CDC: smoking in pregnancy means the baby "may be born too small, even after a full-term pregnancy" and "may be born too early." It "doubles the risk of unusual bleeding during pregnancy and delivery." It raises the risk of birth defects including cleft lip and cleft palate. It "can damage the developing lungs and brain, with damage lasting into childhood." Infants born to mothers who smoked in pregnancy, and infants exposed to smoke after birth, face higher SIDS risk. Official advice: "it's best to quit before pregnancy, but quitting during pregnancy still helps" — "it's never too late to quit."
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Smoking During Pregnancy. <https://www.cdc.gov/tobacco/campaign/tips/diseases/pregnancy.html>
- 备注：别人在屋里抽，孕妇吸到的二手烟对胎儿一样算数。所以同住的家人也别在屋里抽。酒也一样：没有哪个喝酒的量是已知安全的，孕期最省事的做法就是一口不喝。烟酒对成年人自己的账见第 2 节（原书第 2 节）。
  Note: Secondhand smoke counts the same for the fetus, so everyone at home should keep it out of the house. Same for alcohol: no amount is known to be safe — the simplest rule in pregnancy is zero. For what tobacco and alcohol cost adults themselves, see Section 2 (original book, Section 2).

### 5. 有子痫前期高危因素的，孕 12 周后开始每天一片小剂量阿司匹林 / With preeclampsia risk factors, start daily low-dose aspirin after week 12
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：十几美元。每天一片，要一直吃到生。难在天天不忘。
  Cost: Around ten dollars. One pill a day until delivery — the hard part is not forgetting.
- 说人话：子痫前期是孕期的一种高血压病。上一胎得过、怀多胎、有慢性高血压、孕前有糖尿病、有肾病或自身免疫病的人算高危。高危的人从孕 12 周起每天一片小剂量阿司匹林，子痫前期低约一成半，早产和孩子在生产前后死亡都低约两成。其他人别自己吃。美国药店的低剂量阿司匹林就是 81 毫克，不用切。
  In plain terms: Preeclampsia is a hypertensive disorder of pregnancy. High-risk means: preeclampsia in a prior pregnancy, multiples, chronic hypertension, pregestational diabetes, kidney disease, or autoimmune conditions like lupus. High-risk patients starting daily low-dose aspirin after week 12 cut preeclampsia by about 15%, and preterm birth and perinatal death by about 20% each. Others shouldn't self-prescribe. U.S. drugstores sell low-dose aspirin as 81 mg — no splitting needed.
- 收益：美国预防服务工作组给的是 B 级推荐：对子痫前期高危的人，在孕 12 周之后开始吃小剂量阿司匹林（81 毫克/天）来预防。子痫前期是孕期的一种高血压疾病。把多项研究合起来算，结果如下，95% CI 是可信范围。子痫前期 RR 0.85（95% CI 0.75 至 0.95，16 项研究），低约 15%。早产 RR 0.80（95% CI 0.67 至 0.95，13 项研究），低约 20%。围产期死亡 RR 0.79（95% CI 0.66 至 0.96，11 项研究），低约 21%；围产期死亡就是生产前后这段时间里的死亡。小于胎龄儿／宫内生长受限 RR 0.82（95% CI 0.68 至 0.99，16 项研究），低约 18%；说的是胎儿长得比同孕周的偏小。
  Benefit: The U.S. Preventive Services Task Force gives a grade-B recommendation: high-risk patients start low-dose aspirin (81 mg/day) after week 12. Pooled results (95% CI = confidence interval): preeclampsia RR 0.85 (95% CI 0.75–0.95, 16 studies), ~15% lower; preterm birth RR 0.80 (95% CI 0.67–0.95, 13 studies), ~20% lower; perinatal death RR 0.79 (95% CI 0.66–0.96, 11 studies), ~21% lower (deaths around the time of birth); small-for-gestational-age / growth restriction RR 0.82 (95% CI 0.68–0.99, 16 studies), ~18% lower.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Aspirin Use to Prevent Preeclampsia and Related Morbidity and Mortality: Preventive Medication. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/low-dose-aspirin-use-for-the-prevention-of-morbidity-and-mortality-from-preeclampsia-preventive-medication>
- 备注：算高危的有：上一胎得过子痫前期、怀的是双胎或者多胎、有慢性高血压。还有怀孕前就有 1 型或者 2 型糖尿病、有肾病，以及有系统性红斑狼疮、抗磷脂综合征这类自身免疫病。不在这几类里的不要自己吃。这个药要医生开处方，产检的时候把病史说清楚，让医生判断。
  Note: High-risk = prior preeclampsia, twins/multiples, chronic hypertension, pregestational type 1 or 2 diabetes, kidney disease, or autoimmune conditions like lupus or antiphospholipid syndrome. Others shouldn't self-prescribe. It needs a doctor's order — lay out your history at a prenatal visit and let the doctor decide.

### 6. 孕 24 周及以后做一次妊娠期糖尿病筛查，别嫌喝糖水麻烦 / Get screened for gestational diabetes at/after week 24 — don't skip it because the glucose drink is annoying
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：多数保险列为预防性检查；无保险自费费用因机构而异，本条不写数字。要空腹去，抽几次血，占掉半个上午。
  Cost: Most insurers cover it as preventive care; self-pay without insurance varies by facility — no numbers in this item. Go fasting, several blood draws, half a morning gone.
- 说人话：妊娠期糖尿病就是怀孕之后才出现的血糖高。孕 24 周以后花半个上午，喝一次糖水、抽几次血，就能查出来。不查也不管，胎儿长得过大、难产、挨一刀、子痫前期和新生儿低血糖的风险都会往上走。
  In plain terms: Gestational diabetes is high blood sugar that appears during pregnancy. After week 24, half a morning — one glucose drink, a few blood draws — finds it. Unscreened and unmanaged, it raises the risks of an oversized baby, difficult labor, C-section, preeclampsia, and newborn low blood sugar.
- 收益：美国预防服务工作组的 B 级推荐：对没有症状（自己没觉出不舒服）的孕妇，在孕 24 周及以后筛查妊娠期糖尿病。妊娠期糖尿病就是怀孕之后才出现的血糖高。只做一次筛查的，这一次要放在孕 24 周及以后。孕 24 周之前就筛查好不好，工作组的结论是现有证据不足以判断好处和坏处（I 级）。妊娠期糖尿病不管，会推高这几样的风险：巨大儿（胎儿长得过大）、难产、剖宫产、子痫前期和新生儿低血糖。
  Benefit: USPSTF grade-B recommendation: screen asymptomatic pregnant patients for gestational diabetes at or after 24 weeks. Screening only once? Do it at/after week 24. Screening before week 24: insufficient evidence of benefits vs. harms (grade I). Unmanaged gestational diabetes raises the risks of macrosomia (oversized baby), difficult delivery, C-section, preeclampsia, and newborn hypoglycemia.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Gestational Diabetes: Screening. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/gestational-diabetes-screening>
- 备注：查出来的人里，大多数靠调饮食和运动就能把血糖控住，需要打胰岛素的是少数。别因为怕查出来要打针，就跳过这一次。生完之后还要复查血糖：得过妊娠期糖尿病的人，以后得 2 型糖尿病的风险明显更高。
  Note: Most who screen positive control it with diet and exercise; only a minority need insulin. Don't skip it out of fear of needles. Recheck blood sugar after delivery: a history of gestational diabetes markedly raises later type 2 diabetes risk.

### 7. 背下这张「立刻去医院」的清单，孕期和产后一年内都算数 / Memorize this "go to the hospital NOW" list — it counts during pregnancy and for a year postpartum
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。花五分钟记住，最好也让同住的人知道。
  Cost: No money. Five minutes to memorize — make sure everyone at home knows it too.
- 说人话：任何一条出现，当天就去医院，别在家等一晚。头痛不好或越来越重、看东西变样、发烧到 38 ℃。手或脸肿得厉害、喘不上气、胸痛或心跳快。肚子剧痛不缓解、胎动停了或明显变少、阴道出血或流液。一条胳膊或腿又肿又痛、想伤害自己或孩子。产后一年内也算。
  In plain terms: If any one of these appears, go to the hospital that day — don't wait overnight. Headache that won't quit or keeps worsening, vision changes, fever of 38°C (100.4°F). Severe hand or face swelling, trouble breathing, chest pain or racing heart. Severe belly pain that won't ease, stopped or sharply reduced fetal movement, vaginal bleeding or fluid leakage. One arm or leg severely swollen and painful, thoughts of harming yourself or the baby. It all counts for a year after delivery too.

  Benefit: The CDC's urgent maternal warning signs: headache that doesn't get better or gets worse, dizziness or fainting, vision changes, temperature of 38°C/100.4°F or higher; severe hand or face swelling, thoughts of harming yourself or the baby, trouble breathing, chest pain or fast heartbeat; severe nausea and vomiting, severe belly pain that doesn't go away, stopped or reduced fetal movement; vaginal bleeding or fluid leakage during pregnancy, heavy or abnormal vaginal bleeding/discharge postpartum; one arm or leg severely swollen, red, or painful. - 来源：Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Hear Her — Urgent Maternal Warning Signs. <https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html>；World Health Organization. Maternal mortality fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/maternal-mortality>
- 备注：这张清单的用处，是把「再忍忍」和「必须马上走」分开。头痛加上看东西发花，再加上上腹部疼，指向的是子痫前期（孕期的高血压疾病）。一条小腿又肿又痛，指向的是深静脉血栓（见第 13 节）（原书第 13 节）。产后大出血按世界卫生组织的说法，可以在几小时内夺走一个健康女性的生命。任何一条出现就直接去医院，不要先在家等一晚。

One swollen, painful calf points to deep-vein thrombosis (see Section 13) (original book, Section 13). Per WHO, postpartum hemorrhage can kill a healthy woman within hours. If any sign appears, go straight to the hospital — don't wait overnight at home.

### 8. 破水了就地平躺、垫高臀部、打 911，不要走动也不要洗澡 / If your water breaks: lie flat where you are, elevate your hips, call 911 — don't walk around or shower
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。
  Cost: No money.
- 说人话：破水了不是慢慢收拾东西再出发。正确做法是就地躺下、把屁股垫高、打 911。站着走动，羊水流得更快，脐带可能跟着被冲下来。那是能要孩子命的急症。

- 收益：医疗机构的处置原则：发现破水后马上找个地方平躺，防止羊水流出；不要随意走动；立即拨打急救电话。人站着的时候羊水流失更快，胎儿的脐带可能跟着掉出来，这叫脐带脱垂，是危及胎儿的急症。所以要躺下，把臀部垫高，可以取左侧卧位。（处置原则通用；美国急救电话是 911。）
  Benefit: The clinical guidance: on suspected rupture, lie flat right away to keep fluid in; don't walk around; call emergency services immediately. Upright, fluid drains faster and the cord can prolapse — cord prolapse is a fetal emergency. So lie down, hips elevated, left side if comfortable. (The principles are universal; the U.S. emergency number is 911.)

- 来源：北京市卫生健康委员会. 每周急救话题：准妈妈羊水早破怎样办？ <https://wjw.beijing.gov.cn/bmfw_20143/jkzs/jzjj/202001/t20200115_1575632.html>；北京市顺义区妇幼保健院. 孕晚期在家破水怎么办？ <https://www.bch-syfy.cn/Html/News/Articles/9453.html>（处置原则通用；急救电话改为 911）

  Note: Labor's three signs are regular contractions, bloody show, and water breaking. The first two allow calm packing; broken water is the only "lie down and call for transport" one. Showering, squatting on the toilet, or driving yourself after rupture are classic mistakes. Note the time and the fluid's color for the doctor. Yellow-green fluid or meconium particles suggest fetal distress (oxygen deprivation). Add regular contractions, sharply reduced movement, or fever with chills — and stress to the doctor that this is an emergency.

### 9. 想要无痛分娩就直接说，它不增加剖宫产的风险 / If you want an epidural, just ask — it doesn't raise C-section risk
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：美国医院分娩镇痛是常规服务，走保险，自付部分因保险计划而异，本条不写数字。要提前问医院和麻醉科能不能做。难在得自己开口提。
  Cost: Epidurals are routine in U.S. hospitals, billed through insurance; your out-of-pocket share depends on the plan — no numbers in this item. Ask the hospital and anesthesia team in advance whether it's available. The hard part is speaking up yourself.
- 说人话：无痛分娩就是在腰上打一针麻药，把生孩子的疼压下去。它比别的止疼办法更管用，也不会让你更可能挨一刀。「打了无痛腰疼一辈子」同样没有依据。在美国这是麻醉医生的常规操作，不用「争取」，产检时跟产科医生说一声就行。

- 收益：Cochrane 系统综述的结论是：硬膜外镇痛在减轻产痛、提高产妇对镇痛的满意度上，比不用硬膜外的办法更有效。无痛分娩用的就是硬膜外镇痛。同一篇还说，「硬膜外镇痛对剖宫产风险和长期腰痛没有影响」。器械助产（用器械把孩子接出来）增加的现象，在 2005 年以后的研究中已经看不到。

- 来源：Anim-Somuah M, Smyth RMD, Cyna AM, Cuthbert A (2018). Epidural versus non-epidural or no analgesia for pain management in labour. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (5), CD000331. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000331.pub4>

  Note: The two most viral claims — "lifelong back pain" and "it leads to C-section" — have no basis. The real side effects: low blood pressure, fever, leg weakness, trouble urinating, longer first and second stages, and more likely need for oxytocin. Your call — just don't skip it over rumors.   Benefit: Enroll within the special enrollment period and coverage is retroactive to the birth date. Prematurity, jaundice phototherapy, and newborn pneumonia are common — and expensive — reasons for newborn hospitalization; insured vs. uninsured is a huge difference.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：UnitedHealthcare, Newborn insurance coverage. <https://www.uhc.com/news-articles/benefits-and-coverage/how-do-i-get-health-insurance-for-my-new-baby>；Beem, How to Add a Newborn to Health Insurance. <https://trybeem.com/blog/how-to-add-a-newborn-to-health-insurance/>
- 备注：出院前问医院 billing 出生登记和保险对接需要什么材料，别等账单来了再补。
  Note: Before discharge, ask the hospital's billing office what paperwork the birth record and insurance handoff need — don't wait for the bill to arrive.

### 15. 加州没有户口：出生登记由医院完成，父母办好 SSN、出生证明和保险三件事 / No hukou in California: the hospital files the birth record — parents handle the SSN, the birth certificate, and insurance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱（certified copy 工本费除外）。
  Cost: No money (except certified-copy fees).
- 说人话：加州没有户口制度，也没有「一个月内申报」的时限。出生登记由医院直接报给 vital records。父母要办的三件事：SSN（医院代填表，见第 12 条）、出生证明 certified copy（县书记官，见第 12 条）、加上健康保险（见第 14 条）。护照、入学、看病，都从这三件开始。
  In plain terms: California has no hukou system and no "register within a month" deadline. The hospital files the birth record with vital records directly. Parents handle three things: the SSN (hospital files the form — see item 12), certified copies of the birth certificate (county clerk — see item 12), and health insurance (see item 14). Passports, school enrollment, and medical care all start from these three.
- 收益：没有落户卡点，打疫苗、入园、入学凭出生证明和保险办理，不用等某个月龄。
  Benefit: No registration bottleneck — vaccinations, daycare, and school enrollment run on the birth certificate and insurance, with no age deadline to beat.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 备注：非婚生子女同样登记，不影响出生证明和 SSN 办理。
  Note: Children born outside marriage are registered the same way — no effect on the birth certificate or SSN.

### 16. 产后 6 周那次复查别跳过，它同时是产后抑郁的筛查 / Don't skip the 6-week postpartum checkup — it doubles as postpartum-depression screening
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：走保险的常规产后复查。占半天时间。难在刚出月子懒得动。
  Cost: A routine postpartum visit billed through insurance. Takes half a day. The hard part is moving at all in the newborn haze.
- 说人话：这次复查含伤口、子宫恢复、盆底功能检查，还包含一次产后抑郁筛查。产后一年内都可能出大问题，别觉得出了月子就安全了。
  In plain terms: This visit covers wound healing, uterine recovery, and pelvic-floor function — plus a postpartum-depression screening. Serious complications can strike up to a year postpartum; don't assume you're safe past the first month.
- 收益：产后复查看伤口长得怎么样、子宫恢复得怎么样、盆底功能好不好。产后抑郁筛查用专门的问卷，有治疗，可以恢复。产后一年内都可能出现严重并发症。第 7 条那张「立刻去医院」的清单里，「有伤害自己或伤害孩子的念头」「产后阴道出血或异常分泌物」「一侧肢体严重肿痛」都属于产后。
  Benefit: The postpartum visit checks wound healing, uterine involution, and pelvic-floor function. Postpartum depression has a dedicated screening questionnaire, treatments, and recovery. Serious complications can appear up to a year after delivery — the "go now" list in item 7 (thoughts of harming yourself or the baby, abnormal postpartum bleeding/discharge, one severely swollen limb) is postpartum too.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：CDC, Hear Her — Urgent Maternal Warning Signs. <https://www.cdc.gov/hearher/maternal-warning-signs/index.html>（产后一年内预警）
- 备注：产后抑郁不是矫情，靠家里人劝一句「想开点」也解决不了。它有专门的筛查问卷，有治疗，可以恢复。有自杀念头时的处置见第 1 节（原书第 1 节）。避孕方案也在这次谈：产后不来月经，不等于不会怀孕。加州 Medi-Cal 产后覆盖 12 个月（待核实）。
  Note: Postpartum depression isn't melodrama, and "just cheer up" doesn't fix it. It has a dedicated screening questionnaire, treatments, and recovery. For suicidal thoughts, see Section 1 (original book, Section 1). Discuss contraception at this visit too: no postpartum periods doesn't mean no pregnancy. California Medi-Cal postpartum coverage extends to 12 months (to be verified).
