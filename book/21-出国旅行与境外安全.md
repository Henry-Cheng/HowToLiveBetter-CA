# 21. 出国、旅行与境外安全·加州版

本章是原书第 21 节的加州版。法律与制度内容按美国联邦和加州现行规定写：从美国出发去国外旅行，出发前看的是美国国务院（Department of State）的旅行建议（Travel Advisory）；到了国外，管你的是所在国的法律，不是美国的。美国使领馆能帮你到哪一步、不能帮你到哪一步，有明确的界线，本章逐条写清。

This chapter is the California edition of Section 21 of the original book. Legal and institutional content follows current U.S. federal and California rules: when you travel abroad from the United States, you check the U.S. Department of State's Travel Advisories before departure; once abroad, you are subject to the laws of the country you are in, not U.S. law. There is a clear line between what a U.S. embassy or consulate can and cannot do for you, and this chapter spells it out item by item.

### 1. 出发前查一次国务院旅行建议（Travel Advisory），标了第 4 级「Do Not Travel」的地方就别去 / Check the State Department Travel Advisory before you go; don't go where it's Level 4 "Do Not Travel"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=寿命 -->
- 成本：不花钱。在 travel.state.gov 上按国家查一遍，几分钟。
  Cost: Free. Look up your destination country on travel.state.gov; it takes a few minutes.
- 说人话：国务院把每个国家的旅行风险分成四级。1 级：正常注意（Exercise Normal Precautions）；2 级：提高警惕（Exercise Increased Caution）；3 级：重新考虑是否前往（Reconsider Travel）；4 级：不要前往（Do Not Travel）。4 级是最高级，意思是当地有危及生命的风险，美国政府在紧急情况下能提供的帮助非常有限，官方建议美国公民不要去、已经在那里的尽快在安全的情况下离开。
  In plain terms: The State Department rates every country on a four-level scale. Level 1: Exercise Normal Precautions; Level 2: Exercise Increased Caution; Level 3: Reconsider Travel; Level 4: Do Not Travel. Level 4 is the highest: it means life-threatening risks exist and the U.S. government's ability to help in an emergency is very limited. The official advice is not to go, and for U.S. citizens already there to leave as soon as it is safe to do so.
- 收益：分级标准是公开的，同一个国家内部不同地区还可能有不同的级别（比如某几个州单独标 4 级）。名单随局势变，本书不抄名单。出行前、以及行程有变时，自己去 travel.state.gov 查目的国的当期页面，看清是几级、因为什么原因（犯罪、恐怖主义、内乱、绑架等）被标的级。
  Benefit: The rating criteria are public, and different regions within one country can carry different levels (e.g., specific states rated Level 4). The list changes with conditions, so this book does not copy it. Before your trip — and whenever plans change — check the current country page on travel.state.gov yourself to see the level and the reasons behind it (crime, terrorism, civil unrest, kidnapping, etc.).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：U.S. Department of State. Travel Advisory. <https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/destination.html>
- 备注：4 级建议的页面里有一句标准措辞：建议出行前立好遗嘱、留下 DNA 样本，以备最坏的情况。这不是吓唬人，是官方文本。保险起见，去 3 级及以上地区前，把这条也当真。
  Note: Level 4 advisory pages carry a standard line: travelers are advised to write a will and leave DNA samples before going, in case of worst-case scenarios. That's official wording, not scare tactics — take it seriously before traveling to any Level 3+ destination.

### 2. 出发前登记 STEP，再把国务院的两个紧急电话存进手机、抄一份放钱包 / Enroll in STEP before departure, save the State Department's two emergency numbers in your phone, and keep a paper copy in your wallet
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。STEP 登记十几二十分钟，存两个号码几分钟。
  Cost: Free. STEP enrollment takes about 15–20 minutes; saving two numbers takes a few minutes.
- 说人话：STEP（Smart Traveler Enrollment Program，智慧旅行者登记计划）是国务院给美国公民和国民的免费服务，在 step.state.gov 登记你的行程（去哪、住哪、待多久、紧急联系人）。登记后，目的国的美国使领馆会把安全提醒、示威、疫情、自然灾害等警报发到你邮箱；真出了事，使领馆也知道你在那儿、能联系上你或你的紧急联系人。紧急电话有两个：在美国和加拿大境内打 1-888-407-4747，在其他国家打 +1-202-501-4444。
  In plain terms: STEP (Smart Traveler Enrollment Program) is a free service from the State Department for U.S. citizens and nationals. Register your trip at step.state.gov — where you're going, where you're staying, how long, and an emergency contact. Once enrolled, the U.S. embassy or consulate in your destination will email you alerts about security, demonstrations, health threats, and natural disasters; and in a real emergency, the embassy knows you're there and can reach you or your emergency contact. There are two emergency numbers: 1-888-407-4747 from the U.S. and Canada, +1-202-501-4444 from anywhere else.
- 收益：STEP 不代替旅行保险，也不保证撤侨，但它是使领馆在危机中找到你的主要渠道。两个紧急电话接的是国务院的海外公民服务处（Overseas Citizens Services），家人从美国打 1-888-407-4747 也能请他们帮你转消息。手机常和护照一起丢，所以号码再手抄一份放钱包，或留一份给家人。
  Benefit: STEP doesn't replace travel insurance and doesn't guarantee evacuation, but it's the main channel through which an embassy can find you in a crisis. Both emergency numbers reach the State Department's Office of Overseas Citizens Services; family members calling from the U.S. at 1-888-407-4747 can also ask them to relay a message to you. Phones and passports tend to get lost together, so hand-copy the numbers into your wallet or leave a copy with family.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：U.S. Department of State. Smart Traveler Enrollment Program. <https://travel.state.gov/content/travel/en/international-travel/before-you-go/step.html>；U.S. Department of State. Travelers — Emergency Information & Resources: "Please call 1 (888) 407-4747 (U.S. and Canada) or 1 (202) 501-4444 (overseas)". <https://www.state.gov/travelers/>；U.S. Department of State. Emergency Financial Assistance for U.S. Citizens Abroad. <https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/help-abroad/financial.html>
- 备注：STEP 在 2024 年 9 月换了新系统（走 Login.gov 登录），以前登过记的老账户没有自动迁移，要重新登记。只收邮件提醒、不想注册账号的，可以选 guest subscription（访客订阅）。
  Note: STEP moved to a new system in September 2024 (sign-in via Login.gov); old registrations did not carry over, so re-enroll. If you only want email alerts without creating an account, a guest subscription option is available.

### 3. 知道美国领事保护能做什么、不能做什么：能探视被拘押的你，不能把你捞出来，费用还得自己还 / Know what U.S. consular protection can and cannot do: they can visit you in detention, they cannot get you out, and you repay any money they front
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。花几分钟把这条界线读明白。
  Cost: Free. A few minutes to understand where the line is.
- 说人话：你在国外被抓，美国使领馆能做的是：去探视你、联系你的家人、给你当地律师和医生的名单、在你护照丢了时发紧急护照、帮你找当地的救助渠道。它不能做的：把你从监狱里弄出来、替你付罚款和账单、替你打官司当律师、当你的翻译、替你调查犯罪、替你订机票酒店。身无分文回不了国，使领馆可以提供紧急回国贷款（repatriation loan），但那是贷款不是赠款，要还，还清之前你的护照会被限制。
  In plain terms: If you're arrested abroad, a U.S. embassy or consulate can visit you, contact your family, give you lists of local attorneys and doctors, issue an emergency passport if yours is lost, and point you to local relief channels. What it cannot do: get you out of jail, pay your fines or bills, act as your lawyer, serve as your interpreter, investigate crimes for you, or book your flights and hotels. If you're destitute and can't get home, the embassy can offer an emergency repatriation loan — but it's a loan, not a grant; you must repay it, and your passport stays restricted until you do.
- 收益：在国外你受的是当地法律管辖，美国领事官员会要求当地按公正程序对待你，但改变不了当地法院的判决。费用上国务院写得清楚：紧急情况下帮你垫的交通、食宿、医疗等费用，是要还的。所以出国前把钱和保险备好，别把「出事了政府会兜底」当成计划。
  Benefit: Abroad you are subject to local law; U.S. consular officers will ask local authorities to treat you fairly but cannot change a local court's ruling. On money, the State Department is explicit: transportation, lodging, medical and other costs it fronts for you in an emergency must be repaid. So have your money and insurance sorted before you go — don't plan on "the government will cover it."
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：U.S. Department of State. Emergency Financial Assistance for U.S. Citizens Abroad（repatriation loans；"Your passport will be restricted until you pay the loan back"）. <https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/help-abroad/financial.html>；U.S. Department of Justice. Consular Assistance to American Crime Victims（"Consuls cannot get you out of jail… cannot investigate crimes, provide legal advice… or pay legal, medical or other fees"）. <http://justice.gov/sites/default/files/usao-vt/legacy/2015/01/12/Passport%20Safer%20Travel.pdf>
- 备注：政府组织的撤侨（比如包机）也不是免费的，要签还款承诺（promissory note），费用按大约一张到安全地的单程商业机票算。出事前先自己买好能走的机票，永远比等撤侨便宜。
  Note: U.S. government-organized evacuations (e.g., chartered flights) aren't free either — you sign a promissory note, and the cost runs about the price of a one-way commercial ticket to the safe haven. Buying your own way out before things collapse is always cheaper than waiting for an evacuation.

### 4. 买一份含境外医疗和医疗转运的旅行保险，别只买航班延误险 / Buy travel insurance with overseas medical and medical evacuation coverage — not just trip-delay coverage
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：一份短期的旅行保险，几十到几百美元。
  Cost: A short-term travel insurance policy runs from tens to a few hundred dollars.
- 说人话：美国的医保（包括 Medicare）在境外基本不管用，使领馆也不替你垫医药费。真正能拖垮一个家的不是门诊费，是重伤后的住院，以及把人运回国的医疗转运——美国疾控中心的说法是，从偏远地方紧急转运到好医院，自己掏钱可能要花 10 万美元以上。买保险主要看两个额度：境外医疗费用，紧急医疗转运与送返。
  In plain terms: U.S. health insurance (including Medicare) generally doesn't cover you abroad, and the embassy won't front your medical bills. What can actually wreck a family's finances isn't the clinic visit — it's hospitalization after a serious injury, plus medical evacuation to get you home. The CDC's figure: emergency transport from a remote area to a good hospital can cost more than $100,000 out of pocket. When buying insurance, check two limits first: overseas medical expenses, and emergency medical evacuation/repatriation.
- 收益：10 万美元是疾控中心给出的下限说法，不是费用区间。买之前确认三件事：你要去的国家在不在承保范围里；潜水、滑雪、骑摩托这类高风险活动是不是被排除；理赔是保险公司直接和医院结算，还是你先垫付回来再报。去免签的国家也一样要买。
  Benefit: That $100,000 is the CDC's lower-bound illustration, not a price range. Before buying, confirm three things: whether your destination country is covered; whether high-risk activities like diving, skiing, or motorbiking are excluded; and whether the insurer pays the hospital directly or you pay first and claim later. Buy it even for visa-free destinations.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Travelers' Health: Travel Insurance：「Medical evacuation insurance covers emergency transportation from a remote area to a high-quality hospital, which could otherwise cost more than $100,000.」. <https://wwwnc.cdc.gov/travel/page/insurance>；其余为作者经验，无直接文献 / remainder is author experience, no direct source
- 备注：信用卡自带的旅行保险通常只保行程取消和行李，医疗和转运额度很低或没有，出发前看清条款，别把它当医疗险用。
  Note: The travel coverage bundled with credit cards usually only covers trip cancellation and baggage, with little or no medical or evacuation benefit — read the terms before departure and don't treat it as health insurance.

### 5. 「境外高薪招聘」一律当诈骗看：月薪离谱、不问经验、先飞边境城市、替你保管护照，去了人和护照都不在自己手上 / Treat every "high-paying overseas job offer" as a scam: absurd salary, no experience asked, fly to a border city first, they hold your passport — once you go, neither you nor your passport is in your own hands
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是亲手推掉一份看起来很好的工作。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is turning down a job that looks great on paper.
- 说人话：识别特征基本固定：月薪远高于同岗位、不要求学历经验、不签正规合同、让你先飞到边境城市集合、代办签证并替你保管护照。这种活去了，人和护照都不在自己手上，想走也走不了——东南亚一些电诈园区就是这么从各国绑人、骗人的。美国公民也中过招。出发前先查目的国的旅行建议（见第 1 条），3 级、4 级的地方别去。
  In plain terms: The red flags are consistent: pay far above market for the role, no education or experience required, no real contract, told to fly to a border city first, they "handle" your visa and hold your passport. If you go, neither you nor your passport is in your own hands — scam compounds in parts of Southeast Asia recruit and trap people from many countries exactly this way, and U.S. citizens have been victimized too. Check the destination's Travel Advisory before any such trip (see item 1); don't go to Level 3 or 4 areas.
- 收益：真去了，人身自由和护照都不在自己手上，使领馆在当地的行动能力也受限（见第 3 条）。不去，是成本最低的一道防线。
  Benefit: Once you're in, your freedom and your passport are gone, and what the embassy can do locally is limited (see item 3). Not going is the cheapest line of defense there is.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：U.S. Department of State. Travel Advisory（按目的国查询当期级别）. <https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/travel-advisories/destination.html>；其余为作者经验与公开报道归纳，无直接文献 / remainder is author synthesis of experience and public reporting, no direct source
- 备注：原书本条后半段（回国后 6 个月至 3 年不准出境）是中国出入境管理规定的处罚，只管中国公民，加州版不适用。美国这边没有对等的出境限制。
  Note: The second half of this item in the original book (a 6-month to 3-year exit ban after returning) is a penalty under Chinese exit-entry regulations that applies only to Chinese citizens — it does not apply in the California edition, and there is no U.S. equivalent.

### 7. 护照、签证页都拍照存两份（云端 + 家人），丢了先在当地报警，再找使领馆补紧急护照 / Photograph your passport and visa pages and store them in two places (cloud + family); if lost, file a local police report first, then get an emergency passport from the embassy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。出发前拍几张照片、发一份给家人。
  Cost: Free. Take a few photos before departure and send a copy to family.
- 说人话：护照丢了，美国使领馆能给你发紧急护照（emergency passport）让你先回国，前提是你能证明自己是谁。手里有清晰的护照资料页照片、签证页照片，补办就快得多。你还得先在当地报警拿到回执，很多国家的出境检查和保险理赔都要这份材料。手机常和护照一起丢，照片别只存在手机里：云端存一份、发给一位家人一份。
  In plain terms: If your passport is lost, a U.S. embassy or consulate can issue you an emergency passport to get home — but first you have to prove who you are. Clear photos of your passport data page and visa pages make reissuance much faster. You'll also need to file a local police report and get the receipt; many countries' exit checks and insurance claims require it. Phones and passports tend to disappear together, so don't keep the photos only on your phone: store one copy in the cloud and send another to a family member.
- 收益：找使领馆的入口就是第 2 条存好的两个紧急电话。云盘登录要收手机验证码的，手机一丢就可能登不进去，出发前在另一台设备上试登一次，确认不靠这部手机也能打开。
  Benefit: The way to reach the embassy is the two emergency numbers you saved in item 2. If your cloud login needs a texted verification code, losing your phone can lock you out — test the login on another device before departure to confirm you can get in without this phone.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：U.S. Department of State. Travelers（"Report Your Passport Lost or Stolen"）. <https://www.state.gov/travelers/>；作者经验，无直接文献 / author experience, no direct source
- 备注：银行卡、常用药处方也一并拍下来。另外打印一份纸质复印件，和原件分开放。
  Note: Photograph your bank cards and regular prescriptions too. Also print one paper copy and keep it separate from the originals.

### 8. 境外自驾先确认当地认不认美国驾照：只认 AAA 和 AATA 发的国际驾照（IDP），网上卖的多数是废纸 / Before driving abroad, confirm the destination accepts your U.S. license: only IDPs issued by AAA and AATA count — most sold online are worthless
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：IDP 工本费 20 美元，另加两张护照照片的钱。AAA 网点可当场办，邮寄要一到两周。
  Cost: The IDP itself costs $20, plus two passport photos. AAA branches issue it on the spot; mail applications take one to two weeks.
- 说人话：国际驾照（IDP）只是你美国驾照的翻译件，本身不代替驾照，用的时候必须和驾照一起出示，有效期一年。在美国，只有两个机构经国务院授权可以发 IDP：AAA（American Automobile Association）和 AATA（American Automobile Touring Alliance）。加州 DMV 不发，网上那些卖「国际驾照」的公司发的都没有法律效力。能不能用美国驾照开车，看目的地自己的规定：有的直接认美国驾照，有的要 IDP，有的完全不认，没确认清楚就别开。
  In plain terms: An International Driving Permit (IDP) is only a translation of your U.S. driver's license — it doesn't replace it, must be carried with it, and is valid for one year. In the United States, exactly two organizations are authorized by the State Department to issue IDPs: AAA (American Automobile Association) and AATA (American Automobile Touring Alliance). The California DMV doesn't issue them, and "international driver's licenses" sold by random websites have no legal standing. Whether you can drive on a U.S. license depends on the destination's own rules: some accept it directly, some require an IDP, some don't accept it at all — don't drive until you've confirmed.
- 收益：在多数国家，无证驾驶是刑事犯罪或重罚，而且出了事保险大概率拒赔，这才是真正的成本。查法有两个：看国务院目的国页面的交通部分，或者直接让租车公司给你一份书面确认。去加拿大和墨西哥自驾不需要 IDP。
  Benefit: In most countries, driving without a valid license is a criminal offense or a heavy fine — and your insurance will likely deny the claim after a crash, which is the real cost. Two ways to check: read the driving-and-transportation section of the State Department's country page, or get written confirmation from the rental company. No IDP is needed to drive in Canada or Mexico.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：U.S. Department of State（经其授权在美签发 IDP 的仅 AAA 与 AATA；IDP 为驾照翻译件、须与驾照同用、有效期一年、费用 20 美元）. <https://www.us-passport-service-guide.com/international-driving-permit.html>；American Automobile Touring Alliance. <https://www.idp-usa.com/aata-international-driving-permit>
- 备注：IDP 不能提前太久办，有效期从签发日算一年。租车公司的证件要求经常比当地法律更严，订车时一并确认。
  Note: Don't get the IDP too early — it's valid one year from issuance. Rental companies often set stricter document rules than local law, so confirm with them when booking.

### 10. 别替陌生人签签证经济担保：I-134 签了要负伪证责任，I-864 是有法律约束力的长期合同 / Don't sign visa financial sponsorship for strangers: Form I-134 carries perjury liability, and Form I-864 is a legally binding long-term contract
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是当面推掉一次人情。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is saying no to someone's face.
- 说人话：在美国，请人来访、担保移民，落到纸面上有两份表。I-134（Affidavit of Support，用于非移民签证、假释等人道情形）本身不是长期财务合同，但你在上面签字要负伪证责任，填假信息会被追诉。I-864（Affidavit of Support，用于亲属移民等拿绿卡的情形）则是和美国政府签的有法律约束力的合同：被担保人入籍、或攒满 40 个季度（约 10 年）的工作记录之前，你的资助义务一直都在，提供福利的机构可以起诉你追偿。一次人情，换来的是多年的法律义务。
  In plain terms: In the U.S., vouching for someone's visit or immigration comes down to two forms on paper. Form I-134 (Affidavit of Support, used for nonimmigrant visas, parole, and humanitarian cases) is not itself a long-term financial contract — but you sign it under penalty of perjury, and false statements can be prosecuted. Form I-864 (Affidavit of Support, used for family immigration and other green-card cases) is a legally binding contract with the U.S. government: your support obligation lasts until the sponsored person naturalizes or is credited with 40 quarters (about 10 years) of work, and agencies that provide benefits can sue you to recover costs. One favor can buy you years of legal obligation.
- 收益：担保的法律后果落在签字人身上，不是被担保人身上。签之前先弄清对方用的是哪份表、义务有多长；拿不准就找移民律师问，别凭人情签字。
  Benefit: The legal consequences of sponsorship fall on the signer, not the beneficiary. Before signing, find out which form is involved and how long the obligation runs; if unsure, ask an immigration attorney — don't sign as a favor.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：USCIS Form I-134（"signing under penalty of perjury"；I-134 不产生 I-864 那样的长期可执行合同）. <https://ilabacalaw.com/blog/immigration-help/what-is-form-i-134/>；Form I-864 为可执行的政府合同，义务持续至被担保人入籍或满 40 个工作季度. <https://pdfguru.com/forms/i-134-form>
- 备注：这和原书第 8 节「不帮陌生人带东西」是同一类风险：材料以你的名义出具，后果就落在你身上。具体执行细节因个案而异，签字前咨询移民律师。
  Note: This is the same class of risk as "don't carry things for strangers" (Section 8 of the original book): paperwork in your name means consequences on you. Enforcement details vary by case — consult an immigration attorney before signing.

### 11. 出境前把行程、住处和同行人发给一位家人，约好多久联系一次 / Before departure, send your itinerary, lodging, and travel companions to a family member, and agree how often you'll check in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=寿命 -->
- 成本：不花钱。出发前发一条消息，STEP 里也登记一遍（见第 2 条）。
  Cost: Free. Send one message before departure, and register the trip in STEP too (see item 2).
- 说人话：人在境外失联，家里人可以打 1-888-407-4747 找国务院海外公民服务处，请他们联系目的国使领馆帮你转消息、协助寻找。前提是国内有人知道你该在哪、该什么时候联系。把航班号、住址、同行人电话和约好多久联系一次，发给一个家人就够。
  In plain terms: If you go missing abroad, family can call 1-888-407-4747 to reach the State Department's Office of Overseas Citizens Services and ask them to have the destination country's embassy relay a message or help locate you. That only works if someone back home knows where you're supposed to be and when you're supposed to check in. Flight numbers, lodging address, companions' phone numbers, and your check-in frequency — one message to one family member covers it.
- 收益：要去信号差或治安差的地方，再把「多久没消息就报警、找使领馆」这句话说明白。上面这些求助程序，都要先有家人知道你的行程才能启动。
  Benefit: Heading somewhere with bad signal or bad security? Spell out in advance: "if you haven't heard from me in X days, call the police and the embassy." All of these rescue procedures start with someone back home knowing your itinerary.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：U.S. Department of State. Emergency Financial Assistance for U.S. Citizens Abroad（海外公民服务处联系方式）. <https://travel.state.gov/en/international-travel/help-abroad/financial.html>；作者经验，无直接文献 / author experience, no direct source
