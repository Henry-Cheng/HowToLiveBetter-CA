# 24. 看病：怎么少花钱少走弯路 / Seeing a Doctor: How to Spend Less and Avoid Detours

口径：金钱与时间。这一节讲看病本身怎么走：保险怎么选，先去哪儿看，急诊怎么办，没保险怎么办。美国没有"分级诊疗"这套行政体系，但保险计划（HMO/PPO）用自付比例逼你走同样的路：先找家庭医生，急事去 urgent care，真要命才去急诊。
Scope: Money and time. This section covers how to navigate care itself: how to pick insurance, where to go first, what to do in an emergency, and what to do with no insurance. The U.S. has no government-run tiered-care system, but insurance plans (HMO/PPO) steer you down the same path with cost-sharing: start with your primary care doctor, go to urgent care for urgent issues, and save the ER for true emergencies.

### 1. 常见病先在社区看，经基层逐级转诊上去，住院起付线接着算 / Start with Your Primary Care Doctor; HMOs Require a Referral
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难在忍住不直接约专科。
  Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is resisting the urge to book a specialist directly.
- 说人话：HMO 保险要求先找家庭医生（PCP），看专科、做检查要他开转诊单（referral），没有就报销不了。PPO 可以直接约专科，但自付得多。急诊不用转诊。
  In plain terms: HMO plans require you to start with a primary care physician (PCP) — you need their referral to see a specialist or get tests, or insurance won't pay. PPO plans let you book specialists directly but cost you more out of pocket. No referral is needed for the emergency room.
- 收益：HMO（健康维护组织）用"守门人"制度控费：PCP 负责首诊和转诊，跳过他直接看专科，保险可以一分不报。PPO（优选医疗组织）允许直接看网内专科，自付比例更高。选保险时先看自己常看的医生在不在网内（in-network），网外（out-of-network）贵几倍。
  Benefit: HMOs control costs with a "gatekeeper" model: the PCP handles first contact and referrals, and skipping them to see a specialist can mean zero coverage. PPOs allow direct access to in-network specialists with higher cost-sharing. When choosing a plan, first check whether your regular doctors are in-network — out-of-network care costs multiples more.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：各保险计划说明书（以自己计划的 Evidence of Coverage 为准）；常识
- 备注：Covered California（加州医保市场）买的个人保险多是 HMO 或 EPO。换工作、搬家、生孩子是特殊投保期（special enrollment），不用等 open enrollment。
  Note: Individual plans bought on Covered California (the state marketplace) are mostly HMOs or EPOs. Job changes, moves, and childbirth trigger a special enrollment period — you don't have to wait for open enrollment.

### 2. 同一个统筹地区（医保参保地）里，级别越低报销比例越高，差距约 10 个百分点 / Same Illness, Different Door: the ER Costs the Most, Your PCP the Least
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难在忍住不往急诊跑。
  Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is resisting the ER for everything.
- 说人话：同一个病，去急诊最贵，urgent care（紧急护理诊所）次之，家庭医生最便宜。差别主要在 copay（定额自付）：急诊一次几百美元，urgent care 几十，家庭医生十几二十。能等的病别去急诊。
  In plain terms: For the same illness, the ER is the most expensive, urgent care is next, and your primary care doctor is cheapest. The difference is mostly the copay: hundreds of dollars per ER visit, tens at urgent care, ten or twenty at your PCP. Don't take a non-urgent problem to the ER.
- 收益：美国保险用自付金额给你分流：ER copay 常在 100–500 美元，urgent care 30–75 美元，PCP 门诊 15–40 美元（各计划不同）。更重要的是网内网外：在网内（in-network）看病，保险按合同价结算；网外可能全额自付。看病前先在保险公司官网或 App 查医生在不在网内。
  Benefit: U.S. insurance steers you with cost-sharing: ER copays are often $100–500, urgent care $30–75, PCP visits $15–40 (varies by plan). Even more important is in-network vs. out-of-network: in-network care is billed at contracted rates, while out-of-network care can leave you paying the full bill. Check the insurer's website or app to confirm a doctor is in-network before your visit.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：各保险计划说明书；常识
- 备注：copay 金额各计划不同，上面的数字是常见范围；准确数字以自己计划的 Evidence of Coverage 或保险卡背面的电话为准。
  Note: Exact copay amounts vary by plan — the figures above are typical ranges; check your plan's Evidence of Coverage or call the number on the back of your insurance card for the exact numbers.

### 3. 要去大医院，走基层转诊或者医院的转诊中心，别找号贩子 / Need a Specialist? Get a PCP Referral — There Are No Ticket Scalpers Here
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。要多办一道转诊手续，等新病人号可能要几周。
  Cost: Costs nothing. One extra referral step, and a new-patient appointment may take weeks.
- 说人话：美国没有号贩子，专家号也不用抢。HMO 走 PCP 转诊，PPO 直接在保险网内约。医院官网都有"找医生"（find a doctor）和转诊电话。新病人等几周是常态，等不了就去 urgent care。凡是收钱"代抢号"的，都是诈骗。
  In plain terms: There are no ticket scalpers for doctor appointments in the U.S., and you don't need to fight for specialist slots. HMOs go through PCP referrals; PPOs book directly in-network. Hospital websites all have "find a doctor" tools and referral lines. Waiting a few weeks as a new patient is normal — if you can't wait, go to urgent care. Anyone charging to "grab you an appointment" is running a scam.
- 收益：美国实行预约制，专科新病人等 2–6 周很常见，号源不稀缺到需要抢。真有急事：urgent care 当天能看，急诊 24 小时开。付费"代抢号""内部号"专门骗不了解情况的新移民，钱付了号也没有。
  Benefit: The U.S. runs on appointments; waiting 2–6 weeks as a new specialist patient is normal, and slots aren't scarce enough to scalp. For truly urgent issues, urgent care sees you same-day and the ER is open 24/7. Paid "appointment-grabbing" services target newcomers who don't know the system — you pay, and no appointment materializes.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验与常识
- 备注：转诊单（referral）一般有有效期和次数限制，过期重开。
  Note: Referrals usually have an expiration date and a visit limit — get a new one when it lapses.

### 4. 跨省异地就医之前先在本地问一句，必要性原则上要副主任医师以上评估 / Care Out of State: Most California Plans Only Cover Emergencies
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。出发前多问一句。
  Cost: Costs nothing. One extra question before you travel.
- 说人话：大多数加州的保险计划（尤其是 Covered California 买的），出州只保急诊。在外地看门诊、做手术，回来可能一分不报。出发前先查计划的网外条款，长期在外州住的家人要换当地的保险。
  In plain terms: Most California insurance plans (especially those bought on Covered California) cover only emergencies once you leave the state. Routine visits or surgery out of state may come back entirely unpaid. Check your plan's out-of-network terms before traveling; family members living long-term in another state should switch to a local plan.
- 收益：美国医保按州和网络划分：HMO/EPO 的网内医院基本只在加州境内。出州急诊按急诊报（联邦 No Surprises Act 也管），但非急诊的门诊和住院，网外自付极高甚至不报。去外州上学、常住的孩子，优先买学校所在州的保险或学校的 student health plan。
  Benefit: U.S. coverage is divided by state and network: HMO/EPO in-network hospitals are essentially California-only. Out-of-state emergencies are covered as emergencies (the federal No Surprises Act applies), but non-emergency outpatient care and hospital stays out-of-network cost you dearly or aren't covered at all. For kids studying or living in another state, get a plan in the school's state or the school's student health plan.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：各保险计划说明书；常识
- 备注：短期旅行可考虑旅行保险。急诊在全美都按急诊报，这是联邦法律定的。
  Note: Consider travel insurance for short trips. Emergency care is covered as emergency care nationwide — that's federal law.

### 5. 每次看完病，把病历、检查报告和影像自己存一份 / Keep Your Own Copy of Your Medical Records — It's Your Federal Right
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难在每次看完病都记得去要一份。
  Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is remembering to request it after every visit.
- 说人话：拿自己的病历是联邦法律给你的权利。门诊记录、化验单、影像报告都能要，医院要在 30 天内给。现在多数医院有 patient portal（病人端口），化验结果和影像直接在里面看，记得下载存档。
  In plain terms: Getting your own medical records is your right under federal law. Visit notes, lab results, and imaging reports are all requestable, and the hospital must provide them within 30 days. Most hospitals now have a patient portal where results and images appear — download and archive them yourself.
- 收益：HIPAA 隐私规则（45 CFR §164.524）规定：病人有权查看和复制自己的健康信息，医院须在 30 天内提供（只能延长一次 30 天）。换医生、看第二意见、报保险、真起纠纷，靠的都是这一套材料。影像（CT/MRI）要 DICOM 原文件，不只是报告。
  Benefit: The HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR §164.524) gives patients the right to inspect and obtain copies of their health information, which the provider must furnish within 30 calendar days (extendable once by 30 days). Switching doctors, getting a second opinion, filing insurance claims, or pursuing a dispute all depend on having these records. For imaging (CT/MRI), get the original DICOM files, not just the report.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, Individuals' Right under HIPAA to Access their Health Information, 45 CFR §164.524. <https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/privacy/guidance/access/index.html>
- 备注：医院可收合理的工本费。去世亲属的病历，遗产执行人或近亲属按州法可申请。
  Note: Providers may charge a reasonable, cost-based fee. For a deceased relative's records, the executor or next of kin may request them under state law.

### 7. 急重的伤病直奔急诊预检分诊台，别去挂号窗口排队 / In the ER, Go to the Triage Desk — Not the Registration Window
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。进门多走几步找到分诊台。把最要命的那个症状，用一句话说清。
  Cost: Costs nothing. A few extra steps to find the triage desk. State the most dangerous symptom in one sentence.
- 说人话：急诊不按先来后到，按病情轻重排序。进门先找分诊台（triage），护士评级后重的先看。胸痛、喘不上气、意识不清、大出血，直接说。真要命的情况打 911，救护车会提前通知医院准备。
  In plain terms: The ER doesn't run first-come-first-served — it runs by severity. Head for the triage desk at the entrance; the nurse assigns a priority level and the sickest go first. Say it plainly: chest pain, trouble breathing, unconsciousness, heavy bleeding. For truly life-threatening situations call 911 — paramedics notify the hospital en route so it's ready.
- 收益：美国急诊用 ESI 五级分诊：1 级立即抢救，5 级最轻。分诊护士定级，看的是生命体征和症状，不是你觉得有多疼。觉得被定低了，就补充说清最危险的症状。等的过程中病情变化，立刻回分诊台重新评估。
  Benefit: U.S. emergency departments triage on a five-level ESI scale: level 1 gets immediate resuscitation, level 5 is the least urgent. The triage nurse grades you on vital signs and symptoms, not on how much pain you feel. If you think you were under-triaged, spell out the most dangerous symptom. If your condition changes while waiting, go straight back to triage for reassessment.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：急诊医学常识；各医院分诊流程
- 备注：急诊 copay 高（见第 2 条），分诊 4–5 级的轻症等几小时很常见，能等的去 urgent care。
  Note: ER copays are high (see item 2), and level 4–5 minor cases routinely wait hours — if it can wait, go to urgent care.

### 8. 没带钱、没带证件、说不清自己是谁，急诊也必须先救 / No Insurance, No ID, No Money: the ER Must Still Screen and Stabilize You
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。
  Cost: Costs nothing.
- 说人话：联邦法律 EMTALA 规定：急诊室必须先做筛查，查出急症必须先稳定病情，不能先问你有没有保险、付不付得起。所以别因为没钱在家硬扛。这条只管"先救命"那一段，后面的住院账单该多少还是多少。
  In plain terms: The federal law EMTALA requires it: the ER must screen you first, and if it finds an emergency, stabilize you first — it may not ask about insurance or ability to pay first. So don't tough it out at home for lack of money. This covers only the "save your life first" part; the hospital bill afterward is still the hospital bill.
- 收益：EMTALA（42 U.S.C. §1395dd(h)）："医院不得为了询问付费方式或保险状况而拖延筛查和稳定治疗。"付不付得起和能不能进急诊抢救是两回事。但注意：EMTALA 只保筛查和稳定，不保免费住院；账单出来后，没保险的人去找医院的 financial assistance（慈善医疗）谈减免。
  Benefit: EMTALA (42 U.S.C. §1395dd(h)): a hospital "may not delay provision of an appropriate medical screening examination" or stabilizing treatment "in order to inquire about the individual's method of payment or insurance status." Ability to pay has nothing to do with getting emergency care. But note: EMTALA covers screening and stabilization, not a free hospital stay — once the bill arrives, uninsured patients should ask the hospital's financial assistance (charity care) office for a reduction.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：42 U.S.C. §1395dd. <https://www.casemine.com/act/us/64098a2ad37f141103ef6cfa/amp>
- 备注：急诊的 out-of-network 账单另有联邦法律管：No Surprises Act（2022 年 1 月 1 日生效）规定急诊按网内价结算，医院不得向病人收差价（balance billing）。加州 AB 72（适用于 2017 年 7 月 1 日后签发或续签的计划）：在网内医疗机构（医院、门诊手术中心、实验室、影像中心）接受非急诊服务时，如果给你看病的是网外医生，病人只付网内自付额（copay、coinsurance、deductible），医生不得向病人收差价；多收的须在 30 天内退还，逾期按年息 15% 计。急诊此前已有加州法保护（2009 年加州最高法院 Prospect 案），AB 72 不管急诊。来源：加州保险局 AB 72 说明 <https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/110-health/60-resources/upload/AB-72-Fact-Sheet-Consumer_Protection_for_Surprise_Medical_Bills-rev2.pdf>。欠下医疗债：SB 1061（2025 年 1 月 1 日生效）禁止把医疗债报给征信局（Civ. Code §§1785.13(a)(7)、1785.20.6、1785.27），讨债公司也不能拿信用报告施压；故意违规的，相关医疗债本身无效；2025 年 7 月 1 日起，医疗债合同必须包含法定披露语，否则无效。医院有 charity care / financial assistance 义务：AB 1020（2022 年 1 月 1 日生效，HSC §§127400–127446）把慈善医疗/折扣资格提到家庭收入 400% 联邦贫困线；禁止工资扣押（法院命令除外）、禁止卖自住房抵债；分期计划免息，"合理分期"不超过家庭月收入 10%。先找医院谈减免，再谈分期。没保险先看 Medi-Cal（加州白卡）自己够不够格，收入低的去 Covered California 或县卫生局问。
  Note: Out-of-network ER bills are separately governed by federal law: the No Surprises Act (effective January 1, 2022) requires emergency services to be billed at in-network rates — no balance billing the patient. California's AB 72 (applies to plans issued or renewed on or after July 1, 2017): when you receive non-emergency services at an in-network facility (hospital, ambulatory surgery center, lab, imaging center) but the treating doctor is out-of-network, you pay only in-network cost-sharing (copays, coinsurance, deductibles) — the doctor may not balance-bill you, and overpayments must be refunded within 30 days with 15% annual interest thereafter. Emergency care was already protected under prior California law (the 2009 California Supreme Court decision in Prospect); AB 72 does not cover emergencies. Source: California Department of Insurance AB 72 fact sheet <https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/110-health/60-resources/upload/AB-72-Fact-Sheet-Consumer_Protection_for_Surprise_Medical_Bills-rev2.pdf>. On medical debt: SB 1061 (effective January 1, 2025) bars reporting medical debt to credit bureaus (Civ. Code §§1785.13(a)(7), 1785.20.6, 1785.27), so collectors can't use your credit report as leverage; a willful violation voids the underlying medical debt; and starting July 1, 2025, medical-debt contracts must include a statutory disclosure or they are void. Hospitals have charity care / financial assistance obligations: AB 1020 (effective January 1, 2022; HSC §§127400–127446) raised charity/discounted-care eligibility to 400% of the federal poverty level; prohibits wage garnishment (except by court order) and liens on a primary residence; installment plans are interest-free, and a "reasonable payment plan" is capped at 10% of family monthly income. Negotiate a reduction with the hospital first, then a payment plan. Uninsured? Check whether you qualify for Medi-Cal (California Medicaid); if your income is low, ask at Covered California or the county health department.

### 9. 伤残鉴定要等治疗终结之后再做，做早了等级会评低 / Wait Until Treatment Is Done Before the Disability Rating — Early Ratings Come Back Lower
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：工伤走保险，不花自己的钱。要等伤情稳定，通常是伤后几个月。难在伤还没好利索就想赶紧了结，得忍住。
  Cost: Workers' comp covers it — none of your own money. You must wait until the condition stabilizes, usually months after the injury. The hard part is holding off when you just want it over with.
- 说人话：赔多少钱看评级，评级看的是治完之后还剩多少功能障碍。加州工伤要等伤情稳定（maximal medical improvement，MMI）再做永久伤残评定。骨头没长好就去评，评的是当时那个样子，等于自己把等级压低。人身伤害和解也一样：治疗结束前别签字。
  In plain terms: The payout follows the rating, and the rating measures what's left after treatment is done. California workers' comp does the permanent disability rating only after maximal medical improvement (MMI). Get rated while the bone is still healing and you're rated as you look that day — you've just lowered your own grade. Same for personal-injury settlements: don't sign before treatment ends.
- 收益：加州工伤保险在 MMI 之后按 AMA 指南做永久伤残评定；评早了，功能还在恢复，等级直接被压低。人身伤害（车祸、滑倒）索赔同理：律师都会等治疗结束、拿到最终诊断再谈和解，签了和解书就不能再要钱。
  Benefit: California workers' comp rates permanent disability under the AMA Guides only after MMI; rate too early, while function is still recovering, and the grade comes back lower. Personal-injury claims (car crashes, slip-and-falls) work the same way: lawyers wait until treatment ends and the final diagnosis is in before negotiating — once you sign a settlement, you can't ask for more.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：加州工伤保险实务常识；具体评定指南版本待核实
- 备注：治疗期间的病历和影像自己存一份（见第 5 条），缺影像是评级被压低最常见的原因。
  Note: Keep your own copies of records and imaging during treatment (see item 5) — missing imaging is the most common reason ratings come back low.

### 10. 治完之后确实留下功能障碍，去户籍地县级残联申请残疾人证 / Lasting Disability: U.S. Benefits Run Through Two Separate Systems
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：申请不花钱。难在要跑流程、等很长时间。
  Cost: Applying costs nothing. The hard part is the process and the long wait.
- 说人话：美国没有"残疾人证"这个东西。想领残疾相关的待遇，走两套系统：一是 Social Security 的伤残补助（SSDI/SSI），按月发钱，申请很难，要证明干不了任何工作；二是 DMV 的残疾人停车牌（placard），医生填表就能办。短期伤残（怀孕、手术恢复）走加州 EDD 的 State Disability Insurance。
  In plain terms: There is no "disability certificate" in the U.S. Disability-related benefits run through two separate systems: Social Security disability (SSDI/SSI), which pays monthly but is hard to get — you must prove you can't do any work; and the DMV disabled parking placard, which just needs your doctor's form. Short-term disability (pregnancy, surgery recovery) goes through California EDD's State Disability Insurance.
- 收益：SSDI（社安伤残保险）给交过社安税、因病残不能工作的人发月补助；SSI（补充保障收入）给低收入残疾人。两套都要经 SSA 的医学认定，通过率低、耗时长。DMV 残疾人停车牌是另一套：医生证明行动不便就能申请，和领钱无关。短期伤残（通常一年内能恢复的）走加州 EDD 的 SDI，按工资发最多 52 周。
  Benefit: SSDI pays monthly to people who paid Social Security taxes and can't work due to disability; SSI covers low-income people with disabilities. Both require SSA medical determination — low approval rates, long waits. The DMV disabled parking placard is separate: a doctor's certification of mobility impairment gets it, and it has nothing to do with money. Short-term disabilities (usually recoverable within a year) go through California EDD's SDI, paying up to 52 weeks based on wages.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：作者经验与常识；ssa.gov、dmv.ca.gov、edd.ca.gov 官网为准
- 备注：SSDI/SSI 的申请细节和通过率，待核实请以 ssa.gov 为准。这三套（SSDI/SSI、停车牌、SDI）互不替代。
  Note: For SSDI/SSI application details and approval rates, verify at ssa.gov. These three systems (SSDI/SSI, placard, SDI) don't substitute for one another.

### 11. 谢救过你的医生，走感谢信、锦旗和满意度评价，别走红包：准则禁的是财物，不是谢意 / Thank-You Notes, Not Cash: There's No Red-Envelope Culture in U.S. Medicine

- 成本：不花钱。写一封感谢信，或者填一次满意度评价，十几分钟。
  Cost: Costs nothing. Ten-odd minutes for a thank-you letter or a satisfaction survey.
- 说人话：美国看病没有送红包的习惯，也买不到优先。真想谢救命的医生，写一封感谢信、在医院的满意度调查里点名表扬，比什么都管用。贵重礼物很多医院政策不让收，别让对方为难。
  In plain terms: There's no red-envelope custom in U.S. medicine, and you can't buy priority. To thank the doctor who saved your life, write a thank-you letter or name them in the hospital's satisfaction survey — that lands better than anything. Many hospitals' policies bar expensive gifts; don't put them in an awkward spot.
- 收益：美国医疗体系里没有"红包"对应的东西：排号、床位、手术安排都不靠送礼。医生收病人贵重礼物受医院政策和职业伦理约束，真收了反而给医生惹麻烦。满意度评价会计入医院考核，点名表扬是能落到医生头上的实在好处。
  Benefit: There's no red-envelope equivalent in U.S. medicine: appointments, beds, and surgery slots don't run on gifts. Doctors accepting valuable gifts from patients face hospital policies and professional-ethics constraints — accepting can actually cause the doctor trouble. Satisfaction scores feed into hospital evaluations, so a named compliment is a tangible benefit that reaches the doctor.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验与常识

### 12. 种牙之前先问清包干价含哪些项目，优先找承诺参与价格治理的机构，别信低价广告 / Dental Implants: Get an Itemized Treatment Plan Before You Commit
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。看牙前多问两家，当面把价目问清，十几分钟。
  Cost: Costs nothing. Ask two clinics and get the itemized prices in person — ten-odd minutes.
- 说人话：美国种牙医保基本不报，一颗全套下来动辄几千美元。签字前要一份逐项列明的治疗计划：种植体、牙冠、骨粉、上颌窦提升各多少钱。广告上的低价往往只含种植体，加价都在后面。缺一两颗牙不一定非种，牙桥和活动假牙也问问。
  In plain terms: U.S. dental insurance barely covers implants — a full implant routinely runs several thousand dollars. Before signing, get an itemized treatment plan: how much for the implant post, the crown, bone grafting, sinus lift, each. Advertised low prices usually cover only the post; the add-ons come later. Missing one or two teeth doesn't have to mean implants — ask about bridges and dentures too.
- 收益：美国牙科保险年报销上限通常只有 1000–2000 美元，种植牙常被列为不报或只报一部分；Medicare（红蓝卡）不包牙科。价格水分大：同一颗牙不同诊所报价能差一倍。拿两家治疗计划对比，是最实在的省钱办法。
  Benefit: U.S. dental insurance annual maximums are typically only $1,000–2,000, and implants are often excluded or only partly covered; Medicare doesn't cover dental at all. Prices vary wildly — quotes for the same tooth can differ twofold between clinics. Comparing two written treatment plans is the most practical way to save.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验与常识；各牙科保险计划说明书
- 备注：具体价格因地区和诊所差异大，待核实请以两家以上诊所的书面治疗计划为准。牙科旅游（去墨西哥等）便宜但维权难，慎选。
  Note: Prices vary widely by region and clinic — verify against written treatment plans from at least two clinics. Dental tourism (e.g., Mexico) is cheaper but hard to hold accountable — choose carefully.
