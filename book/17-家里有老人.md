# 17. 家里有老人·加州版

口径：金钱与人身自由。家里有老人，多数麻烦不是钱不够，是没提前把话说清楚、把字签好。本章法律与制度内容按加州和美国联邦现行规定写；健康科学类条目（压疮、白内障）的证据和数字沿用原书。最后两条讲长期卧床的压疮和白内障手术，按死亡率算账，不和本节其他条目比较。

*Scope: money and personal freedom. When there are elderly people at home, most trouble comes not from a lack of money but from failing to talk things through and sign the paperwork in advance. The legal and institutional content in this chapter follows current California and U.S. federal rules; the health-science items (pressure ulcers, cataract surgery) keep the original book's evidence and numbers. The last two items are judged by mortality, not compared with the rest of this section.*

### 1. 趁老人清醒，签好医疗和财务两份授权文件 / While your parent is still lucid, sign both a health care and a financial authorization
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：Advance Health Care Directive（预立医疗指示）用加州法院官网的法定表格，免费。Durable Power of Attorney（持久财务授权书）找律师做通常几百美元。难在开口：要当面说到将来他自己管不了自己的那一天。
  Cost: The Advance Health Care Directive uses the statutory form from the California courts' website — free. A Durable Power of Attorney for finances typically costs a few hundred dollars through an attorney. The hard part is starting the conversation: you have to talk face to face about the day they can no longer manage on their own.
- 说人话：趁老人头脑还清楚，让他签两份文件。第一份是 Advance Health Care Directive（预立医疗指示，加州遗嘱认证法典 Probate Code §4701 的法定表格，法院官网可免费下载）：指定一个医疗代理人，并写明要不要抢救、要不要插管这类意愿，医生判定他不能自己做决定时生效。第二份是 Durable Power of Attorney（持久财务授权书）：指定一个人替他管银行账户、付账单、处理房产，"durable" 的意思就是他失能之后这份授权仍然有效。两份都没有，等老人不能做决定了，家人只能去法院申请 conservatorship（成年人监护），耗时耗钱，还容易引发家庭矛盾。
  In plain terms: While your parent is still lucid, have them sign two documents. The first is an Advance Health Care Directive (the statutory form under California Probate Code §4701, free to download from the courts' website): it names a health care agent and records wishes about resuscitation, intubation, and the like, taking effect when a physician determines they can no longer make their own decisions. The second is a Durable Power of Attorney for finances: it names someone to handle bank accounts, pay bills, and manage property — "durable" means it stays valid after incapacity. Without either document, the family must petition the court for a conservatorship once the elder loses capacity — slow, expensive, and a common trigger for family conflict.
- 收益：加州遗嘱认证法典第 4.7 编（§§4600–4806，加州《统一医疗决定法》）规定，有行为能力的成年人可以签 Advance Health Care Directive，指定医疗代理人并给出医疗指示；§4701 给出法定表格，用这份表格不是强制的，但照着填最保险。后立的指示撤销冲突的旧指示（§4698）。财务方面，Durable Power of Attorney 在本人失能后继续有效。两份都没有的，适用 Probate Code §1800 起的 conservatorship 程序，由法院指定监护人。
  Benefit: Division 4.7 of the California Probate Code (§§4600–4806, California's Uniform Health Care Decisions Act) lets a competent adult sign an Advance Health Care Directive naming a health care agent and giving health care instructions; §4701 provides the statutory form — using it isn't mandatory, but filling it out is the safest route. A later directive revokes conflicting earlier ones (§4698). On the financial side, a Durable Power of Attorney remains effective after the principal's incapacity. With neither document in place, the conservatorship process under Probate Code §1800 et seq. applies, and the court appoints a conservator.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Probate Code §§4600–4806, §4701（法定表格）. <https://www.courts.ca.gov/documents/Advanced-HealthCare-Directive-Form_031620.pdf>
- 备注：这份医疗指示不需要公证：要么两名见证人签字，要么公证，二选一。代理人不能是照护机构的工作人员或主治医生（亲属或同事除外）。签完把复印件给代理人、主治医生和家人各留一份。/ Note: The directive does not require notarization: either two witnesses sign or it is notarized — one of the two suffices. The agent may not be staff of the care facility or the supervising physician (except a relative or coworker). Give copies to the agent, the primary physician, and family members.

### 2. 把遗嘱立了；加州房子贵，生前信托（living trust）是通行做法 / Make a will; in California, where homes are expensive, a revocable living trust is the standard approach
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：自书遗嘱（holographic will）自己手写、签名，不花钱。找律师做遗嘱加生前信托，通常 $1,500–$3,000 起。难在开口：要和家里人谈身后事。
  Cost: A holographic will — written entirely in your own hand and signed — costs nothing. Having an attorney draft a will plus a living trust typically starts at $1,500–$3,000. The hard part is starting the conversation about what happens after death.
- 说人话：加州认两种主要遗嘱形式：见证遗嘱（本人签字＋两名见证人，Probate Code §6110）和自书遗嘱（全文手写＋签名，§6111），形式错了整份作废。更重要的是：加州走法院 probate（遗嘱认证）又贵又慢（通常一年以上，费用按遗产总额法定比例收），所以有房一族通行的做法是立 revocable living trust（可撤销生前信托），把房子、银行账户转到信托名下，去世后由继任受托人直接分配，不走法院。签了信托却没把资产转进去等于白签。遗产很小的可用小额遗产宣誓书（small estate affidavit，§13100）或自住房简易程序（§13151）走法院。
  In plain terms: California recognizes two main will forms: an attested will (signed by you plus two witnesses, Probate Code §6110) and a holographic will (entirely handwritten and signed, §6111) — get the formalities wrong and the whole will is void. More importantly: probate in California is expensive and slow (usually a year or more, with statutory fees based on the gross estate), so homeowners standardly set up a revocable living trust, transfer the house and bank accounts into it, and let a successor trustee distribute everything without court involvement. A trust you never fund is a trust that does nothing. Very small estates can use the small estate affidavit (§13100) or the primary-residence petition (§13151) instead.
- 收益：遗嘱形式要求见 Probate Code §§6110–6111；后立的遗嘱撤销冲突的旧遗嘱。生前信托的资产不走 probate。小额程序：§13100 小额遗产宣誓书适用于 2025 年 4 月 1 日之后去世、个人财产总额不超过 $208,850 的情形（需去世满 40 天）；§13151 自住房简易法院程序适用于价值不超过 $750,000 的自住房（AB 2016，2025 年 4 月 1 日起）。
  Benefit: Will formalities are in Probate Code §§6110–6111; a later will revokes conflicting earlier ones. Assets in a living trust skip probate. Small-estate procedures: the §13100 small estate affidavit applies where death occurred on or after April 1, 2025 and personal property totals no more than $208,850 (after a 40-day wait); the §13151 petition covers a primary residence worth up to $750,000 (AB 2016, effective April 1, 2025).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Probate Code §§6110–6111, §13100, §13151. <https://ridleylawoffices.com/small-estate-affidavit-california-2026/>（加州遗产律师 2026 年 9 月更新的限额说明）
- 备注：有来源称 2026 年 4 月 1 日之后去世的限额上调至 $239,700，与上文 $208,850 冲突，待核实。信托签完必须做"注资"（funding）：去 county recorder 把房产 deed 转到信托名下，银行账户改抬头，否则这些资产去世后仍要走 probate。/ Note: One source claims the limit rose to $239,700 for deaths on or after April 1, 2026, conflicting with the $208,850 figure above — to be verified. After signing a trust you must "fund" it: record a new deed transferring the house into the trust at the county recorder and retitle bank accounts, or those assets still go through probate.

### 3. 老人的钱单独放一个账户，大额支出定一个双人确认的规矩 / Keep the elder's money in a separate account and require a second person's sign-off on large spending
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。和家里人商量一次，定下一个数额就行。难在怎么开口，别让老人觉得你在防着他。
  Cost: Free. One family conversation to agree on a threshold amount. The hard part is raising it without making the elder feel distrusted.
- 说人话：骗老人买保健品、买收藏品、买高收益"理财"的，套路都一样：先花几个月和他处熟，再一次性收割。事先约好一条规矩，超过多少钱的支出要先跟某个人说一声。这等于把掏钱那一刻往后拖，拖过最冲动的那一阵。
  In plain terms: Scams targeting elders — supplements, collectibles, "high-return" investments — all run the same playbook: spend months building trust, then take the money in one move. Agree in advance on a rule: any spending above a set amount gets a second person's sign-off first. That pushes the payment past the most impulsive moment.
- 收益：针对老人的保健品、收藏品、理财骗局，套路都是先用几个月把信任攒足，再一次性收割。事先约好"超过多少钱的支出要先和某个人说一声"。这等于把掏钱这个决定往后拖，拖过最冲动的那一阵。
  Benefit: Scams aimed at elders all build trust over months, then collect in one move. Agreeing in advance that "spending above $X needs a second person's okay" delays the payment decision past the most impulsive moment.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验，无直接文献；防骗通则见第 8 节（原书第 8 节）和第 13 节（原书第 13 节）
- 备注：别把这件事讲成"怕你被骗"。讲成"家里的钱统一记账"，老人更容易接受。也别把老人的所有钱都交给子女保管，那会引出另一类纠纷。/ Note: Don't frame it as "we're afraid you'll get scammed." Frame it as "the family keeps one set of books" — elders accept that more easily. And don't hand all of the elder's money to the children to hold; that breeds a different kind of dispute.

### 4. 给老人备好一句随时能说出口的推辞："我要回去和家里人商量" / Give the elder one ready-made line: "I need to check with my family first"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。和老人对一次话，几分钟就行。
  Cost: Free. One conversation with the elder, a few minutes.
- 说人话：推销和诈骗靠的是现场压力，让人当场不好意思拒绝。事先和老人商量好一句话："我要回去和家里人商量。"他随时能说出口，当场有台阶下。这比事后再跟他讲道理管用。
  In plain terms: Sales pitches and scams run on in-person pressure that makes it embarrassing to say no on the spot. Agree on one sentence in advance — "I need to check with my family first" — so the elder always has a graceful exit. That works better than reasoning with them afterward.
- 收益：推销和诈骗都靠现场施压。事先商量好一句"我要回去和家里人商量"，让老人有台阶可下。这比事后讲道理有效。
  Benefit: Pitches and scams both rely on on-the-spot pressure. Having "I need to check with my family first" ready gives the elder a face-saving exit, which beats post-hoc reasoning.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验，无直接文献
- 备注：和第 3 条（老人的钱单独放一个账户）一起用。这句话是给老人留个台阶，让他不用当场硬顶，不是用来防着他的。被推销围住的时候，最难的是当着一屋子人开口拒绝，比判断真假还难。/ Note: Use together with item 3 (separate account for the elder's money). The line gives the elder an exit so they don't have to stand their ground on the spot — it's not about distrusting them. When surrounded by salespeople, the hardest part is saying no in front of a roomful of people, harder than judging true from false.

### 5. 凡是让老人先交钱、又许诺回报的"养老投资"都别碰：那是老人金融诈骗 / Never touch any "senior investment" that asks the elder to pay first and promises returns: that's elder financial exploitation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。把下面这几种名目念给老人听一遍，几分钟。以后遇上就直接拒绝：不去听课，不去参观，不留电话。难在老人要是已经去过几次、和人处熟了，就不容易劝回来。
  Cost: Free. Read the list of schemes below to the elder once, a few minutes. After that, refuse on sight: no seminars, no tours, no phone numbers left behind. The hard part is when the elder has already attended a few times and gotten friendly with the people.
- 说人话：让老人先交钱、又许诺回报的养老名目，都是同一套骗局：养老公寓"床位预订"、养老会员卡、高收益养老理财、免费旅游加储值返利。判断不靠"这家看着正规"，靠"有没有让你先交钱、有没有承诺回报"。老人如果已经被骗，立刻打电话给 county 的 Adult Protective Services（成人保护服务）报告，也可以打全国老人诈骗热线。能拦住的只有掏钱那一步，配合第 3 条的双人确认规矩用。
  In plain terms: Any senior-living pitch that asks the elder to pay first and promises returns is the same scam: "reserved" assisted-living beds, senior membership cards, high-yield senior investments, free tours with stored-value rebates. Judge by "did they ask you to pay first and promise returns," not by "the place looks legitimate." If the elder has already paid, call the county's Adult Protective Services to report it right away, and consider the national elder fraud hotline. The only step you can block is the payment itself — use it together with item 3's second-person rule.
- 收益：美国联邦贸易委员会（FTC）和 FBI 每年都把老人诈骗列为高发类型，话术和原书第 5 条列的五种表现形式一一对应：先交钱、承诺回报、拉人听课旅游。本土资源：各 county 的 Adult Protective Services 受理老人受虐和经济剥削报告；全国老人诈骗热线 1-833-372-8311。
  Benefit: The FTC and FBI consistently rank elder fraud among the most-reported scam types, and the scripts match the five patterns listed in the original item 5: pay first, promised returns, seminar and tour recruitment. Local resources: each county's Adult Protective Services takes reports of elder abuse and financial exploitation; the national elder fraud hotline is 1-833-372-8311.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验；FTC 老人诈骗统计与各县 Adult Protective Services 受理范围（具体热线号码待核实）
- 备注：已经交了钱，除了报告，还要立刻联系银行看能不能止付或争议这笔交易（dispute）。"以房养老"是另一套路径，见第 6 条。/ Note: If money has already been paid, besides reporting, contact the bank immediately about stopping the payment or disputing the transaction. "Aging in place with your home equity" schemes are a separate track — see item 6.

### 6. 只有 FHA 承保的反向抵押贷款（HECM）是正规的，其他"以房养老"都别碰，绝不抵押房子去买理财 / Only the FHA-insured reverse mortgage (HECM) is legitimate; never mortgage the house to buy investments
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。家里的房产证（deed），不交给任何以"以房养老"名义上门的人。抵押、担保、借贷这些协议，签字前先给子女看一遍。难在上门的人往往很热情、跑了很多趟，老人不好意思回绝。
  Cost: Free. Never hand the house deed to anyone pitching "aging in place with your home." Have the children review any mortgage, guarantee, or loan paperwork before signing. The hard part is that the salespeople are warm, persistent, and hard to turn away.
- 说人话：正规的反向抵押贷款只有一种，叫 Home Equity Conversion Mortgage（HECM），由联邦住房管理局（FHA）承保：62 岁以上、拥有住房完全产权的老人，把房子抵押给贷款机构，房子照住，按约定领钱直到去世，而且放款前必须接受 HUD 认可机构的强制咨询。上门推销的那种和它毫无关系，是骗老人抵押房子，再拿借来的钱买它推荐的理财，往往是庞氏骗局。有人连房子被抵押了都不知道，最后房子没了，还背着贷款。
  In plain terms: There is exactly one legitimate reverse mortgage, the Home Equity Conversion Mortgage (HECM), insured by the Federal Housing Administration (FHA): homeowners 62 and older with full equity mortgage the house to a lender, keep living in it, and receive payments for life — and mandatory counseling from a HUD-approved agency is required before closing. Door-to-door pitches have nothing to do with it: they trick elders into mortgaging the house and putting the borrowed money into recommended investments, usually a Ponzi scheme. Some victims didn't even know their house had been mortgaged until they lost it and still owed the loan.
- 收益：HECM 是联邦层面唯一受保的反向抵押贷款产品，借款人须年满 62 岁、强制接受 HUD 认可机构的咨询，房子产权仍归老人。不法分子的"以房养老"与之完全无关，操作是诱骗老人办房产抵押、再把借来的钱买它推荐的理财产品。收益率超过 6% 就要打问号、超过 8% 就很危险、"保本高收益"就是金融诈骗，这条线在美国同样适用。
  Benefit: The HECM is the only federally insured reverse mortgage product; borrowers must be 62 or older and complete mandatory counseling from a HUD-approved agency, and the elder keeps title to the home. Scammers' "home-based retirement" pitches are entirely unrelated: they induce elders to mortgage the house and invest the proceeds in recommended products. The rule of thumb still applies in the U.S.: promised yields above 6% deserve skepticism, above 8% are dangerous, and "guaranteed high returns" is financial fraud.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：HUD（美国住房和城市发展部）HECM 项目说明（具体页面链接待核实）；FHA 承保要求与强制咨询制度
- 备注：给 B 是因为依据的是联邦机构的项目说明和消费者指引，不是随机试验。签字和空白合同的一般规则见第 8 节第 17 条（原书第 8 节），被骗之后怎么止付见第 8 节第 2 条（原书第 8 节）。/ Note: Graded B because it rests on federal program descriptions and consumer guidance, not randomized trials. General rules on signing and blank contracts are in section 8 item 17 (original section 8); what to do after being scammed is in section 8 item 2 (original section 8).

### 7. 家里老人需要长期照护：加州没有全国统一的长期护理保险，但 Medi-Cal 为符合条件的老人支付长期护理费用，住在家里可用 IHSS / When an elder needs long-term care: California has no universal long-term-care insurance, but Medi-Cal pays for qualifying elders' long-term care, and IHSS covers care at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：去 county 社会服务局交一次 Medi-Cal 申请；IHSS 要再交一份医生填的健康证明表（Health Care Certification），并接受社工上门评估。申请本身不花钱。
  Cost: File one Medi-Cal application at the county social services office; IHSS additionally requires a Health Care Certification form completed by the physician and an in-home assessment by a social worker. Applying is free.
- 说人话：长期护理费用（养老院、长期住院护理）贵得吓人，加州没有类似原书第 7 条那种全国统一的长期护理保险，但 Medi-Cal（加州的 Medicaid）为符合收入和资产条件的老人支付长期护理费用。想住在自己家里的，可以申请 IHSS（In-Home Supportive Services，居家支持服务）：政府出钱请人上门，帮忙洗澡、做饭、打扫、陪诊，家人也可以注册当照护者领工资。两件事的共同门槛是先符合 Medi-Cal 资格。
  In plain terms: Long-term care (nursing homes, extended facility care) is terrifyingly expensive. California has no universal long-term-care insurance like the one in the original item 7, but Medi-Cal (California's Medicaid) pays long-term-care costs for elders who meet its income and asset rules. Elders who want to stay home can apply for IHSS (In-Home Supportive Services): the program pays for someone to come help with bathing, cooking, cleaning, and medical appointments — family members can even enroll as paid caregivers. Both routes share one gate: qualifying for Medi-Cal first.
- 收益：2026 年 1 月起，非 MAGI 类 Medi-Cal（覆盖 65 岁以上、残障和长期护理人群）恢复资产审查：个人 $130,000、夫妻 $195,000，每多一名家庭成员加 $65,000（上限 10 人）；自住房、一辆车、日常生活用品不计入。2026–27 年度州预算已通过：这组限额维持到 2027 年 6 月 30 日，自 2027 年 7 月 1 日起降为个人 $21,000、夫妻 $31,000。收入仍是硬门槛。IHSS 要求：加州居民、65 岁以上或残障、有 Medi-Cal 资格、住在自己家里、医生开健康证明、有经评估的照护需求。
  Benefit: Since January 2026, non-MAGI Medi-Cal (covering people 65+, people with disabilities, and long-term-care recipients) reinstated its asset test: $130,000 for an individual, $195,000 for a couple, plus $65,000 per additional household member (up to 10); the primary home, one vehicle, and household goods don't count. IHSS requires: California resident, 65+ or disabled, Medi-Cal eligible, living at home, a physician's Health Care Certification, and assessed care needs.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A

Medi-Cal estate recovery rules still exist — see an estate attorney about protecting the house. IHSS authorizes up to about 283 hours per month, set by the social worker's assessment.

### 8. 家里有人长期卧床，把压疮当头号敌人：上电动气垫床、定时翻身、每天看一遍骨头突出的地方 / For a bedridden elder, treat pressure ulcers as enemy number one: alternating-pressure mattress, scheduled repositioning, daily skin checks over bony areas
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：家用电动交替压力气垫床垫，$100–$200 起。翻一次身只要几分钟。难的是每隔两三小时就得翻一次，夜里也不停，一个人扛不下来。
  Cost: A home alternating-pressure mattress overlay starts around $100–$200. Each repositioning takes only minutes. The hard part is doing it every two to three hours around the clock — more than one person can sustain alone.
- 说人话：髋部骨折开刀前就有压疮的老人，30 天内去世的可能性高约两成。换电动气垫床，新长压疮的少约六成。按时翻身真正做到位，少约七成。别让人长时间趴着。每天看一遍骶尾、足跟、髋部、肩背，发现压了不褪的红，当天找护士或医生。
  In plain terms: Elders who already had pressure ulcers before hip-fracture surgery were about 20% more likely to die within 30 days. Switching to a powered air mattress cut new ulcers by about 60%. Getting repositioning truly done on schedule cut them by about 70%. Don't leave someone lying face-down for long. Check the sacrum/tailbone, heels, hips, and shoulders/back daily; if you find redness that doesn't fade when pressed, call a nurse or doctor that day.
- 收益：19520 例髋部骨折手术患者（美国 NSQIP 2016 至 2019 年）。开刀前就有压疮的人，30 天内死亡的比例更高。把其他因素算进去之后，这个关联仍然成立（OR 1.2，几率增加 21%，P=0.004）。这些人还更容易出别的问题：深静脉血栓（OR 1.59）、肺炎（OR 1.39）、非计划再住院（OR 1.43），平均住院日也多 0.4 天。324 名住院老人（平均 86 岁）随访中位 18 个月。压疮能单独预测长期死亡（HR 1.500，95% CI 1.059 至 2.126，P=0.020）。65 项随机试验放在一起算（网络荟萃分析）。电动主动气流支撑面对比标准病床垫，压疮发生率更低（RR 0.42，95% CI 0.29 至 0.63，中等确定性）。电动混合气流支撑面是 RR 0.22（0.07 至 0.66）。11 项试验 4462 人的 Cochrane 综述（2026 年更新）。2 小时翻一次和 4 小时翻一次比，差别说不清（RR 1.05，95% CI 0.79 至 1.39，确定性极低）。1226 名 ICU 患者的试验里，用可穿戴传感器实时提醒并给出视觉警示，把 2 小时翻一次真正落实下来。压疮发生率低于常规护理（RR 0.28，95% CI 0.10 至 0.75，中等确定性）。116 人的试验中，俯卧位（趴着）那组的压疮发生率高于仰卧位（平躺）那组（RR 4.55，95% CI 2.31 至 8.98）。
  Benefit: 19,520 hip-fracture surgery patients (U.S. NSQIP 2016–2019). Those with preoperative pressure ulcers had higher 30-day mortality, and the association held after adjustment (OR 1.2, 21% higher odds, P=0.004). They also had more complications: deep-vein thrombosis (OR 1.59), pneumonia (OR 1.39), unplanned readmission (OR 1.43), and 0.4 extra hospital days. In 324 hospitalized elders (mean age 86) followed a median 18 months, pressure ulcers independently predicted long-term mortality (HR 1.500, 95% CI 1.059–2.126, P=0.020). A network meta-analysis of 65 randomized trials: powered active air surfaces vs. standard hospital mattresses lowered ulcer incidence (RR 0.42, 95% CI 0.29–0.63, moderate certainty); powered hybrid air surfaces RR 0.22 (0.07–0.66). An 11-trial, 4,462-person Cochrane review (2026 update): 2-hour vs. 4-hour repositioning was inconclusive (RR 1.05, 95% CI 0.79–1.39, very low certainty). In a 1,226-patient ICU trial, wearable sensors with real-time visual prompts that actually achieved 2-hour repositioning beat usual care (RR 0.28, 95% CI 0.10–0.75, moderate certainty). In a 116-person trial, the prone (face-down) group had higher ulcer incidence than the supine group (RR 4.55, 95% CI 2.31–8.98).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Porter SB, Pla R, Chow JH, et al. (2022). Preoperative Pressure Ulcers, Mortality, and Complications in Older Hip Fracture Surgery Patients. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 6, CD009958. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD009958.pub4>
- 备注：给 B 的原因有三条。一是死亡率那两项研究只看到"有压疮的人后来处境更差"，这不等于"防住压疮就能把死亡率降下来"。二是翻身间隔那几项随机试验的结论很不牢靠。三是气垫床那条证据要硬一些，但研究里比的是医院病床的垫子，套到家用床垫上属于往外推。每天查骶尾、足跟、髋部、肩背，看有没有"压下去不褪色的红"，这是护理的常规做法，没有单独的文献。突然一条腿肿起来，按深静脉血栓处理，见第 13 节第 11 条（原书第 13 节）。/ Note: Graded B for three reasons. Daily checks of the sacrum, heels, hips, and back for non-blanching redness are standard nursing practice without standalone literature. A suddenly swollen leg should be treated as possible deep-vein thrombosis — see section 13 item 11 (original section 13).

### 9. 老人的白内障已经影响看路，就去眼科评估做手术；两只眼都有的，第二只别拖太久 / When cataracts affect how the elder sees the road, get an eye evaluation for surgery; if both eyes are affected, don't delay the second one too long
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：先陪老人去眼科查一次，由医生判断该不该做。一只眼做一次手术，多按日间手术做，当天就能回家。两只眼都有白内障的，要分两次做。Medicare 覆盖"医学必需"的白内障手术，人工晶体选基础款通常自付很少，升级款（多焦、散光矫正）要自己加钱，术前问清楚。
  Cost: Accompany the elder to an eye exam first and let the doctor decide. Each eye is a separate outpatient surgery, home the same day. With cataracts in both eyes, two separate procedures are needed. Medicare covers medically necessary cataract surgery; a standard replacement lens usually means little out-of-pocket cost, while premium lenses (multifocal, astigmatism-correcting) cost extra — ask before surgery.
- 说人话：70 岁以上的女性尽快做第一只眼的白内障手术，一年里跌倒的次数少约三成四，骨折的人也少了。但摔过跤的人数没有少。第二只眼做不做，跌倒上没看出明显差别。做了手术的老人，一年内髋部骨折低约一成六。
  In plain terms: Women over 70 who got first-eye cataract surgery promptly had about 34% fewer falls over a year, and fewer fractures. But the number of people who fell at least once didn't drop. For the second eye, no clear difference in falls was seen. Elders who had surgery had about 16% lower odds of hip fracture within a year.
- 收益：一项随机试验找了 306 名 70 岁以上、有白内障的女性。一半在约 4 周内做第一只眼的手术，另一半按常规等 12 个月。一年里，尽快做手术的人跌倒次数少约 34%（跌倒率之比 0.66，95% CI 0.45 到 0.96）。跌过至少一次的人，手术组 49%，等待组 45%，没有少。跌过两次以上的人，手术组 18%，等待组 25%。手术组 4 人骨折（3%），等待组 12 人（8%）。Cochrane 综述收了这项试验，结论是女性第一只眼的手术减少了跌倒次数，第二只眼的手术没有。第二只眼的随机试验找了 239 名 70 岁以上的女性。手术组跌倒率低 32%，但差别可能是碰巧（跌倒率之比 0.68，95% CI 0.39 到 1.19）。美国医保 5% 抽样里，有 1113640 名 65 岁以上的白内障病人（2002 到 2009 年）。做了手术的人，一年内髋部骨折的几率低约 16%（OR 0.84，95% CI 0.81 到 0.87）。绝对差是 0.20 个百分点，约每 1000 人少 2 例。
  Benefit: A randomized trial enrolled 306 women over 70 with cataracts. Half had first-eye surgery within about 4 weeks; the other half waited 12 months per usual care. Over one year, the prompt-surgery group had about 34% fewer falls (rate ratio 0.66, 95% CI 0.45–0.96). The share who fell at least once didn't drop (49% vs. 45%). Those who fell twice or more: 18% vs. 25%. Fractures: 4 (3%) vs. 12 (8%). A Cochrane review including this trial concluded first-eye surgery reduced fall counts in women, second-eye surgery did not. A second-eye trial in 239 women over 70 found 32% fewer falls, possibly chance (rate ratio 0.68, 95% CI 0.39–1.19). In a 5% U.S. Medicare sample of 1,113,640 cataract patients 65+ (2002–2009), those who had surgery had about 16% lower odds of hip fracture within a year (OR 0.84, 95% CI 0.81–0.87) — an absolute difference of 0.20 percentage points, about 2 fewer per 1,000.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Harwood RH, Foss AJ, Osborn F, Gregson RM, Zaman A, Masud T (2005). Falls and health status in elderly women following first eye cataract surgery: a randomised controlled trial. British Journal of Ophthalmology, 89(1), 53-59. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bjo.2004.049478> ; Foss AJ, Harwood RH, Osborn F, Gregson RM, Zaman A, Masud T (2006).
