# 13. 紧急情况：先做什么 / Emergencies: What to Do First

本节讲出了事先做什么、再做什么、什么不要做。急救和野外的条目算的是特定死因和活下来的比例。威胁和骗局的条目算的是钱和人身自由。两类数字不互相换算。条目的顺序按「这事多常见 × 做对做错差多少条命」从高到低排。这些条目首先是给你自己和配偶、直系亲属用的。心脏骤停有 79.2% 发生在家里（中国人群数据），你最可能救的就是家里人。对朋友同事次之。对陌生人也不是一点好处没有，但这一档最低。所以这类条目把保护自己的动作和免责的条款（加州好撒玛利亚人法）写在前面。

This section covers what to do first, what to do next, and what not to do. The first-aid and wilderness items are scored on specific causes of death and survival odds; the threat-and-scam items are scored on money and personal safety. The two kinds of numbers don't convert into each other. Items are ordered by "how common × how many lives the right move saves versus the wrong one," highest first. These items are first for you, your spouse, and your immediate family—79.2% of cardiac arrests happen at home (data from a Chinese population), so the person you're most likely to save is family. Friends and coworkers come next. Strangers aren't zero benefit, but they're the lowest tier. So these items put self-protection and liability protection (California's Good Samaritan law) up front.

本节只收几分钟内要做的动作。认出骗局、被人拿把柄要钱，处置要按天算，不在这一节。反诈的硬规则和七种高发骗局见第 8 节第 3 条（反诈硬规则）。被人拿隐私照片、裸聊录像要钱见第 8 节第 32 条（对方拿把柄来要钱）。钱已经转出去了，立刻打 911，同时联系银行要求冻结，并到 FTC 的 ReportFraud.ftc.gov 报案，见第 8 节第 2 条（止付）。

This section only covers actions for the first few minutes. Spotting scams or being blackmailed plays out over days, not here. Hard anti-fraud rules and the seven most common scams: Section 8, item 3. Someone demanding money with your private photos or a recorded video chat: Section 8, item 32. If money has already been sent, call 911 right away, contact your bank to freeze the transfer, and file a report with the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov—see Section 8, item 2.

本节条目按主题分成下面几块，括号里是条号。
Items in this section are grouped by theme below (item numbers in parentheses).

### 1. 有人倒地没呼吸，立刻用力按压胸口，让旁人打 911 并找 AED / Someone Collapses and Isn't Breathing: Push Hard and Fast on the Chest; Have Someone Call 911 and Find an AED
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。American Red Cross 的心肺复苏课，半天到一天，学一次够用多年。没学过也能做：双手叠起来放在两乳头连线的中点，每秒大约 2 次用力往下压，不要停。
  Cost: Free. An American Red Cross CPR class takes half a day to a day, and one class serves you for years. Even with no training you can do it: stack both hands at the center of the line between the nipples, push hard about twice per second, and don't stop.
- 说人话：你最可能按的是自家人：心脏骤停有 79.2% 发生在家里（中国人群数据）。有人按压，活着出院的比例从 3.9% 升到 16.1%。叫不应、没呼吸，就直接按。位置在两乳头连线的中点，双手叠起来用力压，每秒 2 次，不停。让第二个人打 911、拿 AED，你的手别停。
  In plain terms: The person you're most likely to do CPR on is family: 79.2% of cardiac arrests happen at home (data from a Chinese population). When a bystander does compressions, the share who leave the hospital alive rises from 3.9% to 16.1%. No response, no breathing—start pushing. Hands stacked at the center of the nipple line, push hard twice a second, no pauses. Have a second person call 911 and fetch the AED while your hands keep going.
- 收益：79 项研究、142,740 人合起来算（荟萃分析）：心跳停在医院外面（院外心脏骤停）的人，活着出院的总共只有 7.6%。在场的人做了心肺复苏的，活着出院的比例从 3.9% 升到 16.1%。有人在旁边看见发作的占 53%，真得到在场者按压的只有 32%。中国 BASIC-OHCA 登记了 38,227 例急救接诊病例（中国人群数据）。其中 30,282 例（79.2%）的心脏骤停发生在家中。这批人里，在场者按压率 20.3%，活着出院的 1.2%。顺序是先按压，再让第二个人打电话和拿 AED。机器开机后照着它的语音做。
  Benefit: A meta-analysis of 79 studies with 142,740 people: of those whose hearts stopped outside a hospital (out-of-hospital cardiac arrest), only 7.6% left the hospital alive in total. When a bystander performed CPR, survival to discharge rose from 3.9% to 16.1%. The event was witnessed in 53% of cases, but only 32% actually received bystander compressions. China's BASIC-OHCA registry recorded 38,227 EMS-treated cases (data from a Chinese population). Of these, 30,282 (79.2%) arrested at home; in that group bystander CPR was 20.3% and survival to discharge was 1.2%. The order is: compress first, then have a second person call and fetch the AED. Once the machine is on, follow its voice prompts.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Sasson C 等 (2010). Predictors of survival from out-of-hospital cardiac arrest: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Circulation: Cardiovascular Quality and Outcomes. <https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCOUTCOMES.109.889576> ; Zheng J 等 (2023). Incidence, process of care, and outcomes of out-of-hospital cardiac arrest in China: a prospective study of the BASIC-OHCA registry. The Lancet Public Health. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-2667(23)00173-1> ; California Health & Safety Code §1799.102（加州好撒玛利亚人法）
- 备注：不满 1 岁的孩子手法不同，要吹气，见本节第 44 条（婴儿心肺复苏）。不要先掐人中，不要先找家属，不要先拍视频。也不要因为怕按断肋骨就不敢用力。加州好撒玛利亚人法（California Health & Safety Code §1799.102）保护善意施救的普通路人：只有存在重大过失（gross negligence）或故意不当行为，才承担民事责任。
  Note: Infants under 1 need a different technique with rescue breaths—see item 44 (infant CPR) in this section. Don't pinch the philtrum first, don't go find family first, don't start filming. And don't hold back for fear of breaking ribs. California's Good Samaritan law (Health & Safety Code §1799.102) protects ordinary bystanders who help in good faith: you're only on the hook for gross negligence or willful misconduct.

### 2. 老人摔倒、有人倒地，先蹲下喊他、打 911，别急着把人扶起来；陌生人这档走开也合法，停下来就别上手搬 / An Older Adult Falls: Squat Down, Call to Them, Call 911—Don't Rush to Lift; With a Stranger, Walking Away Is Legal, but If You Stop, Don't Move Them by Hand
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。蹲下去问一句、打个电话，几分钟。难的是忍住伸手去扶的那一下。对陌生人还要多算一笔：打 911 会留下你的号码，可能被回电，也可能被问一次话。
  Cost: Free. Squat down, ask a question, make a call—a few minutes. The hard part is stopping yourself from grabbing them up. With a stranger there's one more cost to factor in: calling 911 leaves your number, and you may get a callback or be asked a few questions.
- 说人话：这条的主线是自家老人和身边熟人。先看有没有呼吸，没有就按第 1 条（用力按压胸口）压胸。有呼吸也别抱起来：人可能是先中风、先晕厥才摔的，硬扶会加重。蹲下叫他，问他记不记得自己怎么倒的。腰背疼、像骨折的一律不搬，原地打 911。
  In plain terms: This one is mainly for your own older family members and people you know. First check for breathing—if none, do chest compressions per item 1. Even with breathing, don't scoop them up: they may have fallen because of a stroke or a faint, and rough lifting makes it worse. Squat down, call to them, ask if they remember how they fell. Back pain or anything that looks like a fracture: don't move them at all; call 911 from right there.
- 收益：处置原则按中国卫生部《老年人跌倒干预技术指南》：跌倒是 65 岁以上老年人伤害死亡的首位原因；「发现老年人跌倒，不要急于扶起，要分情况进行处理」。意识不清的，立即拨打急救电话。有呕吐的，把头偏向一侧并清理口鼻。呼吸心跳停止的，立即做胸外按压。「如需搬动，应保证平稳，尽量平卧」。意识清楚的，先问对跌倒过程有没有记忆，「如不能记起，可能为晕厥或脑血管意外」。有剧烈头痛、口角歪斜、言语不利、手脚无力的，「立即扶起老年人可能加重脑出血或脑缺血，使病情加重」。有骨折迹象、腰背疼痛、大小便失禁的，「如无相关专业知识，不要随便搬动」。法律上，加州和美国大多数州一样，普通路人对陌生人没有法律上的施救义务（no duty to rescue）。加州好撒玛利亚人法（Health & Safety Code §1799.102）保护善意施救的路人，只有重大过失或故意不当行为才担责。医学上的红线只有一条：**别上手搬**——硬扶可能加重脑出血或脊柱损伤。
  Benefit: The handling principles follow China's Ministry of Health falls guidelines for older adults: falls are the leading cause of injury death in adults 65 and older; "don't rush to lift a fallen older adult—handle it by situation." Unconscious: call emergency services immediately. Vomiting: turn the head to the side and clear the mouth. No breathing or pulse: start chest compressions at once. "If moving is needed, keep it steady and as flat as possible." If conscious, first ask whether they remember the fall—"if they can't, consider syncope or a cerebrovascular event." Severe headache, face droop, slurred speech, or limb weakness: "lifting immediately may worsen brain bleeding or ischemia." Signs of fracture, back pain, or incontinence: "don't move them without professional knowledge." Legally, California—like most US states—imposes no general legal duty on an ordinary bystander to rescue a stranger (no duty to rescue). California's Good Samaritan law (Health & Safety Code §1799.102) protects good-faith bystanders; liability only attaches for gross negligence or willful misconduct. The medical red line is a single one: **don't lift them with your hands**—rough lifting can worsen brain bleeding or spinal injury.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：卫生部 (2011). 老年人跌倒干预技术指南（「老年人跌倒后的处理」）. 全文 PDF 见广东省疾病预防控制中心 <http://cdcp.gd.gov.cn/gcdcdfiles/editor/uploadfile/20111027094832117.pdf>；California Health & Safety Code §1799.102（加州好撒玛利亚人法）
- 备注：打完 911 别挂电话，按调度员说的做。这套判断首先是给自家老人和熟人用的。防跌倒本身见第 1 节第 13 条（练平衡和腿部力量）。老人磕过头之后的迟发症状见本节第 10 条。
  Note: After calling 911, stay on the line and do what the dispatcher says. This decision framework is first and foremost for your own older family members and people you know. For fall prevention itself, see Section 1, item 13 (balance and leg-strength training). For delayed symptoms after a head bump in an older adult, see item 10 in this section.

### 3. 突然嘴歪、一侧胳膊没劲、说话说不清，立刻打 911，不要等、不要自己开车去 / Sudden Face Droop, One-Sided Weakness, Slurred Speech: Call 911 Now—Don't Wait, Don't Drive Yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。三个动作，看一遍就会：让他笑一下、双手平举、说一句完整的话。
  Cost: Free. Three moves, one look and you've got them: have them smile, hold both arms out flat, say one complete sentence.
- 说人话：嘴歪、一侧胳膊抬不起、说话说不清，立刻打 911。发病 3 小时内打药通开血管，恢复到生活基本能自理的可能性大约是不打药的 1.75 倍。拖到 3 到 4.5 小时只剩 1.26 倍。超过 4.5 小时就看不出差别了。记下症状开始的准确时间。别喂水喂药，别自己开车。
  In plain terms: Face droop, one arm that won't lift, slurred speech—call 911 now. Clot-busting drugs given within 3 hours of onset make functional independence about 1.75 times as likely as no drug. At 3 to 4.5 hours it's down to 1.26 times. Past 4.5 hours there's no visible difference. Write down the exact time symptoms started. Don't give water or pills, don't drive yourself.
- 收益：把 16 项静脉溶栓随机试验、6756 名患者的个体数据合起来算（荟萃分析）。静脉溶栓就是打药把堵住的血管通开。发病 3 小时内用阿替普酶溶栓，恢复到生活基本能自理（改良 Rankin 评分 0–1）的比值比 1.75，95% CI 1.35–2.27（可信范围）。这个结果出现的可能性约为不溶栓的 1.75 倍。拖到 3 到 4.5 小时是 1.26（1.05–1.51）。超过 4.5 小时是 1.15（0.95–1.40），统计上已经看不出差别。
  Benefit: A meta-analysis pooling individual data from 16 randomized trials of intravenous thrombolysis, 6,756 patients. Thrombolysis means drugging the blocked vessel open. Alteplase within 3 hours of onset gave an odds ratio of 1.75 for functional independence (modified Rankin score 0–1), 95% CI 1.35–2.27—about 1.75 times as likely as no thrombolysis. At 3 to 4.5 hours it's 1.26 (1.05–1.51). Past 4.5 hours it's 1.15 (0.95–1.40), statistically indistinguishable from nothing.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Emberson J, Lees KR, Lyden P, et al. (2014). Effect of treatment delay, age, and stroke severity on the effects of intravenous thrombolysis with alteplase for acute ischaemic stroke: a meta-analysis of individual patient data from randomised trials. Lancet, 384(9958), 1929-1935. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(14)60584-5>
- 备注：溶栓越早越有效，超过 4.5 小时就基本没用了。打 911 而不是自己开车，是因为救护车会直接送到有卒中中心（stroke center）的医院，还会提前通知对方准备。记下症状开始的准确时间，医生要用它决定能不能溶栓。别喂水喂药，别等「休息一下看看」。
  Note: The earlier the clot-buster, the better; past 4.5 hours it's basically useless. Call 911 instead of driving yourself because the ambulance goes straight to a hospital with a stroke center and radios ahead so they're ready. Write down the exact onset time—the doctor needs it to decide whether thrombolysis is an option. Don't give water or pills, and don't "rest and see."

### 4. 突然天旋地转站不稳、看东西成双、一只眼发黑，或者手指点不到自己鼻尖，也按卒中打 911 / Sudden Spinning Dizziness, Double Vision, One Eye Going Dark, or Missing Your Own Nose: Treat It as Stroke, Call 911
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。在「脸歪、抬手、说话」这三个动作（第 3 条，突然嘴歪、说话说不清）之外再加两个。一个是让他站直闭眼，看晃不晃，再问他看东西有没有重影、有没有缺一块。另一个是自己怀疑时伸手指点鼻尖，闭上眼再点一次。
  Cost: Free. Two more checks on top of the "face, arms, speech" trio (item 3). One: have them stand straight with eyes closed and see if they sway, then ask about double vision or a missing patch of sight. The other: if you suspect it in yourself, touch your nose with a finger, then do it again with eyes closed.
- 说人话：只看「脸歪、抬手、说话」三条，一成多的卒中会被漏掉，因为有的卒中一开始既不歪嘴也不瘫手。把站不稳、看东西重影或缺一块也算进去，漏掉的降到 4.4%。突然天旋地转站不住、看东西成双、手指点不到自己鼻尖，一样打 911，一样记下时间。
  In plain terms: "Face, arms, speech" alone misses more than one in ten strokes, because some strokes start with neither a drooping mouth nor a limp hand. Adding unsteadiness and double or patchy vision drops the miss rate to 4.4%. Sudden spinning dizziness you can't stand through, double vision, a finger that can't find your nose—call 911 just the same, and note the time just the same.
- 收益：美国肯塔基大学卒中中心 2014 年回顾了 736 例急性缺血性卒中（血管堵住引起的中风）的病历。其中 14.1% 的病人发病时完全没有脸歪、胳膊无力、说话不清这三个症状（FAST）。把「走路不稳、腿没劲、视觉症状」也算进筛查，漏掉的比例从 14.1% 降到 4.4%。
  Benefit: The University of Kentucky stroke center reviewed 736 acute ischemic stroke charts in 2014. Of those patients, 14.1% had none of the three FAST symptoms—face droop, arm weakness, speech trouble—at onset. Adding "unsteady gait, leg weakness, visual symptoms" to the screen cut the miss rate from 14.1% to 4.4%.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Aroor S, Singh R, Goldstein LB (2017). BE-FAST (Balance, Eyes, Face, Arm, Speech, Time): Reducing the Proportion of Strokes Missed Using the FAST Mnemonic. Stroke, 48(2), 479-481. <https://doi.org/10.1161/STROKEAHA.116.015169>

### 6. 一只眼又胀又痛、发红，看灯有一圈彩虹，还头痛恶心想吐，当天去眼科急诊 / One Eye Swollen, Painful, and Red, Halos Around Lights, Plus Headache and Nausea: Same-Day Eye Emergency

### 8. 突然像被撕开一样的剧痛，从胸口往后背、往腰一路挪，打 911 并说清「疼在移动」/ Sudden Tearing Pain Ripping from Chest to Back to Lower Back: Call 911 and Say the Pain Is Moving

### 9. 突然一记「这辈子最疼的头痛」，一小时内就到最疼，立刻去急诊做头颅 CT / A Sudden "Worst Headache of My Life" That Peaks Within an Hour: Go to the ER for a Head CT Now

### 12. 大出血先用手死死压住伤口，四肢压不住就上止血带，同时打 911 / Severe Bleeding: Press Hard on the Wound First; Tourniquet a Limb That Won't Stop; Call 911 at the Same Time

- 说人话：皮肤烫红、说话含糊、意识混乱是热射病。从头晕到昏迷可以只有几十分钟，病死率极高。立刻挪到阴凉通风处、脱掉衣服、用凉水和湿毛巾降温，同时打 911。意识不清的一口水都不要喂，会呛。
  In plain terms: Hot red skin, slurred speech, confusion—that's heatstroke. Dizziness to coma can take just tens of minutes, and it kills at a terrifying rate. Move to shade with airflow at once, strip clothes, cool with cool water and wet towels, and call 911 at the same time. Not a drop of water for someone unconscious—they'll aspirate.
- 收益：美国国家气象局：热射病是「严重的医疗急症」。它的表现是体温高于 103°F（39.4°C）、意识混乱、说话含糊、皮肤发烫发红。处理是立刻叫急救、移到凉处、用凉水和湿布降温、「不要给液体」。热衰竭的表现是大汗、乏力、皮肤湿冷、脉快而弱，处理是移到凉处、松开衣服、小口喝水；呕吐、恶化或者超过 1 小时不缓解就就医。荒野医学会 2024 年更新的热病指南说，冷水浸泡是治重症中暑最重要的办法，降温最快。没有冰水就用手边最凉的水，泡不了就喷水加扇风，速度大约是浸泡的一半。
  Benefit: The US National Weather Service: heatstroke is "a serious medical emergency"—body temperature above 103°F (39.4°C), confusion, slurred speech, hot red skin. Treatment: call emergency services at once, move to somewhere cool, cool with cool water and wet cloths, "do not give fluids." Heat exhaustion looks like heavy sweating, weakness, cool clammy skin, fast weak pulse—move to cool air, loosen clothes, sip water; vomiting, worsening, or no improvement after an hour means medical care. The Wilderness Medical Society's 2024 heat-illness guideline update: cold-water immersion is the most important treatment for severe heat illness and cools fastest. No ice water? Use the coolest water at hand; if immersion isn't possible, spray water plus fanning runs about half as fast.
