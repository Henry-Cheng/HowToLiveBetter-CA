# 9. 普通人容易踩的法律红线 / Legal Red Lines Ordinary People Cross Too Easily

口径：这一节是第 8 节的镜像版——第 8 节讲"怎么保护自己"，这一节讲"别主动触犯法律"。每条都写明：做了是什么罪、要坐多久的牢、罚多少钱。法条编号都和官方原文核对过。
Scope: This section mirrors Chapter 8 — where Chapter 8 is about "protecting yourself," this one is about "don't break the law yourself." Each item states: what crime it is, how much jail time, and how much in fines. Every statute number cited has been checked against the official text.

**网上发帖和转发**：不转不知真假的灾情消息（第 1 条），谎报紧急情况是犯罪；侮辱英烈（第 2 条，略去）、境外网站（第 3 条，略去）为中国大陆专属条目；色情内容的两条重罪红线（第 4 条）。
**Posting and forwarding online**: don't forward unverified disaster alerts (Item 1) — false emergency reports are a crime; Items 2 (insulting national heroes) and 3 (foreign websites) are China-specific and omitted; two felony red lines for pornographic content (Item 4).

**钱的事**："兼职"要你用自己的卡收钱转账，是洗钱（第 5 条）；帮人"包装材料"贷款，是联邦贷款欺诈（第 6 条）；捡到银行卡不取钱（第 7 条）；优惠用真实身份领，不批量注册小号（第 8 条）。
**Money matters**: a "gig" asking you to receive and move money is money laundering (Item 5); "dressing up" loan applications is federal loan fraud (Item 6); found a bank card — don't use it (Item 7); claim deals with your real identity, don't mass-register fake accounts (Item 8).

**身体和人身**：不满 18 岁不能发生关系（第 18 条）；动手前先算清账（第 19 条）；要债不扣人、不关人（第 15 条）。
**Bodily and personal lines**: under 18 means no sex (Item 18); count the cost before hitting someone (Item 19); never detain anyone over a debt (Item 15).

**其余按场景**：不从阳台往外扔东西（第 9 条）；不买来路不明的仿真枪（第 10 条）；无人机先在 FAA 注册（第 11 条）；不偷拍、不装针孔摄像头（第 12 条）；打牌不抽头、不当庄（第 13 条）；不买、不卖、不养受保护的野生动物（第 14 条）；身份证、社保号不借人（第 16 条）；被警察拦下配合但别放弃权利（第 17 条）；孩子养不了走安全弃婴或合法收养，收钱把孩子给人是重罪（第 20 条）；不伪造事故骗理赔（第 21 条）；不买卖器官（第 22 条）；别嫖娼（第 23 条）；盗版和汉化搬运的边界（第 24 条）；同人二创卖钱先拿许可（第 25 条）；防身用品辣椒喷雾和电击器有条件合法，警棍类违法（第 26 条）。
**The rest by scenario**: don't throw things from balconies (Item 9); skip sketchy replica guns (Item 10); register your drone with the FAA first (Item 11); no voyeurism or hidden cameras (Item 12); no cut, no bank, no hosted-for-profit card games (Item 13); don't buy, sell, or keep protected wildlife (Item 14); never lend your ID or SSN (Item 16); comply when stopped by police but don't waive your rights (Item 17); can't raise the child — use safe surrender or legal adoption; taking money for a child is a serious felony (Item 20); don't fake accidents for insurance payouts (Item 21); don't trade organs (Item 22); don't solicit prostitution (Item 23); the piracy and fan-translation line (Item 24); get permission before selling fan works (Item 25); pepper spray and stun guns are legal with limits, billy clubs are illegal (Item 26).

### 1. 群里不转发不知真假的灾情、疫情、警情消息；谎报紧急情况是犯罪 / Don't Forward Unverified Disaster Alerts; False Emergency Reports Are a Crime
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。转发前看一眼是不是官方通报，拿不准就不转。
  Cost: Free. Before forwarding, check whether it's an official alert; if you're not sure, don't forward it.
- 说人话：美国没有"散布谣言罪"，转发灾情消息本身一般不违法。但两条红线别碰：一是谎报警情、灾情引来应急响应，是犯罪（PC §148.3），最低 120 天、最高 1 年监禁，造成死伤的升重罪；二是编造炸弹威胁（PC §148.1），wobbler，可按轻罪或重罪起诉。"我只是转了一下"在谎报警情上不免责。
  In plain terms: The US has no "spreading rumors" crime, and forwarding disaster news is generally not illegal. But two red lines stay untouched: first, falsely reporting emergencies or disasters to trigger an emergency response is a crime (PC §148.3) — 120 days minimum up to 1 year in jail, elevated to a felony if it causes death or injury; second, fabricating bomb threats (PC §148.1), a wobbler chargeable as a misdemeanor or felony. "I was just forwarding" is no defense for false emergency reports.
- 收益：PC §148.3：明知虚假仍谎报紧急情况，轻罪最低 120 天、最高 1 年监禁、1,000 美元罚款；导致死亡或重伤的为重罪（按 §1170(h) 监禁、最高 $10,000 罚款）。PC §148.1：虚假炸弹报告，wobbler（轻罪最高 1 年，或重罪 16 个月/2 年/3 年），另可能被判赔应急响应费用。第一修正案保护一般性言论，但虚假报警不在保护范围内（加州）。
  Benefit: PC §148.3: knowingly making a false emergency report — a misdemeanor with 120 days minimum up to 1 year in jail and a $1,000 fine; a felony if it causes death or serious injury (imprisonment per §1170(h), up to $10,000 fine). PC §148.1: false bomb reports — a wobbler (misdemeanor up to 1 year, or felony 16 months/2 years/3 years), plus possible restitution for emergency response costs. The First Amendment protects general speech, but false emergency reports are outside that protection (California).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Penal Code §§148.1, 148.3. https://www.shouselaw.com/ca/defense/penal-code/148-3/
- 备注：灾情信息认准官方渠道（NWS、县警局、Cal OES）。
  Note: For disaster information, rely on official channels (NWS, county sheriff, Cal OES).

### 4. 色情内容：成年人自愿观看不违法，但儿童色情是联邦重罪，报复性色情传播违法 / Pornography: Legal for Consenting Adults, but Child Porn Is a Federal Felony and Revenge Porn Is a Crime
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。不转发、不建群、不卖"资源"。
  Cost: Free. Don't forward, don't form groups, don't sell "collections."
- 说人话：成年人之间自愿看、传色情内容，在美国一般不违法。但两条是重罪红线：一是任何涉及未成年人的色情内容，联邦重罪（18 USC §2252）：接收、传播、运输是强制最低 5 年、最高 20 年；单纯持有最高 10 年（受害人未满 12 岁最高 20 年）。二是未经同意传播他人私密影像（PC §647(j)(4)），轻罪。工作场合发黄色内容还可能构成性骚扰。
  In plain terms: Consensual adult pornography is generally not illegal in the US. But two red lines are felonies: first, any sexual content involving minors is a federal felony (18 USC §2252) — receiving, distributing, or transporting carries a mandatory minimum of 5 years up to 20 years; mere possession up to 10 years (20 if the victim is under 12); second, distributing someone's intimate images without consent (PC §647(j)(4)) is a misdemeanor. Sending sexual content at work can also constitute sexual harassment.
- 收益：18 U.S.C. §2252：涉未成年人色情内容，接收/传播/运输强制最低 5 年、最高 20 年（再犯 15–40 年）；单纯持有最高 10 年。PC §647(j)(4)：未经同意传播私密影像，轻罪最高 6 个月监禁、1,000 美元罚款；民事可索赔（Civ. Code §1708.85）。网上为实施犯罪而接触未成年人：PC §288.3（重罪）；为淫秽目的约未成年人见面：PC §288.4（wobbler）（美国/加州）。
  Benefit: 18 U.S.C. §2252: child sexual abuse material — receipt/distribution/transportation carries a 5-year mandatory minimum up to 20 years (15–40 for repeat offenders); mere possession up to 10 years. PC §647(j)(4): distributing intimate images without consent — a misdemeanor with up to 6 months in jail and a $1,000 fine; civil damages available (Civ. Code §1708.85). Contacting a minor to commit a felony online: PC §288.3 (felony); arranging to meet a minor for lewd purposes: PC §288.4 (wobbler) (United States/California).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：18 U.S.C. §2252. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2252>；California Penal Code §§647(j)(4), 288.3, 288.4；California Civil Code §1708.85
- 备注：别给未成年人发任何性相关内容。
  Note: Never send anything sexual to a minor.

### 5. "兼职"要你用自己的卡收钱、取现、转账，不管给多少好处费都不做 / "Gig" Asking You to Receive and Move Money? It's Money Laundering — Walk Away
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。放弃这类"日结几百、带卡去银行"的活。
  Cost: Free. Pass up these "a few hundred a day, take your card to the bank" gigs.
- 说人话：这类兼职挣的是几百块，代价可能是联邦重罪。帮诈骗团伙收钱、取现、转账，是洗钱（18 USC §1956，每项最高 20 年监禁）或"花用赃款"（18 USC §1957，超 1 万美元最高 10 年）。换成虚拟币转走也一样。"我不知道钱是哪来的"不一定是挡箭牌。
  In plain terms: These gigs pay a few hundred dollars; the price can be a federal felony. Receiving, withdrawing, and transferring money for fraud rings is money laundering (18 USC §1956 — up to 20 years per count) or "spending dirty money" (18 USC §1957 — over $10,000 means up to 10 years). Converting it to crypto doesn't change that. "I didn't know where the money came from" isn't necessarily a defense.
- 收益：18 U.S.C. §1956 洗钱：最高 20 年监禁、50 万美元或涉案金额两倍罚款。18 U.S.C. §1957：明知是犯罪所得仍进行超 1 万美元的资金交易，最高 10 年监禁（§1957 不要求证明藏匿意图，举证门槛更低）。加州收受赃物罪（PC §496）：wobbler，财物 ≤$950 轻罪（县监狱最高 1 年），>$950 可按重罪（16 个月/2 年/3 年）、罚款最高 $10,000（美国/加州）。
  Benefit: 18 U.S.C. §1956 money laundering: up to 20 years in prison, fines up to $500,000 or twice the amount involved. 18 U.S.C. §1957: conducting over $10,000 in transactions with known criminal proceeds — up to 10 years (§1957 doesn't require proving concealment intent, so it's easier to charge). California receiving stolen property (PC §496): a wobbler — misdemeanor up to 1 year in county jail if the property is worth $950 or less, felony (16 months/2 years/3 years) if more, fines up to $10,000 (United States/California).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：18 U.S.C. §§1956, 1957. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1956>；California Penal Code §496
- 备注：银行卡不外借，见第 8 节第 8 条。
  Note: Never lend your bank card — see Chapter 8, Item 8.

### 6. 有人拉你去"包装材料"贷款、按贷款额分你好处费，一个都不做 / "Dress Up" Loan Applications for a Cut? That's Federal Loan Fraud
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。直接拒绝。不把身份证和信用记录借人用，不签没看过的贷款合同。
  Cost: Free. Refuse outright. Don't lend your ID or credit profile, and don't sign loan paperwork you haven't read.
- 说人话：帮人"包装材料"贷款、拿分成，按联邦贷款欺诈办（18 USC §1014）：最高 30 年监禁、100 万美元罚款。钱贷在谁名下，债就是谁的，"我当时是想还的"不顶用。
  In plain terms: "Dressing up" loan applications for a cut is federal loan fraud (18 USC §1014): up to 30 years in prison and a $1,000,000 fine. Whoever's name is on the loan owns the debt — "I intended to pay it back" doesn't help.
- 收益：18 U.S.C. §1014：为影响银行决定作虚假陈述，最高 30 年监禁、100 万美元罚款。伪造政府公文、印章另有州与联邦罪名。贷款合同谁签字谁是借款人（美国）。
  Benefit: 18 U.S.C. §1014: making false statements to influence a bank's decision — up to 30 years in prison and a $1,000,000 fine. Forging government documents or seals triggers separate state and federal charges. Whoever signs the loan contract is the borrower (United States).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：18 U.S.C. §1014. https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=1999&num=0&req=granuleid:USC-1999-title18-section1014
- 备注：替人取现转账见第 5 条。自己被冒名贷款，查信用报告见第 8 节第 9 条。
  Note: For withdrawing and transferring cash for others, see Item 5. If a loan was taken in your name, check your credit reports — see Chapter 8, Item 9.

### 7. 捡到银行卡不取钱，捡到钱包联系失主或交给警察，不揣走 / Found a Bank Card? Don't Use It — Return Wallets to Owners or Police
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。打电话找失主，或送到警局。
  Cost: Free. Call to find the owner, or hand it to the police.
- 说人话：捡到银行卡去取钱、刷卡，是身份盗窃/欺诈（PC §530.5，"可轻可重"：轻罪最高 1 年监禁，重罪 16 个月、2 年或 3 年）。捡到钱包手机，联系失主或交给警察，别揣走。
  In plain terms: Withdrawing or swiping with a found bank card is identity theft/fraud (PC §530.5, a wobbler: up to 1 year in jail as a misdemeanor, 16 months, 2, or 3 years in prison as a felony). Found a wallet or phone? Contact the owner or hand it to police — don't pocket it.
- 收益：PC §530.5：故意获取、使用他人个人身份信息用于非法目的，wobbler，轻罪最高 1 年监禁，重罪 16 个月、2 年或 3 年监禁。拾得遗失物：Civil Code §§2080–2080.10，§2080.1(a) 要求拾得价值 $100 以上、失主不明的财物，须在合理时间内交市警局/县警长并作书面宣誓说明；失主 90 天内可认领（加州）。
  Benefit: PC §530.5: willfully obtaining and using another person's personal identifying information for unlawful purposes — a wobbler, up to 1 year in jail as a misdemeanor, 16 months, 2, or 3 years in prison as a felony. Found property: Civil Code §§2080–2080.10 — §2080.1(a) requires turning property worth $100 or more with an unknown owner over to city police or the county sheriff within a reasonable time, with a sworn written statement; the owner has 90 days to claim it (California).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Penal Code §530.5. https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/penal-code/pen-sect-530-5/；California Civil Code §§2080–2080.10
- 备注：原则：不是你的别动。
  Note: The rule: if it's not yours, don't touch it.

### 8. 优惠用自己的真实身份领，不批量注册小号薅羊毛 / Claim Deals With Your Real Identity — Don't Mass-Register Fake Accounts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。放弃"羊毛任务群"。
  Cost: Free. Quit the "deal-hunting task groups."
- 说人话：用自己真实身份、一个号领一次券，是平台给你的优惠。批量注册假账号、编造交易多领，按诈骗办：联邦电汇欺诈（18 USC §1343）最高 20 年监禁（涉金融机构或重大灾难救济的最高 30 年）；金额小也可能吃州盗窃罪（PC §484）。教别人、卖"教程"是另一档罪名。
  In plain terms: Claiming a coupon once with your real identity is the platform's gift to you. Mass-registering fake accounts and fabricating transactions to claim more is fraud: federal wire fraud (18 USC §1343) carries up to 20 years (30 years if it involves a financial institution or major disaster relief); small amounts can still mean state theft charges (PC §484). Teaching others or selling "tutorials" is a separate offense tier.
- 收益：18 U.S.C. §1343 电汇欺诈：以欺诈手段通过电子通讯骗取财物，最高 20 年监禁/$250,000 罚款。加州盗窃罪按金额分级（PC §490.2，$950 以下轻罪）。平台可封号、追回优惠（美国/加州）。
  Benefit: 18 U.S.C. §1343 wire fraud: using electronic communications to defraud — up to 20 years and $250,000 in fines. California theft is graded by amount (PC §490.2 — under $950 is a misdemeanor). Platforms can ban accounts and claw back deals (United States/California).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：18 U.S.C. §1343. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1343>；California Penal Code §490.2
- 备注：界线不在薅了多少，在有没有编假的。
  Note: The line isn't how much you took — it's whether you fabricated anything.

### 9. 不从阳台、窗户往外扔东西，烟头、垃圾袋、酒瓶都算 / Don't Throw Anything From Balconies or Windows
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。烟头、垃圾袋、酒瓶都拿到屋里扔。
  Cost: Free. Take cigarette butts, trash bags, and bottles inside to throw away.
- 说人话：加州没有"高空抛物罪"这个罪名，但往楼下扔东西砸到人，按袭击/殴打论处：持致命武器袭击（PC §245(a)(1)）是"可轻可重"的罪，重罪可判 2、3、4 年；砸死人按谋杀或过失杀人论处。没砸到人也可能吃乱扔垃圾、危害公共安全的罚单。
  In plain terms: California has no "throwing objects from heights" crime, but throwing things down that hit someone is prosecuted as assault or battery: assault with a deadly weapon (PC §245(a)(1)) is a wobbler — up to 2, 3, or 4 years in prison as a felony; killing someone means murder or manslaughter charges. Even missing can earn littering or public-endangerment citations.
- 收益：PC §245(a)(1)：用致命武器或可能造成严重伤害的方式袭击他人，wobbler（轻罪县监狱最高 1 年，重罪 2、3、4 年）。加州乱扔垃圾：PC §374.3（非法倾倒，infraction；第 4 次及以上可按轻罪，最高 $5,000/6 个月）；一般乱扔 PC §374.4(a)。故意向行驶车辆扔东西：Vehicle Code §23110(a) 轻罪（最高 6 个月），意图造成重伤的 §23110(b) 为重罪（最高 3 年）。砸伤砸死按伤害、杀人罪论处（加州）。
  Benefit: PC §245(a)(1): assaulting someone with a deadly weapon or by means likely to cause great bodily injury — a wobbler (misdemeanor up to 1 year in county jail, felony 2/3/4 years). California littering: PC §374.3 (illegal dumping — infraction; 4th and later offenses can be a misdemeanor, up to $5,000/6 months); general littering PC §374.4(a). Intentionally throwing at moving vehicles: Vehicle Code §23110(a) misdemeanor (up to 6 months); §23110(b) felony (up to 3 years) if intended to cause great bodily injury. Injuring or killing someone is prosecuted as assault, battery, or homicide (California).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：California Penal Code §§245(a)(1), 374.3, 374.4(a)；California Vehicle Code §23110
- 备注："有危险"本身在某些城市就可处罚。
  Note: In some cities, creating the danger alone is punishable.

### 10. 不买来路不明的仿真枪：加州禁止在公共场合展示仿真枪 / Skip Sketchy Replica Guns — Displaying Imitation Firearms in Public Is Illegal in California
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。想打枪去正规靶场。
  Cost: Free. If you want to shoot, go to a licensed range.
- 说人话：加州禁止在公共场合公开显示仿真枪（PC §20170），违者按违规处罚（初犯 $100、再犯 $300，第三次及以上升轻罪）。拿仿真枪指人，可能被当成真枪按持枪抢劫、袭击论处，警察也可能依法开枪。真枪在加州管制严格：购买须经 DOJ 背景审查与 10 天（240 小时）等待期（PC §27540，经销商等待期内不得交付：§26815）。
  In plain terms: California bans openly displaying imitation firearms in public (PC §20170) — infraction penalties ($100 first offense, $300 for repeats, misdemeanor from the third offense on). Pointing a replica gun at someone can be treated like a real gun: armed robbery or assault charges, and police may lawfully open fire.
