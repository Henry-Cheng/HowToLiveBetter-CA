# 1. 不要早死 / Don't Die Early

这一节讲三件事：车祸、火灾、中毒这类意外和伤害死亡；少数几种证据最硬的疫苗和筛查；身体出了什么信号就该去查一查。收益那一栏的数字，来自随机分组的试验、跟踪一大批人很多年的研究，或者官方统计。成本那一栏是作者按市面价格的粗略估计，只用来互相比较，不要拿去引用。

This section covers three things: deaths from accidents and injuries like car crashes, fires, and poisoning; the few vaccines and screenings with the strongest evidence; and which bodily warning signs mean you should get checked. The numbers in the Benefit line come from randomized trials, long-term cohort studies, or official statistics. The Cost line is a rough estimate at market prices, only for comparing items against each other—don't cite it.

### 1. 系安全带，前排后排都系 / Buckle up, front and back seats
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。每次上车多花 2 秒。
  Cost: Free. Two extra seconds each time you get in the car.
- 说人话：坐前排系上安全带，出车祸时被撞死的概率大约降一半。坐轻型卡车前排的降六成。美国 2022 年死在车里的人，能查到系没系带的，有一半没系。
  In plain terms: Buckling up in the front seat cuts the chance of dying in a crash by about half; in the front of a light truck, by about 60%. Of the people who died in cars in the US in 2022 and whose belt status was known, half weren't buckled.
- 收益：美国交通安全主管部门（NHTSA）估计，系安全带能让轿车前排乘员受致命伤的风险下降 45%。轻型卡车前排下降 60%。2022 年美国死在乘用车（载人的小汽车）里的人当中，能查到系没系带的，有 50% 没系。世界卫生组织（WHO）的口径是，安全带让车内人员的死亡风险最多降低 50%。WHO 估计中国 2021 年死于道路交通事故的有 248,099 人，相当于每 10 万人里死 17.4 人（中国人群数据）
  Benefit: The US National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) estimates seat belts cut fatal-injury risk by 45% for front-seat car occupants and 60% for front-seat light-truck occupants. In 2022, among US passenger-vehicle deaths with known belt status, 50% were unbelted. The WHO says seat belts reduce in-vehicle death risk by up to 50%. WHO estimated 248,099 road-traffic deaths in China in 2021, or 17.4 per 100,000 people (data from China).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：NHTSA (2024). Occupant Protection in Passenger Vehicles: 2022 Data (DOT HS 813 573). <https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813573> ; WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries> ; WHO GHO RS_196/RS_198 (China, 2021). <https://ghoapi.azureedge.net/api/RS_196?$filter=SpatialDim%20eq%20%27CHN%27>
- 备注：后排也要系。同一份报告里，第二排被撞死的人有 60% 没系带。45%、60% 这两个数，是美国交通安全主管部门（NHTSA）拿事故数据库算出来的估计（引 Kahane 2015），不是随机分组的试验。WHO 那个中国死亡人数是推算出来的，比公安交管登记的数字高好几倍。两边统计的范围不一样，这里只用 WHO 的口径。
  Note: Buckle up in the back too—60% of second-row deaths in the same report were unbelted. The 45% and 60% figures are NHTSA estimates from crash databases (citing Kahane 2015), not randomized trials. The WHO's China death figure is modeled and several times higher than police-registered numbers; the scopes differ, so only the WHO figure is used here.

### 2. 骑摩托车、电动自行车戴头盔并扣好 / Wear a helmet on motorcycles and e-bikes, chin strap buckled
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一顶合格的头盔约 20 到 50 美元。每次戴上花 5 秒。
  Cost: A certified helmet costs roughly $20–50. Five seconds to put on each time.
- 说人话：戴头盔并且把扣带扣好，摩托车骑手出事时死亡的概率低约四成。头部受伤低约七成。扣带要系紧，松松垮垮挂在头上不算戴。
  In plain terms: Wearing a helmet with the chin strap buckled cuts a motorcyclist's death risk by about 40% and head-injury risk by about 70%. The strap must be snug—perched loosely on your head doesn't count.
- 收益：Cochrane 把多项研究合起来一起算。摩托车骑行者戴头盔，死亡风险下降 42%。原值 OR 0.58，可信范围 95% CI 0.50 到 0.68。头部损伤下降 69%（OR 0.31，95% CI 0.25 到 0.38）
  Benefit: A Cochrane review pooled multiple studies. Helmeted motorcyclists had 42% lower death risk (OR 0.58, 95% CI 0.50–0.68) and 69% lower head-injury risk (OR 0.31, 95% CI 0.25–0.38).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Liu BC 等 (2008). Helmets for preventing injury in motorcycle riders. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD004333.pub3>
- 备注：头盔要把扣带扣好，挂在车把上不算戴。上面的数据来自摩托车。电动自行车没有直接研究，是照着推过来的，因为撞上以后头部受伤的方式相同。加州对自行车和电助力车头盔有按年龄和车型区分的具体规定，细节待核实。
  Note: Buckle the chin strap—a helmet hanging on the handlebar doesn't count. The numbers above come from motorcycles; there are no direct e-bike studies, and the extrapolation is used because head injuries happen the same way in a crash. California has specific helmet rules for bicycles and e-bikes that vary by age and bike class; the details are to be verified.

### 3. 装烟雾报警器；用燃气取暖、做饭或车库连着住宅的，再装一氧化碳报警器 / Install smoke alarms; add a CO alarm if you use gas appliances or have an attached garage
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：烟雾报警器一个约 10 到 30 美元。一氧化碳报警器一个约 25 到 50 美元。装好以后每年换一次电池（或买十年长效电池款）。
  Cost: A smoke alarm costs roughly $10–30; a carbon monoxide alarm roughly $25–50. Replace batteries yearly (or buy a 10-year sealed-battery model).
- 说人话：一个能正常工作的烟雾报警器，能把住宅火灾里死亡的概率降约六成。起火时有四成人在睡觉，光靠鼻子是醒不过来的。家里用燃气、用壁炉，或者车库连着住宅的，再加一个一氧化碳报警器。
  In plain terms: A working smoke alarm cuts the chance of dying in a home fire by about 60%. In about 40% of fatal fires people are asleep—your nose won't wake you. If your home uses gas, has a fireplace, or has an attached garage, add a carbon monoxide alarm.
- 收益：美国北卡罗来纳州研究了一批住宅火灾，回头比对出事和没出事的家庭。家里有一个能正常工作的烟雾报警器，死亡风险下降约六成。原值 OR 0.39，可信范围 95% CI 0.18 到 0.83。美国 2018 到 2020 年，有人住的住宅里发生的致死火灾，24% 家里没有烟雾报警器，41% 起火时人在睡觉。中国 2018 年报告一氧化碳中毒死亡 11,523 例。这些死亡里，12 月有 72.59%、1 月有 67.42%、2 月有 66.48% 发生在家中（中国人群数据）
  Benefit: A North Carolina study compared households with and without fatal fires: a working smoke alarm was associated with about 60% lower death risk (OR 0.39, 95% CI 0.18–0.83). In US fatal home fires in 2018–2020, 24% of homes had no smoke alarm and 41% of fires started while people slept. China reported 11,523 carbon monoxide deaths in 2018, with 72.59% (December), 67.42% (January), and 66.48% (February) of those deaths occurring at home (data from China).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Marshall SW 等 (1998). Fatal residential fires: who dies and who survives? JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.279.20.1633> ; USFA (2022). Fatal Fires in Residential Buildings (2018-2020), Topical Fire Report Series 22(2). <https://www.usfa.fema.gov/downloads/pdf/statistics/v22i2.pdf> ; You J 等 (2020). Number of Deaths due to Carbon Monoxide Poisoning by Month and by Place of Death — China, 2018. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2020.008>
- 备注：给 B 的原因：烟雾报警器这边只有一项研究，做法是回头比对出事和没出事的家庭，不是随机分组的试验。一氧化碳报警器本身没有降低死亡率的研究。这里的收益是按「中毒死亡集中在冬季、集中在家中」推出来的。加州法律要求住宅安装烟雾报警器，用燃气设备、壁炉或连体车库的住宅也要装一氧化碳报警器（具体条文待核实）。
  Note: Grade B because the smoke-alarm evidence comes from a single retrospective case-comparison study, not a randomized trial, and there are no mortality studies on CO alarms themselves—the benefit is inferred from CO deaths concentrating in winter and at home. California law requires smoke alarms in homes, and CO alarms in homes with gas appliances, fireplaces, or attached garages (exact statute to be verified).

### 4. 燃气软管到期就换，不自己改管道，闻到燃气味先撤出再打 911 / Replace gas hoses on schedule; never modify pipes yourself; smell gas? Get out and call 911
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一根合规的燃气连接软管十几到几十美元。换的时候顺手看一眼有没有过期、开裂。
  Cost: A compliant gas connector hose costs roughly ten to tens of dollars. Check for expiration or cracking whenever you replace one.
- 说人话：老化开裂的软管是家里燃气泄漏最常见的原因之一。到期就换，不自己改管道、不自己拆燃气表。有人上门以「安检」名义推销高价阀门和报警器，不必当场掏钱。闻到燃气味：别碰任何电器开关（开也不开、关也不关），打火机别碰；能安全做到就开门窗通风，人立刻撤到室外，到室外再打 911 或燃气公司电话。
  In plain terms: Aging, cracked hoses are one of the most common causes of home gas leaks. Replace them on schedule; never modify gas piping or remove the meter yourself. If someone shows up for an "inspection" and hard-sells expensive valves or alarms, you don't have to buy on the spot. If you smell gas: don't touch any electrical switches (neither on nor off), no lighters; ventilate only if you can do it safely, get everyone outside immediately, and call 911 or the gas company from outside.
- 收益：原书此处引用《城镇燃气管理条例》，把「及时更换使用年限已届满的燃气燃烧器具、连接管」写成用户义务，并禁止擅自改装户内燃气设施。加州版改为通用建议：按年限更换软管和老旧灶具、不私改管道，能去掉最常见的泄漏源；闻到燃气味先撤出再求助，避免在泄漏现场打电话或开关电器引燃
  Benefit: The original edition cited China's Urban Gas Management Regulations, which make timely replacement of expired burners and connectors a user obligation and prohibit unauthorized modification of indoor gas facilities. The California edition states this as general advice: replacing hoses and aging appliances on schedule and never modifying pipes removes the most common leak sources; evacuating before calling for help avoids igniting a leak by flipping switches or using a phone at the scene.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：原书引用：国务院 (2010). 城镇燃气管理条例（国务院令第 583 号）第二十七条、第二十八条、第二十条、第二十九条. <http://www.gov.cn/gongbao/content/2010/content_1758214.htm>；深圳市住房和建设局 (2024). 安全用气小知识（二）. <https://zjj.sz.gov.cn/zcjzts/content/post_11882410.html>；张家港市应急管理局 (2024). 燃气安全使用知识. <https://www.zjg.gov.cn/zjgszwz/yjaqzs/202411/72b4fad7399c48378302460c6ce2d55b.shtml>
- 备注：原引中国条例，此处为通用安全建议，不说明换管能少出多少事故，所以只给 C 级。美国各州燃气安全法规不同，加州具体条文待核实。一氧化碳中毒是另一件事，见第 13 节。
  Note: The original cited a Chinese regulation; this is restated as general safety advice. No official statistics show how much scheduled replacement reduces accidents, hence grade C. US gas safety rules vary by state; the California-specific provisions are to be verified. Carbon monoxide poisoning is a separate topic—see Section 13.

### 5. 不采、不买、不吃野生蘑菇，任何「土办法鉴别」都不成立 / Don't pick, buy, or eat wild mushrooms—no folk test works
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的地方是放弃一种季节性的野味。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is giving up a seasonal delicacy.
- 说人话：光 2025 年一年，中国就有 828 起蘑菇中毒，2165 人中招，13 人死。那一年还新认出 34 种以前没记录过会毒人的蘑菇。银针试毒、虫子吃过就没毒、颜色鲜艳才有毒，这些说法全都不成立。
  In plain terms: In 2025 alone, China recorded 828 mushroom-poisoning incidents, 2,165 people affected, 13 dead—and 34 newly recorded toxic species that year. Silver-needle tests, "bugs ate it so it's safe," and "only colorful ones are poisonous" are all false.
- 收益：中国疾控中心 2025 年调查了 828 起蘑菇中毒事件，涉及 2165 人，死亡 13 人。病死率 0.6%，指中毒的人里有 0.6% 死亡。2019 到 2024 年每年 276 到 676 起，病死率在 0.87% 到 2.86% 之间。光 2025 年就鉴定出 138 种有毒蘑菇，其中 34 种是国内第一次记录到会引起中毒（中国人群数据）
  Benefit: China's CDC investigated 828 mushroom-poisoning incidents in 2025: 2,165 people affected, 13 deaths, a 0.6% case-fatality rate. From 2019 to 2024 there were 276–676 incidents per year with fatality rates of 0.87%–2.86%. In 2025 alone, 138 toxic species were identified, 34 of them newly recorded as causing poisoning in China (data from China).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Mushroom Poisoning Outbreaks — China, 2025. China CDC Weekly (2026). <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2026.120>；同刊 2024 年度报告 <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2025.106>
- 备注：吃错了就立刻催吐，然后带上剩下的蘑菇或者拍的照片去急诊（ER）。在美国可拨打 911，或致电 Poison Control 1-800-222-1222（poison.org）。毒蘑菇中毒分好几种类型，认出是哪一种蘑菇直接决定医生怎么治。每年都有新种类被记录到能毒人，所以「本地人认得清」这个前提本身站不住。银针验毒、和大蒜同煮、虫子吃过就没毒、颜色鲜艳才有毒，这些说法全都不成立。
  Note: If you've eaten the wrong ones, induce vomiting right away and go to the ER with the leftover mushrooms or photos. In the US, call 911 or Poison Control at 1-800-222-1222 (poison.org). Mushroom poisoning has several types, and identifying the species directly determines treatment. New toxic species are recorded every year, so "locals know them all" doesn't hold. Silver-needle tests, cooking with garlic, "bugs ate it so it's safe," and "only colorful ones are poisonous" are all false.

### 6. 电动自行车电池安全：不改装、用原装充电器、不在室内过夜充电 / E-bike battery safety: no modifications, use the original charger, don't charge indoors overnight
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的地方是每次都按规矩充电、存放。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is charging and storing it properly every time.
- 说人话：别买改装电池，别用杂牌充电器，别整夜充着不管，别把车和电池放在卧室、楼道或堵住逃生通道的地方充电。锂电池一旦起火，几十秒里就整片烧开，同时冒出剧毒的烟。车停在逃生通道上起火，烧的就是你唯一的逃生路线。
  In plain terms: Don't buy modified batteries, don't use off-brand chargers, don't leave it charging overnight unattended, and don't charge the bike or battery in bedrooms, hallways, or anywhere blocking your escape route. Once a lithium battery catches fire it engulfs in tens of seconds and gives off highly toxic smoke. A fire on your escape route burns the only way out.
- 收益：原书引用中国应急管理部规章，明文禁止在高层建筑公共门厅、疏散走道、楼梯间、安全出口停放电动自行车或充电，违者个人罚 500 至 1000 元；并引用 2021 年全国电动自行车火灾 1.8 万起、死 65 人，其中烧在建筑物内的 3782 起死了 60 人。加州版保留通用安全建议：锂电池火灾发展极快且毒烟致命，充电和存放位置直接决定火灾时有没有路可逃
  Benefit: The original edition cited Chinese fire-safety rules prohibiting e-bike parking or charging in lobbies, corridors, stairwells, and exits of tall residential buildings, plus 2021 national figures: 18,000 e-bike fires, 65 deaths, with 3,782 indoor fires killing 60. The California edition keeps the general safety advice: lithium-battery fires develop extremely fast with lethal toxic smoke, and where you charge and store the bike determines whether you have a way out.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A

### 7. 量血压，高了就吃药降到达标 / Measure your blood pressure; take medication to reach target
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：家用电子血压计约 30 到 60 美元。量一次 1 分钟。降压药多为仿制药，有保险每月几美元。难的地方是没症状也要接着吃。
  Cost: A home blood pressure monitor costs roughly $30–60. One minute per reading. Generic blood-pressure drugs usually cost a few dollars a month with insurance. The hard part is keeping on taking them when you feel fine.
- 说人话：血压每降 10 mmHg，出大的心血管毛病的概率降两成，中风降 27%，心衰降 28%，死亡概率降 13%。中国 35 到 75 岁的人里，44.7% 有高血压，真正控制住的只有 7.2%（中国人群数据）。
  In plain terms: Every 10 mmHg drop in blood pressure cuts major cardiovascular events by about 20%, stroke by 27%, heart failure by 28%, and death by 13%. In China, 44.7% of people aged 35–75 have hypertension, but only 7.2% have it controlled (data from China).
- 收益：把 123 项试验、61 万余人合起来一起算。收缩压每降 10 mmHg，主要心血管事件低约两成。原值 RR 0.80，可信范围 95% CI 0.77 到 0.83。收缩压就是血压两个数里高的那个。卒中（中风）低约 27%（RR 0.73）。心力衰竭低约 28%（RR 0.72）。总死亡率下降 13%（RR 0.87，95% CI 0.84 到 0.91）。中国给 35 到 75 岁的 170 万人做过筛查：44.7% 有高血压。这些人里，知道自己有的占 44.7%，在治疗的占 30.1%，控制住的只有 7.2%（中国人群数据）
  Benefit: Pooling 123 trials with over 610,000 people: every 10 mmHg lower systolic pressure (the higher of the two numbers) meant about 20% fewer major cardiovascular events (RR 0.80, 95% CI 0.77–0.83), 27% fewer strokes (RR 0.73), 28% less heart failure (RR 0.72), and 13% lower total mortality (RR 0.87, 95% CI 0.84–0.91). A Chinese screening of 1.7 million people aged 35–75 found 44.7% had hypertension; of those, 44.7% knew it, 30.1% were treated, and only 7.2% had it controlled (data from China).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Ettehad D 等 (2016). Blood pressure lowering for prevention of cardiovascular disease and death: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(15)01225-8> ; Lu J 等 (2017). Prevalence, awareness, treatment, and control of hypertension in China (China PEACE Million Persons Project). Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(17)32478-9>
- 备注：先去量一次血压。降到多少算达标（130 还是 140）还有讨论，但「知道自己高血压并把它降下来」这件事本身没有争议。
  Note: Start by measuring your blood pressure once. There's still debate over the exact target (130 vs 140), but "know you have it and bring it down" is not controversial.

### 8. 35 岁以后只要超重，就去查一次空腹血糖，正常也每三年再查 / If you're overweight after 35, check fasting glucose once; repeat every 3 years if normal
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：抽一次血就行，有保险多为体检覆盖，自费约十几到几十美元。
  Cost: One blood draw, usually covered as part of a checkup with insurance; roughly ten to tens of dollars out of pocket.
- 说人话：花几十块抽一管血，就能在一点症状都没有的时候查出糖尿病前期。美国官方的筛查建议是：35 到 70 岁、超重或肥胖的人去查。查出来正常的，每三年再查一次。美国按体重指数 25 算超重，对亚裔放宽到 23。
  In plain terms: One blood draw can catch prediabetes before you feel a thing. The official US screening advice: adults aged 35–70 who are overweight or obese should be screened. If normal, repeat every 3 years. The US counts BMI ≥25 as overweight, lowered to ≥23 for Asian Americans.
- 收益：美国预防服务工作组（USPSTF）是美国官方给筛查建议的机构。它现在的建议是：对 35 到 70 岁、超重或肥胖的成年人筛查糖尿病前期和 2 型糖尿病，哪怕一点症状都没有。超重指体重指数 ≥25，肥胖指 ≥30。对亚裔美国人，它建议把界线降到体重指数 ≥23，因为同样的体重指数下，亚裔的糖尿病风险更高。这条建议的推荐等级是 B。它还写明：「对血糖正常的成年人，每 3 年筛查一次是合理的做法」
  Benefit: The US Preventive Services Task Force (USPSTF), the official US screening-guideline body, currently recommends screening adults aged 35–70 with overweight or obesity for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes, even with no symptoms. This is a grade B recommendation, and it states that re-screening every 3 years is reasonable for adults with normal blood sugar.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：US Preventive Services Task Force (2021). Screening for Prediabetes and Type 2 Diabetes. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/screening-for-prediabetes-and-type-2-diabetes>：「at a lower BMI (≥23) if the patient is Asian American」
- 备注：查出糖尿病前期不等于就要吃药。相当一部分人光靠改生活方式就能把它扭回去。
  Note: Prediabetes doesn't mean you must start medication—many people reverse it with lifestyle changes alone.

### 9. 开车不超速、不酒驾 / Don't speed or drink and drive
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。开慢一点，一趟多花几分钟。难的地方是饭局上少喝。
  Cost: Free. Driving a bit slower costs a few minutes per trip. The hard part is drinking less at dinner.

- 收益：世界卫生组织（WHO）的说法：平均车速每升高 1%，出人命的事故风险升高 4%。酒驾的风险，从很低的血液酒精浓度起就开始上升
  Benefit: Per the WHO, each 1% increase in mean speed raises the risk of a fatal crash by 4%. Drink-driving risk starts rising from very low blood alcohol concentrations.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries>

### 10. 给 4 岁以下儿童用安全座椅，不要抱在怀里 / Use a car seat for children under 4—don't hold them in your arms
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->

  In plain terms: Car seats cut fatal-injury risk by about 70% for infants under 1 and about half for children aged 1–4. Holding a child in your arms gives no such protection—you can't hold on in a sudden stop.
- 收益：美国交通安全主管部门（NHTSA）估计，用安全座椅能让轿车里 1 岁以下婴儿受致命伤的风险下降 71%。1 到 4 岁的幼儿下降 54%。世界卫生组织（WHO）的口径是婴儿死亡下降 71%
  Benefit: NHTSA estimates car seats reduce fatal-injury risk by 71% for infants under 1 in cars and 54% for toddlers aged 1–4. The WHO's figure is a 71% reduction in infant deaths.

- 来源：NHTSA (2024). Occupant Protection in Passenger Vehicles: 2022 Data (DOT HS 813 573). <https://crashstats.nhtsa.dot.gov/Api/Public/ViewPublication/813573> ; WHO (2025). Road traffic injuries fact sheet. <https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/road-traffic-injuries>

### 11. 家里有小孩就给窗户和阳台装限位器，纱窗不算防护 / Install window guards if you have kids—screens don't count
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一副窗户限位器或者儿童安全锁十几到几十美元。装上去十分钟。
  Cost: Window guards or child safety locks cost ten to tens of dollars per window. Ten minutes to install.
- 说人话：一副限位器几十块钱。纽约市给高风险住户免费装窗户护栏、又上门讲解，一个区上报的儿童坠落数三年里降了一半。后来纽约干脆立法，要求房东给住着小孩的房子装护栏。纱窗一推就开，不算防护。

NYC later made landlords install guards in apartments with children. Window screens pop out with a push—they're not protection.
- 收益：纽约市从 1972 年起办了一个叫「孩子不会飞」的项目，在高风险城区免费发窗户护栏，还上门讲解。布朗克斯区上报的坠落数，1973 到 1975 年下降 50%。此后纽约市修改卫生法典，强制房东为住有 10 岁及以下儿童的公寓安装窗户护栏
  Benefit: New York City's "Children Can't Fly" program, started in 1972, distributed free window guards in high-risk neighborhoods with home education. Reported falls in the Bronx dropped 50% from 1973 to 1975, after which NYC amended its health code to require landlords to install window guards in apartments housing children aged 10 and under.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Spiegel CN, Lindaman FC (1977). Children can't fly: a program to prevent childhood morbidity and mortality from window falls. American Journal of Public Health. <https://doi.org/10.2105/AJPH.67.12.1143>；(2021). Unintentional Window Falls in Children and Adolescents. ### 12. 儿童近水不离视线，划船、野泳穿救生衣 / Keep kids in sight near water; wear life jackets on boats
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：救生衣约 15 到 50 美元。另外要搭上看着孩子的那份注意力。
  Cost: A life jacket costs roughly $15–50. Plus the attention it takes to watch the child.
- 说人话：落水的时候穿着救生衣，淹死的概率大约只有不穿的一半。溺水至今是中国 1 到 14 岁孩子的头号死因（中国人群数据）。农村的溺水死亡率大约是城市的两倍（中国人群数据）。
  In plain terms: Wearing a life jacket when you fall in the water roughly halves the chance of drowning. Drowning remains the leading cause of death for children aged 1–14 in China (data from China), where rural drowning mortality is about twice the urban rate (data from China).
- 收益：用美国海岸警卫队的数据做的研究，把条件相近的人配成对来比。坐休闲船只落水的人，穿着救生衣的溺亡风险低约一半。原值是校正后 RR 0.51，可信范围 95% CI 0.35 到 0.74。中国 20 岁以下的溺水死亡率，从 2013 年的每 10 万人 6.60 人降到 2021 年的每 10 万人 3.28 人。农村约为城市的 2 倍。溺水仍然是 1 到 14 岁儿童的头号死因。2021 年 0 到 19 岁的伤害死亡里，溺水占 31.1%，道路交通占 27.9%（中国人群数据）
  Benefit: A study using US Coast Guard data matched comparable cases: recreational boaters who fell overboard wearing a life jacket had about half the drowning-death risk (adjusted RR 0.51, 95% CI 0.35–0.74). In China, drowning mortality under age 20 fell from 6.60 to 3.28 per 100,000 between 2013 and 2021; rural rates were about twice urban rates, and drowning remained the top killer of children aged 1–14, accounting for 31.1% of injury deaths aged 0–19 in 2021 versus 27.9% for road traffic (data from China).

China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2023.198> ; Zhou J 等 (2024). Injury Mortality of Children and Adolescents Aged 0–19 Years — China, 2010–2021. China CDC Weekly. <https://doi.org/10.46234/ccdcw2024.057>

  Note: Grade A covers only the life-jacket half. "Never out of sight" is consensus advice, grade C on its own—no randomized trial exists or will. For winter ice play, see Item 41 (measure ice thickness first).

### 13. 60 岁以上练平衡和腿部力量，改造家里的浴室和楼梯 / Balance and leg strength after 60; fix up the bathroom and stairs
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->

Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD012424.pub2> ; Gillespie LD 等 (2012). Interventions for preventing falls in older people living in the community. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007146.pub3> ; Lu Z 等 (2021). Characteristics of Falls Among Older People — China, 2018. ### 14. 查乙肝，没抗体就打疫苗 / Check your hepatitis B status; vaccinate if you're not immune
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：查一次约 20 到 50 美元，多为体检覆盖。成人乙肝疫苗 3 针，多数保险覆盖。半年之内打完。
  Cost: The blood test costs roughly $20–50, usually covered in a checkup. The 3-dose adult hepatitis B series is usually covered by insurance; finish within six months.
- 说人话：查一次几十块。启东那项跟踪了 30 年的试验里，一出生就打了乙肝疫苗的人，后来得原发性肝癌的概率低 84%，身上带着乙肝病毒的比例低 72%。成人查出没抗体的补三针。
  In plain terms: The blood test is cheap. In the 30-year Qidong trial, people vaccinated against hepatitis B at birth had 84% less primary liver cancer and 72% fewer chronic infections later in life. Adults without antibodies get three catch-up doses.
- 收益：启东那项试验按群体随机分组，不是按个人分组，跟踪了 30 年。新生儿接种乙肝疫苗，原发性肝癌下降 84%。可信范围是 95% CI 23% 到 97%。乙肝表面抗原 HBsAg 阳性率下降 72%（95% CI 68% 到 75%），这个阳性率就是查出身上带着病毒的人所占的比例。中国全人群的 HBsAg 阳性比例，1992 到 2014 年下降 52%，5 岁以下下降 97%（中国人群数据）
  Benefit: The Qidong trial randomized whole communities (not individuals) and followed them for 30 years. Neonatal hepatitis B vaccination cut primary liver cancer by 84% (95% CI 23%–97%) and HBsAg positivity—i.e., the share carrying the virus—by 72% (95% CI 68%–75%). China's population HBsAg prevalence fell 52% from 1992 to 2014, and 97% in children under 5 (data from China).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Qu C 等 (2014). Efficacy of neonatal HBV vaccination on liver cancer and other liver diseases over 30-year follow-up of the Qidong hepatitis B intervention study. PLoS Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1001774> ; Cui F 等 (2017). Prevention of Chronic Hepatitis B after 3 Decades of Escalating Vaccination Policy, China. Emerging Infectious Diseases. <https://doi.org/10.3201/eid2305.161477>
- 备注：肝癌下降 84% 那个数来自一出生就打的人。同一项试验里，到青少年才补打的，防住病毒携带的效力只有 21%。成人打主要图的是不再被感染，不能直接套那个 84%。已经感染的人打疫苗没用，要去肝病科定期复查。怎么算已经感染：化验单上乙肝表面抗原 HBsAg 那一项是阳性。美国 ACIP 建议 19 到 59 岁没打过疫苗的成人补种乙肝疫苗（最新口径待核实）。
  Note: The 84% liver-cancer figure comes from people vaccinated at birth; catch-up vaccination in adolescence was only 21% effective against chronic infection in the same trial. Adult vaccination prevents new infections—don't apply the 84% directly. Vaccination doesn't help those already infected; they need regular liver follow-up. You're infected if HBsAg (hepatitis B surface antigen) is positive. The US ACIP recommends catch-up hepatitis B vaccination for unvaccinated adults aged 19–59 (latest wording to be verified).

### 15. 被铁钉、木刺扎到或者伤口沾了泥土，当天去处理，顺便问破伤风要不要打 / Puncture wound or dirty cut? Get it treated the same day and ask about tetanus
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：去 urgent care 或 primary care 处理伤口，有保险多为常规覆盖，自费约几十到上百美元。医生说要打的话，加强针多为保险覆盖。
  Cost: Wound care at urgent care or primary care is usually covered with insurance; roughly tens to a hundred dollars out of pocket. A booster, if the doctor recommends one, is usually covered too.
- 说人话：破伤风一旦发病，大约每 10 个人里死 1 个。深的伤口、沾了泥土的伤口风险最高。伤口又深又脏、离上一针满 5 年的，要加强 1 针。当天去处理，别拖到伤口肿起来再说。
  In plain terms: Once tetanus sets in, about 1 in 10 cases is fatal. Deep wounds and wounds contaminated with soil are highest risk. For a deep, dirty wound when your last shot was 5+ years ago, you need a booster. Go the same day—don't wait until it's swollen.
- 收益：美国的破伤风病例里，大约每 10 例死 1 例。深的伤口、被泥土污染的伤口，风险更高。中国 2024 年的诊疗规范把伤口分成两档。6 小时内处理、没沾泥土粪便和动物口水的浅伤口，算低风险。超过 6 小时才处理、沾了这些东西，或者是扎伤、撕脱伤、挤压伤、烧烫伤、冻伤、伤口里留着异物的，算高风险。打过 3 针以上、最后一针不到 5 年的，什么都不用打。5 到 10 年的，高风险伤口加强 1 针。满 10 年的，不管哪档都加强 1 针。没打全 3 针或者记不清的，要把疫苗补全，高风险伤口还要同时打被动免疫制剂（免疫球蛋白或抗毒素这类直接给抗体的针）
  Benefit: In the US, about 1 in 10 tetanus cases is fatal; deep, soil-contaminated wounds carry the highest risk. China's 2024 treatment guideline sorts wounds into two tiers: shallow wounds treated within 6 hours without soil/feces/saliva contamination are low risk; wounds treated after 6 hours, contaminated ones, or puncture, avulsion, crush, burn, frostbite, or foreign-body wounds are high risk. With 3+ doses and the last within 5 years, nothing more is needed; 5–10 years, a booster for high-risk wounds; 10+ years, a booster regardless. With an incomplete or unknown series, complete the vaccines, and add passive immunization (immunoglobulin or antitoxin) for high-risk wounds.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：美国疾病控制与预防中心. 破伤风：「Tetanus bacteria can get into someone's body through broken skin, usually through injuries.」「Tetanus can lead to death (1 in 10 cases in the United States are fatal).」「People who didn't complete the primary series or who aren't up to date with their 10-year tetanus booster shots are also at increased risk.」「Vaccination also helps prevent tetanus in people with wounds, depending on their tetanus vaccination history.」<https://www.cdc.gov/tetanus/about/index.html>
- 备注：到了医院让医生判断，别自己拿主意，也别嫌麻烦不问。不是所有伤口都要打。医生会看伤口属于哪一档，再问你上一次打针是什么时候，然后决定。「小时候打过就一辈子有效」是错的。在美国，破伤风加强针每 10 年一次（Tdap 或 Td）；伤口又深又脏且超过 5 年没加强的，要补 1 针。去之前想一想自己最后一次打破伤风针是哪一年。
  Note: Let the doctor decide—don't skip asking out of hassle. Not every wound needs a shot; the doctor will classify the wound and ask when your last dose was. "One childhood shot protects for life" is wrong. In the US, tetanus boosters (Tdap or Td) are due every 10 years, and a deep, dirty wound with no booster in 5+ years needs one. Before you go, try to recall the year of your last tetanus shot.

### 16. 接种 HPV 疫苗，越早越好 / Get the HPV vaccine—the earlier, the better
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：每针约两三百美元，一共 2 到 3 针。26 岁以下多为保险覆盖。每次跑一趟，半天。
  Cost: Roughly $200–300 per dose, 2–3 doses total, usually covered by insurance through age 26. Half a day per visit.
- 说人话：瑞典 167 万女性的数据里，17 岁以前打过 HPV 疫苗的人，后来得浸润性宫颈癌的概率只有没打的约八分之一。17 到 30 岁才打的，也只有一半左右。所以越早打越值。
  In plain terms: In Swedish data on 1.67 million women, those vaccinated before 17 had about one-eighth the invasive cervical cancer risk of the unvaccinated; those vaccinated at 17–30 had about half. The earlier, the better the deal.
- 收益：瑞典跟踪了 167 万名女性做统计：打过疫苗的人，浸润性宫颈癌的发病率比没打的低。17 岁以前接种的低约 88%（IRR 0.12，95% CI 0.00 到 0.34，括号里是原值和它的可信范围）。17 到 30 岁接种的低约 53%（IRR 0.47，95% CI 0.27 到 0.75）
  Benefit: Sweden followed 1.67 million women: vaccinated women had lower invasive cervical cancer incidence—about 88% lower when vaccinated before 17 (IRR 0.12, 95% CI 0.00–0.34) and about 53% lower when vaccinated at 17–30 (IRR 0.47, 95% CI 0.27–0.75).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Lei J 等 (2020). HPV Vaccination and the Risk of Invasive Cervical Cancer. New England Journal of Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1917338>
- 备注：9 到 14 岁打收益最大。美国 ACIP 建议 11 到 12 岁常规接种（男女都打），26 岁前可补种，27 到 45 岁和医生商量决定（口径待核实）。打过疫苗也还是要做宫颈癌筛查，疫苗替代不了筛查，见第 18 条。
  Note: Vaccination at 9–14 gives the biggest benefit. The US ACIP recommends routine vaccination at 11–12 for all genders, catch-up through 26, and shared decision-making at 27–45 (wording to be verified). Vaccinated people still need cervical cancer screening—the vaccine doesn't replace it; see Item 18.

### 17. 女性 40 岁起做乳腺癌筛查，每两年一次钼靶 / Breast cancer screening from 40: mammogram every 2 years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：钼靶多为保险覆盖，自费约 100 到 300 美元。每两年花半天。
  Cost: Mammograms are usually covered by insurance; roughly $100–300 out of pocket. Half a day every two years.

  Benefit: The USPSTF, the official US screening-guideline body, currently recommends: "biennial screening mammography for women aged 40 to 74" (grade B recommendation). For women 75+ and for supplemental ultrasound or MRI for dense breasts, it judges the evidence "insufficient."
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：US Preventive Services Task Force (2024). Breast Cancer: Screening. <https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/breast-cancer-screening>
- 备注：父母、子女或者亲兄弟姐妹得过乳腺癌的，以及带有 BRCA 突变的，属于高危，要单独找医生定方案，本条不适用。

### 18. 21 岁起做宫颈癌筛查，优先 HPV 检测 / Cervical cancer screening from 21, HPV test preferred
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：HPV 检测多为保险覆盖，自费约几十到上百美元。结果阴性的，几年查一次就行。取样几分钟。
  Cost: HPV testing is usually covered by insurance; roughly tens to a hundred dollars out of pocket. If negative, repeat every few years. Sampling takes minutes.
- 说人话：印度农村那项试验里，一辈子只做过一次 HPV 检测的女性，死于宫颈癌的概率低约一半，查出晚期宫颈癌的概率低约五成。美国 USPSTF 建议 21 到 65 岁女性做宫颈癌筛查。
  In plain terms: In the rural India trial, women screened just once in their lives with an HPV test had about half the cervical-cancer death risk and about half the advanced-cancer risk. The US USPSTF recommends cervical cancer screening for women aged 21–65.
- 收益：印度农村那项试验按群体随机分组，不是按个人分组。对象是 30 到 59 岁的女性。只做一次 HPV 检测筛查，死于宫颈癌的风险就低约 48%。原值 HR 0.52，可信范围 95% CI 0.33 到 0.83。查出晚期宫颈癌的风险低约 53%（HR 0.47，95% CI 0.32 到 0.69）
  Benefit: The rural India trial randomized whole communities. Among women aged 30–59, a single round of HPV screening cut cervical-cancer death risk by about 48% (HR 0.52, 95% CI 0.33–0.83) and advanced-cancer detection by about 53% (HR 0.47, 95% CI 0.32–0.69).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Sankaranarayanan R 等 (2009). HPV screening for cervical cancer in rural India. New England Journal of Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa0808516>
- 备注：打过 HPV 疫苗的人也要筛查，因为疫苗盖不住全部的病毒型别。这项研究少见的地方是：它是随机分组的筛查试验，直接拿死亡人数当结果，而且只筛了一轮。
  Note: Vaccinated women still need screening—the vaccine doesn't cover all virus types. What's rare about this study: it's a randomized screening trial with deaths as the endpoint, and it used just one screening round.

### 19. 45 岁起做结直肠癌筛查：粪便免疫化学检测或肠镜 / Colorectal cancer screening from 45: FIT or colonoscopy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：粪便检测（FIT）约 20 到 50 美元，每 1 到 2 年做一次，多为保险覆盖。肠镜多为保险覆盖，结果阴性的 10 年做一次就行。做肠镜前要留出一天清肠。
  Cost: A stool FIT test costs roughly $20–50 every 1–2 years, usually covered by insurance. Colonoscopy is usually covered too; if negative, once every 10 years is enough. Set aside a day for bowel prep before a colonoscopy.
- 说人话：每一两年做一次几十块的粪便隐血检查，死于结直肠癌的概率低约 16%，至少查过一轮的人低 25%。被叫去做一次肠镜的人，往后十年得结直肠癌的概率从 1.20% 降到 0.98%。美国 USPSTF 建议 45 到 75 岁做筛查。
  In plain terms: A cheap stool blood test every year or two cuts colorectal-cancer death risk by about 16% (25% for those completing at least one round). People invited to a one-time colonoscopy saw 10-year colorectal-cancer incidence fall from 1.20% to 0.98%. The US USPSTF recommends screening from ages 45 to 75.
- 收益：Cochrane 把多项研究合起来一起算。做粪便隐血筛查的人，死于结直肠癌的风险低约 16%。原值 RR 0.84，可信范围 95% CI 0.78 到 0.90。至少参加过一轮的人低约 25%（RR 0.75）。再看 NordICC 那项随机试验。被邀请去做肠镜的人，10 年里结直肠癌的发病从 1.20% 降到 0.98%。原值 RR 0.82，可信范围 95% CI 0.70 到 0.93
  Benefit: A Cochrane review pooled multiple studies: fecal occult blood screening cut colorectal-cancer death risk by about 16% (RR 0.84, 95% CI 0.78–0.90), and by about 25% (RR 0.75) among those completing at least one round. In the randomized NordICC trial, people invited to colonoscopy had 10-year colorectal-cancer incidence of 0.98% versus 1.20% (RR 0.82, 95% CI 0.70–0.93).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Hewitson P 等 (2007). Screening for colorectal cancer using the faecal occult blood test, Hemoccult. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD001216.pub2> ; Bretthauer M 等 (2022). Effect of Colonoscopy Screening on Risks of Colorectal Cancer and Related Death. New England Journal of Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2208375>
- 备注：争议。NordICC 是按「被邀请去做肠镜」的人来算的，结直肠癌死亡 0.28% 对 0.31%（RR 0.90，95% CI 0.64 到 1.16），差别还在误差范围内。这说明肠镜对死亡率的好处以前被高估过。粪便检测在死亡率上的证据反而更硬。家里有人得过这个病的要更早开始。
  Note: Controversy. NordICC analyzed by invitation: colorectal-cancer deaths were 0.28% vs 0.31% (RR 0.90, 95% CI 0.64–1.16), within the margin of error—suggesting colonoscopy's mortality benefit was previously overestimated. The stool-test mortality evidence is actually stronger. Start earlier with a family history.

### 20. 每年打流感疫苗 / Get a flu shot every year
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一针约 20 到 50 美元，药房和诊所多为保险覆盖或免费接种。每年秋天打一次。
  Cost: Roughly $20–50 a dose, usually covered by insurance or free at pharmacies and clinics. Once every fall.
- 说人话：心梗之后打流感疫苗的那项试验里，一年内死亡的比例从 4.9% 降到 2.9%。美国 CDC 建议所有 6 个月以上的人每年打流感疫苗，有心血管病的人和老人尤其别落下。
  In plain terms: In the trial that vaccinated people after a heart attack, one-year mortality fell from 4.9% to 2.9%. The US CDC recommends an annual flu shot for everyone 6 months and older—especially people with heart disease and older adults.
- 收益：一项试验在心肌梗死之后做，2571 人随机分组。医生和病人都不知道打的是哪一针。打流感疫苗的人，12 个月里的总死亡率是 2.9%。打安慰剂的是 4.9%。原值 HR 0.59，可信范围 95% CI 0.39 到 0.89。安慰剂就是不含有效成分的对照针。心血管死亡的 HR 是 0.59。另一项研究把多项研究合起来一起算。打流感疫苗的人主要心血管事件为 3.6%，对照为 5.4%。原值 RR 0.66，可信范围 95% CI 0.53 到 0.83。心血管死亡 RR 0.74（95% CI 0.42 到 1.30，这点差别还在误差范围内，算不上有统计学意义）
  Benefit: A trial randomized 2,571 post-heart-attack patients, double-blind. The flu-vaccine group had 2.9% total mortality at 12 months versus 4.9% with placebo (HR 0.59, 95% CI 0.39–0.89); cardiovascular death HR was 0.59. A meta-analysis pooling multiple studies found major cardiovascular events in 3.6% of vaccinated versus 5.4% of controls (RR 0.66, 95% CI 0.53–0.83); cardiovascular death RR 0.74 (95% CI 0.42–1.30, not statistically significant).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Fröbert O 等 (2021). Influenza Vaccination After Myocardial Infarction: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo-Controlled, Multicenter Trial. Circulation. <https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.121.057042> ; Behrouzi B 等 (2022). Association of Influenza Vaccination With Cardiovascular Risk: A Meta-analysis. JAMA Network Open. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2022.8873>；Demicheli V 等 (2018). Vaccines for preventing influenza in the elderly. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD004876.pub4>（备注里说的那份 Cochrane 汇总）
- 备注：争议。A 级只对应有心血管病的人。对本来健康的老年人，Cochrane 综述只给出一个结论：一季流感的发病从 6% 降到 2.4%。它把这条证据的可信度评为「低」，死亡率方面的证据评为「极低」。综述出处是 Demicheli V 等 2018 年那篇。
  Note: Controversy. Grade A applies only to people with cardiovascular disease. For otherwise healthy older adults, the Cochrane review concludes only that one season's flu incidence fell from 6% to 2.4%, rating that evidence "low" and the mortality evidence "very low" (Demicheli et al. 2018).

### 21. 50 岁以后打带状疱疹疫苗（Shingrix） / Get the shingles vaccine (Shingrix) after 50
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：重组带状疱疹疫苗打两针，合计约三四百美元，多数保险覆盖 50 岁以上人群。
  Cost: The recombinant shingles vaccine is two doses, roughly $300–400 total, usually covered by insurance for people 50+.
- 说人话：三期试验里，这支疫苗把带状疱疹挡掉了 97%，各个年龄段都差不多。这笔钱买回来的，是九成七的发病率降幅。
  In plain terms: In phase 3 trials this vaccine blocked 97% of shingles cases, across all age groups. That's what the money buys: a 97% incidence reduction.
- 收益：一项三期试验跨 18 个国家，有 1.54 万人。试验把人随机分成两组，一组打疫苗，一组打对照针，平均跟踪 3.2 年。结果疫苗组 6 例、安慰剂组 210 例发生带状疱疹。换算成每千人年是 0.3 对 9.1，每千人年的意思是 1000 个人观察一年里出现的例数。原文写「疫苗对带状疱疹的总体效力为 97.2%（95% CI 93.7–99.0，P<0.001）」。各年龄组的效力在 96.6% 到 97.9% 之间
  Benefit: A phase 3 trial across 18 countries enrolled 15,400 people, randomized to vaccine or placebo and followed for a mean 3.2 years: 6 shingles cases in the vaccine group versus 210 with placebo—0.3 versus 9.1 per 1,000 person-years. The paper reports overall efficacy of 97.2% (95% CI 93.7–99.0, P<0.001), ranging 96.6%–97.9% across age groups.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Lal H, Cunningham AL, Godeaux O, et al. (2015). Efficacy of an adjuvanted herpes zoster subunit vaccine in older adults. New England Journal of Medicine, 372(22), 2087-2096. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1501184>
- 备注：它在性价比上排得靠后，原因是贵。带状疱疹本身很少要命，麻烦在带状疱疹后神经痛，可能疼上几个月到几年。打完疫苗，打针的地方疼、发烧的比例都不低。其中最重的那一档（三级症状）是 17.0% 对 3.2%。美国 CDC/ACIP 建议 50 岁以上打两针 Shingrix（口径待核实）。
  Note: It ranks lower on value for money because it's expensive. Shingles itself rarely kills; the trouble is postherpetic neuralgia, which can hurt for months to years. Injection-site pain and fever are common after vaccination; the most severe grade of symptoms was 17.0% versus 3.2%. The US CDC/ACIP recommends two doses of Shingrix from age 50 (wording to be verified).
