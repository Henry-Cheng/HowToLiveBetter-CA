### 2. 盯住美国 F-1 新规的官司：「最长四年、读完 30 天内走」原定 2026 年 9 月 15 日生效，前一天被法院暂停，眼下仍是「读完为止」 / Watch the F-1 rule lawsuit: "four years max, leave within 30 days" was to take effect September 15, 2026 — paused by a court the day before; "duration of status" still applies for now
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。把 I-20 上的项目结束日期抄进日历，提前 90 天提醒一次。I-20 是学校发给你的留学资格证明。另外每学期看一次学校国际学生办公室的通知，看这场官司有没有新进展。
  Cost: Free. Copy the program end date from your I-20 into your calendar with a 90-day advance reminder. The I-20 is the school-issued certificate of your student eligibility. Also check your school's international student office notices each semester for developments in this lawsuit.
- 说人话：美国原本要把 F-1 改成最长四年、读完 30 天内走。生效前一天，联邦法院把它暂停了。所以眼下还是老规矩：读多久算多久（duration of status），读完有 60 天准备走人。暂停不是废除，政府还能上诉，规矩随时可能再变。
  In plain terms: The U.S. was about to switch F-1 to a fixed maximum of four years with only 30 days to depart after finishing. A federal court paused it the day before it took effect. So the old rule still applies: duration of status for as long as you study, with 60 days to depart afterward. A pause is not a repeal — the government can appeal, and the rule could change again at any time.
- 收益：国土安全部 2026 年 7 月 17 日公布了一项规则，原定 2026 年 9 月 15 日生效。内容是 F-1 学生不再按「读完为止」入境，改成固定期限，最长四年，到期要另外申请延期。读完或者实习结束后，只留 30 天准备离境。2026 年 9 月 14 日，马萨诸塞州联邦地区法院的 Saylor 法官依《行政程序法》第 705 条，推迟了这项规则的生效。暂停管全国，管整份规则。法院没有撤销这项规则，撤销的请求被驳回，但允许以后再提。案子还在继续审。按法院这道命令，F、J、I 三类签证的人仍按「读完为止」入境，60 天离境宽限期照旧，也不需要另外申请延期。
  Benefit: On July 17, 2026, the Department of Homeland Security published a rule set to take effect September 15, 2026: F-1 students would no longer be admitted for "duration of status" but for a fixed period of at most four years, requiring a separate extension application on expiry, with only 30 days to depart after finishing study or practical training. On September 14, 2026, Judge Saylor of the U.S. District Court for the District of Massachusetts postponed the rule's effective date under Section 705 of the Administrative Procedure Act. The stay applies nationwide, to the entire rule. The court did not vacate the rule — the vacatur request was denied without prejudice to refiling — and the case continues. Under the order, F, J, and I visa holders are still admitted for duration of status, the 60-day departure grace period stands, and no separate extension application is needed.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A

U.S. Department of Homeland Security, No. 1:26-cv-13799-FDS (D. Mass. Sept. 14, 2026)，法院案卷. <https://www.courtlistener.com/docket/74661796/presidents-alliance-on-higher-education-and-immigration-v-united-states/>；Yale Office of International Students & Scholars (2026-09-14). Important Update: Court Action on the D/S Rule. <https://oiss.yale.edu/news/important-update-court-action-on-the-ds-rule>；AILA (2026). - 备注：本条截至 2026 年 9 月 25 日。政府可以向第一巡回上诉法院上诉，国土安全部也可以改好规则重新发布。所以要读好几年的人，别假定老规矩会一直不变。法院定了 2026 年 10 月 2 日再开庭听双方汇报进展，过了那天再看一次学校的通知。新规如果以后生效，要延期就得在期限到期之前另外申请，不会自动续上。老规矩下，提前不读了的，离境期限和读完的不一样，先问学校国际学生办公室。J 签证在同一份规则里，细节和 F-1 不一样，按自己的签证类别去查。
  Note: This item is current as of September 25, 2026. The government may appeal to the First Circuit, and DHS may reissue a revised rule — so anyone studying for several years should not assume the old rule lasts forever. The court set October 2, 2026 for another status hearing; check your school's notices after that date. If the new rule ever takes effect, extensions require a separate application before expiry — nothing renews automatically. Under the old rule, leaving a program early carries different departure deadlines than completing it — ask your school's international student office first. J visas are covered by the same rule but with different details; check your own visa category.

### 3. 美国 F-1 打工的三类许可：校内、 CPT、OPT，红线各不同，超了就是违反身份 / Three kinds of F-1 work authorization in the U.S.: on-campus, CPT, and OPT — each with its own red line; crossing it violates your status
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。自己按周把工时记下来。难在雇主会说「多干几个小时没人查」，这话不能信。
  Cost: Free. Track your own hours weekly. The hard part is ignoring employers who say "a few extra hours, nobody checks" — don't believe it.
- 说人话：F-1 没有通用的打工许可，只有三类。第一类校内打工（on-campus）：学期中每周不超过 20 小时，假期可全职，不用移民局批准，但职位要符合校内打工的定义（开学 30 天前不能开工）。第二类 CPT（Curricular Practical Training，课程实习）：必须是课程的组成部分，DSO（学校指定的国际学生负责人）在 SEVIS 里授权后才能开工，不用等移民局。第三类 OPT（Optional Practical Training，选择性实习）：和专业相关，最长 12 个月，先由 DSO 在 SEVIS 里推荐，再向移民局申请工卡（EAD），拿到卡、到了卡上的开始日期才能开工。STEM 专业毕业可再延 24 个月。红线：CPT 全职满 12 个月，同等学历层次的 OPT 资格就没了；三类里任何一类 unauthorized 的工作，都是违反身份。
  In plain terms: F-1 carries no general work authorization — only three categories. First, on-campus employment: no more than 20 hours per week while school is in session, full-time during breaks; no USCIS approval needed, but the job must qualify as on-campus work (and can't start more than 30 days before classes). Second, CPT (Curricular Practical Training): must be an integral part of your curriculum, authorized by your DSO (Designated School Official) in SEVIS before you start — no USCIS wait. Third, OPT (Optional Practical Training): work related to your major, up to 12 months — your DSO recommends it in SEVIS first, then you apply to USCIS for an EAD (Employment Authorization Document); you may not start until the physical card is in hand and its start date arrives. STEM graduates can extend another 24 months. Red lines: 12 or more months of full-time CPT kills OPT eligibility at the same educational level; any unauthorized work in any category violates your status.
- 收益：校外打工（非 CPT/OPT）要读满一学年 + DSO 认定经济困难 + 移民局批准，门槛很高，别指望。打两份工要合起来算时数。I-765（工卡申请）还在审的时候不能开工。
  Benefit: Off-campus work outside CPT/OPT requires a full academic year completed plus DSO-certified economic hardship plus USCIS approval — a high bar, don't count on it. Hours across two jobs add together. You may not start work while the I-765 (EAD application) is still pending.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：8 CFR 214.2(f)(9)（校内打工学期中每周 20 小时上限；假期可全职）. <http://ice.gov/sevis/schools/reg>；USCIS Policy Manual, Vol. 2, Part F, Ch. 6（校内打工条件；校外打工需读满一学年）. <https://uscis.gov/policy-manual/volume-2-part-f-chapter-6>；DHS Study in the States. F-1 Postsecondary（开学 30 天前可申请校内打工；校外打工需 DSO + 移民局批准）. <https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/guide/f-1/f-1-postsecondary>；SEVP and SEVIS（OPT 最长 12 个月；STEM 可延 24 个月；CPT 需 DSO 授权；全职 CPT 满 12 个月失 OPT 资格）. <https://assist.applyboard.com/hc/en-us/articles/6478379998861-How-SEVP-and-SEVIS-Work-for-International-Students>
- 备注：DSO 是你在学校的第一联系人：开工前先问 DSO 这个职位算不算合规，比事后解释便宜得多。SEVIS 记录被终止，当天所有工作授权一起作废。
  Note: Your DSO is your first point of contact at school — ask whether a position qualifies before starting; it's far cheaper than explaining afterward. If your SEVIS record is terminated, all work authorization dies the same day.

### 4. 身份的根：SEVP 认证学校、I-20、全日制在读，SEVIS fee $350 面签前交 / The root of your status: an SEVP-certified school, the I-20, full-time enrollment — and the $350 SEVIS fee paid before your visa interview
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：SEVIS fee（I-901）F-1 学生 $350，面签前在 fmjfee.com 交，一次交清；换学校转 SEVIS 记录的不用重交。
  Cost: The SEVIS I-901 fee is $350 for F-1 students, paid once at fmjfee.com before the visa interview; transferring your SEVIS record to a new school doesn't require paying again.
- 说人话：F-1 身份是四样东西撑起来的。第一，学校必须是 SEVP（Student and Exchange Visitor Program）认证的——没认证的学校发不了 I-20，申之前先查学校在不在 SEVP 名单里。第二，I-20（Certificate of Eligibility）：学校录取你后发的留学资格证明，上面有你的 SEVIS 号和项目结束日期，面签和入境都要带。第三，SEVIS fee $350：交给国土安全部的，不是签证费（签证费另交），面签至少 3 天前付，打印收据带着。第四，全日制在读 + SEVIS 记录保持 Active：休学、退学、被开除，身份当天就断，打工资格也当天没（见第 3 条）。搬家 10 天内要告诉 DSO 更新 SEVIS（另见第 5 条）。
  In plain terms: F-1 status stands on four things. First, the school must be SEVP (Student and Exchange Visitor Program) certified — uncertified schools can't issue I-20s, so check the SEVP list before applying. Second, the I-20 (Certificate of Eligibility): the school-issued proof of student eligibility after admission, carrying your SEVIS number and program end date — bring it to the visa interview and to entry. Third, the $350 SEVIS fee: paid to Homeland Security, separate from the visa fee, at least 3 days before the interview — print the receipt. Fourth, full-time enrollment with an Active SEVIS record: a leave of absence, withdrawal, or dismissal ends your status the same day, and work authorization dies with it (see item 3). Report address changes to your DSO within 10 days for SEVIS updates (see also item 5).
- 收益：这四样缺一不可，但最容易被忽略的是第一样：交了申请费才发现学校没 SEVP 认证，钱和时间都白花。DSO 管你的 SEVIS 记录，任何变动先问 DSO。
  Benefit: All four are required, but the first is the easiest to overlook: discovering after paying application fees that the school lacks SEVP certification wastes both money and time. Your DSO manages your SEVIS record — ask the DSO before any change.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：ICE. I-901 SEVIS Fee FAQs（F-1 学生 $350；面签前缴纳；fmjfee.com）. <http://www.ice.gov/sevis/i901/faq>；Yale OISS. Paying the SEVIS Fee（$350 一次性；至少提前 3 天；转学不用重交）. <https://oiss.yale.edu/getting-started/new-students/applying-for-your-us-visa/applying-for-an-f-1-visa/paying-the-sevis-fee>；DHS Study in the States. Working in the United States（F-1 打工资格以 SEVIS Active 为前提）. <https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/students/work/working-in-the-united-states>
- 备注：课业压力大想减课之前先问 DSO：减到多少就不算全日制了。这一条比打工更早出事。
  Note: Before cutting back on courses under academic pressure, ask your DSO where the full-time line is. This trips people up earlier than work rules do.

### 5. 美国：搬家后 10 天内必须报新地址 / In the U.S.: report a new address within 10 days of moving
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。在线提交一次改址，十分钟。
  Cost: Free. One online address update, ten minutes.
- 说人话：在美国换了住的地方，法律要求 10 天之内通知移民局（USCIS），交 AR-11 表。不报不是小事：一来它本身违法，二来移民局寄给你的信会寄丢。F-1 学生还要另外让 DSO 更新 SEVIS 里的地址，两边都要报。
  In plain terms: After moving in the U.S., the law requires notifying USCIS within 10 days via Form AR-11. Skipping it isn't trivial: it's a legal violation in itself, and USCIS mail to you will go astray. F-1 students must additionally have their DSO update the address in SEVIS — both filings are required.
- 收益：8 CFR 265.1 写的是「按法律要登记的外国人」，F-1 在内。十分钟的事，别拖。
  Benefit: 8 CFR 265.1 covers "aliens required to be registered" — F-1 students included. Ten minutes; don't put it off.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：8 CFR 265.1. <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-265/section-265.1>；USCIS. AR-11, Alien's Change of Address Card. <https://www.uscis.gov/ar-11>
- 备注：报了移民局，学校那边的 SEVIS 记录还要另外更新，别只报一边。
  Note: Filing with USCIS doesn't update SEVIS — update the school-side record separately.
