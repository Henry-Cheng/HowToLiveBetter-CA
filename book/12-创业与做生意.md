# 12. 创业与做生意 / Starting and Running a Business

口径：金钱。/ Scope: Money.

本节口径是金钱，换回的是「别亏光」和「别被罚」。在美国做生意，税务、雇佣、许可三件事是刚性的：加州 LLC 每年 800 美元的特许经营税没收入也照交（见第 12 条），雇第一个人就要买工伤保险（见第 16 条），卖食品、卖酒、做金融，证没下来就开张等于把自己送上门（见第 7 条）。本节 24 条，按创业的真实顺序排：先想清楚亏不亏得起，再选主体、注册、拿证，然后是税务、雇人、进货、退场。

This section is about money — what buys you "don't lose everything" and "don't get fined." Doing business in the U.S. has three rigid pillars: taxes, employment, and licensing. A California LLC owes $800 a year in franchise tax even with zero revenue (see item 12); hiring your first employee triggers mandatory workers' comp insurance (see item 16); selling food, alcohol, or financial products without the right license is volunteering for enforcement (see item 7). These 24 entries follow the real order of starting up: first decide whether you can afford the loss, then pick an entity, register, get licensed, then handle taxes, hiring, sourcing, and exit.

> 本节主题分组：① 亏得起再上：第 1–2 条（资金与担保）；② 选主体与注册：第 3–6 条；③ 证照与行业红线：第 7–11 条（食品专 4 条）；④ 税务：第 12–14 条；⑤ 钱进钱出：第 15 条；⑥ 雇人：第 16–17 条；⑦ 进货与生产：第 18–21 条；⑧ 退场：第 22–24 条。

> Section map: ① Only play what you can lose: items 1–2 (capital and guarantees); ② Entity choice and registration: items 3–6; ③ Licenses and industry red lines: items 7–11 (four food-specific); ④ Taxes: items 12–14; ⑤ Money in and out: item 15; ⑥ Hiring: items 16–17; ⑦ Sourcing and production: items 18–21; ⑧ Exit: items 22–24.

### 1. 只拿亏得起的钱创业，不拿买房首付、孩子学费、养老钱去赌 / Only Risk Money You Can Afford to Lose — Never Gamble the House Down Payment, the Kids' Tuition, or Retirement Savings
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是把创业资金和家庭资金物理隔开：单独开一个商业银行账户，创业的钱只从这里出
  Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is physically separating startup money from family money: open a separate business bank account and let startup spending come only from there.
- 收益：美国没有"有限公司"替你挡住一切：个人独资（sole proprietorship）和普通合伙（general partnership）是无限责任，债主可以直接追你的个人财产；LLC 和公司虽然有限责任，但你签了个人担保（personal guarantee，见第 2 条）等于自己把盾牌摘了。更狠的是加州的夫妻共同财产制（community property, Family Code §760）：婚姻存续期间挣的钱、买的资产都是共同财产，一方创业欠的债，共同财产要拿来还（§910）。所以"亏得起"的标准是：这笔钱全亏光，房贷不断供、孩子学费有着落、退休账户不动。
  Benefit: In the U.S., a "limited liability" entity doesn't shield everything: sole proprietorships and general partnerships carry unlimited liability — creditors can go straight after your personal assets; LLCs and corporations limit liability, but signing a personal guarantee (see item 2) is taking the shield off yourself. Worse, California is a community-property state (Family Code §760): earnings and assets acquired during the marriage belong to both spouses, and the community estate is liable for debts either spouse incurs (§910). So the "can afford to lose" test is: if this money goes to zero, the mortgage still gets paid, the kids' tuition is covered, and retirement accounts are untouched.
- 说人话：个人独资和普通合伙亏了，债主直接拿你的房子车子；结了婚的，配偶的共同财产也跑不掉。创业的钱全亏光还不影响房贷和学费，才叫"亏得起"。
  In plain terms: With a sole proprietorship or general partnership, creditors take your house and car; if you're married, the community property is on the line too. "Affordable to lose" means: even if it's all gone, the mortgage and tuition are safe.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Cal. Fam. Code §760 (community property defined). <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=760.&lawCode=FAM>; §910 (community estate liable for either spouse's debts). <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=910.&lawCode=FAM>; Cal. Corp. Code §17703.04 (LLC members not personally liable solely as members). <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=17703.04.&lawCode=CORP>
- 备注：有限责任不是免死金牌：公私账混用、资本严重不足，法院可以"刺破面纱"（alter ego / piercing the veil）让你个人还债。另见第 3 条。
  Note: Limited liability isn't a magic shield: commingling funds or gross undercapitalization lets courts "pierce the veil" (alter ego) and make you pay personally. See also item 3.

### 2. 不给公司贷款签个人担保，房东要担保就换房东 / Don't Personally Guarantee the Company's Loans; If the Landlord Demands One, Find Another Landlord
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难在银行、房东、SBA 贷款都爱要个人担保，拒绝可能意味着租不到那个铺面、贷不到那笔款
  Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is that banks, landlords, and SBA lenders all love personal guarantees — refusing can mean losing that space or that loan.
- 收益：个人担保（personal guarantee）的意思是：公司还不上，你个人还，债主不用先告公司，可以直接告你、查封你的个人资产。SBA 贷款通常要求占股 20% 以上的股东签担保；商业租约里房东也常塞这一条。签了之后，第 1 条说的有限责任就白搭了：公司破产，担保不跟着破产，你还得还。配偶一起签的，共同财产（community property）也搭进去。能谈的只有两条：把担保金额封顶（cap），或者约定公司连续盈利几年后担保自动解除。
  Benefit: A personal guarantee means: if the company can't pay, you pay — the creditor doesn't have to sue the company first; it can sue you directly and seize your personal assets. SBA loans typically require shareholders with 20%+ ownership to guarantee; commercial leases often bury one in the fine print. Once signed, the limited liability from item 1 is moot: the company's bankruptcy doesn't discharge your guarantee — you still owe. If your spouse co-signs, the community property goes in too. The only negotiable points are capping the guarantee amount and having it automatically release after the company stays profitable for a set number of years.
- 说人话：签了个人担保，公司欠的钱就是你欠的钱，公司破产你照样还。能不签就不签，非签不可就谈封顶金额和自动解除条款。
  In plain terms: Sign a personal guarantee and the company's debt is your debt — the company's bankruptcy doesn't save you. Don't sign if you can avoid it; if you must, negotiate a cap and an automatic-release clause.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：U.S. Small Business Administration. Personal guarantees and SBA loans. <https://www.sba.gov/>

### 3. 开张前选对主体：个人独资最省事但无限责任，LLC 是小生意的默认答案 / Pick the Right Entity Before You Open: Sole Proprietorship Is Easiest but Unlimited; an LLC Is the Default Answer for Small Business
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：个人独资（sole proprietorship）零注册费；LLC 向加州州务卿（Secretary of State）交 Articles of Organization（LLC-1），注册费 70 美元，之后每年 800 美元特许经营税（见第 12 条）。花几小时想清楚加填表
  Cost: A sole proprietorship costs nothing to start; an LLC files Articles of Organization (LLC-1) with the California Secretary of State, $70 filing fee, plus $800 a year in franchise tax (see item 12). A few hours of thinking and form-filling.
- 收益：个人独资：不用注册、报税简单（收入直接进个人税表 Schedule C），但无限责任——生意欠的钱就是你欠的钱。普通合伙（general partnership）：两人以上默认就是它，合伙人连带无限责任，一个合伙人闯祸大家一起赔。LLC：成员仅以成员身份不承担公司债务（Corp Code §17703.04(a)），税务上默认穿透（不用交公司层面的所得税），是小生意的默认选择。公司（corporation）：C-corp 双重征税，S-corp 穿透但股东人数和身份限制多，融资、发期权才用得上。记住：选了 LLC 也要签 operating agreement（运营协议），公私账分开，否则有限责任被刺破（见第 1 条备注）。
  Benefit: Sole proprietorship: no registration, simple taxes (income flows to your personal return on Schedule C), but unlimited liability — the business's debts are your debts. General partnership: two or more people default into it, with joint and several unlimited liability — one partner's mess is everyone's mess. LLC: members aren't personally liable for company debts solely as members (Corp. Code §17703.04(a)), and taxes pass through by default (no corporate-level income tax) — the default choice for small business. Corporation: C-corp faces double taxation; S-corp passes through but has shareholder limits — only worth it when raising capital or granting options. Remember: even with an LLC, sign an operating agreement and keep business and personal money separate, or the liability shield gets pierced (see the note in item 1).
- 说人话：一个人小本生意，默认选 LLC：注册 70 美元，每年 800 美元税，债务不追到你个人。个人独资省了注册费，代价是无限责任。合伙做生意不签协议，默认就是无限连带责任。
  In plain terms: For a one-person small business, the default is an LLC: $70 to register, $800 a year in tax, debts don't follow you personally. A sole proprietorship saves the registration fee at the price of unlimited liability. Partnering without an agreement defaults to joint and several unlimited liability.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Corp. Code §17703.04(a) (member not personally liable solely as member). <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=17703.04.&lawCode=CORP>; Cal. Secretary of State. Form LLC-1, Articles of Organization ($70 filing fee). <https://bizfile.sos.ca.gov/>; IRS. Sole Proprietorships (Schedule C). <https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/sole-proprietorships>

### 4. 不当挂名股东、挂名法人：名字借出去，责任跟过来 / Don't Be a Nominee Owner or Officer: Lend Your Name, Inherit the Liability
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不占时间。有人请你"挂个名"，直接拒绝就行
  Cost: Costs nothing and takes no time. If someone asks you to "just put your name on it," just say no.
- 收益：美国 LLC 没有"股东"概念，但有成员（member）、经理（manager）、签字人：你在文件上签了字，法律上你就是负责人。公司欠税，国税局可以向"管钱发工资的人"（responsible person）个人追讨代扣的工资税；公司违法，签字的高管、成员按实际控制人追责。替朋友的公司挂名，等于把第 1 条说的无限责任亲手领回来，还附赠税务连带。朋友跑路，账单和传票写的是你的名字。
  Benefit: A U.S. LLC has no "shareholders," but it has members, managers, and signers: if you signed the documents, legally you are the responsible party. If the company owes payroll taxes, the IRS can pursue the "responsible person" — the one who controlled the money and payroll — personally for withheld taxes; if the company breaks the law, signing officers and members are pursued as persons in control. Lending your name to a friend's company is volunteering for the unlimited liability of item 1, plus tax liability as a bonus. When your friend disappears, the bills and subpoenas carry your name.
- 说人话：名字签在文件上，税单和传票就找你。朋友跑路，你替他还税、替他被告。挂名这事，报酬是人情，代价是无限责任。
  In plain terms: Sign your name on the documents and the tax bills and subpoenas come to you. Your friend vanishes; you pay his taxes and answer his lawsuits. The pay for being a nominee is a favor; the price is unlimited liability.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：IRS. Trust Fund Recovery Penalty / responsible persons for withheld employment taxes. <https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/trust-fund-recovery-penalty-tfrp>
- 备注：本条基于"签字即负责人"的一般法律原则，证据等级为 C。LLC 的成员信息在加州州务卿处是公开记录，挂名藏不住。
  Note: This entry rests on the general legal principle that signing makes you the responsible party — hence grade C. LLC member information is public record at the California Secretary of State; a nominee arrangement hides nothing.

### 5. 加盟前先看 FDD（特许经营披露文件），重点看第 19、20 项 / Before Buying a Franchise, Read the FDD — Focus on Items 19 and 20
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。花几个小时读完 FDD（Franchise Disclosure Document），重点看财务表现和关店数据
  Cost: Costs nothing. Spend a few hours reading the FDD (Franchise Disclosure Document), focusing on financial performance and closure data.
- 收益：联邦 FTC《特许经营规则》（Franchise Rule, 16 CFR Part 436）要求：总部必须在你签约或交钱至少 14 个日历天之前，把 FDD 交到你手上。FDD 有 23 项，必看三项：第 19 项（financial performance representations）——总部敢不敢把加盟店的真实营收、利润写进来，不敢写的要警惕；第 20 项（outlets）——开了多少、关了多少、转让了多少，关店率高的别碰；第 3、4 项——总部有没有诉讼和破产史。加州另有《特许经营投资法》（Franchise Investment Law, Corp Code §31000 起）：在加州卖特许经营，总部必须先向加州金融保护与创新局（DFPI）注册。没给你 FDD 就催你交钱的，转头就走。
  Benefit: The federal FTC Franchise Rule (16 CFR Part 436) requires the franchisor to hand you the FDD at least 14 calendar days before you sign or pay. The FDD has 23 items; three are must-reads: Item 19 (financial performance representations) — whether the franchisor dares to put real franchisee revenue and profit in writing; beware if it won't; Item 20 (outlets) — how many opened, closed, or transferred; walk away from high closure rates; Items 3–4 — the franchisor's litigation and bankruptcy history. California adds its own Franchise Investment Law (Corp. Code §31000 et seq.): selling franchises in California requires the franchisor to register with the DFPI (Department of Financial Protection and Innovation) first. If they pressure you to pay before giving you the FDD, walk away.
- 说人话：加盟先看 FDD：总部 14 天前必须给你；第 19 项看真实赚钱数据，第 20 项看关店率。在加州卖加盟，总部必须先在 DFPI 注册过。没 FDD 就催交钱的，是骗子。
  In plain terms: Read the FDD before buying a franchise: the franchisor must give it to you 14 days ahead; Item 19 shows real earnings data, Item 20 shows closure rates. Selling franchises in California requires prior DFPI registration. Anyone demanding money without an FDD is a scammer.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：FTC Franchise Rule, 16 CFR Part 436 (§436.2: FDD delivery at least 14 calendar days before signing/payment). <https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/federal-register-notices/franchise-rule>; Cal. Corp. Code §§31000 et seq. (Franchise Investment Law; registration with DFPI). <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displayText.xhtml?division=5.&part=1.&lawCode=CORP&title=4>

### 6. 注册前把名称、地址、经营范围定下来，一次跑完州务卿、IRS、县、市 / Before Registering, Lock Down the Name, Address, and Business Purpose — Then Do the Secretary of State, IRS, County, and City in One Pass
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：LLC 注册费 70 美元（州务卿），Statement of Information 每两年 20 美元；DBA（fictitious business name）县登记费几十美元加登报费；EIN（雇主识别号）IRS 免费；seller's permit（销售许可）CDTFA 免费；市 business license 各市自定（几十到几百美元）。花一天跑流程
  Cost: LLC registration $70 (Secretary of State); Statement of Information $20 every two years; DBA (fictitious business name) county filing a few tens of dollars plus newspaper-publication fees; EIN free from the IRS; seller's permit free from CDTFA; city business license varies by city (tens to hundreds of dollars). One day to run the process.
- 收益：在加州开 LLC 的标准流程：① 州务卿网站（bizfile.sos.ca.gov）查名、提交 Articles of Organization（LLC-1，70 美元），指定注册代理人（registered agent）；② IRS 官网免费申请 EIN（千万别花钱找代办）；③ 90 天内提交初始 Statement of Information，之后每两年 20 美元；④ 起的名字和 LLC 法定名不一样（比如招牌名），去县 clerk 登记 fictitious business name（DBA），45 天内在县内报纸每周登一次、连登 4 周（B&P §17917），DBA 有效期 5 年；⑤ 卖有形商品的，去 CDTFA 免费办 seller's permit（代收销售税用）；⑥ 去市里办 business license。顺序别反：先有 LLC 和 EIN，再去开商业银行账户。
  Benefit: The standard California LLC process: ① check the name and file Articles of Organization (LLC-1, $70) on the Secretary of State site (bizfile.sos.ca.gov), designating a registered agent; ② get an EIN free from the IRS website (never pay a middleman); ③ file the initial Statement of Information within 90 days, then $20 every two years; ④ if your trade name differs from the LLC's legal name, register a fictitious business name (DBA) with the county clerk, then publish once a week for 4 consecutive weeks in a county newspaper within 45 days (B&P §17917); a DBA lasts 5 years; ⑤ if you sell tangible goods, get a free seller's permit from CDTFA (for collecting sales tax); ⑥ get a city business license. Don't reverse the order: LLC and EIN first, then open the business bank account.
- 说人话：加州开 LLC：州务卿交 70 美元注册，IRS 免费拿 EIN，招牌名和注册名不一样要去县里登记 DBA 并登报 4 周，卖东西的去 CDTFA 免费办销售许可，最后去市里办营业执照。全套自己跑，一天能办完。
  In plain terms: Opening an LLC in California: $70 to the Secretary of State, free EIN from the IRS, a DBA filing plus 4 weeks of newspaper publication at the county if your sign name differs from the legal name, a free seller's permit from CDTFA if you sell goods, then a city business license. Do it all yourself in a day.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Secretary of State. Business Entities — bizfile. <https://bizfile.sos.ca.gov/>; IRS. Apply for an Employer Identification Number (EIN) Online (free). <https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/apply-for-an-employer-identification-number-ein-online>; Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §17917 (DBA publication: once a week for 4 weeks). <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=17917.&lawCode=BPC>; CDTFA. Seller's permit registration. <https://www.cdtfa.ca.gov/services/>

### 7. 要许可证的行当，证没下来不开张 / If the Business Needs a License, Don't Open Without It
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：各行业牌照费从几十到几千美元不等，审批几周到几个月。难在等证期间房租照交
  Cost: License fees run from tens to thousands of dollars depending on the industry; approvals take weeks to months. The hard part is paying rent while you wait.
- 收益：无证经营在美国是按行业单独立法的：卖酒要 ABC（Alcoholic Beverage Control）牌照；开餐馆要县卫生局的 health permit；搞装修要 CSLB（Contractors State License Board）执照，无照接 500 美元以上的工程在加州是轻罪；做金融、保险、医疗，各有各的牌照。无证经营的通用后果：罚款、停业，严重的按轻罪办（县监加罚款）。开业前先去加州消费者事务局（DCA）或县里查清你的行当要什么证，证下来再开张。
  Benefit: Unlicensed operation in the U.S. is legislated industry by industry: selling alcohol needs an ABC (Alcoholic Beverage Control) license; restaurants need a county health permit; contractors need a CSLB (Contractors State License Board) license — taking a job over $500 unlicensed in California is a misdemeanor; finance, insurance, and healthcare each have their own licenses. The generic consequences of operating unlicensed: fines, shutdown orders, and misdemeanor charges (county jail plus fines) in serious cases. Before opening, check with the California Department of Consumer Affairs (DCA) or your county to find out exactly what your trade requires — open only after the license lands.
- 说人话：卖酒、开餐馆、搞装修、做金融，证没下来就开张，轻则罚款停业，重则轻罪（坐牢加罚款）。开业前先查清要什么证。
  In plain terms: Selling alcohol, running a restaurant, contracting, doing finance — opening without the license means fines and shutdown at best, misdemeanor charges (jail plus fines) at worst. Find out exactly which licenses you need before opening.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code §7028 (unlicensed contracting over $500 a misdemeanor). <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=7028.&lawCode=BPC>; Cal. Dept. of Alcoholic Beverage Control. License types. <https://www.abc.ca.gov/licensing/license-types/>
- 备注：各行业牌照的罚款数额差异大，本条只给通用结论，具体数额动手前查发证机关官网。
  Note: Fine amounts vary widely by industry; this entry gives only the general rule — check the issuing agency's site for exact figures before acting.

The proper three-step exit: ① file a Certificate of Cancellation with the Secretary of State (short-form cancellation if formed within 12 months with no business activity); ② file final returns with the FTB and pay outstanding taxes; ③ then close the bank account and distribute remaining assets. Don't reverse the order: close the account first and you'll have no money when the tax bill lands. If debts can't be paid, individuals file Chapter 7 (liquidation) or Chapter 13 (wage-earner plan); companies file Chapter 7 or Chapter 11 (reorganization). Settle employee wages before dissolving (see item 17) — unpaid wages can follow the owner personally.
- 说人话：不注销 LLC，每年 800 美元一直欠着，FTB 按月加罚金。退场顺序：州务卿注销、FTB 报最后一年税缴清、再关账户。员工工资先结清。
  In plain terms: Never cancel the LLC and the $800 a year keeps piling up, with monthly FTB penalties. Exit in order: cancel with the Secretary of State, file final FTB returns and pay up, then close the account. Settle employee wages first.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：CA Franchise Tax Board. Dissolve / cancel an LLC; final returns required. <https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/limited-liability-company/index.html>; Cal. Secretary of State. Cancel LLC (Certificate of Cancellation). <https://bizfile.sos.ca.gov/>
- 备注：待核实：简易注销（short-form cancellation）的表格编号和适用条件，动手前查州务卿官网。破产各章节的选择请咨询破产律师。
  Note: To be verified: the short-form cancellation form number and eligibility — check the Secretary of State site before acting. Consult a bankruptcy attorney on which chapter fits.

### 24. 花钱请人代运营网店、"无货源"之前先问清：承诺保本保收益的，转头就走 / Before Paying for Store Management or "No-Inventory" Schemes: Anyone Promising Guaranteed Returns — Walk Away
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难在对方的话术专门打"躺赚"心理
  Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is that their pitch is engineered for the "passive income" fantasy.
- 收益："交 5 万代运营、保证月入 2 万""无货源一件代发、稳赚不赔"——承诺保本保收益的，按电汇欺诈（18 U.S.C. §1343，最高 20 年）或州诈骗罪办；FTC 和加州总检察长常年打击这类"商业机会"骗局（business opportunity scams），FTC 的《商业机会规则》要求卖方提前披露诉讼史、退款政策等，拿不出来的就是骗子。识别方法：要你先交大额"服务费""保证金"的；收益承诺写进合同却不敢写退款条款的；公司刚成立、查不到实际经营地址的。被骗了：向 FTC（reportfraud.ftc.gov）、加州总检察长（oag.ca.gov）举报，金额大的请律师走民事欺诈索赔。
  Benefit: "Pay $50,000 for store management, guaranteed $20,000 a month" / "no-inventory dropshipping, can't lose" — guaranteed-return promises are prosecuted as wire fraud (18 U.S.C. §1343, up to 20 years) or state fraud; the FTC and the California Attorney General routinely target these "business opportunity" scams, and the FTC's Business Opportunity Rule requires sellers to disclose litigation history, refund policies, and more up front — if they can't produce it, they're scammers. Tells: demands for large upfront "service fees" or "deposits"; earnings promises in the pitch but no refund clause in the contract; a newly formed company with no verifiable business address. If scammed: report to the FTC (reportfraud.ftc.gov) and the California AG (oag.ca.gov); for large amounts, get a lawyer for civil fraud claims.
- 说人话：承诺保本保收益的代运营，100% 是骗子：金额大了按电汇欺诈最高 20 年。先交大额服务费、不敢写退款条款的，转头就走。被骗了向 FTC 和加州总检察长举报。
  In plain terms: "Guaranteed returns" store-management schemes are 100% scams: at scale they're wire fraud, up to 20 years. Large upfront fees plus no refund clause in writing — walk away. If scammed, report to the FTC and the California AG.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
