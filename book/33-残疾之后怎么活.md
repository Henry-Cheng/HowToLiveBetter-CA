# 33. 残疾之后怎么活·加州版

这一节写的是残疾已经发生之后的日子。怎么防住致残，在第 1 节和第 13 节（原书第 1 节、原书第 13 节），这里不重复。本章的法律与制度内容按加州和美国联邦现行规定写：核心是《美国残疾人法案》（ADA, Americans with Disabilities Act, 42 U.S.C. §12101 起），它禁止公共场所、就业和政府服务中的残疾歧视；收入保障走联邦社保署（SSA）的 SSDI（残疾保险金，须有工作积分）和 SSI（生活补助金，低收入低资产，2026 年联邦最高个人每月 $994，加州另有州补贴）；加州的服务入口是 21 家 regional center（发育障碍，Lanterman Act）和 Department of Rehabilitation（职业康复，dor.ca.gov）。健康科学类条目的证据和数字沿用原书。

*This section is about life after disability has happened. Preventing disability is covered in Sections 1 and 13 (original book, Sections 1 and 13) and not repeated here. The legal and institutional content follows current California and U.S. federal rules: the core is the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA, 42 U.S.C. §12101 et seq.), which bans disability discrimination in public places, employment, and government services; income support runs through the Social Security Administration's SSDI (disability insurance, requires work credits) and SSI (Supplemental Security Income for low income and assets — 2026 federal maximum $994/month for an individual, plus a California state supplement); California's service entry points are the 21 regional centers (developmental disabilities, under the Lanterman Act) and the Department of Rehabilitation (vocational rehabilitation, dor.ca.gov). Health-science items keep the original book's evidence and numbers.*

四种口径分开算，不互相换算。急症和并发症那几条按死亡率算。补贴、待遇、打官司那几条按金钱算。上学、办事那几条按时间算。精神障碍住院、行为能力和监护那几条按人身自由算。

*Four yardsticks are kept separate and never converted into each other. Emergency and complication items are judged by mortality. Benefits, subsidies, and lawsuits by money. Schooling and paperwork by time. Psychiatric hospitalization, legal capacity, and guardianship by personal freedom.*

世界卫生组织的说明书给了三个数字。全球约有 13 亿人存在显著功能障碍，占世界人口的 16%，大约每六个人里有一个。部分残疾人比没有残疾的人早死最多 20 年。残疾人得抑郁、哮喘、糖尿病、卒中、肥胖或者口腔健康不良的风险是两倍。这些差距里，很大一部分不是残疾本身造成的，是看不上病、上不了学、找不到工作、出不了门造成的。所以这一节的条目，一半是身体，一半是手续和权利。

*The WHO fact sheet gives three numbers: about 1.3 billion people worldwide live with significant disability — 16% of the world population, roughly one in six. Some people with disabilities die up to 20 years earlier than those without. Their risk of depression, asthma, diabetes, stroke, obesity, or poor oral health is doubled. Much of that gap isn't caused by the disability itself but by being shut out of care, school, work, and public life. So half of this section's items are about the body, half about paperwork and rights.*

已经写在别处的五件事，这里只指路，不重复。美国没有中国的"残疾人证"制度，伤残评定走各系统的自有标准：工伤走各州 workers' compensation（加州见原书第 19 节对应的 workers' comp 内容，原书第 19 节）；退伍军人走 VA 评级；SSDI/SSI 由 SSA 按自己的医疗标准认定（见本节第 7 条）。残疾人两项补贴对应的是 SSI 和加州州补贴（见本节第 7 条）。重度失能的长期照护对应 Medi-Cal 的居家社区服务（见第 17 节，原书第 17 节）。

*Five topics covered elsewhere are only signposted here. The U.S. has no equivalent of China's disability certificate; each system rates disability by its own rules: workers' comp handles work injuries (California workers' comp corresponds to original Section 19); veterans go through VA ratings; SSA applies its own medical criteria for SSDI/SSI (see item 7). The counterpart of China's "two subsidies" is SSI plus California's state supplement (see item 7). Long-term care for severe disability runs through Medi-Cal home- and community-based services (see Section 17, original book, Section 17).*

本节大部分条目的受益人是你自己和家人，属于最高的两档。涉及别人的地方在备注里单独写。

*Most items in this section benefit you and your family directly — the top two tiers. Where others are affected, the notes say so separately.*

本节条目按主题分成下面几块，括号里是条号。

*Items are grouped by theme below, with item numbers in parentheses.*

**身体和康复**：脊髓损伤后突然剧烈头痛（第 1 条），轮椅坐垫换成减压型（第 5 条），别买能治好瘫痪的疗法（第 6 条），在正规机构做康复（第 16 条），听力下降配助听器（第 17 条）。

***Body and rehabilitation:** sudden severe headache after spinal cord injury (item 1), switching to a pressure-relief wheelchair cushion (item 5), don't buy "cures" for paralysis (item 6), rehab at a proper facility (item 16), hearing aids for hearing loss (item 17).*

**待遇和补贴**：去独立生活中心把待遇一次问全（第 7 条），残疾孩子申请 Early Start 和特殊教育（第 8 条），坡道扶手浴室改造的几条路（第 9 条），报税用好残疾税收优惠（第 11 条）。

***Benefits and subsidies:** getting the full benefits checklist at a Center for Independent Living (item 7), Early Start and special education for disabled children (item 8), options for ramp/grab-bar/bathroom modifications (item 9), disability-related tax breaks (item 11).*

**找工作和维权**：求职时主动说明残疾状况（第 10 条），因残疾被歧视可以投诉和告（第 20 条）。

***Work and rights:** disclosing disability when job hunting (item 10), complaining and suing over disability discrimination (item 20).*

**上学和出行**：带服务犬进公共场所（第 12 条），标化考试申请考试便利（第 13 条），学校不得拒收残疾孩子（第 14 条），下肢残疾也能考驾照（第 15 条）。

***School and mobility:** service dogs in public places (item 12), testing accommodations for standardized exams (item 13), schools can't turn away disabled children (item 14), driver's licenses with lower-limb disability (item 15).*

**心理和照护者**：致残后头十年的自杀风险（第 2 条），照护者顾好自己的身体（第 4 条）。

***Mind and caregivers:** suicide risk in the first decade after disability (item 2), caregivers looking after their own health (item 4).*

**精神障碍和监护**：精神障碍住院以自愿为原则（第 3 条），认定行为能力要向法院申请 conservatorship（第 18 条），监护人按法定顺序定（第 19 条）。

***Psychiatric hospitalization and guardianship:** voluntary admission as the rule (item 3), court petition required to declare incapacity — conservatorship (item 18), statutory order for appointing a conservator (item 19).*

### 1. 脊髓损伤之后突然剧烈头痛、出汗，先扶坐直、松开衣物，同时打 911 / After spinal cord injury: sudden severe headache and sweating — sit the person up, loosen clothing, call 911
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。三个动作，几十秒做完。
  Cost: Free. Three moves, under a minute.
- 说人话：脊髓损伤的人，身体的报警线路断了一截。膀胱憋着、衣服勒着，本人可能没感觉，血压却会猛往上冲。信号是突然剧烈头痛和出汗。这时先把人扶坐直、松开勒紧的衣物、看是不是憋着尿，同时打 911。
  In plain terms: After spinal cord injury, the body's alarm wiring is partly cut. A full bladder or tight clothing may go unfelt while blood pressure spikes dangerously. The signal is sudden severe headache plus sweating. Sit the person upright, loosen tight clothing, check whether they need to urinate — and call 911 at the same time.
- 收益：这种发作叫自主神经反射异常（autonomic dysreflexia）。一篇系统综述纳入 31 项研究，其中 6 项是随机对照试验。综述写明，发作当时的非药物处理是三步：把患者摆成直立体位、松开紧身衣物、去除诱发刺激。这三步的依据是临床共识和生理学数据，属于第 5 级证据。血压持续升高时才用降压药，其中哌唑嗪有第 1 级证据，硝苯地平和前列腺素 E2 有第 2 级证据
  Benefit: This is called autonomic dysreflexia. A systematic review of 31 studies (6 RCTs) states the on-the-spot non-drug response has three steps: upright positioning, loosening tight clothing, and removing the trigger. Those steps rest on clinical consensus and physiological data (level-5 evidence). Blood-pressure drugs are only for sustained hypertension — prazosin has level-1 evidence, nifedipine and prostaglandin E2 level-2.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Krassioukov A, Warburton DE, Teasell R, Eng JJ; Spinal Cord Injury Rehabilitation Evidence Research Team (2009). A systematic review of the management of autonomic dysreflexia after spinal cord injury. Archives of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation, 90(4):682-695. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apmr.2008.10.017>
- 备注：定 B 是因为那三个现场动作背后没有随机对照试验，只有共识和生理学推理。用药那一段是医生的事，家里不要自己给降压药。让人躺平会让血压更高，所以要扶坐直。家属和护工也要会这三步。别把它当普通头痛扛过去。/ Note: Graded B because the three on-the-spot moves have no RCTs behind them — only consensus and physiology. Drugs are the doctor's call; don't give blood-pressure medication at home. Lying flat raises blood pressure further, hence sitting upright. Family and caregivers should know the three steps too. Don't tough it out as an ordinary headache.

### 2. 致残之后的头十年是自杀的高风险期，别当成「想开点」就行的事 / The first decade after disability is a high-risk period for suicide — don't treat it as a matter of attitude
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。打一个电话，或者挂一次号。
  Cost: Free. One phone call, or one appointment.
- 说人话：刚残疾那几年，自己或者家人情绪垮掉，是常见的，不是意志力的问题。英国两家脊髓损伤中心的长期随访发现，这群人的自杀率是普通人的五倍。九成以上的自杀发生在受伤后的头十年。受伤原因本身就是自杀的人，风险还要高出一截。有念头就打 988（电话或短信都行）。
  In plain terms: Emotional collapse in the first years after disability — yours or a family member's — is common, not a failure of willpower. Long-term follow-up at two UK spinal cord injury centers found a suicide rate five times the general population's, with over 90% of suicides in the first ten years after injury. Those injured in a suicide attempt carry even higher risk. If the thoughts come, call or text 988.
- 收益：研究跟踪了 1991 到 2010 年新入院、熬过伤后第一年、出院时有神经功能缺损的 2304 例外伤性脊髓损伤患者，随访到 2014 年底。其中 63 例（2.7%）的受伤原因本身就是自杀未遂。到随访结束共 533 人死亡，其中 4.2% 死于自杀，91% 的自杀发生在受伤后的头十年。按年龄标化的自杀死亡率是每年每 10 万人 62.5 例（95% CI 36.4–88.6），是 2014 年英格兰和威尔士普通人群自杀率（每 10 万人 12.2 例）的五倍
  Benefit: A cohort of 2,304 traumatic spinal cord injury patients admitted 1991–2010 who survived the first year with neurological deficit, followed through 2014. Of these, 63 (2.7%) were injured in a suicide attempt. By follow-up's end 533 had died; 4.2% by suicide, 91% of suicides within the first ten years. Age-standardized suicide mortality was 62.5 per 100,000 per year (95% CI 36.4–88.6) — five times the 2014 general-population rate for England and Wales (12.2 per 100,000).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Savic G, DeVivo MJ, Frankel HL, Jamous MA, Soni BM, Charlifue S (2018). Suicide and traumatic spinal cord injury - a cohort study. Spinal Cord, 56(1):2-6. <https://doi.org/10.1038/sc.2017.98>
- 备注：这项研究只看了脊髓损伤，套到失明、截肢、烧伤这些别的致残原因上属于往外推。它能说明的是时间窗：风险不是出院就过去了，要盯十年。要人陪着说话打 988，见第 29 节第 11 条。家里别囤安眠药和武器，见第 1 节第 25 条（原书第 1 节）。哀伤和情绪卡住半年还在原地，去看精神科或心理医生，见第 29 节第 8 条。/ Note: The study covered spinal cord injury only; extending it to blindness, amputation, or burns is extrapolation. Its point is the time window: risk doesn't end at discharge — watch for ten years. To have someone to talk to, call 988 (see Section 29, item 11). Don't stockpile sleeping pills or weapons at home (see Section 1, item 25 — original book, Section 1). If grief or mood stays stuck after six months, see a psychiatrist or psychologist (see Section 29, item 8).

### 3. 精神障碍住院以自愿为原则；加州非自愿留院走 5150/5250/5270 三档程序 / Psychiatric hospitalization is voluntary by default; involuntary holds in California follow the 5150/5250/5270 process
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。把三档程序记住，需要时能说出口。
  Cost: Free. Memorize the three-step process so you can state it when needed.
- 说人话：家属或单位一句话，不能把一个人留在精神病院住院。加州《Lanterman-Petris-Short Act》只认三种情形：因精神障碍已经伤害自己或有这个危险、已经危害别人或有这个危险、或严重失能（gravely disabled，连自己的衣食住都管不了）。第一步是 5150：警察、县指定的专业人员等依相当理由可留人 72 小时做评估；之后医生可申请 14 天（5250），再之后 30 天（5270.15），后两档要开听证。病人有权请律师、有权申请人身保护令。
  In plain terms: A family member's or employer's word alone can't keep someone in a psychiatric hospital. California's Lanterman-Petris-Short Act recognizes only three grounds: danger to self (or risk of it) due to a mental disorder, danger to others (or risk of it), or grave disability (unable to provide for one's own food, clothing, or shelter). Step one is a 5150: police or county-designated professionals may detain the person up to 72 hours for evaluation on probable cause; then a 14-day hold (5250), then 30 days (5270.15) — the latter two require a hearing. Patients have the right to an attorney and to petition for a writ of habeas corpus.
- 收益：Cal. Welf. & Inst. Code §5150：因精神障碍而伤害自己、伤害他人或严重失能的人，经相当理由可被带走，最长留置 72 小时做评估、评价和危机干预。§5250：14 天认证留置。§5270.15：再延长 30 天。每一档的适用条件和听证程序都写在法条里
  Benefit: Cal. Welf. & Inst. Code §5150: a person who, as a result of a mental health disorder, is a danger to self, a danger to others, or gravely disabled may be taken into custody on probable cause for up to 72 hours of assessment, evaluation, and crisis intervention. §5250: 14-day certification. §5270.15: an additional 30 days. Each step's criteria and hearing procedures are in the statute.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Welf. & Inst. Code §§5150, 5250, 5270.15（Lanterman-Petris-Short Act）. <https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/welfare-and-institutions-code/wic-sect-5150.html>；LA Superior Court 心理健康庭对 5150/5250/5270 流程的说明. <https://www.lacourt.org/division/mentalhealth/MH0021.aspx>
- 备注：原书引用的中国《精神卫生法》在加州不适用。「送去做诊断」和「留下来住院」同样是两道门，前一道低，后一道高。病人拒绝服药一般不能强制，除非法院另有命令或急诊情形。/ Note: The Chinese Mental Health Law cited in the original book does not apply in California. "Being taken in for evaluation" and "being kept for treatment" are likewise two separate gates — the first low, the second high. Patients generally can't be forced to take medication without a separate court order or an emergency.

### 4. 长期照护家里残疾人的那个人，自己的身体要有人盯着 / Whoever cares long-term for a disabled family member needs someone watching their own health
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。把照护的活分出去一部分，自己去做一次体检。
  Cost: Free. Hand off part of the caregiving and get yourself a checkup.
- 说人话：照护者累垮不是矫情。一项美国研究跟踪了八百多位老人四年。一边照护失能的配偶、一边报告自己精神吃力的那批人，死亡风险比不照护的人高约六成。照护但不觉得吃力的人，没有出现这种升高。所以要盯的不是照护本身，是那份长期紧绷。
  In plain terms: Caregiver burnout isn't weakness. A U.S. study followed 800+ elders for four years: those who were caregiving for a disabled spouse *and* reported mental or emotional strain had about 63% higher mortality than non-caregivers. Caregivers who didn't feel strained showed no such increase. So it's not the caregiving — it's the chronic strain.
- 收益：前瞻性人群队列，1993 到 1998 年，平均随访 4.5 年，在美国四个社区做。392 名照护者和 427 名非照护者，年龄 66 到 96 岁，都与配偶同住。四年内 103 人（12.6%）死亡。调整社会人口学因素、已有疾病和亚临床心血管疾病之后，一边照护一边报告有精神或情绪紧张的人，死亡风险比非照护对照高 63%（RR 1.63，95% CI 1.00–2.65）。照护但未报告紧张的人（RR 1.08，0.61–1.90）和配偶失能但本人不照护的人（RR 1.37，0.73–2.58），调整后的死亡率都没有升高
  Benefit: Prospective cohort, 1993–1998, mean follow-up 4.5 years, four U.S. communities. 392 caregivers and 427 non-caregivers, ages 66–96, all living with a spouse. 103 deaths (12.6%) in four years. After adjusting for sociodemographics, prevalent disease, and subclinical cardiovascular disease, strained caregivers had 63% higher mortality than non-caregiving controls (RR 1.63, 95% CI 1.00–2.65). Caregivers without strain (RR 1.08, 0.61–1.90) and non-caregivers with a disabled spouse (RR 1.37, 0.73–2.58) showed no adjusted increase.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Schulz R, Beach SR (1999). Caregiving as a risk factor for mortality: the Caregiver Health Effects Study. JAMA, 282(23):2215-2219. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.282.23.2215>
- 备注：争议：这项研究的可信范围下限正好压在 1.00 上，「没有差别」这个可能性没有被完全排除。人群是与配偶同住的美国老人，套到照护年轻残疾家属的人身上属于往外推。它能说明的是方向：吃力的那部分人风险更高。能分出去的照护活尽量分出去：加州的暂托服务（respite）可通过 regional center、California Caregiver Resource Centers（caregivercalifornia.org）或打 211 查询；符合条件的居家照护看 IHSS（见本节第 7 条）。照护者自己的慢性病别停药，见第 16 节第 1 条（药按医嘱吃满）。这条的受益人是你自己。/ Note: Caveat — the confidence interval's lower bound sits exactly at 1.00, so "no difference" isn't fully excluded. The population was co-residing elderly Americans; extending to caregivers of younger disabled relatives is extrapolation. The direction stands: the strained group carries higher risk. Hand off what you can: respite care in California is available through regional centers, the California Caregiver Resource Centers (caregivercalifornia.org), or by calling 211; home-based care for those who qualify runs through IHSS (see item 7). Don't stop your own chronic-disease meds (see Section 16, item 1). This item's beneficiary is you.

### 5. 长期坐轮椅的，坐垫换成减压型，每天看一遍坐骨和尾骨 / For long-term wheelchair users: switch to a pressure-relief cushion and check the sitting bones and tailbone daily
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一个减压坐垫几十到几百美元。每天看一遍，一分钟。
  Cost: A pressure-relief cushion runs tens to a few hundred dollars. One minute a day for the skin check.
- 说人话：普通海绵坐垫压久了会把皮肤压破，破了愈合很慢，容易感染。养老院里用减压坐垫的老人，坐骨那里的压疮明显更少，但把尾骨一起算就没看出差别。轮椅本身也要按人的尺寸配，别买个通用款就算了。
  In plain terms: Ordinary foam cushions break skin down over time; once broken, it heals slowly and infects easily. In a nursing-home trial, skin-protection cushions clearly cut pressure ulcers at the sitting bones, though counting the tailbone too showed no difference. The wheelchair itself should be fitted to the person — don't settle for a one-size model.
- 收益：随机临床试验在 12 家养老院做，2004 到 2008 年招募 232 名 65 岁以上、每天坐轮椅 6 小时以上、Braden 评分 18 分以下的住户。所有人先配了合身的轮椅，再随机分成两组。一组用皮肤保护型坐垫（空气、黏性流体加泡沫、或者凝胶加泡沫），一组用 7.6 厘米厚的分段式泡沫坐垫。随访 6 个月或者到发生压疮为止。坐骨结节部位的压疮，分段泡沫组 8 例（6.7%），皮肤保护型坐垫组 1 例（0.9%），P=0.04。把坐骨和骶尾部的压疮合起来算，分段泡沫组 21 例（17.6%），皮肤保护型坐垫组 12 例（10.6%），P=0.14，没有做出差别

- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Probate Code §§1800 et seq., §1810, §4672. <https://law.justia.com/codes/california/2010/prob/1810-1813.1.html>；CANHR 对加州 conservatorship 的说明. <https://canhr.org/probate-conservatorships-in-california/>
- 备注：不经过这道程序就替人签字、处分他的财产，事后可能被认定无效。还要记住一点：conservatorship 分管人身和管财产两种，法院按实际需要设，别一概全管。监护人怎么定见本节第 19 条。/ Note: Signing or disposing of someone's property without this process can later be voided. Also note: conservatorship comes in two kinds — of the person and of the estate — and the court tailors it to actual need; don't assume full control. On how conservators are chosen, see item 19.

### 19. 监护人（conservator）按法定顺序定；趁清醒时用预立指示和授权书把人选定下来 / Conservators are appointed in a statutory order — name yours in advance while you're lucid
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。有争议时要走法院解决。趁清醒时签预立指示和授权书，找律师通常几百美元。
  Cost: Free to know the order; contested cases go to court. Signing an advance directive and power of attorney while lucid typically costs a few hundred dollars through an attorney.
- 说人话：成年人失能后，conservator 按顺序来：配偶或同居伴侣，成年子女，父母，兄弟姐妹，最后是其他人。法院按被监护人的最佳利益选。争起来了由法院指定。人还清醒时，可以用预立医疗指示（advance health care directive）指定医疗代理人并提名未来的 conservator，用持久财务授权书（durable power of attorney）指定管钱的人。都签好了，能省掉后面一大半争议。
  In plain terms: After an adult loses capacity, the conservator preference order is: spouse or domestic partner, adult child, parent, sibling, then anyone else — with the court guided by the conservatee's best interests. Disputes are decided by the court. While still lucid, you can name a health care agent and nominate a future conservator in an advance health care directive, and name someone to handle money in a durable power of attorney. Signed in advance, most later disputes never happen.
- 收益：Cal. Probate Code §1812(b)：同等 qualified 的人选中，优先顺序为（1）配偶或同居伴侣、（2）成年子女、（3）父母、（4）兄弟姐妹、（5）其他人；(a) 法院按被监护人的最佳利益选。§1810：被监护人本人的提名优先。§4672：预立医疗指示中的提名与 §1810 同等效力。财务授权书（durable POA）在本人失能后继续有效
  Benefit: Cal. Probate Code §1812(b): among equally qualified candidates, preference goes (1) spouse or domestic partner, (2) adult child, (3) parent, (4) sibling, (5) anyone else; (a) the court is guided by the conservatee's best interests. §1810: the conservatee's own nomination takes priority. §4672: a nomination in an advance health care directive carries §1810 effect. A durable power of attorney for finances stays valid after the principal's incapacity.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Probate Code §§1810–1813, §4672. <https://law.justia.com/codes/california/2010/prob/1810-1813.1.html>
- 备注：中国式的「被监护人伤了别人由监护人赔」在加州不自动成立（待核实），别把两边的规则混用。意定监护的加州对应物就是 advance health care directive + durable power of attorney，趁清醒时签好，具体见第 17 节第 1 条（原书第 17 节）。/ Note: China's rule making guardians automatically liable for the ward's torts does not automatically apply in California (to be verified) — don't mix the two systems. California's counterpart to advance guardianship designation is the advance health care directive plus durable power of attorney; sign both while lucid (see Section 17, item 1 — original book, Section 17).

### 20. 因残疾被拒招、被降薪、不给合理便利：先向 EEOC 或加州民权局投诉，再考虑打官司 / Disability discrimination in hiring, pay, or accommodations: complain to the EEOC or California's Civil Rights Department first, then consider suing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：投诉不花钱。打官司的话律师费几千到几万美元，自己出。证据要自己保存。
  Cost: Complaining is free. A lawsuit runs thousands to tens of thousands in attorney fees, out of pocket. You preserve the evidence yourself.
- 说人话：联邦 ADA Title I（15 人以上雇主）和加州 FEHA（5 人以上雇主，Gov. Code §12940）都禁止在招用、转正、晋升、报酬、社保上歧视残疾人，雇主还须提供合理便利（reasonable accommodation）。时效：EEOC 300 天（加州是 deferral 州）；加州 Civil Rights Department（CRD）3 年（AB 9，2020 年起从 1 年延长）。先走免费的投诉，同时找 Disability Rights California 免费咨询，再考虑打官司。
  In plain terms: Federal ADA Title I (employers with 15+ workers) and California's FEHA (employers with 5+ workers, Gov. Code §12940) both ban disability discrimination in hiring, promotion, pay, and benefits — and employers must provide reasonable accommodation. Deadlines: 300 days for the EEOC (California is a deferral state); 3 years for California's Civil Rights Department (AB 9, extended from 1 year in 2020). Start with the free complaint route, get free counsel from Disability Rights California, and only then consider suing.
- 收益：42 U.S.C. §12112（ADA Title I）：禁止雇主因残疾歧视 qualified 的残疾人，包括招用、晋升、解雇、报酬，并要求合理便利。Cal. Gov. Code §12940（FEHA）：加州的保护面更广，雇主门槛更低（5 人）。时效：EEOC 300 天；CRD 3 年（Gov. Code §12960，AB 9）。投诉后可申请 right-to-sue 再进法院
  Benefit: 42 U.S.C. §12112 (ADA Title I): bars employers from discriminating against qualified people with disabilities in hiring, promotion, firing, and pay, and requires reasonable accommodation. Cal. Gov. Code §12940 (FEHA): broader California protections with a lower employer threshold (5 workers). Deadlines: 300 days (EEOC); 3 years (CRD, Gov. Code §12960 as amended by AB 9). After complaining you can request a right-to-sue notice before going to court.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：42 U.S.C. §12112（ADA Title I）. <https://www.ada.gov/law-and-regs/ada/>；Cal. Gov. Code §§12940, 12960. <https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/>；EEOC 各办公室 timeliness 说明（加州 300 天）. <https://www.eeoc.gov/field-office/sanfrancisco/timeliness>
- 备注：过程成本必须先算。取证是最难的一关：面试被拒时对方很少留下书面理由，能录音、能留下聊天记录就留（注意加州是双方同意录音州，all-party consent，待核实具体适用）。已经入职的按劳动争议留证据。Disability Rights California（disabilityrightsca.org）提供免费法律帮助。这条的受益人是你自己。/ Note: Count the process costs first. Evidence is the hardest part: rejections rarely come with written reasons, so save recordings and chat logs where lawful (California is an all-party-consent recording state — exact application to be verified). Once employed, preserve evidence as in any employment dispute. Disability Rights California (disabilityrightsca.org) offers free legal help. This item's beneficiary is you.
