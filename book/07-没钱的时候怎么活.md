# 7. 没钱的时候怎么活 / How to Survive When You're Broke

口径：金钱。/ Scope: Money.

这一节算的是钱和保障。能领多少钱、能免多少钱、能得到什么服务、要满足什么条件，都写在里面，不折算成寿命。政策每年都在变，各县也不一样，所以金额和门槛都以你所在县当年公布的为准。条目的排序是：不花钱、手续少、见效快的排在前面。要攒长期资格的、各地差别大的排在后面。只凭经验、没有官方文件的放在最后。

This section is about money and safety nets: how much you can get, what you can get for free, what services exist, and what conditions apply—no conversion into life-years. Policies change every year and differ by county, so treat the amounts and thresholds below as pointers and always check your county's current publications. Items are ordered by: free, low-hassle, and fast-acting first; items that need long-term eligibility or vary a lot by county later; experience-only items with no official source last.

本节条目按主题分成下面几块，括号里是条号。第 12 条为中国大陆专属流程，加州版略去。

Items are grouped by theme below (item numbers in parentheses). Item 12 is a mainland-China-only process and is omitted in the California edition.

**丢了工作 / Lost your job**：申领失业保险金（第 1 条）/ File for unemployment insurance (Item 1)，免费职业培训（第 13 条）/ Free job training (Item 13)，求职用系统方法（第 14 条）/ Systematic job hunting (Item 14)。

**被欠工钱 / Owed wages**：向 Labor Commissioner 投诉（第 2 条）/ File with the Labor Commissioner (Item 2)，申请法律援助（第 3 条）/ Get free legal aid (Item 3)，先分清雇你的是谁（第 22 条）/ First figure out who hired you (Item 22)。

**救急兜底 / Emergency safety nets**：找收容所和 211（第 4 条）/ Shelters and 211 (Item 4)，县里的紧急现金援助（第 6 条）/ County emergency cash aid (Item 6)，低收入综合援助（第 7 条）/ Low-income assistance programs (Item 7)，残疾人补助（第 8 条）/ Disability benefits (Item 8)，证件丢了去 DMV 补办（第 11 条）/ Replace lost ID at the DMV (Item 11)，实在没地方睡（第 21 条）/ Nowhere to sleep (Item 21)。

**医保 / Health coverage**：没医保走 Medi-Cal 或 Covered California（第 9 条）/ Medi-Cal or Covered California (Item 9)，重病先走医院慈善医疗（第 10 条）/ Hospital charity care first (Item 10)，工作断档医保不断（第 18 条）/ Keep coverage across job gaps (Item 18)，按需配补充险（第 20 条）/ Supplemental insurance if needed (Item 20)。

**找活和重新开始 / Finding work and starting over**：免费的 CalJOBS 和就业中心（第 5 条）/ Free CalJOBS and job centers (Item 5)，不交押金不签"培训贷"（第 15 条）/ No deposits, no "training loans" (Item 15)，有案底或破产过也有重来的路（第 19 条）/ A record or bankruptcy isn't the end (Item 19)。

**住和吃 / Housing and food**：申请 Section 8 和公租房（第 16 条）/ Section 8 and public housing (Item 16)，把住和吃压到最低（第 17 条）/ Push housing and food costs down (Item 17)。

### 1. 失业了先去 EDD 申请失业保险金 / Lost your job? File for Unemployment Insurance with the EDD first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。在 edd.ca.gov 上用 UI Online 申请，或打电话申请。要有 SSN（或合法工作许可）。
  Cost: Free. File online at edd.ca.gov (UI Online) or by phone. You'll need a Social Security number (or work authorization).
- 说人话：被裁员、公司倒闭、工时被砍到不够生活，都算"不是你的错失业"，可以申请。每周 $40 到 $450，看你过去工资多少，最多领 26 周。第一个星期是等待周，不发钱。自己辞职的一般领不到，除非能证明是"不得已的好理由"（比如被欠薪、被骚扰、要搬家照顾家人）。被错误归类成 contractor 的也可以申请，EDD 会重新判定你的身份。
  In plain terms: Layoffs, shutdowns, or hours cut below livable levels all count as unemployment "through no fault of your own." Benefits run $40 to $450 a week depending on your past wages, for up to 26 weeks. The first week is an unpaid waiting week. Quitting on your own usually disqualifies you, unless you can show "good cause" (unpaid wages, harassment, a forced move). Misclassified contractors can apply too—the EDD will redetermine your status.
- 收益：要同时满足几个条件。一是 base period（申请日往前推 5 个完整季度中的前 4 个）里，工资最高的那个季度 ≥ $1,300；或者最高季度 ≥ $900 且整个 base period 总工资 ≥ 最高季度工资的 1.25 倍。二是非本人过错失业。三是能工作、能到岗、每周在找工作、每两周认证一次。每周金额按最高季度工资算，$40–$450。2026 年加州平均每周发 $375 左右。失业金联邦要交所得税（可自愿预扣 10%），加州不收州税。
  Benefit: You must meet several conditions: (1) in your base period (the first 4 of the last 5 completed calendar quarters), your highest-earning quarter was at least $1,300—or at least $900 with total base-period wages of at least 1.25× that quarter; (2) unemployment through no fault of your own; (3) able and available to work, looking for work weekly, and certifying every two weeks. The weekly amount is computed from your highest-quarter wages: $40–$450. California's average weekly benefit in 2026 is about $375. UI benefits are federally taxable (you can elect 10% withholding) but exempt from California state income tax.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Employment Development Department (EDD). Eligibility for unemployment benefits. <https://edd.ca.gov/en/unemployment/eligibility/>；EDD Quick Statistics: minimum/maximum weekly benefit amount $40/$450. <http://edd.ca.gov/en/about_edd/quick_statistics/>
- 备注：等待周不发钱，这是法定设计，不是 EDD 拖你。申诉要在决定作出后 20 天内书面提出。金额每年由 EDD 公布，以当年为准。
  Note: The unpaid waiting week is by law, not EDD stalling. Appeals must be filed in writing within 20 days of the determination. Amounts are published annually by the EDD—use the current year's figures.

### 2. 被欠薪就去 Labor Commissioner 免费投诉，拖欠最后工资每天罚一天工资 / Owed wages? File a free claim with the Labor Commissioner — late final pay costs the employer a day's wages per day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。在 dir.ca.gov 在线填表，或去当地办公室。要备齐工资条、工时记录、转账记录、聊天记录。欠薪要在 3 年内提出。难在要一趟趟跑、一份份找证据，中间容易泄气。
  Cost: Free. File online at dir.ca.gov or at a local office. Bring pay stubs, time records, payment records, and messages. Wage claims must be filed within 3 years. The hard part is the legwork—trips and paperwork, and not giving up halfway.
- 说人话：加州没有"劳动仲裁"这套，去州政府的 Labor Commissioner（劳工专员办公室，属 DIR）投诉，全程免费。调查员会开听证会（Berman hearing），裁多少公司就得付多少。公司故意拖欠你最后那笔工资的，每拖一天罚一天工资，最多罚 30 天（waiting time penalty）。但"裁下来"不等于"拿到钱"：公司没财产、老板跑路的，赢了也可能拿不到。
  In plain terms: California has no labor-arbitration track like China's—you file with the state Labor Commissioner (part of the DIR), free of charge. An investigator holds a hearing (a "Berman" hearing) and whatever is awarded, the employer must pay. If the employer willfully withholds your final paycheck, the penalty is a full day's wages for every day it's late, up to 30 days (the "waiting time" penalty). But an award isn't cash in hand: if the company has no assets or the boss skipped town, you can win and still collect nothing.
- 收益：Labor Commissioner 受理欠薪、加班费、最低工资、最后工资拖欠的投诉。时效 3 年。Labor Code §203：故意拖欠最后工资的，按员工日工资每天罚一天，最多 30 天。2026 年加州最低工资 $16.50/小时（部分城市更高）。被错误归类为 contractor 的也可以投诉，见第 22 条。
  Benefit: The Labor Commissioner takes complaints for unpaid wages, overtime, minimum wage, and late final pay, with a 3-year filing window. Labor Code §203: willfully withholding final wages costs the employer a full day's wages per day of delay, up to 30 days. California's 2026 minimum wage is $16.50/hour (higher in some cities). Misclassified "contractors" can file too—see Item 22.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Department of Industrial Relations, Division of Labor Standards Enforcement (Labor Commissioner's Office). <https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/>；Cal. Labor Code §§ 201–203 (final pay and waiting time penalties).
- 备注：投诉和打官司是两条路，也可以直接去法院告。公司在加州以外、你人在加州干活的，一般仍受加州法律管。赢了拿不到钱的风险真实存在，先看对方还有没有经营。
  Note: Filing with the Commissioner and suing in court are separate paths—you can also go straight to court. If you worked in California for an out-of-state company, California law generally still covers you. The risk of an uncollectible award is real; check whether the business is still operating first.

### 3. 打不起官司就找免费法律援助，讨薪、被非法解雇这类案子本来就在范围内 / Can't afford a lawyer? Get free legal aid — wage theft and wrongful firing are exactly what it's for
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。去 lawhelpca.org 按县找法律援助机构，或打 211 转介。个人告 $12,500 以下的案子可以走小额法庭（small claims），不用请律师。
  Cost: Free. Find a legal aid office by county at lawhelpca.org, or dial 211 for a referral. Claims under $12,500 by an individual can go to small claims court—no lawyer needed.
- 说人话：讨薪、要加班费、被非法解雇、房东乱扣押金、家暴保护令，都有免费法律援助。加州各县都有 Legal Aid：湾区有 Bay Area Legal Aid，洛杉矶有 Legal Aid Foundation of Los Angeles。法院也有自助中心（self-help center），帮你填表。记住 211 这个号码：食物、住房、水电费、法律转介都能问。
  In plain terms: Wage claims, overtime, wrongful termination, landlord deposit disputes, restraining orders—free legal aid covers all of these. Every California county has legal aid offices (Bay Area Legal Aid in the Bay Area, Legal Aid Foundation of Los Angeles in LA). Courts run self-help centers that walk you through the forms. And remember 211: one number for food, housing, utility help, and legal referrals.
- 收益：讨薪、押金这类案子，免费法律援助直接帮你办了，省下几千美元律师费；$12,500 以下的个人案子走小额法庭，立案费几十美元，不用请律师也能告。
  Benefit: For wage claims, deposit disputes, and similar cases, free legal aid handles the matter for you — saving thousands of dollars in attorney's fees; individuals can file claims up to $12,500 in small claims court for a filing fee of tens of dollars, no lawyer required.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：LawHelpCA (State Bar of California legal aid directory). <https://www.lawhelpca.org/>；California Courts Self-Help. <https://selfhelp.courts.ca.gov/>；211 California. <https://www.211ca.org/>
- 备注：小额法庭个人上限 $12,500（Code of Civil Procedure §116.221；企业等主体 $6,250，§116.220），2024 年起从 $10,000 上调。立案费几十美元。法律援助要查收入，收入太高可能排不上，届时法院自助中心是退路。
  Note: Small claims cap is $12,500 for individuals (Code Civ. Proc. §116.221; $6,250 for businesses and other entities, §116.220), raised from $10,000 in 2024. Filing fee is tens of dollars. Legal aid is means-tested—if your income is too high, the court self-help center is the fallback.

### 4. 走投无路打 211 找收容所：管住处、管转介 / Nowhere left to turn? Call 211 for a shelter — a bed and referrals
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。打 211（有中文等多语言服务）或上 211ca.org。紧急危险打 911。难在要开口跟人说自己没地方去。
  Cost: Free. Dial 211 (multilingual, including Chinese) or visit 211ca.org. For immediate danger, call 911. The hard part is saying out loud that you have nowhere to go.
- 说人话：加州没有中国"救助站"那样的一站式机构，对应的是各县的 emergency shelter（紧急收容所）+ 211 转介。211 能帮你找当晚有空床的收容所，还能转食物、衣服、洗澡、医疗。冬天各县开 warming center（取暖中心）。收容所是自愿进出，不管饭的豪华程度，只管有地方睡、有基本吃喝。
  In plain terms: California has no single "rescue station" like China's—instead each county runs emergency shelters, with 211 as the referral hub. 211 can find you a bed for tonight plus food, clothing, showers, and medical referrals. Counties open warming centers in winter. Shelters are voluntary in and out; expect a bed and basic meals, not comfort.
- 收益：211 是加州官方的社会服务转介总机，覆盖食物、住房、水电费、就业。收容所床位由各县和非营利组织运营，位置和空位每天变，打 211 问最新信息最准。
  Benefit: 211 is California's official social-services referral hub, covering food, housing, utilities, and jobs. Shelter beds are run by counties and nonprofits; locations and availability change daily—211 has the freshest information.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：211 California. <https://www.211ca.org/>；U.S. HUD Homelessness Resources (shelter locator by county).
- 备注：床位先到先得，下午就打电话问，别等到半夜。带身份证件方便登记，但没证件一般也不拒绝。
  Note: Beds are first-come, first-served—call in the afternoon, not at midnight. Bring ID if you have it; most shelters won't turn you away for lacking it.

### 5. 找活先用免费的 CalJOBS 和 America's Job Center，不找收费中介 / Job hunting? Start with free CalJOBS and America's Job Center — never pay a middleman
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。在 caljobs.ca.gov 注册；去本县的 America's Job Center of California（AJCC）。不知道地址就打 211 问。
  Cost: Free. Register at caljobs.ca.gov; walk into your county's America's Job Center of California (AJCC). Don't know the address? Dial 211.
- 说人话：CalJOBS 是 EDD 办的官方免费招工平台，登记、投简历、看招聘都不花钱。每个县都有 AJCC，就业指导、改简历、模拟面试、用电脑打印全免费。收"介绍费""上岗押金"的中介直接拉黑——加州法律禁止雇主向雇员收押金（见第 15 条）。
  In plain terms: CalJOBS is the EDD's official free job platform—register, post your résumé, browse openings, all free. Every county has an AJCC offering career counseling, résumé help, mock interviews, and free computer/printer use. Any "agent" charging placement fees or job deposits is a red flag—California law bars employers from collecting deposits from employees (see Item 15).
- 收益：AJCC 由联邦 WIOA 经费支持，对失业者、低收入者免费提供职业服务。EDD 要求领失业金的人在 CalJOBS 登记并每周找工作，顺手就把求职和领钱两件事一起办了。
  Benefit: AJCCs are federally (WIOA) funded and free for unemployed and low-income job seekers. The EDD requires UI claimants to register on CalJOBS and search for work weekly—so one registration covers both job hunting and your benefit requirement.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Employment Development Department, CalJOBS. <https://www.edd.ca.gov/en/jobs/>；America's Job Center of California locator. <https://www.edd.ca.gov/en/office_locator/>
- 备注：零工、日结活当场核对工钱，留下对方联系方式和干活记录。被欠了钱走第 2 条。
  Note: For day labor, confirm the pay on the spot and keep the contact's info plus a record of the work. If they stiff you, see Item 2.

### 6. 突发变故先申请县里的紧急现金援助 / Sudden crisis? Apply for county emergency cash aid first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。去本县的社会服务局（county social services / human services agency）申请，带上身份证件、收入证明、房租账单。通常跑 1 到 2 趟。
  Cost: Free. Apply at your county's social services (human services) agency with ID, proof of income, and rent bills. Usually 1–2 trips.
- 说人话：加州没有全国统一的"临时救助"，各县自己办。单身成人没收入，申请 General Relief / General Assistance（各县名字不同）：每月几百美元现金，解燃眉之急。有孩子的低收入家庭走 CalWORKs：每月现金 + 就业服务 + 托儿补助。金额、时长各县自己定，批下来之前先问清。
  In plain terms: California has no single national "temporary aid" program—each county runs its own. Single adults with no income apply for General Relief / General Assistance (names vary by county): a few hundred dollars a month in cash for emergencies. Low-income families with kids go through CalWORKs: monthly cash plus employment services and childcare help. Amounts and time limits are set county by county—ask before you count on it.
- 收益：General Relief 是加州各县给无其他收入来源的成年人的最后现金兜底；CalWORKs 是联邦 TANF 在加州的版本，给有孩子的贫困家庭发现金并要求参加就业活动。两项都由县社会服务局受理。
  Benefit: General Relief is counties' last-resort cash aid for adults with no other income; CalWORKs is California's TANF program, paying cash to poor families with children tied to work activities. Both are handled by county social services.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：California Department of Social Services (CDSS), CalWORKs. <https://www.cdss.ca.gov/calworks>；各县 General Relief 计划（如 Los Angeles County DPSS）。
- 备注：洛杉矶县 General Relief 单身成人约 $221/月（待核实，各县不同）。部分县的 General Relief 视为借款，日后从收入中扣还，申请时问清楚。CalWORKs 有 48 个月终身时限（家有小孩的豁免情形除外）。
  Note: LA County General Relief is about $221/month for a single adult (待核实/unverified—varies by county). Some counties treat General Relief as a loan recouped from future income—ask upfront. CalWORKs has a 48-month lifetime limit (with exemptions for families with young children).

### 7. 收入低就申请 CalFresh、Medi-Cal 和水电费折扣 / Low income? Apply for CalFresh, Medi-Cal, and utility discounts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。在 getcalfresh.org 或 benefitscal.com 在线申请，或去县办公室。难在要把收入、房租、账单摊开给人查，还要定期复核。
  Cost: Free. Apply online at getcalfresh.org or benefitscal.com, or at the county office. The hard part is laying your income, rent, and bills bare—and the periodic recertifications.
- 说人话：CalFresh（加州版食品券）：没收入的单人家庭每月最高 $298（2025 年 10 月–2026 年 9 月标准；2026 年 10 月起调到 $306），打到 EBT 卡上，只能买食物，不能买熟食和日用品。SSI/SSP：65 岁以上或残疾、收入资产都低的人，联邦 SSI + 加州 SSP，2026 年单人独立居住最高 $1,233.94/月，自动带 Medi-Cal。水电费：CARE/FERA 计划给低收入家庭电费、燃气费打折（约七折到八五折），去电力公司网站申请。
  In plain terms: CalFresh (California's SNAP): a single-person household with no income gets up to $298/month (Oct 2025–Sep 2026; $306 starting Oct 2026) on an EBT card—groceries only, no hot food or household goods. SSI/SSP: for people 65+ or disabled with low income and assets—federal SSI plus California's SSP, up to $1,233.94/month for a single person living independently in 2026, with Medi-Cal automatic. Utilities: the CARE/FERA programs discount electricity and gas for low-income households (roughly 15–30% off)—apply on your utility's website.
- 收益：CalFresh 按家庭人数和净收入算，1 人 $298、2 人 $546、3 人 $785、4 人 $994（FY2026 上限）。SSI 领受人自动符合 Medi-Cal。CARE 要求家庭收入在联邦贫困线 200% 以下。
  Benefit: CalFresh scales by household size and net income—caps of $298 (1 person), $546 (2), $785 (3), $994 (4) in FY2026. SSI recipients get Medi-Cal automatically. CARE requires household income at or below 200% of the federal poverty level.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：CDSS All County Information Notice I-46-25 (CalFresh FY2026 maximum allotments, eff. 10/1/2025). <https://cdss.ca.gov/Portals/9/Additional-Resources/Letters-and-Notices/ACINs/2025/I-46_25.pdf>；CDSS, SSI Total Monthly Payment Amounts 2026 (single aged/disabled, independent living: $1,233.94). <https://cdss.ca.gov/Portals/13/SHD/ParaRegIndex/SSI%20Monthly%20Payment%20Amounts%202026.pdf>；GetCalFresh. <https://www.getcalfresh.org/>
- 备注：CalFresh 金额每年 10 月按食品价格调整，以当年 CDSS 通知为准。CARE 折扣比例各电力公司略有不同（待核实）。领 CalFresh 不影响移民身份中的"公共负担"判定之外的其他福利资格——非公民申请前建议先咨询移民法律援助（见第 3 条）。
  Note: CalFresh amounts adjust every October with food prices—use the current CDSS notice. CARE discount rates vary slightly by utility (待核实/unverified). Noncitizens should check with immigration legal aid before applying (see Item 3), as rules differ by status.

### 8. 残疾或重病干不了活：看加州伤残保险（SDI）和 SSI/SSP / Disabled and can't work? Look at California State Disability Insurance (SDI) and SSI/SSP

### 18. 工作断档别慌：社保积分不清零，医保空窗有接续 / Work gaps are fine: Social Security credits never expire, and health coverage can bridge the gap
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。在 ssa.gov 查自己的积分和收入记录。
  Cost: Free. Check your credits and earnings record at ssa.gov.

Premium-free Medicare (Part A) needs 40 quarters.   Benefit: Credits only decide eligibility—the benefit amount comes from your 35 highest-earning years. Self-employed people earn credits through self-employment tax. COBRA decisions are due within 60 days; miss it and it's gone.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Social Security Administration, retirement credits. <https://www.ssa.gov/benefits/retirement/planner/credits.html>；California Department of Managed Health Care, COBRA/Cal-COBRA. <https://www.dmhc.ca.gov/HealthCareinCalifornia/TypesofPlans/KeepYourHealthCoverage(COBRA).aspx>

### 19. 有案底、破产过，加州法律上都有重来的路 / A record or a bankruptcy? California law gives you a way back
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：expungement 申请有少量法庭费用（可申请减免）；破产 Chapter 7 申请费约 $338（待核实）。每一步都要准备材料，难在要一遍遍跟人讲过去的事。
  Cost: Expungement carries small court fees (waivable); Chapter 7 bankruptcy filing is about $338 (待核实/unverified). Each step needs paperwork—and retelling your past, over and over.
- 说人话：刑满之后，多数轻罪和部分重罪可申请 expungement（Penal Code §1203.4）：案底撤销后，找私企工作可以说"无定罪"。加州 Fair Chance Act：5 人以上的雇主在发 conditional offer 之前不许问案底，问了就是违法。破产走联邦法：Chapter 7 清算免债（收入低于州中位数才能走），Chapter 13 是 3–5 年还款计划；一申请就自动冻结所有追债。

- 收益：expungement 后，Fair Chance Act 下雇主也不能考虑已撤销的定罪。破产的 automatic stay 立刻停止工资扣押、催收电话和诉讼。咨询先找法律援助（第 3 条），别先找收费的"破产中介"。
  Benefit: After expungement, employers can't consider the dismissed conviction even under the Fair Chance Act. Bankruptcy's automatic stay immediately stops wage garnishment, collection calls, and lawsuits. Talk to legal aid first (Item 3)—not a paid "bankruptcy consultant."
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A

  Note: Sex offenses and some violent felonies can't be expunged. Since SB 731, California automatically seals many old records for eligible people (details 待核实/unverified). Chapter 7 can generally be filed only once every 8 years (待核实/unverified).

### 22. 被欠了工钱，先分清雇佣关系：加州用 ABC 测试 / Owed wages? Check your classification first: California uses the ABC test
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。先弄清：公司是不是把你叫成 contractor 却当雇员使。去 Labor Commissioner 投诉时，身份由政府判定。

Most "contractor" and "partner" setups fail at least one prong—legally you're an employee, entitled to back wages and overtime. Domestic workers (nannies, caregivers) are protected too, with their own Domestic Worker Bill of Rights, overtime included.

- 来源：Cal. Labor Code §2775 et seq. (AB 5, Dynamex ABC test); Cal. Labor Code §§ 1450–1460 (Domestic Worker Bill of Rights).
