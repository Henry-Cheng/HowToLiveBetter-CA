# 2. 不要慢慢死 / Don't Die Slowly

本节只收两类慢性风险：一类对总死亡率影响大，一类证据硬。条目按性价比从高到低排。有几条标了「随机试验」，意思是把人随机分成两组来比，这种结果最可信。其余数字都来自只跟踪记录、不做分组的研究。这类研究算出的 HR、RR 等风险比里混着两种干扰：这些人本来就不一样，有人是先病了才这么做。所以这些数字只说明方向和大概多大，不代表照做就能降这么多。

This section covers only two kinds of chronic risk: those that move all-cause mortality a lot, and those backed by hard evidence. Entries are ranked by cost-effectiveness, highest first. A few entries are tagged as randomized trials—meaning people were randomly split into two groups and compared—which is the most trustworthy kind of result. All other numbers come from observational studies that only tracked people without randomizing them. The hazard ratios (HR) and relative risks (RR) from such studies carry two kinds of confounding: these people were different to begin with, and some changed their behavior because they were already sick. So these numbers indicate direction and rough magnitude only—they don't mean you'll cut your risk by exactly that much by following them.

本节条目按主题分成下面几块，括号里是条号。

### 22. 每天吃一小把坚果 / Eat a small handful of nuts a day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：每天吃 28 克，一年约几十到上百美元。顺手就吃，不占时间。
  Cost: 28 grams a day runs tens to around a hundred dollars a year. Just grab and eat—no time needed.
- 说人话：常吃坚果的人，同期死亡的概率比不吃的人低约两成。一周吃一次就能看出差别，低约一成。吃到每天一把，差别最明显。
  In plain terms: Regular nut eaters die at about a 20% lower rate than non-eaters. Even once a week shows a difference—about 10% lower. A handful a day shows the clearest difference.
- 收益：两项美国跟踪研究，11.9 万人，300 多万人年。人年就是人数乘以各自跟踪的年数。和不吃坚果的人比：每周吃不到 1 次的人死亡风险低约 7%（风险比 0.93，95% CI 0.90–0.96，这是可信范围）。每周吃 1 次的低约 11%（0.89，0.86–0.93）。每周 2–4 次的低约 13%（0.87，0.83–0.90）。每周 5–6 次的低约 15%（0.85，0.79–0.91）。每周 7 次以上的低约 20%（0.80，0.73–0.86）
  Benefit: Two U.S. cohort studies with 119,000 people and over 3 million person-years (people multiplied by their years of follow-up). Versus non-eaters: less than once a week, about 7% lower mortality (hazard ratio 0.93, 95% CI 0.90–0.96); once a week, about 11% lower (0.89, 0.86–0.93); 2–4 times a week, about 13% lower (0.87, 0.83–0.90); 5–6 times a week, about 15% lower (0.85, 0.79–0.91); 7+ times a week, about 20% lower (0.80, 0.73–0.86).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Bao Y, Han J, Hu FB, et al. (2013). Association of nut consumption with total and cause-specific mortality. New England Journal of Medicine, 369(21), 2001-2011. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1307352>
- 备注：买原味不加盐的。坚果热量不低，别当零食敞开吃。要注意这类研究只是长期跟踪一群人，吃坚果的人整体生活方式更好，所以数字要打个折
  Note: Buy plain, unsalted nuts. Nuts are calorie-dense—don't snack on them freely. Note these are long-term observational studies, and nut eaters have better lifestyles overall, so discount the numbers accordingly.

### 23. 把一部分红肉换成鱼和禽肉 / Swap some red meat for fish and poultry
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不占时间。这是把肉换一种，不是多买。难在要改点口味偏好。
  Cost: Costs nothing and takes no time. It's swapping one meat for another, not buying more. The hard part is adjusting taste preferences.
- 说人话：每周多吃两份加工肉或者红肉，同期死亡的概率高约 3%。换成同样份数的鱼或者禽肉，就看不出升高。这条的效应在本节里偏小，属于顺手就换、不必较劲的事。
  In plain terms: Two extra servings of processed or red meat a week raises mortality by about 3%. Swapping in the same servings of fish or poultry shows no increase. This effect is small by this section's standards—a casual swap, not worth obsessing over.
- 收益：把美国 6 项跟踪研究、近 3 万人合起来算。每周多吃两份加工肉，死亡风险高约 3%（风险比 1.03，95% CI 1.02–1.05，可信范围）。每周多吃两份没加工的红肉，也高约 3%（1.03，1.01–1.05）。同样的吃法换成禽肉，是 0.99（0.97–1.02）；换成鱼，是 0.99（0.97–1.01）。这两项都没看出统计上的差别
  Benefit: Pooling 6 U.S. cohort studies with nearly 30,000 people: two extra servings of processed meat a week raised mortality by about 3% (hazard ratio 1.03, 95% CI 1.02–1.05); two extra servings of unprocessed red meat also about 3% (1.03, 1.01–1.05). The same swap to poultry was 0.99 (0.97–1.02); to fish, 0.99 (0.97–1.01)—neither statistically significant.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Zhong VW, Van Horn L, Greenland P, et al. (2020). Associations of Processed Meat, Unprocessed Red Meat, Poultry, or Fish Intake With Incident Cardiovascular Disease and All-Cause Mortality. JAMA Internal Medicine, 180(4), 503-512. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2019.6969>
- 备注：每周两份带来的差别很小，别指望靠换肉多活几年。同样花钱吃肉，可以优先挑鱼和禽肉
  Note: Two servings a week makes a tiny difference—don't expect extra years from swapping meat. For the same meat budget, favor fish and poultry.

### 24. 把一部分精米白面换成全谷物 / Swap some refined grains for whole grains
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：糙米、燕麦、全麦面比精米白面贵一点，不占时间。难在口感要慢慢适应。
  Cost: Brown rice, oats, and whole-wheat pasta cost a bit more than white rice and flour; no time needed. The hard part is adjusting to the texture.
- 说人话：每天多吃 90 g 全谷物的人，同期死亡的概率低约 17%。90 g 大约是三份。吃到每天 200 g 左右，还在继续往下降。
  In plain terms: People eating 90 g more whole grains a day die at about a 17% lower rate. 90 g is about three servings. Benefits keep increasing up to about 200 g a day.
- 收益：把多项跟踪研究合起来算（只记录、不分组）：全谷物每天多吃 90 g，死亡风险低约 17%（RR 0.83）。90 g 大约是 3 份。这份好处一直持续到每天 210–225 g。另一项汇总里，每天多吃 1 份低约 8%（RR 0.92）。
  Benefit: Pooling multiple cohort studies (observational, no randomization): each 90 g/day of whole grains cut mortality by about 17% (RR 0.83); 90 g is about 3 servings. The benefit continued up to 210–225 g a day. In another pooled analysis, each extra daily serving cut mortality by about 8% (RR 0.92).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Aune D 等 (2016). Whole grain consumption and risk of cardiovascular disease, cancer, and all cause and cause specific mortality: systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis of prospective studies. BMJ. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.i2716>；Schwingshackl L 等 (2017). Food groups and risk of all-cause mortality: a systematic review and meta-analysis of prospective studies. American Journal of Clinical Nutrition. <https://doi.org/10.3945/ajcn.117.153148>
- 备注：不必全换成全谷物，换一半就已经在好处最明显的那一段了。要注意这类研究只是跟踪记录，吃全谷物的人本来就更健康，数字偏大。各项研究之间的结果差得也远（I² 83%，这个数越大说明各研究结论越不一致）。
  Note: No need to go 100% whole grain—swapping half already lands you in the steepest part of the benefit curve. Note these are observational studies, and whole-grain eaters are healthier to begin with, so the numbers run high. Results also vary widely across studies (I² 83%—the higher this number, the more the studies disagree).

### 25. 每周喝三次以上茶 / Drink tea three or more times a week
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一年几十到几百元。泡一杯几分钟，不占什么时间。
  Cost: Tens to a couple hundred dollars a year. A few minutes to brew—barely any time.
- 说人话：有喝茶习惯的人，同期死亡的概率低约 15%（中国人群数据）。从 50 岁算起，平均多活 1.26 年，多出 1.41 年不得心血管病的时间。
  In plain terms: Habitual tea drinkers die at about a 15% lower rate (China-based data). From age 50, they live 1.26 years longer on average, with 1.41 extra years free of cardiovascular disease.
- 收益：中国一项 10.09 万人的研究（China-PAR），其中一半人跟踪满了 7.3 年。有喝茶习惯的人，死亡风险低约 15%（风险比 0.85，95% CI 0.79–0.90，这是可信范围）。以 50 岁为起点算：有喝茶习惯的人，没得动脉粥样硬化性心血管病的年数多 1.41 年，预期寿命多 1.26 年
  Benefit: A Chinese study of 100,900 people (China-PAR), half followed for the full 7.3 years: habitual tea drinkers had about 15% lower mortality (hazard ratio 0.85, 95% CI 0.79–0.90). From age 50, they gained 1.41 extra years free of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease and 1.26 years of life expectancy.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Wang X, Liu F, Li J, et al. (2020). Tea consumption and the risk of atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease and all-cause mortality: The China-PAR project. European Journal of Preventive Cardiology, 27(18), 1956-1963. <https://doi.org/10.1177/2047487319894685>；茶叶霉菌毒素：Cui P 等 (2020). Quantitative analysis and dietary risk assessment of aflatoxins in Chinese post-fermented dark tea. Food and Chemical Toxicology. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.fct.2020.111830>；Zhou H 等 (2022). Mycotoxins in Tea (Camellia sinensis (L.) Kuntze): Contamination and Dietary Exposure Profiling in the Chinese Population. Toxins. <https://doi.org/10.3390/toxins14070452>；绿茶提取物与肝损伤：Hu J 等 (2018). The safety of green tea and green tea extract consumption in adults - Results of a systematic review. Regulatory Toxicology and Pharmacology. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.yrtph.2018.03.019>
- 备注：争议在于：这类研究只是跟踪记录，中国男性喝茶的人里抽烟喝酒的比例高。作者已经扣掉一部分这类影响，但扣不干净。还有一点，别喝滚烫的，热饮温度见本节关于热饮温度的一条。有人担心茶叶里有霉菌毒素。国内查过 158 份黑茶，有 2 份检出黄曲霉毒素，按喝茶的量算，吃进去的量低于国际上的可接受值。另一项查了 352 份茶、16 种霉菌毒素，只有黑茶里的赭曲霉毒素 A 平均含量超过限量。按国人喝茶的量算，两项都没算出膳食风险。茶叶放在干燥的地方，受潮发霉的别喝。绿茶提取物胶囊不一样：高浓度的儿茶素一次大量吞下去会伤肝，冲泡的茶没有这个问题。所以这里说的只是泡的茶，别拿提取物保健品代替
  Note: The debate: these are observational studies, and Chinese men who drink tea also smoke and drink more; the authors adjusted for some of this but couldn't fully. Also, don't drink it scalding—see this section's item on hot-drink temperature. Some worry about mold toxins in tea. Chinese testing of 158 dark teas found aflatoxin in 2 samples, at intakes below international acceptable levels for typical tea consumption. Another study of 352 teas and 16 mycotoxins found only ochratoxin A in dark tea exceeding limits on average; neither study found dietary risk at Chinese consumption levels. Store tea somewhere dry; don't drink tea that's gotten damp and moldy. Green-tea-extract capsules are different: swallowing concentrated catechins in bulk can injure the liver—brewed tea has no such problem. So this item means brewed tea only; don't substitute extract supplements.

### 26. 每天喝三到四杯咖啡，不加糖不加奶盖 / Drink three to four cups of coffee a day—no sugar, no whipped cream
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：自己冲每天不到一美元。冲一杯几分钟，不占什么时间。
  Cost: Home-brewed costs under a dollar a day. A few minutes to brew—barely any time.
- 说人话：每天喝三到四杯咖啡的人，同期死亡的概率比不喝的人低约 17%。
  In plain terms: People drinking three to four cups of coffee a day die at about a 17% lower rate than non-drinkers.
- 收益：一篇综述把 201 项汇总研究的结果又合到一起算，这些汇总底下的原始研究都只记录、不分组。结果：每天喝 3–4 杯咖啡的人和不喝的人比，死亡风险低 17%（相对风险 0.83，95% CI 0.79–0.88，这是可信范围）
  Benefit: An umbrella review pooled the results of 201 meta-analyses, whose underlying studies were all observational with no randomization. Result: people drinking 3–4 cups of coffee a day had 17% lower mortality than non-drinkers (relative risk 0.83, 95% CI 0.79–0.88).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Poole R, Kennedy OJ, Roderick P, et al. (2017). Coffee consumption and health: umbrella review of meta-analyses of multiple health outcomes. BMJ, 359, j5024. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.j5024>
- 备注：争议在于：原作者写明证据几乎全来自跟踪记录，还说「需要可靠的随机对照试验才能判断是否为因果」。意思是要把人随机分成两组来比，光看跟踪记录断不了因果。做的时候喝黑咖啡，加糖加奶盖会把好处抵消掉。孕妇，还有心律失常、焦虑、失眠的人，不适用这一条
  Note: The debate: the authors state the evidence is almost entirely observational and that "reliable randomized trials are needed to determine causality"—you'd need to randomize people into groups; observation alone can't settle cause and effect. Drink it black; sugar and whipped cream cancel the benefit. Pregnant people and those with arrhythmia, anxiety, or insomnia should skip this one.

### 27. 每天吃够 5 份（约 400 g）水果蔬菜 / Eat at least 5 servings (~400 g) of fruits and vegetables a day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：每天几美元。每天要花点时间洗和切。难在要天天买、天天吃够量。
  Cost: A few dollars a day. Some daily time washing and chopping. The hard part is buying and eating enough, every day.
- 说人话：每天多吃 200 g 蔬菜水果，同期死亡的概率低约一成。每天吃够 5 份的人，比只吃 2 份的人低约 13%。再往上吃，不会更低。
  In plain terms: Each extra 200 g of fruits and vegetables a day cuts mortality by about 10%. People eating 5 servings a day die at about a 13% lower rate than those eating 2. Eating more doesn't lower it further.
- 收益：把多项跟踪研究合起来算（只记录、不分组）：每天多吃 200 g 果蔬，死亡风险低约 10%（RR 0.90）。这份好处一直持续到每天 800 g。美国两项大型跟踪研究加上 26 项研究的汇总：每天吃够 5 份的人，比只吃 2 份的人死亡风险低约 13%（HR 0.87）。最好的搭配是 2 份水果加 3 份蔬菜，再多不再降。
  Benefit: Pooling multiple cohort studies (observational, no randomization): each extra 200 g/day of fruits and vegetables cut mortality by about 10% (RR 0.90), with benefits continuing up to 800 g/day. Two large U.S. cohort studies plus a pooled analysis of 26 studies: 5 servings a day meant about 13% lower mortality than 2 servings (HR 0.87). The best mix was 2 servings of fruit plus 3 of vegetables; more didn't add benefit.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Aune D 等 (2017). Fruit and vegetable intake and the risk of cardiovascular disease, total cancer and all-cause mortality: a systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis of prospective studies. International Journal of Epidemiology. <https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyw319>；Wang DD 等 (2021). Fruit and Vegetable Intake and Mortality: Results From 2 Prospective Cohort Studies of US Men and Women and a Meta-Analysis of 26 Cohort Studies. Circulation. <https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.120.048996>
- 备注：吃够 5 份就行，不用凑到 10 份，两项研究在这一点上的答案一致。要注意这类研究只是跟踪记录，干扰明显：吃菜多的人，收入、教育、运动都和别人不一样。所以「风险低约 10%」已经是上限了。
  Note: Five servings is enough—no need to push to 10; both studies agree on this. Note these are observational studies with obvious confounding: heavy produce eaters differ in income, education, and exercise too. So "about 10% lower risk" is already an upper bound.

### 28. 少吃超加工食品（薯片、方便面、糕点、速食） / Eat less ultra-processed food (chips, instant noodles, pastries, ready meals)
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，换着买而已。要自己做饭，或者花时间挑没怎么加工过的食材，每天都得占些时间。难在要长期对抗「方便就好」的惯性。
  Cost: Costs nothing—just buy different things. But cooking from scratch or hunting down minimally processed ingredients takes time every day. The hard part is fighting the "convenience wins" inertia long term.
- 说人话：超加工食品吃得最多的人，同期死亡的概率比吃得最少的人高约两成，心血管死亡高五成。不过这类证据被评为不太确定，数字要往回打折看。
  In plain terms: The heaviest ultra-processed-food eaters die at about a 20% higher rate than the lightest, with cardiovascular mortality 50% higher. But this evidence is rated as uncertain, so discount the numbers.
- 收益：一篇综述把多项汇总研究又合到一起算，这些汇总底下的原始研究都只记录、不分组。结果：超加工食品吃得多的人比吃得少的人，死亡风险高约 21%（RR 1.21），心血管病死亡高约 50%（RR 1.50）。这两项的证据分级分别是「高度提示」和「令人信服」。但按通行的证据打分法（GRADE）评，确定性是低或极低。
  Benefit: An umbrella review re-pooled multiple meta-analyses whose underlying studies were all observational with no randomization. Result: high versus low ultra-processed-food intake meant about 21% higher mortality (RR 1.21) and about 50% higher cardiovascular mortality (RR 1.50). These were graded "highly suggestive" and "convincing" respectively—but by the standard GRADE rating, certainty was low or very low.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Lane MM 等 (2024). Ultra-processed food exposure and adverse health outcomes: umbrella review of epidemiological meta-analyses. BMJ. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj-2023-077310>
- 备注：争议在三点。一是这套分类（NOVA）把营养差别很大的食物划成了一类。二是它和含糖饮料、加工肉（第 7、18 条）大量重叠，分不清到底是哪一项在起作用。三是按通行的证据打分法（GRADE）评，等级也低。所以做到那两条之后，这一条还能多带来多少好处，说不准。反方的证据就是同一篇综述给出的低评级本身，目前没有得出相反结论的原始研究。所以先把第 7、18 条（不喝含糖饮料、少吃加工肉）做到，再看这一条。
  Note: Three debates. First, the NOVA classification lumps nutritionally very different foods together. Second, it overlaps heavily with sugary drinks and processed meat (items 7 and 18), so it's unclear which one is doing the work. Third, the standard GRADE rating is low anyway. So how much extra benefit this adds on top of those two items is unclear. The "opposing" evidence is the same review's own low rating—no primary study has reached the opposite conclusion. Do items 7 and 18 first, then consider this one.

### 29. 做饭、取暖不烧煤和柴，换成电或燃气 / Don't cook or heat with coal or firewood—use electricity or gas
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：在加州，家庭基本都用电或天然气，这条基本不适用；个别用壁炉、柴炉取暖的，换成电或燃气设备一次性几百到上千美元。不长期占时间。
  Cost: In California, nearly all homes already use electricity or natural gas, so this item barely applies; the rare household heating with a wood stove or fireplace would spend hundreds to a thousand-plus dollars once to switch to electric or gas. No ongoing time cost.
- 说人话：做饭烧煤或烧柴的人，同期死亡的概率高约一成；取暖烧煤柴的高约 14%（中国人群数据）。已经改用电或燃气的人，比还在烧煤柴的人死亡概率低 13%–33%。
  In plain terms: People cooking with coal or firewood die at about a 10% higher rate; those heating with solid fuels, about 14% higher (China-based data). People who switched to electricity or gas die at 13%–33% lower rates than those still burning solid fuels.
- 收益：中国有一项慢性病往后跟踪的研究，只记录、不分组，27.1 万名没有心血管病的成人。做饭用煤柴这类固体燃料的人，死亡风险比用电或燃气的人高约 11%（HR 1.11）。取暖用固体燃料的人高约 14%（HR 1.14）。已经从固体燃料换成清洁燃料的人，和还在烧的人比，做饭这项低约 13%（HR 0.87）。取暖这项低约 33%（HR 0.67）。室外的 PM2.5 另有一笔账。把 104 项跟踪研究合起来算，长期吸到的浓度每高 10 µg/m³，自然原因死亡风险高约 8%（RR 1.08）。自然原因就是非意外的病死。
  Benefit: A Chinese prospective cohort study (observational, no randomization) of 271,000 adults without cardiovascular disease: cooking with solid fuels like coal or wood meant about 11% higher mortality than electricity or gas (HR 1.11); heating with solid fuels, about 14% higher (HR 1.14). People who had switched from solid to clean fuels versus those still burning: cooking about 13% lower (HR 0.87), heating about 33% lower (HR 0.67). Outdoor PM2.5 is a separate account: pooling 104 cohort studies, each 10 µg/m³ of long-term exposure added about 8% to natural-cause mortality (RR 1.08); natural causes means non-accidental disease deaths.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Yu K 等 (2018). Association of Solid Fuel Use With Risk of Cardiovascular and All-Cause Mortality in Rural China. JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2018.2151>；Chen J, Hoek G (2020). Long-term exposure to PM and all-cause and cause-specific mortality: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Environment International. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envint.2020.105974>
- 备注：这类研究只是跟踪记录。换了燃料的家庭往往也更富裕，「风险降到 0.67 倍」这个数里混着家境的影响。至于室外的 PM2.5，个人能做的有限，无非是搬家、戴口罩、用净化器。空气净化器没有以死亡率为终点的研究，所以本节不给数字。
  Note: These are observational studies. Households that switched fuels also tend to be wealthier, so the "risk down to 0.67" figure mixes in affluence effects. As for outdoor PM2.5, individuals can do little beyond moving, masking, or air purifiers. No air-purifier study has used mortality as an endpoint, so this section gives no number for them.

### 30. 热的东西放一放再喝，不喝滚烫的茶、汤和咖啡 / Let hot drinks cool—don't drink scalding tea, soup, or coffee
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，每次多等两三分钟。难在嘴馋的时候忍住不喝。
  Cost: Costs nothing; wait two or three extra minutes each time. The hard part is holding back when you're craving it.
- 说人话：喝很烫的茶，得食管癌的概率是喝温茶的 8 倍。就算只是「热」，也有 2 倍。倒出来不到两分钟就喝，风险是等四分钟以上再喝的 5 倍。
  In plain terms: Drinking very hot tea carries 8 times the esophageal cancer risk of drinking it warm. Even "hot" doubles it. Drinking within 2 minutes of pouring carries 5 times the risk of waiting 4+ minutes.
- 收益：在伊朗北部的食管癌高发区，拿 300 名患者和 571 名没得病的人对比。下面都和喝温茶的人比。喝「热」茶的人，得食管鳞癌的机会约为 2.07 倍（比值比，95% CI 1.28–3.35，可信范围）。喝「很热」的约为 8.16 倍（3.93–16.9）。倒出后不到 2 分钟就喝的人，约为等 4 分钟以上再喝的人的 5.41 倍（2.63–11.1）
  Benefit: In a high-risk esophageal cancer area in northern Iran, 300 patients were compared with 571 healthy controls. All below versus warm-tea drinkers: "hot" tea, about 2.07 times the odds of esophageal squamous carcinoma (95% CI 1.28–3.35); "very hot," about 8.16 times (3.93–16.9). Drinking within 2 minutes of pouring versus waiting 4+ minutes, about 5.41 times (2.63–11.1).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Islami F, Pourshams A, Nasrollahzadeh D, et al. (2009). Tea drinking habits and oesophageal cancer in a high risk area in northern Iran: population based case-control study. BMJ, 338, b929. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.b929>；Loomis D, Guyton KZ, Grosse Y, et al. (2016). Carcinogenicity of drinking coffee, mate, and very hot beverages. Lancet Oncology, 17(7), 877-878. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S1470-2045(16)30239-X>
- 备注：国际癌症研究机构把 65 ℃ 以上的热饮列为 2A 类，意思是很可能致癌。同一份评估认定咖啡本身不致癌。中国的潮汕、太行山一带食管癌高发，和趁烫喝的习惯有关
  Note: The International Agency for Research on Cancer classifies drinks above 65°C as Group 2A—probably carcinogenic. The same assessment found coffee itself non-carcinogenic. In China's Chaoshan and Taihang Mountain regions, high esophageal cancer rates are linked to the habit of drinking things scalding.

### 31. 白天出门晒晒太阳，别整天不见光 / Get some sun during the day—don't avoid daylight entirely
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。每天十几分钟，上下班路上和午休就够了。
  Cost: Costs nothing. Ten-odd minutes a day—your commute and lunch break suffice.
- 说人话：刻意躲太阳的女性，预期寿命比晒得最多的人少 0.6 到 2.1 年。不吸烟但躲太阳的人，寿命和晒得最多的吸烟者差不多。
  In plain terms: Women who deliberately avoided the sun lived 0.6 to 2.1 years less than the most sun-exposed. Non-smokers who avoided the sun lived about as long as smokers with the most sun exposure.
- 收益：瑞典一项研究，2.95 万名女性，跟踪 20 年。和晒太阳最多的那组比，刻意回避晒太阳的人预期寿命少 0.6 到 2.1 年。作者写道「不吸烟但回避晒太阳的人，预期寿命与晒太阳最多组里的吸烟者相当」
  Benefit: A Swedish study of 29,500 women followed for 20 years: compared with the most sun-exposed group, deliberate sun avoiders lived 0.6 to 2.1 years less. The authors wrote that "non-smokers who avoided sun exposure had a life expectancy similar to smokers in the highest sun exposure group."
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Lindqvist PG, Epstein E, Nielsen K, et al. (2016). Avoidance of sun exposure as a risk factor for major causes of death: a competing risk analysis of the Melanoma in Southern Sweden cohort. Journal of Internal Medicine, 280(4), 375-387. <https://doi.org/10.1111/joim.12496>
- 备注：争议在于：瑞典纬度高、日照少，结论不能直接搬到加州——加州日照强得多，晒伤和皮肤癌是另一面的风险。而且回避晒太阳的人，可能本来就体弱、不爱出门。另外别晒到晒伤，正午强光下还是要遮挡，因为晒太阳会增加皮肤癌风险
  Note: The debate: Sweden is high-latitude with little sunshine, so the finding doesn't transfer directly to California—California sun is far stronger, and sunburn and skin cancer are the flip-side risks. Sun avoiders may also have been frailer and less outdoorsy to begin with. And don't get burned: cover up under strong midday sun, since sun exposure raises skin cancer risk.

### 32. 把 BMI 控制在 20–25，超重就减 / Keep BMI between 20–25; lose weight if you're overweight
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。饮食加运动每天都要占时间。这是全节最费毅力的一条：要长期管住嘴、迈开腿，减下来之后维持比减更难。
  Cost: Costs nothing. Diet plus exercise take time every day. This is the most willpower-intensive item in the whole section: it takes long-term discipline to eat less and move more, and keeping it off is harder than losing it.
- 说人话：死亡的概率在 BMI 20–25 这一段最低。BMI 到 27.5–30 高约两成，30–35 高约四成半，35–40 接近翻倍。东亚人对超重更敏感，BMI 每高 5，风险高约四成。
  In plain terms: Mortality is lowest at BMI 20–25. At 27.5–30 it's about 20% higher, at 30–35 about 45% higher, at 35–40 nearly double. East Asians are more sensitive to excess weight: each 5 BMI points adds about 40% to the risk.
- 收益：把 239 项往后跟踪的研究的个人数据合起来算（只记录、不分组）。这份分析只留了三类人：从不吸烟的、入组时没有慢性病的、入组后又活过 5 年的。结果：死亡风险在 BMI 20–25 最低。BMI 25–27.5 高约 7%（HR 1.07）。27.5–30 高约 20%（HR 1.20）。30–35 高约 45%（HR 1.45）。35–40 高约 94%（HR 1.94）。40–60 是 2.76 倍（HR 2.76）。BMI 25 以上的人每高 5 kg/m²，东亚人群的风险高约 39%（HR 1.39）。
  Benefit: Pooling individual-participant data from 239 prospective studies (observational, no randomization). This analysis kept only three groups: never-smokers, people with no chronic disease at enrollment, and people who survived the first 5 years. Result: mortality was lowest at BMI 20–25. BMI 25–27.5 was about 7% higher (HR 1.07); 27.5–30 about 20% higher (HR 1.20); 30–35 about 45% higher (HR 1.45); 35–40 about 94% higher (HR 1.94); 40–60, 2.76-fold (HR 2.76). Among people above BMI 25, each 5 kg/m² added about 39% to the risk in East Asian populations (HR 1.39).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Global BMI Mortality Collaboration (2016). Body-mass index and all-cause mortality: individual-participant-data meta-analysis of 239 prospective studies in four continents. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(16)30175-1>；Flegal KM 等 (2013). Association of all-cause mortality with overweight and obesity using standard body mass index categories: a systematic review and meta-analysis. JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2012.113905>（争议方）
- 备注：争议。有一方认为「胖一点反而活得久」。他们汇总后得到：超重的人死亡风险低约 6%，轻度肥胖的低约 5%。双方分歧主要在要不要把三类数据剔掉：抽烟的人、已经得病的人、刚入组头几年的数据。之所以要剔，是因为重病的人会先瘦下来。那项汇总 239 项研究的分析把这三类剔掉之后，超重带来的风险又出现了。另外还要注意，没有随机试验证明减重本身能降低总死亡率。上面的收益是拿不同胖瘦的人横着比出来的，不代表减掉就能降这么多。要减重的人不必在吃饭时间上下功夫，早餐和 16:8 轻断食都没有额外好处，见第 6 节第 26 条（不要指望吃早餐或 16:8 帮你控制体重）
  Note: Debated. One camp argues "a little extra weight helps you live longer," pooling to about 6% lower mortality for the overweight and 5% lower for mildly obese. The disagreement is mainly about whether to exclude three kinds of data: smokers, people already ill, and the first few years of follow-up. The exclusions exist because seriously ill people lose weight first. After the 239-study analysis excluded them, the excess-weight risk reappeared. Also note: no randomized trial has shown that losing weight itself lowers all-cause mortality. The benefits above compare leaner versus heavier people cross-sectionally—they don't mean losing weight cuts your risk by that much. And meal timing won't help you lose weight: neither breakfast nor 16:8 fasting adds benefit—see section 6, item 26.

### 33. 每周吃四次以上辣椒 / Eat chili peppers four or more times a week
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不占时间。
  Cost: Costs nothing and takes no time.
- 说人话：每周吃四次以上辣椒的人，同期死亡的概率比很少吃的人低约 23%，心血管死亡低约三分之一。
  In plain terms: People eating chili peppers 4+ times a week die at about a 23% lower rate than rare eaters, with cardiovascular mortality about a third lower.
- 收益：意大利一项 2.28 万人的研究，其中一半人跟踪满了 8.2 年，共 1236 人去世。每周吃辣椒 4 次以上的人，死亡风险比很少吃的人低约 23%（风险比 0.77，95% CI 0.66–0.90，可信范围）。心血管死亡低约 34%（0.66，0.50–0.86）
  Benefit: An Italian study of 22,800 people, half followed for the full 8.2 years, with 1,236 deaths. People eating chili peppers 4+ times a week had about 23% lower mortality than rare eaters (hazard ratio 0.77, 95% CI 0.66–0.90), with cardiovascular mortality about 34% lower (0.66, 0.50–0.86).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Bonaccio M, Di Castelnuovo A, Costanzo S, et al. (2019). Chili Pepper Consumption and Mortality in Italian Adults. Journal of the American College of Cardiology, 74(25), 3139-3149. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2019.09.068>
- 备注：争议在于：这是在地中海饮食的背景下做的跟踪记录，吃辣的人可能整体饮食更接近当地的传统吃法。还要注意，有胃食管反流、痔疮、肠易激的人，吃辣会加重症状，不必勉强
  Note: The debate: this was observed against a Mediterranean-diet background, and chili eaters may have eaten closer to local traditions overall. Also note: if you have acid reflux, hemorrhoids, or IBS, spicy food worsens symptoms—don't force it.

### 34. 每天喝一两份奶或酸奶 / Have one or two servings of milk or yogurt a day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：每天一美元左右，不占时间。
  Cost: About a dollar a day; no time needed.
- 说人话：每天喝两份以上奶或者酸奶的人，同期死亡的概率比不喝的人低约 17%。
  In plain terms: People having two-plus servings of milk or yogurt a day die at about a 17% lower rate than non-drinkers.
- 收益：PURE 跟踪研究覆盖 21 个国家、13.6 万人，平均跟踪 9.1 年，共 6796 人去世。每天吃两份以上奶制品的人，死亡风险比不吃的人低约 17%（风险比 0.83，95% CI 0.72–0.96，可信范围），趋势 P=0.0052
  Benefit: The PURE cohort covered 21 countries and 136,000 people followed for an average of 9.1 years, with 6,796 deaths. People eating two-plus servings of dairy a day had about 17% lower mortality than non-eaters (hazard ratio 0.83, 95% CI 0.72–0.96; trend P=0.0052).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Dehghan M, Mente A, Rangarajan S, et al. (2018). Association of dairy intake with cardiovascular disease and mortality in 21 countries from five continents (PURE): a prospective cohort study. Lancet, 392(10161), 2288-2299. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(18)31812-9>
- 备注：争议在于：PURE 纳入了大量中低收入国家，在那里能天天喝奶本身就说明家境不错，这层干扰很难排干净。另外乳糖不耐受的人改喝酸奶或者低乳糖奶，不必硬喝牛奶
  Note: The debate: PURE included many low- and middle-income countries, where being able to drink milk daily itself signals affluence—hard to fully adjust for. If you're lactose intolerant, switch to yogurt or lactose-free milk; don't force plain milk.

### 35. 鸡蛋不用戒，但别每天三四个 / Eggs are fine—just not three or four a day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不占时间。
  Cost: Costs nothing and takes no time.
- 说人话：每天多吃半个全蛋，同期死亡的概率高约 7%。把这半个换成蛋清、鱼、禽肉或者坚果，各项死亡风险反而都更低。不用戒鸡蛋，只是别每天吃三四个。
  In plain terms: Each extra half whole egg a day raises mortality by about 7%. Swapping that half for egg white, fish, poultry, or nuts lowers mortality across the board instead. No need to give up eggs—just don't eat three or four a day.
- 收益：美国一项 52.1 万人的跟踪研究，共 12.9 万人去世。每天多吃半个全蛋，死亡风险高约 7%（风险比 1.07，95% CI 1.06–1.08，这是可信范围）。把这半个全蛋换成等量的蛋清、禽肉、鱼、奶制品或者坚果豆类，死亡风险会更低，心血管、癌症、呼吸系统这三项的死亡风险也都更低
  Benefit: A U.S. cohort study of 521,000 people with 129,000 deaths: each extra half whole egg a day raised mortality by about 7% (hazard ratio 1.07, 95% CI 1.06–1.08). Swapping that half for equal amounts of egg white, poultry, fish, dairy, or nuts and legumes lowered mortality—and cardiovascular, cancer, and respiratory mortality too.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Zhuang P, Wu F, Mao L, et al. (2021). Egg and cholesterol consumption and mortality from cardiovascular and different causes in the United States: A population-based cohort study. PLoS Medicine, 18(2), e1003508. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1003508>
- 备注：争议很大：也有长期跟踪大批人的研究，以及多项研究的汇总，没发现鸡蛋和死亡率有关。美国膳食指南（Dietary Guidelines for Americans，美国农业部/卫生部）已不再设胆固醇每日上限。要避免的只是每天吃三四个，一天一个不必担心
  Note: Heavily debated: other long-term cohort studies and pooled analyses found no link between eggs and mortality. The U.S. Dietary Guidelines for Americans (USDA/HHS) no longer set a daily cholesterol cap. Only avoid three or four a day; one a day is nothing to worry about.

### 36. 有条件就泡澡，别只冲淋浴 / Take a bath when you can—don't only shower
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：多花些水费电费。每次半小时。
  Cost: A bit more on the water and power bill. Half an hour each time.
- 说人话：几乎每天泡澡的人，心血管事件的风险比每周泡不到两次的人低约 28%，脑出血低约 46%。
  In plain terms: Near-daily bathers have about 28% lower cardiovascular-event risk than those bathing twice a week or less, with brain hemorrhage about 46% lower.
- 收益：日本一项 3.0 万人的研究，跟踪 19 年，共 2097 例心血管事件。几乎每天泡澡的人，比每周泡不超过两次的人心血管事件风险低约 28%（风险比 0.72，95% CI 0.62–0.84，这是可信范围）。冠心病低约 35%（0.65，0.45–0.94）。脑卒中低约 26%（0.74，0.62–0.87）。脑出血低约 46%（0.54，0.40–0.73）
  Benefit: A Japanese study of 30,000 people followed for 19 years, with 2,097 cardiovascular events. Near-daily bathers had about 28% lower cardiovascular-event risk than those bathing no more than twice a week (hazard ratio 0.72, 95% CI 0.62–0.84); coronary heart disease about 35% lower (0.65, 0.45–0.94); stroke about 26% lower (0.74, 0.62–0.87); brain hemorrhage about 46% lower (0.54, 0.40–0.73).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Ukai T, Iso H, Yamagishi K, et al. (2020). Habitual tub bathing and risks of incident coronary heart disease and stroke. Heart, 106(10), 732-737. <https://doi.org/10.1136/heartjnl-2019-315752>
- 备注：争议在于：这是在日本人群和浴缸文化下做出的结果，在美国日常泡澡的习惯远没有那么普遍。另外水别太烫，也别泡太久。对老年人和有心脑血管病的人，水温过高、泡得太久反而危险，日本每年有大量老人在浴缸里猝死
  Note: The debate: this comes from Japanese people and a bathtub culture; daily bathing is far less common in the U.S. Also, don't make the water too hot or soak too long. For older adults and people with cardiovascular disease, overly hot water and long soaks are actually dangerous—many elderly Japanese die suddenly in bathtubs each year.

### 37. 把午睡控制在半小时内，别超过一小时，非得睡一两小时才撑得住就去查原因 / Keep naps under 30 minutes, never over an hour; if you need 1–2 hours to function, get checked
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。睡前定个闹钟就行，几乎不用毅力。
  Cost: Costs nothing. Just set an alarm before you doze off—barely any willpower needed.
- 说人话：午睡不超过一小时的人，死亡和慢性病的风险没有升高，脑子还更清醒一些。睡过一小时的人，冠心病风险高约三成，糖尿病和肥胖高约两成。所以定个闹钟，半小时叫醒自己。要是每天非得睡一两小时才撑得住，那是该去查身体的信号。
  In plain terms: Nappers staying under an hour show no higher mortality or chronic-disease risk—and think a bit more clearly. Napping over an hour is tied to about 30% higher coronary risk and about 20% higher diabetes and obesity risk. So set an alarm and wake yourself in half an hour. If you need one to two hours every day just to function, that's a signal to get checked out.
- 收益：一篇伞形综述汇总了 16 篇荟萃分析、244 个健康结局。伞形综述就是把已有的汇总研究再汇总一遍。结论是：午睡少于 60 分钟的人，总死亡率和慢性病风险都没有升高，脑力改善也最明显（SMD 0.69，95% CI 0.37–1.00，这是可信范围）。20 到 30 分钟的短午睡对运动表现的改善最大（SMD 0.99，0.67–1.31）。午睡超过 60 分钟的人，冠心病风险高约 30%，糖尿病和肥胖风险高约 20%。另一项研究让 1338 名 56 岁以上的老人戴腕表实测午睡，不靠自己填问卷。跟踪最长 19 年，期间 926 人去世。午睡每多 1 小时，死亡风险高约 13%（HR 1.13，95% CI 1.04–1.23）。每天多睡一次，高约 7%（HR 1.07，1.02–1.13）。在上午睡的人，比在下午早些时候睡的人高约 30%（HR 1.30，1.03–1.64）。
  Benefit: An umbrella review pooled 16 meta-analyses covering 244 health outcomes. (An umbrella review re-pools existing meta-analyses.) Conclusion: nappers under 60 minutes showed no higher total mortality or chronic-disease risk, and the clearest cognitive improvement (SMD 0.69, 95% CI 0.37–1.00). 20–30-minute naps improved athletic performance most (SMD 0.99, 0.67–1.31). Napping over 60 minutes was tied to about 30% higher coronary risk and about 20% higher diabetes and obesity risk. Another study had 1,338 adults over 56 wear wrist devices to measure naps objectively—no self-reports. Over up to 19 years of follow-up, 926 died. Each extra napping hour added about 13% to mortality (HR 1.13, 95% CI 1.04–1.23); each extra nap per day added about 7% (HR 1.07, 1.02–1.13); morning nappers ran about 30% higher than early-afternoon nappers (HR 1.30, 1.03–1.64).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Du P, Li J, Hua Z, 等 (2026). Multiple Health Outcomes of Daytime Napping: A Comprehensive Umbrella Review. Public Health Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.3389/phrs.2026.1609013>；Gao C, Cai R, Zheng X, 等 (2026). Objectively Measured Daytime Napping Patterns and All-Cause Mortality in Older Adults. JAMA Network Open. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2026.7938>；Dashti HS 等 (2021). Genetic determinants of daytime napping and effects on cardiometabolic health. Nature Communications. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-20585-3>
- 备注：争议。上面两项都是只记录、不分组的研究。午睡时间长的人，本来就更可能有睡眠呼吸暂停、贫血、甲状腺功能减退、抑郁或者别的慢性病。所以「睡得久的人死得多」里有多少是午睡本身造成的，说不清。用基因做的因果推断（孟德尔随机化，45.3 万人加 54.1 万人）只支持一件事：午睡越频繁，血压和腰围越高一点。它没有查到午睡对冠心病和糖尿病有因果影响。所以别把「少睡午觉」当成降死亡率的手段。「去查原因」那半是作者建议，属于 C 级：白天困到必须长睡，先查夜里的睡眠，打呼噜和睡觉憋气要查睡眠呼吸暂停，再查血常规和甲状腺。短午睡怎么提神见第 3 节第 11 条（下午困了睡 10 分钟）。夜间睡多久见本节第 13 条（每晚睡 7 小时）。
  Note: Debated. Both studies above are observational with no randomization. Long nappers are more likely to already have sleep apnea, anemia, hypothyroidism, depression, or other chronic conditions—so how much of "long nappers die more" is caused by napping itself is unclear. Genetic causal inference (Mendelian randomization, 453,000 plus 541,000 people) supports only one thing: more frequent napping slightly raises blood pressure and waist size. It found no causal effect of napping on coronary disease or diabetes. So don't treat "napping less" as a mortality-reduction strategy. The "get checked" half is the author's suggestion, grade C: if daytime sleepiness forces long naps, check nighttime sleep first—snoring and gasping mean screening for sleep apnea—then blood counts and thyroid. For using short naps to recharge, see section 3, item 11; for nighttime sleep duration, see this section's item 13.

### 38. 熬夜之后第二天晚上就补觉，别攒到周末 / After a late night, catch up the next night—don't save it for the weekend
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。补的那一两个小时本来就想睡。
  Cost: Costs nothing. You wanted those one or two hours of sleep anyway.
- 说人话：少睡之后第二天晚上没补上的人，死亡风险比作息规律的人高约 15%，少得厉害又不补的高约 42%。补上了的人看不出风险升高。所以熬了夜，第二天晚上就早点上床。
  In plain terms: People who don't catch up the night after sleeping too little die at about a 15% higher rate than the schedule-regular; those deeply sleep-deprived and not catching up, about 42% higher. Those who catch up show no elevated risk. So after a late night, get to bed early the next night.
- 收益：英国生物银行 85,618 人，平均年龄 61.8 岁，戴腕表记录逐日睡眠，中位随访 8 年。研究先按每个人自己的睡眠需求算出「少睡的夜」，指比自己需要的少睡 2.5 小时以上。少睡那段之后的第一晚算「补觉夜」，这一晚比自己需要的多睡就算补上了。按这个分成五种模式。和作息规律的人相比，少睡又不补的全因死亡风险高约 15%（HR 1.15，95% CI 1.01–1.31，这是可信范围）。累计少睡超过 3.5 小时又不补的高约 42%（HR 1.42，1.24–1.63）。补上了的两组都没有显著关联，其中少睡后补是 HR 1.12（0.98–1.28）。本来就睡得短的人里，少睡不补高约 19%（1.19，1.01–1.40），严重少睡不补高约 38%（1.38，1.17–1.63）。这个结果在另一批 4,586 人的美国健康调查里也重复出来了。
  Benefit: UK Biobank, 85,618 people with mean age 61.8, wrist-measured daily sleep, median 8 years of follow-up. The study first calculated each person's own sleep need, defining "short-sleep nights" as 2.5+ hours below need. The first night after a short-sleep stretch counted as the "catch-up night"—sleeping more than needed that night counted as catching up. Five patterns were defined. Versus the schedule-regular: short sleep without catch-up meant about 15% higher all-cause mortality (HR 1.15, 95% CI 1.01–1.31); cumulative shortfall over 3.5 hours without catch-up, about 42% higher (HR 1.42, 1.24–1.63). Neither catch-up group showed significant association (short-then-caught-up: HR 1.12, 0.98–1.28). Among habitual short sleepers, short-without-catch-up was about 19% higher (1.19, 1.01–1.40), severe-short-without-catch-up about 38% higher (1.38, 1.17–1.63). The result replicated in a separate 4,586-person U.S. health survey.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Li X, Zhang M, Li Z, 等 (2026). Acute sleep rebound following sleep restriction is associated with reduced mortality risk. Nature Communications. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-72461-1>
- 备注：这是只记录、不分组的研究。补不上的人可能本来就更忙、身体更差，这一层排不干净。「补上了的两组看不出风险升高」只是没查出差别，它们的点估计仍然大于 1，补了也不等于没事。所以熬了夜要尽快还上，也不能因此随便熬夜。这项研究里的补觉指少睡之后第一晚多睡一会儿，不包括攒到周末一次补。把「工作日熬、周末补」固定成每周的节奏，叫社会时差，本身和心血管病有关，见本节第 13 条（每晚睡 7 小时左右，作息固定）。周末也固定起床那条见第 3 节第 2 条（固定起床时间，周末也一样），和本条不冲突：本条让你当晚早点上床，不是让你第二天睡到中午。
  Note: This is an observational study with no randomization. People who can't catch up may be busier or sicker to begin with—unadjustable. "No elevated risk in the catch-up groups" just means no detected difference; their point estimates still exceed 1, so catching up doesn't equal zero harm. Pay back sleep debt fast after a late night—but don't use it as license to stay up. In this study, catch-up meant sleeping extra the first night after short sleep, not banking it until the weekend. Making "weekday late nights, weekend catch-up" a weekly rhythm is social jet lag, itself tied to cardiovascular disease—see item 13. The fixed-weekend-wake-time rule is section 3, item 2, and doesn't conflict with this one: this item says go to bed earlier that night, not sleep till noon the next day.

### 39. 上夜班干得越久心血管风险越高，能转岗就趁早转 / The longer you work nights, the higher the heart risk—switch sooner if you can
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：转岗或换工作可能少拿钱，夜班津贴也没了。找工作要花时间，下决心也难。如果是挑工作的时候把这笔账算进去，成本是零。
  Cost: Switching roles or jobs may mean less pay, and the night-shift differential goes away. Job hunting takes time, and deciding is hard. If you factor this in when choosing a job, the cost is zero.
- 说人话：上夜班的人，心血管病比不上夜班的人多约 13%，心血管死亡多约 27%。而且干得越久越多：每多上 5 年夜班，心血管病再多约 7%。所以这笔账按年数算，能转岗就趁早。至于夜班致癌，证据比流传的说法软得多。
  In plain terms: Night-shift workers have about 13% more cardiovascular disease and 27% more cardiovascular death than day workers. And it compounds with tenure: every 5 extra years of night shifts adds about 7% more cardiovascular disease. So do this math by years—switch sooner if you can. As for night shifts causing cancer, the evidence is far softer than the rumors.
- 收益：把 23 项跟踪研究合起来算，只记录、不分组。上夜班的人心血管事件风险高约 13%（RR 1.13，95% CI 1.10–1.16，这是可信范围）。心血管死亡高约 27%（RR 1.27，1.18–1.36）。按年数看：每多上 5 年夜班，心血管病发生率再高约 7%（RR 1.07，1.04–1.09），心血管死亡再高约 5%（RR 1.05，1.03–1.06）。分病看，冠心病发生高约 22%（1.22，1.16–1.28），冠心病死亡高约 22%（1.22，1.10–1.36），缺血性心脏病死亡高约 39%（1.39，1.06–1.84），卒中死亡高约 49%（1.49，1.04–2.12）。卒中的发生率那一项不显著（1.06，0.95–1.18）。
  Benefit: Pooling 23 cohort studies (observational, no randomization): night-shift workers had about 13% higher cardiovascular-event risk (RR 1.13, 95% CI 1.10–1.16) and about 27% higher cardiovascular death (RR 1.27, 1.18–1.36). By tenure: each extra 5 years of night shifts added about 7% to cardiovascular incidence (RR 1.07, 1.04–1.09) and about 5% to cardiovascular death (RR 1.05, 1.03–1.06). By disease: coronary incidence about 22% higher (1.22, 1.16–1.28), coronary death about 22% higher (1.22, 1.10–1.36), ischemic heart disease death about 39% higher (1.39, 1.06–1.84), stroke death about 49% higher (1.49, 1.04–2.12). Stroke incidence was not significant (1.06, 0.95–1.18).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Xi J, Ma W, Tao Y, 等 (2025). Association between night shift work and cardiovascular disease: a systematic review and dose-response meta-analysis. Frontiers in Public Health. <https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2025.1668848>；Esposito G, Bravi F, Santucci C, 等 (2025). Night shift work and breast cancer risk in healthcare workers: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Occupational Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1093/occmed/kqaf040>；Shen QM, Li ZY, Tan YT, 等 (2026). Night shift work and risk of total and site-specific cancer: results from a prospective cohort study among Chinese men. Scandinavian Journal of Work, Environment & Health. <https://doi.org/10.5271/sjweh.4290>；Czeisler CA, Johnson MP, Duffy JF, 等 (1990). Exposure to bright light and darkness to treat physiologic maladaptation to night work. New England Journal of Medicine, 322(18), 1253-1259. <https://doi.org/10.1093/NEJM199005033221801>
- 备注：这些都是只记录、不分组的研究。上夜班的人在吸烟、体重、收入上本来就和别人不一样，校正不干净。夜班致癌这件事，证据比流传的说法软得多。汇总 12 项研究、12,132 例乳腺癌的分析里，干过夜班的人风险只高约 5%，可信范围跨过了 1（RR 1.05，0.96–1.14）。干满 20 年那一档高约 25%（1.25，1.01–1.55），但作者做影响分析后这一项就不显著了，校正可能的发表偏倚后接近 1，原话是这个关联「远未确立」。另一项跟踪中国男性 16.1 年、8,202 例癌症的研究里，夜班和总癌症及多数常见癌都没有关联，只有胰腺癌在累计干 11 到 20 年那一档高约 59%（HR 1.59，1.09–2.31）。所以这条按心血管算账，不按癌症。不是谁都该离开夜班岗位，按年数算的这笔账要你自己算。已经在干的，先把能改的改掉：戒烟见本节第 1 条（戒烟，越早越好），血压血脂见本节第 12 条（有高血压、高血脂就按医嘱规律吃药），夜班之后的觉怎么补见本节第 38 条（熬夜之后第二天晚上就补觉）。另有两条能试的对策：把进食集中在白天，以及用强光加白天严格遮光把生物钟搬过去。后者是唯一被证明真能搬动生物钟的办法。普通室内照明下连上六个夜班，生物钟纹丝不动；换成 7000 到 12000 勒克斯的强光加白天近乎全黑，四天就整个倒了过来。**夜里的光要够亮、白天睡觉要严格遮光、下班路上戴墨镜挡晨光，三样缺一不可**，漏掉最后一样等于白做。两条对策的终点都只到代谢指标和睡眠，没有试验测过照做能不能把心血管风险降下来。休息日过回白天的生活，钟还会被打回去。剂量、原始数字和其余来源，连同身体怎么认时间，都在 [docs/生物钟和夜班.md](../docs/生物钟和夜班.md)。
  Note: All of these are observational studies with no randomization. Night-shift workers differ from others in smoking, weight, and income—adjustment is imperfect. On night shifts causing cancer, the evidence is far softer than rumored. Pooling 12 studies with 12,132 breast cancer cases: ever-night-shift workers ran only about 5% higher risk, with the confidence interval crossing 1 (RR 1.05, 0.96–1.14). The 20-years-full tier ran about 25% higher (1.25, 1.01–1.55), but that faded in influence analysis and neared 1 after correcting for possible publication bias—the authors' words were that the association is "far from established." In another study tracking Chinese men for 16.1 years with 8,202 cancers, night shifts were linked to neither total cancer nor most common cancers—only pancreatic cancer in the 11-to-20 cumulative-years tier, about 59% higher (HR 1.59, 1.09–2.31). So this item does its math on cardiovascular risk, not cancer. Not everyone should leave night shifts—run the per-year math yourself. If you're on nights, fix what's fixable first: quitting smoking (item 1), blood pressure and cholesterol (item 12), and post-night-shift sleep (item 38). Two more strategies to try: concentrate eating in the daytime, and shift your body clock with bright light plus strict daytime darkness. The latter is the only method proven to actually move the clock. Under ordinary indoor lighting, six straight night shifts don't budge the clock at all; with 7,000–12,000 lux bright light plus near-total daytime darkness, it fully flips in four days. **Night light must be bright enough, daytime sleep must be strictly dark, and sunglasses must block morning light on the way home—all three are required**; skipping the last one wastes the effort. Both strategies were only measured to metabolic and sleep endpoints—no trial has tested whether they lower cardiovascular risk. The clock gets knocked back if you live on daytime hours on rest days. Doses, original numbers, remaining sources, and how the body tells time are all in [docs/生物钟和夜班.md](../docs/生物钟和夜班.md).

### 40. 买正规渠道的食用油，霉变的花生制品别碰 / Buy cooking oil from reputable sources; avoid moldy peanut products
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：买正规品牌、标签齐全的食用油，钱差不多。难在说服还迷信「自榨更香更健康」的长辈。
  Cost: Reputable brands with complete labels cost about the same. The hard part is persuading older relatives who still believe "home-pressed is tastier and healthier."
- 说人话：花生受潮发霉会长黄曲霉毒素，它是明确的致癌物，主要伤肝（中国人群数据：广州管住小作坊之后，常吃自榨油的居民肝功能异常少了约三成半。广西的孕妇吃自榨花生油，孩子出生体重偏低的风险高约九成，早产高约八成）。
  In plain terms: Moldy peanuts grow aflatoxin—a definite carcinogen that mainly attacks the liver (China-based data: after Guangzhou regulated small oil workshops, liver-function abnormalities fell by about 35% among regular home-pressed-oil users; in Guangxi, pregnant women eating home-pressed peanut oil had about 90% higher risk of low-birth-weight babies and about 80% higher risk of preterm birth).
- 收益：黄曲霉毒素是国际癌症研究机构定的 1 类致癌物（确定能让人得癌），主要是肝癌。广州一项研究测了 2010 到 2022 年小作坊自榨花生油里的黄曲霉毒素 B1。中位数 1.29 μg/kg，中间一半样品在 0.12 到 6.58 之间。当地出台小作坊管理条例后，油里的毒素马上降了 2.865 μg/kg，之后每年再降 2.593。常吃自榨油那片居民的肝功能异常比例随之下降（PR 0.650，95% CI 0.469–0.902，可信范围），低约 35%。广西一项跟踪 1611 名孕妇的研究里，81.7% 吃自榨花生油。吃的人孩子低出生体重的风险高约九成（aOR 1.9，1.1–3.2），早产高约八成（aOR 1.8，1.1–3.0）。
  Benefit: Aflatoxin is an IARC Group 1 carcinogen (definitely carcinogenic to humans), mainly causing liver cancer. A Guangzhou study measured aflatoxin B1 in workshop home-pressed peanut oil from 2010 to 2022: median 1.29 μg/kg, middle half of samples between 0.12 and 6.58. After local workshop regulations, toxin levels dropped immediately by 2.865 μg/kg and then by 2.593 per year. Liver-function abnormalities among regular home-pressed-oil consumers fell accordingly (PR 0.650, 95% CI 0.469–0.902), about 35% lower. A Guangxi cohort of 1,611 pregnant women, 81.7% of whom ate home-pressed peanut oil: their babies had about 90% higher low-birth-weight risk (aOR 1.9, 1.1–3.2) and about 80% higher preterm risk (aOR 1.8, 1.1–3.0).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Lei J, Li Y, Wang Y, 等 (2024). The impact of small food workshops management regulations on aflatoxin B1 in home-made peanut oil and the liver function of high-consumption area residents: an interrupted time series study in Guangzhou, China. Frontiers in Public Health, 12. <https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2024.1484414>；Zhong Y, Lu H, Jiang Y, 等 (2024). Effect of homemade peanut oil consumption during pregnancy on low birth weight and preterm birth outcomes: a cohort study in Southwestern China. Global Health Action, 17. <https://doi.org/10.1080/16549716.2024.2336312>；IARC (2012). Chemical Agents and Related Occupations. IARC Monographs on the Evaluation of Carcinogenic Risks to Humans, Vol 100F. <https://publications.iarc.fr/123>
- 备注：在美国，花生制品的黄曲霉毒素有 FDA 的限量标准（以 FDA 现行规定为准）；买正规渠道、标签齐全的产品即可。两项中国研究都只是跟踪记录、没有分组对照。广州那项的终点是肝功能化验，不是肝癌和死亡。受潮发霉的花生和花生制品直接扔掉，别舍不得。本条受益人主要是你和一起吃饭的家人。
  Note: In the U.S., the FDA sets limits on aflatoxin in peanut products (per current FDA regulations); buying properly labeled products from reputable sources is enough. Both Chinese studies are observational with no controls. The Guangzhou study's endpoint was liver-function tests, not liver cancer or death. Throw out damp, moldy peanuts and peanut products—don't try to salvage them. This item mainly benefits you and the family you eat with.

### 41. 做饭用植物油代替猪油和黄油，不必为了健康换着买油，也别指望亚麻籽油护心 / Cook with vegetable oil instead of lard or butter; no need to rotate oils for health, and don't count on flaxseed oil for your heart
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不多花钱。植物油本来就是大多数家庭的主力。难在改掉用猪油炒菜、拌饭的口味习惯。
  Cost: Barely any extra cost—vegetable oil is already most households' main oil. The hard part is breaking the habit of cooking or dressing rice with lard.
- 说人话：少吃猪油黄油这类饱和脂肪、换成植物油，心脏病和中风这类事少约一成七，但总的死亡风险没看出变化。大豆油、玉米油、菜籽油之间换来换去，没有证据说有什么区别。亚麻籽油吃多了，心脏病风险也没看出下降。
  In plain terms: Cutting saturated fat like lard and butter in favor of vegetable oil reduces heart attacks, strokes, and similar events by about 17%, but total mortality doesn't budge. Rotating between soybean, corn, and canola oil shows no evidence of any difference. Eating more flaxseed oil shows no heart benefit either.
- 收益：Cochrane 汇总了 15 项随机试验、56,675 人，每项至少做两年。少吃饱和脂肪，心血管事件合计低约 17%（RR 0.83，0.70–0.98）。总死亡率几乎不变（RR 0.96，0.90–1.03），心血管死亡也不变（RR 0.95，0.80–1.12）。换成多不饱和脂肪（大豆油、玉米油的主要成分）和换成碳水，效果没看出差别。换成单不饱和脂肪（菜籽油、橄榄油的主要成分）的数据太少，说不清。另一篇 Cochrane 汇总 19 项随机试验、6461 人，多吃 omega-6（亚油酸）对总死亡率 RR 1.00（0.88–1.12），对心血管事件 RR 0.97（0.81–1.15），看不出好处也看不出害处。第三篇汇总 86 项随机试验、162,796 人，多吃植物来源的 omega-3（亚麻籽油、紫苏油的主要成分），总死亡率 RR 1.01（0.84–1.20），冠心病事件 RR 1.00（0.82–1.22），基本没有作用。
  Benefit: Cochrane pooled 15 randomized trials with 56,675 people, each lasting at least two years. Cutting saturated fat lowered combined cardiovascular events by about 17% (RR 0.83, 0.70–0.98). Total mortality barely moved (RR 0.96, 0.90–1.03), nor did cardiovascular death (RR 0.95, 0.80–1.12). Replacing with polyunsaturated fat (the main component of soybean and corn oil) or with carbohydrates showed no difference. Data on replacing with monounsaturated fat (the main component of canola and olive oil) was too thin to judge. A second Cochrane review pooled 19 randomized trials with 6,461 people: more omega-6 (linoleic acid) gave total-mortality RR 1.00 (0.88–1.12) and cardiovascular-event RR 0.97 (0.81–1.15)—neither benefit nor harm. A third pooled 86 randomized trials with 162,796 people: more plant-source omega-3 (the main component of flaxseed and perilla oil) gave total-mortality RR 1.01 (0.84–1.20) and coronary-event RR 1.00 (0.82–1.22)—essentially no effect.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Hooper L, Martin N, Jimoh OF, 等 (2020). Reduction in saturated fat for cardiovascular disease. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (5), CD011737. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD011737.pub3>；Hooper L, Al-Khudairy L, Abdelhamid AS, 等 (2018). Omega-6 fats for the primary and secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (11), CD011094. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD011094.pub4>；Abdelhamid AS, Brown TJ, Brainard JS, 等 (2020). Omega-3 fatty acids for the primary and secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (3), CD003177. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD003177.pub5>；U.S. Department of Agriculture and U.S. Department of Health and Human Services. Dietary Guidelines for Americans（美国版膳食指南）
- 备注：争议：有人主张大豆油、玉米油里的 omega-6 太多会促炎，所以要少吃、换油。上面 omega-6 那篇的随机试验没看出害处，证据质量作者自评为低。收益量级按心血管事件低 17% 定为中，总死亡率没变。哪种油都要控制用量，美国膳食指南同样建议烹调用油适量、控制总量。
  Note: The debate: some argue the omega-6 in soybean and corn oil is pro-inflammatory, so you should eat less and switch oils. The randomized trials in the omega-6 review above showed no harm (the authors self-rated the evidence quality as low). Benefit is rated "medium" on the 17% lower cardiovascular events; total mortality unchanged. Every oil should be used in moderation—the U.S. Dietary Guidelines likewise advise keeping cooking-oil amounts moderate and controlled.

### 42. 炒菜、煎炸时打开抽油烟机，从开火一直开到做完 / Turn on the range hood when stir-frying or deep-frying—keep it on from start to finish
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：家里装了抽油烟机的，只多花一点电费。没装的，买一台连安装要两三百到上千美元。难在每次开火都记得开，别嫌吵就关掉。
  Cost: If you have a range hood, it costs a bit of electricity. If not, buying and installing one runs a few hundred to a thousand-plus dollars. The hard part is remembering to turn it on every time and not switching it off because it's noisy.
- 说人话：不吸烟的中国女性（中国人群数据），炒菜时不开或很少开抽油烟机，得肺癌的风险约是常开的两倍。住过厨房通风差的房子，风险也高约五成，烧煤的人家最明显。
  In plain terms: Among non-smoking Chinese women (China-based data), rarely or never using a range hood while cooking carried about twice the lung cancer risk of always using one. Having lived in a poorly ventilated kitchen raised risk by about 50%, most clearly in coal-burning households.
- 收益：把 10 项病例对照研究合起来算，对象都是不吸烟的中国女性。病例对照是拿肺癌病人和没病的人比，回头问过去的习惯。接触做饭油烟的人，得肺癌的机会约为 1.74 倍（比值比，95% CI 1.57–1.94，可信范围）。其中 4 项比的是接触油烟又不用抽油烟机的人，约为 2.11 倍（1.54–2.89）。台湾一项研究拿 1302 名不吸烟的女性肺癌病人，和 1302 名没病的人对比。只看做饭十年以上的人。三分之二以上的年头都开抽油烟机的，和三分之一以下的比，风险低约一半（比值比 0.49，0.32–0.76）。上海有一项往后跟踪的研究，7.1 万名不吸烟的女性，从 1996 年跟到 2009 年，查出 429 例肺癌。自己说住过厨房通风差的房子的，得肺癌的风险高约 49%（HR 1.49，1.15–1.95）。烧煤又通风差 20 年以上的，约为 2.03 倍（HR 2.03，1.35–3.05）。单看烧没烧过煤，没看出差别（HR 1.03，0.84–1.26）。炒菜用哪种油，也没看出差别。
  Benefit: Pooling 10 case-control studies, all in non-smoking Chinese women (case-control compares lung cancer patients with healthy people and asks about past habits retrospectively). Cooking-oil-fume exposure carried about 1.74 times the odds of lung cancer (95% CI 1.57–1.94); in 4 studies of fume exposure without a range hood, about 2.11 times (1.54–2.89). A Taiwan study compared 1,302 non-smoking female lung cancer patients with 1,302 controls. Among women cooking for 10+ years, those using a range hood in over two-thirds of those years had about half the risk of those using one in under a third (odds ratio 0.49, 0.32–0.76). A Shanghai prospective study followed 71,000 non-smoking women from 1996 to 2009 and found 429 lung cancers. Women reporting having lived in poorly ventilated kitchens had about 49% higher risk (HR 1.49, 1.15–1.95); coal-burning plus poor ventilation for 20+ years, about 2.03-fold (HR 2.03, 1.35–3.05). Coal burning alone showed no difference (HR 1.03, 0.84–1.26); cooking-oil type showed no difference either.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Xue Y, Jiang Y, Jin S, Li Y (2016). Association between cooking oil fume exposure and lung cancer among Chinese nonsmoking women: a meta-analysis. OncoTargets and Therapy, 9, 2987-2992. <https://doi.org/10.2147/OTT.S100949>；Chen TY, Fang YH, Chen HL, 等 (2020). Impact of cooking oil fume exposure and fume extractor use on lung cancer risk in non-smoking Han Chinese women. Scientific Reports, 10, 6774. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-63656-7>；Kim C, Gao YT, Xiang YB, 等 (2015). Home kitchen ventilation, cooking fuels, and lung cancer risk in a prospective cohort of never smoking women in Shanghai, China. International Journal of Cancer, 136(3), 632-638. <https://doi.org/10.1002/ijc.29020>
- 备注：争议：支持开抽油烟机的数字主要来自病例对照研究。病人确诊后回忆过去，容易少报开抽油烟机的次数，数字可能偏大。上海那项往后跟踪的研究里，风险升高主要落在烧煤又通风差的人家。只烧燃气的人家开不开差多少，那项研究给不出单独的数。所以证据等级只给 B，收益按约两倍的风险差定为大。做饭不烧煤柴见本节第 29 条（做饭、取暖不烧煤和柴）。本条受益人主要是家里常掌勺的人。
  Note: The debate: the pro-range-hood numbers come mainly from case-control studies. Patients recalling the past after diagnosis tend to under-report hood use, inflating the numbers. In the Shanghai prospective study, the elevated risk fell mostly on coal-burning, poorly ventilated households; it couldn't give a separate number for gas-only households. Hence grade B, with benefit rated "large" on the roughly twofold risk gap. For not burning coal or firewood, see item 29. This item mainly benefits whoever does most of the cooking at home.
