# 2. 不要慢慢死 / Don't Die Slowly

本节只收两类慢性风险：一类对总死亡率影响大，一类证据硬。条目按性价比从高到低排。有几条标了「随机试验」，意思是把人随机分成两组来比，这种结果最可信。其余数字都来自只跟踪记录、不做分组的研究。这类研究算出的 HR、RR 等风险比里混着两种干扰：这些人本来就不一样，有人是先病了才这么做。所以这些数字只说明方向和大概多大，不代表照做就能降这么多。

This section covers only two kinds of chronic risk: those that move all-cause mortality a lot, and those backed by hard evidence. Entries are ranked by cost-effectiveness, highest first. A few entries are tagged as randomized trials—meaning people were randomly split into two groups and compared—which is the most trustworthy kind of result. All other numbers come from observational studies that only tracked people without randomizing them. The hazard ratios (HR) and relative risks (RR) from such studies carry two kinds of confounding: these people were different to begin with, and some changed their behavior because they were already sick. So these numbers indicate direction and rough magnitude only—they don't mean you'll cut your risk by exactly that much by following them.

### 1. 戒烟，越早越好 / Quit smoking—the earlier, the better
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，反过来还省钱。在加州一天一包烟约 10 美元，戒了这笔钱就留下了。难在戒断期要熬，一般是几周到几个月。
  Cost: Costs nothing—in fact it saves money. A pack a day runs roughly $10 in California, so quitting keeps that money. The hard part is getting through withdrawal, which usually lasts weeks to months.
- 说人话：吸烟的人平均比不吸烟的人少活十年以上。40 岁前戒掉，能把接着吸烟带来的死亡风险抹掉九成左右。戒得越早，捡回来的越多。
  In plain terms: Smokers live on average more than ten years less than non-smokers. Quitting before age 40 wipes out about 90% of the excess mortality risk from continued smoking. The earlier you quit, the more years you get back.
- 收益：美国一项跟踪一群人的研究（只记录、不分组）发现：现在还吸烟的人，比从不吸烟的人预期寿命短 10 年以上。40 岁前戒掉，可以消除继续吸烟所带来死亡风险的约 90%。25–34 岁戒烟约多活 10 年，35–44 岁约多活 9 年，45–54 岁约多活 6 年（原文以寿命年报告）。中国的同类研究（中国人群数据）：2010 年代，城市男性吸烟者的死亡风险是不吸烟者的 1.65 倍（RR 1.65），农村男性是 1.22 倍。主动戒烟满 10 年后，吸烟带来的这部分风险接近消失。
  Benefit: A U.S. cohort study (observational, no randomization) found that current smokers live more than 10 years less than never-smokers. Quitting before age 40 eliminates about 90% of the excess mortality risk from continued smoking. Quitting at ages 25–34 gains about 10 years of life, 35–44 about 9 years, and 45–54 about 6 years (reported in life-years in the original). A comparable Chinese study (China-based data): in the 2010s, urban male smokers had 1.65 times the mortality risk of non-smokers (RR 1.65), rural male smokers 1.22 times. After 10 years of sustained quitting, this excess risk nearly disappears.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Jha P 等 (2013). 21st-century hazards of smoking and benefits of cessation in the United States. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMsa1211128>；Chen Z 等 (2015). Contrasting male and female trends in tobacco-attributed mortality in China: evidence from successive nationwide prospective cohort studies. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(15)00340-2>；Oberg M 等 (2011). Worldwide burden of disease from exposure to second-hand smoke: a retrospective analysis of data from 192 countries. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(10)61388-8>（二手烟那两个数字）
- 备注：吸别人的烟同样会死人：2004 年全球约有 60.3 万人死于二手烟，占当年全球总死亡的约 1%。自己不吸，也要躲开别人的烟，尤其别让孩子吸到。怎么戒，具体办法在后面四条：第 3 条（戒烟药）、第 4 条（定一个戒烟日）、第 5 条（去找戒烟支持）、第 6 条（电子烟）
  Note: Other people's smoke kills too: about 603,000 people died from secondhand smoke worldwide in 2004, roughly 1% of global deaths that year. Even if you don't smoke, stay clear of others' smoke—especially keep it away from children. For how to quit, see the next four items: item 3 (cessation medication), item 4 (set a quit date), item 5 (get cessation support), item 6 (e-cigarettes).

### 2. 别在家里和车里抽烟，也别让客人在家里抽 / Don't smoke in your home or car, and don't let guests smoke indoors
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不占时间。难在要开口，让家人和客人别在家里抽。
  Cost: Costs nothing and takes no time. The hard part is speaking up—asking family and guests not to smoke inside.
- 说人话：全球每年约 60 万人死于吸别人的烟，其中近三成是孩子。长期吸二手烟的人，高血压、心脏病、卒中的风险都高三成上下。在家里吸到的，比在外面吸到的更伤人。
  In plain terms: About 600,000 people die each year from other people's smoke, nearly a third of them children. Long-term secondhand-smoke exposure raises the risk of hypertension, heart disease, and stroke by roughly 30% each. Smoke inhaled at home does more damage than smoke inhaled elsewhere.
- 收益：2004 年全球有 603,000 人死于二手烟，约占全球死亡的 1.0%，其中 28% 是儿童。把 57 项研究合起来算：吸到二手烟的人得高血压的机会，约为没吸到的人的 1.28 倍（比值比）。心脏病是 1.39 倍，心肌梗死是 1.50 倍，卒中是 1.36 倍。在家里吸到二手烟的风险，高于在家外吸到的
  Benefit: In 2004, 603,000 people died from secondhand smoke worldwide, about 1.0% of global mortality, 28% of them children. Pooling 57 studies: secondhand-smoke exposure raised the odds of hypertension about 1.28-fold, heart disease 1.39-fold, myocardial infarction 1.50-fold, and stroke 1.36-fold. Home exposure carried a higher risk than exposure outside the home.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Öberg M, Jaakkola MS, Woodward A, Peruga A, Prüss-Ustün A (2011). Worldwide burden of disease from exposure to second-hand smoke: a retrospective analysis of data from 192 countries. Lancet：「603,000 deaths were attributable to second-hand smoke in 2004, which was about 1·0% of worldwide mortality. 47% of deaths from second-hand smoke occurred in women, 28% in children, and 26% in men」，「61% of DALYs were in children」. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(10)61388-8>；(2026). The Associations Between Secondhand Smoke Exposure and Various Cardiovascular Diseases: A Meta-Analysis. Nicotine & Tobacco Research：57 项研究，「hypertension (OR: 1.28, 95% CI: 1.15 to 1.40), heart disease (OR: 1.39, 95% CI: 1.28 to 1.50), myocardial infarction (OR: 1.50, 95% CI: 1.17 to 1.84), stroke (OR: 1.36, 95% CI: 1.18 to 1.54)」「Home exposure has a higher risk of CVD than non-home exposure」. <https://doi.org/10.1093/ntr/ntaf111>
- 备注：先管家里，因为在家待得久，而且多项研究合起来看，在家吸到的二手烟比在家外危害更大。二手烟造成的健康损失里，61% 落在孩子身上。自己戒烟见本节第 1 条。
  Note: Start with your home, because that's where you spend the most time, and pooled studies show home exposure is more harmful than exposure elsewhere. Children bear 61% of the health burden from secondhand smoke. For quitting yourself, see item 1.

### 3. 戒烟别光靠忍，先去拿药：成功率能翻一倍多 / Don't quit on willpower alone—get medication first: it can more than double your odds
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：尼古丁贴片和咀嚼胶是非处方药，药店柜台就能买。一个疗程 8 到 12 周，粗估几十到上百美元。varenicline（曾用名 Chantix）和安非他酮是处方药，要医生开处方（家庭医生或戒烟门诊）。这笔钱和同期少买的烟大致抵消。
  Cost: Nicotine patches and gum are over-the-counter—available at any pharmacy. A course runs 8 to 12 weeks and roughly tens to a couple hundred dollars. Varenicline (formerly sold as Chantix) and bupropion are prescription drugs, prescribed by a doctor (your primary-care doctor or a cessation clinic). The cost is roughly offset by the cigarettes you won't buy.
- 说人话：光靠忍，多数人戒不掉。吃 varenicline 的人戒成的比例，是吃安慰剂的两倍多。用尼古丁贴片、咀嚼胶这类替代品的，比不用药的高五成左右。贴片和咀嚼胶一起用，还能再高两成多。
  In plain terms: Willpower alone fails for most people. People taking varenicline quit at more than twice the rate of those on placebo. Nicotine patches, gum, and similar replacements raise success by about 50% versus no medication. Patch plus a fast-acting form (gum or lozenge) adds another 20-plus percent.
- 收益：41 项随机试验、17395 人合起来算，varenicline 组的戒烟成功率是安慰剂组的 2.32 倍（RR 2.32，95% CI 2.15–2.51，这是可信范围；高确定性证据）。varenicline 也高于安非他酮（RR 1.36，高约 36%）和单用一种尼古丁替代品（RR 1.25，高约 25%）。尼古丁替代品和不用药比：133 项试验、64640 人，成功率高约 55%（RR 1.55，95% CI 1.49–1.61）。贴片再加一种见效快的剂型（咀嚼胶、含片），比只用一种高约 27%（RR 1.27，95% CI 1.17–1.37，16 项试验、12169 人，高确定性证据）。
  Benefit: Pooling 41 randomized trials with 17,395 people, the varenicline group quit at 2.32 times the placebo rate (RR 2.32, 95% CI 2.15–2.51; high-certainty evidence). Varenicline also beat bupropion (RR 1.36, about 36% higher) and single-form nicotine replacement (RR 1.25, about 25% higher). Nicotine replacement versus no medication: 133 trials, 64,640 people, about 55% higher success (RR 1.55, 95% CI 1.49–1.61). Patch plus a fast-acting form (gum or lozenge) beat a single form by about 27% (RR 1.27, 95% CI 1.17–1.37; 16 trials, 12,169 people; high-certainty evidence).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Livingstone-Banks J, Fanshawe TR, Thomas KH, et al. (2023). Nicotine receptor partial agonists for smoking cessation. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 5, CD006103. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD006103.pub8>；Hartmann-Boyce J, Chepkin SC, Ye W, Bullen C, Lancaster T (2018). Nicotine replacement therapy versus control for smoking cessation. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 5, CD000146. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000146.pub5>；Theodoulou A, Chepkin SC, Ye W, et al. (2023). Different doses, durations and modes of delivery of nicotine replacement therapy for smoking cessation. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 6, CD013308. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD013308.pub2>
- 备注：在美国卖的戒烟药就上面三类，只有尼古丁替代品是非处方药。varenicline 常见的反应是恶心、多梦、睡不好，有精神科病史的要跟医生说明。尼古丁替代品的疗程一般 8 到 12 周，别自己突然停，按医生说的减量。药只解决戒断那几周的难受，不解决「想抽」的场合，所以要和本节第 4 条（定一个戒烟日）、第 5 条（去找戒烟支持）一起用。
  Note: These are the three classes of cessation drugs sold in the U.S.; only nicotine replacement is over-the-counter. Common varenicline side effects are nausea, vivid dreams, and poor sleep—tell your doctor if you have a psychiatric history. Nicotine replacement courses usually run 8 to 12 weeks; don't stop abruptly on your own, taper as your doctor directs. Medication only gets you through the rough withdrawal weeks—it doesn't fix the situations that make you crave a cigarette, so pair it with item 4 (set a quit date) and item 5 (get cessation support).

### 4. 定一个戒烟日，到那天一次停掉，别先慢慢减 / Pick a quit date and stop all at once—don't taper down first
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。就是在日历上挑一天，提前告诉家里人和同事。
  Cost: Costs nothing. Just pick a day on the calendar and tell your family and coworkers in advance.
- 说人话：很多人打算先每天少抽几根，慢慢减到零。可到了第六个月，定好日子一次停掉的人里 22% 没再抽，慢慢减的只有 15.5%。所以定一个日子，到那天一次停掉。
  In plain terms: Many people plan to cut down a few cigarettes a day and taper to zero. But by month six, 22% of those who picked a date and stopped all at once were still smoke-free, versus only 15.5% of the gradual reducers. So set a date and stop all at once.
- 收益：英国 697 名吸烟者随机分成两组。一组在戒烟日当天一次停掉，一组在戒烟日前两周先把烟量减掉 75%。两组都有护士的行为支持，戒烟日前后都用尼古丁替代品。第 4 周时，一次停那组 49.0%（95% CI 43.8–54.2，这是可信范围）没复吸，慢慢减那组 39.2%（34.0–44.4），相对风险比 0.80（0.66–0.93）。到第 6 个月，一次停那组 22.0%（18.0–26.6），慢慢减那组 15.5%（12.0–19.7），相对风险比 0.71（0.46–0.91）。原本就偏好慢慢减的人，第 4 周的成功率也低于偏好一次停的人（38.3% 对 52.2%）。
  Benefit: 697 British smokers were randomized into two groups. One stopped abruptly on quit day; the other cut consumption by 75% over the two weeks before quit day. Both got nurse behavioral support and used nicotine replacement around quit day. At week 4, 49.0% of the abrupt group (95% CI 43.8–54.2) had not relapsed versus 39.2% (34.0–44.4) of the gradual group, risk ratio 0.80 (0.66–0.93). At 6 months, 22.0% (18.0–26.6) of the abrupt group versus 15.5% (12.0–19.7) of the gradual group were abstinent, risk ratio 0.71 (0.46–0.91). People who had originally preferred gradual quitting also did worse at week 4 than those who preferred abrupt quitting (38.3% vs 52.2%).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Lindson-Hawley N, Banting M, West R, Michie S, Shinkins B, Aveyard P (2016). Gradual Versus Abrupt Smoking Cessation: A Randomized, Controlled Noninferiority Trial. Annals of Internal Medicine, 164(9), 585–592. <https://doi.org/10.7326/M14-2805>；Theodoulou A, Chepkin SC, Ye W, et al. (2023). Different doses, durations and modes of delivery of nicotine replacement therapy for smoking cessation. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 6, CD013308（提前用尼古丁替代品那一段）. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD013308.pub2>
- 备注：争议。Cochrane 原先有一篇专门比这两种办法的综述，结论是两者差不多（RR 0.94，95% CI 0.79–1.13，10 项试验、3760 人），但那篇已于 2019 年撤回、不再更新。所以眼下最好的证据是上面这项随机试验，方向支持一次停。另外那项试验里两组都在戒烟日之前就开始用尼古丁替代品，这个做法本身也有证据：提前用比戒烟日当天才开始，成功率高约 25%（RR 1.25，95% CI 1.08–1.44，9 项试验、4395 人，中等确定性证据）。所以合理的排法是先定日子，提前两周贴上，到日子一次停。
  Note: Debated. An earlier Cochrane review comparing the two approaches found them roughly equal (RR 0.94, 95% CI 0.79–1.13; 10 trials, 3,760 people), but it was withdrawn in 2019 and is no longer updated. So the best current evidence is the randomized trial above, which favors abrupt quitting. Also, in that trial both groups started nicotine replacement before quit day—and that practice itself has evidence: starting early beats starting on quit day by about 25% (RR 1.25, 95% CI 1.08–1.44; 9 trials, 4,395 people; moderate-certainty evidence). The sensible sequence: set the date, start the patch two weeks early, then stop all at once.

### 5. 去找戒烟支持，或打戒烟热线 1-800-QUIT-NOW / Get cessation support, or call the quitline 1-800-QUIT-NOW
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：看一次医生几十美元左右（copay 或自费）。行为支持一般是 4 到 8 次咨询，每次半小时到一小时，加起来多在 5 小时以内。打热线不花钱。
  Cost: A doctor visit runs on the order of tens of dollars (copay or self-pay). Behavioral support is usually 4 to 8 counseling sessions, half an hour to an hour each, totaling under 5 hours. Calling the quitline is free.
- 说人话：药和「有人定期问你」是两件事，合起来最管用。一边吃药一边有专人跟进的人，戒成的比例接近只听一句简短建议的人的两倍。电话也算：主动打回来的戒烟热线，能把成功率再提高两三成。
  In plain terms: Medication and "someone checking in on you regularly" are two different things, and the combination works best. People who take medication while getting dedicated follow-up quit at nearly twice the rate of those who only get brief advice. Phone calls count too: a quitline that calls you back proactively can raise success by another 20–30%.
- 收益：52 项试验、19488 人合起来算，药物加行为支持组的戒烟成功率是常规照护或简短建议组的 1.83 倍（RR 1.83，95% CI 1.68–1.98，这是可信范围；高质量证据）。在医疗机构招募的那 43 项试验里是 1.97 倍（1.79–2.18）。电话咨询单独算：在自己打过戒烟热线的人里，接受多次主动回访的比只拿到材料或只谈一次的高约 38%（RR 1.38，95% CI 1.19–1.61，14 项试验、32484 人）；没主动打过热线、由研究方提供电话咨询的，高约 25%（RR 1.25，95% CI 1.15–1.35，65 项试验、41233 人）。两项都是中等确定性证据。
  Benefit: Pooling 52 trials with 19,488 people, medication plus behavioral support quit at 1.83 times the rate of usual care or brief-advice groups (RR 1.83, 95% CI 1.68–1.98; high-quality evidence). In the 43 trials recruiting through healthcare settings, it was 1.97-fold (1.79–2.18). Telephone counseling alone: among people who had called a quitline themselves, those getting multiple proactive callbacks quit about 38% more often than those who only got materials or a single call (RR 1.38, 95% CI 1.19–1.61; 14 trials, 32,484 people); among people who hadn't called a quitline, researcher-provided phone counseling raised quitting by about 25% (RR 1.25, 95% CI 1.15–1.35; 65 trials, 41,233 people). Both are moderate-certainty evidence.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Stead LF, Koilpillai P, Fanshawe TR, Lancaster T (2016). Combined pharmacotherapy and behavioural interventions for smoking cessation. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 3, CD008286. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD008286.pub3>；Matkin W, Ordóñez-Mena JM, Hartmann-Boyce J (2019). Telephone counselling for smoking cessation. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 5, CD002850. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD002850.pub4>
- 备注：在美国怎么找：拨打全国戒烟热线 1-800-QUIT-NOW，免费，会转接到你所在州的戒烟服务；也可以问你的家庭医生或保险公司有没有戒烟支持项目。上面的试验在多个国家做，各州戒烟热线的服务强度差别很大，数字只能当方向看。要配的药见本节第 3 条（戒烟药）。
  Note: How to find help in the U.S.: call the national quitline 1-800-QUIT-NOW—it's free and routes you to your state's cessation services. You can also ask your primary-care doctor or insurer about cessation programs. The trials above were done in multiple countries, and quitline intensity varies a lot by state, so treat the numbers as directional. For medication, see item 3.

### 6. 已经戒不掉再考虑电子烟，本来不吸烟的人别碰 / Consider e-cigarettes only if you can't quit otherwise; non-smokers should stay away
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：设备加烟弹每月几十美元。
  Cost: Devices plus pods run tens of dollars a month.
- 说人话：把烟换成含尼古丁的电子烟，戒成的人比用尼古丁贴片多约六成，这个结论很可靠。但它不是无害的，长期吸的安全数据还没有。本来不吸烟的人吸它没有任何好处。
  In plain terms: Switching from cigarettes to nicotine e-cigarettes helps about 60% more people quit than nicotine patches do—this finding is solid. But e-cigarettes aren't harmless, and long-term safety data doesn't exist yet. People who don't smoke gain nothing from picking them up.
- 收益：一份持续更新的综述纳入 80 项随机试验、29861 人。含尼古丁的电子烟和尼古丁替代疗法比，戒烟成功率高约 61%（RR 1.61，95% CI 1.23–2.12，这是可信范围；高确定性证据，11 项试验、4114 人）。换成绝对数，大约是每 100 人里多 4 人戒成（多 1 到 7 人）。严重不良事件的发生比例两组大致相当（风险差 0.01，95% CI −0.01 到 0.02，中等确定性证据）。和只有行为支持或没有支持比，高约 75%（RR 1.75，95% CI 1.39–2.20，低确定性证据）。
  Benefit: A living systematic review includes 80 randomized trials with 29,861 people. Nicotine e-cigarettes beat nicotine replacement therapy by about 61% (RR 1.61, 95% CI 1.23–2.12; high-certainty evidence; 11 trials, 4,114 people). In absolute terms, that's about 4 extra quitters per 100 people (1 to 7). Serious adverse events were roughly equal in both groups (risk difference 0.01, 95% CI −0.01 to 0.02; moderate-certainty evidence). Versus behavioral support alone or no support, e-cigarettes were about 75% better (RR 1.75, 95% CI 1.39–2.20; low-certainty evidence).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Lindson N, Livingstone-Banks J, Butler AR, et al. (2026). Electronic cigarettes for smoking cessation. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 8, CD010216. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD010216.pub11>
- 备注：争议。世界卫生组织不推荐把电子烟当戒烟手段。理由是长期安全数据不够，还会让年轻人开始接触尼古丁。英国则把它放进了戒烟服务。在美国：联邦法律规定 21 岁以下禁售烟草制品（含电子烟）；FDA 至今只授权过烟草味和薄荷醇味的电子烟；加州在全州范围内禁止零售店销售调味烟草制品（含调味电子烟）。来路不明的烟油不在监管之内，综述里测的都是受监管的含尼古丁产品。顺序上先试本节第 3 条（戒烟药），那边的证据更硬、也更便宜。
  Note: Debated. The WHO does not recommend e-cigarettes as a cessation aid, citing insufficient long-term safety data and the risk of hooking young people on nicotine. The U.K., by contrast, includes them in its cessation services. In the U.S.: federal law bans tobacco sales (including e-cigarettes) to anyone under 21; the FDA has so far authorized only tobacco- and menthol-flavored e-cigarettes; and California bans flavored tobacco products (including flavored e-cigarettes) in retail stores statewide. Unregulated vape liquids fall outside all of this, and the review above tested only regulated nicotine products. Try item 3 (cessation medication) first—its evidence is harder and it's cheaper.

### 7. 不喝含糖饮料，换成无糖的也不算解决 / Skip sugary drinks—switching to diet versions doesn't fix it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不占时间。换成白水或无糖茶还省钱。难在要改掉喝甜饮料的习惯，包括无糖的。
  Cost: Costs nothing and takes no time. Switching to water or unsweetened tea even saves money. The hard part is breaking the sweet-drink habit, including the diet versions.
- 说人话：每天喝两瓶以上甜饮料的人，同期死亡的概率比几乎不喝的人高约两成。每天一到两瓶的，也高一成多。换成无糖的也没见着好处：每天喝两杯以上代糖饮料的人，反而高约四分之一。
  In plain terms: People who drink two-plus bottles of sugary drinks a day die at about a 20% higher rate than near-abstainers. One to two bottles a day is still about 10%-plus higher. Switching to diet versions shows no benefit: people drinking two-plus cups of artificially sweetened drinks a day run about a quarter higher.
- 收益：美国有两项跟踪一群人的大型研究。这两项只记录、不分组，约 11.8 万人、3.6 万例死亡。每天喝 2 份以上的人，死亡风险比每月不到 1 份的人高约 21%（HR 1.21）。每天喝 1–2 份的，高约 14%（HR 1.14）。欧洲 EPIC 跟踪研究只记录、不分组，有 45 万人、10 个国家。每天喝 2 杯以上的人和每月不到 1 杯的人比：含糖软饮这项高约 8%（HR 1.08，95% CI 1.01–1.16）。人工甜味剂饮料（无糖版）高约 26%（HR 1.26，1.16–1.35）。所有软饮合起来算，高约 17%（HR 1.17，1.11–1.22）。
  Benefit: Two large U.S. cohort studies (observational, no randomization) followed about 118,000 people with 36,000 deaths. People drinking 2+ servings a day had about 21% higher mortality than those drinking less than 1 serving a month (HR 1.21); 1–2 servings a day was about 14% higher (HR 1.14). The European EPIC cohort (observational; 450,000 people across 10 countries): 2+ cups a day versus less than 1 cup a month was about 8% higher for sugary soft drinks (HR 1.08, 95% CI 1.01–1.16) and about 26% higher for artificially sweetened (diet) drinks (HR 1.26, 1.16–1.35); all soft drinks combined were about 17% higher (HR 1.17, 1.11–1.22).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Malik VS 等 (2019). Long-Term Consumption of Sugar-Sweetened and Artificially Sweetened Beverages and Risk of Mortality in US Adults. Circulation. <https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.118.037401>；Mullee A, Romaguera D, Pearson-Stuttard J, et al. (2019). Association Between Soft Drink Consumption and Mortality in 10 European Countries. JAMA Internal Medicine, 179(11), 1479-1490. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2019.2478>
- 备注：争议在无糖饮料上。代糖饮料的关联反而比含糖的更强，这很可能是因果反过来了：已经超重或者得了糖尿病的人，才改喝无糖版。所以别据此说代糖更毒。但它至少说明，「换成无糖就没事」这个说法没有证据支持。换成白水或者无糖茶最稳妥。另外，这类研究只是跟踪记录，没做分组试验，喝甜饮料多的人本来生活方式就更差，所以数字可能偏大。
  Note: The debate is about diet drinks. The stronger association for artificially sweetened drinks is likely reverse causation: people who are already overweight or diabetic switch to diet versions. So don't read this as "diet soda is more toxic." But it does show there's no evidence that "switching to diet makes it fine." Water or unsweetened tea is the safest swap. Also, these are observational studies without randomization, and heavy soda drinkers tend to have worse lifestyles overall, so the numbers may be inflated.

### 8. 不嚼槟榔 / Don't chew betel quid
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不占时间，还省下买槟榔的钱。难在要戒掉嚼的习惯。
  Cost: Costs nothing, takes no time, and saves the money spent on betel quid. The hard part is breaking the chewing habit.
- 说人话：嚼槟榔的人，同期死亡的概率比不嚼的人高约两成（亚洲人群研究）。糖尿病和代谢综合征的风险高近五成。口腔癌和食管癌的风险还要另算。
  In plain terms: Betel quid chewers die at about a 20% higher rate than non-chewers (studies in Asian populations). Diabetes and metabolic syndrome risks run nearly 50% higher. Oral and esophageal cancer risks are on top of that.
- 收益：把 17 项亚洲研究、38.8 万人合起来算。嚼槟榔的人死亡风险比不嚼的人高约 21%（相对风险 1.21，P=0.02）。死亡这一项算的是其中的 17.96 万人。得糖尿病的风险高约 47%（1.47）。得代谢综合征的风险高约 51%（1.51）。槟榔本身也是口腔癌和食管癌的已知危险因素
  Benefit: Pooling 17 Asian studies with 388,000 people: chewers had about 21% higher all-cause mortality than non-chewers (relative risk 1.21, P=0.02; mortality analysis covered 179,600 of them). Diabetes risk was about 47% higher (1.47), metabolic syndrome about 51% higher (1.51). Betel quid is also an established risk factor for oral and esophageal cancers.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Yamada T, Hara K, Kadowaki T (2013). Chewing betel quid and the risk of metabolic disease, cardiovascular disease, and all-cause mortality: a meta-analysis. PLoS One, 8(8), e70679. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0070679>
- 备注：嚼的年头越长、每天嚼得越多，口腔癌风险越高。戒了以后，风险会随时间往下走。含烟草的槟榔更危险。在加州，嚼槟榔不常见，主要见于部分南亚、东南亚移民社区的槟榔制品（如 paan）。
  Note: The longer and the more you chew per day, the higher the oral cancer risk. Risk declines over time after quitting. Betel quid containing tobacco is even more dangerous. In California, betel quid chewing is uncommon, seen mostly in some South and Southeast Asian immigrant communities (e.g., paan).

### 9. 把家里的食盐换成低钠盐（钾盐） / Switch to low-sodium (potassium) salt at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一袋比普通盐贵一两美元。买的时候顺手换，不额外占时间。口味几乎不变。
  Cost: A bag costs a dollar or two more than regular salt. Just grab it when you shop—no extra time. Taste is nearly unchanged.
- 说人话：得过中风或 60 岁以上有高血压的人，把家里的盐换成低钠盐，五年内死亡的概率低约 12%，中风低约 14%。
  In plain terms: For people who've had a stroke or are over 60 with hypertension, switching household salt to a low-sodium version cut mortality by about 12% and stroke by about 14% over five years.
- 收益：一项把人随机分成两组的试验，在中国农村做的（中国人群数据），20995 人，都是得过卒中的人或者 60 岁以上的高血压病人，跟踪了 4.74 年。结果：用低钠盐的那组比用普通盐的那组，死亡风险低约 12%（RR 0.88）。卒中低约 14%（RR 0.86）。主要心血管事件低约 13%（RR 0.87）。血钾过高的事件，两组没看出统计上的差别。
  Benefit: A randomized trial conducted in rural China (China-based data) followed 20,995 people—stroke survivors or hypertensive patients over 60—for 4.74 years. The low-sodium-salt group had about 12% lower mortality (RR 0.88), 14% lower stroke (RR 0.86), and 13% lower major cardiovascular events (RR 0.87) than the regular-salt group. Hyperkalemia events showed no statistical difference between groups.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Neal B 等 (2021). Effect of Salt Substitution on Cardiovascular Events and Death. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2105675>；O'Donnell M 等 (2014). Urinary sodium and potassium excretion, mortality, and cardiovascular events. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1311889>（争议方 PURE）
- 备注：争议。PURE 这项研究只长期跟踪记录，不分组。它报告：一天排钠不到 3 g 的人，死亡加心血管事件的风险高约 27%。一天排钠 7 g 以上的人，高约 15%。按它的结果，吃太少和吃太多都不好，中间最好。但本条引的那项中国农村随机试验里，低钠盐只是替换掉一部分，不会把钠压得那么低。另外要注意，试验对象是高危老人。健康年轻人换盐得到的好处要小得多。肾功能不全的人，或者正在吃保钾类药物的人，换盐前先问医生。
  Note: Debated. The PURE study was long-term observation only, no randomization. It reported that people excreting under 3 g of sodium a day had about 27% higher mortality-plus-cardiovascular risk, and those over 7 g about 15% higher—too little and too much both look bad, the middle looks best. But in the Chinese rural randomized trial cited here, low-sodium salt only replaced part of the sodium, so intake never dropped that low. Also note the trial participants were high-risk older adults; healthy young people gain much less from switching. If you have kidney problems or take potassium-sparing drugs, ask your doctor before switching.

### 10. 认真刷牙，每天清一次牙缝，缺牙及时补 / Brush well, clean between your teeth daily, replace missing teeth promptly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：牙线或牙缝刷一年十几二十美元，洗牙每次一两百美元（有牙科保险多为部分报销）。每天多花两三分钟。难在要把清牙缝变成每天都做的事。
  Cost: Floss or interdental brushes run ten to twenty dollars a year; a professional cleaning is one to two hundred dollars a visit (partly covered with dental insurance). It takes two to three extra minutes a day. The hard part is making interdental cleaning a daily habit.
- 说人话：每天用牙线或牙缝刷的人，同期死亡的概率低约一成。牙掉光的老人，死亡的概率高约九成。剩不到 20 颗牙的，高约一倍。
  In plain terms: People who floss or use interdental brushes daily die at about a 10% lower rate. Older adults with no teeth left die at about a 90% higher rate; those with fewer than 20 teeth, about double.
- 收益：日本有一项研究，9676 人，跟踪 6 年。用牙缝清洁工具的人，死亡风险低约 11%（风险比 0.89）。用舌苔清洁工具的人，低约 23%（0.77）。另外把多项研究合起来算，对象是住在社区的老人。一颗牙都没有的人，死亡的机会约为 1.87 倍（比值比，95% CI 1.35–2.59，这是可信范围）。牙齿少于 20 颗的，约为 2.04 倍（1.67–2.49）
  Benefit: A Japanese study of 9,676 people followed for 6 years found about 11% lower mortality among interdental-cleaning-tool users (hazard ratio 0.89) and about 23% lower among tongue-cleaning-tool users (0.77). Pooling multiple studies of community-dwelling older adults: people with no teeth left had about 1.87 times the odds of dying (95% CI 1.35–2.59); those with fewer than 20 teeth, about 2.04 times (1.67–2.49).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Wang K, Matsuyama Y, Kiuchi S, et al. (2026). Routine oral health practices and all-cause mortality. Journal of Dentistry. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jdent.2026.106789>；Ko MJ, Seo S, So JS, et al. (2026). Deteriorated oral health and function as risk factors for physical disability and mortality in community-dwelling older adults: a systematic review and meta-analysis. European Geriatric Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s41999-025-01319-4>
- 备注：争议。这类研究只是跟踪记录。牙不好的人，往往整体健康和经济状况也差。到底是牙拖累了身体，还是身体不好连累了牙，说不清。但这件事成本极低，而且牙周炎和缺牙本身就影响吃东西。种一颗牙要花多少、看牙哪些项目保险报，见第 24 节第 12 条（种牙之前先问清包干价）
  Note: Debated. These are observational studies. People with bad teeth tend to be worse off in overall health and finances too—whether bad teeth drag down the body or a failing body drags down the teeth is unclear. But this costs almost nothing, and gum disease and missing teeth directly affect eating. For what an implant costs and what dental insurance covers, see section 24, item 12.

### 11. 每天走到 7000–8000 步，或者每周累计快走 150–300 分钟 / Walk 7,000–8,000 steps a day, or 150–300 minutes of brisk walking a week
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。按步数算，每天走 60–90 分钟，可以拆进上下班和买菜里。按运动时间算，每天 20–45 分钟。难在要长期坚持下来。
  Cost: Costs nothing. By step count, that's 60–90 minutes a day, which can be folded into commuting and errands. By exercise time, 20–45 minutes a day. The hard part is sticking with it long term.
- 说人话：每天走 7800 步左右的人，同期死亡的概率比只走 3500 步的人低约四成半。按时间算也一样，每周快走 150–300 分钟，比不运动低约三成。多到一定量就不再往下降了，60 岁以上走到 6000–8000 步就够。两种数法达标一个就行。
  In plain terms: People walking about 7,800 steps a day die at about a 45% lower rate than those walking only 3,500. By time it's the same story: 150–300 minutes of brisk walking a week is about 30% lower than no exercise. Benefits flatten past a point—6,000–8,000 steps is enough over age 60. Hitting either metric counts.
- 收益：把 15 项跟踪一群人的研究合起来算，只记录、不分组。这 15 项共 47471 人、3013 例死亡。按每天步数把人分成四档，最低那档中位是 3553 步/天。另外三档的中位是 5801、7842、10901 步/天。这三档的死亡风险比最低档分别低约 40%、45%、53%（HR 0.60、0.55、0.47）。60 岁以上的人走到 6000–8000 步以后，再多走收益就趋平了。60 岁以下的人是在 8000–10000 步以后趋平。另有一项分析把多项研究合起来算：从约 3867 步/天开始有效。之后每多走 1000 步/天，死亡风险降约 15%。按运动时间算：把多项跟踪研究汇总到一起算（只记录、不分组），运动量达到指南下限 1–2 倍的人，死亡风险比不运动的人低约 31%（HR 0.69）。这个量是 7.5–15 MET·h/周，大约合快走 150–300 分钟/周。没达到下限的人也低约 20%（HR 0.80）。达到下限 3–5 倍的人低约 39%（HR 0.61），这是降幅的顶点。再往上加不会更低，但也没害处（10 倍以上是 HR 0.69）。另一项用加速度计测量的汇总研究：中高强度活动最多的那四分之一人，比最少的那四分之一死亡风险低约 48%（HR 0.52）。加速度计是戴在身上实测活动量的仪器。
  Benefit: Pooling 15 cohort studies (observational, no randomization) with 47,471 people and 3,013 deaths. Participants were split into four daily-step quartiles; the lowest had a median of 3,553 steps/day, the other three 5,801, 7,842, and 10,901. The upper three quartiles had about 40%, 45%, and 53% lower mortality than the lowest (HR 0.60, 0.55, 0.47). Benefits flattened past 6,000–8,000 steps for people over 60, and past 8,000–10,000 for those under 60. Another pooled analysis: benefits start around 3,867 steps/day, with about 15% lower mortality per additional 1,000 steps/day. By exercise time: pooling multiple cohort studies (observational), people reaching 1–2 times the guideline minimum had about 31% lower mortality than the inactive (HR 0.69)—that's 7.5–15 MET·h/week, roughly 150–300 minutes of brisk walking per week. Those below the minimum were still about 20% lower (HR 0.80); those at 3–5 times the minimum about 39% lower (HR 0.61), the peak benefit. Going higher didn't add more, but did no harm (above 10×, HR 0.69). A pooled accelerometer-based analysis: the most active quarter had about 48% lower mortality than the least active quarter (HR 0.52). Accelerometers are body-worn devices that measure activity directly.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Paluch AE 等 (2022). Daily steps and all-cause mortality: a meta-analysis of 15 international cohorts. Lancet Public Health. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-2667(21)00302-9>；Banach M 等 (2023). The association between daily step count and all-cause and cardiovascular mortality: a meta-analysis. European Journal of Preventive Cardiology. <https://doi.org/10.1093/eurjpc/zwad229>；Arem H 等 (2015). Leisure time physical activity and mortality: a detailed pooled analysis of the dose-response relationship. JAMA Internal Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2015.0533>；Ekelund U 等 (2019). Dose-response associations between accelerometry measured physical activity and sedentary time and all cause mortality: systematic review and harmonised meta-analysis. BMJ. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.l4570>
- 备注：步数和运动时间说的是同一件事，只是换了个数法，达标一个就行。从 4000 步加到 7000 步这一段最划算，不必非要凑一万步。要注意这类研究只是跟踪记录：走得最少、动得最少的那批人里，混着本来就病着走不动的。他们是快不行了才动得少，不是因为少动才早死。所以算出的差距常常偏大。用仪器实测活动量的那几项，跟踪时间短，参与者偏老，真实好处没有「风险降到 0.52 倍」那么大。但越动越好的趋势是清楚的。
  Note: Step counts and exercise time are the same thing measured two ways—hitting either counts. The jump from 4,000 to 7,000 steps is the best value; no need to force 10,000. Note these are observational studies: the least-active group includes people who were already sick and couldn't move—they move less because they're dying, not the reverse. So the gaps are often overstated. The device-measured studies had short follow-ups and older participants, so the real benefit isn't as big as "risk down to 0.52." But the more-you-move-the-better trend is clear.

### 12. 有高血压、高血脂就按医嘱规律吃药，别自行停 / If you have hypertension or high cholesterol, take your meds as prescribed—don't stop on your own
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：普通仿制药（generic）的降压药和他汀，每月几美元到十几美元。每天吃一次，花不了多少时间。难在要一直吃下去，不能觉得没事了就停。
  Cost: Generic blood-pressure drugs and statins run a few to ten-plus dollars a month. Once a day, barely any time. The hard part is staying on them indefinitely—not stopping just because you feel fine.
- 说人话：血压每降 10 mmHg，死亡的概率降约 13%，中风降约 27%。他汀把低密度胆固醇降 1 个单位，死亡的概率降约一成。按时吃药的人比经常漏吃的人，死亡的概率低三成到四成半。
  In plain terms: Every 10 mmHg drop in blood pressure cuts mortality by about 13% and stroke by about 27%. Statins lowering LDL cholesterol by 1 unit cut mortality by about 10%. People who take their meds on schedule die at 30–45% lower rates than frequent skippers.
- 收益：把多项随机分组试验合起来算：收缩压每降 10 mmHg，死亡风险低约 13%（RR 0.87）。收缩压就是血压里的高压。主要心血管事件低约 20%（RR 0.80）。卒中低约 27%（RR 0.73）。心衰低约 28%（RR 0.72）。吃他汀让低密度脂蛋白胆固醇（LDL）往下降。LDL 每降 1.0 mmol/L，死亡风险低约 10%（RR 0.90）。同样这一降，主要血管事件低约 22%（RR 0.78）。另外把多项跟踪研究合起来算，只记录、不分组。按医嘱吃到 ≥80% 的人，死亡风险比经常漏吃的人低。他汀这一项低约 45%（RR 0.55）。降压药这一项低约 29%（RR 0.71）。
  Benefit: Pooling multiple randomized trials: every 10 mmHg drop in systolic pressure (the top number) cut mortality by about 13% (RR 0.87), major cardiovascular events by about 20% (RR 0.80), stroke by about 27% (RR 0.73), and heart failure by about 28% (RR 0.72). Statins lowering LDL cholesterol: every 1.0 mmol/L drop cut mortality by about 10% (RR 0.90) and major vascular events by about 22% (RR 0.78). Pooling multiple observational cohort studies: people taking ≥80% of prescribed doses had lower mortality than frequent skippers—about 45% lower for statins (RR 0.55), about 29% lower for blood-pressure drugs (RR 0.71).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Ettehad D 等 (2016). Blood pressure lowering for prevention of cardiovascular disease and death: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(15)01225-8>；Cholesterol Treatment Trialists' (CTT) Collaboration (2010). Efficacy and safety of more intensive lowering of LDL cholesterol: a meta-analysis of data from 170 000 participants in 26 randomised trials. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(10)61350-5>；Chowdhury R 等 (2013). Adherence to cardiovascular therapy: a meta-analysis of prevalence and clinical consequences. European Heart Journal. <https://doi.org/10.1093/eurheartj/eht295>
- 备注：只有医生判断该吃药的人才照这个做，健康人不用吃。「坚持吃药」那组数字来自跟踪记录，能坚持吃药的人本身也更自律，所以会把好处说大。前面两组随机分组试验的数字更可靠。本节没有单独列控制血糖这一条：把血糖压得更低对总死亡率的好处，证据不像血压、血脂那样一致。
  Note: This applies only if your doctor says you need medication—healthy people don't need it. The "adherent" numbers come from observational data, and adherent people are generally more disciplined, so the benefit is overstated. The first two sets of numbers, from randomized trials, are more reliable. This section has no separate item on blood-sugar control: the evidence that tighter glucose control lowers all-cause mortality isn't as consistent as for blood pressure and cholesterol.

### 13. 每晚睡 7 小时左右，作息固定 / Sleep about 7 hours a night on a regular schedule
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。要拿出时间来睡，多数人是把刷手机的时间换成睡觉。难在要把上床和起床的点固定住。
  Cost: Costs nothing. The time has to come from somewhere—most people trade phone-scrolling time for sleep. The hard part is keeping bedtime and wake time fixed.
- 说人话：长期睡不到 7 小时的人，同期死亡的概率高约一成。作息越乱，风险越高。睡得最规律的人比最不规律的人低两成到近五成。也就是说，作息规不规律比睡多久更能看出死亡风险。
  In plain terms: Habitual short sleepers (under 7 hours) die at about a 10% higher rate. The more irregular the schedule, the higher the risk—people with the most regular sleep had 20% to nearly 50% lower mortality than the most irregular. In other words, regularity predicts mortality better than duration does.
- 收益：把 16 项跟踪研究合起来算，只记录、不分组，138 万人、11.3 万例死亡。睡得短的人死亡风险高约 12%（RR 1.12）。睡得长的人高约 30%（RR 1.30）。另一项汇总按睡眠时长分档：7 小时那档风险最低。少于 7 小时的，每少 1 小时高约 6%（RR 1.06）。多于 7 小时的，每多 1 小时高约 13%（RR 1.13）。英国生物银行有约 6.1 万人的腕表记录，只记录、不分组。把人按作息规律程度分成五档。较规律的那四档比最不规律的那档，死亡风险低 20%–48%。作息规不规律，比睡多久更能预测死亡。另有一项英国生物银行的研究，用腕表测了 51,562 人的睡眠时刻，其间 3,853 人新发心血管病。社会时差指工作日和周末的睡眠中点差多少。社会时差达到 2 小时以上的人，心血管病风险高约 30%（HR 1.30，95% CI 1.11–1.54）。这个关联在睡眠时长正常的人里同样存在。
  Benefit: Pooling 16 cohort studies (observational, no randomization) with 1.38 million people and 113,000 deaths: short sleepers had about 12% higher mortality (RR 1.12), long sleepers about 30% higher (RR 1.30). Another pooled analysis by sleep duration: risk was lowest at 7 hours; below 7, each hour less added about 6% (RR 1.06); above 7, each hour more added about 13% (RR 1.13). UK Biobank wrist-device records from about 61,000 people (observational) split participants into five regularity bands; the four more regular bands had 20%–48% lower mortality than the least regular. Regularity predicted mortality better than duration. A separate UK Biobank study measured sleep timing in 51,562 people with wrist devices, during which 3,853 developed cardiovascular disease. Social jet lag—the gap between weekday and weekend sleep midpoints—of 2+ hours was tied to about 30% higher cardiovascular risk (HR 1.30, 95% CI 1.11–1.54), even among people sleeping a normal amount.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cappuccio FP 等 (2010). Sleep duration and all-cause mortality: a systematic review and meta-analysis of prospective studies. Sleep. <https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/33.5.585>；Yin J 等 (2017). Relationship of Sleep Duration With All-Cause Mortality and Cardiovascular Events: A Systematic Review and Dose-Response Meta-Analysis of Prospective Cohort Studies. JAHA. <https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.117.005947>；Windred DP 等 (2024). Sleep regularity is a stronger predictor of mortality risk than sleep duration: A prospective cohort study. Sleep. <https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/zsad253>；Kumar N, Krishnamurthy S (2026). Social jet lag is associated with incident cardiovascular disease independent of sleep duration and cardiac genetic risk. Journal of Internal Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1111/joim.70133>
- 备注：要改的是睡得太少和作息乱这两样，不必刻意去压缩睡眠时间。睡得长的人风险高，多半是因果反过来了：抑郁、慢性病、睡眠呼吸暂停会让人睡得多。另外，作息规律这一点目前有两项跟踪研究支持，都是只记录、不分组的，单独看属于 B 级证据。熬夜之后怎么补见本节第 38 条（熬夜之后第二天晚上就补觉）。
  Note: Fix the two things that matter—too little sleep and irregular timing. Don't deliberately compress your sleep. The higher risk in long sleepers is mostly reverse causation: depression, chronic illness, and sleep apnea make people sleep more. Also, the regularity finding currently rests on two observational studies—grade B evidence on its own. For catching up after a late night, see item 38.

### 14. 每周打三次球拍类运动，每次 45 分钟 / Play racquet sports three times a week, 45 minutes each
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：场地费每次十几美元。每周约 2 小时。难在要凑齐人和场地，还得每周都去。
  Cost: Court fees run on the order of ten-plus dollars a session. About 2 hours a week. The hard part is lining up partners and a court—and showing up every week.
- 说人话：常打网球、羽毛球、乒乓球的人，同期死亡的概率比不打的人低约一半，心血管死亡低约 56%。游泳的人低约 28%。跑步和足球在这份数据里没看出差别。
  In plain terms: Regular tennis, badminton, and table-tennis players die at about half the rate of non-players, with cardiovascular mortality about 56% lower. Swimmers are about 28% lower. Running and soccer showed no difference in this dataset.
- 收益：英国一项 8.03 万人的跟踪研究，比的是做某项运动的人和不做这项运动的人。打球拍类运动的人，死亡风险低约 47%（风险比 0.53，95% CI 0.40–0.69，可信范围）。心血管死亡低约 56%（0.44，0.24–0.83）。球拍类指网球、羽毛球、乒乓球。游泳的人这两个数分别是 0.72 和 0.59，有氧操是 0.73 和 0.64，骑车是 0.85。跑步和足球没看出统计上的差别
  Benefit: A British cohort study of 80,300 people compared participants in each sport against non-participants. Racquet-sport players (tennis, badminton, table tennis) had about 47% lower all-cause mortality (hazard ratio 0.53, 95% CI 0.40–0.69) and about 56% lower cardiovascular mortality (0.44, 0.24–0.83). Swimmers were 0.72 and 0.59 on the two measures, aerobics 0.73 and 0.64, cycling 0.85. Running and soccer showed no statistical difference.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Oja P, Kelly P, Pedisic Z, et al. (2017). Associations of specific types of sports and exercise with all-cause and cardiovascular-disease mortality: a cohort study of 80 306 British adults. British Journal of Sports Medicine, 51(10), 812-817. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2016-096822>
- 备注：争议。这类研究只是跟踪记录。打球的人本身就更健康，社交也更多。跑步那组看不出差别，也说明起作用的可能是「什么样的人挑了什么运动」。别因为这条就认为跑步没用，本节另有按运动总量算的条目
  Note: Debated. This is an observational study. Racquet-sport players tend to be healthier and more social to begin with. The null result for running suggests what matters may be "who picks which sport." Don't conclude running is useless from this—this section has other entries on total exercise volume.

### 15. 把爬楼梯、快走赶路这类零碎的用力活动攒到每天四五分钟 / Get 4–5 minutes a day of vigorous bursts—stairs, hurrying
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不用额外挤出锻炼时间。难在要记着走楼梯、走快些，别图省事。
  Cost: Costs nothing and needs no dedicated workout time. The hard part is remembering to take the stairs and walk briskly instead of taking it easy.
- 说人话：平时完全不锻炼的人，只要每天有三次一两分钟的用力活动，同期死亡的概率就比一点没有的人低约四成，心血管死亡低近一半。用力活动指爬楼梯、快步赶路这类。
  In plain terms: People who never exercise but get three 1–2-minute bursts of vigorous activity a day die at about a 40% lower rate than those with none, with cardiovascular mortality nearly halved. Vigorous activity means things like climbing stairs or hurrying on foot.
- 收益：英国生物银行有一项研究，对象是 2.52 万名平时不专门锻炼的人。平均跟踪 6.9 年，其中 852 人去世。有的人每天有 3 次、每次 1–2 分钟的零碎剧烈活动。和完全没有的人比，他们的死亡风险和癌症死亡风险降低 38%–40%。心血管死亡风险降低 48%–49%。每天这类活动累计 4.4 分钟的人，死亡风险和癌症死亡风险降低 26%–30%。心血管死亡风险降低 32%–34%
  Benefit: A UK Biobank study of 25,200 non-exercisers followed for an average of 6.9 years, during which 852 died. People with three daily 1–2-minute bursts of vigorous incidental activity had 38%–40% lower all-cause and cancer mortality than those with none, and 48%–49% lower cardiovascular mortality. Those accumulating 4.4 minutes a day had 26%–30% lower all-cause and cancer mortality and 32%–34% lower cardiovascular mortality.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Stamatakis E, Ahmadi MN, Gill JMR, et al. (2022). Association of wearable device-measured vigorous intermittent lifestyle physical activity with mortality. Nature Medicine, 28, 2521-2529. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-022-02100-x>
- 备注：争议。这项研究用可穿戴设备实测活动量，比让人填问卷回忆可靠。但它仍然只是跟踪记录，没有做分组试验，而且只跟了 6.9 年。研究对象是完全不锻炼的人，已经在规律运动的人不适用这一条
  Note: Debated. This study measured activity with wearables, which beats self-reported questionnaires. But it's still observational—no randomization—and follow-up was only 6.9 years. Participants were non-exercisers; this doesn't apply to people already exercising regularly.

### 16. 每周做 30–60 分钟力量训练 / Do 30–60 minutes of strength training a week
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：徒手深蹲、俯卧撑不花钱。每周练 1–2 次，每次 20–30 分钟。难在要把它排进每周的日程里。
  Cost: Bodyweight squats and push-ups are free. One to two sessions a week, 20–30 minutes each. The hard part is scheduling it into the week.
- 说人话：每周练 30–60 分钟力量的人，同期死亡的概率比不练的人低一到两成。再多练不会更好。力量和有氧一起做效果最好。
  In plain terms: People doing 30–60 minutes of strength training a week die at 10–20% lower rates than non-lifters. More training doesn't add more benefit. Strength plus cardio together works best.
- 收益：把多项跟踪研究合起来算（只记录、不分组）：做力量训练的人比不做的人，死亡风险低 10%–17%。不是练得越多越好，每周约 30–60 分钟时降幅最大，约 10%–20%，再多练不会多出好处。力量和有氧都做的人，比两样都不做的人死亡风险更低。
  Benefit: Pooling multiple cohort studies (observational, no randomization): strength trainers had 10%–17% lower mortality than non-trainers. More isn't better—the biggest reduction, about 10%–20%, came at roughly 30–60 minutes a week, with no added benefit beyond that. People doing both strength and cardio had lower mortality than those doing neither.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Momma H 等 (2022). Muscle-strengthening activities are associated with lower risk and mortality in major non-communicable diseases: a systematic review and meta-analysis of cohort studies. British Journal of Sports Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bjsports-2021-105061>
- 备注：这类研究只是跟踪记录，运动量还是参与者自己报的。「练过头反而变差」那一段的证据很弱，不必为此限制训练量。力量训练对老年人还有防跌倒、保住肌肉的好处，那些写在第 1 节。
  Note: These are observational studies with self-reported exercise. The evidence for "too much training backfires" is weak—don't cap your training over it. Strength training also helps older adults prevent falls and preserve muscle; that's covered in section 1.

### 17. 别连着坐太久，隔一阵起身动一动 / Don't sit for too long at a stretch—get up and move regularly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，起身几下也用不了多少时间。难在坐进去就忘了时间，得设个提醒。
  Cost: Costs nothing and takes moments. The hard part is that you lose track of time once seated—set a reminder.
- 说人话：坐得最久的人，同期死亡的概率是坐得最少的人的 2.6 倍。一次连着坐很久，还要另算一笔风险。但只要每天有 60–75 分钟中等强度活动，久坐多出来的风险基本就被抵消了。
  In plain terms: The most sedentary people die at 2.6 times the rate of the least sedentary. Long unbroken sitting bouts add risk on top of that. But 60–75 minutes of moderate activity a day essentially cancels out the excess risk from sitting.
- 收益：美国有一项跟踪研究，只记录、不分组，用加速度计实测。把人按每天久坐总时长分成四份。久坐最多的那份人，死亡风险是最少那份的 2.63 倍（HR 2.63）。再按一次连着坐多久分成四份：连坐最久的那份人，是连坐最短那份的 1.96 倍（HR 1.96）。所以坐的总量和一次连坐多久，各自单独相关。另一项汇总把上百万人的研究合起来算，只记录、不分组。每天坐 8 小时以上、又几乎不运动的人，死亡风险是另一类人的 1.59 倍（HR 1.59）。另一类人是每天坐不到 4 小时、又最活跃的。每天约 60–75 分钟中等强度活动，可以抵消久坐多出来的风险。最活跃那组即使每天坐 8 小时以上，HR 也只有 1.04，没看出统计上的差别。
  Benefit: A U.S. cohort study (observational) using accelerometers split people into quartiles of total daily sedentary time: the most sedentary quartile had 2.63 times the mortality of the least (HR 2.63). By longest unbroken sitting bout: the longest-bout quartile was 1.96 times the shortest (HR 1.96). Total sitting and bout length each matter independently. Another pooled analysis of over a million people (observational): people sitting 8+ hours a day with almost no exercise had 1.59 times the mortality of the least-sedentary, most-active group (HR 1.59). About 60–75 minutes of moderate activity a day offset the sitting risk—the most active group showed essentially no excess risk even at 8+ hours of sitting (HR 1.04, not statistically significant).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Diaz KM 等 (2017). Patterns of Sedentary Behavior and Mortality in U.S. Middle-Aged and Older Adults: A National Cohort Study. Annals of Internal Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.7326/M17-0212>；Ekelund U 等 (2016). Does physical activity attenuate, or even eliminate, the detrimental association of sitting time with mortality? A harmonised meta-analysis of data from more than 1 million men and women. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(16)30370-1>
- 备注：可靠的结论是：坐多久不如动多少重要，运动量够的时候久坐的风险基本消失。要注意这类研究只是跟踪记录，坐得最多的人里有大量本来就病着的，「风险高到 2.63 倍」里明显含着「先病了才久坐」。另外同一份汇总里，每天看电视 3 小时以上的风险在各个运动量的人身上都存在，最活跃那组只是把这个起点推到 5 小时。
  Note: The solid takeaway: how much you move matters more than how long you sit, and enough exercise essentially erases the sitting risk. Note these are observational studies—the most sedentary group includes many people who were already sick, so the "2.63-fold risk" partly reflects "sick first, then sat." Also in the same pooled analysis, 3+ hours of daily TV carried risk at every activity level; the most active group merely pushed that threshold to 5 hours.

### 18. 少吃加工肉（火腿、培根、香肠、午餐肉） / Eat less processed meat (ham, bacon, sausage, luncheon meat)
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，甚至省钱，也不占时间。难在要少吃自己爱吃的那口。
  Cost: Costs nothing—even saves money—and takes no time. The hard part is eating less of something you love.
- 说人话：火腿、培根、香肠吃得最多的人，同期死亡的概率比吃得最少的人高约两成。每天多吃一份加工肉，概率也高两成。
  In plain terms: The heaviest consumers of ham, bacon, and sausage die at about a 20% higher rate than the lightest consumers. Each extra daily serving of processed meat adds about 20%.
- 收益：把多项跟踪研究合起来算（只记录、不分组）：加工肉吃得最多的那组，比吃得最少的那组死亡风险高约 23%（RR 1.23）。红肉合计高约 29%（RR 1.29）。没加工的红肉高约 10%（RR 1.10），但这一项没看出统计上的差别。另一项汇总是按每天吃的量算的。加工肉每天多吃 1 份，死亡风险高约 23%（RR 1.23）。红肉每天多吃 1 份，高约 10%（RR 1.10）。
  Benefit: Pooling multiple cohort studies (observational, no randomization): the highest processed-meat consumers had about 23% higher mortality than the lowest (RR 1.23); total red meat about 29% higher (RR 1.29); unprocessed red meat about 10% higher (RR 1.10), not statistically significant. Another pooled analysis by daily intake: each extra daily serving of processed meat added about 23% (RR 1.23); each extra serving of red meat, about 10% (RR 1.10).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Larsson SC, Orsini N (2014). Red meat and processed meat consumption and all-cause mortality: a meta-analysis. American Journal of Epidemiology. <https://doi.org/10.1093/aje/kwt261>；Schwingshackl L 等 (2017). Food groups and risk of all-cause mortality: a systematic review and meta-analysis of prospective studies. American Journal of Clinical Nutrition. <https://doi.org/10.3945/ajcn.117.153148>；Johnston BC 等 (2019). Unprocessed Red Meat and Processed Meat Consumption: Dietary Guideline Recommendations From the NutriRECS Consortium. Annals of Internal Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.7326/M19-1621>（争议方 NutriRECS 指南）
- 备注：争议。NutriRECS 指南用一套通行的证据打分法（GRADE），把这些研究评为「不太靠得住」。它只给了一句很弱的建议：照现在这么吃就行。双方争的是证据够不够硬，不是方向。没有研究说加工肉有好处。没加工的红肉影响小，也看不出统计上的差别。要管住的是加工肉。
  Note: Debated. The NutriRECS guidelines applied the standard GRADE evidence rating and judged these studies "not very trustworthy," offering only a weak recommendation to keep eating as you do. The fight is about how solid the evidence is, not the direction. No study says processed meat helps. Unprocessed red meat shows a small, non-significant effect. Processed meat is the one to rein in.

### 19. 少喝或不喝酒 / Drink less, or not at all
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，还省钱，也不占时间。难在社交场合推酒时的一点尴尬。
  Cost: Costs nothing—saves money—and takes no time. The hard part is the mild awkwardness of declining drinks socially.
- 说人话：每周喝过 100 g 纯酒精以后，喝得越多死得越早。100 g 纯酒精大约是 2.5 L 啤酒。40 岁时每周喝 100–200 g 的人，少活半年左右；喝 200–350 g 的少一两年；超过 350 g 的少四五年。按整体健康算，最划算的量是不喝。
  In plain terms: Past 100 g of pure alcohol a week, the more you drink, the earlier you die. 100 g is about 2.5 liters of beer. At age 40, drinking 100–200 g a week costs about half a year of life; 200–350 g costs 1–2 years; over 350 g costs 4–5 years. For overall health, the best amount is zero.
- 收益：把 83 项往后跟踪的研究合起来算，只记录、不分组，60 万名在喝酒的人。死亡风险的最低点，在每周不超过 100 g 纯酒精。这个量大约是 5% 的啤酒 2.5 L，或者 40 度烈酒 300 mL（如威士忌、伏特加）。40 岁时每周喝 100–200 g 的人，预期寿命少约 6 个月。每周喝 200–350 g 的少 1–2 年。超过 350 g 的少 4–5 年。以上三项原文以寿命年报告。全球疾病负担研究 GBD 2016 把各类健康损失合起来算，风险最低的饮酒量是 0 杯/周。还有一项汇总把研究设计上的偏差校正过了。每天喝 1.3–24 g 的人和一辈子不喝的人比，死亡风险低约 7%（RR 0.93）。但这一项没看出统计上的差别。每天喝 45–64 g 的高约 19%（RR 1.19）。每天 65 g 以上的高约 35%（RR 1.35）。
  Benefit: Pooling 83 prospective studies (observational, no randomization) with 600,000 drinkers: mortality risk bottomed out at no more than 100 g of pure alcohol a week—about 2.5 L of 5% beer, or 300 mL of 40% liquor (like whiskey or vodka). At age 40, drinking 100–200 g a week cost about 6 months of life expectancy; 200–350 g cost 1–2 years; over 350 g cost 4–5 years (reported in life-years in the original). The Global Burden of Disease 2016 study, totaling all health losses, found the lowest-risk amount to be 0 drinks per week. Another pooled analysis corrected for study-design biases: drinking 1.3–24 g a day versus lifetime abstinence was about 7% lower (RR 0.93), not statistically significant; 45–64 g a day was about 19% higher (RR 1.19); 65+ g a day about 35% higher (RR 1.35).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Wood AM 等 (2018). Risk thresholds for alcohol consumption: combined analysis of individual-participant data for 599 912 current drinkers in 83 prospective studies. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(18)30134-X>；GBD 2016 Alcohol Collaborators (2018). Alcohol use and burden for 195 countries and territories, 1990–2016: a systematic analysis for the Global Burden of Disease Study 2016. Lancet. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(18)31310-2>；Zhao J 等 (2023). Association Between Daily Alcohol Intake and Risk of All-Cause Mortality: A Systematic Review and Meta-analyses. JAMA Network Open. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2023.6185>；Di Castelnuovo A 等 (2006). Alcohol dosing and total mortality in men and women: an updated meta-analysis of 34 prospective studies. Archives of Internal Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1001/archinte.166.22.2437>（争议方）
- 备注：争议。认为「少喝有好处」的一方，是一份 34 项长期跟踪研究的汇总。它报告：少量饮酒的人总死亡率最多低 17%–18%。它给的量是男性每天不超过 4 杯、女性不超过 2 杯。在这个量以内，喝得多的人死亡率反而低。反方认为，「不喝酒」那一组里混进了因病戒酒的人和本来身体就差的人。有一项汇总专门把这些干扰扣掉，做完以后保护作用就没了。全球疾病负担研究也给出 0 最安全。稳妥的读法是：少喝不会明显伤寿命，多喝一定伤。「为了健康开始喝酒」没有依据。想少喝怎么办见本节第 21 条（先数杯数再找医生）。天天喝的人不能自己硬戒，见本节第 20 条（别自己硬戒）
  Note: Debated. The "a little helps" camp rests on a pooled analysis of 34 long-term cohort studies reporting up to 17%–18% lower total mortality in light drinkers—defined as up to 4 drinks a day for men, 2 for women, with mortality falling as intake rose within that range. The other camp argues the "non-drinker" group was contaminated by sick quitters and people already in poor health. One pooled analysis that adjusted for these biases found the protective effect vanished. The Global Burden of Disease study also says zero is safest. The safe reading: light drinking doesn't clearly shorten life; heavy drinking definitely does. There is no basis for starting to drink "for your health." For cutting down, see item 21; daily drinkers must not quit cold turkey alone, see item 20.

### 20. 每天都喝酒、一停就手抖心慌的人，别自己硬戒 / If you drink daily and shake or panic when you stop, don't quit cold turkey on your own
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：去医院门诊或急诊、看成瘾医学专科，几十美元（copay 或自费）。严重的要住几天院。
  Cost: An outpatient or ER visit, or an addiction-medicine specialist—tens of dollars (copay or self-pay). Severe cases need a few days in the hospital.
- 说人话：天天大量喝酒的人突然停，会出戒断反应。轻的手抖、出汗、心慌、睡不着，重的会抽搐或者出现震颤谵妄，那是会死人的急症。挪威跟踪了三万多人，得过震颤谵妄的那批，往后每年有 8% 的人死亡。想戒就先去医院，说清你每天喝多少。
  In plain terms: Heavy daily drinkers who stop suddenly can get withdrawal. Mild cases: tremor, sweating, racing heart, insomnia. Severe cases: seizures or delirium tremens—a medical emergency that kills. Norway tracked 36,000+ people; those who'd had delirium tremens died at 8% per year afterward. To quit, go to a hospital first and state exactly how much you drink daily.
- 收益：一篇 2026 年的临床综述写明：长期大量饮酒的人在突然停酒或者大幅减量之后会出现戒断综合征，表现包括震颤、自主神经兴奋、焦虑、失眠、感知异常、癫痫发作和震颤谵妄，在普通内科诊疗中「常见且可能危及生命」。挪威的全国登记队列纳入 2009 到 2015 年诊断为酒精依赖、酒精戒断状态或震颤谵妄的 36287 人：震颤谵妄组的年死亡率 8.0%，戒断状态组 5.0%，酒精依赖组 3.6%。震颤谵妄组的标准化死亡比 9.8（95% CI 8.9–10.7，这是可信范围），意思是死亡风险约为同龄一般人群的 9.8 倍。
  Benefit: A 2026 clinical review states that long-term heavy drinkers who stop suddenly or cut down sharply can develop withdrawal syndrome—tremor, autonomic hyperactivity, anxiety, insomnia, perceptual disturbances, seizures, and delirium tremens—described as "common and potentially life-threatening" in general medical care. Norway's national registry cohort included 36,287 people diagnosed with alcohol dependence, alcohol withdrawal, or delirium tremens from 2009 to 2015: annual mortality was 8.0% in the delirium tremens group, 5.0% in the withdrawal group, and 3.6% in the dependence group. The delirium tremens group had a standardized mortality ratio of 9.8 (95% CI 8.9–10.7)—about 9.8 times the mortality of the general same-age population.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Caputo F, Lungaro L, Costanzini A, De Giorgio R, Addolorato G (2026). Alcohol withdrawal syndrome in hospitalized patients: a practical review. European Journal of Internal Medicine, 107103. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ejim.2026.107103>；Bramness JG, Heiberg IH, Høye A, Rossow I (2023). Mortality and alcohol-related morbidity in patients with delirium tremens, alcohol withdrawal state or alcohol dependence in Norway: A register-based prospective cohort study. Addiction, 118(12), 2352–2359. <https://doi.org/10.1111/add.16297>
- 备注：自己怎么判断：几乎天天喝、一天不喝就手抖出汗心慌睡不着、早上要喝一点才舒服，占一条就别自己硬停。医院的常规做法是用苯二氮䓬类药物把这几天顶过去，同时补硫胺素（维生素 B1）。定 B 级是因为挪威那份队列说的是「得过震颤谵妄的人后来怎么样」，不是「自己硬戒会怎么样」，两者之间是推断。酒还是要戒，只是换成去医院戒。每周喝多少算多见本节第 19 条（少喝或不喝酒），想少喝怎么办见本节第 21 条（先数杯数再找医生）。
  Note: How to tell: if you drink almost daily, shake/sweat/race/sleepless when you skip a day, or need a morning drink to feel right—any one of these means don't stop on your own. Hospitals typically bridge those days with benzodiazepines plus thiamine (vitamin B1). It's graded B because the Norwegian cohort shows "what happened to people who had delirium tremens," not "what happens when you quit cold turkey yourself"—the link is inferred. The alcohol still has to go; just quit under medical care. For how much is too much, see item 19; for cutting down, see item 21.

### 21. 想少喝酒，先把一周喝了多少数出来，再找医生聊几分钟 / To drink less, first count your weekly intake, then talk to your doctor for a few minutes
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。数杯数每天记一笔就行。在门诊顺口问一句也不额外花钱。
  Cost: Costs nothing. Just log your drinks each day. Asking during a doctor visit adds nothing.
- 说人话：医生花几分钟看你的饮酒量、说清风险、帮你定个目标，一年后平均每周少喝 20 克纯酒精，大约半升啤酒。谈更久并没有更多好处。已经停不下来的，国外有两种药被证明管用，大约每治 12 个人能多 1 个人不再回到一喝就喝多。
  In plain terms: A few minutes with your doctor reviewing your intake, explaining the risks, and setting a goal cuts about 20 grams of pure alcohol a week a year later—roughly half a liter of beer. Longer counseling adds no extra benefit. For those who can't stop, two medications have proven effective: roughly 1 in 12 treated avoids returning to heavy drinking.
- 收益：34 项随机试验、15197 人合起来算，接受简短干预的人在一年后比只接受最少干预或不干预的人每周少喝 20 克纯酒精（95% CI 少 12 到 28 克，这是可信范围；中等质量证据）。这些研究里受试者的基线饮酒量平均是每周 244 克。简短干预指的是不超过 5 次、总时长不到 60 分钟的建议或生活方式咨询；谈得更久并没有多出好处。药物方面：122 项随机试验、22803 人合起来算，阿坎酸每治疗 12 人（95% CI 8 到 26）可多让 1 人不再回到任何饮酒；口服纳曲酮每天 50 毫克，每治疗 12 人（8 到 26）可多让 1 人不再回到重度饮酒。
  Benefit: Pooling 34 randomized trials with 15,197 people, brief intervention cut weekly intake by 20 grams of pure alcohol at one year versus minimal or no intervention (95% CI 12–28 grams; moderate-quality evidence). Baseline intake in these studies averaged 244 grams a week. Brief intervention means no more than 5 sessions totaling under 60 minutes of advice or lifestyle counseling; longer counseling added no extra benefit. On medication: pooling 122 randomized trials with 22,803 people, acamprosate kept 1 extra person per 12 treated (95% CI 8–26) from returning to any drinking; oral naltrexone 50 mg daily kept 1 extra per 12 treated (8–26) from returning to heavy drinking.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Kaner EF, Beyer FR, Muirhead C, et al. (2018). Effectiveness of brief alcohol interventions in primary care populations. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2, CD004148. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD004148.pub4>；Jonas DE, Amick HR, Feltner C, et al. (2014). Pharmacotherapy for adults with alcohol use disorders in outpatient settings: a systematic review and meta-analysis. JAMA, 311(18), 1889–1900. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2014.3628>
- 备注：收益量级定「小」，是因为这两份证据量到的都是喝了多少，不是死亡率，按本书的规矩只有替代终点就记「小」。药物那部分的试验多在国外做，受试者多数已达到酒精依赖的诊断标准，而且试验里都同时给了心理社会干预，数字是「在这个基础上额外多出来的好处」。在美国，阿坎酸（acamprosate）和纳曲酮（naltrexone）是 FDA 批准用于酒精使用障碍的处方药，以医生和药品说明书为准，别自行网购。已经出现戒断反应的见本节第 20 条（别自己硬戒）。
  Note: Benefit is rated "small" because both evidence sets measured how much people drank, not mortality—and by this book's rule, surrogate endpoints get a "small." The medication trials were mostly done outside the U.S., participants mostly met alcohol-dependence criteria, and all trials also provided psychosocial support, so the numbers are the "extra benefit on top of that." In the U.S., acamprosate and naltrexone are FDA-approved prescription drugs for alcohol use disorder; go by your doctor and the drug label—don't buy them online yourself. If you already have withdrawal symptoms, see item 20.
