# 30. 上学以后的孩子·加州版

第 20 节（原书第 20 节）讲的是孩子刚出生那一年。中间 3 到 18 岁这十几年，写在这一节。分龄管屏幕那一条从 0 岁写起，因为美国儿科学会（AAP）的分档就是 0～2 岁、2～5 岁、学龄儿童三档，拆开写反而不好用。本节回答的是："别耽误学习"和"身体"撞在一起的时候，哪些事不能等。这些事有时间窗口，过了就补不回来。学业该不该那么卷，本节不讨论，只给窗口和数字。健康科学类条目的证据和数字沿用原书；法律与学校制度内容按加州和美国联邦现行规定写。

*Section 20 (original section 20) covers the baby's first year. The dozen-plus years from age 3 to 18 are in this section. The screen-time item starts at age 0 because the American Academy of Pediatrics' tiers are 0–2, 2–5, and school-age — splitting them would be less usable. This section answers: when "don't fall behind in school" collides with the body, what can't wait. These things have time windows; once missed, they can't be made up. Whether schooling should be this intense is not this section's question — only windows and numbers. Health-science items keep the original book's evidence and numbers; legal and school-system content follows current California and U.S. federal rules.*

前八条、第 12 条（散瞳验光）、第 13 条（窝沟封闭）和第 15 到 18 条，算的是死亡和身体、心理上真落下的损害。其中第 16、17 条讲家里怎么管孩子：不打不骂，去上教具体做法的家长课。第 18 条讲别把孩子送进封闭管理的"问题少年"营地。第 9 条（不买"治愈近视"的产品）算钱。第 10 条（睡眠和上学时间）和第 11 条（撑不住可以暂停上学）和第 14 条（孩子爱打游戏）算时间。溺水、交通、头盔见第 1 节（原书第 1 节）。婴幼儿见第 20 节。孩子被骗和游戏充值见第 5 节（原书第 5 节）。急症怎么认、怎么叫车见第 13 节（原书第 13 节）。这些都不在本节重复。

*Items 1–8, item 12 (cycloplegic refraction), item 13 (dental sealants), and items 15–18 are about death and real physical or psychological harm. Items 16–17 cover managing children at home: no hitting or yelling, and take a parenting class that teaches concrete techniques. Item 18 is about not sending children to locked-down "troubled teen" programs. Item 9 (don't buy "myopia cure" products) is about money. Items 10 (sleep and school start times), 11 (pausing school when overwhelmed), and 14 (gaming) are about time. Drowning, traffic, and helmets are in section 1 (original section 1). Infants are in section 20. Scams targeting children and in-game purchases are in section 5 (original section 5). Recognizing emergencies and calling an ambulance are in section 13 (original section 13). None of those are repeated here.*

### 1. 孩子说突然剧痛、而且越来越重，立刻去医院，不要让他忍到放学 / If the child reports sudden, worsening severe pain, go to the hospital now — don't wait until school's out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。请半天假，带孩子去挂急诊（ER）。
  Cost: Nothing out of pocket beyond copays. Take half a day off and bring the child to the emergency room.
- 说人话：有些病是按小时算的。男孩突然一侧阴囊剧痛，是睾丸扭转，也就是睾丸连着的那根血管拧住了，血供断了。6 小时内处理，睾丸保住的比例 97.2%。拖到 19 至 24 小时，只剩 42.5%。这种痛等不到放学，也等不到考完。
  In plain terms: Some conditions are counted in hours. A boy's sudden severe pain on one side of the scrotum is testicular torsion — the cord's blood vessels twist and cut off blood supply. Treated within 6 hours, the testicle is saved 97.2% of the time. Waiting 19–24 hours, only 42.5%. This pain can't wait until school's out or the exam is over.
- 收益：按规矩把 30 组病例报告、2116 例睾丸扭转患者找齐，合起来看。其中 1283 例是按 6 小时一档统计的。从开始痛算起，0 至 6 小时睾丸保住的比例 97.2%。7 至 12 小时 79.3%。13 至 18 小时 61.3%。19 至 24 小时 42.5%。25 至 48 小时 24.4%。超过 48 小时 7.4%。合并成大段看，12 小时内 90.4%，13 至 24 小时 54.0%，超过 24 小时 18.1%。作者的结论是，能保住的时间可以比通常说的 6 至 8 小时更长。所以就算已经痛了很久，也还是要去处理，不要因为"反正晚了"就不去。
  Benefit: A systematic review pooled 30 case series totaling 2,116 torsion patients; 1,283 were reported in 6-hour bands. From pain onset: 97.2% saved at 0–6 hours, 79.3% at 7–12, 61.3% at 13–18, 42.5% at 19–24, 24.4% at 25–48, 7.4% beyond 48 hours. Grouped broadly: 90.4% within 12 hours, 54.0% at 13–24, 18.1% beyond 24. The authors concluded the salvage window can be longer than the commonly cited 6–8 hours — so go even if a long time has passed; don't skip care because "it's too late anyway."
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Mellick LB, et al. (2019). A Systematic Review of Testicle Survival Time After a Torsion Event. Pediatric Emergency Care. <https://doi.org/10.1097/PEC.0000000000001287>
- 备注：提前跟男孩说一句：下面要是突然剧痛，马上讲，不用不好意思。睾丸扭转多发于青春期男孩，不少人因为难以启齿就硬忍。同样按小时算的还有急性阑尾炎（拖久了会穿孔）、糖尿病酮症酸中毒、脑膜炎。这几种本节没有逐条核实过"拖几小时坏多少"。它们共同的信号是：突然发作，一直加重，和以往任何一次都不一样。急症怎么认、怎么叫车（911）见第 13 节（原书第 13 节）。/ Note: Tell boys in advance: if there's sudden severe pain down there, say so immediately — no need to be embarrassed. Torsion peaks in adolescent boys, and many tough it out from shame. Other hour-counted emergencies include acute appendicitis (delay risks rupture), diabetic ketoacidosis, and meningitis — this section hasn't verified hour-by-hour numbers for those. Their shared signal: sudden onset, steadily worsening, unlike anything before. Recognizing emergencies and calling 911 is in section 13 (original section 13).

### 2. 该做的治疗别为了"等考完"往后拖，有些窗口跟着骨龄走，不跟着考试安排走 / Don't delay needed treatment to "wait until exams are over" — some windows follow bone age, not the exam calendar
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，要定的只是早做还是晚做。请假和复诊要占时间，治疗本身的花费另算。难在支具要求每天至少戴 18 小时，孩子得一直忍着。
  Cost: The decision itself is free; it's only about sooner vs. later. Time off and follow-ups take time; treatment costs are separate. The hard part is that a brace must be worn at least 18 hours a day, and the child has to endure it.
- 说人话：脊柱侧弯是脊柱往侧面弯。支具是一件硬壳背心，穿在身上把脊柱撑住。骨头还在长的时候戴，75% 的孩子没弯到要开刀的程度，只观察不戴的只有 42%。能戴的时间跟着骨头长完走，不跟着中考高考走，等考完往往只能开刀。
  In plain terms: Scoliosis is sideways curvature of the spine. A brace is a hard-shell vest that holds the spine in place. Worn while bones are still growing, 75% of children avoid progressing to surgical range, vs. 42% with observation alone. The bracing window follows bone growth, not the exam calendar — waiting until exams are over often leaves surgery as the only option.
- 收益：多家医院一起做的试验，找了 242 例够条件戴支具的青少年特发性脊柱侧弯患者。其中 116 例抽签分组，126 例自己选戴不戴。成败事先定好怎么算：弯度长到 50 度以上算治疗失败，骨头长完时还没到这个度数算治疗成功。按最初分到哪一组来算，不管后来有没有真戴够。抽签戴支具组成功率 75%，抽签只观察组 42%（OR 4.11，95% CI 1.85–9.16），成功的机会约为只观察的 4 倍。把抽签的和自己选的两拨人合起来算，是 72% 对 48%（把两拨人本来的差别扣平后 OR 1.93，1.08–3.46，仍约 1.9 倍）。每天戴的小时数越多，成功率越高（P<0.001，这个关系不太可能是碰巧）。试验因为支具确实有效，提前停掉了。
  Benefit: A multi-center trial enrolled 242 adolescents with idiopathic scoliosis eligible for bracing — 116 randomized, 126 choosing their group. Success was predefined: reaching 50+ degrees meant failure; finishing bone growth below that meant success, analyzed by original assignment regardless of actual wear. Brace group success was 75% vs. 42% for observation (OR 4.11, 95% CI 1.85–9.16) — about 4× the odds of success. Pooling randomized and preference cohorts: 72% vs. 48% (adjusted OR 1.93, 1.08–3.46, still ~1.9×). More hours per day meant higher success (P<0.001, unlikely to be chance). The trial was stopped early because bracing clearly worked.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Weinstein SL, et al. (2013). Effects of bracing in adolescents with idiopathic scoliosis. New England Journal of Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1307337>
- 备注：这里用脊柱侧弯举例，是因为它有抽签分组的试验，有明确的时间窗口。同类的还有斜视、弱视、龋齿，以及先天性心脏病那种可以挑时间做的手术。它们的共同点是越晚做效果越差，或者代价越大。具体窗口问专科医生。怕耽误上课，撑不住可以暂停上学，见第 11 条。/ Note: Scoliosis is the example because it has a randomized trial and a clear time window. Similar cases: strabismus, amblyopia, cavities, and schedulable congenital heart surgeries — all get worse or costlier with delay. Ask the specialist about the specific window. If worried about missing school, pausing school is an option — see item 11.

### 3. 孩子被欺凌，当天就书面报告学校并要求走处理流程，涉及打人、抢钱、传谣的直接报警 / If your child is bullied, report it to the school in writing the same day and demand the formal process; call 911 for assault, robbery, or defamation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。找老师，也找学校的反欺凌负责人，书面留记录。要报警就打 911。难在要顶住"别把事闹大"这类劝法。
  Cost: Free. Contact the teacher and the school's anti-bullying coordinator, in writing, on the record. Call 911 if it involves a crime. The hard part is resisting "don't make a big deal of it."
- 说人话：被同伴欺凌的孩子，冒出自杀念头的可能约是没被欺凌的 2.2 倍，有过自杀行为的约 2.6 倍。加州的 Seth's Law（AB 9，教育法典 §234.1）要求每个学区都有反欺凌政策和投诉处理流程：教职工目击欺凌必须在安全情况下立即干预，学校必须有调查和申诉的时间表。所以当天就书面报告，要求走认定程序，留下书面记录。
  In plain terms: Bullied children are about 2.2× as likely to have suicidal thoughts and 2.6× as likely to attempt suicide as non-bullied peers. California's Seth's Law (AB 9, Education Code §234.1) requires every school district to have an anti-bullying policy and complaint process: staff who witness bullying must intervene immediately when safe, and schools must follow a timeline for investigation and appeals. So report in writing the same day, demand the formal process, and keep a paper trail.
- 收益：34 项研究、284,375 名儿童青少年合起来算。被同伴欺凌和冒出自杀念头相关，OR 2.23（95% CI 2.10–2.37），出现念头的机会约为没被欺凌的 2.2 倍。另有 9 项研究、70,102 人，被欺凌和自杀未遂相关，OR 2.55（1.95–3.34），约 2.6 倍。男女、年龄和研究本身质量不同，结果都一样。网络欺凌和自杀念头的关联比线下欺凌更强。Seth's Law（2012 年 7 月 1 日起生效）要求学区政策明确禁止基于残疾、性别、性别认同与表达、国籍、种族、宗教、性取向的歧视、骚扰、恐吓和欺凌；教职工目击后"应在安全情况下立即干预"（§234.1(b)(1)）；学区必须公布政策和投诉流程，并给被欺凌学生及家庭提供支持资源（§234.5）。
  Benefit: Pooling 34 studies with 284,375 children and adolescents: peer victimization was associated with suicidal ideation, OR 2.23 (95% CI 2.10–2.37) — about 2.2×; and in 9 studies with 70,102 people, with suicide attempts, OR 2.55 (1.95–3.34) — about 2.6×. Results held across sex, age, and study quality. Cyberbullying's link to suicidal ideation was stronger than offline bullying's. Seth's Law (effective July 1, 2012) requires district policies to prohibit discrimination, harassment, intimidation, and bullying based on disability, gender, gender identity and expression, nationality, race/ethnicity, religion, and sexual orientation; staff who witness it "shall take immediate steps to intervene when safe to do so" (§234.1(b)(1)); districts must publicize the policy and complaint process and provide support resources to victims and families (§234.5).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：van Geel M, et al. (2014). Relationship between peer victimization, cyberbullying, and suicide in children and adolescents: a meta-analysis. JAMA Pediatrics. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapediatrics.2013.4143>；Seth's Law (AB 9, 2011), Cal. Education Code §234.1. <https://www.aclusocal.org/seths-law/>
- 备注：孩子身上出现说不清来由的伤，突然不肯上学，东西反复丢失，先按这几个信号去问。这份把多项研究合起来算的分析只是跟踪记录、没有分组对照，所以不能证明单是欺凌就导致自杀，但风险的方向和大小是清楚的。学校有义务作出认定，不能一句"小孩子闹着玩"了事。/ Note: Unexplained injuries, sudden school refusal, repeatedly lost belongings — ask using those signals. The meta-analysis is observational, not randomized, so it can't prove bullying alone causes suicide — but the direction and size of the risk are clear. The school must make a formal determination; "kids being kids" doesn't end the matter.

### 4. 让孩子每天在户外待够 2 小时，这是目前唯一有随机试验支持的防近视办法 / Get your child 2 hours outdoors daily — the only myopia-prevention method backed by a randomized trial
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。但每天要腾出时间，多半得从写作业和上网课的时间里挤。难在天天都要做到，不是做几次就完。
  Cost: Free. But it takes daily time, mostly squeezed from homework and online classes. The hard part is doing it every day, not just a few times.
- 说人话：小学一年级每天多上 40 分钟户外课，三年后新近视的孩子从约四成降到约三成。美国儿科学会也鼓励孩子每天户外活动。阴天也算数，课间到室外站着也算数。
  In plain terms: First-graders who got one extra 40-minute outdoor class each school day had fewer new myopia cases after three years — about three in ten vs. about four in ten. The AAP also encourages daily outdoor play. Cloudy days count; standing outside between classes counts.
- 收益：广州 12 所小学的一年级学生，以学校为单位抽签分组（干预校 952 人，对照校 951 人，平均年龄 6.6 岁）。干预的做法是每个上学日增加 1 节 40 分钟户外活动课，并鼓励家长在课外多带孩子出去。3 年里新近视的比例，干预组 30.4%（853 名合格者中的 259 例），对照组 39.5%（726 名中的 287 例）。两组差值 -9.1 个百分点（95% CI -14.1 至 -4.1，P<0.001），真实差距大致在少 4 到少 14 个百分点之间。3 年里度数的加深量（等效球镜变化）是 -1.42 D 对 -1.59 D，差 0.17 D（P=0.04）。干预组加深得略少一点。D 是量度数的单位。眼轴（眼球前后的长度）的增长，两组看不出差别（P=0.07）。
  Benefit: Twelve Guangzhou primary schools randomized by school (952 intervention, 951 control, mean age 6.6). The intervention added one 40-minute outdoor class each school day plus encouragement for parents to take children outside more. After 3 years, new myopia was 30.4% (259 of 853) in the intervention group vs. 39.5% (287 of 726) in controls — a 9.1-percentage-point difference (95% CI −14.1 to −4.1, P<0.001), the true gap likely 4–14 points. Myopia progression (spherical equivalent change) was −1.42 D vs. −1.59 D, a 0.17 D difference (P=0.04) — slightly less worsening. D is the diopter unit. Axial length growth didn't differ (P=0.07).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：He M, et al. (2015). Effect of Time Spent Outdoors at School on the Development of Myopia Among Children in China: A Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2015.10803>
- 备注：这项试验只做了 3 年，参加的孩子也都是从 6 岁开始的。两组近视的比例差 9.1 个百分点，只能让一部分人不近视，保证不了不近视。读写姿势参考"一尺一拳一寸"：近距离用眼 20 分钟后，远眺 20 秒以上。"待在户外"和"上体育课"不是一回事，课间到室外站着也算数。/ Note: The trial ran only 3 years, all children starting at age 6. The 9.1-point gap means some children avoid myopia — not a guarantee. For reading posture, keep the "one foot, one fist, one inch" rule of thumb; after 20 minutes of near work, look into the distance for 20+ seconds. "Being outdoors" and "PE class" aren't the same thing — standing outside between classes counts.

### 5. 分龄管屏幕：2 岁以下不给屏幕，2 到 5 岁每天不超过 1 小时高质量内容 / Age-based screen rules: no screens under 2, at most 1 hour of high-quality content daily for ages 2–5
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是把屏幕从孩子手里拿走，也难在家长自己当着孩子的面少看。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is taking screens from the child — and parents cutting their own screen use in front of the child.
- 说人话：美国儿科学会（AAP）的建议：18 到 24 个月以下避免屏幕（视频聊天除外）；2 到 5 岁每天不超过 1 小时高质量节目，最好大人陪着一起看、一起讲；学龄儿童和青少年，家里定个媒体使用计划，保证睡眠、运动和作业不被挤掉。理由是长时间近距离用眼会让近视来得早。屏幕和近视的证据没这么硬，真正试出效果的是每天户外 2 小时。
  In plain terms: The AAP recommends: avoid screens under 18–24 months (except video chat); ages 2–5, at most 1 hour daily of high-quality programming, ideally co-viewed with an adult who talks about it; school-age children and teens, set a family media plan that protects sleep, exercise, and homework. The rationale is that long near-work sessions bring myopia earlier. The screen–myopia evidence isn't that strong — what actually proved out in a trial is 2 hours outdoors daily.
- 收益：研究证据要弱得多：一项系统综述收了 33 项研究，11 项进了合并计算，对象从 3 个月到 33 岁。只算手机和平板的，近视的比值比是 1.26（95% CI 1.00 到 1.60，I²=77%）。和电脑合起来算是 1.77（1.28 到 2.45，I²=87%）。作者的结论只写到"可能相关"，并写明 33 项研究没有一项有可靠的屏幕时间测量。屏幕和孩子语言能力的关系也做过荟萃：42 项研究 18905 人，屏幕时间越长语言能力越弱（r=−0.14，95% CI −0.18 到 −0.10）。但一起看、看有教育内容的节目反过来是正相关（r=0.16 和 0.13）。这是相关不是因果，只能说明拿屏幕代替陪孩子说话不划算。
  Benefit: The research evidence is much weaker: a systematic review of 33 studies (11 meta-analyzed, ages 3 months to 33 years) found a myopia odds ratio of 1.26 for smartphones/tablets (95% CI 1.00–1.60, I²=77%), 1.77 including computers (1.28–2.45, I²=87%). The authors concluded only "possible association" and noted none of the 33 studies had reliable screen-time measurement. A 42-study, 18,905-child meta-analysis on language: more screen time meant weaker language skills (r=−0.14, 95% CI −0.18 to −0.10); but co-viewing and educational content were positively associated (r=0.16 and 0.13). That's correlation, not causation — it only shows that replacing conversation with screens is a bad trade.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Foreman J, Salim AT, Praveen A, et al. (2021). Association between digital smart device use and myopia: a systematic review and meta-analysis. The Lancet Digital Health, 3(12), e806-e818. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2589-7500(21)00135-7> ; Madigan S, McArthur BA, Anhorn C, Eirich R, Christakis DA (2020). Associations between screen use and child language skills: a systematic review and meta-analysis. JAMA Pediatrics, 174(7), 665-675. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jamapediatrics.2020.0327>
- 备注：定 B 是因为研究给的比值比下限正好压在 1.00，作者只敢说"可能相关"。所以把它当一条好执行的家规就行，不要当成"不看屏幕就不会近视"。真正有抽签试验撑着的是第 4 条（户外 2 小时）。学龄前也要查眼睛：1～3 岁、4～6 岁、7 岁之后都应当定期做屈光筛查，看远视储备还剩多少，查法见第 12 条（散瞳验光）。/ Note: Graded B because the odds-ratio lower bound sits exactly at 1.00 and the authors only claim "possible association." ### 12. 查出视力不良，去眼科做散瞳验光，之后按医生给的间隔复查 / If vision screening flags a problem, get cycloplegic refraction at an eye clinic, then follow the doctor's follow-up schedule

### 13. 恒磨牙长出来以后做窝沟封闭 / Get dental sealants once the permanent molars come in
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：每颗 $30–$60，不打麻药，也不用磨牙，一次几分钟。Medi-Cal Dental 给儿童覆盖窝沟封闭，有保险的问牙医。

- 说人话：窝沟封闭是把大牙咬东西那一面的沟槽用树脂封住，食物残渣就塞不进去。做完两年，长蛀牙的机会约为不封的八分之一。不封时有 16% 的牙面会蛀，封了只有约 5%。四年后仍然有效。

Resin sealants vs. no sealant: for first permanent molars in ages 5–10, caries at 24 months had OR 0.12 (95% CI 0.08–0.19) — about one-eighth the odds, from 7 trials (1,548 randomized, 1,322 examined), moderate-quality evidence. If 16% of control surfaces decayed by 24 months, sealed teeth were at 5.2% (3.13%–7.37%); at 40% control risk, 6.25% (3.84%–9.63%); at 70%, 19% (12.3%–27.2%). At 48–54 months, OR 0.21 (0.16–0.28) — still about one-fifth. The authors concluded resin sealants prevent 11%–51% of caries at 24 months. No adverse events in the 4 trials that recorded them.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Ahovuo-Saloranta A, et al. (2017). Pit and fissure sealants for preventing dental decay in permanent teeth. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD001830.pub5>
- 备注：第一颗恒磨牙（不会再换的那颗大牙）一般 6 岁左右冒头，第二颗 12 岁左右。封闭要趁牙刚长出来、还没坏的时候做，已经蛀了就只能补。龋齿也是入学体检的检查项，见第 7 条。/ Note: The first permanent molar (the one that never falls out) usually erupts around age 6, the second around 12. Sealants must go on soon after eruption, before decay starts — once decayed, it's fillings. Cavities are also on the school-entry exam checklist (see item 7).

Gaming's most common cost is crowding out sleep and outdoor time. Sleep guidance: item 10; outdoors: item 4 (2 hours daily); screen time: item 5. - 成本：不花钱。难在家长要先消化自己的情绪，当下不说狠话。

### 16. 管孩子别动手打，也别吼骂、骂他笨：常挨打挨骂的孩子，行为和情绪问题都更多 / Discipline without hitting, yelling, or calling them stupid: children who are frequently hit or berated have more behavioral and emotional problems
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难在火上来的那一刻停住手和嘴，还得换一套不打不骂的管法。换成什么管法，见本节第 17 条（教具体做法的家长课）。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is stopping your hands and mouth in the heat of the moment — and switching to a no-hit, no-yell approach. What to switch to: see item 17 (parenting classes that teach concrete techniques).
- 说人话：常挨打的孩子，攻击性和心理问题都更多，和父母也更疏远，挨更重的打也更多。打能不能让孩子当场听话，证据说不清。13 岁时常被吼、被骂笨的孩子，之后闯祸和抑郁都更多，父母平时再亲热也抵不掉。
  In plain terms: Frequently spanked children show more aggression and psychological problems, grow more distant from parents, and get hit harder over time. Whether spanking buys immediate compliance is unclear from the evidence. Children frequently yelled at or called stupid at 13 show more misconduct and depression afterward — parental warmth otherwise doesn't cancel it out.
- 收益：先看打。一篇荟萃分析只算"张开手打屁股或打手脚"这一种。它汇总了 75 项研究、111 个效果值，共 160927 名孩子。17 项结果里有 13 项和挨打明显有关，方向全是坏的。d 是效果大小，0.2 算小，0.5 算中等。小时候的攻击性 d = 0.37（95% CI 0.13–0.61）。外显问题 0.41，指打人、违反规矩这类。内化问题 0.24，指焦虑、抑郁这类。心理健康问题 0.53，和父母关系差 0.51，自尊低 0.15，认知能力差 0.17。被父母施加身体虐待 0.64。长大后的反社会行为 0.36，心理健康问题 0.24。111 个效果值合起来是 d = 0.33（0.29–0.38）。孩子当场听不听话，和挨不挨打关系不明显（d = 0.14，−0.19–0.47），这一项只有 5 个效果值。另一篇综述只看前后跟踪的研究，共 69 项。体罚稳定地预示孩子之后的行为问题变多，没看到它和任何长期的好结果有关。其中 7 项研究看了打的次数，5 项发现打得越多、问题越重。再看吼骂。美国一项研究跟踪了 976 个父母双全的家庭。孩子 13 岁时，父母常吼叫、骂脏话、骂孩子笨或懒，孩子 13 到 14 岁之间闯祸和抑郁症状都增加。父母平时亲不亲热，都抵消不了这个关联。反过来也成立：13 岁时闯祸多的孩子，之后挨骂也更多。法律上，加州允许父母"合理"管教，但造成身体伤害的殴打属于虐待儿童（Penal Code §273d，条号待核实），经常性的谩骂、恐吓也可能触发儿童保护介入。
  Benefit: First, spanking. A meta-analysis of just "open-hand spanking of buttocks or limbs" pooled 75 studies, 111 effect sizes, 160,927 children. Of 17 outcomes, 13 were significantly associated with spanking, all in the bad direction (d: 0.2 = small, 0.5 = medium). Childhood aggression d = 0.37 (95% CI 0.13–0.61); externalizing problems 0.41 (hitting, rule-breaking); internalizing 0.24 (anxiety, depression); mental health problems 0.53; poor parent relationship 0.51; low self-esteem 0.15; poorer cognitive ability 0.17; physical abuse by parents 0.64; adult antisocial behavior 0.36; adult mental health problems 0.24. Combined across 111 effect sizes: d = 0.33 (0.29–0.38). Immediate compliance showed no clear link (d = 0.14, −0.19–0.47, only 5 effect sizes). Another review of 69 longitudinal studies: corporal punishment consistently predicted more later behavior problems, with no long-term good outcome found; of 7 studies on frequency, 5 found more hitting meant worse problems. Now yelling. A U.S. study followed 976 two-parent families: frequent yelling, swearing, or calling the child stupid/lazy at age 13 predicted more misconduct and depressive symptoms from 13 to 14 — parental warmth didn't offset it. The reverse also held: more misconduct at 13 predicted more yelling later. Legally, California permits "reasonable" parental discipline, but hitting that causes bodily injury is child abuse (Penal Code §273d, section number to be verified), and chronic verbal abuse or intimidation can also trigger child-protective intervention.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Gershoff ET, Grogan-Kaylor A. (2016). Spanking and child outcomes: Old controversies and new meta-analyses. Journal of Family Psychology, 30(4), 453–469. <https://doi.org/10.1037/fam0000191>；Heilmann A, Mehay A, Watt RG, et al. (2021). Clinical Psychology Review, 33(1), 196–208. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpr.2012.11.002>；Larzelere RE, Kuhn BR. (2005). Clinical Child and Family Psychology Review, 8(1), 1–37. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s10567-005-2340-z>
- 备注：争议。这些研究几乎都只是跟踪记录、没有分组对照，说明不了谁导致谁。打屁股那篇荟萃里，72% 的研究只看两件事是否一起出现，或者靠事后回忆。另一篇荟萃只收前后跟踪的研究，扣掉孩子原来的行为之后，打屁股和外显问题的关联只剩 0.07，和内化问题只剩 0.10。那篇的作者认为影响很小。还有一篇荟萃发现，"有条件地打"在减少不听话和反社会行为上，比 13 种别的管法里的 10 种效果好。它也发现，打得过重或者以打为主，就比别的管法差。所以反方能说明的是：少量、有条件地打，未必比别的管法差。打得重、常打常骂，两边都认为更差。受益人主要是孩子。看到别人家长期打孩子，可以打电话给 county 的儿童保护热线报告，别自己上门拉架。/ Note: The controversy: these studies are nearly all observational, not randomized — causation unproven. In the spanking meta-analysis, 72% of studies were cross-sectional or retrospective. The main beneficiary is the child. If you see another family's child being hit long-term, call the county child-protection hotline to report it — don't intervene in person.

### 17. 孩子难管、自己总忍不住打骂，去上一期教具体做法的家长课 / If the child is hard to manage and you can't stop hitting or yelling, take a parenting class that teaches concrete techniques
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：社区和非营利机构办的家长课很多是免费或低收费的。有证据的那类课每周或隔周上一次，每次一到两小时，从四周到二十四周不等。回家要照着练。难在坚持上完、天天练。

- 说人话：教家长具体管法的课，在抽签分配的试验里，孩子打人、不守规矩的问题少了，家长的情绪也好了。家长打骂孩子也少了，刚上完课时最明显，一两年后还在，但小了很多。课名里常见的有 Triple P、Incredible Years、亲子互动治疗（PCIT），美国都有开设。

Parents hit and yelled less — most right after the course, with smaller but lasting effects one to two years later. Common program names include Triple P, Incredible Years, and Parent-Child Interaction Therapy (PCIT), all available in the U.S.
- 收益：Cochrane 汇总了 13 项试验，其中 10 项是抽签分组的，共 1078 名家长。孩子 3 到 12 岁，都有行为问题。上过课的家长，孩子的行为问题少了。家长自己评是 SMD −0.53（95% CI −0.72 到 −0.34，可信范围），旁人独立评是 −0.44（−0.77 到 −0.11）。SMD 和 d 是同一把尺子，0.5 算中等。家长的心理健康变好，−0.36（−0.52 到 −0.20）。负面或严厉的管法变少，家长自己评 −0.77（−0.96 到 −0.59），旁人评 −0.42（−0.67 到 −0.16）。作者说这些是短期效果。另一篇荟萃筛出 346 项抽签分组的试验，其中 60 项量了打骂孩子这类行为，分布在 22 个国家。打骂在课刚上完时少了，d = −0.46（−0.59 到 −0.33）。1 到 6 个月后是 −0.24（−0.37 到 −0.11）。7 到 24 个月后是 −0.18（−0.34 到 −0.02），越往后越小。这些数据大多是家长自己报的。
  Benefit: Cochrane pooled 13 trials (10 randomized) with 1,078 parents of children aged 3–12 with behavior problems. Children's behavior problems decreased: parent-rated SMD −0.53 (95% CI −0.72 to −0.34), independently rated −0.44 (−0.77 to −0.11) (SMD and d share the scale; 0.5 = medium). Parents' mental health improved, −0.36 (−0.52 to −0.20). Negative/harsh parenting decreased: parent-rated −0.77 (−0.96 to −0.59), observer-rated −0.42 (−0.67 to −0.16). The authors call these short-term effects. Another meta-analysis filtered 346 randomized trials across 22 countries, 60 measuring violent parenting: hitting/yelling dropped right after the course, d = −0.46 (−0.59 to −0.33); −0.24 (−0.37 to −0.11) at 1–6 months; −0.18 (−0.34 to −0.02) at 7–24 months, fading over time. Most data are parent-reported.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Furlong M, McGilloway S, Bywater T, Hutchings J, Smith SM, Donnelly M. (2012). Behavioural and cognitive-behavioural group-based parenting programmes for early-onset conduct problems in children aged 3 to 12 years. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, (2), CD008225. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD008225.pub2>；Backhaus S, Leijten P, Jochim J, Melendez-Torres GJ, Gardner F. (2023). Effects over time of parenting interventions to reduce physical and emotional violence against children: a systematic review and meta-analysis. eClinicalMedicine, 60, 102003. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.eclinm.2023.102003>
- 备注：给 A 是因为有 Cochrane 和多项抽签试验合并出来的数字。试验里的课有几个共同点。带课的人教具体做法，比如陪孩子玩、夸奖、奖励和管教。家长在课上练习、演练，回家还有作业。只发资料或者只讨论的课被排除在外，所以证据管不到只听一场讲座的那种。挑课时看它是不是连上几周、让你练具体做法。本书不推荐具体机构和收费产品。打骂少了的效果一两年后变小，课上完还得接着练。受益人是孩子，也是你自己。孩子已经有严重的情绪问题，带去精神科或儿童心理科，见本节第 8 条（抑郁筛查）。/ Note: Graded A on Cochrane plus pooled randomized-trial numbers. The effective programs share features: instructors teach concrete techniques (play, praise, rewards, discipline); parents practice and role-play in class with homework. Lecture-only or discussion-only programs were excluded, so the evidence doesn't cover one-off talks. When choosing, look for multi-week courses with skills practice. This book recommends no specific providers or paid products. The violence-reduction effect fades after a year or two — keep practicing after the course. The beneficiaries are the child — and you. If the child already has serious emotional problems, see psychiatry or a child psychologist (item 8, depression screening).

### 18. 别把孩子送进封闭管理的"问题少年"营地：出过虐待丑闻，效果没有证据 / Don't send your child to a locked-down "troubled teen" program: abuse scandals are documented, effectiveness is not
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，还省下一笔每月几千到上万美元的学费。难在孩子闹得最凶的时候，家长忍住不把他交给外人"封闭管教"。
  Cost: Free — and it saves tuition that runs thousands to over ten thousand dollars a month. The hard part is not handing the child to strangers for "closed management" when they're at their worst.
- 说人话：美国这类"问题少年"住宿项目（wilderness programs、therapeutic boarding schools）是一个监管松散的行业，多次曝出虐待、忽视甚至死亡事件，没有可靠证据表明封闭管教能治好行为问题。孩子真到了那一步，先带去精神科或儿童心理科由医生诊断再治，而不是交给营销话术。
  In plain terms: America's "troubled teen" residential industry — wilderness programs, therapeutic boarding schools — is loosely regulated, with repeated documented cases of abuse, neglect, and even deaths, and no reliable evidence that locked-down discipline fixes behavior problems. If the child truly reaches that point, get a diagnosis and treatment plan from psychiatry or a child psychologist first — not from a sales pitch.
- 收益：这类项目没有统一的疗效证据，虐待和忽视的报道则反复出现。加州的住宿照护机构理论上受 Community Care Licensing 管辖，但执法记录参差。把孩子送进去之前，至少查清机构的执照状态、投诉记录和员工资质。
  Benefit: These programs have no consistent efficacy evidence, while reports of abuse and neglect recur. California residential care facilities are nominally under Community Care Licensing, but enforcement records are uneven. Before placing a child, at minimum check the facility's license status, complaint history, and staff qualifications.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者整理，美国媒体对"问题少年"行业虐待事件的报道（具体案例与监管数据待核实）
- 备注：给 C 是因为只有个案报道，没有这类项目出事率的统计，也没有疗效的对照研究。孩子爱打游戏，先按本节第 14 条（孩子爱打游戏）看睡觉、作业和出门活动有没有被挤掉。成绩、睡觉、家里关系一起垮了，劝也停不下来，带孩子去精神科或儿童心理科，由医生诊断再治。已经送进去的，把孩子接回来。孩子说被关、被打，报警（911）。受益人是孩子。/ Note: Graded C because only case reports exist — no incident-rate statistics, no controlled efficacy studies. For heavy gaming, first check sleep, homework, and outdoor time per item 14. When grades, sleep, and family relationships collapse together and nothing helps, see psychiatry or a child psychologist for diagnosis and treatment. If already placed, bring the child home. If the child reports confinement or beatings, call 911. The beneficiary is the child.
