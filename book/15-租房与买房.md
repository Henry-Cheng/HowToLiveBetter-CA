# 15. 租房与买房 / Renting and Buying a Home

口径：金钱。租房最容易亏钱的地方是押金和中介。买房最费钱的地方是贷款利息，还有卖方知道、你不知道的那些事。
Scope: Money. When renting, the easiest places to lose money are the security deposit and intermediaries. When buying, the biggest costs are mortgage interest — and the things the seller knows that you don't.

### 1. 押金的数额、退还时间和扣减情形，必须写进合同 / Security Deposit: Put the Amount, Return Deadline, and Deduction Rules in the Lease
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。签合同时多花十分钟。难在要当着房东的面提出来。
  Cost: Costs nothing. Takes ten extra minutes at signing. The hard part is bringing it up face to face with the landlord.
- 说人话：加州押金现在上限是一个月房租，带不带家具都一样。小房东最多收两个月。搬走后 21 天内必须退，扣了钱要给书面明细。搬入和搬出都拍照留证。
  In plain terms: In California the security deposit is now capped at one month's rent, furnished or not. Small landlords may charge up to two months. It must be returned within 21 days after move-out, with a written itemization of any deductions. Photograph the unit at move-in and move-out.
- 收益：Civil Code §1950.5(c)：2024 年 7 月 1 日起（AB 12），押金不得超过一个月房租。小房东例外可收两个月：房东是自然人（或成员全是自然人的 LLC），名下出租房不超过 2 处、总共不超过 4 个单元。搬走后 21 天内退还，扣款必须给书面明细（§1950.5(g)）。押金的定义很宽，宠物押金、清洁费都算在里面，不能另立名目多收。
  Benefit: Civil Code §1950.5(c): effective July 1, 2024 (AB 12), a security deposit may not exceed one month's rent. The small-landlord exception allows two months: the landlord is a natural person (or an LLC whose members are all natural persons) owning no more than two rental properties with no more than four units total. It must be returned within 21 days after move-out, with a written itemization of deductions (§1950.5(g)). "Security deposit" is defined broadly — pet deposits and cleaning fees count toward the cap and can't be stacked on top.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Civil Code §1950.5. 条文解读见 <https://journal.firsttuesday.us/new-security-deposit-limitations-for-californias-residential-landlords/93075/>
- 备注：任务提示中的"AB 2559"经核实是建筑垃圾押金法案，与租房押金无关；租房押金上限法案是 AB 12（2024 年 7 月 1 日生效），已按 Civil Code §1950.5 原文核对。现役军人租客另有特殊上限，细节待核实。退租那天和房东一起拍照录像，墙面和地面都拍。
  Note: The "AB 2559" mentioned in the assignment turned out to be a construction-debris deposit bill, unrelated to rental deposits; the rental deposit cap is AB 12 (effective July 1, 2024), verified against the text of Civil Code §1950.5. Active-duty servicemember tenants have separate limits — details to be verified. On move-out day, photograph and video the unit with the landlord, walls and floors included.

### 2. 被断水断电、换锁、上门威胁赶人，先报警留证：法规禁止用这些方式逼你腾房 / Lockout, Utility Shutoff, or Threats: Call the Police First — Self-Help Eviction Is Illegal
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。打个电话报警，当场就能做。
  Cost: Costs nothing. One phone call to the police, done on the spot.
- 说人话：房东不能自己换锁、断水断电、扔你的东西来逼你搬走。赶人只有一条合法的路：走法院。遇上了先报警，出警记录是你以后索赔的证据。不要自己动手对抗。
  In plain terms: A landlord may not change the locks, shut off your water or power, or throw out your belongings to force you out. There is only one legal way to remove a tenant: through the courts. If it happens, call the police first — the police report is your evidence for a later damages claim. Don't fight back physically yourself.
- 收益：Civil Code §789.3：房东不得以终止租约为目的，故意中断水电气等公用事业、换锁、拆门窗、搬走租客物品。违者要赔实际损失，外加每天 100 美元罚金（每个诉因最低 250 美元）。加州总检察长 2022-DLE-05 公告：只有法院判决加执行令才能赶人，执行只能由警长（Sheriff）动手；Penal Code §418 把暴力驱赶定为轻罪。欠租不交、收到驱逐通知，都不改变这条。
  Benefit: Civil Code §789.3: a landlord may not, with intent to terminate the tenancy, cut off utilities, change the locks, remove doors or windows, or remove the tenant's property. Violators owe actual damages plus $100 per day (minimum $250 per cause of action). Per California Attorney General bulletin 2022-DLE-05: only a court judgment plus a writ of possession can remove a tenant, and only the Sheriff may carry it out; Penal Code §418 makes forcible eviction a misdemeanor. Owing rent or having received an eviction notice changes none of this.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Attorney General, Information Bulletin 2022-DLE-05. <https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/media/2022-dle-05.pdf>
- 备注：合法驱逐的通知期限：欠租是 3 天交租或搬走（Code of Civil Procedure §1161），3 天不算周末和法院假日；无过错结束月租，住满一年要给 60 天通知，不满一年 30 天（Civil Code §1946.1）。AB 1482 覆盖的房子，涨租上限是 5% 加当地 CPI、最高 10%，一年只能涨一次（Civil Code §1947.12）。
  Note: Lawful eviction notice periods: 3 days to pay or quit for unpaid rent (Code of Civil Procedure §1161), excluding weekends and court holidays; to end a month-to-month tenancy without fault, 60 days' notice if you've lived there a year or more, 30 days if less (Civil Code §1946.1). For homes covered by AB 1482, annual rent increases are capped at 5% plus local CPI, max 10%, once per 12 months (Civil Code §1947.12).

### 3. 中介不得代收代付租金和押金，钱直接给房东 / Pay Rent Only to the Owner or Their Authorized Manager — Beware Rental Scams
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。付款前核对收款人，几分钟。难在要顶住对方让你把钱转给个人的说法。
  Cost: Costs nothing. A few minutes to verify the payee. The hard part is pushing back when someone tells you to send money to a personal account.
- 说人话：加州没有"中介不能代收"这条法律，物业管理公司代收房租是常态。但钱只能给两个人：产权人本人，或产权人书面授权的物业管理公司。签约前去县 recorder（地契登记官）或 assessor（估税官）官网查产权人名字，免费。价格明显偏低、房东说自己在国外、让你电汇的，一律是诈骗。
  In plain terms: California has no law against intermediaries collecting rent — property managers do it routinely. But money should go to only two parties: the property owner, or a property manager with the owner's written authorization. Before signing, look up the owner's name on the county recorder's or assessor's website — it's free. Listings priced far below market, a "landlord abroad," or demands for wire transfers are always scams.
- 收益：加州法律不禁止经纪人或物业代收（代管的客户资金反而必须进信托账户，见第 7 条）。但租房诈骗的重灾区就是"钱给了不是房东的人"：骗子盗用真房源照片，收了押金和首月房租就消失。县 recorder 的地契记录和 assessor 的估税记录是公开的，免费可查，对上名字再付钱。
  Benefit: California law doesn't prohibit brokers or managers from collecting rent (client funds they hold must go into a trust account — see item 7). But the heartland of rental fraud is "money paid to someone who isn't the owner": scammers steal real listing photos, collect the deposit and first month's rent, then vanish. County recorder and assessor records are public and free — match the name before you pay.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验与常识；各县 recorder / assessor 官网（公开记录，各县网址不同）
- 备注：所有款项走支票或银行转账，备注写"某地址某月租金"。二房东转租要有原房东的书面同意，很多租约直接禁止转租。
  Note: Pay by check or bank transfer, with a memo like "rent for [address], [month]." A sublet needs the original landlord's written consent — many leases ban subletting outright.

### 5. 房子租期内被卖掉，租约继续有效，不用搬 / Sold During Your Lease? The Lease Survives — You Don't Have to Move
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。
  Cost: Costs nothing.
- 说人话：租期内房子被卖掉，你的租约跟着房子走。新房东必须按原合同让你住到期满，不用搬，也不用重新谈价钱。被银行法拍了，联邦法律也保护你：定期租约一般能住到期满，新买家要自住也得提前 90 天通知。
  In plain terms: If the building is sold during your lease, the lease goes with the building. The new owner must honor your existing lease through its term — no move, no renegotiation. Even in a bank foreclosure, federal law protects you: a fixed-term lease generally runs to its end, and a buyer who will live there must still give 90 days' notice.
- 收益：加州实行"买卖不破租赁"：新买家取得的是带租约的房子，自动成为你的新房东，租约条款不变。被法拍时，联邦《法拍房客保护法》（PTFA）要求新业主尊重定期租约至期满；无定期租约或新业主自住的，至少提前 90 天通知（Code of Civil Procedure §1161b）。原房东必须把押金转给新房东，否则两人对退还押金承担连带责任（Civil Code §1950.5(j)）。
  Benefit: California follows "sale does not break the lease": the buyer takes the property subject to your lease and steps into the old landlord's shoes, with all terms unchanged. In foreclosure, the federal Protecting Tenants at Foreclosure Act (PTFA) requires the new owner to honor a fixed-term lease through its end; with no fixed term, or if the buyer will occupy the home, at least 90 days' notice is required (Code of Civil Procedure §1161b). The old landlord must transfer your deposit to the new owner, or both are jointly liable for returning it (Civil Code §1950.5(j)).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：FDIC, Protecting Tenants at Foreclosure Act. <https://www.fdic.gov/news/financial-institution-letters/2009/fil09056.html>；California Department of Consumer Affairs, Civil Code §2924.8 notice. <https://solid.dca.ca.gov/publications/ab2610.pdf>
- 备注：合同、转账记录、入住时间的证据留好。新房东让你"重新签一份涨价的合同"，可以不签。AB 1482 覆盖的房子，新房东涨租同样受 5% 加 CPI、上限 10% 的约束。
  Note: Keep your lease, payment records, and proof of when you moved in. If the new owner asks you to "sign a new lease at a higher rent," you may refuse. For AB 1482-covered homes, the new owner's rent increases are still capped at 5% plus CPI, max 10%.

### 6. 签约前核对产权证和抵押情况，所有款项走转账并备注用途 / Before Signing, Verify Ownership and Liens; Pay by Check or Transfer with a Memo
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。查一次半小时。
  Cost: Costs nothing. About half an hour to check.
- 说人话：最常见的两种血本无归，一是租金付给了不是产权人的人，二是房子有大额抵押、后来被法拍。签约前去县 recorder / assessor 官网查产权人和抵押记录，免费。所有钱走支票或转账，备注写"某地址某月租金"，出了纠纷这就是直接证据。
  In plain terms: The two classic ways to lose everything are paying rent to someone who isn't the owner, and renting a heavily mortgaged home that later gets foreclosed. Before signing, check the county recorder's / assessor's website for the owner of record and lien history — it's free. Pay everything by check or bank transfer with a memo like "rent for [address], [month]" — that's direct evidence if there's ever a dispute.
- 收益：美国没有"不动产登记中心跑一趟"的说法，但县 recorder 的地契记录和 assessor 的估税记录都在网上公开可查。买房时 title company 会出 title report，把抵押、欠税、产权纠纷一条条列出来。付款留痕的道理和中国一样：写了用途的转账记录就是直接证据。
  Benefit: There's no single "real estate registration center" to visit in person, but county recorder deed records and assessor tax records are public and searchable online. When buying, a title company issues a title report listing mortgages, tax liens, and title disputes line by line. The logic of a paper trail is the same as in China: a transfer with the purpose written in the memo is direct evidence.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验；各县 recorder / assessor 官网（公开记录）
- 备注：签约时让房东本人到场，本人来不了的，要一份书面授权。二房东转租给你的，要有原房东的书面同意。
  Note: Have the landlord sign in person; if they can't, get a written authorization. If you're renting from a master tenant, get the original landlord's written consent to the sublet.

### 7. 二手房让中介代收房款的，必须走中介在银行开的交易资金专用存款账户 / Home Purchase Funds Must Go Through Escrow or a Broker Trust Account — Never to the Agent Personally
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难在要坚持走托管，可能得跟经纪人多争几句。
  Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is insisting on escrow — you may have to push back on the agent.
- 说人话：加州买房的钱不经过经纪人个人。定金进 escrow（第三方托管）或经纪公司的信托账户，条件满足才放款。卖方必须填 TDS 表，如实披露已知的房子毛病，"现状出售"免不掉这项义务。地税看 Prop 13：税率 1%，估值每年最多涨 2%，你一买就按成交价重估。有 HOA 的房子，月费和业主规约签约前看清。
  In plain terms: In California, home purchase money never goes through the agent personally. The earnest deposit goes into escrow (a neutral third party) or the brokerage's trust account, and is released only when the conditions are met. The seller must complete a TDS form disclosing known defects — an "as-is" clause doesn't waive that duty. Property tax follows Prop 13: a 1% rate, assessed value rising at most 2% a year, reassessed to your purchase price when you buy. If there's an HOA, read the monthly dues and the CC&Rs before signing.
- 收益：Business & Professions Code §10145：经纪人代管的客户资金必须进银行信托账户或中立 escrow，不得混同挪用。Civil Code §1102 起：1–4 单元住宅的卖方必须填写并交付 Transfer Disclosure Statement（TDS），如实披露已知的重大缺陷；"as-is"条款免不掉这项义务（§1102.1）。Prop 13（加州宪法第 XIII A 条）：地税税率 1%，估值年涨幅上限 2%，只有换手或新建才按市价重估。HOA 受 Davis-Stirling 法（Civil Code §4000 起）管辖，买 condo 前看 HOA 预算和会议记录。
  Benefit: Business & Professions Code §10145: client funds a broker holds must go into a bank trust account or a neutral escrow — no commingling. Civil Code §1102 et seq.: sellers of 1–4 unit homes must complete and deliver a Transfer Disclosure Statement (TDS) honestly disclosing known material defects; an "as-is" clause can't waive this (§1102.1). Prop 13 (Cal. Const. art. XIII A): a 1% tax rate, assessed value rising at most 2% per year, reassessed to market value only on change of ownership or new construction. HOAs are governed by the Davis-Stirling Act (Civil Code §4000 et seq.) — review the HOA budget and meeting minutes before buying a condo.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Business & Professions Code §10145. <https://www.lawserver.com/law/state/california/codes/california_business_professions_code_10145>；Transfer Disclosure Statement 解读. <https://journal.firsttuesday.us/real-estate-knowledge-explained/property-disclosures-explained/>；Prop 13 解读. <https://www.hoover.org/research/what-prop-13-and-why-does-it-still-shape-everything>
- 备注：title insurance（产权保险）是标配：贷款买房银行强制买 lender's policy，自己那份 owner's policy 也建议买，保的是过户前没查出来的产权问题。HOA 文件清单各不相同，向经纪人索取完整清单，细节待核实。
  Note: Title insurance is standard: lenders require a lender's policy for a mortgaged purchase, and an owner's policy is strongly recommended — it covers title problems that predate the closing and weren't caught in the search. The exact HOA document package varies; ask the agent for the complete list — details to be verified.

### 8. 别租隔断房：最小出租单位是原设计的房间，厨房、卫生间、阳台不得住人 / Don't Rent Unpermitted Subdivisions or Garage Bedrooms
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。代价是同样的预算，要么住得远一点，要么住得小一点。
  Cost: Costs nothing. The tradeoff: on the same budget, you live farther out or smaller.
- 说人话：车库、私搭的隔间改成卧室出租，在加州大多是违建。可住人的房间至少 70 平方英尺（约 6.5 平米）。被市政查到，严重时房子会被要求整改甚至搬出，押金和预付房租很难拿回来。
  In plain terms: Garage conversions and DIY room subdivisions rented out as bedrooms are usually unpermitted construction in California. A habitable room must be at least 70 square feet (about 6.5 m²). If the city catches it, serious cases get correction orders or even vacate orders — and good luck getting your deposit and prepaid rent back.
- 收益：加州住宅规范（California Residential Code R304.1）要求可住人的房间至少 70 平方英尺。车库改卧室、加建隔间都要申请许可（permit）；没许可的改建属于违建，市政执法部门可要求整改，存在安全隐患的可下令搬出（Health & Safety Code §17980 起）。这类房子还常伴随电线超载、没有烟雾报警器、逃生通道被堵。
  Benefit: California Residential Code R304.1 requires habitable rooms to be at least 70 square feet. Garage-to-bedroom conversions and added partitions need permits; unpermitted work is illegal construction, and city code enforcement can order corrections or, where safety hazards exist, order the unit vacated (Health & Safety Code §17980 et seq.). These units also tend to come with overloaded wiring, no smoke alarms, and blocked escape routes.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：2022 California Residential Code, R304. <https://codes.iccsafe.org/content/CARC2022P2/chapter-3-building-planning>
- 备注：看房时注意三点：墙是不是后来砌的，房间有没有窗，车库门还在不在。各城市的查处流程不同，细节待核实。
  Note: When viewing a unit, check three things: whether the walls were added later, whether the room has a window, and whether the garage door is still there. Enforcement procedures vary by city — details to be verified.
