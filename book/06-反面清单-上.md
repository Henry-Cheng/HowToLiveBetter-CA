# 6. 反面清单 / The Don't-Buy List

本节收的是「看起来便宜又有效」的东西。翻开原始研究之后，它们的好处接近零、等于零，有的还是负的。排的顺序看两件事：大家在这上面花的钱多不多，支持它的证据硬不硬。越靠前的越值得先停掉。

This section collects things that look cheap and effective. Once you open the original studies, the benefits turn out to be close to zero, exactly zero, or even negative. Items are ordered by two things: how much money people spend on them, and how solid the supporting evidence is. The earlier an item appears, the sooner you should stop paying for it.

本节条目按主题分成下面几块，括号里是条号。Items in this section are grouped by theme below, with item numbers in parentheses.

**保健品和补剂 / Supplements**：复合维生素（第 1 条）/ multivitamins (No. 1)，普通鱼油（第 2 条）/ regular fish oil (No. 2)，补维生素 D（第 3 条）/ vitamin D supplements (No. 3)，抗氧化补剂防癌（第 4 条）/ antioxidant supplements for cancer prevention (No. 4)，软骨素治膝关节炎（第 5 条）/ glucosamine and chondroitin for knee arthritis (No. 5)，维生素 C 防感冒（第 6 条）/ vitamin C for colds (No. 6)，花大钱买保健品（第 10 条）/ expensive supplements and tonics (No. 10)，长期吃益生菌（第 11 条）/ long-term probiotics (No. 11)，买排毒清肠产品（第 13 条）/ detox and cleanse products (No. 13)。

**体检和查出来的小毛病 / Checkups and incidental findings**：全身 PET-CT 和肿瘤标志物套餐（第 7 条）/ whole-body PET-CT and tumor-marker panels (No. 7)，通用体检套餐（第 18 条）/ generic checkup packages (No. 18)，尿酸高没痛过别吃药（第 19 条）/ high uric acid without gout—don't start medication (No. 19)，胆囊结石没疼过别切（第 20 条）/ gallstones without pain—don't remove the gallbladder (No. 20)，防肾结石别戒钙（第 21 条）/ preventing kidney stones—don't cut calcium (No. 21)。

**养生说法 / Wellness claims**：靠手环帮你减重（第 8 条）/ fitness trackers for weight loss (No. 8)，买有机食品（第 9 条）/ organic food (No. 9)，洗冷水澡增强免疫力（第 12 条）/ cold showers for immunity (No. 12)，不强迫自己喝水（第 14 条）/ don't force yourself to drink 8 glasses of water (No. 14)，防蓝光眼镜（第 16 条）/ blue-light glasses (No. 16)，把献血当保健（第 17 条）/ blood donation as a health tonic (No. 17)，吃早餐或轻断食控制体重（第 26 条）/ breakfast or fasting for weight control (No. 26)，靠正确搬运姿势防腰痛（第 29 条）/ "correct" lifting posture for back pain (No. 29)。

**花钱买运气和心情 / Buying luck and mood**：花钱算命看星座（第 15 条）/ fortune telling and astrology (No. 15)，买水晶貔貅这类物件（第 22 条）/ crystals and lucky charms (No. 22)，买东西改善心情（第 23 条）/ retail therapy (No. 23)，为往上挪一档多花钱（第 24 条）/ paying more to move one rung up (No. 24)。

**意志力和性 / Willpower and sex**：意志力像肌肉（第 25 条）/ willpower as a muscle (No. 25)，手淫伤身（第 27 条）/ "masturbation harms your health" (No. 27)，性取向矫正（第 28 条）/ conversion therapy (No. 28)。

### 1. 不要为了长寿或防心血管病吃复合维生素 / Don't take a multivitamin for longevity or heart-disease prevention
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：一天约 0.1 到 0.3 美元，一年约 30 到 100 美元（美国市面粗估）。吃它不费时间，一粒的事。吃久了容易产生依赖，觉得哪天没吃就像少做了点什么。
  Cost: roughly $0.10–$0.30 a day, about $30–$100 a year (rough US estimate). It takes no time — one pill. Over time it breeds a mild dependence: skipping a day feels like you neglected something.
- 说人话：1.4 万名男医生吃了十一年复合维生素。心脏和血管出大事的人、死掉的人，和不吃的一样多。把 9 项试验、5 万多人放一起看，对死亡也看不出影响。不买能省下一年几十到上百美元。查出来确实缺某种营养的人、孕妇补叶酸，不在这条里面。
  In plain terms: 14,641 male physicians took a multivitamin for 11 years. Major cardiovascular events and deaths were no different from non-users. Pooling 9 trials with over 50,000 people showed no effect on mortality either. Skipping it saves tens to about a hundred dollars a year. People with a diagnosed deficiency, and pregnant women taking folic acid, are not covered by this item.
- 收益：PHS II 试验找了 14,641 名男医生，跟踪了 11.2 年（这是中位数，一半人比它长、一半人比它短）。吃的人和不吃的人，心脏血管出大事的比例一样。主要心血管事件 HR 1.01（HR 是风险比，1 就是两边一个样）。95% CI 0.91–1.10 是这个数的可信范围。死亡也一样，全因死亡 HR 0.94（0.88–1.02）。美国给预防措施打分的机构 USPSTF，2022 年把 9 项随机分组的对照试验、51,550 人合起来算，结果是多维片和总死亡没有关联。评级 I，意思是证据不够、说不清。它看起来划算，是因为「一片补齐所有营养」听着顺，而且一天只要几美分
  Benefit: The PHS II trial followed 14,641 male physicians for a median of 11.2 years (half longer, half shorter). Users and non-users had identical rates of major cardiovascular events: HR 1.01 (HR is the hazard ratio; 1 means both sides are the same), 95% CI 0.91–1.10, the credible range of this number. Mortality was the same too: all-cause mortality HR 0.94 (0.88–1.02). In 2022, the USPSTF — the US body that grades preventive services — pooled 9 randomized controlled trials with 51,550 people and found no association between multivitamins and total mortality. It gave an "I" rating, meaning the evidence is insufficient to say either way. It looks like a bargain because "one pill covers all your nutrition" sounds right, and it costs only pennies a day.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Sesso HD et al. (2012). Multivitamins in the prevention of cardiovascular disease in men: the Physicians' Health Study II randomized controlled trial. JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2012.14805>；US Preventive Services Task Force (2022). Vitamin, Mineral, and Multivitamin Supplementation to Prevent Cardiovascular Disease and Cancer. JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2022.8970>；Gaziano JM 等 (2012). Multivitamins in the Prevention of Cancer in Men. JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2012.14641>（备注里那项癌症结果）
- 备注：争议：同一批男医生里还看过癌症。吃的人得癌症的比例低约 8%（0.86–0.998，P=0.04，勉强够上「不像碰巧」的线）。因为癌症死掉的比例低约 12%（0.77–1.01）。这个范围跨过了 1，所以可能只是偶然。这点好处很小，而且只在男医生身上看到过。查出来确实缺营养的人、孕妇（补叶酸）、平时吃得实在很偏的人，不算在内，按医生说的补
  Note: Controversy: the same group of male physicians was also followed for cancer. Users had about 8% less cancer (0.86–0.998, P=0.04, barely clearing the "probably not chance" bar). Cancer mortality was about 12% lower (0.77–1.01). This range crosses 1, so it may just be chance. The benefit is tiny and has only been seen in male physicians. People with a diagnosed deficiency, pregnant women (folic acid), and people whose diets are genuinely very unbalanced are excluded — supplement as your doctor advises.

### 2. 不要为了预防心血管病吃普通鱼油 / Don't take regular fish oil to prevent cardiovascular disease
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：一天约 0.2 到 0.5 美元，一年约 50 到 150 美元（美国市面粗估）。吃它不费时间。
  Cost: roughly $0.20–$0.50 a day, about $50–$150 a year (rough US estimate). It takes no time.
- 说人话：两项大试验分别找了 2.6 万人和 1.5 万名糖尿病患者。每天吃 1 克鱼油的人，心脏血管出大事的、死掉的，都和不吃的一样多。「吃鱼有好处」不等于「吃鱼油胶囊有好处」。不买能省下一年几十到上百美元。
  In plain terms: two large trials enrolled 25,871 people and 15,480 diabetes patients respectively. People taking 1 gram of fish oil daily had the same rates of major cardiovascular events and death as non-users. "Eating fish is good for you" is not the same as "fish oil capsules are good for you." Skipping it saves tens to about a hundred dollars a year.
- 收益：VITAL 试验找了 25,871 人，每天吃 1 克鱼油，跟踪了 5.3 年（中位数）。心脏血管出大事和死亡，两边都没看出差别。主要心血管事件 HR 0.92（HR 是风险比，1 就是两边一个样）。95% CI 0.80–1.06 是这个数的可信范围。它跨过了 1，说明看不出差别。全因死亡 HR 1.02（0.90–1.15）。ASCEND 试验找了 15,480 名糖尿病患者，也是每天 1 克，平均跟踪 7.4 年，同样没看出差别。严重血管事件 RR 0.97（0.87–1.08）。全因死亡 RR 0.95（0.86–1.05）。它看起来划算，是因为很多人把「吃鱼有好处」直接当成了「吃鱼油胶囊有好处」
  Benefit: The VITAL trial enrolled 25,871 people taking 1 gram of fish oil daily, followed for a median of 5.3 years. Neither major cardiovascular events nor deaths differed between the two sides. Major cardiovascular events: HR 0.92 (HR is the hazard ratio; 1 means both sides are the same), 95% CI 0.80–1.06 — it crosses 1, meaning no detectable difference. All-cause mortality: HR 1.02 (0.90–1.15). The ASCEND trial enrolled 15,480 diabetes patients, also 1 gram daily, average follow-up 7.4 years, likewise no difference: serious vascular events RR 0.97 (0.87–1.08); all-cause mortality RR 0.95 (0.86–1.05). It looks like a bargain because many people equate "eating fish is good" with "fish oil capsules are good."
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Manson JE et al. (2019). Marine n-3 Fatty Acids and Prevention of Cardiovascular Disease and Cancer. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1811403>；ASCEND Study Collaborative Group (2018). Effects of n-3 Fatty Acid Supplements in Diabetes Mellitus. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1804989>；Bhatt DL 等 (2019). Cardiovascular Risk Reduction with Icosapent Ethyl for Hypertriglyceridemia. New England Journal of Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1812792>（备注里那项高纯度处方鱼油试验）
- 备注：争议：有一项试验是有效的。REDUCE-IT 试验找了 8,179 人，他们本来就有心血管病或糖尿病，都在吃他汀，甘油三酯还偏高（135–499 mg/dL）。他们每天吃 4 g 纯 EPA 的处方药，出事的比例 17.2% 对 22.0%。风险低约四分之一（HR 0.75，0.68–0.83）。但那是给高危病人开的大剂量处方药，不等于普通人吃保健品鱼油。这里说的也不包括吃鱼本身
  Note: Controversy: one trial did work. The REDUCE-IT trial enrolled 8,179 people who already had cardiovascular disease or diabetes, were all on statins, and had elevated triglycerides (135–499 mg/dL). They took 4 g daily of a prescription pure-EPA drug; event rates were 17.2% vs 22.0% — about one-quarter lower risk (HR 0.75, 0.68–0.83). But that was a high-dose prescription drug for high-risk patients, not over-the-counter fish oil for ordinary people. Eating fish itself is also not covered here.

### 3. 不要为了长寿给不缺维生素 D 的人补维生素 D / Don't supplement vitamin D for longevity in people who aren't deficient
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：一天约 0.03 到 0.1 美元，一年约 10 到 30 美元（美国市面粗估）。钱不算多，真正的代价是以为自己已经做了预防。
  Cost: roughly $0.03–$0.10 a day, about $10–$30 a year (rough US estimate). The money is trivial; the real cost is believing you've already done your prevention.
- 说人话：本来就不缺维 D 的人补上 5 年，得癌症、得心血管病和死亡的人数，都和不补的人一样。查出来确实缺的人、有骨质疏松的人、长期晒不到太阳的人不在这条里面，他们按医生说的补。
  In plain terms: people who weren't vitamin D deficient supplemented for 5 years, and rates of cancer, cardiovascular disease, and death were identical to non-users. People with a diagnosed deficiency, osteoporosis, or chronically little sun exposure are not covered by this item — they should supplement as their doctor advises.
- 收益：VITAL 试验找了 25,871 人，每天吃 2000 IU，跟踪了 5.3 年（中位数）。癌症、心脏血管的毛病和死亡都没看出差别。会扩散的那类癌 HR 0.96（HR 是风险比，1 就是两边一个样；95% CI 0.88–1.06 是可信范围）。主要心血管事件 HR 0.97（0.85–1.12）。全因死亡 HR 0.99（0.87–1.12）。D-Health 试验找了 21,315 名 60 岁以上的澳大利亚人。他们每月吃 60,000 IU，跟踪 5 年。这一项也没看出差别，全因死亡 HR 1.04（0.93–1.18）。死亡的比例是 5.3% 对 5.1%。它看起来划算，是因为有些研究只跟踪记录、不做任何干预，发现维 D 水平低的人死得多，这被误读成了「补上就管用」
  Benefit: The VITAL trial enrolled 25,871 people taking 2,000 IU daily, followed for a median of 5.3 years. No difference in cancer, cardiovascular events, or death. Advanced cancers: HR 0.96 (HR is the hazard ratio; 1 means both sides are the same; 95% CI 0.88–1.06 is the credible range). Major cardiovascular events: HR 0.97 (0.85–1.12). All-cause mortality: HR 0.99 (0.87–1.12). The D-Health trial enrolled 21,315 Australians aged 60+, taking 60,000 IU monthly for 5 years — again no difference, all-cause mortality HR 1.04 (0.93–1.18), with death rates 5.3% vs 5.1%. It looks like a bargain because some observational studies — which only track people without intervening — found that people with low vitamin D died more, which was misread as "supplementing works."
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Manson JE et al. (2019). Vitamin D Supplements and Prevention of Cancer and Cardiovascular Disease. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1809944>；Neale RE et al. (2022). The D-Health Trial: a randomised controlled trial of the effect of vitamin D on mortality. Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-8587(21)00345-4>
- 备注：争议：这两项试验都没有特意去招真正缺维生素 D 的人，所以结论只管「本来就不缺的人」。查出确实缺的人、有骨质疏松的人、长期晒不到太阳的人、婴幼儿，还是按医生说的补。这里只看了总死亡和癌症、心脏血管这几件事，别的没看
  Note: Controversy: neither trial specifically recruited people who were truly vitamin D deficient, so the conclusion only covers "people who weren't deficient to begin with." People with a diagnosed deficiency, osteoporosis, chronically little sun exposure, and infants should still supplement as their doctor advises. Only total mortality, cancer, and cardiovascular outcomes were examined here — nothing else.

### 4. 不要为了防癌吃抗氧化补剂（β 胡萝卜素、维生素 E、维生素 A）/ Don't take antioxidant supplements (beta-carotene, vitamin E, vitamin A) to prevent cancer
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一天约 0.1 到 0.3 美元（美国市面粗估）。对吸烟的人来说，真正的成本是肺癌风险跟着往上走。
  Cost: roughly $0.10–$0.30 a day (rough US estimate). For smokers, the real cost is a higher risk of lung cancer.
- 说人话：这一条不只是没用。78 项试验、近 30 万人合起来算，吃抗氧化补剂的人死亡率反而略高一点。两项专门找吸烟者做的大试验里，β 胡萝卜素让得肺癌的比例高了 18% 到 28%，死亡高了 8% 到 17%。吸烟的人、接触过石棉的人尤其别吃。
  In plain terms: this one isn't just useless. Pooling 78 trials with nearly 300,000 people, antioxidant-supplement users had slightly higher mortality. In two large trials specifically enrolling smokers, beta-carotene raised lung cancer rates by 18%–28% and deaths by 8%–17%. Smokers and people exposed to asbestos especially should not take them.
- 收益：Cochrane 把 78 项随机分组的对照试验、296,707 人合起来算。吃抗氧化补剂的人，总死亡 RR 1.02（RR 是风险比，1 就是两边一个样；95% CI 0.98–1.05 是可信范围）。只看设计更严谨、不容易跑偏的那批试验，RR 1.04（1.01–1.07），略高一点。单看 β 胡萝卜素 RR 1.05（1.01–1.09），单看维生素 E RR 1.03（1.00–1.05）。ATBC 试验找了 29,133 名男性吸烟者，每天吃 20 mg，得肺癌的比例高 18%（3%–36%），总死亡高 8%（1%–16%）。CARET 试验找了 18,314 名吸烟者或者接触过石棉的人。他们得肺癌的风险高约 28%（RR 1.28，1.04–1.57）。死亡风险高约 17%（全因死亡 RR 1.17，1.03–1.33）。它看起来划算，是因为「抗氧化就是抗衰老，抗衰老就能防癌」这条推理链，每一步听上去都像常识
  Benefit: Cochrane pooled 78 randomized controlled trials with 296,707 people. Antioxidant-supplement users had total-mortality RR 1.02 (RR is the risk ratio; 1 means both sides are the same; 95% CI 0.98–1.05 is the credible range). Restricting to the better-designed, less bias-prone trials, RR 1.04 (1.01–1.07) — slightly higher. Beta-carotene alone: RR 1.05 (1.01–1.09); vitamin E alone: RR 1.03 (1.00–1.05). The ATBC trial enrolled 29,133 male smokers taking 20 mg daily: 18% more lung cancer (3%–36%) and 8% higher total mortality (1%–16%). The CARET trial enrolled 18,314 smokers or asbestos-exposed people: about 28% higher lung cancer risk (RR 1.28, 1.04–1.57) and about 17% higher mortality risk (all-cause mortality RR 1.17, 1.03–1.33). It looks like a bargain because the chain "antioxidant = anti-aging, anti-aging = cancer prevention" sounds like common sense at every step.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Bjelakovic G et al. (2012). Antioxidant supplements for prevention of mortality in healthy participants and patients with various diseases. Cochrane Database Syst Rev. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007176.pub2>；The Alpha-Tocopherol, Beta Carotene Cancer Prevention Study Group (1994). The effect of vitamin E and beta carotene on the incidence of lung cancer and other cancers in male smokers. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM199404143301501>；Omenn GS et al. (1996). Effects of a combination of beta carotene and vitamin A on lung cancer and cardiovascular disease. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM199605023341802>
- 备注：美国那个负责给预防措施打分的官方机构 USPSTF，2022 年给 β 胡萝卜素和维生素 E 评了 D 级，意思是建议别用。吸烟的人和接触过石棉的人已经因此受了害。蔬菜水果里本身含的抗氧化物不算在内，照吃
  Note: The USPSTF — the official US body that grades preventive services — gave beta-carotene and vitamin E a D grade in 2022, meaning it recommends against their use. Smokers and asbestos-exposed people have already been harmed by them. Antioxidants naturally present in vegetables and fruit are not covered — keep eating those.

### 5. 不要指望氨糖（葡萄糖胺）/软骨素治膝关节炎 / Don't expect glucosamine/chondroitin to treat knee arthritis
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：一天约 0.3 到 1 美元，一年约 100 到 300 美元（美国市面粗估）。
  Cost: roughly $0.30–$1 a day, about $100–$300 a year (rough US estimate).
- 说人话：膝盖有骨关节炎的人吃 24 周假药，也有六成说没那么疼了。吃氨糖或软骨素的人只比这多出四五个百分点，小到可能只是碰巧。一年花一两百到几百美元，买到的基本就是那六成的心理作用。
  In plain terms: knee-osteoarthritis patients who took a placebo for 24 weeks — six in ten said their pain eased. Glucosamine or chondroitin users beat that by only four or five percentage points, small enough to be chance. Spending a few hundred dollars a year buys you essentially that 60% placebo effect.
- 收益：GAIT 试验找了 1,583 名膝骨关节炎患者，吃 24 周。吃安慰剂（假药）的人里，也有 60.1% 说疼痛缓解了。氨糖只比它高 3.9 个百分点（P=0.30）。软骨素高 5.3 个百分点（P=0.17）。两样一起吃高 6.5 个百分点（P=0.09）。这几个差距都太小，不能排除只是碰巧。同一个试验里用来做对照的真药塞来昔布，高 10.0 个百分点（P=0.008）。它看起来划算，是因为「补软骨的原料」听起来正好对症，而且光吃假药就有六成人说有效
  Benefit: The GAIT trial enrolled 1,583 knee-osteoarthritis patients for 24 weeks. Among placebo-takers, 60.1% reported pain relief. Glucosamine beat that by only 3.9 percentage points (P=0.30); chondroitin by 5.3 points (P=0.17); the two together by 6.5 points (P=0.09). These gaps are all too small to rule out chance. The real drug used as a control in the same trial, celecoxib, beat placebo by 10.0 points (P=0.008). It looks like a bargain because "supplying the raw material for cartilage" sounds exactly on-target, and even the placebo alone helped six in ten.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Clegg DO et al. (2006). Glucosamine, chondroitin sulfate, and the two in combination for painful knee osteoarthritis. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa052771>
- 备注：争议：同一个试验里，一开始就疼得厉害的那批人，两样一起吃的有效比例是 79.2%，对照组 54.3%（P=0.002）。但作者说这是事后从数据里翻出来的，只能当线索，不能当结论。膝关节炎真正有证据的做法是减重和练肌肉，见第 2 节
  Note: Controversy: in the same trial, among patients who started out with severe pain, the combination worked for 79.2% vs 54.3% on placebo (P=0.002). But the authors say this was dug out of the data after the fact — a clue, not a conclusion. The approaches for knee arthritis that actually have evidence are weight loss and muscle strengthening; see Section 2.

### 6. 不要靠维生素 C 预防感冒 / Don't rely on vitamin C to prevent colds
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：冲剂一包约 0.3 到 0.5 美元，一年约 50 到 150 美元（美国市面粗估）。
  Cost: roughly $0.30–$0.50 per packet, about $50–$150 a year (rough US estimate).
- 说人话：天天吃维 C 基本不会让你少得感冒，感冒了才开始吃，效果也时有时无。天天吃能把一次感冒缩短约 8%，也就是少难受半天，值不值一年几十上百美元自己算。马拉松选手和在寒冷地区行军的士兵是例外，他们吃了得感冒的人能少一半。
  In plain terms: taking vitamin C daily barely reduces how often you catch colds, and starting it after you're already sick works inconsistently. Daily use shortens a cold by about 8% — roughly half a day less misery; whether that's worth tens to a hundred dollars a year is your call. Marathon runners and soldiers on cold-weather marches are the exception: among them, colds dropped by about half.
- 收益：Cochrane 把 29 项对照试验、11,306 人合起来算。普通人天天吃维 C，基本不减少得感冒的次数。得感冒的比例 RR 0.97（RR 是风险比，1 就是两边一个样）。95% CI 0.94–1.00 是可信范围。感冒之后才开始吃的那些试验，原文说「未见一致效应」：有的试验有效，有的没有。同一篇综述里，长期天天吃能把感冒难受的时间缩短约 8%（成人，95% CI 3%–12%），儿童缩短 14%（7%–21%）。马拉松运动员、滑雪的人和在寒冷地区行军的士兵是例外。5 项试验、598 人里，得感冒的人少约一半。发生率 RR 0.48（95% CI 0.35–0.64）。它看起来划算，是因为便宜、酸甜好喝，而且「维 C 防感冒」这个说法已经传了半个世纪
  Benefit: Cochrane pooled 29 controlled trials with 11,306 people. Ordinary people taking vitamin C daily barely caught fewer colds: incidence RR 0.97 (RR is the risk ratio; 1 means both sides are the same), 95% CI 0.94–1.00. Trials that started vitamin C only after a cold began showed, in the original wording, "no consistent effect": some worked, some didn't. The same review found that long-term daily use shortened miserable cold days by about 8% (adults, 95% CI 3%–12%) and 14% in children (7%–21%). Marathon runners, skiers, and soldiers marching in the cold are the exception: in 5 trials with 598 people, colds dropped by about half, incidence RR 0.48 (95% CI 0.35–0.64). It looks like a bargain because it's cheap, tastes sweet and tangy, and "vitamin C prevents colds" has been repeated for half a century.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Hemilä H, Chalker E (2013). Vitamin C for preventing and treating the common cold. Cochrane Database Syst Rev. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD000980.pub4>
- 备注：争议：同一篇综述里，天天吃确实能缩短感冒难受的时间。成人缩短约 8%（3%–12%），儿童缩短 14%（7%–21%）。算下来一次感冒少病半天左右。马拉松运动员、滑雪的人、在寒冷地区行军的士兵是例外。5 项试验、598 人里，得感冒的人少了约一半（RR 0.48，0.35–0.64）。绝大多数人不属于这两类
  Note: Controversy: the same review did find that daily use shortens cold misery — about 8% in adults (3%–12%) and 14% in children (7%–21%), roughly half a day less per cold. Marathon runners, skiers, and soldiers marching in the cold are the exception: in 5 trials with 598 people, colds dropped by about half (RR 0.48, 0.35–0.64). The vast majority of people belong to neither group.

### 7. 不要给没有症状的自己做「全身 PET-CT」或「肿瘤标志物套餐」/ Don't get a whole-body PET-CT or a tumor-marker panel when you have no symptoms
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：全身 PET-CT 一次约 2000 到 5000 美元（美国自费价粗估），还要挨一次电离辐射。肿瘤标志物套餐几十到几百美元。真正的成本在后面：查出来一处「异常」，其实是虚惊一场（这叫假阳性），接下来的复查、取组织化验、开刀，钱和罪都在这一段。
  Cost: a whole-body PET-CT costs roughly $2,000–$5,000 out of pocket in the US (rough estimate), plus a dose of ionizing radiation. A tumor-marker panel runs tens to a few hundred dollars. The real cost comes later: an "abnormality" that turns out to be a false alarm (a false positive), followed by repeat scans, biopsies, and surgery — that's where the money and the suffering go.
- 说人话：没症状的人去查，查出来最多的是虚惊一场。做全身 CT 的人八成多至少查出一处「异常」，将近四成被叫回去复查，可大多数是良性的。女性查卵巢癌标志物，死于卵巢癌的反而略多一点，还有人因为虚惊一场白做了一次手术。在美国，现在还有直接面向消费者的全身 MRI 筛查服务，一次几千美元，同样缺乏证据表明它能降低死亡率。
  In plain terms: when asymptomatic people get scanned, most of what turns up is a false alarm. Among people who got whole-body CT, over 80% had at least one "abnormality" and nearly 40% were called back for follow-up — yet most findings were benign. Women screened with ovarian-cancer markers died of ovarian cancer slightly more often, and some had unnecessary surgery over a false alarm. In the US, direct-to-consumer whole-body MRI screening services now sell for a few thousand dollars a scan, with likewise no evidence they reduce mortality.
- 收益：美国那个给预防措施打分的机构 USPSTF，对没有症状的女性用 CA-125 和超声筛查卵巢癌给了 D 级，意思是建议不做。依据是 PLCO 试验。因卵巢癌死亡的比例，筛查组 0.34%、常规组 0.29%。RR 1.18（95% CI 0.82–1.71）。查了没有好处。因为查错了（假阳性）而白挨一刀的比例，各试验里是 0.2%–3.25%，这些人里最多 15% 出现严重的手术并发症。另一项研究给 1,192 名没有症状的人做全身 CT 筛查。其中 86% 至少查出一处「异常」，37% 被建议进一步检查。原文说「大多数发现按描述是良性的」。它看起来划算，是因为「一次查全身、早发现早治疗」太符合直觉
  Benefit: The USPSTF — the US body that grades preventive services — gave a D grade to screening asymptomatic women for ovarian cancer with CA-125 and ultrasound, meaning it recommends against it. The basis is the PLCO trial: ovarian-cancer deaths were 0.34% in the screened group vs 0.29% with usual care, RR 1.18 (95% CI 0.82–1.71) — screening brought no benefit. Unnecessary surgery due to false positives ran 0.2%–3.25% across trials, with up to 15% of those patients suffering serious surgical complications. In another study, 1,192 asymptomatic people got whole-body CT screening: 86% had at least one "abnormality" and 37% were advised to get further workup; per the original text, "most findings were benign as described." It looks like a bargain because "scan the whole body once, catch it early" feels irresistible.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：US Preventive Services Task Force (2018). Screening for Ovarian Cancer: US Preventive Services Task Force Recommendation Statement. JAMA（同一份建议另有 USPSTF 官方页）. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2017.21926>、<https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/ovarian-cancer-screening>；Furtado CD et al. (2005). Whole-body CT screening: spectrum of findings and recommendations in 1192 patients. Radiology. <https://doi.org/10.1148/radiol.2372041741>
- 备注：肿瘤标志物那部分有随机对照试验撑着。全身 CT 和 PET-CT 那部分只有「看做过的人后来怎么样」这类跟踪记录，没有分组对照，证据要弱一档，相当于 B 级。真正有证据的那几项筛查，像结直肠癌、宫颈癌、乳腺癌、高危的人做低剂量胸部 CT，是另一回事，见第 1 节。已经有症状的人，或者已经确诊癌症的人做 PET-CT，那是看病不是筛查，另当别论
  Note: The tumor-marker part is backed by randomized controlled trials. The whole-body CT and PET-CT part rests only on observational follow-up — "watching what happened to people who got scanned" — with no randomized comparison, so the evidence is one grade weaker, equivalent to grade B. The screenings that do have evidence — colorectal, cervical, and breast cancer screening, and low-dose chest CT for high-risk people — are a different matter; see Section 1. PET-CT for people who already have symptoms, or who already have a cancer diagnosis, is diagnosis and treatment, not screening — not covered here.

### 8. 不要指望手环/手表的运动量化帮你减重 / Don't expect a fitness tracker or smartwatch to slim you down
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：设备 50 到 800 美元（美国市面粗估）。另外每天还要分神去看那些数据。
  Cost: $50–$800 for the device (rough US estimate). Plus the daily mental overhead of checking the numbers.
- 说人话：超重的人做同样的饮食和运动安排，戴运动手环的人两年减了 3.5 公斤，不戴的人反而减了 5.9 公斤。想减重，花钱买设备帮不上忙，真正管用的是运动本身。
  In plain terms: overweight people on identical diet-and-exercise programs lost 3.5 kg over two years with a fitness tracker — while those without one lost 5.9 kg. If you want to lose weight, buying a device won't help; what works is the exercise itself.
- 收益：IDEA 试验找了 471 名超重或肥胖的成年人，跟了 24 个月。大家做的饮食和运动安排是一样的，区别只在戴不戴可穿戴设备。戴的那组减重 3.5 公斤（95% CI 2.6–4.5，这是可信范围）。不戴的那组减了 5.9 公斤（5.0–6.8），反而更多。两组差 2.4 公斤（1.0–3.7，P=0.002）。它看起来划算，是因为「量化才能管理」这句话听着有道理，而且设备买一次就完事
  Benefit: The IDEA trial enrolled 471 overweight or obese adults and followed them for 24 months. Everyone got the same diet-and-exercise program; the only difference was wearing a tracker or not. The tracker group lost 3.5 kg (95% CI 2.6–4.5, the credible range); the no-tracker group lost 5.9 kg (5.0–6.8) — more. The gap was 2.4 kg (1.0–3.7, P=0.002). It looks like a bargain because "what gets measured gets managed" sounds wise, and a device is a one-time purchase.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Jakicic JM et al. (2016). Effect of Wearable Technology Combined With a Lifestyle Intervention on Long-term Weight Loss: The IDEA Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA. <https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2016.12858>
- 备注：这只有一项试验，用的还是 2010 年代初戴在胳膊上的设备，不能据此说「所有可穿戴设备都没用」。运动本身是有效的，见第 2 节。结论只有一句：想减重，设备不是必须花的钱
  Note: This is a single trial, and it used early-2010s armband devices — not grounds to declare "all wearables are useless." Exercise itself works; see Section 2. The conclusion is one sentence: if you want to lose weight, a device is not money you need to spend.

### 9. 不要为了「更健康」花溢价买有机食品 / Don't pay the organic premium for "better health"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：一般是同类普通食品价格的 1 到 3 倍。
  Cost: typically 1 to 3 times the price of comparable conventional food.
- 说人话：没有有力的证据说有机食品更有营养。真正看了人吃完以后健康怎么样的研究只有 3 项，都没看出差别。有机农产品被检出农药残留的比例确实低 30 个百分点，但检出不等于超标。多吃蔬菜水果本身有用，是不是有机的不影响这一点。
  In plain terms: there is no strong evidence that organic food is more nutritious. Only 3 studies actually looked at health outcomes in people who ate it, and none found a difference. Organic produce does test positive for pesticide residues 30 percentage points less often — but detected does not mean over the legal limit. Eating more vegetables and fruit is what helps; organic or not makes no difference to that.
- 收益：有一篇系统综述，把 17 项在人身上做的研究和 223 项测食品成分的研究合起来看。结论是「缺乏有力证据表明有机食品比常规食品显著更有营养」。这里面只有 3 项研究看的是人吃了以后健康怎么样（看的是过敏和有症状的感染），两边没看出差别。有机农产品被检出农药残留的比例低 30 个百分点，但检出不等于超标。它看起来划算，是因为「没农药就更安全，更安全就更健康」，每一步听上去都像常识
  Benefit: A systematic review pooled 17 human studies and 223 studies measuring food composition. Its conclusion: "the published literature lacks strong evidence that organic foods are significantly more nutritious than conventional foods." Only 3 of the studies looked at health outcomes in people (allergies and symptomatic infections) — no difference either way. Organic produce tested positive for pesticide residues 30 percentage points less often, but detected does not mean over the limit. It looks like a bargain because "no pesticides = safer = healthier" sounds like common sense at every step.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Smith-Spangler C et al. (2012). Are organic foods safer or healthier than conventional alternatives?: a systematic review. Ann Intern Med. <https://doi.org/10.7326/0003-4819-157-5-201209040-00007>
- 备注：直接看人吃了以后健康怎么样的研究非常少。所以结论是「没有证据说它更好」，不是「已经证明它没好处」。对环境的影响、动物福利、口味好不好，这里不管。多吃蔬菜水果本身有效，是不是有机的不影响这一点
  Note: Very few studies directly measured health outcomes in people. So the conclusion is "no evidence it's better," not "proven no benefit." Environmental impact, animal welfare, and taste are not covered here. Eating more vegetables and fruit works; organic or not doesn't change that.

### 10. 不要花大钱买保健品、礼品装滋补品来「调理身体」/ Don't spend big on supplements and gift-box tonics to "rebalance your body"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：从几十到几百美元不等。做成礼品装之后，价钱常常翻好几倍。
  Cost: anywhere from tens to hundreds of dollars; gift-box packaging often multiplies the price severalfold.
- 说人话：多维、鱼油、维 D 这些常见成分，本节前六条已经一样样查过，都没看出管用。包装上那句「This statement has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease.」（本声明未经 FDA 评估，本产品不用于诊断、治疗、治愈或预防任何疾病），是美国法律要求必须印的免责声明，不能反过来当成「FDA 背书」。这笔钱多半是买给父母、提着上门的，那份心意没有问题，只是东西选错了。
  In plain terms: the common ingredients — multivitamins, fish oil, vitamin D — were each examined in items 1–6 of this section, and none panned out. The sentence printed on the label — "This statement has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease." — is a disclaimer US law requires on the package; it must not be read backwards as an "FDA endorsement." This money is usually spent on parents or carried as a gift when visiting — the sentiment is fine, only the product choice is wrong.
- 收益：换回的是省下的这笔钱。理由在本节前 6 条：保健品里最常见的几种成分，多维、鱼油、维 D、抗氧化剂、氨糖、维 C，在随机分组对照试验里都没看出预期的好处，各自的数字在那 6 条的收益栏里。在美国，1994 年的《膳食补充剂健康与教育法》（DSHEA）规定，膳食补充剂上市前不需要经过 FDA 审批——「天然」「支持免疫」这类说法不需要事先证明有效就能印上包装。监管规定只说明它能宣称什么，不能当成它没用的证据，也不能当成它有用的证据。虚假的功效宣传由 FTC（联邦贸易委员会）负责打击。它看起来划算，是因为「不治病，只调理」这句话怎么说都不算错，谁也没办法证明它是假的。而且送礼时掏这笔钱，靠的是人情，不是证据
  Benefit: What you get back is the money saved. The reasons are in items 1–6 of this section: the most common supplement ingredients — multivitamins, fish oil, vitamin D, antioxidants, glucosamine, vitamin C — showed none of their expected benefits in randomized controlled trials; the numbers are in each item's benefit field. In the US, the Dietary Supplement Health and Education Act of 1994 (DSHEA) provides that dietary supplements do not need FDA approval before going to market — claims like "natural" or "supports immunity" can be printed on the label without proving efficacy first. What the regulation says about permitted claims is not evidence that the product is useless, nor evidence that it works. False efficacy claims are policed by the FTC (Federal Trade Commission). It looks like a bargain because "it doesn't treat disease, it just rebalances" can never be proven wrong — and because gift money runs on sentiment, not evidence.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：美国 FDA. Dietary Supplements（DSHEA 1994：膳食补充剂上市前不需要 FDA 审批；包装须印 "This statement has not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease."；虚假宣传由 FTC 打击）. <https://www.fda.gov/food/dietary-supplements>
- 备注：结论是「没有证据支持」，不是「已经证明无效」，所以只给 C 级。成分写得明确的产品，按前几条去查对应的研究。查出确实缺营养的人、正在治病的人，按医生说的来。要否掉的是产品，不是心意。老人自己买了、已经在吃的，只要没拿它顶替正在吃的药、也没吃出问题，就不必上门去争这一场，把该打的疫苗和该做的筛查安排上更要紧。同样的钱换成下面这些，每一项在书里都能翻到出处。陪老人去打每年的流感疫苗，见第 1 节第 20 条。50 岁以后的带状疱疹疫苗见第 1 节第 21 条。65 岁以上的肺炎球菌疫苗见第 1 节第 22 条。买个血压计，盯着他把降压药按医嘱吃满、把血压降到达标，见第 1 节第 7 条。把家里的浴室和楼梯改一改，陪他练平衡和腿部力量，见第 1 节第 13 条。到了年纪的癌症筛查，陪他去做一次，见第 1 节第 17 到 19 条。老人已经长期卧床的，压疮防护和长期护理保险见第 17 节第 7、8 条。要提东西上门，换成水果、米面、他爱吃的东西，比滋补品实在。真正要拦的是两类：一类是先交钱的投资养老，一类是会顶替掉药的，见第 17 节第 5 条
  Note: The conclusion is "no evidence of support," not "proven ineffective" — hence grade C. For products with clearly listed ingredients, check the corresponding studies in the earlier items. People with a diagnosed deficiency or under treatment should follow their doctor. What's rejected is the product, not the sentiment. If an elderly parent bought some themselves and is already taking it, and it isn't replacing prescribed medication or causing problems, don't pick that fight — getting their vaccines and screenings scheduled matters more. The same money buys each of the following, every one sourced in this book: accompany them for the yearly flu shot, Section 1 No. 20. The shingles vaccine after age 50, Section 1 No. 21. The pneumococcal vaccine at 65+, Section 1 No. 22. Buy a blood-pressure cuff and make sure they take their antihypertensives as prescribed until blood pressure is at target, Section 1 No. 7. Modify the bathroom and stairs, and do balance and leg-strength exercises with them, Section 1 No. 13. Age-appropriate cancer screening — go with them once, Section 1 Nos. 17–19. For a bedridden elder, pressure-sore prevention and long-term-care insurance, Section 17 Nos. 7–8. When visiting with gifts, bring fruit, rice and flour, or food they actually like — more solid than tonics. Two categories genuinely need blocking: pay-first "investment retirement" schemes, and anything that would replace real medication; see Section 17 No. 5.

### 11. 不要在没有肠道问题时长期吃益生菌 / Don't take probiotics long-term when your gut is fine
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：一天约 0.5 到 2 美元，一年约 200 到 700 美元（美国市面粗估）。
  Cost: roughly $0.50–$2 a day, about $200–$700 a year (rough US estimate).
- 说人话：45 项在健康成年人身上做的试验合起来看，吃益生菌不能让肠道里的菌长期变个样，停了就回到原样，血脂也没有改善。吃抗生素吃出腹泻这类特定情况另有证据，不在这条里面。
  In plain terms: pooling 45 trials in healthy adults, probiotics don't durably reshape the gut microbiome — it reverts once you stop — and blood lipids don't improve either. Specific situations like antibiotic-associated diarrhea have their own evidence and aren't covered here.
- 收益：一篇综述把 45 项在健康成年人身上做的试验合起来看，结论是「未能支持益生菌能引起肠道菌群的持久改变，也未能改善健康成人的血脂」。肠道里的菌确实会变，但只是暂时的，停了就回去。它看起来划算，是因为「肠道健康」这个说法足够宽、怎么套都不算错，而且每天一瓶单价很低
  Benefit: A review pooled 45 trials in healthy adults and concluded there is no support for probiotics causing lasting changes to the gut microbiome, nor for improving blood lipids in healthy adults. The gut flora does change — but only temporarily, reverting after you stop. It looks like a bargain because "gut health" is vague enough to never be wrong, and the per-day price feels low.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Khalesi S et al. (2019). A review of probiotic supplementation in healthy adults: helpful or hype? Eur J Clin Nutr. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41430-018-0135-9>
- 备注：同一篇综述也说健康人身上有一些小的改善，比如大便更成形、软硬合适，女性阴道里的乳杆菌变多。吃抗生素引起的腹泻，以及某一个特定菌株用在某一种特定的病上，各自有单独的证据，另当别论。这里只针对健康人每天当保健品吃
  Note: The same review noted some small improvements in healthy people — e.g., better-formed stools, more vaginal lactobacilli in women. Antibiotic-associated diarrhea, and specific strains for specific conditions, each have their own evidence — different matters. This item only covers healthy people taking probiotics daily as a supplement.

### 12. 不要为了「增强免疫力」逼自己洗冷水澡 / Don't force yourself into cold showers to "boost immunity"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。每天要难受 30 到 90 秒，冬天想坚持得靠毅力。有心血管病的人还要当心，冷刺激对他们有风险。
  Cost: free. Thirty to ninety seconds of daily discomfort; sticking with it through winter takes willpower. People with cardiovascular disease should be careful — cold shock carries risks for them.
- 说人话：3018 人的试验里，洗冷水澡的人请病假的次数少了 29%，但请假的总天数和别人一样，所以谈不上少生病。2025 年把 11 项试验合起来看，冲完冷水之后，身体发炎的指标反而短暂升高，免疫的指标没变化。爱洗随便洗，只是别把它当成一笔健康投资。
  In plain terms: in a 3,018-person trial, cold-shower takers called in sick 29% fewer times — but total sick days were identical, so they weren't actually sick less. A 2025 pooling of 11 trials found inflammatory markers briefly rose after cold immersion, while immune markers didn't change. Shower cold if you enjoy it — just don't treat it as a health investment.
- 收益：荷兰做过一项随机分组对照试验，3,018 人，洗完热水澡之后再冲 30 秒、60 秒或 90 秒冷水。这些人请病假的次数少了 29%（IRR 0.71，这是次数之比）。但原文写明「病假天数没有显著组间差异」：总共病了多少天，两边一样。生活质量、工作效率、焦虑也都没看出有意义的差别。2025 年一篇系统综述把 11 项随机分组试验、3,177 人合起来算。泡完冷水的当下和 1 小时后，身体发炎的指标反而升高。免疫指标没变化。压力只在 12 小时那个时间点降低。作者自己说证据「受限于 RCT 少、样本小」，意思是这类随机分组试验数量太少、每项人也不多。它看起来划算，是因为不花钱，而且「吃苦就有效」这个直觉太顺
  Benefit: A Dutch randomized controlled trial had 3,018 people finish their hot shower with 30, 60, or 90 seconds of cold water. They called in sick 29% fewer times (IRR 0.71, a ratio of counts). But the original text states "no significant between-group difference in sickness absence days": total days sick were identical. Quality of life, work productivity, and anxiety showed no meaningful differences either. A 2025 systematic review pooled 11 randomized trials with 3,177 people: inflammatory markers rose right after cold immersion and at 1 hour; immune markers didn't change; stress dropped only at the 12-hour mark. The authors themselves say the evidence is "limited by the small number of RCTs and small samples." It looks like a bargain because it's free, and "no pain, no gain" is an irresistible intuition.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Buijze GA et al. (2016). The Effect of Cold Showering on Health and Work: A Randomized Controlled Trial. PLOS ONE. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0161749>；Cain T et al. (2025). Effects of cold-water immersion on health and wellbeing: A systematic review and meta-analysis. PLOS ONE. <https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0317615>
- 备注：争议：请病假的次数确实少了 29%，这个差距不像是碰巧。但生病的总天数并没有少，所以更像是「病了也照常去上班」，而不是真的少生病。运动员训练完之后用冷水帮助恢复，那是另一个问题，这里不管。冷水澡本身对健康人没有害处，只是别把它当成健康投资
  Note: Controversy: sick-call frequency did drop 29%, a gap unlikely to be chance. But total sick days didn't fall, so it looks more like "going to work sick anyway" than actually getting sick less. Athletes using cold water for post-training recovery is a different question, not covered here. Cold showers aren't harmful to healthy people — just don't treat them as a health investment.

### 13. 不要买排毒、清肠、酵素、碱性水这类产品 / Don't buy detox, cleanse, enzyme, or alkaline-water products
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：几十到几百美元（美国市面粗估）。靠饿肚子来「排毒」的那种，还可能把血糖饿低、把营养搞缺。
  Cost: tens to hundreds of dollars (rough US estimate). The starve-yourself-to-detox kind can also crash your blood sugar and leave you malnourished.
- 说人话：排毒这个产业做得很大，可支持它的临床证据非常少，也没人拿市面上卖的排毒饮食认真做过试验。有人去查饮食酸碱、碱性水和癌症有没有关系，够格的研究只找到 1 篇，也没看出关联。
  In plain terms: the detox industry is huge, yet clinical evidence supporting it is vanishingly thin — nobody has seriously trialed the detox diets actually sold on the market. One review examined dietary acid load, alkaline water, and cancer: out of 8,278 papers, only 1 qualified, and it found no association.
- 收益：那篇综述的结论是「尽管排毒产业蓬勃发展，支持这些饮食的临床证据非常少」，而且「没有针对商业排毒饮食的随机对照试验」。另一篇系统综述专门查饮食的酸碱、碱性水和癌症有没有关系：翻了 8,278 篇文献，只有 1 篇够格收进来，结果也没看出关联。作者写的是「向公众推广碱性饮食和碱性水用于癌症预防或治疗是不合理的」。它看起来划算，是因为「身体里有毒素要排出来」「体质发酸会致癌」这两个前提听上去很像生理学
  Benefit: That review's conclusion: "despite the hot detox industry, the clinical evidence for these diets is very limited," and "there are no randomized controlled trials of commercial detox diets." Another systematic review examined dietary acid load, alkaline water, and cancer: 8,278 papers screened, only 1 qualified, and it found no association. The authors wrote that "promoting alkaline diet and alkaline water to the public for cancer prevention or treatment is not justified." It looks like a bargain because "toxins must be flushed out" and "an acidic body causes cancer" both sound like physiology.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：Klein AV, Kiat H (2015). Detox diets for toxin elimination and weight management: a critical review of the evidence. J Hum Nutr Diet. <https://doi.org/10.1111/jhn.12286>；Fenton TR, Huang T (2016). Systematic review of the association between dietary acid load, alkaline water and cancer. BMJ Open. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2015-010438>
- 备注：只能说「没有证据支持」，所以只给 C 级。把身体代谢产生的废物清出去，本来就是肝和肾在干的活。只要这两个器官没有生病，就不需要额外做什么「排毒」
  Note: All that can be said is "no evidence of support" — hence grade C. Clearing metabolic waste is the liver's and kidneys' job. As long as those two organs are healthy, no extra "detoxing" is needed.

### 14. 不要为了凑够「每天 8 杯水」强迫自己喝水 / Don't force yourself to drink "8 glasses of water a day"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。要花一点时间和精力去记着喝，晚上起夜也会变多。
  Cost: free. It costs a little time and attention to keep track, and more nighttime trips to the bathroom.
- 说人话：有人专门去追「每天 8 杯水」的出处，一项支持它的研究都没找到。食物和别的饮料里本来就带着大量水分，所以用不着喝那么多。渴了就喝，看看尿的颜色就够了。天热干活、剧烈运动、得过肾结石的人，还有不太觉得渴的老人，要另外主动喝。
  In plain terms: someone traced the origin of "8 glasses a day" and found not a single supporting study. Food and other drinks already carry plenty of water, so you don't need that much. Drink when thirsty and check your urine color — that's enough. People working in heat, exercising intensely, those with a history of kidney stones, and older people who don't feel thirsty should drink proactively.
- 收益：有一篇文献回顾专门查「8×8」这个说法，指的是每天 8 杯、每杯 8 盎司水。它的结论是「未找到任何支持 8×8 的科学研究」。它还写道，「对数千名成年人的食物与液体摄入调查强烈提示不需要如此大量」，因为食物和其他饮品已经提供了大量水分。这个说法看起来划算，是因为水不要钱、8 这个数字又整齐，而且「多喝水」这话在任何场合说出来都显得没坏处
  Benefit: A literature review examined the "8×8" claim — 8 glasses of 8 ounces of water a day. Its conclusion: "no scientific studies were found in support of 8×8." It added that dietary surveys of thousands of adults "strongly suggest such a large amount is not needed," since food and other beverages already provide plenty of water. The claim looks like a bargain because water is free, 8 is a tidy number, and "drink more water" sounds harmless in any setting.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：Valtin H (2002). "Drink at least eight glasses of water a day." Really? Is there scientific evidence for "8 x 8"? Am J Physiol Regul Integr Comp Physiol. <https://doi.org/10.1152/ajpregu.00365.2002>
- 备注：要否掉的是「每天必须喝够几杯」这个数字，不是说多喝水有害。渴了就喝，看看尿的颜色，这样就够了。在高温下干活的人、剧烈运动的人、得过肾结石的人，以及上年纪之后不太觉得渴的人要主动喝，另当别论
  Note: What's rejected is the number — "you must drink N glasses a day" — not drinking water itself. Drink when thirsty, check urine color, that's enough. People working in heat, exercising intensely, those with kidney-stone history, and older people who feel less thirst should drink proactively — a different matter.

### 15. 不要花钱算命、看塔罗、看星座来做决定 / Don't pay for fortune telling, tarot, or astrology to make decisions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是放弃一条拿主意的捷径，以后得自己想。
  Cost: free. The hard part is giving up a shortcut for decisions — you'll have to think for yourself from now on.
- 说人话：有个经典的课堂实验：学生拿到的「个人性格分析」其实人手一份、一模一样，大家却普遍觉得很准。算命显得准，道理就在这里。占星师挑对的次数和瞎猜差不多。代价不只几十美元：河南息县那个算命团伙（中国案例），收香火钱和最高 3888 元（约合 550 美元）的「供养套餐」，主犯判了十一年。在美国，FTC 和各州检察长也会打击打着「灵媒」「能量疗愈」旗号的诈骗。
  In plain terms: a classic classroom experiment gave every student the same "personal character analysis" — identical copies — yet nearly everyone rated it highly accurate. That's why fortune telling feels accurate. Professional astrologers pick correctly about as often as random guessing. The cost isn't just tens of dollars: in one Chinese case (Xixian County, Henan), a fortune-telling ring collected "incense money" and "offering packages" up to 3,888 yuan (about $550); the ringleader got 11 years in prison. In the US, the FTC and state attorneys general also prosecute scams run under "psychic" or "energy healing" banners.
- 收益：经典课堂实验：学生做完性格测验后拿到的「个人分析」其实人人都一样，却普遍认为很准。作者的结论是「被本人或分析者接受，不能证明解释正确」。39 个学生按 0 到 5 分给这份分析打准确度分，34 人打了 4 分或 5 分，按表里的分布算平均约 4.26 分。这就解释了算命为什么总显得准，这个现象叫巴纳姆效应。占星术也做过双盲检验，结果发表在 Nature 上。占星师要从三份性格测验结果里挑出和星盘对得上的那一份。116 份里挑中 40 份，纯靠瞎猜也能挑中 38.5 份，占星师事先自己预计能挑中 58.5 份。官方案例：河南息县检察院通报过一个网络算命诈骗团伙（中国案例），先用免费看手相把人引进来，再收「香火钱」和最高 3888 元的「供养套餐」。被害人 50 余人，追回赃款 200 余万元，主犯判有期徒刑十一年，并处罚金 10 万元
  Benefit: The classic classroom experiment: students received "personal analyses" that were all identical, yet rated them highly accurate. The author's conclusion: "acceptance by the person or the analyst does not prove the interpretation correct." Of 39 students rating the analysis 0–5, 34 gave it 4 or 5 — averaging about 4.26 from the table's distribution. That explains why fortune telling always feels accurate; the phenomenon is called the Barnum effect. Astrology was also tested double-blind, published in Nature: astrologers had to pick which of three personality-test results matched a natal chart. They got 40 of 116 right, versus 38.5 expected by pure chance — while the astrologers themselves had predicted 58.5. Official case: prosecutors in Xixian County, Henan (a Chinese case) reported an online fortune-telling fraud ring that lured people in with free palm readings, then charged "incense money" and "offering packages" up to 3,888 yuan. Over 50 victims, over 2 million yuan recovered; the ringleader got 11 years in prison plus a 100,000-yuan fine.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Forer BR (1949). The fallacy of personal validation: A classroom demonstration of gullibility. Journal of Abnormal and Social Psychology 44(1):118-123（表 1 评分分布：2 分 1 人、3 分 4 人、4 分 18 人、5 分 16 人，N=39）. <https://doi.org/10.1037/h0059240>；Carlson S (1985). A double-blind test of astrology. Nature 318:419-425（表 2：首选 116 份，挑中 40 份，随机期望 38.5，占星师预计 58.5；「The data are consistent with chance, inconsistent with astrological hypothesis」）. <https://doi.org/10.1038/318419a0>，第二作者主页全文 <https://muller.lbl.gov/papers/Astrology-Carlson.pdf>；最高人民检察院 (2026). 河南息县：历经三年持续追踪打掉一批网络算命诈骗团伙（中国案例）. <https://www.spp.gov.cn/zdgz/202607/t20260728_732935.shtml>
- 备注：这两篇都只是单独的一项研究。Forer 那篇原文没有直接写平均分，4.26 是按原文表 1 的分布算出来的。这里只说「它预测不准」，不评价任何信仰。当娱乐花几十美元不算在内，拿它来定辞职、结婚、投资才是亏钱的地方。
  Note: Both are single studies. Forer's paper doesn't state the average directly; 4.26 is computed from Table 1's distribution. This item only says "its predictions are inaccurate" — no judgment on any belief. Spending tens of dollars on it as entertainment isn't covered; the money-loser is letting it decide your resignation, marriage, or investments.
