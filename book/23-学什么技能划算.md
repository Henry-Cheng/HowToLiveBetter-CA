# 23. 学什么技能划算 / Which Skills Are Worth Learning

这一节算的是钱和时间，第 2 条另外算死亡率。前六条回答一个问题：还要不要接着读书。第 1 条先说法律划掉了哪一段选项，第 2 条算读书和寿命的关系，第 3 条看美国人的学历都在哪一档。第 4 条讲读不起的时候有哪些资助，第 5 条讲没进四年制大学还有什么路，第 6 条把「读书还是打工」算成一道题，给一个自己动手算的方法。后七条回答另一个问题：决定学点东西之后怎么挑。美国按职业分的工资统计，劳工统计局的《职业展望手册》公开可查，所以这里直接引用：多读一年书工资大概多多少（也叫教育回报率）、执照和认证怎么分、培训补贴从哪里来、挑技能时看哪个维度更不容易被顶掉。第 14 到 19 条回答第三个问题：定下来学什么之后，怎么学才省时间。这六条算的是时间，不和钱做换算。第 20 到 23 条回答第四个问题：已经在工作了，执照和认证怎么分、去哪考、别踩什么坑、考下来值多少。
This section does the math on money and time; Item 2 separately does the math on mortality. The first six items answer one question: should you keep going to school? Item 1 starts with which options the law takes off the table, Item 2 does the math on schooling and lifespan, Item 3 looks at where Americans' education levels actually sit. Item 4 covers what aid exists when money is tight, Item 5 covers what roads remain if you didn't get into a four-year university, and Item 6 turns "work or school" into a math problem with a do-it-yourself method. The next seven answer a second question: once you've decided to learn something, how do you pick? U.S. wage statistics by occupation are public in the Bureau of Labor Statistics' Occupational Outlook Handbook, so they're cited directly here: roughly how much more a year of schooling pays (the return to education), how licenses differ from certifications, where training subsidies come from, and which dimension makes a skill harder to displace. Items 14–19 answer a third question: once you've settled on what to learn, how do you learn it in less time? Those six do the math on time, with no conversion into money. Items 20–23 answer a fourth question: if you're already working, how do licenses and certifications differ, where do you get them, what pitfalls to avoid, and what they're actually worth.

本节条目按主题分成下面几块，括号里是条号。
The items in this section are grouped by theme below, with item numbers in parentheses.

**还要不要接着读书**：按年龄划线（第 1 条），读书和死亡率（第 2 条），美国的学历结构（第 3 条），读不起先按政策算（第 4 条），高中之后的路（第 5 条），读书还是打工怎么算（第 6 条）。
**Whether to keep studying**: age cutoffs (Item 1), schooling and mortality (Item 2), America's education profile (Item 3), run the policy math before giving up for money (Item 4), roads after high school (Item 5), how to do the work-vs.-school math (Item 6).

**学什么怎么挑**：多受一年教育的回报（第 7 条），分清执照和认证（第 8 条），培训走政府免费渠道（第 9 条），自动化最难顶掉哪类技能（第 10 条），注册学徒制（第 11 条），查 BLS 和 EDD 的本地需求（第 12 条），选能直接上岗的短周期项目（第 13 条）。
**How to pick what to learn**: the return to a year of education (Item 7), telling licenses apart from certifications (Item 8), free government training channels (Item 9), which skills automation displaces last (Item 10), registered apprenticeship (Item 11), checking local demand via BLS and EDD (Item 12), short programs that lead straight to a job (Item 13).

**怎么学省时间**：合上书自己考（第 14 条），摊到几天（第 15 条），别把划重点当主要学法（第 16 条），混着练（第 17 条），别按视觉型听觉型挑学法（第 18 条），出题考自己（第 19 条）。
**How to learn in less time**: quiz yourself with the book closed (Item 14), spread it across days (Item 15), don't make highlighting your main method (Item 16), mix it up (Item 17), don't pick methods by visual/auditory type (Item 18), quiz yourself by writing questions (Item 19).

**执照和认证**：执照还是认证（第 20 条），靠考试拿证看对应机构（第 21 条），别买假证代考（第 22 条），不等于涨工资（第 23 条）。
**Licenses and certifications**: license or certification (Item 20), exam-based credentials and their bodies (Item 21), no fake credentials or stand-ins (Item 22), not equal to a raise (Item 23).

### 1. 不满 16 周岁没有「去打工」这个选项：联邦法按年龄划线，14 到 15 岁只能做轻活、限工时 / Under 16, "going to work" isn't really an option: federal law draws lines by age—at 14–15 only light work with limited hours
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。花一分钟算一下自己多大。
  Cost: Free. Spend one minute figuring out how old you are.
- 说人话：联邦《公平劳动标准法》按年龄划线：不满 14 岁基本不能去企业打工；14 到 15 岁只能做非危险的轻活，上学期间每天不超 3 小时、每周不超 18 小时，晚上 7 点之后不能干（暑假到 9 点）；16 到 17 岁工时不限，但 18 岁以下一律不得从事 17 类危险职业。加州还要求 18 岁以下打工先办工作许可，先去学校要这张表。
  In plain terms: The federal Fair Labor Standards Act draws lines by age: under 14, you basically can't work for a business; at 14–15, only non-hazardous light work, no more than 3 hours on a school day and 18 hours in a school week, and no work after 7 p.m. (9 p.m. in summer); at 16–17, hours are unlimited but no one under 18 may do any of 17 hazardous occupations. California additionally requires workers under 18 to get a work permit first—ask your school for the form.
- 收益：《公平劳动标准法》的童工条款规定：14 岁以下不得受雇从事非农业工作（送报、演艺等少数例外除外）；14 到 15 岁只能在规定时段内做非制造业、非危险性的工作，上学日每天不超 3 小时、每周不超 18 小时，非上学日每天不超 8 小时、每周不超 40 小时，早上 7 点前、晚上 7 点后（6 月 1 日到劳动节期间为晚上 9 点后）不得工作；16 到 17 岁工时不限，但 18 岁以下不得从事劳工部认定的 17 类危险职业，包括开叉车、操作动力机械、屋顶作业、挖掘等。加州要求 18 岁以下未成年人就业持有工作许可（work permit），由学校发放
  Benefit: The FLSA's child-labor provisions provide: under 14, no non-agricultural employment (with narrow exceptions like newspaper delivery and entertainment); at 14–15, only non-manufacturing, non-hazardous work within set hours—no more than 3 hours on a school day, 18 hours in a school week, 8 hours on a non-school day, 40 hours in a non-school week, and no work before 7 a.m. or after 7 p.m. (after 9 p.m. from June 1 through Labor Day); at 16–17, no hour limits, but no one under 18 may work in 17 hazardous occupations designated by the Department of Labor, including driving forklifts, operating power-driven machinery, roofing, and excavation. California requires workers under 18 to hold a work permit issued by their school.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：U.S. Department of Labor, Fact Sheet #43: Child Labor Provisions of the Fair Labor Standards Act (FLSA)；加州工作许可要求见学区规定（具体条文待核实）
- 备注：肯要一个十几岁、没学历的孩子的岗位，正好是本节第 10 条（挑抗自动化的技能）讲的那一类：流程固定，最容易被机器顶掉。出了工伤要赔，前提是有合法的雇佣关系和证据，黑工这两样都没有 / Note: The jobs willing to hire a teenager with no credentials are exactly the kind Item 10 (pick automation-resistant skills) warns about: fixed routines, easiest for machines to take over. And if you're hurt at work, compensation requires a lawful employment relationship plus evidence—off-the-books work has neither.

### 2. 把「读书有没有用」也放进死亡率账里：每多受一年教育，成人死亡风险降约 1.9% / Put "does school pay off" into the mortality ledger too: each extra year of education lowers adult mortality risk by about 1.9%
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。花的是多读那几年的时间和精力，学费那部分见本节第 4 条（助学金和学费减免）。难在要一直读下去，中间不放弃。
  Cost: Free. What it costs is the time and energy of those extra years; the tuition part is in Item 4 (grants and fee waivers). The hard part is staying the course without quitting midway.
- 说人话：每多读一年书，成年后的死亡风险平均低约 1.9%。18 到 49 岁的人最明显，每多一年低约 2.9%，70 岁以上只低 0.8%。能一直读下去的人往往本来条件就好，这里面有多少是读书本身的作用，分不清。
  In plain terms: Each extra year of schooling means about 1.9% lower adult mortality risk on average. It's clearest at ages 18–49—about 2.9% lower per year—and only 0.8% lower past 70. People who stay in school tend to start out better off, so how much of this is schooling itself can't be separated out.
- 收益：把此前的研究找齐再合在一起算（系统综述与荟萃分析）。检索范围是 1980 年 1 月到 2023 年 5 月，最终纳入 603 项研究、59 个国家 70 个地点、10355 个观测。教育和成人死亡率（各种原因加起来的死亡）是剂量反应关系（读得越多降得越多）。每多受一年教育，死亡风险平均下降 1.9%（95% 不确定区间 1.8–2.0，真实值大致落在这一段里）。18 至 49 岁的人每多一年下降 2.9%（2.8–3.0）。70 岁以上的人下降 0.8%（0.6–1.0）。男女之间没看出差别。按国家发展水平（社会人口指数）分开看，也没看出差别
  Benefit: A systematic review and meta-analysis gathering prior research. Search window: January 1980 to May 2023, ending with 603 studies, 70 locations in 59 countries, and 10,355 observations. Education and adult all-cause mortality show a dose–response relationship (more schooling, lower mortality). Each additional year of education lowers mortality risk by 1.9% on average (95% uncertainty interval 1.8–2.0, the true value roughly in this range). Ages 18–49: 2.9% lower per year (2.8–3.0). Over 70: 0.8% lower (0.6–1.0). No difference seen between men and women, nor across country development levels (socio-demographic index).
- 证据等级：A（争议）/ Evidence grade: A (disputed)
- 来源：IHME-CHAIN Collaborators (2024). Effects of education on adult mortality: a global systematic review and meta-analysis. The Lancet Public Health, 9(3), e155-e165. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2468-2667(23)00306-7>
- 备注：争议。作者自己报告了发表偏倚（p<0.0001），意思是结果好看的研究更容易被发出来。各项研究之间的结果也差得很开。纳入的都是跟踪观察，不是把人分组做的试验。读书年数和家庭条件、地区、职业搅在一起，这 1.9% 里有多少是读书本身带来的、有多少是「能一直读下去的人本来条件就好」，这类研究分不开。反过来的路也分不开：小时候就有病、有心理问题的人，更容易辍学，也更容易早死，这会让读书的作用看起来比实际大。所以它能有力说明「多读书和活得久是同向的」，但不等于「你去读书就能延寿 1.9%」。收益量级定为小：读者面对的通常是再多读三四年，按每年 1.9% 算加起来不到一成，按全书的界线只算小。受益人是自己 / Note: Disputed. The authors themselves reported publication bias (p<0.0001), meaning flattering results get published more easily. Results also varied widely across studies. All included studies were observational follow-ups, not randomized trials. Years of schooling are tangled with family background, region, and occupation—these studies can't separate how much of the 1.9% is schooling itself versus "people who stay in school started out better off." Reverse causation can't be separated either: children who were already sick or struggling psychologically drop out more and die earlier, making schooling look more protective than it is. So it strongly shows "more schooling and longer life move together," but not "going to school extends your life by 1.9%." Benefit rated small: readers usually face three or four more years, which at 1.9% a year adds up to under ten percent—small by this book's cutoffs. The beneficiary is yourself.

### 3. 判断「学历是不是贬值了」之前，先看美国的学历结构：25 岁以上成年人约四成有本科学历 / Before deciding whether "degrees are devalued," look at America's education profile: about four in ten adults 25 and older hold a bachelor's degree
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。看一眼下面的数字就行。
  Cost: Free. Just glance at the numbers below.
- 说人话：人口普查局 2024 年的数据：25 到 39 岁的人里 42.8% 有本科及以上学历，40 到 54 岁里 41.5%，55 岁以上里 34.2%。25 岁以上女性 40.1%，男性 37.1%。本科在年轻人里接近一半，在全年龄段也不是「人人都有」。
  In plain terms: Census Bureau 2024 data: 42.8% of people 25–39 hold a bachelor's degree or higher, 41.5% of those 40–54, and 34.2% of those 55 and older. Among everyone 25 and older, 40.1% of women and 37.1% of men. A bachelor's is near half among the young—and far from universal across all ages.
- 收益：美国人口普查局 2025 年 9 月发布的 2024 年教育程度数据：25 至 39 岁人口中 42.8% 拥有学士及以上学位，40 至 54 岁为 41.5%，55 岁及以上为 34.2%。25 岁及以上人口中，女性 40.1%、男性 37.1% 拥有学士及以上学位
  Benefit: The Census Bureau's 2024 educational-attainment data, released September 2025: 42.8% of people 25–39 hold a bachelor's degree or higher, 41.5% of those 40–54, and 34.2% of those 55+. Among everyone 25 and older, 40.1% of women and 37.1% of men hold a bachelor's or higher.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：U.S. Census Bureau (2025). Educational Attainment in the United States: 2024（2025 年 9 月发布）
- 备注：这些数只说明学历在人群里稀不稀缺。某个具体的人读书划不划算，要按本节第 6 条（读书还是打工怎么算）的方法自己算。年轻人里有本科学历的比例比全年龄段高，拿全年龄段的数跟同龄人比会低估竞争。受益人是自己 / Note: These numbers only show how scarce a degree is in the population. Whether school pays off for a specific person needs Item 6's (work vs. school math) method. Degree-holding is more common among the young than across all ages, so comparing yourself to peers using all-age numbers understates the competition. The beneficiary is yourself.

### 4. 「读不起」先按政策算一遍：FAFSA、Pell Grant、Cal Grant，加州社区大学每学分 46 美元的注册费符合条件可全免 / "Can't afford it"—run the policy math first: FAFSA, Pell Grants, Cal Grants, and California community-college enrollment fees of $46 per unit waived for those who qualify
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。开学前去学校的资助办公室问一次，交一次材料。
  Cost: Free. Visit the school's financial-aid office once before the term starts and file the paperwork once.
- 说人话：先填 FAFSA（联邦助学金申请表），这是大多数资助的入口。Pell Grant 是给低收入本科生的联邦助学金，2025–26 学年最高 7395 美元，不用还。加州居民还有 Cal Grant，按学校类型给钱。加州社区大学每学分 46 美元的注册费，符合条件的低收入居民可以全免。钱不够可以推迟、可以换个办法，很少到必须现在退学去打工的地步。
  In plain terms: Start by filing the FAFSA (the federal student-aid form)—it's the gateway to most aid. The Pell Grant is a federal grant for low-income undergraduates, up to $7,395 for 2025–26, and it doesn't have to be repaid. California residents also have Cal Grants, with amounts varying by school type. California community colleges charge $46 per unit in enrollment fees, fully waived for qualifying low-income residents. Short on money, you can defer or find another way—it's rarely truly "drop out and work now or else."
- 收益：FAFSA（studentaid.gov）是联邦助学金的统一申请入口，决定 Pell Grant、联邦助学贷款和多数州资助的资格。Pell Grant 2025–26 学年最高 7395 美元，不用偿还。Cal Grant 是加州的助学金，分 A、B、C 三类：2025–26 学年，Cal Grant A 在加州大学最高 14934 美元、州立大学 6450 美元、社区大学学士项目 3024 美元；Cal Grant B 另有每年 1648 美元的生活补助；Cal Grant C 面向职业技校学生。加州社区大学的注册费为每学分 46 美元，加州低收入居民可通过 California College Promise Grant（CCPG）申请全免。助学贷款是贷款不是补助，毕业后要还，但它把「现在拿不出学费」和「这几年划不划算」拆成了两件事
  Benefit: The FAFSA (studentaid.gov) is the single application for federal student aid, determining eligibility for Pell Grants, federal student loans, and most state aid. The Pell Grant maxes out at $7,395 for 2025–26 and never has to be repaid. Cal Grants are California's aid program in three types, A, B, and C: for 2025–26, Cal Grant A covers up to $14,934 at UC, $6,450 at CSU, and $3,024 for community-college bachelor's programs; Cal Grant B adds a $1,648 yearly living allowance; Cal Grant C is for vocational and technical students. California community colleges charge $46 per unit in enrollment fees, fully waived for qualifying low-income California residents through the California College Promise Grant (CCPG). Student loans are loans, not grants—you repay them after graduation—but they separate "can't pay tuition now" from "are these years of school worth it."
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：U.S. Department of Education, Federal Pell Grant program（2025–26 学年最高 $7,395）；California Student Aid Commission, 2025–26 Cal Grant award amounts. <https://csac.ca.gov/post/what-are-cal-grant-award-amounts>；California College Promise Grant（社区大学 $46/学分注册费减免）
- 备注：受益人是自己和子女。Cal Grant 的具体金额每年按加州学生援助委员会的表走，申请要赶在每年 4 月 2 日的截止日期前交 FAFSA。先别把钱不够当成结论 / Note: The beneficiary is yourself and your children. Cal Grant amounts follow the California Student Aid Commission's yearly tables, and you must file the FAFSA by the April 2 deadline each year. Don't treat "not enough money" as the final answer yet.

### 5. 高中之后路没断：社区大学免试入学、GED、技校 / The road doesn't end after high school: community colleges admit without entrance exams, plus the GED and trade schools
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。填志愿之前，查一次本州的社区大学和技校目录。
  Cost: Free. Before applying, check your state's community-college and trade-school listings once.
- 说人话：加州社区大学是开放入学，不看一考定音的入学考试，高中毕业（或同等学历）就能进，读完两年可以转加州大学和州立大学。没拿到高中文凭的，可以考 GED（普通教育发展证书），效力等同于高中文凭。想直接学手艺的，还有技校（trade school）和成人学校。没进四年制大学，是换一条路，不是停在高中学历。
  In plain terms: California community colleges have open admissions—no single make-or-break entrance exam; a high-school diploma (or equivalent) gets you in, and after two years you can transfer to UC or CSU. Without a diploma, you can earn a GED (General Educational Development certificate), treated as the equivalent of a high-school diploma. If you want to learn a trade directly, there are trade schools and adult schools. Not getting into a four-year university means switching roads, not stopping at a high-school education.
- 收益：加州社区大学系统实行开放入学政策，招收高中毕业生和持有同等学历者，不设统一入学考试；完成转学课程后可转入加州大学（UC）或加州州立大学（CSU）接着读本科。GED 考过即获得等同于高中文凭的证书，美国高校和雇主普遍认可。技校和成人学校提供电工、焊接、汽修、护理助理等职业课程，学制短、直接对接岗位
  Benefit: California's community-college system has an open-admissions policy, enrolling high-school graduates and equivalency holders with no unified entrance exam; after completing transfer coursework, students can move on to the University of California (UC) or California State University (CSU) for a bachelor's. Passing the GED certifies the equivalent of a high-school diploma, widely recognized by U.S. colleges and employers. Trade schools and adult schools offer short occupational programs—electrician, welding, auto repair, nursing assistant—that lead straight to jobs.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验；各校招生政策以学校官网为准
- 备注：受益人是自己和子女。挑专业时，用本节第 10 条（挑抗自动化的技能）的维度过一遍：要到现场、环境不标准的专业更难被顶掉。报技校之前，按第 8 条（分清执照和认证）验一遍它发的证。招生宣传里承诺「包分配」「保过」的一律不要 / Note: The beneficiary is yourself and your children. When picking a program, run it through Item 10's (pick automation-resistant skills) lens: hands-on, non-standard-environment programs are harder to displace. Before enrolling in a trade school, verify its credential per Item 8 (tell licenses apart from certifications). Ignore any pitch promising "guaranteed placement" or "guaranteed pass."

### 6. 把「读书还是打工」算成一道题：早挣的几年工资，对上往后几十年的周薪差 / Turn "work or school" into a math problem: a few years of early wages versus decades of weekly-pay gaps
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。坐下来算一次，大概半小时。
  Cost: Free. Sit down and do it once—about half an hour.

### 8. 花钱考证之前，先分清执照（license）和认证（certification）：执照看州政府，认证看发证机构 / Before paying for a credential, tell licenses apart from certifications: licenses come from state governments, certifications from the issuing body
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。查一次，十几分钟。
  Cost: Free. Look it up once—about ten minutes.
- 说人话：执照是政府发的上岗许可，电工、护士、承包商、理发师都要有，无照干活违法，查的是州政府的执照委员会。认证是行业机构发的水平证明，比如 CompTIA、AWS，含金量看雇主认不认。报名前按三步查：这行上岗要不要州执照，发证机构是不是正经机构，证书编号能不能在发证机构官网查到。查不到的，报名费就是白花。

### 10. 挑技能时优先看「要不要动手、要不要现场判断」，这类最难被自动化顶掉 / When picking a skill, ask first "does it need hands and on-site judgment"—those are hardest for automation to displace

### 13. 同样的钱和时间，优先选能直接上岗的短周期项目：比如商用驾照（CDL）/ With the same money and time, prefer short programs that lead straight to a job—like the commercial driver's license (CDL)
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：要交短期课程的学费，钱不多。时间上要占一段。难在学到一半还没见效时，按说好的条件停下来。
  Cost: Short-course tuition, not much money. It takes a stretch of time. The hard part is stopping on your preset terms when you're halfway through with nothing to show yet.
- 说人话：挑项目只看一条：学完有没有一个具体岗位能投简历。重型卡车司机就是例子：2024 年中位年薪 57440 美元，门槛是考下 CDL，不用四年大学。社区大学的短期证书（比如几个月的护理助理、IT 支持）也是这个路子。开工前就写好退出条件：学到什么程度还没找到活就停。
  In plain terms: Judge a program by one thing: is there a specific job to apply for when it ends? Heavy-truck driving is the example: median $57,440 a year in 2024, and the bar is passing the CDL—no four-year degree needed. Community-college short certificates (a few months for nursing assistant or IT support) follow the same logic. Before starting, write down your exit condition: at what point with no job offer do you stop.
- 收益：OOH 显示重型卡车司机 2024 年中位年薪 57440 美元，入行要求是商用驾照，各州另有年龄和体检要求。CDL 培训多为几周到几个月的全日制课程，周期比学位短得多（具体时长各校不同，待核实）。社区大学和成人学校的短期职业证书同理：学制以月计，结业对接明确岗位
  Benefit: The OOH shows heavy-truck drivers at a $57,440 median in 2024, with the commercial driver's license as the entry requirement plus state age and medical rules. CDL training is typically a full-time course of weeks to a few months—far shorter than a degree (exact length varies by school, to be verified). Community-college and adult-school short occupational certificates work the same way: months-long programs ending at a defined job.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook: Heavy and Tractor-Trailer Truck Drivers（2024 年中位年薪 $57,440，需 CDL，2026-10-05 核实）
- 备注：判断标准就一条：学完之后有没有一个具体的岗位能投简历。学一年半载、结业后还要「再积累经验」的项目，实际成本远高于标价。退出条件同样适用：开工前就写清楚「学到什么程度还没找到活就停」/ Note: The test is one thing: is there a specific job to apply for afterward? Programs that take a year and a half and still demand "more experience" after graduation cost far more than their sticker price. The exit-condition rule applies too: before starting, write down "at what point with no job do I stop."

### 14. 学完先合上书自己考一遍，别回头重读 / After studying, close the book and quiz yourself—don't just reread
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱，总时间也不用增加。把原来用来重读的那段时间，改成合上书默写或者做题。难在自测当场就把没记住的暴露出来，感觉比重读难受。
  Cost: Free, and no extra total time. Turn the time you'd spend rereading into writing from memory or doing problems with the book closed. The hard part is that quizzing exposes what you didn't retain on the spot—it feels worse than rereading.
- 说人话：读完再读一遍感觉很熟，其实记不牢。合上书考自己一遍，效果明显更好，课堂上加了小测验，成绩提高属于中等幅度。给十种常用学法排名时，自测排在最高一档。读完一节先默写要点，再翻回去对。
  In plain terms: Reading something twice feels familiar, but it doesn't stick. Closing the book and quizzing yourself works markedly better—in-class quizzes raise grades by a moderate amount. When ten common study methods were ranked, self-testing landed in the top tier. After each section, write out the key points from memory, then check.
- 收益：222 项独立研究、48478 名学生的荟萃分析：课堂测验让学生的学业成绩提高，效应量属于中等（g = 0.499）。效果大小受几个条件影响：对照组用的是什么学法、测验形式和最终考试是否一致、材料是否匹配、有没有给纠正性反馈、测验重复了几次、在哪里什么时候测、干预持续多久、用的是什么实验设计。另一份逐一评估十种常用学习方法的综述，把「练习性测验」和「分散练习」评为最高一档（high utility），理由是它们对不同年龄、不同能力的学习者都有效，在多种考核形式和真实教育场景里都被验证过
  Benefit: A meta-analysis of 222 independent studies with 48,478 students: classroom quizzes improved academic performance, with a moderate effect size (g = 0.499). The size of the effect depended on several conditions: what the control group did instead, whether quiz and final-exam formats matched, whether materials matched, whether corrective feedback was given, how many times quizzing was repeated, where and when testing happened, how long the intervention lasted, and the experimental design. A separate review rating ten common learning techniques placed "practice testing" and "distributed practice" in the highest tier (high utility), because they work for learners of different ages and abilities and have been validated across many assessment formats and real educational settings.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Yang C, Luo L, Vadillo MA, Yu R, Shanks DR (2021). Testing (quizzing) boosts classroom learning: A systematic and meta-analytic review. Psychological Bulletin, 147(4), 399–435. <https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000309>；Dunlosky J, Rawson KA, Marsh EJ, Nathan MJ, Willingham DT (2013). Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: Promising Directions From Cognitive and Educational Psychology. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266>
- 备注：自测是为了逼着把东西从脑子里调出来，不为打分。做法：读完一节合上书先默写要点，再翻回去对。有纠正性反馈的效果更好，所以对完答案要看清错在哪。收益量级记「大」，是按效应量和评级判的。这两份证据量的是成绩，没有量小时数，所以没按节省时间的阈值套。自测和第 15 条（摊到几天）是同一份综述里评级最高的两样，配合用 / Note: Self-testing is about forcing material out of your head, not about scoring. The method: after each section, close the book, write the key points from memory, then check. Corrective feedback makes it work better, so study where you went wrong. The benefit is rated "large" based on the effect size and ratings. These two evidence sources measured grades, not hours saved, so the time-savings thresholds weren't applied. Self-testing and Item 15 (spread across days) are the two highest-rated techniques in the same review—use them together.

### 15. 把同样的时间摊到几天，别一次学完 / Spread the same study time across days—don't cram it all at once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱，总时间也不变。变的只是排法：同样 3 小时，拆成三天各 1 小时。难在要提前几天开始，不能拖到最后一晚。
  Cost: Free, and total time is unchanged. Only the schedule changes: the same 3 hours split into 1 hour a day over three days. The hard part is starting days ahead instead of leaving it to the last night.
- 说人话：同样的时间，分几天学比一次学完记得牢。317 个实验都在测这件事，结论一致。还有一条实用规律：你要记到多久以后，复习间隔就该拉多长。明天考就当天复习，一个月后考就隔几天过一遍。
  In plain terms: The same study time, split across days, sticks better than doing it all at once. 317 experiments tested exactly this, and they agree. There's also a practical rule: the longer you need to remember something, the longer the gap between reviews should be. Exam tomorrow—review today; exam in a month—revisit every few days.
- 收益：一份汇总了 184 篇文章、317 个实验、839 次分散练习效果评估的荟萃分析。它比较了两件事：连着学完和分开几次学（间隔效应），以及间隔短和间隔长（延迟效应）。结论是复习间隔和「要记到多久之后」共同决定最终测验的保持率：要保持的时间越长，能带来最佳保持率的复习间隔也越长。另一份逐一评估十种常用学习方法的综述，把分散练习评为最高一档（high utility）
  Benefit: A meta-analysis pooling 184 articles, 317 experiments, and 839 distributed-practice evaluations. It compared two things: learning in one sitting vs. spaced sessions (the spacing effect), and short vs. long gaps (the lag effect). The conclusion: the review gap and "how long you need to remember" jointly determine retention on the final test—the longer the retention needed, the longer the gap that gives the best retention. A separate review of ten common techniques rated distributed practice in the highest tier (high utility).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cepeda NJ, Pashler H, Vul E, Wixted JT, Rohrer D (2006). Distributed practice in verbal recall tasks: A review and quantitative synthesis. Psychological Bulletin, 132(3), 354–380. <https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.3.354>；Dunlosky J, Rawson KA, Marsh EJ, Nathan MJ, Willingham DT (2013). Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266>
- 备注：和第 14 条（合上书自测）叠加用最好：把自测本身分散到几天做。Cepeda 那份荟萃分析里的材料多是词表记忆这类，复杂内容的最佳间隔没有给出通用天数。「考得越远、间隔越长」是原文给的规律，没有具体日程。考前一晚突击当场有用，问题是忘得快 / Note: Best combined with Item 14 (quiz yourself with the book closed): spread the self-quizzing itself across days. Most materials in Cepeda's meta-analysis were word-list memorization; no universal day counts were given for complex material. "The farther the test, the longer the gap" is the paper's rule—no specific schedule. Cramming the night before works for the moment; the problem is how fast it fades.

### 16. 别把划重点、反复读、写摘要当成主要学法 / Don't make highlighting, rereading, or summarizing your main study method
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱，反而省时间。难在这几样做起来最顺手，也最有「我在学习」的感觉。
  Cost: Free, and it even saves time. The hard part is that these feel the most natural and give the strongest "I'm studying" feeling.
- 说人话：学生最常用的是划重点和反复读，可在十种常用学法的排名里，这两样都在最低一档。写摘要、关键词记忆法、给课文配图想象也在最低一档。它们不是完全没用，只是起作用的条件太窄，同样的时间换成自测更划算。
  In plain terms: Highlighting and rereading are what students use most, but in a ranking of ten common study techniques, both sit in the lowest tier. Summarizing, keyword mnemonics, and mental imagery for texts are down there too. They're not completely useless—their effective conditions are just narrow, and the same time spent self-testing buys more.
- 收益：一份评估十种常用学习方法的综述，按「效果能不能跨学习条件、跨学生特征、跨材料、跨考核形式推广」给评级。评为最高一档的是练习性测验和分散练习。评为中等一档的是精细提问、自我解释、交错练习。评为最低一档的五种是：写摘要、划重点（或者画线）、关键词记忆法、为课文配图想象、反复阅读。综述写明：多数学生都说自己在用反复阅读和划重点，但这两种并不能稳定提高成绩，应当换成别的方法，比如用练习性测验代替反复阅读
  Benefit: A review rating ten common learning techniques by "does the effect generalize across learning conditions, student traits, materials, and test formats." Highest tier: practice testing and distributed practice. Middle tier: elaborative interrogation, self-explanation, interleaved practice. Lowest tier, five techniques: summarization, highlighting/underlining, keyword mnemonics, imagery for text, and rereading. The review states: most students report using rereading and highlighting, but neither reliably improves performance—switch to something else, e.g., replace rereading with practice testing.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Dunlosky J, Rawson KA, Marsh EJ, Nathan MJ, Willingham DT (2013). Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques: Promising Directions From Cognitive and Educational Psychology. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 14(1), 4–58. <https://doi.org/10.1177/1529100612453266>
- 备注：划线可以用来「标出一会儿要自测哪几处」，出问题的是把划线本身当成学习。综述也说明，最低一档不等于零，只是能起作用的条件窄、还需要更多研究。替代动作见第 14 条（合上书自测）和第 15 条（摊到几天）/ Note: Highlighting is fine for "marking what to quiz yourself on later"—the problem is treating highlighting itself as studying. The review also notes the lowest tier doesn't mean zero, just narrow conditions and a need for more research. For replacements, see Item 14 (quiz yourself with the book closed) and Item 15 (spread across days).

### 17. 几种题型混着练，别一种连做二十道 / Mix problem types in practice—don't drill twenty of one kind in a row
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。把练习册按题型顺序做改成打乱着做。难在混着练当场正确率更低，感觉像退步。
  Cost: Free. Change the workbook from in-type-order to shuffled. The hard part is that mixed practice scores lower on the spot—it feels like going backward.
- 说人话：同一类题连做一大串，当场做得很顺，过后不一定分得清该用哪个方法。把几类题打乱着练，长期效果更好，属于中等幅度。但这招挑材料：练图像、练分类最有效，数学题小一些，背单词反而不如分块练。
  In plain terms: Drilling one problem type in a long streak feels smooth in the moment, but later you may not tell which method each problem needs. Shuffling several types together works better long-term, by a moderate amount. But this trick is picky about material: best for images and categorization, smaller for math problems, and for vocabulary, blocked practice actually wins.
- 收益：一份纳入 59 项研究、238 个效应量、158 个样本的多层荟萃分析。交错练习相对于分块练习的总体效应属于中等（Hedges' g = 0.42）。按材料分：绘画类最好（g = 0.67），其他视觉材料也较好；数学任务的效应较小（g = 0.34）；说明性文本和味觉材料的总体效应不显著；词汇类反而是分块练习更好（g = −0.39）。回归分析显示：类别之间越相似、同一类别内部越不相似、材料越复杂，交错的好处越大
  Benefit: A multilevel meta-analysis of 59 studies, 238 effect sizes, 158 samples. Interleaved vs. blocked practice: overall moderate effect (Hedges' g = 0.42). By material: drawing tasks best (g = 0.67), other visual materials also good; math tasks smaller (g = 0.34); expository text and taste materials not significant overall; vocabulary favored blocked practice (g = −0.39). Regression showed interleaving helps more when categories are similar to each other, items within a category are dissimilar, and material is complex.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Brunmair M, Richter T (2019). Similarity matters: A meta-analysis of interleaved learning and its moderators. Psychological Bulletin, 145(11), 1029–1052. <https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000209>
- 备注：第 16 条（别把划重点当主要学法）引的那份综述把交错练习评为中等一档，理由是当时研究还不够多。两份合起来看，用法是：需要「认出这是哪一类、该用哪个方法」的内容混着练，比如数学题型、病例、法条适用；需要死记的内容分块练，比如单词、名词解释 / Note: The review cited in Item 16 (don't make highlighting your main method) rated interleaved practice in the middle tier, because there weren't enough studies yet. Taken together, the usage is: interleave material where you must "recognize which category this is and which method applies"—math problem types, medical cases, which statute applies; block-drill material that must be memorized cold—vocabulary, definitions.

### 18. 别按「我是视觉型、他是听觉型」挑学法 / Don't pick study methods by "I'm a visual learner, he's an auditory learner"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。只是不再按这个分类挑材料、挑课程，也不再花钱做风格测评。
  Cost: Free. Just stop picking materials and courses by this classification, and stop paying for style assessments.
- 说人话：很多人相信每个人有自己的学习风格，比如视觉型、听觉型，按风格教才学得好。认真检验这件事的研究很少，几乎没有一项支持它，有几项的结果还正好相反。人确实各有偏好，但没有证据说按偏好上课就学得更好，不用花钱做风格测评。
  In plain terms: Many people believe everyone has a learning style—visual, auditory—and that teaching to it works best. Few studies have tested this rigorously, almost none support it, and a few found the opposite. People do have preferences, but there's no evidence that matching instruction to preference improves learning—don't pay for a style assessment.
- 收益：一份受托评估学习风格证据的综述指出：要证明「按学习风格施教」有效，必须先按风格给学生分组，再把每组学生随机分配到不同教法，最后用同一份测验考。有效的判据是出现一种特定的交互作用：对一种风格最有效的教法，对另一种风格不是最有效的。综述的结论是：几乎没有发现这种交互作用；关于学习风格的文献虽然很多，但用了合格实验设计的研究极少，其中几项的结果还直接和「匹配假说」相反。作者据此认为，现有证据不足以支持把学习风格测评用进教育实践
  Benefit: A commissioned review of learning-styles evidence: to prove "teaching to learning styles" works, you must group students by style, randomly assign each group to different teaching methods, and test everyone with the same exam. The bar is a specific interaction: the method best for one style must not be best for another. The review's conclusion: almost no such interaction was found; while the learning-styles literature is large, few studies used a valid experimental design, and several results directly contradicted the matching hypothesis. The authors concluded the evidence doesn't support putting learning-style assessments into educational practice.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Pashler H, McDaniel M, Rohrer D, Bjork R (2008). Learning Styles: Concepts and Evidence. Psychological Science in the Public Interest, 9(3), 105–119. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x>
- 备注：综述同时说明，这不等于所有版本的学习风格理论都被测过并被否定，很多根本没被测过。要避免的是花钱做风格测评、按风格挑培训班这两件事。挑培训的标准见第 9 条（培训走政府免费渠道）和第 13 条（选能直接上岗的短周期项目）/ Note: The review also notes this doesn't mean every version of learning-styles theory was tested and refuted—many were never tested at all. What to avoid is paying for style assessments and picking courses by style. For picking training, see Item 9 (use free government channels) and Item 13 (prefer short programs that lead straight to a job).

### 19. 要背的内容按将来的用法出题考自己，别原样背一遍就算完 / For material to memorize, quiz yourself the way you'll use it—don't just recite it once
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不多花时间。变的只是练法：把「原样背一遍」换成「按将来的用法出题考自己」。难在得先想清楚这些东西将来要怎么用。
  Cost: Free, and no extra time either. Only the method changes: swap "recite it once as-is" for "quiz yourself the way you'll use it." The hard part is first getting clear on how you'll actually use it later.
- 说人话：合上书考自己，好处不只停在背过的那几个字上。192 项对比、一万多人合起来算，它对换了题型的题、对应用题和推理题也管用，幅度中等偏小。但有两种情况几乎没用：把问法和答案对调，以及只在书上见过、从来没被考过的内容。
  In plain terms: Quizzing yourself with the book closed helps beyond the exact words you memorized. Pooling 192 comparisons with over ten thousand people, it also works on rephrased questions, application problems, and reasoning questions, by a small-to-moderate amount. But it's nearly useless in two cases: swapping the question and answer around, and material you only ever saw in the book and were never quizzed on.
- 收益：一项荟萃分析汇总了 40 多年的研究，含 67 篇已发表和未发表的文章、122 个实验、192 个迁移效应量，总人数 10382 人。和「再看一遍但不考」的对照相比，练习性测验带来的学习确实能迁移到别的情境，效应量 d=0.40（95% CI 0.31 到 0.50）。迁移最强的几种情况是：换一种题型考、考应用题和推理题、医学诊断类的问题、考意思相近的线索词。迁移最弱的几种是：把刺激和反应对调、初学时见过但没被考过的材料、例题类问题。调节因素中，练时和考时的作答形式是否一致、有没有做过带详细反馈的提取练习、以及初次测验答对没有，都强烈影响会不会出现正向迁移。作者还用两种方法校正了发表偏倚，调节因素的效应量受影响很小，但截距估计被明显压低：上面这些条件一个都不具备时，常常看不到正向迁移
  Benefit: A meta-analysis of 40+ years of research: 67 published and unpublished articles, 122 experiments, 192 transfer effect sizes, 10,382 people total. Compared with "restudy without quizzing," practice testing does transfer to new situations, effect size d = 0.40 (95% CI 0.31–0.50). Strongest transfer: different question formats, application and reasoning questions, medical-diagnosis problems, paraphrased cues. Weakest: swapping stimulus and response, material seen but never tested during initial learning, example-type problems. Moderators: whether practice and test answer formats matched, whether retrieval practice with detailed feedback was done, and whether the first quiz was answered correctly all strongly affected transfer. The authors corrected for publication bias two ways: moderator effect sizes barely moved, but the intercept estimate dropped substantially—when none of these conditions hold, positive transfer often doesn't appear.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Pan SC, Rickard TC (2018). Transfer of test-enhanced learning: meta-analytic review and synthesis. Psychological Bulletin, 144(7), 710-756. <https://doi.org/10.1037/bul0000151>
- 备注：「背」这个词平时把两件事混在一起：把材料再过一遍（反复读、划重点），和合上书主动回想。前一类在那份给十种学法排队的综述里评级最低，想看评级本身见第 16 条（别把划重点当主要学法）。要做的是后一类：合上书主动回想，具体见第 14 条（合上书自测），而且要分几天做，见第 15 条（摊到几天）。做法是先想清楚将来怎么用：是要在卷子上选出来，还是要在现场判断出来，然后照那个形式出题考自己。反过来对调着考一遍也值得做，因为这份荟萃分析里对调方向的迁移最弱，两个方向都得练到 / Note: The word "memorizing" mixes two things: going over the material again (rereading, highlighting) and actively recalling with the book closed. The first kind got the lowest rating in the review that ranked ten techniques—see Item 16 (don't make highlighting your main method) for the ranking itself. Do the second kind: active recall with the book closed, per Item 14 (quiz yourself with the book closed), spread across days per Item 15 (spread across days). The method: first get clear on how you'll use it later—pick it out on a test, or judge it on site—then quiz yourself in that format. It's also worth quizzing in the reverse direction, because this meta-analysis found transfer weakest when direction was swapped; both directions need practice.

### 20. 先分清你要的是执照还是认证：执照是州政府发的从业门槛，认证是行业机构发的水平证明 / First figure out whether you need a license or a certification: a license is the state government's bar to practice, a certification is an industry body's proof of skill
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。查一次对应行业的发证机构，半小时左右。
  Cost: Free. Look up the issuing body for your trade once—about half an hour.
- 说人话：美国没有中国的职称评审体系，对应的是两套东西：执照（license），政府发的，不持证上岗违法，比如护士、电工、承包商；认证（certification），行业机构发的，证明你会某项技能，比如 PMP、CompTIA、AWS，值钱的前提是雇主认。先弄清你要的是哪一种，再去找对应的发证机构。
  In plain terms: The U.S. has no equivalent of China's professional-title review system. The counterparts are two things: licenses, issued by the government—working without one is illegal, e.g., nurses, electricians, contractors; and certifications, issued by industry bodies, proving you can do something, e.g., PMP, CompTIA, AWS—valuable only if employers recognize them. Figure out which one you need, then go to the right issuer.
- 收益：执照由州政府按行业管理，申请条件、考试和续期都由州执照委员会定，跨州执业通常要重新申请或走互认。认证由行业协会或企业管理，考过就发证，不绑定政府，效力来自雇主和行业的认可度。两种都不是「评一次管终身」：执照要续期，认证多有有效期，要重考或攒继续教育学分
  Benefit: Licenses are managed by state governments per trade—eligibility, exams, and renewals are set by state licensing boards, and working across states usually means reapplying or going through reciprocity. Certifications are managed by industry associations or companies: pass the exam, get the credential, no government involved, and value comes from employer and industry recognition. Neither is "once and done": licenses need renewal, and most certifications expire, requiring re-examination or continuing-education credits.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：各州执照委员会官网；认证以发证机构（如 CompTIA、AWS、PMI）的当年考试大纲为准
- 备注：先问你要进的那个行业：上岗要不要执照，要的话走州执照委员会；不要的话，再看雇主认哪个认证，别考雇主不认的证 / Note: Ask your target industry first: does the job legally require a license—if so, go through the state licensing board; if not, find out which certification employers actually recognize, and don't pay for one they don't.

### 21. 会计这类靠考试拿的，看州会计委员会；IT 看 CompTIA/AWS；技工看州执照 / For exam-based credentials like accounting, check the state board of accountancy; for IT, CompTIA/AWS; for trades, the state license
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：报名费按各机构标准交。备考要占几个月的业余时间。
  Cost: Registration fees per each body's schedule. Preparation takes months of spare time.
- 说人话：有些领域是考出来的：会计要过各州会计委员会的 CPA 考试，IT 考 CompTIA 或 AWS 的认证考试，电工水管工考州执照。报名前先对照报考条件：学历、工作年限、前置证书。已经拿到的相关证书，有的可以直接抵条件。
  In plain terms: Some fields are exam-based: accountants pass the CPA exam through their state board of accountancy, IT workers take CompTIA or AWS certification exams, electricians and plumbers take state license exams. Before registering, check the eligibility rules: education, work experience, prerequisite credentials. Some credentials you already hold may count toward the requirements.
- 收益：CPA 由各州会计委员会管理，报考通常要求会计相关学历和一定学分，各州具体数字不同，以本州委员会当年公布为准。IT 认证由发证机构定规则：CompTIA 按 A+、Network+、Security+ 分级，AWS 按助理、专业、专项分级，考过即发证。技工执照由州执照委员会管理，多要求学徒时长加考试。三种都是「考过就有」，不另设评审
  Benefit: The CPA is managed by state boards of accountancy, typically requiring accounting-related education and a set number of credit hours—the exact numbers differ by state, so go by your state board's current publication. IT certifications follow the issuer's rules: CompTIA tiers A+, Network+, Security+; AWS tiers associate, professional, specialty—pass and you're certified. Trade licenses are managed by state licensing boards, usually requiring apprenticeship hours plus an exam. All three are "pass and you're in," with no separate review.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：各州会计委员会（如 California Board of Accountancy）；CompTIA、AWS 官网考试大纲；各州技工执照委员会
- 备注：你的领域是考、是评、是执照，先查对应机构的当年要求。考试的报名时间和科目，以当年的考试公告为准 / Note: Whether your field is exam-based, review-based, or license-based, check the relevant body's current requirements first. Registration dates and exam subjects follow that year's official announcement.

### 22. 别买假证、别找代考：查实就是欺诈，执照会被吊销 / Don't buy fake credentials or hire exam stand-ins: if caught it's fraud, and licenses get revoked
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难在忍住「花钱包过」的广告。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is resisting "pay to pass" ads.
- 说人话：花钱买的假证、找人代考，查实就是欺诈。执照会被吊销，工作会丢，还可能吃官司。文凭工厂卖的「学位」，雇主和政府都不认。申报和考试时签的字都有法律效力，造假被发现，几年内别想再考。
  In plain terms: Bought fake credentials or a hired exam stand-in, once caught, is fraud. Licenses get revoked, jobs get lost, and lawsuits can follow. "Degrees" from diploma mills aren't recognized by employers or the government. The signatures on applications and exams carry legal weight—get caught faking, and you won't be testing again for years.
- 收益：各州执照委员会对欺诈、作假取得执照的，标准处理是吊销执照并禁止重新申请。联邦贸易委员会长期打击文凭工厂：其颁发的学位不被认可，用假学历求职可按欺诈处理。考试作弊被抓，发证机构通常禁考数年。情节严重的，检方可以按欺诈罪起诉
  Benefit: State licensing boards standardly respond to fraudulently obtained licenses with revocation and a ban on reapplying. The Federal Trade Commission has long pursued diploma mills: their degrees aren't recognized, and using a fake credential to get hired can be treated as fraud. Caught cheating on an exam, and the certifying body typically bars you for years. Serious cases can be prosecuted as fraud.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：各州执照委员会纪律处分规定；Federal Trade Commission 关于文凭工厂的消费者提示
- 备注：辨真假有个办法：正规执照能在州执照委员会官网查到持证人信息，正规认证能在发证机构官网验到编号。承诺「包过」「不用本人参加」「证书查不到也能用」的，基本可以判定有问题 / Note: There's a way to tell real from fake: legitimate licenses show up in the holder lookup on the state licensing board's website, and legitimate certifications verify by number on the issuer's site. Anyone promising "guaranteed pass," "no need to show up," or "works even if it doesn't verify" is almost certainly a scam.

### 23. 拿到证不等于涨工资：先问清雇主认不认、给不给加钱 / A credential doesn't equal a raise: ask first whether the employer recognizes it and pays more for it
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。问一次雇主或在职的人就行。
  Cost: Free. Just ask an employer or someone doing the job once.
- 说人话：执照是上岗的门槛，不是加薪的保证。很多岗位有证才能干，但工资照样按市场定。认证更直接：雇主不认的证，考了也白考。花钱考证之前，先问在职的人：这张证你们老板认吗，加钱吗。
  In plain terms: A license is the bar to enter the job, not a guarantee of higher pay. Many jobs require the credential to work, but wages still follow the market. Certifications are even more direct: a cert your employer doesn't recognize is a wasted exam. Before paying for one, ask people doing the job: does your boss recognize this credential, and does it pay more?
- 收益：执照解决的是「能不能干」，工资解决的是「值多少钱」，两件事。劳工统计局的工资数据按职业给，不按「有没有证」给，说明同一职业内工资差主要来自经验、地区和雇主。认证的溢价只存在于认它的雇主那里，考之前问清，比考完再问便宜得多
  Benefit: A license answers "may I do this job"; wages answer "what is it worth"—two different questions. Bureau of Labor Statistics wage data is published by occupation, not by "credentialed or not," which tells you that within-occupation pay gaps come mostly from experience, location, and employer. A certification's premium exists only at employers that recognize it—asking before you pay is far cheaper than asking after.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验；U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Outlook Handbook（工资按职业发布）
- 备注：证据等级定 C，是因为没有全国统一的「有证涨多少」的数字。能照着做的是：考证前先问雇主或在职的人，这张证认不认、加不加钱 / Note: Graded C because there is no national number for "how much more a credential pays." What you can do: before paying for an exam, ask employers or people in the job whether they recognize the credential and pay more for it.
