# 31. 十八岁之后有哪几条路·加州版

本章是原书第 31 节的加州版：一张美国版路径地图，不比较哪条路更划算。十八岁在美国意味着：可以投票、签合同、参军（17 岁经父母同意即可），但买酒要到 21 岁。本节只收门槛和待遇能逐条核对的几条路：四年制大学、社区大学转学（加州 TAG 转学保障）、学徒制、技工与职业证书、参军、间隔年与直接工作。每条写清门槛和成本（美元）。选哪条路是你自己的事。

This chapter is the California edition of Section 31 of the original book: a U.S. path map, not a ranking of which path is better. Turning 18 in the United States means you can vote, sign contracts, and enlist (17 with parental consent) — but you can't buy alcohol until 21. This section covers only paths whose thresholds and benefits can be verified item by item: four-year universities, community college transfer (California's TAG guarantee), apprenticeships, trade and professional certificates, military service, and gap years / going straight to work. Each path lists its thresholds and costs (in USD). Which path to take is your call.

### 1. 先把门槛对一遍：各条路的年龄和学历要求 / First, check the thresholds: age and education requirements for each path
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。花十分钟对一遍，看自己够不够得着。
  Cost: Free. Ten minutes to check which ones you qualify for.
- 说人话：这几条路的门槛。投票、签合同：18 岁。买酒：21 岁（联邦），加州娱乐大麻也是 21 岁。Selective Service 登记：男性 18 到 25 岁，满 18 岁 30 天内（见第 9 条）。参军：17 岁（未满 18 要父母同意）到各军种上限（陆军 35，海军、空军 41，太空军 42），公民或绿卡，高中毕业或 GED（见第 6 条）。学徒制：通常 18 岁，高中或 GED（见第 4 条）。技工执照：多为 18 岁+，部分要求先完成学徒或课程（见第 5 条）。打工：联邦 16 岁可做非危险工作，18 岁以下禁做危险工种；加州 18 岁以下打工要工作许可（work permit）。社区大学：开放入学，18 岁或高中毕业（见第 3 条）。加州 16 岁可考驾照。
  In plain terms: The thresholds for these paths. Voting and signing contracts: 18. Buying alcohol: 21 (federal); recreational cannabis in California is also 21. Selective Service registration: men ages 18–25, within 30 days of turning 18 (see item 9). Military enlistment: 17 (parental consent required under 18) up to each branch's cap (Army 35; Navy and Air Force 41; Space Force 42), U.S. citizen or green card holder, high school diploma or GED (see item 6). Apprenticeship: typically 18, high school diploma or GED (see item 4). Trade licenses: usually 18+, some require completing an apprenticeship or coursework first (see item 5). Work: federal law allows non-hazardous work at 16, bans hazardous jobs under 18; in California, workers under 18 need a work permit. Community college: open enrollment at 18 or with a high school diploma (see item 3). California driver's license at 16.
- 收益：够得着门槛之后，还要过体检、背景审查这几关。读书还是打工那道题怎么算、技能怎么挑，见原书第 23 节（原书第 23 节）。开店办公司的本钱和退场，见原书第 12 节（原书第 12 节）。失业之后的保障，见原书第 7 节（原书第 7 节）。这些都不重复。
  Benefit: After clearing the thresholds, there are still medical exams and background checks. On whether to study or work and how to pick skills, see Section 23 of the original book. On startup capital and shutting down a business, see Section 12 of the original book. On safety nets after job loss, see Section 7 of the original book. None of that is repeated here.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：U.S. Selective Service System（登记年龄，见第 9 条来源）；各军种征兵条件（见第 6 条来源）；California Division of Apprenticeship Standards（见第 4 条来源）
- 备注：各军种年龄上限会调整，以征兵官和官网当期说法为准。加州未成年人工作许可的具体规则以加州劳工局当期规定为准。
  Note: Branch age caps change over time — go by what recruiters and official sites currently say. California's work-permit rules for minors follow the current rules of the California labor agency.

### 2. 四年制大学：UC、CSU、私立，门槛是高中毕业 + 课程要求，成本一年几万，助学金先填 FAFSA / Four-year university: UC, CSU, or private — threshold is a diploma plus course requirements; sticker price is tens of thousands per year; start with FAFSA for aid
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：UC 州内学费 2025–26 学年新生约 $14,934/年，住校一年总费用约 $4 万–$4.8 万（含住宿伙食）；CSU 学费更低；私立一年 $6 万起。申请费 UC 每所 $80（2025 年标准，以当期为准）。
  Cost: UC in-state tuition for new 2025–26 students is about $14,934/year; total on-campus cost runs about $40,000–$48,000/year including housing and meals. CSU tuition is lower; private schools start around $60,000/year. UC application fee is $80 per campus (2025 figure; check current rates).
- 说人话：UC 要求 A-G 课程（高中四年英语、三年数学等）+ GPA，CSU 类似但门槛略低，私立各校自己定。付不起先看助学金：填 FAFSA（联邦助学金申请），联邦 Pell Grant 2025–26 最高 $7,395/年（不用还）；加州居民还有 Cal Grant，Cal Grant A 可覆盖 UC 州内学费（2025–26 最高 $14,934），Cal Grant B 另有生活补助。国际学生一般拿不到联邦和州助学金。
  In plain terms: UC requires A-G coursework (four years of high school English, three of math, etc.) plus GPA; CSU is similar with a slightly lower bar; privates set their own. If you can't afford it, look at aid first: file FAFSA (the federal aid application). The federal Pell Grant maxes at $7,395/year for 2025–26 (free money, not a loan). California residents also get Cal Grant — Cal Grant A can cover UC in-state tuition (up to $14,934 for 2025–26), and Cal Grant B adds a living-expense allowance. International students generally can't get federal or state aid.
- 收益：四年制学位仍是很多职业的硬门槛。先算净价（net price）：学费减去助学金，再决定。社区大学读两年再转（见第 3 条）是同一张文凭、便宜一半以上的走法。
  Benefit: A bachelor's degree is still a hard requirement for many careers. Compute the net price first (tuition minus aid) before deciding. Two years at community college then transferring (see item 3) earns the same diploma for less than half the cost.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Legislative Analyst's Office. The 2025-26 Budget: University of California（2025–26 新生州内学费 $14,934）. <https://lao.ca.gov/reports/2025/4998/University-of-California-022725.pdf>；UC Santa Cruz Financial Aid. Cost to Attend（2026–27 住校总费用 $47,685）. <https://financialaid.ucsc.edu/managing-aid/budget-cost-to-attend/>；California Student Aid Commission. Cal Grant Award Types（Cal Grant A 2025–26 UC 最高 $14,934）. <http://csac.ca.gov/cal-grant/award-types>；Federal Pell Grant 2025–26 最高 $7,395. <https://berkeleycollege.edu/catalogs/undergraduate-2025-2026/financial-aid/federal-financial-aid-programs/index.html>
- 备注：UC 学费按入学年份锁定涨幅（cohort 制），转学生按转入 UC 那年算。FAFSA 每年都要重新填，截止日期别错过。
  Note: UC tuition is cohort-based (locked to your entering year); transfer students use their UC entry year. FAFSA must be refiled every year — don't miss the deadline.

### 3. 社区大学转学：$46 一学分，TAG 给六所 UC 保底录取 / Community college transfer: $46 per unit, and TAG guarantees admission to six UC campuses
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：加州居民 $46/学分，全日制一年学费约 $1,400（全美最低一档）；书本杂费另算。转学申请本身 UC 每所 $80。
  Cost: California residents pay $46/unit — about $1,400/year full-time in tuition, among the lowest in the nation; books and fees are extra. UC transfer applications are $80 per campus.
- 说人话：加州社区大学（CCC）开放入学，读完 60 个可转学分、GPA 达标就能转 UC/CSU。TAG（Transfer Admission Guarantee，转学录取保障）是加州独有的：6 所 UC（Davis、Irvine、Merced、Riverside、Santa Cruz、Santa Barbara）给社区大学学生保底录取——每年 9 月 1–30 日交 TAG 申请，先修满 30 个 UC 可转学分，GPA 达到该校该专业当年的线（常见 3.2–3.4，以当年 TAG 矩阵为准），录取基本锁定。Berkeley、UCLA、UCSD 不参加 TAG，但也大量收转学生。哪些课能转，去 assist.org 按学校和专业查对接表。
  In plain terms: California community colleges (CCC) have open enrollment; complete 60 transferable units with a qualifying GPA and you can transfer to UC or CSU. TAG (Transfer Admission Guarantee) is California-only: six UC campuses (Davis, Irvine, Merced, Riverside, Santa Cruz, Santa Barbara) guarantee admission to community college students — file the TAG application each September 1–30, complete 30 UC-transferable units first, and meet that campus and major's GPA line for the year (commonly 3.2–3.4; go by the current TAG matrix), and admission is essentially locked. Berkeley, UCLA, and UCSD don't offer TAG but still admit large numbers of transfers. Check which courses transfer at assist.org by college and major.
- 收益：前两年学费不到 UC 的十分之一，文凭和直录完全一样。这是加州性价比最高的本科路径，没有之一。
  Benefit: The first two years cost less than a tenth of UC tuition, and the diploma is identical to direct admission. This is California's best-value bachelor's path, bar none.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Victor Valley College. Transfer Admission Guarantee（TAG 申请期 9 月 1–30 日；30 个可转学分等要求）. <https://www.vvc.edu/transfer-admission-guarantee-programs>；UC Irvine TAG（GPA 要求示例）. <https://www.collegevine.com/faq/177219/transfer-tips-from-community-college-to-uc-irvine>；LA Valley College. Tuition & Fees（居民 $46/学分）. <https://www.lavc.edu/cost-tuition/fees>
- 备注：TAG 各校各专业 GPA 线每年微调，以当年 UC 公布的 TAG 矩阵为准（待核实）。TAG 只能保一所 UC，但常规申请可以同时申其他 UC。
  Note: Per-campus, per-major TAG GPA lines shift slightly each year — go by the UC-published TAG matrix for that year (to be verified). TAG covers one UC, but you can still apply to other UCs through regular admission.

### 4. 学徒制：加州 DAS 注册项目，边拿工资边学手艺，3–4 年出师 / Apprenticeship: California DAS-registered programs — earn while you learn a trade, journey out in 3–4 years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：学徒期间拿工资（起薪一般是出师工资的一定比例，随技能上涨），课堂培训通常由项目或雇主承担，自己一般不交学费。
  Cost: Apprentices earn wages from day one (starting at a fraction of journey-level pay, rising with skill); classroom instruction is usually covered by the program or employer — typically no tuition out of pocket.
- 说人话：注册学徒制（Registered Apprenticeship）是加州劳工局学徒标准处（Division of Apprenticeship Standards, DAS）注册的正规项目：带薪在岗培训 + 每年约 144 小时的课堂理论课。项目 1–6 年不等，多数 3–4 年。门槛通常是 18 岁、高中或 GED，有些工种要体能测试或入学考试。电工、水管工、钣金、电梯安装是最典型的几个，出师（journey level）后时薪在加州很可观。项目名单在 DAS 官网按工种和地区可查。
  In plain terms: Registered Apprenticeship programs are formal programs registered with the California Division of Apprenticeship Standards (DAS): paid on-the-job training plus about 144 hours per year of classroom theory. Programs run 1–6 years, most commonly 3–4. Thresholds are typically 18 and a high school diploma or GED; some trades add fitness tests or entrance exams. Electrician, plumbing, sheet metal, and elevator installation are the classic tracks — journey-level hourly pay in California is strong. The program list is searchable on the DAS site by trade and region.
- 收益：不欠学贷、四年后拿的是执照级手艺 + 四年工资，而不是文凭 + 贷款账单。课堂学分很多还能转成社区大学学分。
  Benefit: No student debt — after four years you hold a licensed-level trade plus four years of wages, not a diploma plus a loan statement. Much of the classroom work can also convert to community college credit.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Apprenticeship Coordinators Association. FAQs（DAS 注册；项目 1–6 年、多数 3–4 年；每年约 144 小时课堂；通常 18 岁起、无年龄上限；可转大学学分）. <http://calapprenticeship.org/faqs.php>；California Code of Regulations（学徒项目规范）. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/california/5-CCR-53413>
- 备注：热门项目（如电工）申请窗口经常爆满，有的限量收 2000 份申请就关，看到开放就尽早交。工会项目和非工会项目都有，待遇和排队时间不同。
  Note: Popular programs (electrician especially) fill fast — some close the window after 2,000 applications, so apply as soon as it opens. Both union and non-union programs exist, with different pay and wait times.

### 5. 技工与职业证书：执照就是饭碗，考证费用几百到几千美元 / Trade and professional certificates: the license is the livelihood; exam and training costs run hundreds to a few thousand dollars
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：举例：CNA（护士助理）培训课程几百到一两千美元、几周到几个月；商用驾照（CDL）培训学校 $3,000–$7,000（以当期为准）；电工执照考试费一百多美元（但要先走完学徒年限）。
  Cost: Examples: CNA (certified nursing assistant) courses run several hundred to a couple thousand dollars over weeks to months; commercial driver's license (CDL) schools cost $3,000–$7,000 (check current prices); electrician license exam fees are around a hundred-plus dollars (but only after completing apprenticeship years).
- 说人话：美国很多蓝领高薪岗位看的不是学历，是执照：电工（学徒→journeyman→master）、水管工、HVAC（暖通空调）、CDL 卡车司机、CNA 护士助理、美容美发（cosmetology）等。路径一般是课程 + 考试 + 有的要实习小时数，发证的是加州各执照局。选之前查三件事：该执照在加州是不是强制（无照上岗违法）、考试通过率、本地真实工资。
  In plain terms: Many well-paid U.S. blue-collar jobs don't ask for degrees — they ask for licenses: electrician (apprentice → journeyman → master), plumber, HVAC technician, CDL truck driver, CNA nursing assistant, cosmetology, and more. The path is usually coursework + exam, sometimes plus supervised hours, licensed by California's various licensing boards. Before choosing, check three things: whether the license is legally required in California (working unlicensed is illegal), exam pass rates, and real local wages.
- 收益：执照是排他性门槛，有证的人供给有限，工资下不来。CNA、CDL 这类几个月就能上岗，是急需用钱时最快的合法路径之一。
  Benefit: A license is an exclusive gate — the supply of licensed workers is limited, which props up wages. Credentials like CNA or CDL get you working in months, among the fastest legitimate paths when you need money now.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者归纳，各执照费用与培训价格以发证机构当期公布为准（待核实）/ author synthesis; fees and training prices per each licensing body's current publications (to be verified)
- 备注：凡是收钱「包过」「内部拿证」的都是骗局，真执照没有捷径。跨州搬家前查执照互认（reciprocity），有的州不认加州证。
  Note: Anyone selling "guaranteed pass" or "inside-track licenses" is a scammer — real licenses have no shortcuts. Before moving states, check license reciprocity; some states don't honor California licenses.

### 6. 参军的基本条件：17 岁起，公民或绿卡，高中毕业或 GED，ASVAB 达标 / Basic enlistment requirements: 17+, U.S. citizen or green card holder, high school diploma or GED, qualifying ASVAB score
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。从找征兵官到入伍要过体检（MEPS）、背景审查，前后几个月。
  Cost: Free. From first recruiter meeting to shipping out, you'll clear the MEPS medical exam and a background check — several months end to end.
- 说人话：各军种大同小异：17 岁可报名（未满 18 要父母同意），年龄上限陆军 35、海军和空军 41、太空军 42；公民或绿卡（军官一般要公民）；高中毕业证或 GED（有毕业证的选择多）；ASVAB 的 AFQT 分数一般至少 31 分；通过征兵站体检和体能、身高体重标准；无严重犯罪记录（部分可申请豁免）。
  In plain terms: The branches are similar: enlist at 17 (parental consent required under 18); age caps are Army 35, Navy and Air Force 41, Space Force 42; U.S. citizen or green card holder (officers generally must be citizens); high school diploma or GED (diploma holders get more options); AFQT score on the ASVAB usually at least 31; pass the MEPS medical exam and height/weight and fitness standards; no serious criminal record (some waivers available).
- 收益：现役起薪按军衔和军龄定，另加住房补贴（BAH）、伙食补贴，医保全包。签约奖金（enlistment bonus）按紧缺专业给，签之前问清、写进合同。
  Benefit: Active-duty base pay is set by rank and years of service, plus housing allowance (BAH) and food allowance, with full health coverage. Enlistment bonuses depend on in-demand specialties — ask before signing and get it in the contract.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Military.com. Join the Military: Basic Eligibility（各军种年龄、公民、ASVAB 要求）. <https://www.military.com/join-armed-forces/join-the-military-basic-eligibility.html>；U.S. Army eligibility（17–35 岁，公民/绿卡，高中/GED，AFQT 31）. <https://usmilitary.com/can-i-join-the-us-army/>
- 备注：年龄上限和分数要求会随征兵形势调整，以征兵官和官网当期说法为准。绿卡持有者能当兵，但安全许可（security clearance）受限，部分岗位报不了。
  Note: Age caps and score cutoffs move with recruiting conditions — go by current recruiter and official guidance. Green card holders can enlist, but security-clearance limits keep some jobs off-limits.

### 7. 参军最实在的一块：Post-9/11 GI Bill，36 个月学费 + 住房补贴 + 书本费 / The most tangible military benefit: the Post-9/11 GI Bill — 36 months of tuition, housing allowance, and books money
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。退役后向 VA 申请，学校每学期认证一次在读。
  Cost: Free. Apply through the VA after separation; the school certifies your enrollment each term.
- 说人话：2001 年 9 月 10 日之后服过至少 90 天现役（因公致残荣誉退役的 30 天即可），就有 Post-9/11 GI Bill。给 36 个月：公立大学的州内学费全包（直接打给学校），每月住房补贴（按学校邮编、E-5 有家属标准），每年最高 1000 美元书本费。私立和国外学校有年度上限（以 VA 当期公布为准）。现役期间还有每年最高 4500 美元的学费补助（Tuition Assistance），先用它，GI Bill 留到退役后用。
  In plain terms: With at least 90 days of active duty after September 10, 2001 (30 days if honorably discharged for a service-connected disability), you earn the Post-9/11 GI Bill. It pays for 36 months: full in-state tuition at public schools (paid directly to the school), a monthly housing allowance (E-5 with-dependents rate for the school's zip code), and up to $1,000 per year for books. Private and foreign schools have an annual cap (check the VA's current published rate). While on active duty there's also Tuition Assistance (up to $4,500/year) — use that first and save the GI Bill for after separation.
- 收益：36 个月基本够读完一个四年制学位。这是当兵回报里最值钱、最确定的部分，服役前先算进计划里。
  Benefit: 36 months is roughly enough for a four-year degree. This is the most valuable and most certain part of the compensation for serving — factor it into your plan before you enlist.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. Benefits Comparison Table（Post-9/11 GI Bill：90 天现役，36 个月，学费、住房补贴、书本费）. <https://benefits.va.gov/gibill/docs/BenefitsComparisonTable.pdf>；Post-9/11 GI Bill benefits（住房补贴按学校邮编 E-5 标准；书本费每年最高 $1,000）. <https://guidance.aero/post-911-gi-bill-benefits-education-training-va-gov/>
- 备注：2013 年 1 月 1 日之后退役的，福利没有 15 年过期限制；之前退役的一般 15 年内要用完。Yellow Ribbon 项目可补私立学校学费缺口，详见 VA 官网。
  Note: If your service ended on or after January 1, 2013, the benefit doesn't expire after 15 years; earlier separations generally must use it within 15 years. The Yellow Ribbon Program can cover private-school tuition gaps — see the VA site for details.

### 8. 退役没有「包分配」：就业靠退伍军人优先录用 + USERRA 复职权，福利靠 VA 房贷和伤残补偿 / No guaranteed placement after separation: jobs via veterans' preference and USERRA reemployment rights; benefits via the VA home loan and disability compensation
