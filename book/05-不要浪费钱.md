# 5. 不要浪费钱 / 5. Don't Waste Money

这一节只算钱：能省下多少，每年收益差多少，一笔钱长期平均下来能拿回多少。这些不换算成寿命或时间。涉及投资的条目只讲怎么算账，只用核实过的历史数据，都不构成投资建议。加州版把中国特有的行政流程条目略去，把金融监管口径换成美国（SEC、FTC、FDIC 等），证据等级与原文一一对应。

This section is only about money: how much you can save, how much annual returns differ, and how much a sum of money returns on average over the long run. Nothing here is converted into lifespan or time. Items involving investing only explain the math, only use verified historical data, and none of it constitutes investment advice. The California edition omits China-specific administrative procedures and swaps Chinese regulatory references for U.S. ones (SEC, FTC, FDIC, etc.); evidence grades match the original one-to-one.

### 1. 关掉所有自动续费，改为到期手动续 / Turn Off All Auto-Renewals and Renew Manually Instead
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。把苹果 App Store、Google Play 的订阅列表和信用卡/借记卡的定期扣款翻一遍，一次 10 到 20 分钟。以后每次续费，要自己多点一次。
  Cost: Nothing. Go through your Apple App Store and Google Play subscription lists plus recurring charges on your credit/debit cards once — 10 to 20 minutes. After that, each renewal takes one extra manual tap.
- 说人话：省下来的，是那些你早就不用、每月还在扣钱的订阅。重点查三处：手机设置里的订阅（iPhone 在"设置-Apple ID-订阅"，安卓在 Google Play-付款和订阅），以及银行账单上的定期扣款。加州法律要求商家清楚告知自动续费条款，并提供简便的取消方式（具体条款待核实）。
  In plain terms: What you save is the money still being deducted every month for subscriptions you stopped using long ago. Check three places: subscriptions in your phone settings (iPhone: Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions; Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions), and recurring charges on your bank statements. California law requires sellers to clearly disclose auto-renewal terms and provide an easy way to cancel (exact provisions to be verified).
- 收益：省下的钱，等于你已经不用、却还在扣费的那些订阅加起来的钱。每个人不一样，没有统一数字。
  Benefit: What you save equals the total of the subscriptions you're no longer using but still paying for. It varies by person; there is no single number.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：国家市场监督管理总局 (2021, 2025 修正). 网络交易监督管理办法（总局令第 37 号，第十八条）. <https://www.samr.gov.cn/zw/zfxxgk/fdzdgknr/fgs/art/2025/art_4b47c79b8d994a42bba4835997688faa.html>
- 备注：你确实经常用的服务，年付一般比月付便宜，留着自动续费反而划算。要关的是你早就忘了、却还在扣钱的那些。加州自动续费法的具体条文编号待核实。
  Note: For services you genuinely use all the time, annual billing is usually cheaper than monthly, so keeping auto-renewal makes sense. Turn off the ones you forgot about but are still paying for. The exact citation of California's automatic renewal law is to be verified.

### 2. 每年报税季核对一次预扣税，拿足你应得的退税 / Check Your Tax Withholding Every Tax Season and Claim the Refund You're Owed
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。用 IRS 的免费报税渠道（Free File）或报税软件，每年 2 到 4 月花一两个小时。
  Cost: Nothing. Use the IRS Free File program or tax software; spend an hour or two each year during the February–April filing season.
- 说人话：这几种人报税时通常能拿回钱：全年只工作了几个月，年中换过工作，或者抚养子女、房贷利息、学贷利息、慈善捐赠这些扣除没用足。工资预扣多了，等于全年在给政府免息贷款；预扣少了，可能欠税加罚款。换工作或收入大变时，用 W-4 表调一次预扣。
  In plain terms: These people usually get money back at tax time: those who worked only part of the year, changed jobs mid-year, or didn't fully use deductions for dependents, mortgage interest, student-loan interest, or charitable giving. Over-withholding means giving the government an interest-free loan all year; under-withholding can mean owing tax plus penalties. When you change jobs or your income shifts a lot, adjust withholding with Form W-4.
- 收益：把全年收入和该拿的扣除、抵税额算一次总账，多预扣的退给你，少预扣的要补上。标准扣除和分项扣除哪个高用哪个，别漏掉你符合条件的抵税额。
  Benefit: Reconcile your full-year income against the deductions and credits you're entitled to: over-withheld tax comes back as a refund, under-withheld tax must be paid. Take whichever is higher — the standard deduction or itemized deductions — and don't miss credits you qualify for.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：国家税务总局 (2025). 个人所得税综合所得汇算清缴管理办法（第六条）及其解读. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/202502/content_7006465.htm>；IRS Free File. <https://www.irs.gov/free-file>
- 备注：原文的"12 万元/400 元"门槛和七项专项附加扣除是中国税制，加州版不适用。IRS Free File 的收入门槛每年调整，以当年 IRS 公布为准（待核实）。
  Note: The original's thresholds and seven special deductions are Chinese tax rules and don't apply in California. The IRS Free File income threshold changes yearly — check the current IRS figure (to be verified).

### 4. 每年重算一次手机和宽带套餐，用不上的降档 / Re-Shop Your Phone and Internet Plans Every Year; Downgrade What You Don't Use
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。查一次账单和实际用量，十几分钟。难的是顶住客服挽留，或者花半小时换运营商。
  Cost: Nothing. Check one bill against your actual usage — a dozen minutes. The hard part is resisting the retention rep, or spending half an hour switching carriers.
- 说人话：你用的老套餐，往往比现在新卖的贵。先在运营商 App 里查近半年实际用了多少流量和通话，再按这个用量去比：三大运营商的老套餐、虚拟运营商（MVNO）的低价套餐，哪个便宜用哪个。宽带也一样，每年对比一次同地址能装的几家。运营商拖着不给你降档或携号转网，可以向 FCC 投诉。
  In plain terms: Your old plan is often more expensive than what's sold now. First check your actual data and minutes over the last six months in the carrier's app, then shop by that usage: compare your carrier's current plans and cheap MVNO plans, and pick whatever's cheapest. Do the same yearly for home internet among the providers at your address. If a carrier stalls your downgrade or number port-out, you can file a complaint with the FCC.
- 收益：老套餐往往比在售套餐贵，一个月差几十美元，一年就是几百美元白扔。虚拟运营商用同一张网络，话费可以便宜一大截，代价是客服和漫游权益可能打折。
  Benefit: Old plans are often tens of dollars a month more than current ones — hundreds of dollars a year wasted. MVNOs ride the same networks for much less, with the trade-off of thinner customer service and roaming perks.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：工业和信息化部 (2019). 携号转网服务管理规定（工信部信管〔2019〕242 号）. <http://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2019-11/13/content_5451483.htm>；FCC Consumer Complaint Center. <https://consumercomplaints.fcc.gov>
- 备注：原文的携号转网规章是中国规定，美国携号转网本身通常顺畅，难的是客服挽留。FCC 投诉入口以官网为准。
  Note: The original's porting regulation is Chinese; in the U.S. porting itself is usually smooth — the friction is the retention rep. Use the FCC's official complaint portal.

### 5. 不买彩票 / Don't Buy Lottery Tickets
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。不买就是省钱。
  Cost: Nothing. Not buying is saving.
- 说人话：彩票的数学在全球都一样：拿出来当奖金的钱只占销售额的一部分，剩下归运营和公益，长期期望一定是负的。中国的数据：乐透数字型彩票奖金最低占销售额 50%，双色球 51%，每投 1 元长期平均拿回 0.51 元（中国数据）。美国的强力球（Powerball）、兆彩（Mega Millions）和加州彩票同样如此，只是各州奖金占比不同（待核实）。
  In plain terms: The math of lotteries is the same worldwide: only part of sales goes to prizes, the rest to operations and public causes, so the long-run expected return is always negative. China data: at least 50% of lotto sales go to prizes, 51% for the Double Color Ball game — 1 yuan bet returns 0.51 yuan on average long-term (China data). The same holds for Powerball, Mega Millions, and the California Lottery, though each state's prize share differs (to be verified).
- 收益：长期平均下来一定亏，这是制度定死的，和运气无关。
  Benefit: You lose on average in the long run — that's baked into the system and has nothing to do with luck.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：财政部 (2015). 关于规范和加强彩票资金构成比例管理的通知（财综〔2015〕94 号）. <http://m.mof.gov.cn/czxw/201511/t20151113_1560814.htm>
- 备注：长期平均下来一定亏，这是制度定死的，和运气无关。要买也行，但只能当成几美元一次的娱乐花销，不能当成理财。美国各州彩票奖金占比的具体数字待核实。
  Note: The long-run loss is structural, not about luck. Buying is fine as a few-dollars entertainment expense, never as an investment. Exact U.S. state lottery prize-share figures are to be verified.

### 6. 看到「高收益」「保本」「稳赚」直接走开 / Walk Away from "High Returns," "Guaranteed," and "Can't Lose"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。听到这几个词的时候停一下就行。
  Cost: Nothing. Just pause when you hear these words.
- 说人话：美国版的铁律同样简单：承诺高回报又低风险、保本稳赚的，基本都是骗局，最常见的是庞氏骗局（Ponzi scheme）——拿后人的钱付前人的"收益"。FTC 的警告口径是：任何要求你用加密货币付款的"投资机会"，基本都是骗局。中国监管者画过三条线：收益率超过 6% 就要打问号，超过 8% 很危险，10% 以上就要准备赔光本金（中国数据，2018 年陆家嘴论坛讲话）。
  In plain terms: The U.S. version of the rule is just as simple: anyone promising high returns with low risk, guaranteed principal, or "can't lose" is almost certainly running a scam — most commonly a Ponzi scheme, which pays early "returns" with later investors' money. The FTC's warning: any "investment opportunity" that asks you to pay in cryptocurrency is basically a scam. A Chinese regulator once drew three lines: question anything above 6%, be wary above 8%, and prepare to lose everything above 10% (China data, 2018 Lujiazui Forum remarks).
- 收益：正规投资从不承诺保本保收益。SEC 和 FTC 反复提醒：收益和风险永远同向，"无风险高回报"在数学上不存在。掉进庞氏骗局或非法集资，亏掉的本金通常追不回来。
  Benefit: Legitimate investments never promise guaranteed principal or returns. The SEC and FTC repeat the same warning: return and risk always move together — "risk-free high returns" don't exist mathematically. Money lost to a Ponzi scheme or illegal fundraising is usually unrecoverable.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：郭树清 (2018). 防范化解金融风险 奋力跨越重大关口（第十届陆家嘴论坛讲话，国家金融监督管理总局官网）. <https://www.nfra.gov.cn/cn/view/pages/ItemDetail.html?docId=182418>；SEC Investor.gov, "Ponzi Schemes". <https://www.investor.gov/protect-your-investments/fraud/types-fraud/ponzi-scheme>
- 备注：6%、8%、10% 这三条线是监管者按经验画的，不是法律条文（中国数据）。不构成投资建议。
  Note: The 6%/8%/10% lines are a regulator's rule of thumb, not law (China data). Not investment advice.
### 7. 不只还信用卡最低还款，不为消费开分期或消费贷；非借不可，先把利息换成年利率（APR）再比 / Don't Just Pay the Credit Card Minimum or Finance Consumption; If You Must Borrow, Compare APRs First
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。要在账单日之前留够全额还款的现金。难的是忍住不用分期把大额开销摊薄。非借不可时，花几分钟问清每笔贷款的年利率（APR）。
  Cost: Nothing. Keep enough cash to pay the full balance by the due date. The hard part is resisting the urge to split big purchases into installments. If you must borrow, spend a few minutes getting the APR of each loan.
- 说人话：只还最低还款，剩下没还的部分从消费那天起按年利率计息，免息期（grace period）同时作废。比哪笔贷款贵，只看 APR（年利率）：美国法律要求贷款方明确披露 APR，它要把利息和直接相关费用都折进去。分期或"先买后付"（BNPL）看着每期只多一点钱，但因为本金每月在减少，实际年利率往往比标出来的费率高得多。
  In plain terms: Paying only the minimum means the unpaid balance accrues interest from the purchase date at the card's APR, and the grace period is gone. To compare loans, look only at APR: U.S. law requires lenders to disclose APR clearly, and it must fold interest plus directly related fees into one yearly rate. Installment plans or "buy now, pay later" look cheap per payment, but because the principal shrinks each month while the fee is charged on the original amount, the true annualized rate is often far higher than the advertised fee.
- 收益：信用卡最低还款是按天计息的贷款，年化下来非常贵。用了最低还款以后，没还的那部分本金每天全额算利息，免息期作废。分期还本的，要按每期还完后实际占用的本金算真实成本。比两笔贷款时，让对方给按复利算的年利率（APR），别看月费率、日息这些障眼法。
  Benefit: The credit-card minimum payment is a daily-interest loan that annualizes to a very expensive rate. After using it, the unpaid balance accrues interest in full every day and the grace period is void. For installment plans, compute the true cost against the principal you actually still owe after each payment. When comparing two loans, ask for the compounding-based APR — ignore monthly-fee or daily-rate window dressing.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：中国人民银行 (2021). 中国人民银行公告〔2021〕第 3 号（第一、三、四条，附件《采用内部收益率法计算贷款年化利率示例》第三例）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/2021-03/31/content_5597156.htm>；CFPB, "What is APR?" <https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-apr-en-2057/>
- 备注：原文的 18.25%、13.8% 等具体数字是中国语境下的算例（中国数据），道理通用：本金逐月减少、费用按原金额收时，真实年化约为名义费率的 1.8 到 2 倍。真周转不开的时候，优先找利率更低的正规渠道借，不要拖着最低还款滚利息。
  Note: The original's 18.25% and 13.8% figures are worked examples in the Chinese context (China data); the logic is universal: when principal shrinks monthly but fees are charged on the original amount, the true annualized rate is roughly 1.8–2x the nominal fee. If cash is truly tight, borrow from a cheaper legitimate channel first — don't let minimum payments compound.

### 8. 不给主播打赏、不给游戏充值、不冲动下单 / Don't Tip Streamers, Don't Top Up Games, Don't Buy on Impulse
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。花几分钟做三件事：关掉一键支付和免密支付，设好家长控制/购买确认，把 App 里绑的支付方式删掉。难的是当场忍住不点那一下。
  Cost: Nothing. Spend a few minutes on three things: turn off one-click and password-free payments, set up parental controls / require purchase confirmation, and remove saved payment methods from apps. The hard part is resisting the tap in the moment.
- 说人话：花出去的打赏和充值基本追不回来。中国的数据：2024 年国内游戏市场人均年花费约 480 元（中国数据）。美国这边同样：直播打赏、游戏内购一旦成交，成年人很难要回来。最省力的办法是提前设防：关掉免密支付、开购买确认、把支付方式解绑。
  In plain terms: Tips and top-ups are basically unrecoverable once spent. China data: average per-person game spending was about 480 yuan a year in 2024 (China data). It's the same in the U.S.: livestream tips and in-game purchases are very hard for adults to get back once made. The cheapest defense is set up in advance: turn off password-free payments, require purchase confirmation, unbind payment methods.

### 11. 拒绝电子产品的延长保修（延保）/ Decline Extended Warranties on Electronics
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。代价是保修期过了以后，万一要修，得自己掏钱，不过这种事概率不大。

- 收益：算法适用于自己掏得起一次维修费的普通人。算之前先扣掉厂家本来就给的保修期，有些信用卡还自带延长保修，先查。延保和厂家保修重叠的那段，等于花钱买了本来就有的东西。

### 15. 不频繁交易股票，行情暴涨暴跌的时候更要少动手 / Don't Trade Stocks Frequently — Especially When the Market Swings Wildly

  Benefit: Frequent and infrequent traders earned nearly the same before costs — so the gap comes mostly from trading fees. The gap is small in calm years and largest in hot markets — which is exactly when you most need to sit still.

### 16. 不借钱投资、不加杠杆、不买自己看不懂的 / Don't Invest Borrowed Money, Don't Use Leverage, Don't Buy What You Don't Understand
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是行情好的时候，要忍住不去想"借点钱多赚一点"。
  Cost: Nothing. The hard part is resisting "borrow a little to earn a little more" when markets are hot.
- 说人话：借钱买股票（margin），一旦跌破券商的维持线，你又补不上保证金，就会被强制卖掉（margin call）。账面上的亏损当场变成真亏，连"等它涨回来"的机会都没有。融资账户本身有资金门槛，不是给普通人日常用的工具。用信用卡套现或消费贷去投资，也是变相的杠杆。
  In plain terms: Buying stocks with borrowed money (margin) means that if you fall below the broker's maintenance line and can't add funds, you get forcibly sold out (a margin call). Paper losses become real losses instantly, with no chance to "wait for it to come back." Margin accounts have funding minimums — they're not everyday tools for ordinary people. Cashing out credit cards or using consumer loans to invest is leverage by another name.
- 收益：杠杆会把亏损放大。跌破维持线又补不上钱，券商直接替你卖掉，没有"等它涨回来"的机会。
  Benefit: Leverage magnifies losses. Fall below the maintenance line without topping up, and the broker sells for you — no waiting for a rebound.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：中国证监会 (2015). 证券公司融资融券业务管理办法（证监会令第 117 号，第十二条）. <https://www.csrc.gov.cn/csrc/c100028/c1654005/content.shtml>；FINRA, "Margin Account". <https://www.finra.org/investors/learn-to-invest/key-investing-concepts/margin-account>
- 备注："看不懂的不买"这句没有直接的文献支持，属于共识，单算是 C 级。用消费贷或者信用卡套现去投资，也是变相的杠杆，它们的利率见本节第 7 条。不构成投资建议。美国融资账户的具体门槛（如 pattern day trader 资金要求）待核实。
  Note: "Don't buy what you don't understand" has no direct literature support — it's consensus, graded C on its own. Using consumer loans or credit-card cash advances to invest is also leverage in disguise; see item 7 for their rates. Not investment advice. Exact U.S. margin-account thresholds (e.g., pattern-day-trader funding requirements) are to be verified.

- 说人话：主动基金合起来买的那堆股票，已经很接近整个市场了。但它们多收的那笔管理费，是实打实从你到手的收益里扣掉的。标普道琼斯每年统计：2024 年，美国主动管理的大盘股票基金有 65% 跑输标普 500；统计了 24 年，平均每年 64% 跑输。能把多收的费挣回来的基金，很少。
  In plain terms: Active funds collectively hold something very close to the whole market — but their extra management fees come straight out of your returns. S&P Dow Jones tracks this yearly: in 2024, 65% of U.S. actively managed large-cap funds trailed the S&P 500; over 24 years of tracking, 64% trailed in an average year. Few funds earn enough extra to cover their own fees.
- 收益：在 401(k)、IRA 或普通券商账户里，把长期不动的那部分钱放进低费率的宽基指数基金，等于用最低的成本拿到市场的平均回报。
  Benefit: In a 401(k), IRA, or regular brokerage account, putting the money you won't touch for years into a low-cost broad-market index fund gets you the market's average return at the lowest cost.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Fama EF, French KR (2010). Luck versus Skill in the Cross-Section of Mutual Fund Returns. The Journal of Finance 65(5):1915-1947. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2010.01598.x>；S&P Dow Jones Indices (2025). SPIVA U.S. Scorecard Year-End 2024. <https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/en/spiva/article/spiva-us/>；Harvey CR, Liu Y (2022). Luck versus Skill in the Cross Section of Mutual Fund Returns: Reexamining the Evidence. The Journal of Finance, 77(3). <https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13123>（争议方）
- 备注：争议：有一篇文章重新检验过这个结论，认为"几乎没有基金有技能"这个结论可能下得过重了。但指数基金费率低这一点照样成立。不构成投资建议。
  Note: Disputed: one paper re-examined the finding and argued the "almost no fund has skill" conclusion may be overstated. That index funds charge lower fees still holds. Not investment advice.

### 18. 同类基金优先选费率低的 / Among Similar Funds, Pick the Cheapest
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。买之前多看一眼 expense ratio（费率）是多少。
  Cost: Nothing. Just check the expense ratio before buying.
- 说人话：一年的费率每高 1 个百分点，20 年后到手的钱少约 18%，30 年后少约 26%。在同一类基金里挑费率低的，是这件事上唯一一个你能提前确定的东西。
  In plain terms: Each extra percentage point of annual fees leaves you with about 18% less after 20 years and about 26% less after 30 years. Among similar funds, picking the cheaper one is the only thing you can know for sure in advance.
- 收益：费率的差别会按复利一点点吃掉本金。看 expense ratio 时，把管理费、托管费、12b-1 销售服务费都算进去，别只看其中一项。
  Benefit: Fee differences eat principal through compounding, a little at a time. When checking the expense ratio, count management fees, custody fees, and 12b-1 distribution fees together — not just one of them.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：Fama EF, French KR (2010). 见第 11 条. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.2010.01598.x>
- 备注：这个算式只是照公式算出来的，前提是两只基金扣费之前赚得一样多。实际还要算上申购费、赎回费。中国 2023 年起的公募基金费率改革（主动权益类管理费上限 1.2%）是中国数据（中国数据），加州版不适用。不构成投资建议。
  Note: The formula assumes both funds earn the same before fees; in practice also count loads and redemption fees. China's 2023 mutual-fund fee reform (1.2% management-fee cap on active equity funds) is China data (China data) and doesn't apply in California. Not investment advice.

### 19. 别把钱押在一只股票、一个平台、一套房上 / Don't Put All Your Money in One Stock, One Platform, or One House
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是要接受一件事：你不会正好押中最赚的那一个。
  Cost: Nothing. The hard part is accepting one thing: you won't happen to pick the single biggest winner.
- 说人话：几样东西只要不是完全一起涨、一起跌，把钱分开放，平均能赚到的不变，账户的起伏却会小一些。反过来，钱全押在一只股票、一个平台、一套房上，它一出事，就等于你全部出事。
  In plain terms: As long as assets don't move in perfect lockstep, spreading money around keeps the average expected gain the same while shrinking the ups and downs. Put everything in one stock, one platform, or one house, and its disaster is your disaster.
- 收益：这是 Markowitz 1952 年提出的组合选择基本结论，此后几十年一直沿用。分散只能让账户的涨跌小一些，不保证你不亏。
  Benefit: This is the core result of Markowitz's 1952 portfolio selection theory, used ever since. Diversification only dampens swings — it doesn't guarantee you won't lose.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Markowitz, H. (1952). Portfolio Selection. The Journal of Finance, 7(1), 77–91. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1952.tb01525.x>
- 备注：定成 B 级，是因为那篇经典文献是纯数学推导，没有算出"分散能少亏多少钱"的实际数字。买宽基指数基金（见本节第 17 条）本身就是最省事的分散办法。不构成投资建议。
  Note: Graded B because the classic paper is pure math — it never computed "how much less diversification loses" in practice. Buying a broad-market index fund (see item 17) is itself the laziest diversification. Not investment advice.

### 20. 有雇主 401(k) 匹配先拿满，再考虑税前存退休账户 / Grab the Full 401(k) Employer Match First, Then Consider Pre-Tax Retirement Contributions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：开户不花钱。真正的代价是 401(k)、传统 IRA 里的钱 59 岁半之前取出来通常有罚款，等于锁到接近退休。
  Cost: Opening the account is free. The real price: money in a 401(k) or traditional IRA usually faces a penalty if withdrawn before age 59½ — effectively locked until near retirement.
- 说人话：顺序只有两步。第一步，如果雇主给 401(k) 匹配（比如你存 5% 他跟 3%），先把匹配拿满——这是白给的钱，不拿等于降薪。第二步，再考虑往 401(k) 或传统 IRA 里税前多存：存进去当年少交税，取出来时再交。税率越高的人，税前存越划算。Roth 那边反过来：现在交税，退休后取免税，适合现在税率低、以后税率会高的人。
  In plain terms: Just two steps. First: if your employer matches 401(k) contributions (e.g., you put in 5%, they add 3%), grab the full match — it's free money, and skipping it is a pay cut. Second: consider adding pre-tax money to a 401(k) or traditional IRA — you pay less tax now and pay tax when you withdraw. The higher your tax rate, the more pre-tax saving pays off. Roth works the other way: pay tax now, withdraw tax-free in retirement — better if your rate is low now and will be higher later.
- 收益：雇主匹配是确定性的 50% 到 100% 即时回报（看匹配比例），没有任何投资能稳定做到。税前存的省税额等于"你的边际税率 × 存入金额"。
  Benefit: The employer match is a certain, instant 50%–100% return (depending on the match formula) — no investment reliably does that. Pre-tax saving saves "your marginal tax rate × amount contributed."
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：财政部、税务总局 (2024). 关于在全国范围实施个人养老金个人所得税优惠政策的公告（财政部 税务总局公告 2024 年第 21 号）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202412/content_6992498.htm>；IRS, Retirement Plans. <https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans>
- 备注：原文的个人养老金（每年 12000 元、领取时 3%）是中国制度，加州版不适用。每年的 401(k)/IRA 缴存上限以当年 IRS 公布为准（待核实）。别为了"薅羊毛"把应急金锁进去（应急金见第 27 条）。账户里的钱还要你自己选投资标的，是会亏的，选产品的规则和本节第 17、18 条一样。
  Note: The original's individual pension account (12,000 yuan/year, 3% tax on withdrawal) is a Chinese system and doesn't apply in California. Annual 401(k)/IRA contribution limits follow the current IRS figures (to be verified). Don't lock your emergency fund in for "free money" (see item 27). You still choose the investments inside the account, and they can lose money — the same rules as items 17 and 18 apply.

### 21. 健身房按次付费或短周期付费，除非有一年以上的稳定记录 / Pay Per Visit or Short-Term for the Gym, Unless You Have a Year of Steady Attendance
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：按次买，单次价格比年卡摊下来贵一些。你要老实估计自己一个月到底去几次。
  Cost: Pay-per-visit costs more per session than an annualized membership. Be honest about how many times a month you'll actually go.
- 说人话：美国那份三年的数据里，选月费 70 美元以上合同的会员，平均每月只去 4.3 次，折合每次超过 17 美元。而按次买 10 次卡，每次只要 10 美元。这些人在会籍期间平均多付了 600 美元。除非你已经有一年以上稳定去的记录，否则按次买或者买短期的更划算。
  In plain terms: In a three-year U.S. dataset, members on contracts over $70/month went only 4.3 times a month on average — over $17 per visit — while a 10-visit pass cost $10 a visit. They overpaid by $600 on average during their memberships. Unless you already have a year of steady attendance, pay-per-visit or short-term is cheaper.
- 收益：这项研究看的是美国 3 家健身俱乐部 7,752 名会员 3 年的数据。选月费超过 70 美元合同的会员平均多付了 600 美元。另外，月付会员超过一年还没退卡的比例，比年付会员高 17%。
  Benefit: The study tracked 7,752 members across 3 U.S. gyms for 3 years. Members on $70+/month contracts overpaid by $600 on average. Monthly members were also 17% more likely than annual members to still be paying after a year without going.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：DellaVigna S, Malmendier U (2006). Paying Not to Go to the Gym. American Economic Review 96(3):694-719. <https://doi.org/10.1257/aer.96.3.694>
- 备注：这只是一项研究，用的是美国数据。但背后的道理很普遍：人会高估自己将来的自控力。如果你已经有连续一年、每周去两次以上的记录，那年卡确实更便宜。
  Note: It's a single study on U.S. data — but the underlying logic is universal: people overestimate their future self-discipline. If you already have a year of going twice a week or more, the annual membership really is cheaper.

There's only one advance move: get refund terms in writing, keep payment proof, and pay by credit card rather than debit or cash when you can — credit cards carry chargeback rights, so the money can be recovered. - 来源：国务院 (2024). 中华人民共和国消费者权益保护法实施条例（国务院令第 778 号，2024 年 7 月 1 日施行）. <https://www.gov.cn/zhengce/zhengceku/202403/content_6940159.htm>

So prepay as little as possible (see item 21), and chase with the contract and complaints if it goes wrong.

### 24. 不为「划线价」和大促囤货 / Don't Stock Up for "Was" Prices and Mega-Sales
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。代价是有些真折扣你也放过了。你得记住自己常买那几样东西的日常价。难的是大促当天忍住不囤。
  Cost: Nothing. The price is missing some real deals. You need to remember the everyday price of the things you buy regularly. The hard part is not stockpiling on sale day.
- 说人话：人判断一样东西贵不贵，靠的是最先看到的那个数字，之后再怎么调整都调不够。所以"原价 999 现价 499"会让你系统性地高估它有多便宜——那个划掉的价格，不一定真卖过。加州法律禁止虚假的"原价"宣传（具体条款待核实）。下单前查一下历史价格（比如 CamelCamelCamel 这类比价工具），只看它过去实际卖过多少钱。

So "was $999, now $499" systematically inflates how cheap it feels — the crossed-out price may never have been real. California law bans deceptive "former price" claims (exact provision to be verified). Before buying, check the price history (tools like CamelCamelCamel) and only look at what it actually sold for.
- 收益：锚定效应是实验室里得出的结论，没有人算过囤货实际亏多少钱。囤货还有几笔你不会记在账上的成本：东西过期，占地方，以及"反正家里有，那就多用点"。
  Benefit: The anchoring effect comes from lab studies; nobody has measured how much stockpiling actually loses. Stockpiling also has costs you never book: expiration, space, and "it's already at home, so use more."

- 来源：Tversky A, Kahneman D (1974). Judgment under Uncertainty: Heuristics and Biases. Science 185(4157):1124-1131. <https://doi.org/10.1126/science.185.4157.1124>
- 备注：原文"促销前七天最低成交价"的规定是中国法规（中国数据），美国以加州反虚假标价规定为准（待核实）。只囤这三种都满足的东西：保质期长，确定会用完，单价确实比以前的价格低。
  Note: The original's "lowest price in the seven days before the promotion" rule is Chinese regulation (China data); in the U.S., California's deceptive-pricing rules apply (to be verified). Only stock up when all three hold: long shelf life, certain to be used up, and a unit price truly below its past price.

### 28. 提前还房贷之前先做一道比较题，不要凭感觉 / Before Prepaying Your Mortgage, Do the Math First

- 说人话：提前还房贷，等于稳稳赚到你的房贷利率。所以只问一件事：你能长期稳定拿到的税后收益，比房贷利率高吗？高就别急着还，不高或说不准就还。等额本息前几年利息占得多，越早还省得越多。贷款机构的提前还款试算工具能直接算出数字。
  In plain terms: Prepaying the mortgage earns you your mortgage rate, risk-free. So ask one question: can you reliably earn more after tax elsewhere, long-term?   Benefit: The math: let r be your mortgage rate (per your contract), and g the after-tax annualized return of your best alternative investment. Prepaying earns a certain r. If g reliably exceeds r, keep the money invested. If g is below r or unknowable, prepaying locks in a risk-free r.

- 来源：全国银行间同业拆借中心受中国人民银行授权 (2026). 2026 年 8 月 20 日贷款市场报价利率（LPR）公告. <https://www.chinamoney.com.cn/chinese/rdgz/20260820/3399885.html>

  Note: Only the method here, no verdict. Reliably beating your mortgage rate with investments is rare. Either way, keep enough emergency cash first. Not investment advice.

### 43. 想退保就自己找保险公司办，别找第三方"代办退保" / Cancel a Policy Yourself with the Insurer; Don't Hire Third-Party "Cancellation Agents"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。自己打保险公司客服电话或者去官网办，比交给中介多跑一两趟。
  Cost: Nothing. Calling the insurer or using their website yourself takes one or two more steps than handing it to an agent.
- 说人话：网上喊"全额退保、不成功不收费"的，多半是收割：他们要拿走退回来的钱的三到五成，还要你的身份证、银行账户和验证码。退了保，保障也就没了。觉得被误导了，先找保险公司官方客服投诉协商，谈不拢向加州保险局（California Department of Insurance）投诉，全程免费。一时交不起保费，可以先问保险公司能不能办减额交清、保单贷款这类服务。
  In plain terms: Online ads screaming "full refund, no win no fee" are mostly harvesting operations: they take 30%–50% of whatever comes back, plus your ID, bank accounts, and verification codes. Once surrendered, the coverage is gone. If you were misled, complain to the insurer's official customer service first; if that fails, file with the California Department of Insurance — free throughout. If premiums are temporarily unaffordable, ask the insurer about reduced paid-up or policy-loan options first.
- 收益：自己办，一分中介费不花，个人信息不经过第三方。真被误导销售，官方投诉渠道比"代办"更可能要回公道。
  Benefit: Doing it yourself costs no agent fees and keeps personal data away from third parties. If you were genuinely mis-sold, official complaint channels beat "agents" at getting redress.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：金融监管总局、中央网信办、公安部、中国人民银行、中国证监会 (2026-02-06). 关于警惕不法「代理维权」短视频及直播陷阱的风险提示. <https://www.cac.gov.cn/2026-02/06/c_1772110875635198.htm>；California Department of Insurance, File a Complaint. <https://www.insurance.ca.gov/01-consumers/101-help/>
- 备注：原文的"代理退保"风险提示是中国监管部门发的（中国数据），"全额退保"话术、30% 到 50% 代理费、教人编造理由投诉这些套路，在美国同样存在。退不退要自己算：保障还用得上的，交不起就先问减额交清。自己在 free look 期里退，见第 41 条。
  Note: The original's "agency surrender" warnings came from Chinese regulators (China data); the playbook — "full refund" pitches, 30%–50% agent fees, coaching fake complaints — exists in the U.S. too. Decide yourself whether to surrender: if the coverage is still needed and premiums are tight, ask about reduced paid-up first. For free-look cancellation, see item 41.

### 44. 保单和退休账户上把受益人写成具体的人，出了事记得去领钱 / Name Specific Beneficiaries on Policies and Retirement Accounts — and Remember to Claim
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。投保或开户时填一次，以后改受益人按保险公司/券商的流程办。
  Cost: Nothing. Fill it in once when buying the policy or opening the account; update later per the insurer/broker's process.
- 说人话：寿险、401(k)、IRA 上的受益人指定，效力优先于遗嘱——写了谁，钱就给谁，写错了遗嘱救不回来。没写受益人，保险金就按遗产走，要先拿去还去世的人欠的债。受益人写了"妻子某某"，后来离婚了，出事时可能算没写。离婚、再婚、孩子出生以后，回头把受益人改一次。家里人至少要知道你在哪几家公司买过保险、在哪开的退休账户，否则没人去领，钱就躺着。
  In plain terms: Beneficiary designations on life insurance, 401(k)s, and IRAs override your will — whoever is named gets the money, and a will can't fix a wrong name. With no beneficiary named, the payout goes through the estate and first pays the deceased's debts. Name "my wife X," then divorce, and it may count as unnamed. After divorce, remarriage, or a child's birth, update beneficiaries. Family should at least know which insurers and brokerages hold your policies and accounts — otherwise nobody claims and the money sits.
- 收益：寿险保额通常在数十万美元级，401(k)/IRA 余额也常常是家里最大的一笔钱。写对受益人+家里人知道去哪领，等于把这笔钱真正交到家人手里。
  Benefit: Life-insurance payouts are typically in the hundreds of thousands of dollars, and 401(k)/IRA balances are often a family's largest asset. Correct beneficiaries plus family knowing where to claim is what actually delivers the money.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：全国人大常委会 (2015 修正). 中华人民共和国保险法第二十六、三十九、四十一、四十二条. <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/detail?id=2c909fdd678bf17901678bf7c4060811>
- 备注：原文的"寿险 5 年、其他保险 2 年"诉讼时效是中国保险法（中国数据），美国各州和各类保单的时效不同，以保单条款和所在州法律为准（待核实）。理赔要及时，别拖。离婚、再婚、孩子出生后记得更新受益人。
  Note: The original's "5 years for life insurance, 2 years for others" limitation periods are Chinese Insurance Law (China data); U.S. states and policy types differ — go by your policy and state law (to be verified). File claims promptly, don't delay. Update beneficiaries after divorce, remarriage, or a child's birth.

### 45. 加密货币合法但高风险：别碰"带单""稳赚"，任何要求用加密货币付款的"投资机会"基本都是骗局 / Crypto Is Legal but High-Risk: Avoid "Guaranteed" Schemes — Any "Investment" Demanding Crypto Payment Is Almost Certainly a Scam
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。不买币，不进"带单""稳赚"的币圈群。代价是币价上涨的时候赚不到。
  Cost: Nothing. Don't buy coins, don't join "copy-trading" or "guaranteed profit" crypto groups. The price is missing out when prices rise.
- 说人话：美国版和中国版完全不同：加密货币在美国合法，但它是高风险投机品，不是货币。记住三条。第一，波动极大，一天跌掉三四成历史上发生过多次，拿不住就别碰。第二，FTC 的警告口径：任何要求你用加密货币付款的"投资机会"，基本都是骗局；"老师带单、稳赚不赔"的群，99% 是杀猪盘。第三，税：IRS 把加密货币当财产（property），买卖、换币、消费都要报税，亏了也不能当没发生过。
  In plain terms: The U.S. version is the opposite of China's: crypto is legal in the U.S., but it's a high-risk speculation, not money. Remember three things. First, extreme volatility — 30%–40% single-day drops have happened repeatedly; if you can't stomach it, stay out. Second, the FTC's warning: any "investment opportunity" demanding crypto payment is basically a scam; "guru-led, guaranteed profit" groups are pig-butchering scams 99% of the time. Third, taxes: the IRS treats crypto as property — trades, swaps, and spending are all taxable events, and losses don't vanish by ignoring them.
- 收益：不碰，等于一次性避开波动亏损和几乎所有的币圈骗局。真想了解，先拿亏得起的钱、走合规交易所，并且做好这笔钱归零的准备。
  Benefit: Staying out dodges both volatility losses and nearly all crypto scams in one move. If you truly want exposure, use only money you can afford to lose, stick to compliant exchanges, and be prepared for it to go to zero.

- 备注：原书基于中国监管口径：中国禁止虚拟货币相关业务活动，个人投资损失自担、交易不受法律保护（中国数据）。加州版改为美国口径：合法但高风险。不构成投资建议。
