# 1. 不要早死 / Don't Die Early

这一节讲三件事：车祸、火灾、中毒这类意外和伤害死亡；少数几种证据最硬的疫苗和筛查；身体出了什么信号就该去查一查。收益那一栏的数字，来自随机分组的试验、跟踪一大批人很多年的研究，或者官方统计。成本那一栏是作者按市面价格的粗略估计，只用来互相比较，不要拿去引用。

This section covers three things: deaths from accidents and injuries like car crashes, fires, and poisoning; the few vaccines and screenings with the strongest evidence; and which bodily warning signs mean you should get checked. The numbers in the Benefit line come from randomized trials, long-term cohort studies, or official statistics. The Cost line is a rough estimate at market prices, only for comparing items against each other—don't cite it.

### 22. 50 岁以上打肺炎球菌疫苗 / Get the pneumococcal vaccine from 50
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：通常一次接种，多为保险覆盖。
  Cost: Usually a single vaccination, typically covered by insurance.
- 说人话：8.45 万老人的试验里，肺炎球菌疫苗把疫苗管得住的那几型肺炎挡掉约 46%，把最凶险的侵袭性感染挡掉 75%。但对「所有原因的肺炎」没看出效果，别指望它管住全部肺炎。
  In plain terms: In a trial of 84,500 older adults, the pneumococcal vaccine blocked about 46% of pneumonia caused by the covered strains and 75% of the most dangerous invasive infections. It showed no effect on all-cause pneumonia—don't expect it to prevent every pneumonia.
- 收益：荷兰把 8.45 万名 65 岁以上老人随机分成两组来比。用的是 13 价肺炎球菌结合疫苗。它对疫苗覆盖型肺炎的效力是 45.6%。可信范围是 95.2% CI 21.8–62.5。疫苗覆盖型就是这支疫苗管得住的那几个菌型。对侵袭性肺炎球菌病的效力是 75.0%（95% CI 41.4–90.8），这是最凶险的那一类感染。对全部原因加在一起的肺炎，它没有效果
  Benefit: A Dutch trial randomized 84,500 adults 65+ to the 13-valent pneumococcal conjugate vaccine or placebo: 45.6% efficacy against vaccine-type pneumonia (95.2% CI 21.8–62.5) and 75.0% against invasive pneumococcal disease—the most dangerous kind (95% CI 41.4–90.8). No effect on all-cause pneumonia.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Bonten MJ, Huijts SM, Bolkenbaas M, et al. (2015). Polysaccharide conjugate vaccine against pneumococcal pneumonia in adults. New England Journal of Medicine, 372(12), 1114-1125. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1408544>
- 备注：它只防疫苗覆盖的那几个型别，不是打了就不得肺炎。试验用的是 13 价结合疫苗，美国现在用的具体价型和接种程序以 CDC/ACIP 最新口径为准，待核实。
  Note: It only prevents disease from the covered strains—vaccination doesn't mean no more pneumonia. The trial used the 13-valent conjugate vaccine; the specific formulations and schedule now used in the US follow the latest CDC/ACIP guidance, to be verified.

### 23. 查幽门螺杆菌，阳性就根除 / Test for H. pylori; eradicate it if positive
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：碳 13 或碳 14 呼气试验约 100 到 200 美元，保险覆盖情况不一。根除要同时吃几种药，两周药费约几十到上百美元。难的地方是这两周的药要按点吃满。
  Cost: A carbon-13 or carbon-14 breath test costs roughly $100–200, with variable insurance coverage. Eradication takes several drugs together for two weeks, costing tens to a hundred dollars. The hard part is taking every dose on schedule for the full two weeks.
- 说人话：山东临朐那项跟踪了 22 年的试验里，把幽门螺杆菌根除掉的人，后来得胃癌的概率低约一半，死于胃癌的概率低约四成。查一次一两百块，根除要吃两周药。
  In plain terms: In the 22-year Linqu, Shandong trial, people who eradicated H. pylori had about half the stomach-cancer risk and about 40% lower stomach-cancer death risk. The test costs a couple hundred dollars; eradication is two weeks of pills.
- 收益：山东临朐那项随机分组的试验跟踪了 22 年。做过幽门螺杆菌根除治疗的人，得胃癌的风险低约一半。原值 OR 0.48，可信范围 95% CI 0.32 到 0.71。死于胃癌的风险低约 38%（HR 0.62，95% CI 0.39 到 0.99）
  Benefit: The randomized Linqu trial followed participants for 22 years: H. pylori eradication cut stomach-cancer risk by about half (OR 0.48, 95% CI 0.32–0.71) and stomach-cancer death risk by about 38% (HR 0.62, 95% CI 0.39–0.99).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Li WQ 等 (2019). Effects of Helicobacter pylori treatment and vitamin and garlic supplementation on gastric cancer incidence and mortality: follow-up of a randomized intervention trial. BMJ. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.l5016>
- 备注：根除以后还可能再感染，所以家里人一起查、一起治。这项试验的人群是胃癌高发区的居民。住在低发区的人，实际拿到的好处会小得多。
  Note: Reinfection is possible after eradication, so have the whole household tested and treated together. The trial population lived in a high stomach-cancer-risk region; people in low-risk areas get substantially less benefit.

### 24. 高危吸烟者每年做一次低剂量胸部 CT / Annual low-dose chest CT for high-risk smokers
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一次约 100 到 300 美元，10 分钟做完，符合筛查条件的多为保险覆盖。另外要算上虚惊一场之后的复查，和那段时间的提心吊胆。
  Cost: Roughly $100–300 per scan, 10 minutes, usually covered by insurance when you meet screening criteria. Plus the follow-ups after false alarms and the anxiety in between.
- 说人话：符合条件的重度吸烟者每年做一次低剂量 CT，死于肺癌的概率比只拍胸片低 20%，总的死亡概率低 6.7%。代价是查出可疑结果后的复查，和那一段时间的提心吊胆。
  In plain terms: High-risk heavy smokers who get a yearly low-dose CT have 20% lower lung-cancer death risk and 6.7% lower total mortality than those getting only chest X-rays. The price is follow-up scans after suspicious findings and the worry in between.
- 收益：美国 NLST 随机试验收了 53,454 人，都是 55 到 74 岁、吸烟史 30 包年以上、戒烟不超过 15 年的人。包年是算烟量的单位，每天一包抽一年算一包年。结果是这样。做低剂量 CT 的人比只拍胸片的人，肺癌死亡相对下降 20.0%。可信范围 95% CI 6.8 到 26.7。总死亡率相对下降 6.7%，可信范围 95% CI 1.2 到 13.6
  Benefit: The US NLST randomized trial enrolled 53,454 people aged 55–74 with 30+ pack-years of smoking who had quit less than 15 years earlier (a pack-year = one pack a day for a year). The low-dose CT group had 20.0% lower lung-cancer mortality than the chest-X-ray group (95% CI 6.8–26.7) and 6.7% lower total mortality (95% CI 1.2–13.6).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：National Lung Screening Trial Research Team (2011). Reduced Lung-Cancer Mortality with Low-Dose Computed Tomographic Screening. New England Journal of Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1102873>
- 备注：这条只适用于高危吸烟者。同一项试验里，做低剂量 CT 的人有 24.2% 查出可疑结果，这些人里 96.4% 是虚惊一场（假阳性）。不吸烟的人做这项检查，等于花钱买焦虑。美国 USPSTF 的现行筛查条件是 50 到 80 岁、吸烟史 20 包年以上、现在仍在吸或戒烟不到 15 年（口径待核实）。
  Note: This applies only to high-risk smokers. In the same trial, 24.2% of the CT group had suspicious findings, 96.4% of which were false alarms. For non-smokers this scan is just paid-for anxiety. The current USPSTF screening criteria are ages 50–80, 20+ pack-years, currently smoking or quit less than 15 years ago (wording to be verified).

### 25. 抑郁或有自杀念头时打 988，家里不囤安眠药和农药 / Call 988 when depressed or suicidal; don't stockpile sleeping pills or pesticides
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。打一个电话。另外把药锁起来，农药不放在家里。
  Cost: Free. One phone call. Plus locking up medications and keeping pesticides out of the house.
- 说人话：把能要命的东西从手边拿走，这类做法的证据一直在变强：管住镇痛药以后，相关自杀降 43%；常有人跳楼的地方装上防护，降 86%。所以家里不囤安眠药和农药。想不开的时候先打 988，拨打或发短信都行，全天 24 小时有人接。
  In plain terms: Removing lethal means from reach has steadily strengthening evidence: restricting painkillers cut related suicides by 43%; barriers at common jumping sites cut them by 86%. So don't stockpile sleeping pills or pesticides at home. When things feel unbearable, call or text 988 first—answered around the clock.
- 收益：一篇把近 10 年的研究系统梳理了一遍的综述：限制人拿到致死手段，这方面的证据一直在增强。管控镇痛药以后，和镇痛药有关的自杀下降 43%。常有人跳楼的地点加了防护之后，下降 86%（79% 到 91%，括号里是这个数的可信范围）。学校里开展的认知类项目，让自杀未遂低约 55%（OR 0.45，95% CI 0.24 到 0.85）。治抑郁的药物和心理治疗，是预防自杀的重要一环。在美国，988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline 拨打或短信 988 全天可用
  Benefit: A systematic review of a decade of research: the evidence for restricting access to lethal means keeps strengthening. Restricting painkillers cut related suicides by 43%; barriers at common jumping sites cut them by 86% (95% CI 79%–91%). School-based cognitive programs cut suicide attempts by about 55% (OR 0.45, 95% CI 0.24–0.85). Medication and psychotherapy for depression are key parts of suicide prevention. In the US, the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline is reachable by call or text, 24/7.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Zalsman G 等 (2016). Suicide prevention strategies revisited: 10-year systematic review. Lancet Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S2215-0366(16)30030-X>
- 备注：给 B 的原因：限制手段那边的证据多数是生态学研究，做法是比较政策出台前后的数字；热线本身没有降低死亡率的证据。自杀的冲动往往只持续几分钟到几小时，把能致死的东西挪远一步，本身就是干预。这个时间尺度的数据，以及未遂之后的长期结局，见本节第 32 条（自杀念头一冒出来先告诉身边的一个人）。被救回来之后会留下什么，见本节第 33 条。
  Note: Grade B because most means-restriction evidence is ecological (before-after policy comparisons), and hotlines themselves have no mortality-reduction evidence. Suicidal urges often last only minutes to hours—moving lethal means one step further away is itself an intervention. For the time scale and long-term outcomes after an attempt, see Item 32 (tell someone the moment suicidal thoughts appear). For what survives an attempt leaves behind, see Item 33.

### 26. 备齐灭火器、灭火毯、逃生呼吸面罩和急救包，每年检查一次 / Keep extinguishers, a fire blanket, escape hoods, and a first-aid kit; check yearly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：灭火器、灭火毯、每人一只逃生面罩，再加一个急救包，一套约 50 到 150 美元。买一次就行，以后每年花十分钟检查一遍。
  Cost: An extinguisher, a fire blanket, one escape hood per person, plus a first-aid kit—roughly $50–150 for the set. Buy once, then spend ten minutes checking everything each year.
- 说人话：灭火器、灭火毯、逃生面罩、急救包，都在美国红十字会和 Ready.gov 的家庭应急清单思路里。一套一两百美元，买一次就行。上门做过家庭安全指导的那些研究里，伤害发生率低约四分之一。
  In plain terms: Extinguishers, fire blankets, escape hoods, and first-aid kits are all in the spirit of the American Red Cross and Ready.gov home emergency checklists. The set costs one to two hundred dollars, bought once. Studies of home safety guidance visits found injury rates about a quarter lower.
- 收益：Cochrane 把 98 项研究、2,605,044 人合起来一起算。家庭安全干预让伤害发生率降到 IRR 0.89。可信范围是 95% CI 0.78 到 1.01。其中上门开展的干预，伤害发生率低约 25%（IRR 0.75，95% CI 0.62 到 0.91）
  Benefit: A Cochrane review pooled 98 studies with 2,605,044 people: home safety interventions brought injury rates to IRR 0.89 (95% CI 0.78–1.01), and in-home interventions cut them by about 25% (IRR 0.75, 95% CI 0.62–0.91).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Kendrick D 等 (2012). Home safety education and provision of safety equipment for injury prevention. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD005014.pub3>
- 备注：争议。同一份 Cochrane 汇总里，不发装备的那一类干预，受伤率低约两成。原值 IRR 0.78，可信范围 95% CI 0.66 到 0.92。发了装备的那一组并没有做得更好。降低伤害的主要是入户教育和改造，所以装备的收益记「中」，不记「大」。买灭火器认准 UL 认证标志，贮压式的指针要停在绿区。烟雾报警器和一氧化碳报警器见本节第 3 条。止血带的用法见第 13 节第 12 条。
  Note: Controversy. In the same Cochrane review, interventions without equipment distribution cut injury rates by about 20% (IRR 0.78, 95% CI 0.66–0.92)—the equipment group did no better. The injury reduction came mainly from in-home education and modification, so equipment gets a "medium" benefit, not "large." Buy extinguishers with a UL certification mark; the gauge needle on a stored-pressure unit should sit in the green zone. For smoke and CO alarms see Item 3; for tourniquet use see Section 13, Item 12.

### 27. 尿里出现肉眼看得见的血，哪怕不疼、哪怕第二天就干净了，也要去查一次 / Visible blood in your urine? Get checked—even if painless, even if it clears up the next day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：尿常规约 20 到 50 美元，泌尿系超声一两百美元。医生认为需要的话，再加一个膀胱镜，约上千美元。多为保险覆盖。
  Cost: A urinalysis costs roughly $20–50, a urinary-tract ultrasound a couple hundred. If the doctor wants a cystoscopy, around a thousand dollars. Usually covered by insurance.
- 说人话：60 岁以上的人出现一次肉眼看得见的血尿，最后查出膀胱癌的概率约 2.8%，40 到 59 岁约 1.2%。这个数不算高，但也高到必须去查一次。哪怕第二天不出血了也要去查。
  In plain terms: For people 60+, one episode of visible blood in the urine turns out to be bladder cancer about 2.8% of the time; 1.2% at ages 40–59. Not high, but high enough to warrant one workup—even if the bleeding stops the next day.
- 收益：英国拿社区全科门诊（初级医疗）的电子病历做了一项研究，4915 例膀胱癌对 21718 例对照。出现肉眼血尿的人里，最后真查出膀胱癌的比例是这样。60 岁以上是 2.8%，可信范围 95% CI 2.5 到 3.1。40 到 59 岁是 1.2%，可信范围 0.6 到 2.3。这个比例的学名叫阳性预测值。只有在显微镜下才看得见的血尿，同样有意义。比值比 20，可信范围 95% CI 12 到 33。60 岁以上这个比例为 1.6%，可信范围 1.2 到 2.1
  Benefit: A UK study using community primary-care records compared 4,915 bladder-cancer cases with 21,718 controls. Among people with visible blood in the urine, the share ultimately diagnosed with bladder cancer (the positive predictive value) was 2.8% at 60+ (95% CI 2.5–3.1) and 1.2% at 40–59 (95% CI 0.6–2.3). Microscopic-only hematuria mattered too: odds ratio 20 (95% CI 12–33), with a 1.6% predictive value at 60+ (95% CI 1.2–2.1).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Price SJ, Shephard EA, Stapley SA, Barraclough K, Hamilton WT (2014). Non-visible versus visible haematuria and bladder cancer risk: a study of electronic records in primary care. British Journal of General Practice, 64(626), e584-e589. <https://doi.org/10.3399/bjgp14X681409>
- 备注：「不疼」是关键。结石和感染引起的血尿，多半同时腰腹绞痛，或者尿频、尿急、尿痛。肿瘤引起的往往一点感觉都没有，而且会自己停几天再出现，很多人就这么放过了。正在吃抗凝药，不是可以不查的理由。剧烈运动之后、女性经期前后的血尿，可以先复查一次再定。收益记「小」，是因为只有检出率，没有「早查能降低多少死亡」的效应量。
  Note: "Painless" is the key. Bloody urine from stones or infection usually comes with flank or abdominal cramps, or frequent, urgent, painful urination. Tumor-related bleeding often has no feeling at all and stops for days before returning—many people let it go. Being on blood thinners is no excuse to skip the workup. Blood after intense exercise or around menstruation can be rechecked once first. Benefit is rated "small" because there's only a detection rate, no effect size for "how much early checking reduces death."

### 28. 勃起功能出了问题，先去查心血管，别当成单纯的「那方面的事」 / Erectile trouble? Check your heart first—don't treat it as just a bedroom issue
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：去 primary care 看一次。量血压、抽血查血糖和血脂，约一两百美元，多为保险覆盖。跑一趟，等几天结果。
  Cost: One primary-care visit. Blood pressure, blood glucose, and lipids cost roughly one to two hundred dollars, usually covered by insurance. One trip, results in a few days.
- 说人话：有勃起功能障碍的男性，后来得心血管病的比别人多约四成，心梗多约六成。越年轻的人差得越多。这个毛病常常比胸痛先出现。所以先去查血压、血糖、血脂，别自己买药压过去。
  In plain terms: Men with erectile dysfunction go on to have about 40% more cardiovascular disease and 60% more heart attacks—and the younger the man, the bigger the gap. Cardiovascular death didn't separate (RR 1.19, 0.97–1.46). The younger the man, the bigger the multiple.

  Note: All 14 studies were cohorts, not randomized trials—so the "40% more" is association, not causation. An occasional episode doesn't count; it becomes a problem when it persists for 3+ months. The workup is the same batch as Items 7 and 8: blood pressure, fasting glucose, plus lipids, smoking, and waistline. Don't buy "male enhancement" supplements online. The legitimate drugs are prescription-only, and their labels list nitrates as a contraindication—never combine them with nitroglycerin-type drugs, which can crash blood pressure. For improvement see Item 29. Benefit is rated "medium": 44% is a between-group risk gap, not "deaths avoided by one checkup"—but unlike Item 27's signal, the hypertension, diabetes, and lipid problems found here all have ready treatments.

### 29. 减重、戒烟、把血压血糖控住，勃起功能能跟着好转 / Lose weight, quit smoking, control BP and glucose—erections improve too
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，还可能省钱。难的是长期坚持：管住吃、动起来、把烟戒掉。

  Benefit: A meta-analysis pooled 6 randomized trials with 740 men, with lifestyle interventions or medication for cardiovascular risk factors and at least 6 weeks of follow-up. IIEF-5 questionnaire scores rose by an average of 2.66 points (95% CI 1.86–3.47); excluding the 2 statin trials (143 men), the 4 lifestyle-only trials (597 men) still gained 2.40 points (1.19–3.61). The IIEF-5 is a 5-question survey scored 5–25, with 22–25 meaning no dysfunction and 5–7 severe.

### 30. 性行为全程用安全套，不和别人共用针具 / Use a condom every time, from start to finish; never share needles
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一只约一两美元。难的地方不在钱，在于每一次都用，而且从一开始就戴上。
  Cost: About one or two dollars each. The hard part isn't money—it's using one every time, from the very start.
- 说人话：每一次都全程戴，异性之间传染艾滋病的概率大约降七成以上。「大部分时候戴」的效果，和不戴差不了多少。梅毒、淋病这些也一并挡住。
  In plain terms: Consistent, from-start-to-finish use cuts heterosexual HIV transmission by over 70%. "Most of the time" works barely better than never. Syphilis, gonorrhea, and the rest get blocked too.
- 收益：一项研究把 25 项研究、10676 对异性伴侣合起来一起算。这些伴侣都是一方感染 HIV、一方没感染，学名叫血清不一致。和从来不用的人相比，坚持每次都用安全套的人，HIV 传播风险低约 71%。原值 RR 0.29，可信范围 95% CI 0.20 至 0.43。和用得断断续续的人相比，低约 77%（RR 0.23，95% CI 0.13 至 0.40）。作者的结论是，安全套虽然不完美，但「坚持使用可使 HIV 传播降低 70% 以上」。同一层保护还覆盖梅毒、淋病、衣原体等其他性传播感染
  Benefit: One analysis pooled 25 studies with 10,676 heterosexual serodiscordant couples (one partner HIV-positive, one not). Compared with never-users, consistent condom users had about 71% lower HIV transmission risk (RR 0.29, 95% CI 0.20–0.43); compared with inconsistent users, about 77% lower (RR 0.23, 95% CI 0.13–0.40). The authors concluded condoms are imperfect but "consistent use reduces HIV transmission by over 70%." The same protection covers syphilis, gonorrhea, chlamydia, and other STIs.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Giannou FK, Tsiara CG, Nikolopoulos GK, et al. (2016). Expert Review of Pharmacoeconomics & Outcomes Research, 16(4), 489-499. <https://doi.org/10.1586/14737167.2016.1102635>；Fonner VA 等 (2016). - 备注：「不规律使用」和「不用」的差距很小，要每次、全程都用才有效。共用针具是另一条独立的高效传播途径，和性行为无关，注射毒品、纹身、穿孔都算。高风险人群还可以用暴露前预防（PrEP），就是事先吃药挡住感染。把 18 项研究合起来算，凡是超过七成人按时吃药的试验，吃 PrEP 的人感染 HIV 的风险比吃安慰剂的低约七成。原值是风险比 0.30，可信范围 95% CI 0.21 至 0.45。按时吃药的人少的那些试验，则看不到保护效果。PrEP 是处方药，去感染科问，别自己网购。已经暴露之后怎么办见第 13 节。
  Note: "Inconsistent use" differs little from no use—only every-time, start-to-finish use works. Sharing needles is a separate, highly efficient transmission route, unrelated to sex: injecting drugs, tattoos, and piercings all count. High-risk people can also use pre-exposure prophylaxis (PrEP)—medication taken beforehand to block infection. Pooling 18 studies, in trials where over 70% took it as directed, PrEP users had about 70% lower HIV risk than placebo (risk ratio 0.30, 95% CI 0.21–0.45); trials with poor adherence showed no protection. <!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。多数县卫生部门和社区诊所提供免费保密检测，抽一管血。难的地方是开口去查这一步。

### 32. 自杀念头一冒出来先告诉身边的一个人，把这几十分钟交出去 / The moment suicidal thoughts appear, tell someone nearby—hand over the next few dozen minutes
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的地方是当时开口，以及承认自己这几个小时不能一个人待着。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is speaking up in the moment and admitting you can't be alone for the next few hours.
- 说人话：自杀未遂的人里将近一半说，从念头冒出来到动手不超过 10 分钟，所以要熬过去的往往就是这几十分钟。四分之三的人那段时间和身边人有过接触，只是没开口。因自伤进过医院的人，1 年后约 2% 死于自杀，9 年以上约 7%，绝大多数人后来没有死于自杀。
  In plain terms: Nearly half of suicide attempters say less than 10 minutes passed between the first thought and the act—so what you have to survive is often just those few dozen minutes. Of people hospitalized for self-harm, about 2% die by suicide within a year and about 7% after 9+ years—the vast majority do not.
- 收益：82 名自杀未遂者在入院 3 天内接受访谈：47.6%（39 人）说，从这一次第一次冒出自杀念头到真的动手，不超过 10 分钟。这个过程拖得长一些的人，自杀意图评分明显更高。冲动性量表的得分和过程长短没有关系。76.8%（63 人）说，在这段时间里和伴侣、家人或者朋友有过某种接触。作者的结论是：能干预的时间窗通常很短，专业人员在这个阶段能介入的余地有限，所以要把识别信号和干预办法普及到普通人。另有一篇系统综述汇总了 90 项随访研究。因自伤就诊的人，1 年后再次自伤但没死的比例取中间值为 16%。1 年后死于自杀的为 2%。随访 9 年以上的，约 7% 死于自杀
  Benefit: 82 suicide attempters were interviewed within 3 days of admission: 47.6% (39 people) said less than 10 minutes passed between the first thought and the act. Longer intervals correlated with higher suicidal-intent scores; impulsivity scores didn't correlate with interval length. 76.8% (63 people) reported some contact with a partner, family member, or friend during that window. The authors concluded the intervention window is usually short with limited room for professionals, so recognizing warning signs and knowing how to intervene must be taught to ordinary people. A separate systematic review of 90 follow-up studies found a median 16% repeat non-fatal self-harm at one year, 2% suicide death at one year, and about 7% suicide death with 9+ years of follow-up.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Deisenhammer EA, Ing CM, Strauss R, Kemmler G, Hinterhuber H, Weiss EM (2009). - 备注：给 B 的原因：Deisenhammer 那项只有 82 人，出自一家医院，靠当事人事后回忆。Owens 那篇给的是 90 项做法各异的研究取中间的那个比例，不是把它们合成一个数。同一篇里还明确写着，自伤者的自杀风险仍是普通人群的几百倍。所以「绝大多数人后来没有死于自杀」说的是这批人整体的情况，不能当成对某一个人的保证，该看的病还是要看。把致死手段挪远和 988 见本节第 25 条。情绪低落时先做的几件事见第 3 节第 18 条。救回来之后的后遗症见本节第 33 条。身边人流露出这种念头时你能做什么，见第 8 节第 15 条。
  Note: Grade B because the Deisenhammer study had only 82 people from one hospital relying on retrospective recall, and Owens reported a median across 90 heterogeneous studies rather than a pooled estimate. ### 33. 别把「救回来」当成兜底：喝农药、吸入一氧化碳之后急诊能保住的是命，保不住肺和脑子 / Don't count on being "saved": after pesticide or carbon monoxide poisoning, the ER can save your life, not your lungs or brain
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。只是先知道这件事。
  Cost: Free. Just knowing this in advance.
- 说人话：百草枯没有解药，喝下去的人四成多救不回来。它会让肺发炎、慢慢变硬，肾和肝也跟着受伤。一氧化碳中毒被救回来的人，六周后将近一半脑子不如从前，做了高压氧的也还有四分之一。

### 38. 有高危性行为的人去问暴露前预防药（PrEP），按时吃能少感染七成以上 / At high risk? Ask about PrEP—taken as directed, it cuts infections by over 70%

- 成本：这是处方药，无保险每月自费上千美元，多数保险覆盖。开药前要查 HIV、肾功能和乙肝。之后每 3 个月复查一次。难的是天天按时吃。

### 41. 上野外冰面前先打孔量厚度，不到 10 厘米别上，河上的冰和冰钓洞边别去 / Before going on wild ice, drill and measure—stay off under 4 inches; avoid river ice and fishing holes

- 成本：最省事的是去有人管的正规冰场，不用多花钱。非要上野外的冰，就带冰钻或冰镩打孔量厚度。再买一对救生冰锥，就是两根带握柄的尖钉，挂在胸前。这些加起来约 10 到 30 美元。每走一段量一次，要多花些时间。
  Cost: Easiest is a supervised, maintained rink—no extra cost. If you insist on wild ice, bring an ice auger or chisel to drill and measure, plus a pair of ice picks (two gripped spikes worn on a cord around your neck): roughly $10–30 total. Measuring every stretch takes extra time.
- 说人话：美国和加拿大的底线是：新结的透明冰至少 4 英寸（约 10 厘米）才能步行和冰钓。发白的冰结实程度只有一半，厚度要翻倍。同一片冰，这里一尺厚，几步外可能只有一两寸，所以要边走边量。
  In plain terms: The US and Canadian baseline: new, clear ice must be at least 4 inches (~10 cm) for walking and ice fishing. White ice is only half as strong—double the thickness. On the same lake, ice can be a foot thick here and an inch or two a few steps away, so measure as you go.
- 收益：美国明尼苏达州自然资源部的指南只针对新结的透明冰：不到 4 英寸（约 10 厘米）别上，4 英寸可以步行和冰钓。积雪化了又冻上的白冰，结实程度只有透明冰的一半左右，厚度要按两倍算。同一片水面，冰可能这里一英尺厚，几英尺外只有一两英寸。流水上的冰通常危险，溪流、桥和涵洞附近尤其如此。岸边的冰也可能比远处薄。美国国家气象局给的步行、冰钓、滑冰门槛也是 4 英寸。加拿大职业健康与安全中心转述加拿大红十字会的建议：几个人一起滑冰、步行要 15 厘米，人多的滑冰聚会要 20 厘米。这些都是安全指南，没有研究算过照着做能少死多少人（美国、加拿大）
  Benefit: The Minnesota Department of Natural Resources guidelines cover only new, clear ice: stay off under 4 inches (~10 cm); 4 inches supports walking and ice fishing. White ice from refrozen snow is about half as strong—double the thickness. On the same water, ice can be a foot thick in one spot and an inch or two a few feet away. Ice over moving water is usually dangerous, especially near streams, bridges, and culverts; shore ice can be thinner than ice farther out. The National Weather Service also sets 4 inches for walking, ice fishing, and skating. Canada's Centre for Occupational Health and Safety, citing the Canadian Red Cross, recommends 15 cm for small-group skating or walking and 20 cm for large skating parties. These are safety guidelines—no study has calculated how many deaths they prevent (US, Canada).

The beneficiaries are mainly yourself and the kids you bring along.

### 42. 装好护罩、戴上护目镜加面屏再用角磨机，双手握稳，别装木工锯片切木头 / Guards on, goggles plus face shield, both hands on the grinder—never put a wood saw blade on it

- 成本：一副护目镜加一个面屏，十几到几十美元。每次开机前看一眼护罩和片子，换片前拔掉插头，多花一两分钟。难在嫌麻烦时也不拆护罩、不单手干活。
  Cost: Goggles plus a face shield cost ten to tens of dollars. Check the guard and disc before each use, unplug before changing discs—an extra minute or two. The hard part is never removing the guard or one-handing it, even when it's a hassle.
- 说人话：角磨机转得极快，片子碎了会飞出来伤人，装上木工锯片还会突然反弹。护罩、护目镜加面屏、双手握稳，这几样花不了几个钱。
  In plain terms: Angle grinders spin extremely fast—a shattered disc becomes shrapnel, and a wood saw blade can kick back violently. Guard, goggles plus face shield, two hands on the handles: all cheap.
- 收益：加拿大职业健康与安全中心和澳大利亚昆士兰州的安全机构列的做法如下。开机前检查护罩和片子，有裂纹、缺口、变形的不用。片子上标的最高转速，不能低于机器上标的转速。片子的直径和中间的孔要和机器配套。换片前断开电源。要磨要切的东西先固定住。戴防护眼镜或护目镜，外面再加面屏。双手握住两个手柄，脚下站稳。切割片不能拿来打磨，那样片子更容易碎。片子完全停转之前不把机器放下。火花和碎屑朝向没人的地方，周围不放易燃的东西。昆士兰的通报说，切割片边缘的设计速度约为每秒 70 米。把 356 毫米的大片装到 230 毫米的机器上，边缘速度至少到每秒 120 米，片子容易碎。2016 年 2 月，当地一名工人用 230 毫米的角磨机时被碎片击中胸部，内伤死亡。国内有两份事故调查报告。2019 年哈尔滨一名工人把切割片换成带齿锯片，没装护罩就去切木板。锯片卷住裤子，割断大腿动脉，失血死亡。2024 年深圳福田一名工人用装着木工锯片、没装护罩和辅助手柄的角磨机切木门框。机器反弹，割破颈动脉死亡（加拿大、澳大利亚 / 中国案例）。韩国 2011 到 2018 年的急诊伤害监测里，被高速旋转的切割工具弄伤的有 8697 人次，其中 4603 人次在用砂轮机这一类工具。他们伤在手上的占 46.4%，伤在头部的占 23.0%。和其他同类工具比，砂轮机伤成重伤的可能高约一倍（OR 2.07，95% CI 1.88–2.29）。四川绵阳一家医院两年收治 36 名角磨机伤眼的住院病人。手术后戴上眼镜的最好视力仍低于 0.05 的有 10 人（中国人群数据）。土耳其一家医院一年的 742 例手和前臂急性外伤里，角磨机造成 82 例。除 8 例外都不是上班时伤的，44% 是退休的人。日本一家创伤中心 2017 到 2018 年的 15 名角磨机伤者里，9 人是装上锯片切木头这类错用。其中 2 人整根手指断掉，1 人断了一截

At a Japanese trauma center, 9 of 15 grinder injuries in 2017–2018 were misuse like cutting wood with a saw blade; 2 lost whole fingers, 1 lost part of a finger.
