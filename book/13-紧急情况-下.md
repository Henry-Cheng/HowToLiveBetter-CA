# 13. 紧急情况：先做什么 / Emergencies: What to Do First

本节讲出了事先做什么、再做什么、什么不要做。急救和野外的条目算的是特定死因和活下来的比例。威胁和骗局的条目算的是钱和人身自由。两类数字不互相换算。条目的顺序按「这事多常见 × 做对做错差多少条命」从高到低排。这些条目首先是给你自己和配偶、直系亲属用的。心脏骤停有 79.2% 发生在家里（中国人群数据），你最可能救的就是家里人。对朋友同事次之。对陌生人也不是一点好处没有，但这一档最低。所以这类条目把保护自己的动作和免责的条款（加州好撒玛利亚人法）写在前面。

This section covers what to do first, what to do next, and what not to do. The first-aid and wilderness items are scored on specific causes of death and survival odds; the threat-and-scam items are scored on money and personal safety. The two kinds of numbers don't convert into each other. Items are ordered by "how common × how many lives the right move saves versus the wrong one," highest first. These items are first for you, your spouse, and your immediate family—79.2% of cardiac arrests happen at home (data from a Chinese population), so the person you're most likely to save is family. Friends and coworkers come next. Strangers aren't zero benefit, but they're the lowest tier. So these items put self-protection and liability protection (California's Good Samaritan law) up front.

本节只收几分钟内要做的动作。认出骗局、被人拿把柄要钱，处置要按天算，不在这一节。反诈的硬规则和七种高发骗局见第 8 节第 3 条（反诈硬规则）。被人拿隐私照片、裸聊录像要钱见第 8 节第 32 条（对方拿把柄来要钱）。钱已经转出去了，立刻打 911，同时联系银行要求冻结，并到 FTC 的 ReportFraud.ftc.gov 报案，见第 8 节第 2 条（止付）。

This section only covers actions for the first few minutes. Spotting scams or being blackmailed plays out over days, not here. Hard anti-fraud rules and the seven most common scams: Section 8, item 3. Someone demanding money with your private photos or a recorded video chat: Section 8, item 32. If money has already been sent, call 911 right away, contact your bank to freeze the transfer, and file a report with the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov—see Section 8, item 2.

本节条目按主题分成下面几块，括号里是条号。

### 23. 中暑的人只要清醒，就少量多次喝凉的淡盐水或运动饮料，别猛灌、别喝酒和高糖饮料 / Heat Illness, Awake Only: Sip Cool Lightly Salted Water or Sports Drinks, Small Amounts Often—No Gulping, No Alcohol or Sugary Drinks
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。一瓶运动饮料几美元。
  Cost: Free. A sports drink costs a few dollars.
- 说人话：只有清醒的人才喝。凉的淡盐水或运动饮料少量多次喝，一小时 500 到 1000 毫升。别猛灌，吐出来呛进气管更要命。不喝酒和高糖饮料，它们会让人丢更多水。先用凉水擦身、冰袋敷脖子腋下大腿根降温，再补水。意识不清、说话含糊的，一口都不喂。
  In plain terms: Only someone awake drinks. Cool lightly salted water or sports drinks, small amounts often—500 to 1,000 mL per hour. Don't gulp; vomiting and aspirating is deadlier. No alcohol or high-sugar drinks—they make you lose more water. Cool the body first (cool-water sponging, ice packs on neck, armpits, groin), then rehydrate. Unconscious or slurring: not a drop.
- 收益：中国疾控中心《公众高温中暑预防与紧急处理指南（2014 版）》写着「至少每小时喝 2~4 杯凉水（500~1000ml），水温不宜过高，饮水应少量多次」。还写着「不要饮用含酒精或大量糖分的饮料。这些饮料会导致失去更多的体液」。对热射病则明确写「不要给病人喝水」。美国 NIOSH：热衰竭要「鼓励小口频繁地喝凉水」；热痉挛每 15 到 20 分钟补一次含糖和电解质的运动饮料，「不要用盐片」。
  Benefit: China's CDC public heatstroke guideline (2014) says "drink at least 2–4 cups of cool water per hour (500–1,000 mL), not too cold, in small amounts often," and "don't drink alcohol or high-sugar beverages—they cause more fluid loss." For heatstroke it states plainly: "do not give the patient water." US NIOSH: for heat exhaustion "encourage frequent small sips of cool water"; for heat cramps, a sugary electrolyte sports drink every 15 to 20 minutes, "do not use salt tablets."
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：中国疾病预防控制中心职业卫生与中毒控制所 (2014). 公众高温中暑预防与紧急处理指南（2014 版）. <https://niohp.chinacdc.cn/sndt/201405/t20140530_97623.htm> ; US CDC/NIOSH. Heat-Related Illnesses. <https://www.cdc.gov/niosh/heat-stress/about/illnesses.html>
- 备注：出大汗后只灌白开水，会把血里的盐冲淡，所以水里要含一点盐。一次猛灌容易吐，吐的东西呛进气管比中暑本身更快要命。降温永远排在补水前面，见第 22 条。意识不清、说话含糊的，一口都不要喂。
  Note: Chugging plain water after heavy sweating dilutes blood salt—so the water needs a little salt in it. Gulping invites vomiting, and aspirated vomit kills faster than heat illness itself. Cooling always comes before rehydration—see item 22. Unconscious or slurring: not a drop.

### 24. 火灾时贴地爬、摸门再开门、门烫就别开，走楼梯不坐电梯，出去了别回头 / Fire: Stay Low, Feel Doors Before Opening, Hot Door Stays Shut, Stairs Not Elevators, Once Out Stay Out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：家里装 1 到 2 个独立烟感，几十美元。提前看一眼楼道的两条出口。
  Cost: One or two standalone smoke alarms for the home, tens of dollars. Take one look ahead of time at both exits from your hallway.
- 说人话：趴到地上，从烟底下爬向出口。开门前先摸门把和门板，烫、或者门缝在冒烟，就别开这扇门，换第二条路。开门要慢，一冒浓烟立刻关上。走楼梯不坐电梯，一断电就困在里面。出去了不为拿东西回头。衣服着火就停下、倒地、打滚，用手护住脸。
  In plain terms: Get low and crawl out under the smoke. Before opening a door, feel the handle and the door—hot, or smoke seeping through the gap, and that door stays shut; take the second way out. Open slowly; at the first heavy smoke, close it again. Stairs, not elevators—a power cut traps you inside. Once out, don't go back for belongings. Clothes on fire: stop, drop, and roll, shielding your face with your hands.
- 收益：美国 FEMA 逃生原文：「趴到地上，从烟下面爬向出口」。开门前先摸门把和门板，「如果是热的，或者门缝有烟，别开这扇门，用第二条路」。开门要慢，一有浓烟立刻关上。衣服着火就「停下、倒地、打滚」，用手护脸。出不去就关门，用布或者胶带堵住门缝和通风口，打电话报位置。逃生不要坐电梯，火不大时尽量往楼下跑，用湿毛巾或口罩蒙住口鼻，趴着或弯腰撤离。
  Benefit: From US FEMA's escape guidance: "get low and crawl out under the smoke." Before opening a door, feel the handle and panel—"if hot, or smoke is seeping in, don't open that door; use your second way out." Open slowly; close it again at the first heavy smoke. Clothes on fire: "stop, drop, and roll," shielding the face. Can't get out: close the door, seal gaps and vents with cloth or tape, call and report your location. Never take the elevator in a fire; when the fire is small, head downstairs; cover nose and mouth with a wet towel or mask; crouch or bend low to get out.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：US FEMA Ready.gov. Home Fires. <https://www.ready.gov/home-fires>
- 备注：不要坐电梯，断电就困在里面。不要为拿财物回头。不要往上跑，除非确认楼顶门开着。不要跳楼。湿毛巾对折 3 次叠成 8 层，水别太多，太湿反而喘不上气。烟和毒气太浓的时候，毛巾也挡不住。CAL FIRE 是加州的州级消防机构；平时把当地 fire department 的非紧急电话存好——但着火永远打 911。
  Note: Don't take the elevator—a power cut traps you inside. Don't go back for valuables. Don't run upward unless you've confirmed the roof door is open. Don't jump. Fold a wet towel three times into 8 layers; don't soak it, too-wet towels make breathing harder. In heavy smoke and toxic gas, no towel saves you. CAL FIRE is California's state fire agency; save your local fire department's non-emergency number ahead of time—but a fire always means 911.

### 25. 看到有人溺水，先叫人和打 911，伸竿、抛漂浮物，自己不下水 / Someone Drowning: Shout for Help and Call 911 First, Reach with a Pole, Throw Something That Floats—Don't Go In Yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是忍住往下跳的冲动。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is fighting the urge to jump in.
- 说人话：溺水的人会本能地把施救者往水下按，下水的人自己也可能淹死。顺序是：大喊叫人 → 打 911 → 递长杆、绳子 → 抛救生圈、空桶、矿泉水瓶 → 人上岸没呼吸就立刻按压。不结手拉手的链子下水。全球每年约 30 万人溺亡，这是 1 到 4 岁孩子的第四位死因。
  In plain terms: A drowning person will instinctively push a rescuer under; the person who goes in can drown too. The order: shout for help → call 911 → reach with a long pole or rope → throw a life ring, empty bucket, or water bottle → once they're ashore and not breathing, start compressions at once. Don't form a human chain into the water. About 300,000 people drown worldwide each year; it's the fourth-leading cause of death for children ages 1 to 4.
- 收益：WHO：全球每年约 30 万人溺亡，92% 在中低收入国家。溺水是 1 到 4 岁儿童的第四位死因，5 到 14 岁的第三位死因。WHO 推荐的干预是「安全救援和复苏培训」，比如先学会不下水的救法。顺序是：大喊叫人 → 打电话 → 递长杆、绳子，抛救生圈、空桶、矿泉水瓶 → 人上岸后无呼吸立刻按压。
  Benefit: WHO: about 300,000 drowning deaths per year worldwide, 92% in low- and middle-income countries. Drowning is the fourth-leading cause of death for children 1–4 and the third for ages 5–14. WHO's recommended interventions are "safe rescue and resuscitation training"—including learning no-entry rescues first. The order: shout for help → call → reach with a pole or rope, throw a life ring, empty bucket, or water bottle → once ashore and not breathing, compress at once.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：WHO. 溺水 实况报道. <https://www.who.int/zh/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/drowning>
- 备注：C 级原因是「不下水」这条规则没有找到官方原文，只有 WHO 的死亡数字和「安全救援」的方向。不要手拉手结成链子下水。没受过训练就不要去拖人，溺水者会把施救者往下按。「不下水」同样是自利规则：下水的人自己淹死，水里那个是谁都一样。真要救的多半是自家孩子，怎么防在第 1 节第 12 条（儿童近水不离视线）。有人掉进冰窟窿，同样不跑上冰面去拉，冰面怎么防见第 1 节第 41 条（野外冰面先量厚度）。
  Note: Graded C because no official source states the "don't go in" rule verbatim—only WHO's death figures and the "safe rescue" direction. Don't form a hand-holding chain into the water. Without training, don't try to drag someone out; a drowning person pushes the rescuer down. "Don't go in" is also a self-interest rule: if you drown going in, the person in the water is no better off whoever they are. The one you're most likely saving is your own child—prevention is Section 1, item 12. Someone through the ice: likewise don't run onto the ice to pull them; ice safety is Section 1, item 41.

### 26. 有人噎住说不出话，站到背后做 5 次拍背加 5 次腹部冲击，倒下就做心肺复苏 / Someone Choking Who Can't Speak: Stand Behind Them for 5 Back Blows Plus 5 Abdominal Thrusts; If They Collapse, Start CPR
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。看一遍动作：一手握拳顶在肚脐上方，另一手包住，向内向上快速冲击。
  Cost: Free. Watch the move once: make a fist above the navel, grip it with the other hand, thrust inward and upward fast.
- 说人话：先问「你噎住了吗，能说话吗」。还能用力咳、能出声的不要动手，让他咳。说不出话就站到背后，5 次拍背接 5 次腹部冲击，反复做到东西出来或者人倒下。倒下就做心肺复苏，同时叫人打急救电话。孕妇和很胖的人改冲胸口。别伸手去嘴里掏看不见的东西。
  In plain terms: First ask, "Are you choking? Can you speak?" If they can cough hard or make sound, hands off—let them cough. If they can't speak, stand behind them: 5 back blows then 5 abdominal thrusts, repeating until the object comes out or they collapse. If they collapse, start CPR and have someone call emergency services. For pregnant or very obese people, thrust on the chest instead. Don't reach into the mouth for something you can't see.
- 收益：美国国立医学图书馆原文：先问「你噎住了吗，能说话吗」。「能用力咳嗽、能说话的不要动手」，咳嗽本身多能把异物咳出。说不出话就「反复做 5 次拍背接 5 次腹部冲击，直到异物出来或人失去意识」。「失去意识就开始心肺复苏」，同时叫人打急救电话。孕妇、肥胖者改做胸部冲击。
  Benefit: From the US National Library of Medicine: first ask "Are you choking? Can you speak?" "If they can cough forcefully or speak, hands off"—coughing usually clears it. If they can't speak, "repeat 5 back blows followed by 5 abdominal thrusts until the object comes out or the person loses consciousness." "If they lose consciousness, begin CPR," while someone calls emergency services. Pregnant or obese people get chest thrusts instead.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：US National Library of Medicine, MedlinePlus. Choking – adult or child over 1 year. <https://medlineplus.gov/ency/article/000047.htm>
- 备注：不要往嘴里伸手掏看不见的东西，会推得更深。不要拍还能咳嗽的人。噎住最常发生在自家饭桌上，受益的主要是家里人。American Red Cross 的急救培训也是这一套：1 岁以上的孩子和成人，按 5 次拍背加 5 次腹部冲击循环来教。网上不少老教程只教腹部冲击，那是旧版。不满 1 岁的孩子不按肚子，改按胸口，见本节第 43 条（婴儿噎住）。
  Note: Don't reach into the mouth for what you can't see—it pushes the object deeper. Don't slap the back of someone who can still cough. Choking happens most at the family dinner table, so family benefits most. American Red Cross first-aid training teaches the same: for children over 1 and adults, cycles of 5 back blows plus 5 abdominal thrusts. Plenty of old online tutorials teach abdominal thrusts only—that's the outdated version. For babies under 1, no abdominal thrusts—use chest thrusts; see item 43 in this section.

### 27. 在沙漠、荒野迷路或车坏了，留在原地或车旁，先报位置，再遮阳保温，省水，不乱走 / Lost in the Desert or Backcountry, or Vehicle Breaks Down: Stay Put or Stay with the Vehicle, Report Your Position First, Then Shade, Insulation, Conserve Water, Don't Wander
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：进荒野前告诉别人路线、目的地、返回日期、车型车牌。每人每天 4 升水起。买一台卫星通讯器几百美元，租一次几十美元起。难的是守住「留在原地不乱走」。
  Cost: Before heading into the backcountry, tell someone your route, destination, return date, vehicle model, and plate. At least 4 liters of water per person per day. A satellite communicator costs hundreds of dollars to buy, tens and up to rent. The hard part is holding the line on "stay put, don't wander."
- 说人话：留在原地或者车旁，车是最大的遮阳物。顺序是：有信号先发位置再打电话 → 遮阳保暖 → 省水，白天不动、夜里凉了再动 → 不乱走 → 用反光镜、烟、三堆火发信号。进去之前把路线、目的地、返回日期和车型车牌告诉别人。
  In plain terms: Stay put or stay with the vehicle—the vehicle is your biggest shade. The order: if you have signal, send your location before calling → shade and insulation → conserve water, stay still by day and move only when it's cool at night → don't wander → signal with a mirror, smoke, three fires. Before going in, tell someone your route, destination, return date, vehicle model, and plate.

### 28. 发抖、说话含糊、走路打晃是失温，换掉湿衣、包起来，先暖躯干，喝热甜饮不喝酒 / Shivering, Slurred Speech, Staggering: That's Hypothermia—Change Out of Wet Clothes, Wrap Up, Warm the Core First, Hot Sweet Drinks Not Alcohol

- 成本：一张铝箔保温毯几美元，再加一件备用干衣。学会看信号不花钱。
  Cost: A foil emergency blanket costs a few dollars, plus a spare set of dry clothes. Learning the signs is free.
- 说人话：体温降到 35℃ 以下就是失温，人会发抖、说话含糊、走路打晃、想不清楚事。换掉湿衣服，用保温毯或者干衣裹起来，先暖躯干、不先暖手脚。给热的甜饮料，不给酒，酒会压住寒战。不抖了不是好转，是加重。没有意识就做心肺复苏。

Stopping shivering isn't improvement, it's worsening. No consciousness: do CPR.
- 收益：美国国家气象局原文：体温 95°F（35°C）以下就是失温，「觉得冷、迟钝、想不清楚，立刻看医生」。处理是「立即就医，把人移到有暖气的室内，先从身体中心开始复温」，给热饮。「酒精会减少寒战」，所以不喝。「失去意识做心肺复苏」。冻伤用温水或者体温回暖，「不要用热水或壁炉一类辐射热」。荒野医学会 2019 年失温指南是这个领域的执业指南。
  Benefit: From the US National Weather Service: below 95°F (35°C) is hypothermia—"feeling cold, sluggish, confused, get medical attention." Frostbite gets lukewarm water or body heat—"not hot water or radiant heat like a fireplace." The Wilderness Medical Society's 2019 hypothermia guideline is the field's practice standard.

- 备注：不要搓手脚。不要泡热水澡。不要硬拖硬拽地搬，失温重的人心脏容易骤停。不要因为「不抖了」就以为好转，不打哆嗦（寒战停止）是加重。
  Note: Don't rub the hands and feet. Don't soak in a hot bath. Don't drag or manhandle—severe hypothermia makes the heart prone to arrest. Don't mistake "stopped shivering" for improvement; shivering stopping means it's worse.

### 29. 被蛇咬，坐下别动、摘掉戒指手表、尽快送医院打抗蛇毒血清，不切、不吸、不绑死 / Snakebite: Sit Down and Stay Still, Remove Rings and Watch, Get to a Hospital for Antivenom Fast—No Cutting, No Sucking, No Tourniquet

- 成本：不花钱。提前知道附近哪家医院有抗蛇毒血清。难的是忍住自己动手处理伤口。

Photograph the snake from far away if you can—don't try to catch it.

### 30. 地震时趴下、护住头颈、抓牢：Drop, Cover, and Hold On / Earthquake: Drop, Cover, and Hold On

- 成本：不花钱。提前看一眼自己家出门要几步、走哪条路、哪张桌子结实。

- 说人话：加州州长应急服务办公室（Cal OES）口径：Drop, Cover, and Hold On——趴下，用手或软物护住头颈，钻到结实桌子下面抓牢桌腿，直到摇晃停止。不要往外跑，不要坐电梯，不要在摇晃中下楼梯。加州多为木结构住宅，整体垮塌少，伤主要来自坠物和摇晃中摔倒。
  In plain terms: The California Governor's Office of Emergency Services (Cal OES) line is Drop, Cover, and Hold On—drop down, protect your head and neck with your hands or something soft, get under a sturdy table and hold its legs until the shaking stops. Don't run outside, don't take the elevator, don't go down stairs mid-shaking. California homes are mostly wood-frame, whole-building collapse is rare, and injuries come mainly from falling objects and falling while moving.
- 收益：美国 FEMA 对本国住宅给的是「就地趴下、护住头颈、抓牢，不要往外跑」，那里的伤主要来自坠物和摇晃中移动摔倒。1988 年亚美尼亚地震是苏联式预制板楼成片垮塌。那次的病例对照研究写着，「第一下震动之后离开建筑是保护性行为」。待在室内的人受伤的可能性，是跑出去的 4.40 倍（比值比 4.40），95% CI 2.24–8.71（可信范围）。在建筑里受伤的可能性，是在室外的 12.20 倍（3.62–63.79）。在 5 层以上的楼里，是在更矮的楼里的 3.65 倍（2.12–6.33）。1994 年北岭地震的研究还提示，伸手去接东西、扶住「看起来稳」的东西会加重受伤。
  Benefit: US FEMA's guidance for American homes is "drop where you are, protect your head and neck, hold on—don't run outside," where injuries come mainly from falling objects and falling while moving. People who stayed indoors were injured at 4.40 times the odds of those who went out (OR 4.40, 95% CI 2.24–8.71). Being inside a building carried 12.20 times the injury odds of being outside (3.62–63.79), and being on the 5th floor or higher 3.65 times the odds of lower floors (2.12–6.33). Research on the 1994 Northridge earthquake also suggests that reaching for things or bracing "stable-looking" objects worsens injuries.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：US FEMA Ready.gov. Earthquakes. <https://www.ready.gov/earthquakes>；Armenian HK, Noji EK, Oganesian AP (1992). A case-control study of injuries arising from the earthquake in Armenia, 1988.   Note: The dispute is real, but the two conclusions are about two kinds of buildings. Armenia's blocks collapsed wholesale—there was nowhere to hide, so getting out won. In California, the default is Drop, Cover, and Hold On: wood-frame homes rarely pancake, while running blindly risks falling ceilings, light fixtures, or tripping mid-shake. In any case: never jump, never take the elevator, never descend stairs mid-shaking. Outside, stay clear of tall buildings, overpasses, power poles, glass facades, and billboards.

### 31. 遇到黑熊、响尾蛇、美洲狮，别跑、别尖叫、别对视，侧身慢慢退开 / Black Bear, Rattlesnake, Mountain Lion: Don't Run, Don't Scream, Don't Stare—Back Away Slowly, Angled Off
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。进山带根登山杖，结伴走、出声走，让动物先知道你来了。难的是站住不跑。
  Cost: Free. Bring a trekking pole in the hills, hike in company, make noise—let animals know you're coming. The hard part is standing your ground instead of running.
- 说人话：加州常见的是黑熊（black bear）、响尾蛇（rattlesnake）、美洲狮（mountain lion），还有野猪（feral pig）。原则一样：不投喂、不接近、保持距离。黑熊：别跑（上下坡都跑得像赛马），别爬树，站住、低声一直跟它说话、让自己显得大，等它不动了再侧身慢慢退开；黑熊扑上来不装死，往车里或屋里退，退不掉就拿手边东西反击。美洲狮：别跑、别蹲下，保持面对它，把自己变大、挥动手臂、说话出声；被扑倒就反击。响尾蛇：退开、给它让路，别伸手去抓、别试图挪开它。被咬按第 29 条，被抓咬按第 13 条。

Black bear: don't run (they run like racehorses uphill and down), don't climb a tree—stand your ground, keep talking in a low voice, make yourself look big; when it holds still, back away slowly at an angle. If a black bear charges, don't play dead—get to a car or building; if you can't, fight back with whatever's at hand. Mountain lion: don't run, don't crouch; face it, look big, wave your arms, make noise; if knocked down, fight back. Rattlesnake: back off and give it the trail—don't reach for it or try to move it. Bites: item 29. Scratches and bites: item 13.
- 收益：美国国家公园管理局关于熊的原文有六句。一句是「不要跑，熊上下坡都能跑得像赛马一样快」。一句是「保持冷静，大多数熊不想攻击你，只想被放着不管」。一句是「用低沉的声音持续跟熊说话」。一句是「熊不动时，侧着身慢慢退开，这样既能盯着熊又不会绊倒」。一句是「让自己显得尽量大」。还有一句是「不要爬树，灰熊和黑熊都会爬树」。被黑熊攻击时「不要装死，逃向车或房子，逃不了就用手边任何东西反击」。核心原则放之四海：不投喂、不接近、保持距离。
  Benefit: Six verbatim lines from the US National Park Service on bears: "don't run—bears can run like racehorses uphill and down"; "stay calm—most bears don't want to attack you, they want to be left alone"; "keep talking to the bear in a low voice"; "when the bear holds still, back away slowly at an angle so you can watch it without tripping"; "make yourself look as big as possible"; "don't climb a tree—grizzlies and black bears both climb." - 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：US National Park Service. Staying Safe Around Bears. <https://www.nps.gov/subjects/bears/safety.htm>
- 备注：B 级只对应熊。加州的熊是黑熊，灰熊只在州旗上——野外已经没有了，所以「棕熊扑上来装死」那套在加州用不上。美洲狮和响尾蛇的「不跑、拉开距离」是加州渔猎局（CDFW）一贯的官方提示口径（CDFW 响尾蛇页：https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Reptiles/Rattlesnake；美洲狮 Keep-Me-Wild 页：https://wildlife.ca.gov/Keep-Me-Wild/Mountain-Lion）。不要拿食物引，不要凑近拍照，不要带狗进有野猪的山。
  Note: The B grade covers bears only. California's bears are black bears—grizzlies live on the state flag alone; there are none left in the wild, so the "play dead for a brown bear" routine doesn't apply in California. The "don't run, keep distance" line for mountain lions and rattlesnakes matches the California Department of Fish and Wildlife's standing guidance (CDFW rattlesnake page: https://wildlife.ca.gov/Conservation/Reptiles/Rattlesnake; mountain lion Keep-Me-Wild page: https://wildlife.ca.gov/Keep-Me-Wild/Mountain-Lion). Don't lure with food, don't edge closer for photos, don't bring dogs into hills with wild pigs.

### 32. 野外遇上雷雨，钻进有硬顶的车或建筑，避开山脊、孤树、水面和金属 / Thunderstorm in the Open: Get into a Hard-Topped Vehicle or Building; Avoid Ridges, Lone Trees, Water, and Metal

### 33. 上高原按睡眠海拔（当晚睡在多高）慢慢升，症状加重就下撤，不要硬扛 / High Altitude: Ascend by Sleeping Elevation (How High You Sleep), Descend If Symptoms Worsen—Don't Tough It Out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：多花一到两天做适应。预防药是处方药，要在出发前问医生。难的是行程排得紧时还肯留出这几天。
  Cost: One or two extra days for acclimatization. Preventive medication is prescription-only—ask a doctor before the trip. The hard part is keeping those days when the itinerary is tight.
- 说人话：算的是睡在多高，不是白天到过多高。睡在 2450 米以上就有高原病风险。过了 3000 米之后，每晚睡觉的高度只往上加 500 米，每升 1000 米多留一天适应。头痛加恶心、走路打晃、意识变化，或者不动也喘不上气，就往下走。下撤排在吸氧和吃药前面。
  In plain terms: Count how high you sleep, not how high you day-trip. Sleeping above 2,450 meters carries altitude-illness risk. Above 3,000 meters, raise sleeping elevation by only 500 meters a night, adding an acclimatization day per 1,000 meters gained. Headache plus nausea, staggering, mental changes, or breathlessness at rest—go down. Descent outranks oxygen and pills.
- 收益：睡在 2450 米以上就有高原病风险。高原脑水肿和肺水肿（脑子和肺里积水），不及时往低处撤会死。
  Benefit: Sleeping at or above 2,450 meters puts you at risk for altitude illness. High-altitude cerebral and pulmonary edema (fluid in the brain and lungs) kill if you don't descend promptly.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：美国疾病控制与预防中心. 黄皮书·高海拔旅行与高原病：「Any unacclimatized traveler proceeding to a sleeping altitude of ≥2,450 m (≥8,000 ft)—and sometimes lower—is at risk for altitude illness」；上升速度「500 m (1,650 ft) per night in sleeping altitude once above 3,000 m (9,800 ft)」，每上升 1000 米加一天适应日；乙酰唑胺预防「125 mg twice a day; 250 mg twice a day if >100 kg body weight」；「If symptoms become worse despite rest or treatment at the same elevation」必须下撤. <https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/environmental-hazards-risks/high-altitude-travel-and-altitude-illness.html>；荒野医学会 (2024). 急性高原病预防、诊断与治疗临床实践指南（2024 更新）. Wilderness & Environmental Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.wem.2023.05.013>
- 备注：算的是「睡在多高」，不是白天到过多高。头痛加恶心、走路打晃、意识变化，或者不动也喘不上气，都是往下走的信号。下撤优先于吸氧和吃药，吸着氧继续往上是错的。
  Note: It's "how high you sleep," not how high you day-trip. Headache plus nausea, staggering, mental changes, or breathlessness at rest are all descend-now signals. Descent outranks oxygen and pills—climbing higher on oxygen is wrong.

### 34. 被蜱虫叮住，用尖头镊子贴着皮肤稳稳拉出来，别涂油别用火烧；加州重点防莱姆病 / Tick Bite: Grip with Fine-Tipped Tweezers Close to the Skin and Pull Steady—No Oil, No Fire; in California, Watch for Lyme Disease
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一把尖头镊子。
  Cost: A pair of fine-tipped tweezers.
- 说人话：用尖头镊子贴着皮肤夹住蜱的头部，稳稳地朝外拉出来。别涂油，别用火烧，别扭着拽，那会刺激它把带病原体的体液挤进皮肤，还容易把口器留在肉里。拔完记下日期。加州的蜱主要传莱姆病（Lyme disease）。之后几周里发烧、起疹（尤其靶心样红斑）、乏力，看病时主动说被蜱咬过。
  In plain terms: Grip the tick's head with fine-tipped tweezers as close to the skin as you can, and pull steadily outward. No oil, no fire, no twisting—it agitates the tick into squeezing pathogen-carrying fluid into the skin, and the mouthparts snap off inside. Note the date after removal. In California, ticks mainly transmit Lyme disease. Fever, rash (especially a bull's-eye), or fatigue in the following weeks: tell the doctor about the tick bite yourself.
- 收益：涂油、用火烧、扭着拽，会刺激蜱虫，把带病原体的体液挤进皮肤。这么做也容易把它扎在肉里的那部分嘴（口器）留在里面。美国 CDC 估计全美每年约 476,000 人被诊断并治疗莱姆病（官方上报只捕捉到一部分）；加州的传病蜱主要是太平洋黑腿蜱（western blacklegged tick, Ixodes pacificus）。被蜱咬后 3–30 天（平均约 7 天）出现扩大的红斑（不一定是标准靶心样，也可能是均匀的红斑），伴随发烧、乏力，越早用抗生素越好（美国）。
  Benefit: Oil, fire, and twisting agitate the tick and force pathogen-carrying fluid into the skin, and tend to leave the embedded mouthparts behind. The CDC estimates about 476,000 Americans are diagnosed and treated for Lyme disease each year (official surveillance captures only part of it); in California the main vector is the western blacklegged tick (Ixodes pacificus). An expanding rash 3–30 days after a bite (average ~7 days; not always a textbook bull's-eye—sometimes a uniform red patch), plus fever and fatigue: the earlier antibiotics start, the better (United States).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：美国疾病控制与预防中心. 被蜱叮咬之后：「Grasp the tick as close to the skin's surface as possible using clean fine-tipped tweezers」「Pull tick away from the skin with steady, even pressure」「Do not use petroleum jelly, heat, nail polish, or other substances to try and make the tick detach from the skin. This may agitate the tick and force infected fluid from the tick into the skin」. <https://www.cdc.gov/ticks/after-a-tick-bite/index.html>；CDC 莱姆病负担估计（每年约 476,000 美国人确诊治疗）
- 备注：拔完把日期记下来。之后几周里发烧、起皮疹、乏力，看病时主动说被蜱咬过。医生不问、你不说，很容易被按感冒处理。
  Note: Write down the date after removal. Fever, rash, or fatigue in the following weeks: volunteer the tick bite at the visit—if the doctor doesn't ask and you don't say, it gets filed as a cold.

### 35. 野外的水一律烧开再喝，滚开一分钟就够，不用因为海拔高多煮 / Wilderness Water: Always Boil Before Drinking—One Full Rolling Minute Is Enough, No Extra Time for Altitude
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：多背一套炉具和燃料的重量。
  Cost: The extra weight of a stove and fuel.
- 说人话：所有能靠喝水致病的微生物，在沸点温度下几秒就死了。再滚一分钟，只是给「看走眼」留的余量，不用因为海拔高就多煮。烧不了就用过滤器或者消毒片，这两样各有覆盖不到的病原体，能烧还是烧。看着清澈的溪水、雪水一样可能有寄生虫。
  In plain terms: Every microbe that can make you sick through water dies within seconds at boiling temperature. The extra full minute is just margin for misjudging the boil—no extra time needed for altitude. If you can't boil, use a filter or disinfection tablets, though each misses some pathogens; boil when you can. Clear-looking streams and snowmelt can carry parasites too.
- 收益：所有能靠喝水致病的微生物，在水烧开的温度下几秒就被杀死（灭活）。「高海拔水烧不到位」是常见误解。
  Benefit: All organisms that can cause illness from drinking water are killed within seconds at boiling temperature. "Water doesn't get hot enough at altitude" is a common myth.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：美国疾病控制与预防中心. 黄皮书·旅行者的水消毒：「All organisms that may cause illness from drinking water are killed within seconds at boiling temperature」「CDC recommends that travelers boil water for a full minute to account for user variability in identifying boiling points and to add a margin of safety」「Although the boiling point for water decreases with increasing elevation, at common travel elevations the temperature needed to achieve boiling is still well above the temperature required to inactivate enteric pathogens」. <https://www.cdc.gov/yellow-book/hcp/preparing-international-travelers/water-disinfection-for-travelers.html>
- 备注：同一页也写了不必真的烧开：60 ℃ 保持 30 分钟同样有效，烧开只是不用温度计就能看见的终点。烧不了就用过滤器或者化学消毒片，两者各有覆盖不到的病原体，能烧就烧。看起来清澈的溪水、雪水一样可能有寄生虫，别凭外观判断。
  Note: The same page notes you don't strictly need a boil: 30 minutes at 60°C works too—boiling is just the no-thermometer endpoint you can see. If you can't boil, use a filter or chemical tablets; each misses some pathogens, so boil when you can. Clear-looking streams and snowmelt can carry parasites—don't judge by looks.

### 36. 荒郊野外被陌生人索财，钱给他，不动手，记特征，脱身后打 911 / Robbed by a Stranger in a Remote Area: Give the Money, Don't Fight, Note Details, Call 911 After You're Clear
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：身上的现金。难的是压住火气。
  Cost: The cash on you. The hard part is swallowing your anger.
- 说人话：钱给他，不动手，不还嘴。美国民间持枪多，对方可能带枪，为几百块拼命不值。在加州，抢劫（robbery）是重罪（felony）。他拿了钱转身走，你追上去打就不再算正当防卫。脱身后立刻记下体貌、口音、车牌和逃跑方向，打 911。
  In plain terms: Give the money. Don't fight, don't talk back. Guns are common in the US—the other person may be armed, and your life isn't worth a few hundred dollars. In California, robbery is a felony. Once they've taken the money and turned to leave, chasing them down to hit them is no longer self-defense. After you're clear, immediately note appearance, accent, plates, and direction of flight, then call 911.
- 收益：命比钱贵。在加州，抢劫（PC §211）是重罪：二级抢劫按 PC §213 判州监狱 2、3、5 年；一级抢劫 3、4、6 年（结伙入室抢劫 3、6、9 年）。正当防卫只保护「制止正在进行的不法侵害」：对方拿了钱转身走，你追上去打就不再是防卫。脱身后立刻记下体貌、口音、车牌、逃跑方向，打 911。
  Benefit: Life is worth more than money. In California, robbery (PC §211) is a felony: second-degree robbery brings 2, 3, or 5 years in state prison under PC §213; first-degree 3, 4, or 6 years (3, 6, or 9 years for home-invasion robbery in concert). Self-defense only covers stopping an ongoing attack: once they've taken the money and walked away, chasing them to hit them is no longer defense. After you're clear, immediately note appearance, accent, plates, and direction of flight, then call 911.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：California Penal Code §§211, 213
- 备注：C 级原因是「给钱不对抗更安全」没有直接文献。不要跟去偏僻处「取钱」。不要交手机密码。不要上对方的车。先给钱只限当面被堵住、人身安全受威胁的场合，脱身后照样报警。网上被人拿隐私照片、裸聊录像来要钱是反过来的，一分钱都不能给，见第 8 节第 32 条（对方拿把柄来要钱）。在加州，随身带某些武器防身本身也可能违法，见第 9 节第 26 条。
  Note: Graded C because "hand over the money, don't resist" has no direct literature. Don't follow them somewhere secluded to "get money." Don't hand over your phone passcode. Don't get in their car. Handing over cash applies only when you're cornered face-to-face with your safety threatened—still call the police after. Online blackmail with private photos or recorded video chats is the reverse: not a cent, see Section 8, item 32. In California, carrying certain weapons for self-defense may itself be illegal—see Section 9, item 26.

### 37. 撞见一群人打架，退开走人，别上去拉架、别围观、别捡地上的家伙；要报警就退到安全距离打 911 / Walking into a Group Fight: Back Off and Leave, Don't Break It Up, Don't Gawk, Don't Pick Up Weapons; Call 911 from a Safe Distance If You Call
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是忍住上前拉一把、以及站着看热闹这两种冲动。报警之后可能被回访做一次笔录，最多搭进半天。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is resisting both urges: stepping in to pull people apart, and standing around to watch. After calling, you may be asked back once for a statement—half a day at most.
- 说人话：你一动手，劝架就成了参加打架。在加州，参与打架斗殴可能被控袭击（assault，PC §240：有现时能力企图实施暴力伤害，轻罪最高 6 个月县监狱/$1,000）或殴打（battery，PC §242：故意非法使用武力，轻罪最高 6 个月/$2,000）。围观也容易被误伤。该做的是退开走人。愿意报警就退到安全距离打 911，说清地点、几个人、有没有拿刀棍、有没有人躺下不动。
  In plain terms: The moment you lay hands on, breaking up the fight becomes joining the fight. In California, joining a brawl can get you charged with assault (PC §240: attempt plus present ability to inflict violent injury — a misdemeanor, up to 6 months in county jail/$1,000) or battery (PC §242: willful and unlawful use of force — a misdemeanor, up to 6 months/$2,000). Gawking gets you hurt by mistake too. The move is to back off and leave. If you want to call, do it from a safe distance: location, how many people, any knives or sticks, anyone down and not moving.
- 收益：在加州，没有法律要求路人必须上手制止；但一旦动手参与，就可能从证人变成被告。上手拉架、捡地上的家伙，都会把你变成参与者。「我是来劝架的」不是护身符，认定看的是你动没动手、打了谁。退开、打 911、说清地点人数，既帮了忙又不把自己搭进去。
  Benefit: In California, no law requires a bystander to physically intervene; but the moment you join in with your hands, you can go from witness to defendant. Pulling people apart or picking up a weapon on the ground both make you a participant. "I was just breaking it up" is no shield—what counts is whether you used force and on whom. Back off, call 911, give location and headcount: that helps without costing you.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：California Penal Code §§240, 242
- 备注：对方已经倒地或者跑了，你还追上去打，就不再算防卫。看到有人被围着单方面挨打，法律上你确实可以制止，但徒手上去多半只是再添一个伤员。喊人、远远录像、报警更管用。自己被卷进冲突见第 8 节第 10 条，被陌生人索财见本节第 36 条。
  Note: If the other side is already down or running and you chase them to hit them, that's no longer defense. If you see someone being beaten one-sidedly by a group, the law does allow you to intervene—but going in bare-handed mostly adds one more casualty. Shouting for help, filming from far away, and calling the police work better. If you're pulled into a conflict yourself, see Section 8, item 10; for being robbed by a stranger, see item 36 in this section.

### 38. 可能被 HIV 暴露了，72 小时内去拿阻断药（PEP），越早越好 / Possible HIV Exposure: Get Post-Exposure Prophylaxis (PEP) Within 72 Hours—Sooner Is Better
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一个 28 天疗程的药费在美国较贵，看保险。当晚就得跑一趟急诊。难的是当场承认自己可能被传染，立刻出门。
  Cost: A 28-day course runs expensive in the US, depending on insurance. It means a trip to the ER that same night. The hard part is admitting on the spot that you may have been exposed, and walking out the door.
- 说人话：怀疑自己被传染上艾滋病，72 小时是硬线，最好 2 小时内就吃上药。药要连吃 28 天。超过 72 小时这条路就没了，所以别等到第二天再决定。直接去急诊（ER）或 urgent care，别等。
  In plain terms: If you suspect HIV exposure, 72 hours is a hard line—ideally the first dose within 2 hours. The drugs run 28 days straight. Past 72 hours this option is gone, so don't sleep on the decision. Go straight to an ER or urgent care; don't wait.
- 收益：接触之后的预防用药叫暴露后预防（英文缩写 PEP），应「尽可能在 2 小时内进行预防性用药，最好在 24 小时内、不超过 72 小时」。还要「连续服用 28 天」。超过 72 小时就不再建议启动，这是一条硬时限。
  Benefit: The preventive drugs after exposure are called post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP): start "ideally within 2 hours, preferably within 24 hours, and no later than 72 hours," then "take continuously for 28 days." Past 72 hours it's no longer recommended to start—a hard cutoff.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：US CDC, "Antiretroviral Postexposure Prophylaxis After Sexual, Injection Drug Use, or Other Nonoccupational Exposure to HIV — CDC Recommendations, United States, 2025" (MMWR 2025-05-08, 取代 2016 版）. <https://www.cdc.gov/mmwr/volumes/74/rr/rr7401a1.htm>；中国 CDC 暴露后预防指南（2020，试用）作为对照. <https://www.chinacdc.cn/jkyj/crb2/yl/azb/jswj_azb/202410/t20241027_302131.html>
- 备注：去哪里拿：在美国直接去急诊（ER）或 urgent care，当晚就有药，不用等疾控指定门诊。算「暴露」的情况包括没做防护的性行为、安全套破了、被针头扎到、血沾到黏膜上，到底用不用药由医生判断。药必须吃满 28 天，漏几次会让效果明显变差。之后按医嘱复查。阻断药只是补救，日常预防和检测见第 1 节第 30、31 条（全程用安全套、有过高危行为就去查）。
  Note: Where to get it: in the US, go straight to an ER or urgent care—medication is available that same night, no need to wait for a designated clinic. "Exposure" includes unprotected sex, a broken condom, a needlestick, or blood on mucous membranes; the doctor decides whether PEP is warranted. The full 28 days are mandatory—missed doses sharply cut effectiveness. Follow-up testing per the doctor's orders. PEP is only a rescue; routine prevention and testing are Section 1, items 30–31.

### 40. 刀、钢筋、玻璃扎进身体，压住异物周围止血，不要把它拔出来 / Impaled by a Knife, Rebar, or Glass: Press Around the Object to Stop Bleeding—Don't Pull It Out
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是忍住「先拔出来」的本能，守着人等救护车。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is fighting the instinct to "pull it out first" and staying with the person until the ambulance comes.
- 说人话：拔出来可能造成大出血，也会让医生更难判断伤了多深。做法是：打 911，用手或者敷料压住异物周围止血，把异物垫稳别让它晃。肚子上的伤口有内脏露出来，不要往回塞，盖一块干净的湿敷料。胸口的伤口在冒泡的，别用塑料布封死，封死可能把肺压瘪。
  In plain terms: Pulling it out can cause massive bleeding and makes it harder for doctors to judge the depth. The move: call 911, press around the object with your hand or a dressing to stop bleeding, and stabilize the object so it doesn't wobble. If organs protrude from an abdominal wound, don't push them back—cover with a clean moist dressing. A bubbling chest wound: don't seal it airtight with plastic; sealing can collapse the lung.
- 收益：国际红十字与红新月会联合会 2025 年国际急救指南：「急救的人应当压住扎进身体的异物（比如刀）周围，把异物稳住，不要把它拔出来」。「不要从腹部或胸部的伤口里拔出任何扎入的异物，拔出来可能造成大出血，也会让医生更难判断伤得有多重」。「扎在身体里的异物应当稳住；异物随脉搏跳动的，就让它跳，松松地稳住即可」。「急救的人不应把露出来的内脏塞回体内」。没有在往外涌血的开放性胸壁伤口「可以敞着、不加敷料，因为把伤口封死可能让空气积在胸腔里，使肺部分或完全塌陷（气胸）」。所有穿透性胸腹部伤口都应立即呼叫急救。
  Benefit: From the IFRC 2025 international first-aid guidelines: "the first aider should press around an impaled object (e.g., a knife), stabilizing it, not pulling it out." "Do not remove any impaled object from an abdominal or chest wound—removal can cause major bleeding and makes depth assessment harder for doctors." "An impaled object should be stabilized; if it pulses with the heartbeat, let it pulse, stabilizing loosely." "The first aider should not push protruding organs back inside." An open chest wound that's not gushing "can be left open without a dressing, because sealing it may trap air in the chest and partially or fully collapse the lung (pneumothorax)." All penetrating chest or abdominal wounds warrant an immediate emergency call.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B

International First Aid, Resuscitation and Education Guidelines 2025，第 256–257、267–268 页. <https://www.ifrc.org/sites/default/files/2026-03/IFRC%20International%20First%20Aid,%20Resuscitation%20and%20Education%20Guidelines%202025.pdf>

- 成本：不花钱。找块硬板、卷起的杂志或者衣物当支撑。冷的东西隔层布敷。

### 43. 不满 1 岁的婴儿噎住哭不出声，趴着拍背 5 下、翻过来按胸口 5 下，轮着做，别按肚子 / Infant Under 1 Choking and Can't Cry: 5 Back Slaps Face-Down, Flip Over for 5 Chest Thrusts, Alternate—Never Abdominal Thrusts
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。看一遍动作或者上一次婴儿急救课，几分钟到半天。难的是慌乱中先把孩子翻过来趴好。

- 说人话：孩子还能咳、能哭出声，就让他自己咳，你在旁边盯着。哭不出声、脸发紫，就让人打 911，你把孩子脸朝下趴在前臂上，用掌根在两块肩胛骨中间拍 5 下。没出来就翻过来，在胸口正中快速按 5 下，两样轮着做，别按肚子。孩子没反应了就改做心肺复苏。
  In plain terms: If the baby can still cough or cry out, let them cough while you watch closely. If they can't cry and the face turns blue, have someone call 911; lay the baby face-down on your forearm and give 5 slaps with the heel of your hand between the shoulder blades. - 收益：美国心脏协会和美国儿科学会 2025 年儿童基础生命支持指南：窒息（比如异物堵住气道）「是婴儿和儿童的主要死因之一」。婴儿最常呛进去的是液体。还在咳、还能出声的是轻度，让孩子自己咳，同时盯着有没有变重。咳得很弱或者咳不出、哭不出、脸色发紫的是重度。重度要先叫急救，再「反复做 5 次拍背、接 5 次胸部冲击，直到异物出来或者婴儿失去反应」。指南不建议给婴儿做腹部冲击，因为可能伤到肚子里的脏器。婴儿失去反应就开始心肺复苏，先按压，不摸脉搏。吹气前看得见的异物要取出来。指南明写「不要用手指盲目去掏」。

### 44. 不满 1 岁的婴儿没反应、没正常呼吸，开免提打 911，做婴儿心肺复苏：按 30 下吹 2 口气 / Infant Under 1 Unresponsive and Not Breathing Normally: Call 911 on Speakerphone, Do Infant CPR—30 Compressions to 2 Breaths
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。婴儿心肺复苏要上课练一次，半天到一天。难的是对着孩子口鼻吹气，还有不敢用力按。
  Cost: Free. Infant CPR needs one practice class—half a day to a day. The hard parts are breathing into the baby's mouth and nose, and daring to push hard enough.
- 说人话：拍脚底没反应、没正常呼吸，就开免提打 911，手不停。双手环抱胸口用两个拇指按，抱不住就用一只手的掌根，按下去约 4 厘米，每秒约 2 下。每按 30 下，嘴包住孩子口鼻吹 2 口气。婴儿心跳停多半是喘不上气引起的，做了带吹气的心肺复苏，活下来的更多。

Speakerphone removes that problem. For choking-caused arrest, look in the mouth before breaths and remove only what you can see—see item 43 (infant choking).

### 45. 看到有人吸毒过量倒下，先打 911：加州法律保护打电话的人 / Someone Overdosing? Call 911 First — California Law Protects the Caller（新增条目 / New item）
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是压住"打了 911 会不会把自己搭进去"的犹豫。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is pushing past the "will calling 911 get me in trouble?" hesitation.
- 说人话：看到有人嗑药过量倒下（叫不醒、呼吸慢或停、嘴唇发紫），立刻打 911，再做心肺复苏。别因为自己也沾了东西就不敢打——加州 HSC §11376.5（911 好撒玛利亚人法）规定：善意为药物过量者呼叫急救的人，不会因此被按持有毒品、持有吸毒用具的轻罪逮捕、起诉或定罪，也不会因此被认定违反缓刑/假释。法律的本意就是让你先打电话。注意：保护的是"叫急救"这个行为，不保贩毒、也不保别的罪名。另外：加州药房可以无处方卖纳洛酮（Narcan，阿片过量逆转药），家里有人用阿片类药物，备一支能救命。

The law exists so you make the call. Note: it protects the act of seeking help — not dealing, and not other crimes. Also: California pharmacies can sell naloxone (Narcan, the opioid-overdose reversal drug) without a prescription — keep one on hand if anyone in the household uses opioids.
- 收益：H&S Code §11376.5：善意为过量者寻求医疗救助，免于持有毒品/用具轻罪的逮捕、起诉、定罪及缓刑/假释不利后果。纳洛酮在加州药房无处方可得（Bus. & Prof. Code §4052.01）。过量抢救和好撒玛利亚人法（H&S §1799.102）是同一条主线：法律鼓励你先救人（加州）。
  Benefit: H&S Code §11376.5: seeking medical help in good faith for an overdose shields the caller from arrest, prosecution, or conviction for personal-use possession/paraphernalia misdemeanors, and from probation/parole consequences. Naloxone is available without a prescription at California pharmacies (Bus. & Prof. Code §4052.01). Overdose rescue and the Good Samaritan law (H&S §1799.102) share one theme: the law wants you to help first (California).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：California Health & Safety Code §11376.5；California Business & Professions Code §4052.01
- 备注：这条和第 8 节第 29 条（毒品犯罪）是正反两面：持有是违法，但人命关头打电话不被追究。叫了 911 就在现场等急救人员到，告诉他们对方用了什么。
  Note: This is the flip side of Chapter 8, Item 29 (drug offenses): possession is illegal, but calling to save a life isn't punished. After calling 911, stay until medics arrive and tell them what was taken.

### 46. 山火季：收到 evacuation warning 就收拾，收到 order 立刻走；go bag 平时备好 / Wildfire Season: Pack on a Warning, Leave on an Order — Keep a Go Bag Ready（新增条目 / New item）
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=寿命 -->
- 成本：go bag 一次配齐约 $50–150；平时花 1 小时收拾。
  Cost: About $50–150 to assemble a go bag once; an hour of prep.
- 说人话：加州的山火不等人。县里发 evacuation warning（预警）就开始收拾、把车头朝外停好；发 evacuation order（撤离令）就立刻走，别等、别回头拿东西。平时备好 go bag：证件复印件、处方药、3 天食物和水、N95 口罩、充电器、现金、手电。去 readyforwildfire.org 查你家的撤离路线，注册县里的紧急短信警报。
  In plain terms: California wildfires don't wait. On an evacuation warning, start packing and park facing outward; on an evacuation order, leave immediately — don't wait, don't go back for things. Keep a go bag ready: copies of documents, prescriptions, 3 days of food and water, N95 masks, chargers, cash, a flashlight. Check your evacuation routes at readyforwildfire.org and sign up for your county's emergency text alerts.
- 收益：Cal Fire "Ready, Set, Go"：Ready（清理房屋周边可燃物、定撤离计划）、Set（收到预警打包 go bag）、Go（收到撤离令立刻走）。go bag 按 Cal Fire 清单：至少两条撤离路线的地图、处方药和眼镜、备用车钥匙、现金/卡、急救包、手电加电池供电收音机、重要文件复印件、每人 3 天食物和水、宠物用品（加州）。
  Benefit: Cal Fire's "Ready, Set, Go": Ready (clear flammables around the home, make an evacuation plan), Set (pack the go bag on a warning), Go (leave at once on an order). Per Cal Fire's checklist, a go bag holds: a map with at least two evacuation routes, prescriptions and glasses, spare car keys, cash/cards, a first-aid kit, a flashlight plus a battery-powered radio, copies of important documents, 3 days of food and water per person, and pet supplies (California).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：CAL FIRE, Ready for Wildfire. <https://www.readyforwildfire.org/>
- 备注：烟雾天待在室内、开空气净化器；N95 出门才戴，普通布口罩挡不住 PM2.5。开车撤离走官方路线，别自己抄近道——火比人跑得快。
  Note: On smoky days stay indoors with an air purifier running; N95s are for going out — cloth masks don't stop PM2.5. Drive the official evacuation routes, don't improvise shortcuts — fire moves faster than people.
