# 10. 恋爱和结婚划不划算·加州版

本节把「结婚划不划算」拆成钱、时间、健康、法律风险和关系质量几笔账，分开算，每笔账只用能查到出处的数字。本章的法律和制度内容按加州及美国联邦现行规定写——加州是共同财产制（community property）州，也是无过错离婚（no-fault divorce）州，同性婚姻合法；健康科学和关系心理类条目的证据与数字沿用原书，只做本地化说明。这里不做道德判断，也不替你下结论。健康那几笔按死亡风险算，时间那几笔按每天多少分钟算，法律那几笔按法条怎么写算。这三种算法之间不互相换算。

This chapter breaks "is marriage worth it" into separate ledgers — money, time, health, legal risk, and relationship quality — each backed only by numbers with a traceable source. The legal and institutional content follows current California and U.S. federal rules: California is a community-property state and a no-fault-divorce state, and same-sex marriage is legal here. The evidence and numbers for the health-science and relationship-psychology items carry over from the original book, with localization notes only. No moral judgments here, and no verdict on your behalf. Health items are measured in mortality risk, time items in minutes per day, legal items by what the statutes actually say. The three yardsticks don't convert into each other.

本节条目按主题分成下面几块，括号里是条号。

**找对象和谈恋爱**：认识更多人（第 1 条），对方拒绝之后就停下（第 2 条），看行为判断有没有兴趣（第 3 条），轮流回答由浅入深的问题（第 5 条），异地不比同城差（第 6 条）。
Finding a partner and dating: meet more people (item 1), stop when the other person says no (item 2), read actions instead of "signals" (item 3), take turns answering progressively deeper questions (item 5), long-distance is no worse than same-city (item 6).

**关系好不好**：看自己在关系里的感受（第 4 条），吵架对事不对人（第 17 条），一起去做伴侣治疗（第 20 条）。
Relationship quality: trust your own feelings inside the relationship (item 4), argue about the issue, not the person (item 17), try couples therapy together (item 20).

**结婚划不划算的几笔账**：先看登记数据（第 7 条），健康收益按观察性数据打折（第 8 条），时间账按无酬劳动算（第 9 条），算退出成本（第 15 条），为长辈结婚单独记账（第 16 条）。
The ledgers of marriage: look at the registration data first (item 7), discount the health benefits as observational data (item 8), count the time ledger as unpaid labor (item 9), price the exit (item 15), keep a separate ledger for marrying for your parents (item 16).

**领证和婚检**：去 county clerk 办结婚执照（第 13 条），婚前自愿做一次健康检查（第 14 条）。
Getting licensed and premarital health: get the marriage license at the county clerk (item 13), get a voluntary premarital health check (item 14).

**钱和债**：钱账先看法律默认规则（第 10 条），父母出资买房写清是借是送（第 11 条），配偶一方大额借的钱（第 12 条）。
Money and debt: start with the legal default rules (item 10), put parental down-payment help in writing as gift or loan (item 11), debts one spouse takes on alone (item 12).

**同性伴侣和重要事实**：同性伴侣在加州可直接结婚，未婚伴侣办好医疗和遗产文件（第 18 条），别靠隐瞒重要事实结婚（第 19 条）。
Same-sex couples and material facts: same-sex couples can marry directly in California; unmarried partners should get health-care and estate documents in order (item 18); don't marry by hiding material facts (item 19).

### 1. 认识更多人，而不是死磕一个：两个人合不合得来，见面之前预测不出来 / Meet more people instead of fixating on one: you can't predict chemistry before meeting
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。时间花在多认识几个人上，别反复琢磨同一个人。难在忍住不死磕。
  Cost: No money. Spend the time meeting more people instead of ruminating over one person. The hard part is resisting the urge to fixate.
- 说人话：两场闪电约会研究让人在见面前填了 100 多项择偶问卷。问卷只能算出谁普遍容易喜欢别人、谁普遍受欢迎，算不出这两个人之间来不来电。合不合得来只有见了面才知道。所以多认识几个人，比押在一个人身上划算。
  In plain terms: Two speed-dating studies had people fill out 100+ item mate-preference questionnaires before meeting. The questionnaires could predict who is generally easy to like and who is generally popular — but not whether two specific people would click. Chemistry is only knowable face to face. So meeting more people beats betting everything on one person.
- 收益：两场闪电约会研究里，参与者见面前先填了 100 多项与择偶有关的自陈量表（自己给自己打分的问卷）。研究者用随机森林模型（一种找规律的算法）来算。能算出「一个人整体上有多容易喜欢别人」，这些问卷能说明其中 4%–18% 的方差，方差就是人与人之间的差别，问卷只能说明其中一小部分。也能算出「一个人整体上有多受欢迎」，能说明 7%–27%。但「这两个具体的人之间来不来电」算不出来，会前的特质和偏好怎么组合都算不出来。同一批研究者更早还做过一项闪电约会实验。参与者会前自己报了理想型偏好，比如看重外貌还是看重经济前景。这个偏好预测不了他们在现场对具体某个人的兴趣。
  Benefit: In two speed-dating studies, participants completed 100+ self-report mate-preference items before meeting, and researchers ran random-forest models on them. The questionnaires could explain 4%–18% of the variance in how romantically desirable a person generally was, and 7%–27% of how generally popular they were — variance being the differences between people, of which the questionnaires explain only a small slice. But "whether these two specific people click" could not be predicted, no matter how pre-meeting traits and preferences were combined. The same researchers had earlier run a speed-dating experiment in which participants stated their ideal-partner preferences in advance (e.g., valuing looks vs. earning prospects) — those preferences did not predict their live interest in any specific person.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Joel S, Eastwick PW, Finkel EJ (2017). Is romantic desire predictable? Machine learning applied to initial romantic attraction. Psychological Science, 28(10), 1478-1489. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797617714580>；Eastwick PW, Finkel EJ (2008). Sex differences in mate preferences revisited: do people know what they initially desire in a romantic partner? Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 94(2), 245-264. <https://doi.org/10.1037/0022-3514.94.2.245>
- 备注：两项研究只说明「见面之前算不出来」，没说「怎么努力都没用」。合不合得来只能见了面才知道，所以把时间花在多认识几个人上，每个都真正见一面，比全押在一个人身上划算。两项研究都在美国大学完成，对加州读者可直接参考。
  Note: The two studies only show "you can't compute it before meeting" — not "effort is useless." Since chemistry is only knowable face to face, spending time meeting more people, each in person, beats betting everything on one. Both studies were done at U.S. universities, so California readers can apply them directly.

### 2. 对方明确拒绝之后就停下：继续纠缠可能构成跟踪罪，对方还能申请人身保护令 / Stop after a clear rejection: continued pursuit can be stalking, and the other person can get a restraining order
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不花时间。难在忍住：不再发消息，不再去等人。
  Cost: No money, no time. The hard part is restraint: no more messages, no more waiting around for them.
- 说人话：加州没有中国的治安拘留制度，对应的是两条路。刑事上，反复跟踪、骚扰、让对方害怕，可能构成跟踪罪（stalking，加州《刑法典》第 646.9 条），可按轻罪或重罪处罚。民事上，对方可以向法院申请人身保护令：非亲密关系的骚扰走民事骚扰限制令（《民事诉讼法典》第 527.6 条），法院可以命令你不得接近、不得联系。违反限制令会被逮捕。
  In plain terms: California has no administrative-detention system like China's; the counterparts are twofold. Criminally, repeated following or harassment that puts someone in fear can be stalking (California Penal Code §646.9), punishable as a misdemeanor or felony. Civilly, the other person can ask a court for a restraining order — for non-intimate harassment, a civil harassment restraining order (Code of Civil Procedure §527.6) can order you to stay away and make no contact. Violating it gets you arrested.
- 收益：《刑法典》第 646.9 条把"反复跟踪、骚扰并让对方合理害怕"定为犯罪；《民事诉讼法典》第 527.6 条允许受骚扰的人向法院申请临时限制令和听证后的限制令，内容包括禁止骚扰、跟踪、接近、联系。限制令在全州联网，警察可凭此逮捕违反者。
  Benefit: Penal Code §646.9 criminalizes willfully and repeatedly following or harassing someone in a way that makes them reasonably fear for their safety; Code of Civil Procedure §527.6 lets a harassed person petition the court for a temporary restraining order and an order after hearing, enjoining harassment, stalking, contact, and coming within a set distance. Orders are statewide and enforceable by arrest.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Penal Code §646.9；California Code of Civil Procedure §527.6. <https://law.justia.com/codes/california/2017/code-ccp/part-2/title-7/chapter-3/section-527.6/>
- 备注：「死追到底会感动对方」没有任何研究支持，但跟踪罪的案底和限制令是白纸黑字的。追一个人和骚扰一个人的界线，不看你怎么想，看对方有没有明确说过拒绝。
  Note: No research supports "persistence will win them over," but a stalking record and a restraining order are on paper. The line between pursuing someone and harassing them isn't drawn by your intentions — it's drawn by whether they clearly said no.

### 3. 判断对方有没有兴趣，看行为不看「信号」：谁主动、谁安排、谁跟进 / To tell if they're interested, watch actions not "signals": who initiates, who plans, who follows up
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不花多少时间。难在改习惯：别再琢磨对方的表情和语气，改成记录事实。
  Cost: No money, barely any time. The hard part is breaking the habit: stop decoding their expressions and tone, start recording facts.
- 说人话：让 52 对陌生男女聊十来分钟，再互相判断对方有没有在调情。结果是「对方没在调情」判得比较准，「对方在调情」反而看不出来。换成 261 个人看视频判断，结论一样。所以别猜表情和语气，看行为：谁主动发起，谁安排下一次，你不推进时对方跟不跟进。
  In plain terms: Researchers had 52 pairs of strangers chat for about ten minutes, then judge whether the other person had been flirting. "They weren't flirting" was judged fairly accurately; "they were flirting" was not. A second study with 261 video observers found the same. So stop guessing from expressions and tone — watch behavior: who initiates, who schedules the next one, whether they follow up when you don't push.
- 收益：两项研究测了人识别调情的准确率。第一项让 52 对互不相识的异性两两交谈 10 到 12 分钟。谈完各自报告自己有没有在调情，也报告觉得对方有没有在调情。结果是「没有发生调情的互动被判断得更准，发生了调情的互动反而判断得更差」。第二项用 26 段一分钟的视频，给 261 名旁观者判断，结论一致。人能比较可靠地看出「对方没在调情」，却很难看出「对方在调情」。
  Benefit: Two studies measured flirting-detection accuracy. In the first, 52 stranger pairs of opposite sexes talked for 10–12 minutes, then each reported whether they themselves had flirted and whether they thought the other had. Interactions without flirting were judged more accurately; interactions with flirting were judged worse. The second study showed 26 one-minute videos to 261 observers and found the same. People can fairly reliably tell when someone is NOT flirting, but are poor at telling when they ARE.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Hall JA, Xing C, Brooks S (2015). Accurately detecting flirting: error management theory, the traditional sexual script, and flirting base rate. Communication Research, 42(7), 939-958. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0093650214534972>；Perilloux C, Kurzban R (2015). Do men overperceive women's sexual interest? Psychological Science, 26(1), 70-77. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797614555727>（争议方）
- 备注：争议。另有研究认为，不是男性高估了女性的性意图，而是女性填问卷时把自己的意图报低了。到底谁判断得更准，仍无定论。但两派都同意一件事：从表情、语气这类细微线索反推对方心思，准确率很低。看得见的行为要可靠得多：对方是否主动发起，是否安排下一次，是否在你不推进时也跟进。研究对象是美国人，对加州读者直接适用。
  Note: Disputed. Another line of research argues men don't overperceive women's interest — women underreport their own intent on questionnaires. Who judges more accurately is unsettled. But both camps agree on one thing: inferring intentions from subtle cues like expressions and tone is unreliable. Observable behavior is far more reliable: does the other person initiate, schedule the next meeting, follow up when you don't push. The subjects were Americans, so California readers can apply this directly.

### 4. 关系好不好，主要看你自己在这段关系里的感受，不是看对方条件 / How good the relationship is depends mostly on how YOU feel in it — not on your partner's résumé
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不花多少时间。难在改视角：把择偶清单收起来，改成看相处过程。
  Cost: No money, barely any time. The hard part is shifting perspective: put away the partner checklist and look at how it feels day to day.
- 说人话：最能说明关系好不好的，是你自己的感受，不是对方的条件。比如你觉得对方认不认真、欣不欣赏你，你们吵得多不多。你自己的感受比伴侣的说法管用二到四倍。但这段关系往后变好还是变坏，任何问卷都基本算不出来。
  In plain terms: The strongest predictors of relationship quality are your own feelings about the relationship — not your partner's credentials. Things like how committed and appreciative they feel to you, and how much you fight. Your own reports predict two to four times better than your partner's reports. But no questionnaire can meaningfully predict whether the relationship will get better or worse from here.
- 收益：29 个实验室、43 份纵向配对数据的机器学习分析，纵向就是跟着同一批伴侣一路记录。最能预测关系质量的几项，全部来自当事人对这段关系的感受：感觉到对方的承诺、欣赏、性满意度、感觉到对方满意、冲突。跟个人有关的几项里最强的是生活满意度、负面情绪、抑郁、回避型依恋、焦虑型依恋。关于关系的这些项，在刚开始调查时最多能解释 45% 的方差。方差指伴侣与伴侣之间关系质量的差别，45% 是其中能用这些项说清的部分。到研究结束时最多解释 18%。而且「自己报告的那些项」的预测力，是「伴侣报告的那些项」的二到四倍。在自己报告的关系感受之外，再把个体差异和伴侣的报告加进来，没有多出任何预测力。
  Benefit: A machine-learning analysis across 29 labs and 43 longitudinal couples datasets (following the same couples over time). The strongest predictors of relationship quality all came from the person's own feelings about the relationship: perceived partner commitment, appreciation, sexual satisfaction, perceived partner satisfaction, and conflict. The strongest individual-level predictors were life satisfaction, negative affect, depression, avoidant attachment, and anxious attachment. Relationship variables explained up to 45% of the variance at baseline (variance = the differences in relationship quality between couples; 45% is the share these variables account for), and up to 18% by the end of the study. Self-reported relationship variables predicted two to four times better than partner-reported ones — and adding individual differences plus partner reports on top added no predictive power at all.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Joel S, Eastwick PW, Allison CJ, et al. (2020). Machine learning uncovers the most robust self-report predictors of relationship quality across 43 longitudinal couples studies. Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, 117(32), 19061-19071. <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1917036117>
- 备注：同一项研究还发现，这段关系在研究期间是变好还是变坏，用问卷里任何一组自评指标都基本算不出来。所以判断眼下这段关系好不好，问自己的感受比核对对方的条件清单准，但别指望能预测它以后会怎样。关系好坏和身体好坏连在一起的那笔账，见本节第 17 条（把关系好坏当成一笔健康账）。
  Note: The same study found that whether the relationship improved or deteriorated during the study could barely be predicted by any set of self-report measures. So for judging how good the relationship is right now, your own feelings beat checking your partner's credentials — but don't expect to predict where it's headed. For the ledger linking relationship quality to physical health, see item 17 of this chapter (relationship quality as a health account).

### 5. 想拉近距离就轮流回答一组由浅入深的问题，别学话术 / To grow closer, take turns answering a set of progressively deeper questions — skip the pickup lines
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。要花 45 分钟。
  Cost: No money. Takes 45 minutes.
- 说人话：两个陌生人轮流回答 36 道由浅入深的问题，45 分钟后，比光闲聊同样久的人亲近得多。起作用的是轮流说真话、一层层往深里说，不靠话术。这只说明当时更亲近，不代表能发展成长期关系。
  In plain terms: Two strangers taking turns answering 36 progressively personal questions felt much closer after 45 minutes than pairs who just made small talk for the same time. What worked was taking turns telling the truth, layer by layer — not lines or tactics. This only shows closeness in the moment, not that it leads to a long-term relationship.
- 收益：把互不相识的两人配对，让他们轮流回答三组逐步深入的问题，一共 36 题。问题从「你最想请谁吃饭」一直到「上一次当着别人的面哭是什么时候」。45 分钟后的亲近感，显著高于做同样时长闲聊的对照组。也高于该研究中大学生对自己现实中最亲密关系的平均评分。
  Benefit: Stranger pairs took turns answering three sets of increasingly personal questions, 36 in total — from "who would you most like to have dinner with" to "when did you last cry in front of another person." After 45 minutes, closeness was significantly higher than in the small-talk control group, and even higher than the students' average rating of their closest real-life relationships.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Aron A, Melinat E, Aron EN, Vallone RD, Bator RJ (1997). The experimental generation of interpersonal closeness: a procedure and some preliminary findings. Personality and Social Psychology Bulletin, 23(4), 363-377. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0146167297234003>
- 备注：只做过一次实验，对象是大学生，只测了短时间内的亲近感，没测能不能发展成长期关系。实验里的亲近感来自两个人轮流、一层层往深里说自己的真实情况，靠的不是话术、套路或单方面表现。所以聊天时可以「我说一点真实的，也问一句一样深的」，两边轮流。
  Note: One experiment only, on college students, measuring only short-term closeness — not whether it leads to long-term relationships. The closeness came from both people taking turns going deeper with real disclosures, not from lines, routines, or one-sided performance. So in conversation: share something real, then ask something equally deep — take turns.

### 6. 异地不比同城差，真正的坎是搬到一起之后的头三个月 / Long-distance is no worse than same-city — the real hurdle is the first three months after moving in together
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：要花路费，还要花来回路上的时间。见面次数比同城少。难在长期扛住见不着面。
  Cost: Travel money and travel time. Fewer in-person meetings than same-city couples. The hard part is enduring long stretches apart.
- 说人话：异地在分开期间反而更稳，因为见得少，容易把对方想得比实际好。真正的坎在搬到一起之后。追踪数据里约一半异地伴侣走到了同城这一步，另一半在分开期间就结束了。重聚的这一半里，三分之一在 3 个月内分手。
  In plain terms: Long-distance couples are actually more stable while apart, because seeing each other less makes it easy to idealize the partner. The real hurdle comes after moving to the same city. In tracking data, about half of long-distance couples made it to the move-in-together stage; the other half ended while apart. Of those who reunited, a third broke up within 3 months.
- 收益：两项研究的结论一致。异地恋在分开期间反而比同城恋更稳定。原因是异地关系里把对方想得比实际更好的程度明显更高，研究测的是理想化失真、浪漫爱、关系回忆、感知到的一致这几项。而且见面越少，想得越美。但这种稳定只在双方仍然分开时成立，一旦变成同城，异地伴侣更容易分手。追踪异地转同城的那项研究给出的比例是：约一半异地伴侣走到了搬到一起这一步，另一半在分开期间就结束了。在重聚的这一半里，三分之一在重聚后 3 个月内分手。分开期间见面间隔越长、理想化越强，重聚后越不稳。
  Benefit: Two studies agree. Long-distance relationships are more stable than same-city ones while the couple remains apart, because idealization runs higher — measured as idealized distortion, romantic love, relationship memories, and perceived agreement — and the less they meet, the rosier the picture. But this stability only holds while apart; once geographically close, long-distance couples break up more easily. The tracking study found: about half of long-distance couples made it to living together, the other half ended while apart. Of the reunited half, a third split within 3 months of reuniting. Longer gaps between visits and stronger idealization while apart predicted shakier reunions.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Stafford L, Merolla AJ, Castle JD (2006). When long-distance dating partners become geographically close. ### 8. 把健康收益算进去，但按观察性数据打折 / Count the health benefits, but discount them as observational data
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，也不花时间。只要记住一点：这些数字说的是已婚的人里死亡率更低，是两件事同时出现，不是结婚让你活得久。
  Cost: No money, no time. Just remember one thing: these numbers say married people have lower mortality — two things occurring together, not marriage making you live longer.
- 说人话：已婚的人同期死亡的概率比非婚的人低约 12%，从未结过婚的人比已婚的人高约 24%。但这只是两件事同时出现，不是结婚起的作用。本来健康、收入高、社交多的人，既更容易结婚，也更容易活得久。这些数字基本来自欧美，对加州读者可直接当量级参考。
  In plain terms: Married people have about 12% lower concurrent mortality than unmarried people; never-married people run about 24% higher than married people. But that's correlation, not causation. People who are healthier, wealthier, and more socially connected to begin with are both more likely to marry and more likely to live long. These numbers come mostly from Western populations — California readers can use them directly as order-of-magnitude references.
- 收益：一项针对老年人的荟萃分析，把多项研究汇总重算，共 53 组比较、25 万余人。已婚相对非婚的全因死亡 RR 0.88，95% CI 0.85–0.91（RR 是风险比，CI 是可信范围），同期死亡的概率低约 12%。同一项里丧偶 1.11、离异或分居 1.16、未婚 1.11，分别高约 11%、16%、11%。男女无差异。最保守的估计是 RR 0.94，只低约 6%。另一项荟萃分析比的是未婚（从未结婚）相对已婚，用了 95 篇文献、5 亿余人。结果 HR 1.24（1.19–1.30），同期死亡的概率高约 24%。而且年纪越大，差距越小。还有一项按性别拆的荟萃分析，21 项研究、789 万余人：不论男女，非婚都与总死亡、癌症死亡、心血管死亡同时出现。男性这边的心血管死亡和总死亡差距更大。未婚男性相对未婚女性，卒中死亡多 31%，全因死亡多 9%。
  Benefit: A meta-analysis of older adults pooled 53 comparisons with 250,000+ people. Married vs. unmarried all-cause mortality RR 0.88, 95% CI 0.85–0.91 (RR = risk ratio, CI = confidence interval) — about 12% lower concurrent mortality. In the same analysis: widowed 1.11, divorced/separated 1.16, never-married 1.11 — about 11%, 16%, and 11% higher. No sex difference. The most conservative estimate was RR 0.94, only about 6% lower. Another meta-analysis compared never-married vs. married across 95 studies and 500+ million people: HR 1.24 (1.19–1.30), about 24% higher concurrent mortality, with the gap shrinking at older ages. A sex-stratified meta-analysis of 21 studies and 7.89 million people found unmarried status associated with total, cancer, and cardiovascular mortality in both sexes; the cardiovascular and total-mortality gaps were larger for men. Never-married men vs. never-married women: 31% more stroke deaths, 9% more all-cause deaths.
- 证据等级：A（争议） / Evidence grade: A (disputed)
- 来源：Manzoli L, et al. (2007). Marital status and mortality in the elderly: a systematic review and meta-analysis. Soc Sci Med 64:77–94. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.socscimed.2006.08.031>；Roelfs DJ, et al. (2011). The rising relative risk of mortality for singles: meta-analysis and meta-regression. Am J Epidemiol 174(4):379–389. <https://doi.org/10.1093/aje/kwr111>；Wang Y, et al. (2020). Sex differences in the association between marital status and the risk of cardiovascular, cancer, and all-cause mortality: a systematic review and meta-analysis of 7,881,040 individuals. Glob Health Res Policy 5:4. <https://doi.org/10.1186/s41256-020-00133-8>
- 备注：争议在于结婚的人本来就是被挑过的：健康、收入高、社交多的人，既更容易结婚，也更容易活得久。三项荟萃用的都是现成人群的观察数据，只是跟踪记录，没有分组对照。作者自己也写了有发表偏倚或方法局限。Roelfs 还发现研究做得越严谨，算出的差距越小。
  Note: The dispute is selection: married people are a filtered group — healthier, higher-income, more socially connected people are both more likely to marry and more likely to live long. All three meta-analyses used observational cohort data — tracking, not randomized trials. The authors themselves note publication bias and methodological limits. Roelfs found that the more rigorous the study, the smaller the gap.

### 10. 钱账先看法律默认规则，再决定要不要书面约定 / Start the money ledger with the legal defaults, then decide on a written agreement
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。读一遍下面几条默认规则；有需要就签婚前或婚后财产协议（prenuptial/postnuptial agreement），加州要求书面。难在把钱的事提前说开。
  Cost: No money. Read the default rules below once; sign a prenuptial or postnuptial agreement if needed — California requires it in writing. The hard part is talking about money upfront.
- 说人话：不签书面协议，加州默认：婚姻期间挣的工资、经营和投资收益、用婚后收入买的东西，原则上都是夫妻共同财产（community property），离婚时一人一半（Family Code §760）。婚前已有的财产、婚内明确只赠与或只继承给一方的财产，是个人财产（separate property，§770）。双方可以签婚前协议改变默认规则，加州按《统一婚前协议法》（Family Code §§1610–1617）执行：必须书面、双方签字，签之前至少 7 天拿到最终文本，充分披露财产债务，最好各请独立律师。
  In plain terms: Without a written agreement, California defaults to: wages earned during the marriage, business and investment gains, and things bought with marital earnings are community property, split 50/50 at divorce (Family Code §760). Property owned before marriage, and gifts or inheritances clearly given to one spouse, are separate property (§770). Couples can contract around the defaults with a prenuptial agreement, governed by California's Uniform Premarital Agreement Act (Family Code §§1610–1617): it must be in writing and signed by both parties, each side must receive the final text at least 7 days before signing, with full financial disclosure and preferably independent counsel for each.
- 收益：不另作约定时：婚后所得的工资奖金、经营投资收益、婚后购置的财产，推定为夫妻共同财产（§760）；婚前财产、明确只给一方的赠与和继承，是个人财产（§770）。婚前协议必须书面、双方签字（§1611），可约定财产权利义务、离婚时的处置、遗嘱安排等（§1612）；执行力要求自愿、有 7 天审查期、充分披露（§1615）。
  Benefit: Without an agreement: wages, business and investment gains, and property acquired during marriage are presumed community property (§760); premarital property and gifts/inheritances clearly given to one spouse are separate property (§770). A premarital agreement must be in writing and signed by both (§1611), and may cover property rights and obligations, disposition on divorce, and will provisions (§1612); enforceability requires voluntariness, a 7-day review period, and full disclosure (§1615).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Family Code §§760, 770, 1610–1617. <https://legalclarity.org/california-family-code-760-community-property-explained/>；<https://www.findlaw.com/state/california-law/california-prenuptial-agreements.html>
- 备注：中国民法典第 1088 条那种「家务补偿请求权」，加州没有直接对应条款；离婚时的赡养费（spousal support）会综合考虑双方对家庭的贡献、婚姻长短、双方经济状况等因素（Family Code §4320），没有固定公式。书面协议对两边都管用：收入高的一方用得上，家务照料这类没报酬的活干得多的一方也用得上。
  Note: California has no direct equivalent of China's "housework compensation" claim. Spousal support at divorce weighs factors including each spouse's contributions to the family, marriage length, and economic circumstances (Family Code §4320) — no fixed formula. A written agreement serves both sides: the higher earner and the one doing more unpaid housework and caregiving.

### 11. 父母出资买房，在转账的时候就把是借是送写清楚 / When parents help with the down payment, put "gift or loan" in writing at transfer time
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。出资当天写一页说明，写明这笔钱是赠与谁、还是借给谁，出资的父母和子女都签字。如果写的是借款，就让夫妻两个人都签，转账备注也写同一句话。
  Cost: No money. On the day of the transfer, write a one-page statement saying who the money is a gift to — or a loan to — signed by the parents and the child. If it's a loan, have both spouses sign, and put the same wording in the transfer memo.
- 说人话：加州是共同财产制，婚后没有书面约定，父母给的钱推定为夫妻共同财产，房本写谁的名字不决定归属。想让它算「只给自己孩子」的个人财产，靠书面赠与说明加资金追踪（tracing）来证明；想算借款，就按真借款办：双方签字、约定还款、留下还款记录。
  In plain terms: California is a community-property state. Money from parents during the marriage, without a written agreement, is presumed community property — whose name is on the deed doesn't decide ownership. To keep it one spouse's separate property, prove it with a written gift statement plus tracing of the funds; to treat it as a loan, do it like a real loan: both spouses sign, agree on repayment terms, keep repayment records.
- 收益：§760 规定婚姻期间所得推定为共同财产，主张是个人财产的一方承担举证责任，靠资金追踪（tracing）证明来源。书面赠与/借款说明加转账记录，是法庭上最管用的证据。
  Benefit: §760 presumes property acquired during marriage is community property; the spouse claiming separate property carries the burden of proof, via tracing the funds to a separate source. A written gift/loan statement plus transfer records is the most effective evidence in court.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Family Code §760. <https://legalclarity.org/california-family-code-760-community-property-explained/>
- 备注：加州没有中国《婚姻家庭编解释（二）》第 8 条那样的成文判房规则，归属按共同财产推定、书面约定和资金追踪综合判断。写清楚对两边都有用：出钱的父母不用指望离婚时补一张借条，接受出资的一方也不用等到离婚才知道这笔钱算什么。
  Note: California has no codified house-award rule like China's; characterization turns on the community-property presumption, written agreements, and tracing. Writing it down serves both sides: the parents don't have to hope a backdated IOU saves the money at divorce, and the receiving spouse doesn't wait until divorce to learn what the money was.

### 12. 配偶一方大额借的钱：共同财产会被债权人追，别在借条上签字 / Debts one spouse takes on alone: creditors can reach community property — don't sign the note
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。别在配偶的借条、担保书上签「共同借款人」「担保人」。也别在聊天里写「这钱我们会还」这类话，它可能被当成你事后认下了这笔债。难在被催的时候忍住不接话。
  Cost: No money. Don't sign your spouse's loan papers or guarantees as "co-borrower" or "guarantor." Don't write "we'll pay it back" in chats either — it can be read as ratifying the debt after the fact. The hard part is not engaging when collectors call.
- 说人话：加州和中国这条正好反过来。中国是「没签字就不自动成共同债务」，加州是共同财产原则上要对夫妻任何一方在婚内欠的债负责（Family Code §910）——不管谁签的字。所以别在借条上签字，签了你就是共同债务人。唯一的挡板是 §911：你婚内的工资如果存在对方无权支取、且未与共同财产混同的独立账户里，配偶婚前欠的债动不了这笔钱；你自己的婚前个人财产也不对配偶的债负责（§913）。
  In plain terms: California is the mirror image of China's rule. In China, a debt you didn't sign isn't automatically joint; in California, community property is generally liable for debts either spouse incurs before or during marriage (Family Code §910) — regardless of who signed. So don't sign the note: signing makes you a co-debtor. The one shield is §911: your marital earnings kept in an account your spouse can't withdraw from and uncommingled with community funds can't be reached for your spouse's premarital debts; your own premarital separate property isn't liable for your spouse's debts either (§913).
- 收益：§910：共同财产对夫妻任何一方婚前或婚内所欠债务负责；§911：婚内工资单独存放、对方无支取权且未混同时，不对配偶婚前债务负责；§913：一方的个人财产不对配偶的债务负责。
  Benefit: §910: community property is liable for debts either spouse incurs before or during marriage; §911: marital earnings held in a separate, uncommingled account the other spouse can't access aren't liable for the other spouse's premarital debts; §913: one spouse's separate property isn't liable for the other's debts.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Family Code §§910, 911, 913. <https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/family-code/fam-sect-913/>；<https://www.casemine.com/act/us/642421ecd66f1c555c6530f2>
- 备注：买房这类大额借款如果确实用于共同生活，债权人通常能追到共同财产。所以更管用的是第 11 条（父母出资时写清是借是送）那一步。
  Note: For big loans like a mortgage that genuinely funded the shared household, creditors can usually reach community property. So the more effective move is item 11 — documenting parental money as gift or loan at transfer time.

### 17. 把关系好坏当成一笔健康账：吵架对事不对人，别骂人、别挖苦 / Treat relationship quality as a health account: argue about the issue, not the person — no insults, no contempt
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->

  Cost: No money. In a fight, talk only about the matter at hand — no insults, no contempt, no dredging up old grievances. The hard part is controlling your mouth when angry.
- 说人话：夫妻关系好的，身体好一些，死亡风险也低一些。这个关联不大，和饮食对健康的关联差不多大。一项实验里，吵架时一直带着敌意的夫妻，实验做出的小伤口愈合速度只有平和夫妻的六成。

In a crossover experiment, 42 healthy couples each did two 24-hour hospital stays: one with a supportive conversation, one discussing a disagreement. After the conflict visit, experiment-induced blister wounds healed more slowly. Couples showing more hostility in both visits healed at 60% the speed of low-hostility couples, and their blood inflammatory markers (IL-6, TNF) rose more the next morning.

Some studies held the association after adjusting for age and income. Robles notes publication bias in some results. Studies directly comparing sexes found little sex difference. If fights are fierce and unchangeable, try couples therapy together — see item 20 of this chapter. To think through whether to leave, see item 15 of this chapter (exit costs).

- 成本：要花钱。美国按次收费，保险覆盖情况不一，本条不写数字。研究里一般每周去一次，一项试验前后做了约 8 个月。难在两个人都得去，还得坚持做完。
  Cost: It costs money. U.S. therapists charge per session; insurance coverage varies — no numbers in this item. Studies typically ran weekly sessions; one trial lasted about 8 months. The hard part is getting both partners to go and finish.
- 说人话：关系卡住的伴侣一起做完伴侣治疗，对这段关系明显满意多了，大约七成有好转。光排队干等的伴侣，基本没变化。但效果会变淡：跟到第 5 年，约一半还明显好着，约四分之一已经分居或离婚。
  In plain terms: Stuck couples who completed couples therapy ended up markedly more satisfied — about 70% improved. Couples who just waited on a list barely changed. But the effects fade: at the 5-year follow-up, about half were still markedly better, while about a quarter had separated or divorced.
- 收益：2020 年一项荟萃分析汇总了 58 项研究、2092 对伴侣。做完伴侣治疗，关系满意度的前后变化 g = 1.12（可信范围 0.92–1.31），作者说是大效应。g 是效应量。分到排队等候的伴侣，同一段时间 g = 0.12（−0.04 到 0.29），没有明显改善。开始时关系越差的，改善反而越多。2012 年一篇综述写的是，伴侣治疗让 70% 接受治疗的伴侣有好转。另一项随机试验找了 134 对长期、严重不和的夫妻，做了约 8 个月的两种行为伴侣治疗。做完时满意度变化 d = 0.90 和 0.71。到第 5 年，两种做法分别有 50.0% 和 45.9% 的夫妻仍有明显好转。这里的明显好转，作者叫「有临床意义的改善」。同时有 25.7% 和 27.9% 已经分居或离婚。
  Benefit: A 2020 meta-analysis pooled 58 studies and 2,092 couples. After couples therapy, relationship-satisfaction change was g = 1.12 (CI 0.92–1.31) — a large effect, the authors say (g = effect size). Waitlisted couples over the same period: g = 0.12 (−0.04 to 0.29), no meaningful improvement. Couples who started worse improved more. A 2012 review put it at 70% of treated couples improving. In a randomized trial, 134 long-term, severely distressed couples did about 8 months of two types of behavioral couples therapy: satisfaction change d = 0.90 and 0.71 at completion. At year 5, 50.0% and 45.9% still showed clinically significant improvement — while 25.7% and 27.9% had separated or divorced.

- 备注：争议在效果能保持多久。只收随机试验的另一项荟萃分析里，做完时 g = 0.60，半年后 0.44，到 12 个月几乎没保住（g = 0.06）。另一篇汇总 20 项荟萃分析的综述说，有临床意义的效果出现在 40% 到 50% 的人身上。「七成好转」和「四到五成明显好转」用的标准不一样，别混着比。5 年那项试验没有不做治疗的对照，所以说不清离婚是变多还是变少了。2020 年那项汇总的全是异性伴侣，套到自己身上要打折。美国去哪做：通过保险找 in-network 的持照婚姻家庭治疗师（LMFT）或心理学家，先问清对方的资质和受过什么训练。有家暴的先别一起做：结论只适用于轻到中度冲突，控制型、严重伤害的暴力不在其内。被打了先看第 8 节第 43 条（被家暴了）（原书第 8 节）。吵架时怎么说话见本节第 17 条（吵架对事不对人），想清楚要不要走见本节第 15 条（退出成本）。受益人是你自己和配偶。
  Note: The dispute is how long effects last. In another meta-analysis limited to randomized trials: g = 0.60 at completion, 0.44 at six months, nearly gone by 12 months (g = 0.06). A review of 20 meta-analyses puts clinically significant effects at 40–50% of recipients. "70% improved" and "40–50% markedly improved" use different standards — don't mix them. The 5-year trial had no untreated control, so it can't say whether divorce became more or less likely. The 2020 meta-analysis covered only opposite-sex couples — discount for yourself. Where to go in the U.S.: find an in-network licensed marriage and family therapist (LMFT) or psychologist through your insurance; ask about their credentials and training first. With domestic violence, don't go together yet: the findings cover only mild-to-moderate conflict, not controlling or severely injurious violence.
