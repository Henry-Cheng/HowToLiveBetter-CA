# 3. 不要浪费精力 / 3. Don't Waste Your Energy

本节只算精力和时间账。看的是四样东西：你每天能用的注意力、反应快不快、出错多不多、被打断以后要多久才回得来。所有数字都是直接测出来的，来自实验，或者来自跟踪一群人做的记录（队列研究）。这些数字不换算成寿命，也不换算成死亡率。本节多数条目的证据等级是 B。这个领域本来就这样，不代表建议不成立。原版第 19 条讲的是和中国警察、医生、柜员打交道时该有的预期，引的是中国法规，加州版略去。第 15 条（把悲观念头当症状看）和第 22 条（把「别人要求我完美」当症状看）引的是死亡率与自杀风险的数字，口径和本节其他条目不一样，不放在一起比较。

This section only counts energy and time. It looks at four things: how much attention you have each day, how fast you react, how often you make mistakes, and how long it takes to get back on track after an interruption. Every number here was measured directly — from experiments, or from cohort studies that followed groups of people over time. These numbers are not converted into lifespan or mortality. Most items in this section carry evidence grade B; that is simply the state of this field, and it does not mean the advice is unsound. Item 19 in the original edition covered expectations when dealing with Chinese police officers, doctors, and counter clerks, citing Chinese regulations — it is omitted in the California edition. Item 15 (treating pessimistic thoughts as a symptom) and Item 22 (treating "others demand perfection from me" as a symptom) cite mortality and suicide-risk figures, a different metric from the rest of this section, so they should not be compared directly.

本节条目按主题分成下面几块，括号里是条号。
The items in this section are grouped by theme below, with item numbers in parentheses.

**工作时的注意力**：关掉非必要通知（第 1 条），消息每天批量处理（第 5 条），挡住几秒钟的打断（第 6 条），一次只做一件事（第 7 条），开放式办公室用耳塞（第 12 条），每周工作不超过 49 小时（第 13 条）。
**Attention at work**: turn off nonessential notifications (Item 1), batch messages a few times a day (Item 5), block even seconds-long interruptions (Item 6), do one thing at a time (Item 7), use earplugs in an open-plan office (Item 12), work no more than 49 hours a week (Item 13).

**睡觉和熬夜**：固定起床时间（第 2 条），每晚睡够 7 到 8 小时（第 3 条），下午两点以后不碰咖啡因（第 4 条），睡前一小时不看屏幕（第 8 条），到点就睡（第 9 条），深夜不做重大决定（第 10 条），下午困了就睡 10 分钟（第 11 条）。
**Sleep and late nights**: keep a fixed wake time (Item 2), sleep 7 to 8 hours a night (Item 3), no caffeine after 2 PM (Item 4), no screens in the hour before bed (Item 8), go to bed on time (Item 9), no big decisions late at night (Item 10), nap 10 minutes when sleepy in the afternoon (Item 11).

**脑子里的念头**：反复回想时换个动手的活（第 14 条），把悲观念头当症状看（第 15 条），觉得出丑时把估计除以二（第 21 条），把「别人要求我完美」当症状看（第 22 条）。
**Thoughts in your head**: switch to a hands-on task when ruminating (Item 14), treat pessimistic thoughts as a symptom (Item 15), halve your estimate when you feel embarrassed (Item 21), treat "others demand perfection from me" as a symptom (Item 22).

**生气和情绪低落**：生气时先离场（第 17 条），情绪低落时先做的几件事（第 18 条），生气别靠砸东西发泄（第 23 条），把心事连着几天写下来（第 24 条）。
**Anger and low mood**: leave the room when angry (Item 17), first steps when feeling low (Item 18), don't vent anger by smashing things (Item 23), write your worries down over several days (Item 24).

**人际和社交媒体**：学会拒绝不想接的请求（第 16 条），把警察医生当按规则上班的人（第 19 条，加州版略去），给刷同龄人动态的应用设限（第 20 条）。
**People and social media**: learn to say no to unwanted requests (Item 16), treating police officers and doctors as rule-following workers (Item 19, omitted in the California edition), set limits on apps where you scroll peers' updates (Item 20).

### 1. 关掉非必要通知，工作时把手机放到视线之外 / Turn Off Nonessential Notifications and Keep Your Phone Out of Sight While Working
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。花几分钟改一下设置，几乎不需要毅力。
  Cost: Free. It takes a few minutes to change the settings and almost no willpower.
- 说人话：手机响一下，哪怕你没看、没碰，注意力任务的成绩也会掉。掉的幅度和你真去接电话、发短信差不多。还有一种情况：手机就搁在桌上，你忍住不看，脑子能用的余量一样会变少。越离不开手机的人，掉得越多。
  In plain terms: One buzz from your phone hurts your performance on attention tasks even if you don't look at it or touch it — about as much as actually answering a call or sending a text. And there's another scenario: your phone just sits on the desk, you resist looking at it, and your available mental bandwidth shrinks anyway. The more dependent you are on your phone, the bigger the drop.
- 收益：只是收到一条通知，不看也不碰，注意力任务的成绩就明显变差。变差的幅度和真的接打电话、发短信相当。这是实验室里做的，受试者是大学生。另有两项实验显示：即便成功克制住不去看手机，自己的手机搁在旁边，也会让脑子能用的余量变少。手机依赖强的人下降最多。
  Benefit: Merely receiving a notification — without looking at it or touching it — measurably worsens performance on attention tasks, by about as much as actually making calls or texting. That was done in a lab with college students as participants. Two other experiments showed that even when people successfully resisted checking, having their own phone nearby reduced their available cognitive capacity. The drop was largest in people most dependent on their phones.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Stothart, Mitchum & Yehnert (2015). The attentional cost of receiving a cell phone notification. Journal of Experimental Psychology: Human Perception and Performance. <https://doi.org/10.1037/xhp0000100>；Ward, Duke, Gneezy & Bos (2017). Brain Drain: The Mere Presence of One's Own Smartphone Reduces Available Cognitive Capacity. Journal of the Association for Consumer Research. <https://doi.org/10.1086/691462>
- 备注：这些都是实验室里的单次任务。天天这样累积下来是什么结果，没人测过。真正需要随叫随到的岗位，可以只留少数几个联系人的通知。
  Note: These were all one-off tasks in labs. Nobody has measured what daily accumulation looks like. If your job truly requires being on call, keep notifications on for just a few contacts.

### 2. 固定起床时间，周末也一样 / Keep a Fixed Wake Time, Weekends Included
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。周末要少睡懒觉，需要一点毅力。
  Cost: Free. Sleeping in less on weekends takes a little willpower.
- 说人话：作息最乱的那五分之一人，生物钟比最规律的那五分之一晚了约 2.5 小时。等于长期活在时差里。作息越规律的学生，成绩也越好。
  In plain terms: The fifth of people with the most irregular schedules had body clocks about 2.5 hours later than the fifth with the most regular schedules — the equivalent of living in permanent jet lag. Students with more regular schedules also got better grades.
- 收益：61 名大学生记录了 30 天。作息最不规律的五分之一，生物钟（昼夜节律）比最规律的五分之一晚约 2.5 小时。具体是褪黑素开始分泌的时刻 00:08 对 21:32。最困、最想睡的时段也晚约 1.8 小时。作息越规律，学业成绩越好（相关系数 r = 0.37）。
  Benefit: 61 college students kept 30 days of records. The fifth with the most irregular sleep/wake patterns had body clocks (circadian rhythms) about 2.5 hours later than the most regular fifth — specifically, melatonin onset at 00:08 versus 21:32. Their sleepiest, most sleep-inclined window was also about 1.8 hours later. More regular schedules were associated with better academic performance (correlation r = 0.37).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Phillips et al. (2017). Irregular sleep/wake patterns are associated with poorer academic performance and delayed circadian and sleep/wake timing. Scientific Reports. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-017-03171-4>
- 备注：只有这一项研究，人数少，也没有分组对照。研究者推算，生物钟的差别主要来自每天晒到的光不一样。所以固定起床时间要配合早上见光，才有效果。偶尔熬夜之后怎么补，见第 2 节第 38 条（熬夜之后第二天晚上就补觉）：办法是当晚早点上床，不是第二天睡到中午。
  Note: This is a single study with few participants and no control group. The researchers estimated that the circadian difference came mostly from different daily light exposure, so a fixed wake time only works if you also get morning light. For how to recover after an occasional late night, see Section 2, Item 38 (catch up by going to bed early the next night): the fix is an earlier bedtime that night, not sleeping until noon the next day.

### 3. 每晚睡够 7 到 8 小时，别把 6 小时当作够用 / Sleep 7 to 8 Hours a Night; Don't Treat 6 Hours as Enough
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。每天要多留 1 到 2 小时给睡眠。早一点上床，需要一点毅力。
  Cost: Free. You need to set aside 1 to 2 more hours a day for sleep, and going to bed earlier takes a little willpower.
- 说人话：连着两周每晚只睡 6 小时，脑子的表现会掉到相当于两天两夜没合眼的水平。麻烦的是自己感觉不到。自己打分的困倦头几天涨完就不再涨，睡 6 小时的人和睡 4 小时的人打分一样困。所以「我睡 6 小时够用」这句话本身就是缺觉的症状。
  In plain terms: Two straight weeks of 6-hour nights drags cognitive performance down to the level of two full days without sleep. The trouble is you can't feel it. Self-rated sleepiness rises for the first few days and then plateaus — 6-hour sleepers rate themselves just as sleepy as 4-hour sleepers. So "6 hours is enough for me" is itself a symptom of sleep deprivation.
- 收益：48 名 21 到 38 岁的健康成人，连续 14 天每晚只睡 6 小时。他们的脑力表现累计下降，掉到相当于连续 2 晚完全不睡的水平。而自己打分的困倦程度只在头几天上升，之后几乎不再增加。6 小时组和 4 小时组的自评困倦没有差别。这是把人随机分成两组做的对照试验（RCT）。另外，短时间不睡（急性睡眠剥夺，不超过 48 小时）对简单注意力走神的效果大小 g = -0.776，对推理准确率 g = -0.125。这部分是把 70 项研究合起来算的。
  Benefit: 48 healthy adults aged 21 to 38 slept only 6 hours a night for 14 straight days. Their cognitive performance declined cumulatively, down to the level of 2 full nights without sleep. Yet their self-rated sleepiness rose only in the first few days and then barely increased further, and the 6-hour group rated themselves no differently from the 4-hour group. This was a randomized controlled trial (RCT). Separately, short-term total sleep loss (acute deprivation, up to 48 hours) had an effect size of g = -0.776 on lapses in simple attention and g = -0.125 on reasoning accuracy — that part pooled 70 studies.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Van Dongen, Maislin, Mullington & Dinges (2003). The cumulative cost of additional wakefulness. Sleep. <https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/26.2.117>；Lim & Dinges (2010). A meta-analysis of the impact of short-term sleep deprivation on cognitive variables. Psychological Bulletin. <https://doi.org/10.1037/a0018883>
- 备注：「自己觉得不困」不是睡够了的证据。长期睡 6 小时的人只是感觉上习惯了，表现并没有跟上。汇总多项研究的那部分说的是整夜不睡。「长期少睡」只有一项随机对照试验支持。睡多久和死亡风险的关系另算，见第 2 节第 13 条（每晚睡 7 小时左右）：那边按死亡率算，7 小时那档风险最低。
  Note: "I don't feel sleepy" is not evidence that you've slept enough. People who chronically sleep 6 hours have only gotten used to the feeling; their performance hasn't caught up. The pooled-studies part refers to whole nights without sleep. "Chronic short sleep" is supported by just one randomized controlled trial. The relationship between sleep duration and mortality risk is calculated separately — see Section 2, Item 13 (about 7 hours a night): by mortality, the 7-hour group has the lowest risk.

### 4. 下午两点以后不碰咖啡因 / No Caffeine After 2 PM
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。习惯下午喝咖啡的人，要忍住不喝，需要一点毅力。
  Cost: Free. If you're used to afternoon coffee, holding back takes a little willpower.
- 说人话：睡前 6 小时喝一大杯咖啡（400 mg），当晚用仪器测出来少睡 1 小时以上。可自己写的睡眠日记根本看不出差别。一杯普通咖啡（107 mg）要在睡前 8.8 小时以上喝完，才不影响睡眠。十一点睡的人，下午两点就是界线。
  In plain terms: A large coffee (400 mg) 6 hours before bed cut objectively measured sleep by more than an hour that night — yet the participants' own sleep diaries showed no difference. A regular cup of coffee (107 mg) needs to be finished at least 8.8 hours before bedtime to leave sleep unaffected. If you go to bed at 11 PM, 2 PM is your cutoff.
- 收益：睡前 6 小时喝 400 mg 咖啡因，仪器测到的总睡眠时间比喝安慰剂少 1.1 到 1.2 小时。安慰剂就是不含咖啡因的对照饮料。可是受试者自己写的睡眠日记没有报出差别，自己察觉不到。把多项研究合起来算（荟萃分析）：咖啡因平均让总睡眠时间减少 45 分钟。躺在床上真正睡着的比例（睡眠效率）下降 7%。一杯咖啡（107 mg）要在睡前 8.8 小时以上喝完，才不影响睡眠。
  Benefit: 400 mg of caffeine 6 hours before bed reduced objectively measured total sleep by 1.1 to 1.2 hours versus a placebo (a caffeine-free control drink). But the participants' own sleep diaries reported no difference — they couldn't tell. A meta-analysis pooling multiple studies: caffeine cut total sleep by 45 minutes on average and reduced sleep efficiency (the share of time in bed actually asleep) by 7%. A cup of coffee (107 mg) must be finished at least 8.8 hours before bedtime to leave sleep unaffected.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Drake, Roehrs, Shambroom & Roth (2013). Caffeine effects on sleep taken 0, 3, or 6 hours before going to bed. Journal of Clinical Sleep Medicine. <https://doi.org/10.5664/jcsm.3170>；Gardiner et al. (2023). The effect of caffeine on subsequent sleep: A systematic review and meta-analysis. Sleep Medicine Reviews. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.smrv.2023.101764>
- 备注：Drake 那项只有一个实验室做过，用的 400 mg 偏大，大约两三杯咖啡。咖啡因代谢慢的人，截止时间还要更早。少喝咖啡因换回来的是睡眠时长，和第 3 条（每晚睡够 7 到 8 小时）叠加。
  Note: The Drake study was done by a single lab and used a fairly large 400 mg dose — about two to three cups of coffee. Slow caffeine metabolizers need an even earlier cutoff. Cutting caffeine buys back sleep duration, stacking with Item 3 (sleep 7 to 8 hours a night).

### 5. 把邮件和消息改成每天固定几次批量处理 / Batch Email and Messages a Few Set Times a Day
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。要花点时间跟同事说清楚你多久回一次。忍住不随时翻邮件，需要一点毅力。
  Cost: Free. It takes a little time to tell colleagues how often you'll reply, and resisting the urge to check email constantly takes a little willpower.
- 说人话：同一批人对比了两周。把邮件限制成每天只查 3 次的那一周，日常压力明显更低，而收发的邮件数量一样多。不加限制的时候，人平均每天要查 15.5 次。
  In plain terms: The same people were compared across two weeks. In the week they limited email to 3 checks a day, daily stress was clearly lower — while the number of emails sent and received stayed the same. Without the limit, people checked email 15.5 times a day on average.
- 收益：124 名成年人，两周里同一批人前后对比（自身对照）。限制为每天查 3 次邮件的那周，日常压力明显低于不限次数那周（效果大小 Cohen's d = 0.37）。两周收到和回复的邮件数量并没有差别。一开始不加限制时，人们平均每天查邮件 15.5 次。
  Benefit: 124 adults, compared against themselves across two weeks (within-subject design). In the week limited to 3 email checks a day, daily stress was clearly lower than in the unlimited week (effect size Cohen's d = 0.37). The number of emails received and replied to did not differ between the two weeks. At baseline without limits, people checked email 15.5 times a day on average.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Kushlev & Dunn (2015). Checking email less frequently reduces stress. Computers in Human Behavior. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chb.2014.11.005>
- 备注：只有一项研究，效果中等偏小，量的是压力，不是干出来的活。即时消息没人直接研究过，这里只是照着推的。
  Note: A single study, with a small-to-medium effect, measuring stress rather than output. Instant messaging hasn't been directly studied; extending this to it is an inference.

### 6. 做需要连续思考的工作时，把哪怕几秒钟的打断也挡在外面 / When Doing Work That Needs Sustained Thought, Block Even Seconds-Long Interruptions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。关上门，戴上耳机，或者挂一个「勿扰」牌。
  Cost: Free. Close the door, put on headphones, or hang a "do not disturb" sign.
- 说人话：一次平均 2.8 秒的打断，就让接下来的操作出错概率翻倍。4.4 秒的打断翻三倍。现场跟踪还发现，被打断的那件事平均要 25 分钟才回得来，中间还插进两件别的事。被打断的人干得更快，但压力、挫败感和费劲程度全都更高。
  In plain terms: An interruption averaging just 2.8 seconds doubles the chance of making an error on the next step; a 4.4-second interruption triples it. Field observation also found that getting back to the interrupted task took 25 minutes on average, with two other things squeezed in between. Interrupted people worked faster, but reported higher stress, frustration, and effort.
- 收益：平均 2.8 秒的打断，会让按顺序一步步做的任务（序列任务）步骤出错率翻倍。4.4 秒的打断让它翻三倍。这是实验室里做的。另一项是对 24 名办公室白领（信息工作者）的现场观察。他们平均每 11 分钟切换一次工作主题。57% 的工作主题被打断。当天回到被打断的那件事上，平均要 25 分 26 秒。中间还插了 2.26 件别的事。还有一项 48 人的实验：被打断的人做得更快，但报告的压力、挫败感、时间压力和费劲程度都更高。
  Benefit: An interruption averaging 2.8 seconds doubles the step error rate on sequential tasks; a 4.4-second interruption triples it — that was in a lab. A separate field observation of 24 office workers (information workers) found they switched work topics every 11 minutes on average, 57% of work topics got interrupted, and getting back to an interrupted topic took 25 minutes 26 seconds on average, with 2.26 other things squeezed in between. Another 48-person experiment: interrupted people worked faster but reported higher stress, frustration, time pressure, and effort.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Altmann, Trafton & Hambrick (2014). Momentary interruptions can derail the train of thought. Journal of Experimental Psychology: General. <https://doi.org/10.1037/a0030986>；Mark, Gonzalez & Harris (2005). No task left behind? Examining the nature of fragmented work. CHI 2005. <https://doi.org/10.1145/1054972.1055017>；Mark, Gudith & Klocke (2008). The cost of interrupted work: More speed and stress. CHI 2008. <https://doi.org/10.1145/1357054.1357072>
- 备注：那项现场研究把打断分成两类：一类是别人来找你的，一类是自己点开的。关门只挡得住前一类。后一类要配合第 1 条（关掉非必要通知）和第 5 条（批量处理消息）一起用。
  Note: The field study split interruptions into two kinds: other people coming to you, and ones you trigger yourself by opening things. Closing the door only blocks the first kind. For the second, combine this with Item 1 (turn off nonessential notifications) and Item 5 (batch messages).

### 7. 一次只做一件事，不要边开会边回消息 / Do One Thing at a Time; Don't Reply to Messages During Meetings
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。开会时忍住不回消息，需要一点毅力。
  Cost: Free. Resisting the urge to reply to messages during meetings takes a little willpower.
- 说人话：任务一切换，反应就更慢，也更容易出错。经常同时开好几个屏的人，并没有练出同时做几件事的本事，反而在任务切换测试上表现更差。
  In plain terms: Every task switch makes you slower to respond and more error-prone. People who constantly juggle multiple screens haven't trained themselves into multitasking — they actually perform worse on task-switching tests.
- 收益：任务切换以后，反应明显更慢，也更容易出错。这部分来自多项研究的综述。另一项实验室研究的受试者是大学生。经常同时用好几种媒体的人（重度媒体多任务者）更容易被不相干的东西和不相干的记忆带跑。他们在任务切换测试上反而表现更差。
  Benefit: After a task switch, responses are clearly slower and errors more likely — that part comes from a review of multiple studies. In another lab study with college students, heavy media multitaskers were more easily distracted by irrelevant stimuli and irrelevant memories, and performed worse on task-switching tests.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Monsell (2003). Task switching. Trends in Cognitive Sciences. <https://doi.org/10.1016/S1364-6613(03)00028-7>；Ophir, Nass & Wagner (2009). Cognitive control in media multitaskers. PNAS. <https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0903620106>

### 10. 深夜不做重大决定、不发要紧的消息 / Don't Make Big Decisions or Send Important Messages Late at Night
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。只是把事情推到睡醒之后再办。
  Cost: Free. It just means pushing things to after you've slept.
- 说人话：人睡饱的时候，很快就能学会躲开高风险的选项。连着两晚没睡之后，反而一次次挑高风险的，年纪越大越明显。少睡几小时有多大影响没人测过，但要紧的决定还是推到睡醒以后。
  In plain terms: Well-rested people quickly learn to avoid high-risk options. After two nights without sleep, people instead pick high-risk options again and again — and the older they are, the stronger the effect. Nobody has measured how much a few hours of short sleep matters, but important decisions should still wait until after sleep.
- 收益：34 名健康人在 49.5 小时不睡之后，做爱荷华赌博任务。这是一种衡量风险决策的实验。休息充足时，他们能很快学会避开高风险选项。缺觉之后，反而更多地选高风险选项。年纪大的人受影响更大。这是一项实验，只有这一项研究。

### 11. 下午困了就睡 10 分钟，不要睡半小时 / When Sleepy in the Afternoon, Nap 10 Minutes, Not 30
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->

Five minutes had almost no benefit. Twenty minutes' benefits didn't appear until 35 minutes later. Thirty minutes brought a spell of sleep inertia first: performance right after waking was worse than baseline, improving only afterward.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B

- 备注：只有一项实验室研究，受试者都是健康年轻人。睡不着也不必勉强。闭着眼躺 10 分钟有没有用，没有研究支持。午睡睡过一小时反而和更高的死亡率、冠心病风险连在一起，见第 2 节第 37 条（午睡控制在半小时内）。

### 13. 每周工作时间不要超过 49 小时 / Don't Work More Than 49 Hours a Week
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：可能少拿一些加班费。也可能要花力气和上级谈。
  Cost: You might earn less overtime pay, and it may take effort to negotiate with your manager.
- 说人话：一战时英国军工厂的记录显示，每周干 49 小时以内，干多久就出多少活。超过 49 小时，多干一小时多出的活越来越少。一周干 70 小时和干 56 小时出的活几乎一样，多干的那 14 小时是白干。
  In plain terms: Records from British munitions factories in World War I show that up to 49 hours a week, output rose in proportion to hours. Past 49 hours, each extra hour added less and less. A 70-hour week produced almost the same output as a 56-hour week — the extra 14 hours were worked for nothing.
- 收益：一战时期英国军工厂工人的产出记录：每周 49 小时以内，产出与工时成正比。超过 49 小时，产出的增速开始变慢。大约 63 小时达到最大。干 70 小时的产出与干 56 小时几乎没有差别，多干的 14 小时等于白干。

  Note: The data comes from factories a century ago — manual labor, mostly women workers. ### 14. 发现自己在反复回想同一件糟心事时，换个需要动手的活 / When You Catch Yourself Ruminating, Switch to a Hands-On Task
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难在要先察觉自己正在反复回想（这叫反刍），察觉本身就要练。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is first noticing that you're replaying the same upsetting thing (that's called rumination) — and noticing itself takes practice.

  In plain terms: Replaying the same upsetting thing won't produce an answer. It deepens depression, cements negative thoughts, weakens problem-solving, makes it harder to actually act, and wears out the people willing to listen. Once you notice you're going in circles, go do something hands-on.
- 收益：多项研究的综述说，反复回想同一件糟心事（反刍思维）会加重抑郁。它还会强化负面思考，削弱解决问题的能力。它会妨碍实际行动，还会把身边愿意听你说的人耗光。它与焦虑、暴食、酗酒、自伤相关。
  Benefit: A review of multiple studies says ruminating on the same upsetting thing worsens depression. It also strengthens negative thinking, weakens problem-solving ability, gets in the way of real action, and wears out the people willing to listen. It is associated with anxiety, binge eating, alcohol abuse, and self-harm.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Nolen-Hoeksema, Wisco & Lyubomirsky (2008). Rethinking Rumination. Perspectives on Psychological Science. <https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1745-6924.2008.00088.x>
- 备注：综述只给了方向，没有数字。「换个活」是作者自己的做法。文献能撑住的只有一句：把注意力岔开，好过接着在原地绕。至于换成什么活，没人比较过。
  Note: The review only gives the direction, not numbers. "Switch tasks" is the author's own practice. The literature supports just one sentence: diverting attention beats staying stuck in the loop. Nobody has compared which replacement task works best.

### 15. 把「事情肯定会更糟」这类念头当症状看，不当事实看 / Treat "Things Will Surely Get Worse" as a Symptom, Not a Fact
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难在把这种念头当成症状看，而不是当成事实。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is treating this kind of thought as a symptom rather than as a fact.
- 说人话：一项跟踪了 20 年的双生子研究里，悲观程度每高一个档，同期死亡的概率高约 13%，死于心血管病的概率高约 20%。反过来，乐观得分高并没有带来相应的好处。所以要处理的是悲观，不是「不够正能量」。
  In plain terms: In a 20-year twin study, each step up in pessimism was associated with about 13% higher mortality and about 20% higher cardiovascular mortality. Higher optimism scores, on the other hand, brought no corresponding benefit. So the thing to work on is pessimism — not "insufficient positivity."
- 收益：澳大利亚 2978 名 50 岁以上的双生子，平均跟踪 20 年，其间有 1068 人死亡。悲观量表每高 1 个标准差，同期死亡的风险高约 13%（风险比 1.134）。这个数字的可信范围是 95% CI 1.065–1.207，P=8.85×10⁻⁵。心血管死亡高约 20%（1.196，1.045–1.368，P=0.0093）。与癌症死亡无关。乐观得分与死亡率也没有明显关联。
  Benefit: 2,978 Australian twins aged 50 and older were followed for an average of 20 years, during which 1,068 died. Each 1-standard-deviation increase on the pessimism scale was associated with about 13% higher all-cause mortality (hazard ratio 1.134; 95% CI 1.065–1.207; P = 8.85 × 10⁻⁵). Cardiovascular mortality was about 20% higher (1.196; 1.045–1.368; P = 0.0093). There was no association with cancer mortality, and optimism scores showed no significant association with mortality either.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Whitfield JB, Zhu G, Landers JG, Martin NG (2020). Pessimism is associated with greater all-cause and cardiovascular mortality, but optimism is not protective. Scientific Reports, 10, 12609. <https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-69388-y>
- 备注：争议。这只是观察，没有分组。有人会说「其实是先有病才悲观」，作者反驳说，真那样的话癌症死亡也该相关。但仍然可能有没测到的因素。乐观并没有保护作用，所以不必强迫自己「积极思考」。要做的是别把悲观预期当成对未来的准确预测。
  Note: Disputed. This is observational, with no randomization. Some would say "people are pessimistic because they're already sick"; the authors counter that cancer mortality should then be associated too. Unmeasured factors remain possible. Optimism showed no protective effect, so there's no need to force "positive thinking." The point is not to treat pessimistic expectations as accurate predictions of the future.

### 16. 减少让你消耗的人际关系，学会拒绝不想接的请求 / Cut Draining Relationships; Learn to Say No to Requests You Don't Want

### 17. 生气时先离场，把对方当天气而不是当敌人 / When Angry, Leave the Room First; Treat the Other Person Like Weather, Not an Enemy

### 20. 别把「别人过得怎么样」当每日必读：给刷同龄人动态的应用设限或关掉 / Don't Make "How Others Are Doing" a Daily Must-Read: Limit or Quit the Apps Where You Scroll Peers' Updates

### 21. 觉得「所有人都看见我出丑了」的时候，把这个估计除以二 / When You Feel "Everyone Saw Me Embarrass Myself," Halve That Estimate
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。只是在紧张的时候多问自己一句：他们真的看见了吗。
  Cost: Free. Just ask yourself one extra question when you're nervous: did they really see it?
- 说人话：穿一件尴尬的 T 恤走进有人的房间，穿的人以为将近一半人认得出衣服上印的是谁，实际不到四分之一。穿自己觉得体面的 T 恤，估计的人数是实际的六倍。人聊完天，也普遍低估对方有多喜欢自己。所以觉得「所有人都记住我出丑了」时，砍一半仍然偏高。
  In plain terms: Walking into a room wearing an embarrassing T-shirt, wearers thought nearly half the people could identify who was printed on it — in reality fewer than a quarter could. Wearing a T-shirt they felt good about, their estimate was six times the actual number. After conversations, people also systematically underestimated how much the other person liked them. So when you feel "everyone remembers my embarrassment," even halving it is still an overestimate.
- 收益：实验一：让参与者穿上印有 Barry Manilow 头像的 T 恤，走进有其他参与者的房间。穿的人平均估计 46% 的旁观者能正确指出衣服上的人是谁。旁观者实际正确率 23%，估计值恰为实际值的两倍。逐场把估计值减去实际值，平均高估 23 个百分点（95% CI 9–38，可信范围）。剔除只有两名和三名旁观者的那两场之后，为 27 个百分点（11–43）。实验二：改穿参与者自选的、自认为正面的 T 恤。平均估计 48%，旁观者实际正确率 8%，为六倍。另一系列研究有三种场景：陌生人配对交谈、大学新生室友、成人工作坊。结果是人们系统性低估交谈对象对自己的喜欢程度，也低估对方对这次交谈的享受程度。作者称之为「喜欢差」。不同长度的交谈中都存在，在新生室友之间持续数月。

- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Gilovich T, Medvec VH, Savitsky K (2000). The spotlight effect in social judgment: An egocentric bias in estimates of the salience of one's own actions and appearance. The Liking Gap in Conversations: Do People Like Us More Than We Think? Psychological Science, 29(11), 1742–1756. <https://doi.org/10.1177/0956797618783714>
- 备注：定 B 的理由：都是人数不多的实验室与校园研究，场景是穿衣服和跟陌生人聊天。搬到「被同事议论」「在会上说错话」上，没有直接证据。这些数字只说明高估的方向和大致倍数，没法当系数套。别人的看法也不是都不重要。真正在评价你的场合，比如面试、答辩、考核、体检报告，评价是实打实的，该准备就准备。要打折的只是日常里那种「所有人都在看我」的感觉，那部分要对付的是你自己的估计偏差。

These numbers only show the direction and rough magnitude of overestimation — don't apply them as a formula. Others' opinions aren't all unimportant. What deserves discounting is only the everyday feeling that "everyone is watching me" — that's your own estimation bias to deal with.

### 22. 把「别人要求我做到完美」当症状看，不当事实看 / Treat "Others Demand Perfection From Me" as a Symptom, Not a Fact
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是把这种念头当成需要处理的东西，而不是当成对别人真实要求的准确测量。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is treating this thought as something to work on, rather than as an accurate read of what others really demand.
- 说人话：总觉得「周围的人要求我完美，做不到就会被否定」的人，更容易有自杀念头。「我自己要求自己完美」和自杀念头的关系弱得多，只有前一种的三分之一左右。把原本就有的念头扣掉，前一种感觉仍和后来念头变多有关，只是幅度不大。
  In plain terms: People who constantly feel "everyone around me demands perfection, and I'll be rejected if I fall short" are more likely to have suicidal thoughts. The link between "I demand perfection of myself" and suicidal thoughts is much weaker — about a third as strong. Even after accounting for pre-existing thoughts, the former feeling still predicts more thoughts later, though modestly.
- 收益：把多项研究合起来算（荟萃分析）：45 项研究、N = 11,747，人群为本科生、医学生、社区成人与精神科患者，用的是随机效应模型。下面是与自杀念头的加权平均相关。完美主义担忧 r = .28（95% CI .24–.32，可信范围；42 项研究、7,936 人）。完美主义担忧是一个合集，包括社会规定型完美主义、在意犯错、对行动的怀疑、现实与标准的落差等。其中社会规定型完美主义单独算，也是 r = .28（.25–.32，30 项研究、3,640 人）。完美主义追求 r = .10（.07–.13，31 项研究、4,588 人），它是自我导向的完美主义与个人标准的合集。父母批评 r = .20（.11–.29）。再看与自杀未遂的关系。完美主义担忧 r = .12（.07–.17，15 项研究、5,275 人）。社会规定型完美主义 r = .19（.08–.29，8 项研究、689 人）。跟踪一段时间的那部分：把一开始就有的自杀念头扣除后，社会规定型完美主义仍与后来随访时的自杀念头同向变化，幅度不大。自我导向与他人导向的完美主义都看不出关联。
  Benefit: A meta-analysis pooling multiple studies: 45 studies, N = 11,747, covering undergraduates, medical students, community adults, and psychiatric patients, using a random-effects model. Below are the weighted average correlations with suicidal ideation. Perfectionistic concerns r = .28 (95% CI .24–.32; 42 studies, 7,936 people) — a composite including socially prescribed perfectionism, concern over mistakes, doubts about actions, and perceived gaps between reality and standards. Socially prescribed perfectionism alone was also r = .28 (.25–.32; 30 studies, 3,640 people). Perfectionistic strivings r = .10 (.07–.13; 31 studies, 4,588 people) — a composite of self-oriented perfectionism and personal standards. Parental criticism r = .20 (.11–.29). For suicide attempts: perfectionistic concerns r = .12 (.07–.17; 15 studies, 5,275 people); socially prescribed perfectionism r = .19 (.08–.29; 8 studies, 689 people). The longitudinal part: after subtracting baseline suicidal ideation, socially prescribed perfectionism still moved in the same direction as later ideation at follow-up, modestly. Self-oriented and other-oriented perfectionism showed no association.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Smith MM, Sherry SB, Chen S, Saklofske DH, Mushquash C, Flett GL, Hewitt PL (2018). The perniciousness of perfectionism: A meta-analytic review of the perfectionism-suicide relationship. Journal of Personality, 86(3), 522–542. <https://doi.org/10.1111/jopy.12333>
- 备注：定 B 的理由：纳入的研究多数是一次性问卷，看不出先后。相关强度中等（r 约 .28），大致只解释几个到一成的差异。量的是自杀念头和自杀未遂，不是死亡本身，所以收益量级按「只有替代终点」记小。作者自己也说，完美主义担忧与自杀未遂这一组像是有发表偏倚。发表偏倚指结果不好看的研究没被发出来。把估算的缺失研究补回去后，结论方向不变。高标准本身不是问题，也不必因此降低要求。区别在于标准从哪来，以及达不到时会不会觉得「我这个人就完了」。它和第 15 条（把「事情肯定会更糟」这类念头当症状看）里的悲观预期一样，是可以当症状处理的东西。有自杀念头先打 988、把致死手段挪远，见第 1 节第 25 条。身边人流露出这种念头时你能做什么，见第 8 节第 15 条。孩子的抑郁筛查见第 30 节第 8 条。
  Note: Graded B because most included studies were one-time questionnaires that can't establish order. The correlations are moderate (r ≈ .28), explaining roughly a few percent to ten percent of the variation. What was measured was suicidal ideation and attempts, not death itself, so the benefit is rated small on the "surrogate endpoint only" basis. The authors themselves note likely publication bias in the perfectionistic-concerns/suicide-attempts group — meaning studies with unflattering results may not have been published; filling in the estimated missing studies leaves the direction unchanged. High standards aren't the problem, and there's no need to lower them. The difference is where the standard comes from, and whether falling short makes you feel "I'm finished as a person." Like the pessimistic expectations in Item 15 (treating "things will surely get worse" as a symptom), it's something that can be treated as a symptom. If you have suicidal thoughts, call 988 first and move lethal means out of reach — see Section 1, Item 25. For what you can do when someone near you shows such thoughts, see Section 8, Item 15. For depression screening in children, see Section 30, Item 8.

### 23. 生气时别靠砸东西、打沙袋、跑一圈来发泄，先让身体慢下来 / When Angry, Don't Vent by Smashing Things, Hitting a Punching Bag, or Going for a Run — Slow Your Body Down First
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。气头上改做几分钟让心跳慢下来的事：慢慢呼吸，坐下来，静一静。难在当时最想做的恰恰是发泄。
  Cost: Free. In the heat of anger, switch to a few minutes of heart-slowing activity: breathe slowly, sit down, be still. The hard part is that venting is exactly what you most want to do right then.
- 说人话：打沙袋、摔东西、去跑一圈，这类让身体更兴奋的发泄办法，整体上消不了气。慢呼吸、正念、冥想这类让身体静下来的办法，能明显压下怒气和想动手的劲。
  In plain terms: Venting methods that amp the body up — hitting a punching bag, smashing things, going for a run — don't defuse anger overall. Calming methods like slow breathing, mindfulness, and meditation clearly bring down anger and the urge to lash out.
- 收益：把 154 项研究合起来算（荟萃分析），共 184 组样本、10,189 人。让身体平静下来的办法，比如深呼吸、正念、冥想，能降低怒气和攻击性。效果大小 g = -0.63，95% CI -0.82 到 -0.43（可信范围）。这个结果在不同性别、种族、年龄和文化的人里都稳定。让身体更兴奋的办法，比如打沙袋、慢跑、骑车，整体无效。它的效果大小 g = -0.02，95% CI -0.13 到 0.09。作者的结论是：发泄怒气、去跑一圈，都不是管用的消气办法。
  Benefit: A meta-analysis pooling 154 studies — 184 samples, 10,189 people. Calming methods like deep breathing, mindfulness, and meditation reduced anger and aggression, with an effect size of g = -0.63 (95% CI -0.82 to -0.43). This held across genders, races, ages, and cultures. Arousal-increasing methods like hitting a punching bag, jogging, or cycling were ineffective overall: effect size g = -0.02 (95% CI -0.13 to 0.09). The authors' conclusion: venting anger and going for a run are not effective ways to cool down.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Kjærvik SL, Bushman BJ (2024). A meta-analytic review of anger management activities that increase or decrease arousal: What fuels or douses rage? Clinical Psychology Review. <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpr.2024.102414>
- 备注：量的是怒气和攻击性，不是精力。口径记成时间，是作者的归类：少吵一架，就少收拾一次残局。当场能用的办法见第 22 节第 8 条（循环叹息），还有本节第 17 条（生气时先离场）。跑步不是没用。长期看，它对情绪低落有效，见第 22 节第 6 条（心情差就去走或者跑）。它只是不适合拿来当场消气。气头上别开车，别发消息，别做决定。如果是有人在打你，先离开、报警。那时要处理的不是你的情绪，见第 8 节第 43 条（被家暴了）。
  Note: What was measured was anger and aggression, not energy; the time metric is the author's classification — one fewer fight means one less mess to clean up. For in-the-moment techniques, see Section 22, Item 8 (cyclic sighing), and Item 17 of this section (leave the room when angry). Running isn't useless. Over the long term it helps low mood — see Section 22, Item 6 (walk or run when you're down). It's just not suited to cooling down on the spot. When furious: don't drive, don't send messages, don't make decisions. If someone is hitting you, leave first and call the police — that's not an emotion to manage, see Section 8, Item 43 (if you're being abused at home).

### 24. 心里压着一件事，可以连着几天把想法和感受写下来，但别指望它治病 / When Something Weighs on You, Write Down Your Thoughts and Feelings for Several Days — But Don't Expect It to Cure Anything
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=小 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。研究里常见的做法是写 3 到 4 次，每次 15 到 20 分钟。写一件让你难受的事，写你最深的想法和感受。
  Cost: Free. The common protocol in studies is 3 to 4 sessions of 15 to 20 minutes each. Write about something that upsets you — your deepest thoughts and feelings about it.
- 说人话：把糟心事写下来，平均有一点好处，但很小。汇总的结论也不一致，有两项汇总算下来没有效果。它几乎不花钱，可以试。情绪问题重了，别拿它代替看医生。
  In plain terms: Writing down upsetting things has a small average benefit — and a very small one. The pooled conclusions are inconsistent; two pooled analyses found no effect. It costs almost nothing, so it's worth a try. If emotional problems get serious, don't use it as a substitute for seeing a doctor.
- 收益：一项分析汇总了 146 项随机分组的研究（荟萃分析）。研究里的人把对自己重要的事写下来或者说出来。平均效果是正的，也显著，但很小，效果大小 r = .075。另一项汇总只收随机对照试验，30 项试验合起来算，身体健康和心理健康两方面都没有显著效果。针对癌症病人和康复者的 16 项随机试验合起来算，心理、身体、生活质量三方面都不显著。其中心理方面 g = 0.04，95% CI -0.06 到 0.14（可信范围）。
  Benefit: One analysis pooled 146 randomized studies (meta-analysis). Participants wrote or spoke about things important to them. The average effect was positive and significant, but tiny: r = .075. Another pooled analysis limited to randomized controlled trials — 30 trials combined — found no significant effect on either physical or mental health. Pooling 16 randomized trials on cancer patients and survivors found no significant effect on psychological health, physical health, or quality of life; the psychological part was g = 0.04 (95% CI -0.06 to 0.14).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Frattaroli J (2006). Experimental disclosure and its moderators: a meta-analysis. Psychological Bulletin. <https://doi.org/10.1037/0033-2909.132.6.823>；Mogk C, Otte S, Reinhold-Hurley B, Kröner-Herwig B (2006). Health effects of expressive writing on stressful or traumatic experiences - a meta-analysis. Psycho-Social Medicine. <https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC2736499/>；Zachariae R, O'Toole MS (2015). The effect of expressive writing intervention on psychological and physical health outcomes in cancer patients--a systematic review and meta-analysis. Psycho-Oncology. <https://doi.org/10.1002/pon.3802>
- 备注：争议。支持的一方是 146 项研究的汇总，效果为正但很小。反对的一方是两项只收随机对照试验的汇总，都没算出显著效果。成本栏里写几次、每次多久，来自最早的实验设计，Mogk 那篇汇总的摘要里写了。它几乎不花钱，也不花多少时间，一个人就能做。不想找人说、身边也没人可说的时候，可以用它。量的不是精力，口径记成时间是作者的归类。写着写着越来越难受，就停下，改打 988，见第 1 节第 25 条（988）。
  Note: Disputed. The supporting side is the 146-study pooled analysis, with a positive but tiny effect. The opposing side is two pooled analyses limited to randomized controlled trials, both finding no significant effect. The session count and duration in the cost field come from the earliest experimental design, as stated in the Mogk pooled analysis's abstract. It costs almost nothing and little time, and can be done alone. Use it when you don't want to talk to someone, or there's nobody to talk to. What was measured wasn't energy; the time metric is the author's classification. If writing makes you feel worse and worse, stop and call 988 instead — see Section 1, Item 25 (988).
