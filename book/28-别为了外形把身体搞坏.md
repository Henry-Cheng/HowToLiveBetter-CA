# 28. 别为了外形把身体搞坏 / Don't Ruin Your Health for Looks

本节说的是「为了改变外形而对身体动手」的几种做法：饿肚子、吃药、打针、开刀。这几件事，决定多半是和别人比较之后做的，代价却全落在自己身上，而且相当一部分改不回来。

This section covers ways people alter their appearance by doing things to their bodies: starving, pills, injections, surgery. These decisions are usually made after comparing yourself with others, but the costs land entirely on you — and many of them can't be undone.

本节不评价任何人想把自己变成什么样子，那是各人自己的事。同一个目标，可以找「有处方、有资质、有复查」的，也可以「网购、美容院、自己加量」，本节只比这两种做法的风险差多少。算账主要看身体上实际出了什么事，比如失明、住院、死亡。医美那两条还涉及非法行医的刑事责任。

This section doesn't judge what anyone wants to look like — that's each person's own business. For the same goal, you can go the "prescription, licensed provider, follow-up" route or the "buy online, beauty salon, raise the dose yourself" route; this section only compares how much risk differs between the two. The accounting focuses on what actually happened to bodies: blindness, hospitalization, death. The two cosmetic-procedure items also touch on criminal liability for unlicensed medical practice.

### 1. 不要用极端节食、断食或催吐来控制体重，要减就从运动那一侧减 / Don't control weight with extreme dieting, fasting, or purging — if you want to lose weight, do it through exercise
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。反过来还省下买减肥产品的钱。
  Cost: Free. You even save the money you'd spend on diet products.
- 说人话：神经性厌食的人，死亡概率大约是同龄人的 5.9 倍。每 1000 个人跟一年，死 5.1 个。死的人里每五个有一个是自杀。而它的入口就是节食：跟踪 44 所中学的学生三年，严重节食的女生出现进食障碍的概率是不节食的 18 倍。想控制体重就多运动，别少吃到饿。
  In plain terms: People with anorexia nervosa die at about 5.9 times the rate of their peers. For every 1,000 people followed for a year, 5.1 die. Of those who die, one in five dies by suicide. And the entry point is dieting: in a 3-year follow-up of students at 44 secondary schools, girls who dieted severely were 18 times more likely to develop an eating disorder than those who didn't diet. To control weight, exercise more — don't starve yourself.
- 收益：36 项研究合起来算。神经性厌食这一批人累计被跟踪了 166,642 人年（每人被跟踪的年数加总）。他们的标准化死亡比是 5.86，即死亡概率约为同龄人的 5.9 倍。每 1000 人年死亡 5.1 例，就是 1000 个人跟一年死 5.1 个。死的人里约 1/5 死于自杀。贪食症的标准化死亡比是 1.93，其他进食障碍是 1.92，即各约为同龄人的 1.9 倍。另一项研究在澳大利亚维多利亚州的 44 所中学做，跟踪 14–15 岁学生 3 年、同一批学生三年里前后查了 6 波。女生新发进食障碍 21.8/1000 人年，即 1000 个女生跟一年新出 21.8 例。严重节食的女生发病风险是不节食者的 18 倍，中度节食者是 5 倍。再校正既往节食与精神症状，把「以前就节食过」和「本来就有精神症状」的影响扣掉。扣掉之后，BMI（体重除以身高平方算出的胖瘦指标）、运动量和性别都不再预测新发进食障碍。作者结论是「在青少年中用运动而非限食控制体重，发生进食障碍的风险似乎更低」。
  Benefit: 36 studies pooled. The anorexia nervosa group accumulated 166,642 person-years of follow-up (each person's years summed). Their standardized mortality ratio was 5.86 — about 5.9 times the death rate of their peers. 5.1 deaths per 1,000 person-years: 1,000 people followed for a year, 5.1 die. About 1 in 5 of those who died died by suicide. For bulimia nervosa the SMR was 1.93 and for other eating disorders 1.92 — each about 1.9 times peers. Another study at 44 secondary schools in Victoria, Australia followed 14–15-year-olds for 3 years, surveying the same students 6 times. New eating disorders in girls: 21.8 per 1,000 person-years — 21.8 new cases per 1,000 girls followed a year. Girls who dieted severely had 18 times the risk of those who didn't; moderate dieters 5 times. The analysis further adjusted for prior dieting and psychiatric symptoms, removing the influence of "had dieted before" and "already had psychiatric symptoms." After that, BMI (the weight-to-height-squared index), exercise level, and sex no longer predicted new eating disorders. The authors concluded that "in adolescents, controlling weight through exercise rather than food restriction seems to carry lower risk of eating disorders."
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Arcelus J et al. (2011). Mortality rates in patients with anorexia nervosa and other eating disorders. A meta-analysis of 36 studies. Arch Gen Psychiatry. <https://doi.org/10.1001/archgenpsychiatry.2011.74>；Patton GC et al. (1999). Onset of adolescent eating disorders: population based cohort study over 3 years. BMJ. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.318.7186.765>
- 备注：这里说的「严重节食」，指的是长期把吃的压到远低于身体需要，不是指少吃一顿或者不吃夜宵。已经出现催吐、暴食之后自责、反复称重停不下来的，去精神科或者临床心理科看。这是有规范治疗办法的病，不是意志力的问题。BMI 与死亡率的关系见第 2 节第 32 条，那条讲长期体重区间，不讲减重手段。
  Note: "Severe dieting" here means chronically eating far below what the body needs — not skipping one meal or late-night snacks. If purging, guilt after binges, or compulsive repeated weighing have already started, see a psychiatrist or clinical psychologist. This is an illness with established treatments, not a willpower problem. For the BMI–mortality relationship, see section 2, item 32, which covers long-term weight ranges, not weight-loss methods.

### 2. 打针、埋线、开刀之前查两样：机构资质和动手的人有没有执照 / Before injections, thread lifts, or surgery, check two things: the facility's credentials and the practitioner's license
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。查这两样只要几分钟。正规机构一般会公示资质，加州医疗委员会官网可以查医生的执照（具体查询入口待核实）。
  Cost: Free. Checking both takes a few minutes. Legitimate facilities usually display their credentials, and the Medical Board of California's website lets you look up a physician's license (exact lookup page to be verified).
- 说人话：在加州，动针、动刀、用医疗器械的医美都属于医疗行为，只能由持照医生（或在其监督下的有资质人员）在正规医疗机构做。在美容院、工作室、酒店房间里做，动手的很可能没有行医资格，属于无照行医，是犯罪。
  In plain terms: In California, cosmetic procedures involving needles, scalpels, or medical devices count as medical practice — only licensed physicians (or qualified staff under their supervision) in legitimate medical facilities may perform them. Getting it done at a beauty salon, a studio, or a hotel room likely means the person has no medical license: that's unlicensed practice of medicine, a crime.
- 收益：加州法律要求这类侵入性医美操作由持照医疗人员在合规的医疗机构进行，术前要书面告知适应症、禁忌症、医疗风险和注意事项，并取得签字同意（具体条款待核实）。无照行医属于刑事犯罪，可被起诉判刑（具体量刑待核实）。
  Benefit: California law requires such invasive cosmetic procedures to be performed by licensed medical personnel in compliant medical facilities, with written disclosure of indications, contraindications, risks, and precautions and signed consent beforehand (specific provisions to be verified). Unlicensed practice of medicine is a criminal offense, prosecutable with jail time (exact penalties to be verified).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：加州医疗委员会与加州州法（无照行医相关条款；具体条款待核实）
- 备注：上门打针、在工作室里私下操作，光看地点就已经不对。未成年人做医美需要家长同意。做脸、按摩、美甲属于生活美容，不归这套规矩管。但只要动到针和刀，就算医疗行为。
  Note: House-call injections or back-room studio procedures are already wrong on location alone. Minors need parental consent for cosmetic procedures. Facials, massage, and manicures are beauty services, not covered by these rules. But anything involving needles or scalpels counts as a medical procedure.

### 3. 面部填充避开鼻背、眉间、额头这几个部位，更别为省钱找非正规注射 / For facial fillers, avoid the nose bridge, glabella, and forehead — and never cut corners with unlicensed injectors
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。只是换个部位打，或者不做。
  Cost: Free. It's just choosing a different spot — or not doing it.
- 说人话：打填充针后失明的人里，一半多打的是鼻部，其次是眉间、额头和法令纹，八成多用的是玻尿酸（透明质酸）。失明的人只有两成视力完全恢复，一成多部分恢复，将近两成还伤到了脑。目前没有一种抢救办法稳定管用，只能事先避开这些部位。
  In plain terms: Among people who went blind after filler injections, more than half had the nose done, followed by the glabella (between the brows), forehead, and nasolabial folds; over 80% of cases involved hyaluronic acid. Of those who went blind, only about 20% fully recovered vision, about 17% partially recovered, and nearly 20% also suffered brain injury. No rescue treatment works reliably — the only option is avoiding these areas in advance.
- 收益：把全世界期刊报过的病例汇总起来算。2015 年 1 月到 2018 年 9 月新增 48 例注射填充后部分或完全视力丧失。出事最多的部位是鼻部，占 56.3%。接下来是眉间 27.1%、前额 18.8%、鼻唇沟（法令纹那道沟）14.6%。81.3% 的病例填充物为透明质酸，就是玻尿酸。43.8% 出现皮肤改变。18.8% 出现中枢神经系统并发症（脑出了问题）。10 例（20.8%）视力完全恢复，8 例（16.7%）部分恢复，剩下的没恢复。作者结论为各家的处理方式差得很远，没有一种治疗被证明能持续奏效。
  Benefit: Cases reported in journals worldwide, tallied up. From January 2015 to September 2018, 48 new cases of partial or complete vision loss after filler injections. The nose was the most common site, 56.3%, followed by the glabella 27.1%, forehead 18.8%, and nasolabial folds (the smile lines) 14.6%. Hyaluronic acid was the filler in 81.3% of cases. 43.8% had skin changes; 18.8% had central nervous system complications (brain involvement). 10 cases (20.8%) fully recovered vision, 8 (16.7%) partially recovered, the rest did not. The authors concluded that treatment approaches vary wildly and no treatment has proven consistently effective.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Beleznay K et al. (2019). Update on Avoiding and Treating Blindness From Fillers: A Recent Review of the World Literature. Aesthet Surg J. <https://doi.org/10.1093/asj/sjz053>
- 备注：这些数字是把期刊报过的病例一例一例数出来的。里面只有出事的人数，没有打针的总人数。所以算不出「打一针有多大概率失明」，只看得出哪些部位出事多、事后基本救不回来。证据记 B 级也是因为这个。真皮填充剂在美国按医疗器械由 FDA 监管（待核实）。正规机构降低风险的做法本来就是常规：先查清要打的地方底下血管怎么走，用钝头针不用尖针，边退针边少量推，事先备好透明质酸酶。每一样都要求动手的是受过训练的医生，这也是别找非正规渠道的理由。做之前先按本节第 2 条（查机构资质和医生执照）核一遍。
  Note: These numbers come from counting published cases one by one. They include only the number of people harmed, not the total number of injections — so you can't compute "the chance of going blind from one injection," only which sites go wrong most and how rarely they're fixed afterward. That's also why this is grade B. Dermal fillers are regulated by the FDA as medical devices in the U.S. (to be verified). Standard risk-reduction practice at legitimate clinics is routine: map the blood vessels under the injection site first, use blunt cannulas instead of sharp needles, inject small amounts while withdrawing, and have hyaluronidase on hand. Every one of these requires a trained physician — another reason not to use unlicensed channels. Before doing it, verify per item 2 of this section (facility credentials and physician license).

### 4. 不要买承诺「快速瘦」的减肥药、减肥咖啡、瘦身糖果和酵素梅 / Don't buy "rapid weight loss" pills, slimming coffees, diet gummies, or enzyme plums
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。反过来还省下买这些产品的钱。难的是要接受没有「一周掉几斤」这回事。
  Cost: Free. You even save the money you'd spend on these products. The hard part is accepting there's no such thing as "losing several pounds a week."
- 说人话：这类产品里最常查出偷加的是西布曲明（sibutramine），它原来是处方减肥药，2010 年在美国和中国都被撤市了（中国监管动作见备注）。有心脏病或糖尿病的超重的人吃了三年多，没致命的心梗高约三成，中风高约三成六。体重只多减了 1.7 公斤。抽检查出来的时候，你已经吃下去了。
  In plain terms: The most common hidden additive found in these products is sibutramine — originally a prescription weight-loss drug, withdrawn from the market in both the U.S. and China in 2010 (the China regulatory action is in the note). Overweight people with heart disease or diabetes who took it for over three years had about 30% more non-fatal heart attacks and about 36% more strokes. They lost only 1.7 kg more. By the time lab tests catch it, you've already eaten it.
- 收益：SCOUT 试验招了 9,804 人。他们都是 55 岁以上、超重或肥胖，并且有心血管病或 2 型糖尿病。这些人抽签分成吃药和不吃药两组，平均治疗 3.4 年。主要终点是事先定好要数的几件坏事：非致死性心梗、非致死性卒中（中风）、心脏骤停后被救回来、心血管死亡。这几件事的发生率是 11.4% vs 10.0%。HR 1.16（95% CI 1.03–1.31，P=0.02），吃药的人出事概率高约一成六。真实值大致落在高 3% 到高 31% 之间。非致死性心梗 4.1% vs 3.2%，HR 1.28（1.04–1.57），高约三成。非致死性卒中 2.6% vs 1.9%，HR 1.36（1.04–1.77），高约三成六。心血管死亡与全因死亡未增加，全因就是各种原因加起来。抽签之后，西布曲明组平均多减重 1.7 kg。
  Benefit: The SCOUT trial enrolled 9,804 people — all 55 or older, overweight or obese, with cardiovascular disease or type 2 diabetes. They were randomized into drug and no-drug groups, treated for an average of 3.4 years. The primary endpoint was a pre-specified set of bad events: non-fatal heart attack, non-fatal stroke, resuscitated cardiac arrest, cardiovascular death. Event rates: 11.4% vs 10.0%. HR 1.16 (95% CI 1.03–1.31, P=0.02) — about a 16% higher event rate in the drug group, with the true value roughly between 3% and 31% higher. Non-fatal heart attack 4.1% vs 3.2%, HR 1.28 (1.04–1.57), about 30% higher. Non-fatal stroke 2.6% vs 1.9%, HR 1.36 (1.04–1.77), about 36% higher. Cardiovascular death and all-cause death (all causes combined) did not increase. After randomization, the sibutramine group lost on average 1.7 kg more.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：James WPT et al. (2010). Effect of sibutramine on cardiovascular outcomes in overweight and obese subjects. NEJM. <https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1003114>；U.S. FDA 掺杂减肥产品警示（具体页面待核实）
- 备注：SCOUT 招的是本来就有心脏病或者糖尿病的高危人群。这个试验没有证明健康年轻人吃了也一样出事。但正因为分不清谁是高危，监管才干脆让它退市。另一种常被偷加的成分是酚酞（phenolphthalein），它本来是泻药，长期吃多了会打乱体内电解质、诱发心律失常。怎么判断：在美国，正经减肥药也是处方药，只能凭处方拿。打着「咖啡」「糖果」「代餐」旗号卖、又承诺一周掉几斤的，按定义就不该有药效，有药效就说明加了东西。中国 2010 年 10 月停止西布曲明生产销售使用的监管动作，见原版备注（中国案例）。
  Note: SCOUT enrolled high-risk people who already had heart disease or diabetes. The trial doesn't prove healthy young people would have the same outcomes. But precisely because you can't tell who's high-risk, regulators pulled it from the market. Another common hidden additive is phenolphthalein — originally a laxative; chronic overuse disrupts electrolytes and can trigger arrhythmia. How to tell: in the U.S., legitimate weight-loss drugs are also prescription-only. Products sold as "coffee," "gummies," or "meal replacements" that promise several pounds a week shouldn't pharmacologically work at all — if they do work, something's been added. China's October 2010 regulatory action halting sibutramine production and sales is documented in the original edition's note (China case).

### 5. 不要用合成代谢类固醇（「增肌针」「口服药」）来长肌肉 / Don't use anabolic steroids ("muscle shots," oral pills) to build muscle
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，还省下药钱。难的是要接受肌肉长得慢。
  Cost: Free, and you save the drug money. The hard part is accepting that muscle grows slowly.
- 说人话：健身房兴奋剂检测阳性的男性，死亡风险是同龄人的 3 倍，每年跑医院的次数多一倍多。痤疮、男性乳房发育、勃起功能障碍，每十个用药的人里至少一个中招。这类药在美国属于联邦管制物质，没有处方买卖持有都违法（具体分级待核实），能卖给你的渠道本身就在违法。
  In plain terms: Men who tested positive for steroids in gym doping tests had 3 times the death risk of their peers and more than twice as many hospital visits per year. Acne, gynecomastia (male breast development), and erectile dysfunction each hit at least one in ten users. In the U.S. these drugs are federally controlled substances — buying, selling, or possessing them without a prescription is illegal (exact schedule to be verified) — so any channel selling them to you is already breaking the law.
- 收益：丹麦回头翻档案，给每个用药的人配同龄对照。用药的是 2006-01-03 至 2018-03-01 健身房兴奋剂检测雄激素类固醇阳性的 545 名男性，配了 5,450 名对照。另外又用 644 名拒绝受检者和 6,440 名对照复核了一遍。结果：使用者死亡率为对照的 3 倍，HR 3.0（95% CI 1.3–7.0），真实倍数大致在 1.3 到 7 之间。每年住院或就诊次数的中位数是 0.81 次 vs 0.36 次（P<0.0001）。中位数就是一半人多于、一半人少于这个数。这个 P 值说明这个差别几乎不可能是碰巧。痤疮（长痘）、男性乳房发育、勃起功能障碍各影响超过 10% 的使用者，即每十个用药的人里至少一个中招，都明显高于对照（P<0.0001）。复核的那一批结果一致。
  Benefit: Denmark dug through records, matching each user with same-age controls. The users were 545 men who tested positive for anabolic steroids in gym doping tests from 2006-01-03 to 2018-03-01, matched with 5,450 controls; a second check used 644 test refusers with 6,440 controls. Results: users died at 3 times the control rate, HR 3.0 (95% CI 1.3–7.0) — the true multiple roughly between 1.3 and 7. Median hospital visits per year: 0.81 vs 0.36 (P<0.0001). The median means half had more, half fewer; this P value means the difference is almost certainly not chance. Acne, gynecomastia, and erectile dysfunction each affected over 10% of users — at least one in ten — all clearly above controls (P<0.0001). The second check agreed.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Horwitz H et al. (2019). Health consequences of androgenic anabolic steroid use. J Intern Med. <https://doi.org/10.1111/joim.12850>
- 备注：争议：这是跟踪一群人看结果的研究，不是随机分组试验。用药的人本身可能在别的方面也更冒险，3 倍这个数不能全记到药头上。真实倍数可能低到 1.3，也可能高到 7.0，范围很宽。但「痤疮、男性乳房发育、勃起功能障碍各有一成以上的人中招」是眼睛能看见的副作用，受这种偏差影响较小。在美国，这类药属于管制药品，只能凭处方获得。普通蛋白粉、肌酸不在本条之内。麻烦的是「宣称增肌特别快」的产品可能偷加成分。判断方法和减肥产品那条一样（第 4 条）：正经药是处方药。靠「增肌粉」「营养品」名义卖、又承诺几周见效的，真有效就是加了东西。
  Note: Contested: this followed a group of people over time — not a randomized trial. Users may be bigger risk-takers in other ways too, so the 3x figure can't all be blamed on the drugs. The true multiple could be as low as 1.3 or as high as 7.0 — a wide range. But "acne, gynecomastia, and erectile dysfunction each hitting over 10% of users" are visible side effects, less affected by this bias. In the U.S., these drugs are controlled substances, prescription-only. Ordinary protein powder and creatine are not covered here. The tricky part is "builds muscle super fast" products that may contain hidden additives. The test is the same as for weight-loss products (item 4): legitimate drugs are prescription-only. Anything sold as "muscle powder" or "supplements" promising results in weeks — if it really works, something's been added.

### 6. 要吃减肥药就去医院拿处方，别在不用处方就发货的网店买 / If you need weight-loss medication, get a prescription from a doctor — don't buy from online shops that ship without one
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一次门诊（有保险一般是几十美元 copay），加上来回跑一趟，半天时间。
  Cost: One clinic visit (usually a tens-of-dollars copay with insurance), plus the trip there and back — half a day.
- 说人话：处方药必须凭处方卖，在美国，网上卖处方药同样要凭真实处方。不问诊、不要处方、下单就发货的，要么卖的不是它说的药，要么这家店在违规。出了事，没人替你的剂量和禁忌负责。看一次医生，就有医生替你把这一关。
  In plain terms: Prescription drugs may only be sold with a prescription — in the U.S., online sales of prescription drugs likewise require a genuine prescription. A shop that asks no questions, requires no prescription, and ships on order is either not selling what it claims or breaking the law. If something goes wrong, nobody is responsible for your dosage and contraindications. One doctor visit puts a physician in charge of that decision.
- 收益：美国联邦和州法律都要求处方药凭处方销售（具体条款待核实），网上药店同样要凭真实处方、做实名验证。没经过处方审核，页面上就不该提供购买服务。特殊管制的药品不得在网上销售。
  Benefit: U.S. federal and state law both require prescription drugs to be sold on prescription (specific provisions to be verified); online pharmacies likewise must verify genuine prescriptions and the buyer's identity. Without prescription review, a product page shouldn't offer a purchase option. Specially controlled drugs may not be sold online.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：（美国处方药销售法规；具体条款待核实）
- 备注：法条可以逐字核对，但「按处方吃比自己买着吃能少出多少事」没人做过量化研究，所以按「难以量化」记 B 级。本节第 5、7 条说的类固醇和性激素，也走同一套规则。真到了该靠药减重的程度，医生会先看体重指数、身上有没有别的病、有没有不能用药的情况，再定吃不吃、吃哪种。这个判断是买不到的。
  Note: The statutes can be checked word for word, but nobody has quantified "how much safer it is to take it on prescription versus buying it yourself," so this is grade B for being hard to quantify. The steroids and hormones in items 5 and 7 follow the same rules. When medication for weight loss is genuinely warranted, a doctor first looks at BMI, other conditions, and contraindications before deciding whether and which drug to use. That judgment can't be bought.

### 7. 性激素类药只在医生开方并定期复查的前提下用，不要网购、不要自己加量 / Use hormone medications only with a doctor's prescription and regular checkups — don't buy them online or raise the dose yourself
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：定期看医生，加上抽血复查。一年几百美元（看保险），占掉几个半天。
  Cost: Regular doctor visits plus blood-work follow-ups. A few hundred dollars a year (depending on insurance), taking up several half-days.
- 说人话：这条不讨论该不该用，只讨论怎么用。用雌激素的跨性别女性，血管里长血块的比同龄人明显多，每一千人两年多出约 4 个，八年多出约 17 个。这类风险得靠定期复查盯着，网购药、自己调量就没人替你查。
  In plain terms: This item doesn't debate whether to use them, only how. Transgender women on estrogen had clearly more blood clots than their peers: about 4 extra cases per 1,000 people over two years, about 17 extra over eight years. Risks like these need regular follow-up to watch; buying online or adjusting the dose yourself leaves nobody checking.
- 收益：研究者从 Kaiser Permanente 这家医疗集团的电子病历里跟踪了一批人。其中 2,842 名跨性别女性、2,118 名跨性别男性。平均跟踪 4.0 年与 3.6 年。每个人都按出生年、种族、就诊点和入组时间配了约 10 倍的顺性别对照，一共 48,686 名顺性别男性与 48,775 名顺性别女性。跨性别女性的静脉血栓栓塞（血管里长血块堵住）比对照多。和顺性别男性比，2 年是每 1000 人 4.1 例（95% CI 1.6–6.7）。8 年是 16.7 例（6.4–27.5）。算下来，1000 个人里两年多出 4.1 例，八年多出 16.7 例。和顺性别女性比，这两个数是 3.4（1.1–5.6）与 13.7（4.1–22.7）。缺血性卒中（血管堵住那一类中风）与心肌梗死总体各组接近。但在跟踪期内才开始激素治疗的跨性别女性中，静脉血栓与缺血性卒中的差异更明显。跨性别男性的证据不够，下不了结论。作者结论为「这些结果可能提示需要长期警惕跨性别激素中雌激素的血管副作用」。
  Benefit: Researchers followed a group through Kaiser Permanente's electronic health records: 2,842 transgender women and 2,118 transgender men, followed an average of 4.0 and 3.6 years. Each was matched with about 10 times as many cisgender controls by birth year, race, care site, and enrollment date — 48,686 cisgender men and 48,775 cisgender women in total. Transgender women had more venous thromboembolism (blood clots blocking vessels) than controls. Versus cisgender men: 4.1 per 1,000 people at 2 years (95% CI 1.6–6.7), 16.7 at 8 years (6.4–27.5). That works out to 4.1 extra cases per 1,000 people over two years, 16.7 over eight years. Versus cisgender women: 3.4 (1.1–5.6) and 13.7 (4.1–22.7). Ischemic stroke (the blocked-vessel kind) and heart attack were similar across groups overall. But among transgender women who started hormone therapy during follow-up, the gaps in venous thromboembolism and ischemic stroke were more pronounced. Evidence for transgender men was insufficient for conclusions. The authors concluded these results "may indicate a need for long-term vigilance for the vascular side effects of estrogen in transgender hormone therapy."
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Getahun D et al. (2018). Cross-sex Hormones and Acute Cardiovascular Events in Transgender Persons: A Cohort Study. Ann Intern Med. <https://doi.org/10.7326/M17-2785>
- 备注：研究者自己写明了这项研究的短处：有多少人是在医院系统外自己买的药，他们查不到，所以「有没有用药」这条线本来就划不干净。所以用药更该放进正规随访里。这项研究看的只是血管出没出事，不回答激素治疗该不该用、用了有什么好处，那是另一套证据。所有用到性激素的场合都一样，包括避孕药、更年期激素治疗、睾酮补充。凡是自己网购、照着网帖调量、从不查血栓和肝肾指标的，风险都出在「没人盯着」这一段，和为什么用药无关。在美国，正规路径是去内分泌科或相应专科做评估、开方、复查。
  Note: The researchers themselves noted this study's weakness: they couldn't see how many people bought hormones outside the hospital system, so the line for "who used them" was never clean. That's all the more reason to keep use inside proper follow-up. This study only looked at whether vascular events occurred; it doesn't answer whether hormone therapy should be used or what its benefits are — that's a separate body of evidence. It applies to every use of sex hormones, including birth control pills, menopausal hormone therapy, and testosterone supplementation. Anyone buying online, adjusting doses from internet posts, and never checking clotting or liver/kidney markers carries risk from the "nobody's watching" stretch — regardless of why the hormones are used. In the U.S., the legitimate path is evaluation, prescription, and follow-up at an endocrinology or relevant specialty clinic.

### 8. 如果「觉得自己丑」已经到反复照镜子、反复想改的程度，先做一次体像评估再谈手术 / If "feeling ugly" has reached constant mirror-checking and fixating on changes, get a body-image evaluation before discussing surgery
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：去精神科或心理医生那做一次评估，一次门诊几十到上百美元（看保险）。难的是在约手术之前先去这一趟。
  Cost: One evaluation visit with a psychiatrist or psychologist — tens to a couple hundred dollars per visit depending on insurance. The hard part is making this trip before booking the surgery.
- 说人话：去做整形手术的人里约 15% 有躯体变形障碍，就是把别人基本看不出的缺陷放大到影响生活。去皮肤科求美的人里约 13%，两边都是七成多为女性。这种人做完一处又会盯上下一处，手术解决不了。先做一次评估看看自己是不是这种情况，再决定动不动手术。
  In plain terms: About 15% of people seeking plastic surgery have body dysmorphic disorder — magnifying flaws others can barely see until it affects daily life. About 13% among cosmetic dermatology patients, over 70% women in both groups. These people fixate on the next flaw after each procedure; surgery can't solve it. Get one evaluation first to see if this is you, then decide about surgery.
- 收益：按规矩把 33 项研究找齐再合起来算。整形外科就诊者中，躯体变形障碍（把别人基本看不出的缺陷放大到影响生活）患病率 15.04%，各项研究在 2.21%–56.67% 之间。换成人数，每 100 个去做整形的人里约 15 个符合，单看各项研究则从 2 个到 57 个不等。这批人平均年龄 34.54±12.41 岁，后一个数是上下浮动的幅度，74.38% 为女性。皮肤科就诊者是 12.65%，各项研究在 4.52%–35.16% 之间，平均年龄 27.79±9.03 岁，76.09% 为女性。作者建议整形外科医师与皮肤科医师主动评估、把高风险的人认出来，并安排几个科室一起管。
  Benefit: 33 studies systematically found and pooled. Among plastic surgery patients, body dysmorphic disorder (magnifying flaws others can barely see until it affects daily life) had a prevalence of 15.04%, ranging 2.21%–56.67% across studies. In headcount: about 15 of every 100 plastic surgery patients meet the criteria, ranging from 2 to 57 across individual studies. Average age 34.54±12.41 years (the second number is the spread), 74.38% women. Dermatology patients: 12.65%, ranging 4.52%–35.16%, average age 27.79±9.03, 76.09% women. The authors recommend plastic surgeons and dermatologists proactively evaluate, identify high-risk patients, and arrange multidisciplinary care.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：Ribeiro RVE (2017). Prevalence of Body Dysmorphic Disorder in Plastic Surgery and Dermatology Patients: A Systematic Review with Meta-Analysis. Aesthetic Plast Surg. <https://doi.org/10.1007/s00266-017-0869-0>
- 备注：各项研究测出来的比例从 2.21% 一直到 56.67%，差得极大。原因是用的问卷和调查的人群都不一样。所以只能读出「这个比例不低、值得先筛一次」，不能把 15.04% 当准确数字，记 B 级也是因为这个。想做医美的人绝大多数没有心理问题。本条针对的是「反复、持续、已经影响到工作和社交」这一小部分人，手术解决不了他们的问题。判断线索有这几条：一天里花很长时间检查或者遮挡某个部位，因此回避社交或者拍照，做完一处又盯上下一处。
  Note: The measured rates range from 2.21% all the way to 56.67% — a huge spread, because the questionnaires and surveyed populations differed. So all you can take away is "this rate is not low, worth one screening" — don't treat 15.04% as exact; that's also why this is grade B. The vast majority of people seeking cosmetic procedures have no psychological problem. This item targets the small subset whose pattern is "repetitive, persistent, already affecting work and social life" — surgery can't fix their problem. Clues: spending long stretches each day checking or covering a body part, avoiding social events or photos because of it, fixating on the next flaw after each procedure.

### 9. 矫正牙齿要找有正畸资质的医生当面检查、按时复诊，别网购牙套自己在家戴 / For orthodontics, see a qualified orthodontist in person and keep your follow-ups — don't buy aligners online and DIY at home
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=小 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：比网购多出门诊和复诊的钱，每隔一段时间要去一趟诊所。难在疗程常常一两年，要坚持复诊。
  Cost: More than buying online, for the visits and follow-ups — a clinic trip every so often. The hard part is that treatment often runs one to two years, and you have to keep the follow-ups.
- 说人话：透明牙套在美国按医疗器械由 FDA 监管。FDA 说没批准过不经牙医开方就直接卖给个人的牙套。没人当面检查，可能咬合变歪、牙齿移错位置、疼痛，甚至掉牙。
  In plain terms: Clear aligners are regulated by the FDA as medical devices in the U.S. The FDA says it has not authorized any clear aligners sold directly to consumers without a dentist's prescription. Without an in-person exam, you risk an uneven bite, teeth moving the wrong way, pain, even tooth loss.
- 收益：美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）的说法是：「The FDA has not authorized clear aligners that are sold directly to consumers without a prescription and supervision by a dentist or orthodontist」，意思是不经牙医开方和监督、直接卖给个人的透明牙套，它一个都没批准过。FDA 还说：「In-person examination by a dentist or orthodontist can help lower the risks of uneven bite, unintended tooth movement, pain, and tooth loss」，也就是牙医当面检查能降低咬合不齐、牙齿移位、疼痛和掉牙的风险。开始戴以后定期当面复诊，才能确认牙在按计划安全移动。这份是监管文件，没有给出出事的比例。
  Benefit: The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) states: "The FDA has not authorized clear aligners that are sold directly to consumers without a prescription and supervision by a dentist or orthodontist" — not a single aligner sold directly to individuals without a dentist's prescription and supervision has been authorized. The FDA also says: "In-person examination by a dentist or orthodontist can help lower the risks of uneven bite, unintended tooth movement, pain, and tooth loss." Regular in-person follow-ups after you start wearing them are how you confirm teeth are moving safely as planned. This is a regulatory document; it gives no incident rates.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：U.S. Food and Drug Administration (2026). Clear Dental Aligners（页面 2026 年 9 月 9 日更新）. <https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/dental-devices/clear-dental-aligners>
- 备注：收益量级定为小，因为没找到出事比例，只能凭后果判断。正畸属于医疗行为，做医疗美容性质的牙齿矫正，机构和医生都要有资质，查法见本节第 2 条（机构资质、医生执照）。正畸在美国一般走牙科保险或自费，具体覆盖看保险计划（待核实）。种牙之前先问清包干价，见第 24 节第 12 条。
  Note: Benefit is rated small because no incident rates were found — only the consequences to go by. Orthodontics is a medical procedure; for cosmetic orthodontics the facility and the doctor must both be qualified — see item 2 of this section for how to check. In the U.S., orthodontics is usually billed to dental insurance or paid out of pocket, with coverage varying by plan (to be verified). For asking a flat all-in price before implants, see section 24, item 12.
