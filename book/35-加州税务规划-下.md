# 35. 加州税务规划 / 35. California Tax Planning

### 26. 1031 延迟交换：45 天指定、180 天交割，日历日无延期 / The 1031 Exchange: 45 Days to Identify, 180 to Close — Calendar Days, No Extensions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：卖的是投资性/经营性房地产（自住房不行）。必须用 qualified intermediary（QI，合格中介）过手资金——卖房款**不能经过你的手**。QI 费用通常几百到几千美元。
  Cost: For investment/business real estate (not your home). A qualified intermediary (QI) must handle the funds — sale proceeds must **never touch your hands**. QI fees run hundreds to a few thousand dollars.
- 说人话：1031 交换（IRC §1031）让你卖投资房时把资本利得税**递延**（不是免掉）到以后：卖出后 **45 天内书面指定**（identify）要买的新物业，**180 天内完成交割**，两个都是**日历日、无延期**——撞上节假日、春节都不顺延。论坛血泪共识：失败三大死因是 45 天没指定、用过了卖房款（没经 QI）、买了自住房。实操建议：同时指定 3 个备选（200% 规则），或用 DST（Delaware Statutory Trust）做保底，防止 45 天内找不到标的。
  In plain terms: A 1031 exchange (IRC §1031) **defers** (not forgives) capital-gains tax when you sell investment property: **identify** the replacement in writing within **45 days** of sale, **close** within **180 days** — both are **calendar days with no extensions**, holidays and Lunar New Year included. Forum consensus on the top three failure causes: missing the 45-day identification, touching the proceeds (no QI), buying a personal residence. Practical tip: identify 3 backups (the 200% rule) or use a DST (Delaware Statutory Trust) as a safety net in case no target is found in 45 days.
- 收益：递延的税可以几十年不交，期间资金继续生钱；去世时继承人拿 stepped-up basis（见第 17 条思路），递延的税可能永远消失。湾区房东换仓的核心工具。
  Benefit: Deferred tax can go unpaid for decades while the money keeps working; at death, heirs get a stepped-up basis and the deferred tax may vanish forever. The core repositioning tool for Bay Area landlords.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRC §1031；IRS, Like-Kind Exchanges. <https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/like-kind-exchanges-real-property-used-in-business-or-investment>
- 备注："同类财产"指投资性/经营性房地产之间，公寓换商铺可以，投资房换自住房不行。加州还有外州物业的 clawback（第 27 条）。
  Note: "Like-kind" means between investment/business properties — apartment for retail works; investment property for a personal home doesn't. California claws back out-of-state exchanges (item 27).

### 27. 加州 1031 换到外州：FTB 每年追踪，搬离也逃不掉 / California's 1031 Clawback: The FTB Tracks Out-of-State Exchanges Yearly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。但每年要报 FTB Form 3840，报告换到外州的物业情况，直到最终应税处置。
  Cost: Nothing. But you must file FTB Form 3840 every year reporting the out-of-state property, until its final taxable disposition.
- 说人话：加州特色：你把加州的投资房 1031 换到外州（比如德州），联邦层面税递延了，但加州**不认这笔递延一笔勾销**——FTB 要求你每年报 3840 表跟踪那处外州物业；等你最终卖掉（哪怕那时你已搬离加州多年），加州回来征收当初递延的那部分税。论坛加州房东的高频提醒："搬到德州就不用交加州税了"是错的，这笔税跟着物业走，不是跟着人走。漏报 3840 表本身也有罚。
  In plain terms: The California twist: 1031-exchange a California investment property for one out of state (say, Texas) — federally deferred, but California **doesn't let the deferral evaporate**. The FTB makes you file Form 3840 yearly tracking the out-of-state property; when you finally sell (even years after leaving California), California collects the deferred tax then. The frequent warning from California landlords on forums: "moving to Texas ends California tax" is wrong — this tax follows the property, not the person. Skipping Form 3840 draws its own penalty.
- 收益：知道这条，就不会在"搬离加州省税"的算盘里漏掉最大的一笔。真要断干净，咨询 CPA 做最终处置规划。
  Benefit: Knowing this keeps the biggest line item from missing in your "leave California" math. For a truly clean break, plan the final disposition with a CPA.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：FTB Form 3840 说明；California Revenue & Taxation Code §18032
- 备注：这条只针对 1031 换到外州的情形；加州境内的 1031 正常递延。跨州税务是 CPA 的专长，别自己算。
  Note: This applies only to 1031 exchanges into out-of-state property; in-California exchanges defer normally. Multi-state tax is CPA territory — don't DIY it.

### 28. 短租 loophole：平均出租 ≤7 天 + 实质参与，亏损可抵 W-2 / The Short-Term-Rental Loophole: ≤7-Day Average Stays + Material Participation Lets Losses Offset W-2
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=要 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：要真买一套短租房（Airbnb 等），真花时间经营。论坛共识的"实质参与"（material participation）门槛约每年 100 小时以上，且参与度超过其他所有人。
  Cost: Requires actually buying and genuinely operating a short-term rental (Airbnb etc.). Forum consensus on "material participation" is ~100+ hours/year, more than anyone else involved.
- 说人话：正常出租房的亏损是被动亏损，一般不能抵 W-2 工资。但短租有例外：平均出租期 **≤7 天** + 房东**实质参与**经营（自己管入住、维修、定价，留好记录），亏损可直接抵 W-2——**不需要** 750 小时的 real estate professional 身份（第 29 条）。配套玩法是 cost segregation（成本分摊）：把房价拆成 5/7/15 年折旧的部件，叠加 bonus depreciation，前期造出巨额"纸面亏损"。风险：IRS 审计时会查实质参与证据（入住记录、维修工单、平台后台）；"全托管还想抵 W-2"被论坛一致认为是坑——托管了，你的参与时间就不够了。
  In plain terms: Normal rental losses are passive and can't offset W-2 wages. Short-term rentals are the exception: average stays **≤7 days** plus the owner's **material participation** (you handle check-ins, repairs, pricing — with records) lets losses offset W-2 directly — **without** the 750-hour test (item 29). The companion play is cost segregation: split the price into 5/7/15-year components, stack bonus depreciation, manufacture large early paper losses. The risk: audits test participation evidence (booking logs, repair tickets, platform dashboards); "fully outsourced yet offsetting W-2" is universally panned — outsourced hours don't count.
- 收益：高收入 W-2 家庭用一套短租房，一年可能造出几万到十几万美元的纸面亏损抵工资，按 40%+ 综合税率省税数万美元。但这是 IRS 重点盯的领域，证据链必须完整。
  Benefit: A high-income W-2 household can generate tens to hundreds of thousands in paper losses against wages — tens of thousands saved at 40%+ combined rates. Heavily scrutinized; the evidence trail must be airtight.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：IRC §469（被动亏损规则例外）；IRS Publication 925；White Coat Investor 相关分析
- 备注：定成 B 级是因为"100 小时"是行业和论坛的经验线，不是条文数字；IRS 对短租的审计口径在收紧。bonus depreciation 比例逐年变化，动手前查当年数字。
  Note: Graded B because "100 hours" is industry/forum lore, not statute, and IRS scrutiny of STRs is tightening. Bonus depreciation percentages change yearly — check the current figure first.

### 29. Real Estate Professional：750 小时 + 超过其他工作，亏损无限抵普通收入 / Real Estate Professional Status: 750 Hours Plus More Than Any Other Job
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=多 毅力=要 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：每年在房地产经营上花 **750 小时以上**，**且**超过你在其他工作上花的时间。论坛常见形态是夫妻一方全职做（另一方 W-2 不受影响）。
  Cost: 750+ hours/year in real-estate operations, **and** more than you spend at any other job. The common forum shape: one spouse does it full-time while the other's W-2 is unaffected.
- 说人话：拿到这个身份（IRC §469(c)(7)），你的出租亏损就不再是被动亏损，可以**无限**抵普通收入（工资）。两个条件缺一不可：750 小时是硬线，超过其他工作时间意味着全职上班的人基本没戏——这就是为什么通常是夫妻一方辞职或本来就是全职主妇/主夫来做。IRS 审计时要时间日志（log），口头说"我天天在管房子"不算数。
  In plain terms: With this status (IRC §469(c)(7)), rental losses stop being passive and can offset ordinary income **without limit**. Both tests are required: 750 hours is a hard floor, and "more than any other job" rules out full-time employees — which is why it's usually one spouse who quits or is already full-time at home. IRS audits demand a time log; "I manage the houses every day" doesn't count without one.
- 收益：多套出租房 + cost segregation 造出的亏损，一年抵掉几十万美元工资收入都有可能。这是职业房东家庭的核心架构。
  Benefit: Multiple rentals plus cost segregation can wipe out hundreds of thousands in wage income in a year. It's the core architecture for professional-landlord households.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRC §469(c)(7)；IRS Publication 925
- 备注：750 小时和"超过其他工作"都是法定硬条件，选举按年做。时间日志从第一天就开始记，审计时补记可信度大打折扣。
  Note: Both the 750 hours and the "more than any other job" tests are statutory; the election is annual. Start the time log on day one — reconstructed logs lose credibility in audits.

### 30. 折旧抵了税，卖出时按 25% 回冲 / Depreciation You Deducted Gets Recaptured at 25% on Sale
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。但卖房那年要算这笔账：以前每年抵税的折旧（depreciation），卖出时按 **25%** 的税率 recapture（unrecaptured section 1250 gain）。
  Cost: Nothing upfront. But in the sale year, do this math: depreciation deducted over the years is **recaptured at 25%** (unrecaptured section 1250 gain).
- 说人话：出租房每年折旧抵税很爽，但这笔钱不是白给的：卖出时，累计折旧里对应住宅房产的部分，最高按 25% 交税（比长期资本利得的 20% 还高）。很多人第一次卖房才知道。1031 交换（第 26 条）能递延但**消不掉**这笔 recapture——它跟着递延走。完全避开的办法只有去世时的 stepped-up basis（继承人按市价重估基数，折旧一笔勾销）。
  In plain terms: Rental depreciation feels great each year, but it isn't free: on sale, the residential portion of accumulated depreciation is taxed at up to **25%** (higher than the 20% long-term capital-gains rate). Many landlords learn this at their first sale. A 1031 exchange (item 26) **defers but doesn't erase** recapture — it travels with the deferral. The only full escape is the stepped-up basis at death (heirs revalue to market price; depreciation wiped clean).
- 收益：提前知道，就会在持有 vs 卖出的决策里把 25% 算进去，而不是卖完才傻眼。长期持有 + 1031 + 身后 step-up，是一整套传承架构。
  Benefit: Knowing early puts the 25% into your hold-vs.-sell math instead of ambushing you after closing. Long hold + 1031 + step-up at death is a complete legacy architecture.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRC §1250；IRS Publication 544；IRS Publication 551（basis）
- 备注：25% 是上限税率，实际按你的税率档孰低。土地不折旧，只有建筑物部分能折。
  Note: 25% is the maximum rate — you pay the lower of it and your bracket. Land doesn't depreciate; only the building portion does.

### 31. Prop 13 与 Prop 19：加州房产税的低税基是传家宝 / Prop 13 and Prop 19: California's Low Property-Tax Base Is a Family Heirloom
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。理解规则，在买卖和传承时别亲手毁掉低税基。
  Cost: Nothing. Understand the rules so you don't destroy a low tax base by accident when buying, selling, or passing property on.
- 说人话：**Prop 13（1978）**：房产税按 assessed value 的约 1% 交（加地方债券实际约 1.1%–1.2%），assessed value 每年涨幅上限 **2%**——房子市价翻几倍，税基几乎不动。**重估触发**只有两件事：change in ownership（买卖、转让）和 new construction（加建），按市价重估。所以湾区老房子的低税基是真正的传家宝。**Prop 19（2020 通过）**：父母子女间转让的免重估优惠大幅收窄——**只有子女在一年内将其作为自住房**才能保留原税基，出租房、度假房不再享受；同时 55 岁以上老人、残障人士、灾民可把原税基带到加州境内任何县的新房（最多三次）。
  In plain terms: **Prop 13 (1978)**: property tax is ~1% of assessed value (plus local bonds, effectively ~1.1%–1.2%), and assessed value rises at most **2%**/year — market prices can multiply while the tax base barely moves. Reassessment triggers only on **change in ownership** (sale/transfer) or **new construction**, at market value. So a Bay Area home's low tax base is a genuine heirloom. **Prop 19 (passed 2020)**: the parent-child exclusion from reassessment is sharply narrowed — **only if the child uses it as a primary residence within one year** does the original base survive; rentals and vacation homes no longer qualify. Meanwhile, homeowners 55+, disabled persons, and disaster victims can carry their base to a new home anywhere in California (up to three times).
- 收益：保住低税基，一年省的房产税可能是几千到上万美元，且每年复利式拉开差距。加建前先查是否触发重估；父母传房给子女，提前一年规划自住安排。
  Benefit: Preserving a low base saves thousands to tens of thousands yearly in property tax, compounding wider each year. Check reassessment triggers before additions; plan a child's primary-residence move a year ahead for parent-child transfers.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Constitution Article XIII A；各县 Assessor 办公室说明（如 Alameda County Assessor）
- 备注：信托持有、加建、部分转让等情形的重估规则很细，大额操作前咨询房地产税务律师。Prop 19 的细则各县执行口径可能有差异。
  Note: Reassessment rules for trusts, additions, and partial transfers are intricate — consult a real-estate tax attorney before big moves. Prop 19 implementation can vary by county.

### 32. 加州对资本利得没有优惠税率；NIIT 3.8% 的门槛 13 年没调过 / California Has No Preferential Capital-Gains Rate; the NIIT Threshold Hasn't Moved in 13 Years
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。记住两条，卖股票卖房前先算加州这部分。
  Cost: Nothing. Remember two things and price in the California portion before selling stock or property.
- 说人话：第一，加州**不**对长期资本利得设优惠税率——联邦 0%/15%/20% 的优惠，在加州全部按**普通收入**征，九档 1%–12.3%，应税收入超 $1,000,000 的部分再加 1%（实际最高 **13.3%**）。2025 年税级（供参考）：单身 9.3% 档起于约 $72,724，12.3% 档起于约 $742,954；2026 年门槛 FTB 要到 2026 年 12 月才发布（待核实）。第二，联邦的 **NIIT（净投资收入税）3.8%**：MAGI 超单身 $200,000/夫妻 $250,000 **且**有净投资收入时，对两者较小者加征 3.8%——这个门槛**自 2013 年立法以来从未随通胀调整**，工资涨、门槛不动，越来越多中产被动撞线。
  In plain terms: First, California offers **no** preferential capital-gains rate — the federal 0%/15%/20% breaks don't exist here; gains are taxed as **ordinary income** across nine brackets of 1%–12.3%, plus 1% above $1,000,000 of taxable income (effective top **13.3%**). 2025 brackets for reference: single 9.3% starts around $72,724, 12.3% around $742,954; 2026 thresholds come from the FTB in December 2026 (to be verified). Second, the federal **NIIT 3.8%** on net investment income: MAGI above $200,000 single / $250,000 joint **with** investment income triggers 3.8% on the smaller of the two — a threshold **never inflation-adjusted since 2013**, so rising wages push more middle-class households over it passively.
- 收益：卖股票前把"联邦 15%/20% + 加州 9.3%–13.3% + 可能的 3.8%"加总看综合税率，再决定卖多少、分几年卖（配合第 17 条的低收入年）。
  Benefit: Before selling, total up "federal 15%/20% + California 9.3%–13.3% + possible 3.8%" as one combined rate, then decide how much to sell and over how many years (pair with low-income years, item 17).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：FTB（加州税级）；IRC §1411 (NIIT)；IRS Form 8960
- 备注：社安金（Social Security）加州免税，是少数加州比联邦友好的地方。2026 年加州税级门槛待 FTB 发布（待核实），精确测算时再查。
  Note: Social Security benefits are California-tax-free — a rare spot where California is friendlier than federal. California's 2026 bracket thresholds await FTB publication (to be verified); look them up for precise planning.

### 33. FBAR：海外账户加总超 $10,000 就要报，只申报不交税 / FBAR: Report If Overseas Accounts Total Over $10,000 — Disclosure Only, No Tax
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。每年 4/15 前向 FinCEN 电子申报（FinCEN Form 114），**自动延期到 10/15**，不用申请。
  Cost: Nothing. File electronically with FinCEN (Form 114) by 4/15 each year — **automatically extended to 10/15**, no application needed.
- 说人话：美国税务居民（公民、绿卡、满足 substantial presence 的 resident alien），在**任一日历年内任一时点**、全部海外金融账户**加总最高余额超过 $10,000**，就要申报。三个中文圈高频误区：①"没赚钱就不用报"——错，看**余额**不看收益；②"分几个账户存就不到 $10k"——错，是**加总**，含你有签字权的账户；③"报了就行，反正不交税"——对了一半：FBAR 确实**只申报、不产生税**，纯信息披露，但它是独立申报（走 FinCEN 的 BSA E-Filing 系统，**不附在 1040 后面**），漏报罚则极重（第 36 条）。
  In plain terms: U.S. tax residents (citizens, green-card holders, resident aliens meeting the substantial-presence test) must file if **all** foreign financial accounts **combined** exceed $10,000 at **any point** in the calendar year. Three recurring Chinese-language myths: ① "no earnings, no filing" — wrong, it's about **balances**, not gains; ② "split across accounts to stay under $10k" — wrong, it's the **aggregate**, including accounts you merely have signature authority over; ③ "it's just disclosure, no big deal" — half right: FBAR truly creates **no tax**, pure information reporting, but it's a **separate filing** (FinCEN's BSA E-Filing, **not** attached to Form 1040), and non-filing penalties are severe (item 36).
- 收益：申报本身不花一分钱，漏报的代价可能是账户余额的 50%。这是华人家庭性价比最高的"税务动作"。
  Benefit: Filing costs nothing; the price of skipping can be 50% of the account. The highest-ROI "tax move" for Chinese households.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：31 USC §5314；IRS, Report of Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts (FBAR). <https://www.irs.gov/Businesses/Small-Businesses-&-Self-Employed/Report-of-Foreign-Bank-and-Financial-Accounts-FBAR>
- 备注：微信/支付宝里的钱如果算金融账户余额，实务中有争议，保守起见计入加总（具体口径可咨询 CPA）。只报不交税，但报了不等于交了税——利息收入该报税照样报。
  Note: Whether WeChat Pay/Alipay balances count is debated in practice — conservatively include them in the aggregate (ask a CPA for the current position). Filing isn't paying: interest income is still taxable as usual.

### 34. FATCA Form 8938：和 FBAR 是两张网，报了 8938 不能免 FBAR / FATCA Form 8938: A Second Net — Filing It Doesn't Excuse FBAR
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。随 Form 1040 一并报给 IRS（和 FBAR 的 FinCEN 系统是两套）。
  Cost: Nothing. Filed with Form 1040 to the IRS (a separate system from FBAR's FinCEN filing).

### 40. 合法节税和逃税的界限：前者是权利，后者是联邦重罪 / Lawful Planning vs. Evasion: The First Is Your Right; the Second Is a Federal Felony

But 26 USC §7201 makes willful tax evasion a **federal felony**: up to **5 years** in prison plus fines (up to $250,000 for individuals) plus prosecution costs. Three elements: tax owed, an affirmative act of evasion, and willfulness (deliberately violating a known legal duty). Companion charges: false returns §7206 (felony, up to 3 years); willful failure to file §7203 (misdemeanor, up to 1 year/year); civil fraud penalty **75%** of the underpayment (IRC §6663).   Benefit: Play it straight and every dollar saved lets you sleep; play it crooked and you repay the savings with interest — plus freedom. Every "benefit" in this chapter holds only on the lawful side.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：26 USC §7201, §7203, §7206；IRC §6663；Gregory v. Helvering, 293 U.S. 465 (1935)

- 说人话：代购、副业、家教、小费——现金收入一样要报税，这是中文圈"技巧帖"最爱含糊的地方。IRS 刑事调查局的定罪率约 90%，手段包括银行存款分析（bank deposits method）、生活开支与申报收入倒推；现金密集行业还会用行业可比数据筛查异常（具体模型未公开，章节只做定性表述）。更狠的是**举报奖励**（26 USC §7623(b)）：举报导致 IRS 追回税款/罚款/利息，争议金额超 $200 万时，举报人拿追回金额的 **15%–30%** 强制奖励——你的合伙人、前雇员、前任，都可能是举报人。
  In plain terms: Reselling, side gigs, tutoring, tips — cash income is taxable, the favorite blur in Chinese-language "hack" posts. The IRS Criminal Investigation division's conviction rate runs ~90%, using bank-deposits analysis and lifestyle-vs.-reported-income reconstruction; cash-heavy industries get screened against industry comparables (models undisclosed — stated qualitatively here). Worse, **whistleblower rewards** (26 USC §7623(b)): when a tip leads the IRS to recover tax/penalties/interest over $2 million in dispute, the whistleblower gets a mandatory **15%–30%** cut — your partner, ex-employee, or ex could be that whistleblower.
- 收益：如实报，成本是按税率交税；不报，成本是补税 + 利息 + 75% 欺诈罚款（第 40 条）+ 可能的刑事责任。这笔账不用算。
  Benefit: Report honestly and the cost is tax at your rate; don't, and it's back tax + interest + 75% fraud penalty (item 40) + possible criminal exposure. No math needed.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：26 USC §7623(b)；IRS Criminal Investigation 年度报告（定罪率）；IRC §6663
- 备注："IRS 行业现金比例模型"不写具体模型名称和数字，只保留"银行流水和行业可比数据交叉比对"的定性表述。§7623(b) 的 $200 万门槛针对争议金额，另有个人年收入 $200,000 的附加条件。
  Note: No specific model names or figures for IRS cash-industry screening — only the qualitative "bank records cross-checked against industry comparables." The §7623(b) $2M threshold keys off the amount in dispute, with an additional $200,000 annual-income condition for individual targets.

### 43. 虚假慈善捐赠：超 $5,000 没评估报告，等于给 IRS 递审计邀请函 / Fake Charitable Deductions: Over $5,000 Without an Appraisal Is an Audit Invitation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。真捐、留好收据；非现金捐赠超 $5,000（上市公司股票等除外）必须做 **qualified appraisal（合格评估）**。
  Cost: Nothing. Donate for real, keep receipts; non-cash donations over $5,000 (listed securities excepted) require a **qualified appraisal**.
- 说人话：规则是阶梯式的：$250 以上要有受赠机构的书面确认；$500 以上附 **Form 8283**；**超 $5,000** 的非现金捐赠（衣服、家具、艺术品等）必须有合格评估师的评估报告 + Form 8283 Section B + 受赠机构签字。中文圈偶见的"技巧帖"——虚报捐赠额、拿回扣的"捐赠"、给关联方"捐"——英文论坛一致认为是审计高危区。IRS 对"捐赠额占收入比例异常"的筛查很成熟，无评估的大额捐赠是经典靶点。真做了慈善，收据、评估、8283 三件套收好，七年备查。
  In plain terms: The rules are tiered: $250+ needs the charity's written acknowledgment; $500+ attaches **Form 8283**; non-cash gifts **over $5,000** (clothes, furniture, art — listed securities excepted) need a qualified appraiser's report + Form 8283 Section B + the charity's signature. The occasional Chinese-language "hack" — inflating gift values, kickback "donations," donating to related parties — is universally flagged as audit bait on English forums. The IRS screens routinely for donation-to-income ratios that look off; large undocumented non-cash gifts are classic targets. Donate for real, keep the receipt-appraisal-8283 trio for seven years.
- 收益：真捐赠的抵扣（第 7–9 条）本来就很值钱，没必要造假；造假被抓，补税 + 75% 欺诈罚款 + 评估师连带责任。
  Benefit: Real charitable deductions (items 7–9) are already valuable — no need to fake them; getting caught means back tax + 75% fraud penalty + appraiser liability.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS Publication 526（评估要求）；Form 8283 说明书；IRC §170(f)(11)
- 备注：二手衣物捐 Goodwill 这类小额捐赠，按公允价值（thrift-shop value）申报，别按吊牌价。$5,000 的评估门槛是单项或同类物品合计。
  Note: Used-clothing donations to Goodwill-type charities deduct at fair (thrift-shop) value, not the price tag. The $5,000 appraisal threshold applies per item or group of similar items.

### 44. 中文圈"税务技巧帖"的风险：2026 年 IRS 的 AI 审计专盯这类操作 / The Risk in Chinese-Language "Tax Hack" Posts: The IRS's 2026 AI Audits Target Exactly These

Run every "hack" post through three questions: ① which tax year's numbers (2024/2025 figures are widely stale in 2026); ② federal or California (California doesn't recognize HSAs, K-12 529 use, or preferential capital-gains rates); ③ a filing technique or income concealment (the latter is a crime).

  In plain terms: Chinese-language "hacks" seen in this research, ranked by risk. **Stale numbers**: "1099-K over $600" (restored, item 24), old 2024/2025 limits, "SALT is still $10,000." **Wrong jurisdiction**: federal rules mistaken for California's (HSA, 529) — overpaid state tax. **Gray operations**: informal exchange (item 41), "borrowed" dependents, inflated home offices (item 22) — audit bait. **Outright crimes**: structuring, systematic cash underreporting, fake donations (items 40–43). The IRS's 2026 AI-audit push targets exactly these; the "nobody checks" mentality is the riskiest asset. Items 1–39 are the safe zone; 40–44 the high-voltage line.
- 收益：用三问过滤掉 90% 的坑帖，剩下的 10% 对照本章条目核对税年和州。省下的可能是罚款，也可能是自由。
