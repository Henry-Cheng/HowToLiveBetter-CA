# 18. 养孩子划不划算·加州版

这一节算的是钱和时间两笔账。和第 10 节算结婚账一样，这里只把账拆开给你看，不替你下结论。已经怀孕、要照流程一步步办事的，看第 27 节（本章配套加州版）。本章的制度内容按加州及美国联邦现行规定写：美国没有按月发放的育儿补贴，对应的是报税时的税收抵免；联邦没有带薪产假，加州的带薪部分靠 SDI 和 PFL。

This chapter counts two ledgers — money and time. Like Chapter 10's marriage ledger, it only lays the accounts out for you; no verdict on your behalf. If you're already pregnant and working through the steps, see Chapter 27 (California edition of this book). Institutional content follows current California and U.S. federal rules: the U.S. has no monthly child allowance — the counterpart is tax credits at filing time; there's no federal paid parental leave — California's paid part runs through SDI and PFL.

### 1. 先把能领的算进来：联邦儿童税收抵免每孩每年 $2,200（2025 税年起），加州另有幼儿税收抵免 / Count what you can get first: federal Child Tax Credit $2,200/child/year (from tax year 2025), plus California's Young Child Tax Credit
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。报税时申报，孩子和申报人要有 SSN。
  Cost: No money. Claim it at tax filing; the child and the filer need SSNs.
- 说人话：美国没有中国那种直接发到卡上的育儿补贴，对应的是报税时的抵免。联邦儿童税收抵免（Child Tax Credit）：2025 税年起每孩 $2,200（17 岁以下），其中最多 $1,700 可退——没欠税也能拿回来一部分；夫妻联合申报收入超过 $400,000 开始递减。加州另有 Young Child Tax Credit：6 岁以下，每份报税表最高 $1,189（2025 税年），可退，面向低收入（earned income $32,900 以下）。
  In plain terms: The U.S. has no child allowance deposited to your card like China's; the counterpart is credits at tax time. Federal Child Tax Credit: $2,200 per child under 17 starting with tax year 2025 (raised from $2,000 by the 2025 One Big Beautiful Bill), up to $1,700 of it refundable — you can get part back even owing no tax; it phases out above $400,000 for joint filers. California adds the Young Child Tax Credit: under age 6, up to $1,189 per return (tax year 2025), refundable, for lower earners (earned income $32,900 or less).
- 收益：联邦每孩每年 $2,200（2025 税年起，之后随通胀调整），可退部分最高 $1,700；加州 6 岁以下每报税表最高 $1,189，可退。这两笔都不用交税。
  Benefit: Federal $2,200 per child per year (from tax year 2025, inflation-indexed after), refundable portion up to $1,700; California up to $1,189 per return for under-6s, refundable. Neither is taxable.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：TurboTax, Child Tax Credit for 2025-2026. <https://turbotax.intuit.ca/tax-tips/family/child-tax-credit/L9ZIjdlZz>；California Franchise Tax Board, CalEITC / Young Child Tax Credit. <https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/personal/credits/caleitc/index.html?WT.mc_id=akCalEITCNews>
- 备注：这是报税时抵税或退税的钱，不是按月发的；在养孩子的开支里只是很小的一块，别把它当成生不生的依据。先把这两笔确定能拿到的钱算进来，再看还差多少。
  Note: This is tax-time credit/refund money, not a monthly payment — a small slice of child-raising costs, not a basis for the decision. Count these certain amounts first, then see the gap.

### 2. 联邦没有带薪产假；加州的带薪部分靠 SDI＋PFL，保岗靠 PDL＋CFRA＋FMLA / No federal paid leave — California's paid part is SDI + PFL; job protection is PDL + CFRA + FMLA
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。SDI/PFL 是你工资单上 CASDI 缴出来的，雇主要按规定配合申报。
  Cost: No money out of pocket. SDI/PFL are funded by the CASDI deduction on your paystub; employers must cooperate with the filings as required.
- 说人话：联邦 FMLA 只给 12 周无薪假（50 人以上雇主），一分钱工资替代没有。加州带薪部分：SDI 产前最多 4 周加产后顺产 6 周/剖宫产 8 周，接着 PFL 8 周（父母都能休，用于和新生儿 bonding，可在孩子 1 岁内分开休）；2025 年 1 月起替代率 70%–90%（低收入 90%），每周有上限（2025 年最高 $1,681）。保岗部分：怀孕残疾假 PDL 最长 4 个月，之后 CFRA 12 周（孩子出生原因），联邦 FMLA 12 周无薪。在旧金山工作的，雇主可能被要求把 PFL 补足到全薪（PPLO）。
  In plain terms: Federal FMLA gives only 12 weeks unpaid (employers with 50+ employees) — zero wage replacement. California's paid part: SDI up to 4 weeks before birth plus 6 weeks after a vaginal delivery / 8 after a C-section, then PFL 8 weeks (either parent, for bonding with the newborn, usable intermittently within the child's first year); since January 2025 the replacement rate is 70%–90% (90% for lower earners), with a weekly cap ($1,681 max in 2025). Job-protection part: Pregnancy Disability Leave up to 4 months, then CFRA 12 weeks (for the child's birth), federal FMLA 12 weeks unpaid. In San Francisco, employers may have to top PFL up to full pay (PPLO).
- 收益：SDI（产前 4 周＋产后 6–8 周）→ PFL（8 周 bonding）是工资替代链条，2025 年起 70%–90%；PDL（4 个月）→ CFRA（12 周）→ FMLA（12 周无薪）是岗位保护链条。两条链可以叠加，一个孩子出生前后最长可达 4 个月＋12 周的保岗假，其中约 14–16 周是部分带薪的。
  Benefit: SDI (4 weeks pre + 6–8 weeks post) → PFL (8 weeks bonding) is the wage-replacement chain at 70%–90% since 2025; PDL (4 months) → CFRA (12 weeks) → FMLA (12 weeks unpaid) is the job-protection chain. The chains stack: up to 4 months + 12 weeks of protected leave around a birth, roughly 14–16 weeks of it partially paid.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：EDD, Paid Family Leave Benefit Payment Amounts. <https://edd.ca.gov/en/Disability/Calculating_PFL_Benefit_Payment_Amounts>；EDD DI Fund Forecast 2025–2026（SB 951 替代率）. <https://edd.ca.gov/siteassets/files/unemployment/pdf/edddiforecastmay25.pdf>；California Civil Rights Department, Employment FAQ（PDL/CFRA）. <https://calcivilrights.ca.gov/Employment/?content=faq/>；California Government Code §12945. <https://codes.findlaw.com/ca/government-code/gov-sect-12945/>
- 备注：PFL 的 8 周不用一次休完，可以在孩子 1 岁内分开休；父亲/另一方父母同样能休。休假前和 HR 确认三件事：SDI/PFL 的申请时点、健康保险续保、返岗安排。
  Note: PFL's 8 weeks needn't be taken at once — split them within the child's first year; fathers and other parents qualify equally. Before leave, confirm three things with HR: SDI/PFL filing timing, health-insurance continuation, and return-to-work arrangements.

### 3. 知道这条：不得因怀孕、生育、哺乳降工资或者辞退；雇主必须提供哺乳时间和地点 / Know this: no pay cuts or firing for pregnancy, childbirth, or nursing — employers must provide lactation time and space
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。
  Cost: No money.
- 说人话：联邦《怀孕歧视法》加上加州公平就业和住房法（FEHA, Gov. Code §12940），禁止因怀孕、生育、哺乳歧视；怀孕残疾假（PDL）结束有权返岗。哺乳：加州《劳动法典》第 1030–1031 条要求雇主提供合理的哺乳休息时间（尽量与现有休息时间合并）和除厕所隔间之外的私密哺乳地点；联邦法律也有对应要求。真被违法辞退，取证重点是调岗、降薪的书面通知和聊天记录。
  In plain terms: The federal Pregnancy Discrimination Act plus California's Fair Employment and Housing Act (FEHA, Gov. Code §12940) bar discrimination over pregnancy, childbirth, and nursing; Pregnancy Disability Leave carries a right to return. Nursing: California Labor Code §§1030–1031 require employers to provide reasonable break time for expressing milk (running concurrently with existing breaks where possible) and a private lactation space other than a toilet stall; federal law has parallel requirements. If you're unlawfully fired, the evidence priorities are written reassignment/pay-cut notices and chat records.
- 收益：怀孕、生育、哺乳期间降工资、辞退、解约违法；PDL 返岗权受保护；雇主必须提供哺乳时间和私密地点。
  Benefit: Pay cuts, firing, or contract termination over pregnancy, childbirth, or nursing are unlawful; PDL return rights are protected; employers must provide lactation time and a private space.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Labor Code §§1030–1033. <https://law.justia.com/codes/california/2009/lab/1030-1033.html>；CDPH, Know Your Breastfeeding Rights — Lactation Accommodation for Employers. <https://www.cdph.ca.gov/Programs/CFH/DMCAH/CDPH%20Document%20Library/BFP/BFP-Knowing-Your-Breastfeeding-Rights-Employer.pdf>
- 备注：真的被违法辞退了，索赔路径见第 19 节（原书第 19 节）。取证的重点是调岗、降薪的书面通知和聊天记录，这两样要留好。
  Note: If you're unlawfully fired, the claims path is in Section 19 (original book, Section 19). Prioritize keeping written reassignment/pay-cut notices and chat records.

### 4. 时间账按「谁的时间被占掉」算，不按「辛不辛苦」算 / Count the time ledger by whose time is taken — not by how hard it feels
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=多 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱，花的是时间。孩子 3 岁前基本是全天被占住。难在天天如此，没法咬牙几天就过去。
  Cost: No money — it costs time. Before age 3 the child occupies nearly the whole day. The hard part is that it's every day; you can't grit through a few days and be done.
- 说人话：把每天看孩子的小时数、夜里被吵醒的次数，都折算成时间。再乘上干这些活的那个人的时薪。算出来的数，一般远远大于奶粉尿布钱。所以分工最好在生之前就谈好。
  In plain terms: Convert daily childcare hours and nighttime wake-ups into hours, then multiply by the hourly wage of whoever does the work. The number usually dwarfs diaper-and-formula money. So negotiate the division of labor before the birth, not after.
- 收益：把每天照看孩子的时间、夜里被吵醒的次数，都折算成小时。再乘以干这些活的那个人的时薪。得到的数字通常远大于奶粉尿布钱。照看、做饭、夜里起夜这些活虽然没人付钱，也照这样记进成本。算法和第 10 节算家务的那套一样。先谈好分工，再决定生不生。
  Benefit: Convert daily childcare time and nighttime wake-ups into hours, then multiply by the caregiver's hourly wage. The result usually far exceeds diaper-and-formula spending. Childcare, cooking, and night wakings count as costs even though nobody pays for them. Same method as Chapter 10's housework ledger. Negotiate the split before deciding to have the child.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验，无直接文献；口径见第 10 节
- 备注：数字不必算得很准。有用的是两个人在生之前对着同一张表谈分工，免得生完再吵。
  Note: The number doesn't need to be precise. What's useful is two people negotiating the split against the same sheet before the birth — so there's no fight after.

### 5. 钱账分三段算：0 到 3 岁、K–12、K–12 之后 / Split the money ledger in three: ages 0–3, K–12, after K–12
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：花多少钱没有固定数，城市不同、带法不同，差得很远。难在要静下心来把三段账分开算，不能凭感觉拍一个数。
  Cost: No fixed number — it varies enormously by city and parenting style. The hard part is calmly splitting the three ledgers instead of blurting one scary number.
- 说人话：美国农业部最新的一版《家庭子女支出》报告仍是 2015 年数据（2017 年发布）：中等收入双亲家庭把一个孩子养到 17 岁约 $233,610（不含大学）；Brookings 2022 年按高通胀环境重算约 $310,605。报告里住房占约三成，托育和教育占一成六到一成八，西部城市成本最高。0–3 岁的大头是托育方式：自己带、家人带，还是送 daycare（加州 daycare 很贵）。K–12 公立免费，大头是住房和课外。再往后，大头是大学。
  In plain terms: The USDA's latest Expenditures on Children by Families report is still the 2015 data (published 2017): a middle-income two-parent family spends about $233,610 raising a child to 17 (college not included); Brookings recalculated it at about $310,605 under 2022's high-inflation conditions. In the report, housing is about 30%, childcare and education 16–18%, with urban Western cities the priciest. Ages 0–3 hinge on childcare mode: parents, family, or daycare (California daycare is expensive). K–12 public school is free — the money goes to housing and extracurriculars. After that, it's college.
- 收益：USDA 2015：中等收入双亲家庭养一个孩子到 17 岁 $233,610；Brookings 2022 通胀重算：$310,605；住房占比最高（约 29%–31%），其次是托育教育和食品。三段的花法完全不同，混在一起算，只会得到一个吓人的总数。
  Benefit: USDA 2015: $233,610 for a middle-income married-couple family to raise a child to 17; Brookings 2022 inflation recalculation: $310,605; housing the largest share (~29–31%), then childcare/education and food. The three stages spend money in completely different ways — blending them yields only a scary total.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：USDA, How Much Will It Cost to Raise a Child? <https://www.usda.gov/about-usda/news/blog/how-much-will-it-cost-raise-child>；USDA, Expenditures on Children by Families, 2015（PDF）. <https://webarchive.library.unt.edu/web/20170119162626mp_/https://www.cnpp.usda.gov/sites/default/files/crc2015.pdf>
- 备注：报告不含大学费用；加州公立大学学费查 UC/CSU 官网。先把第 1 条的税收抵免算进来，再看还差多少。别拿网上流传的「养大一个孩子要多少万」做决定，那类数字口径不明，你不知道它算了哪些、又漏了哪些。
  Note: The report excludes college — check UC/CSU websites for California public-university tuition. Count the tax credits from item 1 first, then see the gap. Don't decide based on viral "it costs $X to raise a child" figures — their methodology is opaque; you can't tell what's included or left out.

### 6. 为长辈生、为婚姻生、为养老生，各记一笔账 / Having a child for your parents, for your marriage, or for old-age support — ledger each separately
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱，花的是一次认真的谈话。难在要把话说开，不绕过去。
  Cost: No money — one serious conversation. The hard part is saying it plainly instead of dancing around it.
- 说人话：别人希望你生，这可以算你的一个考虑因素。但时间和钱是记在你自己账上的。「养儿防老」要单独算一笔，因为它赌的是二十年后另一个人的经济能力和意愿。把不生的后果也写下来，两边对照。
  In plain terms: Others wanting you to have a child can be one factor in your thinking. But the time and money go on your ledger. "Children as retirement insurance" deserves its own ledger, because it bets on another person's finances and willingness twenty years out. Write down the consequences of not having one too, and compare both sides.
- 收益：思路和第 10 节一样。别人的期待可以算你的一个考虑因素。但成本要记在你自己的账上，不记在他们账上。「养儿防老」尤其要单独算一笔，因为它假定的是二十年后另一个人的经济能力和意愿。
  Benefit: Same logic as Chapter 10. Others' expectations can be one of your considerations. But the costs go on your ledger, not theirs. "Children as old-age support" especially needs its own ledger, since it assumes another person's economic capacity and willingness two decades out.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验，无直接文献；同类分析见第 10 节
- 备注：把「不生的后果」也写下来，和「生的后果」放在一起对照着看。
  Note: Write down "the consequences of not having one" too, and read them side by side with "the consequences of having one."
