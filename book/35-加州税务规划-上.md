# 35. 加州税务规划 / 35. California Tax Planning

本章只讲**合法节税**（tax planning / tax avoidance）：用税法明文给的扣除、递延、免税工具，把该交的税交到最低。不讲、也绝不教逃税（tax evasion）——那是联邦重罪，见第 40 条。美国税制和中国完全不同：联邦 + 加州两套税、资本利得和工资税率分开算、退休账户种类多但规则细。华人圈流传的很多"技巧"要么是旧数字、要么是把联邦规则错当成加州规则，本章按 2025/2026 税年逐条核对，标清税年和"待核实"。

This chapter covers only **lawful tax planning** (tax avoidance): using deductions, deferrals, and exemptions the tax code explicitly provides to pay the lowest tax you legally owe. It does not teach tax evasion — a federal felony (see item 40). The U.S. system differs completely from China's: federal plus California taxes, separate rates for wages vs. capital gains, many retirement accounts with intricate rules. Much "advice" circulating in Chinese-language circles uses outdated numbers or mistakes federal rules for California rules. Every item below is checked against the 2025/2026 tax year, with the tax year stated and open questions marked "待核实" (to be verified).

**本章不是税务或法律建议**。税法每年都变，加州又有自己的特殊规则（比如不承认 HSA 的州税优惠）。金额大的操作——卖房、S corp 选举、大额 Roth 转换——动手前请咨询 CPA 或税务律师。
**This chapter is not tax or legal advice.** Tax law changes every year, and California has its own quirks (e.g., it does not recognize the HSA state-tax benefit). Before big moves — selling property, electing S-corp status, large Roth conversions — consult a CPA or tax attorney.

### 1. 每年年初校准一次预扣税，别等到 4 月才发现欠税 / Calibrate Your Withholding Early Each Year — Don't Discover You Owe in April
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。用 IRS 的 Tax Withholding Estimator，每年 1 到 2 月花半小时；收入大变（换工作、RSU 大额 vest、有副业）时再调一次 W-4。
  Cost: Nothing. Spend half an hour with the IRS Tax Withholding Estimator each January–February; adjust Form W-4 again whenever income shifts a lot (job change, big RSU vest, side income).
- 说人话：预扣税只是"预付"，多退少补。双职工家庭两边都按单身高预扣算、副业收入完全没预扣、RSU 只按 22% 预扣（见第 10 条），都是 4 月补税几千美元的常见原因。反过来预扣太多，等于全年给政府免息贷款。安全港规则（safe harbor）能免欠税罚款：全年预扣+预缴达到上年总税的 100%（上年 AGI 超 $150,000 则为 110%），或当年总税的 90%，取其小。
  In plain terms: Withholding is just prepayment — overpaid gets refunded, underpaid comes due. Dual-income households where both spouses withhold at the higher single rate, side income with zero withholding, and RSUs withheld at only 22% (see item 10) are the classic reasons for a multi-thousand-dollar April bill. Over-withholding the other way is an interest-free loan to the government all year. The safe-harbor rule avoids underpayment penalties: prepay 100% of last year's total tax (110% if last year's AGI exceeded $150,000), or 90% of this year's — whichever is smaller.
- 收益：把补税和罚款消灭在年初。欠税罚款按季度浮动利率算（待核实当年利率，以 IRS 当季公告为准），看着不大，但纯属白交。
  Benefit: Kill the April surprise and the penalty at the start of the year. The underpayment penalty accrues at a floating quarterly federal rate (current rate to be verified against the IRS quarterly announcement) — small, but entirely avoidable.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS, Tax Withholding Estimator. <https://www.irs.gov/individuals/tax-withholding-estimator>；IRS, Estimated Taxes (safe harbor, IRC §6654). <https://www.irs.gov/faqs/estimated-tax/individuals>
- 备注：安全港 100%/110%/90% 是联邦规则；加州也有州级预缴（FTB Form 540-ES），州欠税同样有罚。季度预缴罚息的当前利率待核实。
  Note: The 100%/110%/90% safe harbor is federal; California has its own estimated-tax prepayments (FTB Form 540-ES) with its own penalties. The current penalty interest rate is to be verified.

### 2. 401(k) 拉满：先拿满雇主匹配，再谈别的 / Max Out Your 401(k): Take the Full Employer Match First, Then Everything Else
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：2026 年员工自缴上限 $24,500（税前 pre-tax 和 Roth 合计，50 岁以下）。先确保拿到雇主的全部匹配（match），再考虑拉满。
  Cost: The 2026 employee contribution limit is $24,500 (pre-tax plus Roth combined, under 50). First make sure you capture the entire employer match, then consider maxing out.
- 说人话：雇主匹配是白给的钱，常见公式是"你缴工资的 5%，公司跟 4%"（前 3% 全跟、接下来 2% 跟一半），或者公司直接给所有合格员工 3%。不缴到拿满线，等于主动降薪。税前 401(k) 的好处是当年少交税：边际税率 32% 的人存 $24,500，当年联邦税少交约 $7,840（加州税另算）。50 岁以上每年可多缴 catch-up：2026 年 $8,000；60–63 岁有"超级" catch-up $11,250（总额，不是叠加）。
  In plain terms: The employer match is free money — a common formula is "you contribute 5% of salary, the company adds 4%" (100% on the first 3%, 50% on the next 2%), or a flat 3% to all eligible employees. Contributing below the match threshold is a voluntary pay cut. Pre-tax 401(k) cuts this year's tax bill: at a 32% marginal rate, $24,500 of contributions saves about $7,840 in federal tax (California separate). Workers 50+ get catch-up contributions: $8,000 for 2026; ages 60–63 get a "super" catch-up of $11,250 (a total, not on top of the standard one).
- 收益：匹配部分是 50%–100% 的即时回报，没有任何投资能稳定做到；税前供款按你的边际税率当年省税。注意雇主匹配部分可能有归属期（vesting，常见 2–3 年 cliff 或最长 6 年 graded），提前离职可能拿不走未归属部分。
  Benefit: The match is an instant 50%–100% return no investment can reliably match; pre-tax contributions save tax this year at your marginal rate. Note the employer-match portion may have a vesting schedule (commonly a 2–3 year cliff or up to 6-year graded) — leave early and the unvested part stays behind.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS, COLA increases for dollar limitations (IRC §415). <https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/cola-increases-for-dollar-limitations-on-benefits-and-contributions>；IRS, Retirement Topics — Catch-Up Contributions. <https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-catch-up-contributions>
- 备注：匹配公式和归属期各计划不同，以你公司的 SPD（计划说明）为准。税前还是 Roth 401(k) 的取舍：看现在 vs 退休时的边际税率，现在税率高选税前，预期退休更高选 Roth，拿不准就两边分摊。
  Note: Match formulas and vesting vary by plan — check your company's SPD. Pre-tax vs. Roth 401(k): compare your marginal rate now vs. in retirement; higher now favors pre-tax, higher later favors Roth; split the difference if unsure.

### 3. HSA 三重避税，但加州不认州税优惠 / The HSA's Triple Tax Benefit — Which California Does Not Recognize
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=中 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：2026 年上限个人 $4,400、家庭 $8,750（55 岁以上每年再加 $1,000）。前提是参加合格的高免赔额健康计划 HDHP（2026 年最低免赔额个人 $1,700/家庭 $3,400）。
  Cost: 2026 limits are $4,400 (self-only) / $8,750 (family), plus $1,000/year at 55+. You must be enrolled in a qualifying high-deductible health plan (HDHP): 2026 minimum deductible $1,700/$3,400.
- 说人话：HSA 是美国税法里罕见的"三重避税"：存入免税（走工资 payroll 扣还能省 FICA 社保医保税）、账户里增值免税、用于合格医疗支出取出来也免税。论坛里有个进阶玩法：年轻时自掏腰包付医疗费、把收据攒着，几十年后一次性报销取现，等于把 HSA 当第二个 Roth 养。但**加州特注**：加州不承认 HSA 的州税优惠——缴款在报加州税时要加回去（不能抵州税），账户增值在加州层面也要缴税。联邦三重、加州只剩"取款用于医疗免税"这一重。
  In plain terms: The HSA is a rare triple tax benefit — contributions are deductible (via payroll they also skip FICA taxes), growth is tax-free, and withdrawals for qualified medical expenses are tax-free. The forum-favorite advanced play: pay medical bills out of pocket while young, save the receipts, and reimburse yourself decades later — effectively a second Roth. But the **California catch**: California does not recognize the HSA tax benefit — contributions must be added back on your California return (no state deduction), and account growth is taxable at the state level. Triple at the federal level, only the medical-withdrawal leg survives in California.
- 收益：联邦层面三重免税；工资直接扣还能省 7.65% 的 FICA。加州居民算账时记得把州税优惠划掉，别按"三重"做计划。
  Benefit: Triple tax-free at the federal level; payroll contributions also skip 7.65% FICA. California residents should cross out the state benefit when doing the math — don't plan on "triple."
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-19 (2026 HSA limits)；IRS Publication 969. <https://www.irs.gov/publications/p969>；FTB 对 HSA 加州处理的说明（加州税表说明）
- 备注：HDHP 的具体免赔额和自付上限每年微调，以当年 IRS Rev. Proc. 为准。攒收据玩法要求收据和 HDHP 参保记录长期保存，丢了就取不出来。
  Note: HDHP deductible and out-of-pocket limits adjust yearly — check the current IRS Revenue Procedure. The save-receipts strategy requires keeping receipts and HDHP enrollment records for decades; lose them and the money is stuck.

### 4. Backdoor Roth：高收入者的 Roth 后门，走之前先把"脏水"倒掉 / The Backdoor Roth for High Earners: Drain the "Dirty Water" First
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=中 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：2026 年 IRA 缴款上限 $7,500（Traditional + Roth 所有 IRA 合计，50 岁以上 $8,600）。需要券商账户操作一到两次，外加每年报 Form 8606。
  Cost: The 2026 IRA contribution limit is $7,500 (all Traditional + Roth IRAs combined; $8,600 at 50+). Takes one or two brokerage moves plus Form 8606 each year.
- 说人话：2026 年单身 MAGI 超过 $153,000（夫妻 $242,000）就不能直接存 Roth IRA，但税法没限制你往 Traditional IRA 里**存**钱（只是高收入不能抵税）。做法：先做一笔 nondeductible（不可抵税）的 Traditional IRA 缴款，紧接着转成 Roth（Roth conversion 自 2010 年起无收入限制）。关键坑是 pro-rata 规则：如果你名下已有税前 Traditional/SEP/SIMPLE IRA 余额，转换会被按"税前占比"征税，后门就漏风了。标准解法是先把税前 IRA 余额 rollover 进现雇主的 401(k)，把池子清干净再走后门。
  In plain terms: In 2026, single filers with MAGI above $153,000 (joint $242,000) can't contribute to a Roth IRA directly — but nothing stops you from **contributing** to a Traditional IRA (you just can't deduct it at high income). The move: make a nondeductible Traditional IRA contribution, then immediately convert to Roth (Roth conversions have had no income limit since 2010). The trap is the pro-rata rule: if you hold any pre-tax Traditional/SEP/SIMPLE IRA balance, the conversion is taxed proportionally and the backdoor leaks. The standard fix: roll pre-tax IRA balances into your current employer's 401(k) first, drain the pool, then walk through.
- 收益：每年 $7,500 进入 Roth，之后几十年增值和取款全免税。对边际税率 30%+ 的加州高收入者，这是少数几个还能用的 Roth 通道。
  Benefit: $7,500/year into Roth, with decades of tax-free growth and withdrawals. For high-bracket Californians, it's one of the few Roth doors still open.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS Publication 590-A（"There is no upper limit on how much you can earn and still contribute"）. <https://www.irs.gov/publications/p590a>；IRS Notice 2025-67 (2026 IRA limits)
- 备注：每年必须报 Form 8606 申报 nondeductible basis，否则 IRS 默认你的转换全额应税。12 月 31 日这个时间点看 IRA 余额，跨年操作注意时点。
  Note: File Form 8606 every year to report the nondeductible basis — otherwise the IRS treats the whole conversion as taxable. Balances are measured on December 31, so mind the timing of year-end moves.

### 5. 2026 年起：上年工资超 $150,000，catch-up 只能走 Roth / From 2026: Catch-Up Contributions Must Be Roth If Last Year's Wages Exceeded $150,000
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不额外花钱。这是 SECURE 2.0（IRC §414(v)(7)）的强制规则：2026 税年起，如果你在该计划 sponsor 处的上年工资超过 $150,000，你的 catch-up 缴款必须走 Roth（税后存、退休免税），不能再走税前。
  Cost: Nothing extra. A mandatory SECURE 2.0 rule (IRC §414(v)(7)): from tax year 2026, if your prior-year wages from the plan sponsor exceeded $150,000, catch-up contributions must go in as Roth (after-tax now, tax-free later) — no more pre-tax catch-ups.
- 说人话：大厂 50 岁以上的员工最先撞上这条：2026 年 $8,000 的 catch-up（60–63 岁 $11,250）将被迫税后存，当年的抵税额少了。但换个角度，这等于强制你做了 Roth 化——退休取款免税，对预期退休税率不低的人不一定是坏事。$150,000 看的是上年在**同一雇主**处的工资，换工作重新算。
  In plain terms: Big-tech workers 50+ hit this first: the 2026 catch-up ($8,000; $11,250 at ages 60–63) must go in after-tax, shrinking this year's deduction. The flip side: it's forced Roth-ification — tax-free withdrawals later, which isn't bad if you expect a meaningful retirement tax rate. The $150,000 looks at prior-year wages **from the same plan sponsor**; changing employers resets the clock.
- 收益：规则是强制的，"收益"是别搞错：2026 年还按税前存 catch-up，计划可能直接退回你的钱。提前知道，就不会在报税时措手不及。
  Benefit: The rule is mandatory — the "benefit" is not getting it wrong: pre-tax catch-ups in 2026 may simply be returned by the plan. Knowing early avoids a tax-season surprise.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS, Retirement Topics — Catch-Up Contributions (SECURE 2.0 §325). <https://www.irs.gov/retirement-plans/plan-participant-employee/retirement-topics-catch-up-contributions>
- 备注：政府计划和集体谈判计划有过渡期安排，细节以 IRS final regulations 为准（待核实）。雇主计划必须有 Roth 功能才执行这条，没有 Roth 选项的计划另当别论。
  Note: Governmental and collectively-bargained plans have transition relief — details per IRS final regulations (to be verified). The plan must offer a Roth feature for this to apply.

### 6. SALT 上限提到 $40,000 后，加州人要重算分项扣除 / With the SALT Cap at $40,000, Californians Should Re-Run Itemized Deductions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。报税时把标准扣除和分项扣除都算一遍，取高的那个。
  Cost: Nothing. At tax time, compute both the standard deduction and itemized deductions, and take whichever is higher.
- 说人话：2018–2024 年 SALT（州和地方税）抵扣上限只有 $10,000，加州人交着全美最高的州税，大量中产只能吃标准扣除。大而美法案（One Big Beautiful Bill, 2025-07-04 签署）把 2025 税年的 SALT 上限提到 **$40,000**（单身分开申报 $20,000），2026 年 $40,400，之后每年 +1%，**2030 年回落到 $10,000**。MAGI 超 $500,000 开始按超出部分的 30% 削减（下限 $10,000）。对加州人的意义：州税 + 房产税 + 房贷利息（本金 $750,000 以内，2017-12-15 后购房）+ 慈善，加起来重新可能超过标准扣除（2026 年单身 $16,100/夫妻 $32,200），itemize 值得再算一次。
  In plain terms: From 2018–2024 the SALT deduction was capped at $10,000, so many Californians paying the nation's highest state taxes could only take the standard deduction. The One Big Beautiful Bill (signed 2025-07-04) raised the cap to **$40,000** for 2025 (married filing separately $20,000), $40,400 for 2026, +1% yearly after, **falling back to $10,000 in 2030**. Above $500,000 MAGI it phases down at 30% of the excess (floor $10,000). ### 13. NSO 行权：行权价和市价的差额当年按工资交税 / NSO Exercise: The Spread Is Taxed as Wages That Year

### 14. ISO 行权当年可能触发 AMT：没卖股票也要交现金税 / ISO Exercise Can Trigger AMT: Cash Tax Owed Even If You Sell Nothing
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：ISO（incentive stock option）行权时常规税不征税，但 bargain element 会被加进 AMT（alternative minimum tax，平行税制）的计税基数，可能产生一笔**现金税单**——即使你没卖一股。
  Cost: ISO exercise is tax-free under the regular system, but the bargain element is added to your AMT (alternative minimum tax) base and can produce a **cash tax bill** — even if you sell nothing.
- 说人话：ISO 的优惠是有条件的：行权后持有超 1 年**且**授权后超 2 年再卖（qualifying disposition），全部收益按长期资本利得；任一条件不满足，差额打回普通收入。真正的坑是 AMT：行权当年，bargain element 计入 AMT 收入，可能让你"理论上赚了、实际上要掏现金交税"，之后股价跌了税也不退。2026 年 AMT 更容易触发：免税额单身 $90,100/夫妻 $140,200，但 phaseout 阈值大降到单身 $500,000/夫妻 $1,000,000，削减速率从 25% 翻倍到 50%。每年按 ISO 行权的股票按授权日公允价值有 $100,000 上限，超额自动变 NSO。
  In plain terms: The ISO benefit is conditional: hold more than 1 year after exercise **and** more than 2 years after grant before selling (qualifying disposition), and all gains get long-term capital-gains rates; miss either and the spread reverts to ordinary income. The real trap is AMT: in the exercise year the bargain element counts as AMT income, so you can owe cash tax on "gains" you haven't realized — and a later price drop doesn't refund it. AMT bites more people in 2026: exemptions of $90,100 single / $140,200 joint, but phaseout thresholds slashed to $500,000 single / $1,000,000 joint with the phaseout rate doubled from 25% to 50%. Annual ISO exercises are capped at $100,000 of grant-date fair value; excess becomes NSO automatically.
- 收益：满足 holding period 的 ISO 是税率最低的股权收益（长期资本利得）。但行权前必须做 AMT 测算，大额行权分多年做。
  Benefit: A qualifying ISO disposition gets the lowest rate on equity gains (long-term capital gains). But always model the AMT before exercising; spread big exercises across years.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS Publication 525（Incentive Stock Options）；IRC §422；IRS Rev. Proc. 2024-40 §2.11 (AMT)

  Note: 2026 AMT exemption and phaseout figures await the final IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-31 (to be verified). Excess AMT may come back partly via the AMT credit in later years — cold comfort for this year's cash flow.

### 15. ESPP 八五折：买入不交税，卖出时看是否满足持有期 / ESPP's 15% Discount: No Tax at Purchase; the Sale Depends on the Holding Period
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=中 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：用工资的 1%–15%（各计划不同）参与，通常每半年买一次；每人每年购买上限 $25,000（按 grant 日价值计）。
  Cost: Contribute 1%–15% of salary (varies by plan), usually purchasing twice a year; $25,000/year purchase cap per person (valued at the grant date).
- 说人话：合格 ESPP（IRC §423）一般按**市价八五折**买，买入时不产生收入。卖出分两种：**qualifying disposition**（grant 日起满 2 年且 purchase 日起满 1 年后卖）：普通收入部分 = min（折扣额，卖出价 − 购买价），超出部分按长期资本利得——折扣里只有一小块按工资税。**disqualifying disposition**（提前卖）：购买日市价 − 购买价（整个折扣）全部按普通收入（通常进 W-2），购买日之后到卖出日的涨跌再按资本利得算。八五折买入本身几乎是白捡 17.6%，别因为怕麻烦不参加。
  In plain terms: A qualified ESPP (IRC §423) typically buys at **15% off** market; no income at purchase. Two sale outcomes: a **qualifying disposition** (sell at least 2 years after grant AND 1 year after purchase): ordinary income = the smaller of (discount, sale price − purchase price), the rest long-term capital gains — only a slice of the discount is wage-taxed. A **disqualifying disposition** (sell early): the whole discount (market price on purchase day minus purchase price) is ordinary income (usually on the W-2); movement after purchase day is capital gain/loss. The 15% discount is nearly a free 17.6% — don't skip it out of inertia.
- 收益：八五折是确定性的折扣收益；满足持有期，折扣的大部分按资本利得税率走。券商 1099-B 的 basis 同样可能不准，卖出时自己核对（见第 11 条）。
  Benefit: The 15% discount is a near-certain return; with the holding period, most of it gets capital-gains rates. The broker's 1099-B basis can be wrong here too — verify at sale (item 11).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRC §423；IRS Publication 525/550
- 备注：不是所有公司的 ESPP 都是 qualified plan，非合格计划的税务处理不同，看计划文件。单只股票仓位别太重，见第 16 条的分散思路。
  Note: Not every ESPP is a qualified plan — nonqualified plans are taxed differently; check plan documents. Don't let one stock dominate your portfolio; see item 16's diversification logic.

### 16. Tax-loss harvesting：每年用亏损抵 $3,000 工资收入，小心 wash sale / Tax-Loss Harvesting: Offset $3,000 of Wages Yearly — Mind the Wash Sale
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。需要一个应税券商账户，以及每年花一两个小时检查持仓。
  Cost: Nothing. Needs a taxable brokerage account and an hour or two a year reviewing positions.
- 说人话：卖出亏钱的仓位：亏损先 1:1 抵资本利得，剩下的每年可抵 **$3,000** 普通收入（工资），用不完无限结转以后年度。红线是 **wash sale**：卖出前后 30 天内（含卖出前 30 天、后 30 天），如果你或**配偶**、或你的 IRA/401(k) 买回"实质相同"的证券，亏损作废。主流做法是用"相似但不相同"的基金对做 harvesting 搭档（如 VTI ↔ VOO/SCHB），保持仓位暴露不变。注意：加密货币**目前**不受 wash sale 约束（现行规则，国会多次提案要堵，未来可能变）。
  In plain terms: Sell losers: losses first offset capital gains 1:1, then up to **$3,000**/year offsets ordinary income (wages), with unused losses carried forward indefinitely. The red line is the **wash sale** rule: if you, your **spouse**, or your IRA/401(k) repurchase a "substantially identical" security within 30 days before or after the sale, the loss is disallowed. The standard play uses "similar but not identical" fund pairs (e.g., VTI ↔ VOO/SCHB) to keep market exposure. Note: crypto is **currently** exempt from wash-sale rules (current rule; Congress has repeatedly proposed closing this — may change).
- 收益：每年 $3,000 抵工资，按 30%+ 边际税率省约 $1,000+/年；大额亏损结转以后抵大额利得。自动再投资的分红（dividend reinvestment）也算"买入"，会悄悄触发 wash sale，记得关掉或计入 30 天窗口。
  Benefit: $3,000/year against wages saves ~$1,000+/year at 30%+ rates; big losses carry forward against big future gains. Dividend reinvestment counts as a "purchase" and can silently trigger a wash sale — turn it off or count it in the 30-day window.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS Publication 550（Wash Sales）；Bogleheads, Tax Loss Harvesting. <https://www.bogleheads.org/wiki/Tax_loss_harvesting>
- 备注：wash sale 只管亏损，不管盈利（见第 17 条）。$3,000 上限几十年没随通胀调整过。
  Note: Wash sales only police losses, not gains (item 17). The $3,000 cap hasn't been inflation-adjusted in decades.

### 17. 低收入年份主动实现利得：0% 资本利得档没有 wash sale 限制 / Realize Gains on Purpose in Low-Income Years: The 0% Bracket Has No Wash-Sale Limit
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。操作是卖出盈利仓位、当天买回（抬高成本基础），适合 gap year、读书、提前退休到 RMD 之间的低收入空窗。
  Cost: Nothing. Sell winners and immediately rebuy (stepping up basis) — ideal for gap years, school years, or the low-income window between early retirement and RMDs.
- 说人话：长期资本利得（持有超 1 年）联邦税率 0%/15%/20% 按应税收入划分：2026 年单身应税收入 $49,450 以下、夫妻 $98,900 以下的部分，长期利得税率是 **0%**。低收入年份主动卖出再买回，把成本基础抬高，之后涨得再多，之前这段增值的税永远免了——这叫 tax-gain harvesting。关键区别：wash sale 只限制亏损收割，**盈利收割没有 30 天限制**，当天卖当天买完全合法。Roth conversion ladder（低收入年把税前 401(k)/IRA 转 Roth）是同一思路。
  In plain terms: Long-term gains (held over 1 year) are taxed federally at 0%/15%/20% by taxable income: in 2026, the 0% rate covers gains stacking within $49,450 of taxable income single / $98,900 joint. In a low-income year, deliberately sell and rebuy to step up your basis — the appreciation to date becomes permanently tax-free. This is tax-gain harvesting. The key asymmetry: wash sales only restrict **loss** harvesting; **gain** harvesting has no 30-day limit — selling and rebuying the same day is fully legal. The Roth conversion ladder (converting pre-tax 401(k)/IRA to Roth in low-income years) is the same idea.
- 收益：把未来要按 15%/20% 交税的增值，提前按 0% 锁定。FIRE 圈的经典操作：退休 early 到 72 岁 RMD 之间的空窗年，年年转一点 Roth。
  Benefit: Locks in at 0% appreciation that would otherwise be taxed at 15%/20% later. The FIRE classic: convert a slice to Roth every year in the gap between early retirement and RMDs at 72.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-31（2026 资本利得 0% 线）；IRS Publication 550
- 备注：0% 看的是**应税收入**（AGI 减去扣除），不是工资总额；加州对资本利得没有 0% 优惠（见第 32 条），这招只省联邦税。操作前先测算，别把自己推过 0% 线。
  Note: The 0% threshold keys off **taxable income** (AGI minus deductions), not gross wages; California offers no 0% capital-gains break (item 32) — this saves federal tax only. Model first so you don't overshoot the 0% line.

### 18. 自雇税 15.3%：挣的每一美元先被咬一口，一半可抵所得税 / The 15.3% Self-Employment Tax: Every Dollar Gets Bitten; Half Is Deductible
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：净自雇收入的 **92.35%** 按 15.3% 交（社保 12.4% + 医保 2.9%）；净自雇收入满 $400 就要申报（Schedule SE）。2026 年社保部分只征到 $184,500（wage base），医保 2.9% 无上限；工资+自雇收入合计超单身 $200,000/夫妻 $250,000 的部分再加 0.9% 附加医保税。
  Cost: 15.3% on **92.35%** of net self-employment earnings (12.4% Social Security + 2.9% Medicare); filing required once net earnings hit $400 (Schedule SE). In 2026 Social Security applies up to the $184,500 wage base; the 2.9% Medicare has no cap; wages plus self-employment income above $200,000 single / $250,000 joint attract another 0.9% additional Medicare tax.
- 说人话：W-2 员工的 7.65% FICA 是雇主出一半，自雇是两半都自己出，所以 15.3%。好消息是**一半的自雇税可以做 above-the-line 抵扣**（调减 AGI，在 Schedule 1 上填），相当于所得税打了个折——但注意，它**不减少**自雇税本身的计算基数。副业收入也是自雇收入，别以为"主业有 W-2 预扣了副业就不用管"。
  In plain terms: W-2 workers split the 7.65% FICA with their employer; the self-employed pay both halves — hence 15.3%. The consolation: **half of the SE tax is an above-the-line deduction** (reducing AGI, on Schedule 1) — a discount on income tax — but it does **not** shrink the SE-tax base itself. Side-gig income is self-employment income too; a W-2 day job doesn't cover it.
- 收益：理解这 15.3%，是后面 S corp 选举（第 20 条）和 Solo 401(k)（第 21 条）的基础。一半抵扣在 30%+ 边际税率下，相当于自雇税实际负担打约 85 折。
  Benefit: Grasping the 15.3% is the foundation for the S-corp election (item 20) and Solo 401(k) (item 21). At a 30%+ marginal rate, the half-deduction cuts the effective SE-tax burden by roughly 15%.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS, Self-Employment Tax. <https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/self-employment-tax-social-security-and-medicare-taxes>；SSA (2025-10-24 公布 2026 wage base)
- 备注：0.9% 附加税的 $200,000/$250,000 门槛是法定固定值，从不随通胀调整，越来越多人会被动撞上。
  Note: The $200,000/$250,000 thresholds for the 0.9% additional tax are fixed by statute and never inflation-adjusted — more people drift into them every year.

### 19. 季度预缴四个截止日：4/15、6/15、9/15、次年 1/15，欠交有罚 / Quarterly Estimated Taxes: 4/15, 6/15, 9/15, 1/15 — Penalties for Skipping

- 成本：自雇、副业、房租、大额资本利得——凡是**没被预扣**的收入，都要自己按季预缴。四个截止日：Q1→**4/15**，Q2→**6/15**，Q3→**9/15**，Q4→**次年 1/15**（遇节假日顺延）。触发线：预计当年欠税（扣掉预扣和可退 credit 后）≥ **$1,000**。

Deadlines: Q1→**4/15**, Q2→**6/15**, Q3→**9/15**, Q4→**1/15** of the next year (shifted for holidays). Trigger: expected balance due (after withholding and refundable credits) ≥ **$1,000**.
- 说人话：英文论坛最高频的新手帖："我年终退税了为什么还被罚？"——因为罚的是**按季不足**（Form 2210），不是年底总数。哪怕 4 月算下来你还倒拿退税，之前某季度欠交照样罚。最省心的办法是走安全港（第 1 条）：预缴到上年总税的 100%/110%，数字已知，一次算清。注意加州有**自己**的州级预缴（FTB Form 540-ES），联邦和州两套都要交。
  In plain terms: The most-posted rookie question on English forums: "I got a refund — why was I penalized?" Because the penalty hits **quarterly shortfalls** (Form 2210), not the year-end total. Even with an April refund, a missed quarter still draws a penalty. The lazy-safe route is the safe harbor (item 1): prepay 100%/110% of last year's total tax — a known number, computed once. - 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS, Estimated Tax FAQs；IRS Publication 505；Form 1040-ES. <https://www.irs.gov/faqs/estimated-tax/individuals>
- 备注：W-2 雇员的预扣被视为全年均匀缴纳，所以调高 W-4 预扣可以"事后补救"某一季度的不足——这是年底常用的补救技巧。
  Note: W-2 withholding is treated as paid evenly all year, so raising W-4 withholding can retroactively "cure" a missed quarter — a common year-end rescue trick.

### 20. S Corp 选举：净利润 $50k 以上才值得，工资必须"合理" / The S-Corp Election: Worth It Above ~$50k Profit — and the Salary Must Be "Reasonable"
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=中 时间=多 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：S corp 本身是穿透征税（不交联邦所得税，利润穿 K-1 到个人税表），但合规成本每年约 $1,000–$3,000：发工资跑 payroll、报 1120-S、做账。论坛共识的盈亏平衡点约在**净利润 $50,000–$60,000 以上**。
  Cost: An S-corp is a pass-through (no federal corporate tax; profits flow via K-1 to your personal return), but compliance runs ~$1,000–$3,000/year: running payroll, filing 1120-S, bookkeeping. Forum consensus puts the breakeven around **$50,000–$60,000+** of net profit.
- 说人话：省税机制只有一条：在公司干活的股东必须先拿 **"合理工资"（reasonable compensation）**——走 W-2，交 payroll 税；工资之外的剩余利润以分红（distribution）拿，**不交 15.3% 的自雇税/payroll 税**（所得税还是要交）。省的就是"利润 − 工资"这部分上的 15.3%。红线也在"合理"二字：没有固定公式，看职责、工时、同业可比薪酬；工资定得离谱低（比如年利润 $200k 只发 $20k 工资），IRS 可以把分红**重分类为工资**，追补 payroll 税 + 利息 + 罚款（Rev. Rul. 74-44；Watson 案）。
  In plain terms: One savings mechanism: a working shareholder must first take **"reasonable compensation"** — W-2 wages with payroll tax; remaining profit comes out as distributions **exempt from the 15.3% SE/payroll tax** (income tax still applies). You save 15.3% on (profit − salary). The red line is "reasonable": no fixed formula — duties, hours, comparable salaries decide. Absurdly low wages (e.g., $20k salary on $200k profit) let the IRS **recharacterize** distributions as wages, back-billing payroll tax plus interest and penalties (Rev. Rul. 74-44; the Watson case).
- 收益：例子：净利润 $100k，发 $60k 合理工资，剩下 $40k 分红省 15.3% ≈ $6,120/年，减去 $2k 合规成本，净省 $4k+。利润越高越划算。
  Benefit: Example: $100k profit, $60k reasonable salary — the $40k distribution saves ~$6,120/year in SE tax; minus ~$2k compliance, net ~$4k+. The higher the profit, the better the math.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：IRS, S Corporation Compensation and Medical Insurance Issues. <https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/s-corporation-compensation-and-medical-insurance-issues>
- 备注：定成 B 级是因为"净利润 $50k 盈亏平衡"是论坛共识和行业经验，不是条文；合理工资的判定是个案事实问题。加州 S corp 还有 1.5% 的州净收入税（最低 $800），算账时别漏。
  Note: Graded B because the ~$50k breakeven is forum/industry consensus, not statute, and reasonable compensation is fact-specific. California S-corps also owe a 1.5% state net-income tax (minimum $800) — include it in the math.

<!-- 成本标签: 钱=多 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->

Solo 401(k) money lives in the 401(k) system, keeps the backdoor clean, and can even host a mega backdoor (item 12, if the plan supports it). Forum consensus: if you can open a Solo 401(k), skip the SEP IRA.

### 22. 家庭办公室扣除：简易法 $5/平方英尺，最多 $1,500 / Home-Office Deduction: $5/Sq Ft Simplified, Max $1,500

  Benefit: The simplified method saves at most $1,500 × your marginal rate — a few hundred dollars; the actual method can win in a big Bay Area rental, but factor in depreciation recapture.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS, Simplified Option for Home Office Deduction. <https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/simplified-option-for-home-office-deduction>
- 备注：简易法超额不可结转，两种方法每年可以换。用实际法且房子是自己的，卖房时折旧 recapture 按 25%（见第 30 条）。
  Note: Simplified-method excess doesn't carry forward; you may switch methods each year. With the actual method on a home you own, depreciation is recaptured at 25% on sale (item 30).

### 23. 加州 PTE 穿透实体税：实体层面交 9.3%，绕开联邦 SALT 上限 / California's Passthrough Entity Tax: Pay 9.3% at the Entity Level, Around the Federal SALT Cap
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=中 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：加州 AB 150（2021）允许 S corp、LLC、合伙企业**自愿选择**在实体层面按 **9.3%** 交加州所得税。2025 税年起联邦 SALT 上限已提到 $40,000（第 6 条），这条的相对价值下降，但对高收入企业主仍有用。
  Cost: California AB 150 (2021) lets S-corps, LLCs, and partnerships **elect** to pay California income tax at the **entity level** at **9.3%**. With the federal SALT cap now $40,000 from 2025 (item 6), this matters less than before — but still helps high-income owners.
- 说人话：原理是"换个口袋交税"：实体交的 9.3% 在**联邦层面算企业费用**抵扣（不受 SALT 上限限制），股东个人再拿加州的等额抵免（credit），避免双重征税。SALT 上限 $10,000 年代，这是加州企业主最重要的 workaround；现在上限 $40,000，只有加州税远超 $40,000 的高收入股东才继续受益。实操：每年按时缴（注意 6 月 15 日前的预缴要求），报 Form 3804/3893；立法已延续到 **2030 纳税年度**。
  In plain terms: It's "paying from a different pocket": the 9.3% paid at the entity level is a **federal business-expense deduction** (outside the SALT cap), and owners get an offsetting California credit — no double tax. In the $10,000 SALT-cap era this was California owners' biggest workaround; with the $40,000 cap, only owners whose California tax far exceeds $40,000 still benefit. Mechanics: pay on time each year (mind the prepayment due before June 15), file Form 3804/3893; the law now runs through **tax year 2030**.
- 收益：适用人群每年省的是（加州税 − $40,000）× 联邦边际税率的部分。加州税 $80,000、联邦 35% 的人，一年多省约 $14,000。
  Benefit: For those it fits, the annual saving is roughly (California tax − $40,000) × your federal marginal rate. At $80,000 of California tax and 35% federal, that's ~$14,000/year.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：California AB 150；FTB Form 3804 说明；LegalClarity 加州 PTE 指南. <https://legalclarity.org/californias-passthrough-entity-tax-a-guide-to-form-3804/>
- 备注：定成 B 级是因为网上有"2026 年到期"的旧说法，延续到 2030 年的立法建议写书时再复核一次。选举是按年做的，每年都要重新选。
  Note: Graded B because outdated "expires 2026" claims still circulate — re-verify the extension to 2030 when finalizing. The election is annual; you must re-elect each year.

### 24. "1099-K 超 $600 就申报"是误传：门槛已恢复为 $20,000 且 200 笔 / The "$600 1099-K" Myth: The Threshold Is Back to $20,000 and 200 Transactions
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。知道正确门槛，别被中文圈 2026 年的旧视频吓到。
  Cost: Nothing. Know the right threshold; don't be spooked by outdated 2026 Chinese-language videos.
- 说人话：事情经过：IRS 原计划把第三方支付（Venmo、PayPal、eBay 等）的 1099-K 门槛从"年超 $20,000 **且**超 200 笔"降到 $600，中文 YouTube 很多 2026 年视频还在按 $600 讲。但大而美法案**追溯恢复**了旧门槛：2025 税年起，只有年超 **$20,000 且**超 200 笔才发 1099-K（IRS 官方 FAQ 2025-10-23 更新）。另外 2026 纳税年度起，1099-NEC（劳务报酬）的门槛从 $600 提到 **$2,000**。但 IRS 的原话同样成立："没收到 1099-K 不代表收入免税"——Zelle/Venmo 的商业收款记录平台都有，代购、副业的现金收入一样要报（见第 42 条）。
  In plain terms: The backstory: the IRS planned to cut the third-party payment (Venmo, PayPal, eBay) 1099-K threshold from "over $20,000 **and** 200 transactions" to $600, and many 2026 Chinese-language videos still teach $600. But the One Big Beautiful Bill **retroactively restored** the old threshold: from tax year 2025, 1099-Ks go out only above **$20,000 and** 200 transactions (IRS FAQ updated 2025-10-23). Separately, from tax year 2026 the 1099-NEC (contractor pay) threshold rises from $600 to **$2,000**. The IRS's other sentence stands too: "no 1099-K doesn't mean the income is tax-free" — platforms keep records of Venmo/Zelle business receipts; reselling and gig cash is still reportable (item 42).
- 收益：别为收不到的表焦虑，也别拿"没收到表"当不报税的借口。副业记账用独立账户，省得和私人转账混在一起说不清。
  Benefit: Don't stress over forms you'll never get — and don't use "no form" as an excuse not to report. Run side income through a separate account so it doesn't tangle with personal transfers.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：IRS, Form 1099-K FAQs（中文）. <https://www.irs.gov/zh-hant/newsroom/form-1099-k-faqs-what-to-do-if-you-receive-a-form-1099-k>；Public Law 119-21
- 备注：$20,000 **且** 200 笔是"且"的关系，两个条件同时满足才发。个人之间 AA 转账、还钱不算商业收款。
  Note: It's $20,000 **and** 200 transactions — both conditions. Splitting bills and personal repayments aren't business receipts.

### 25. LLC 本身不省一分税；加州单人 LLC 每年还有 $800 最低税 / An LLC Saves Zero Tax by Itself; California Charges Every LLC $800/Year Minimum
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=中 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：加州 LLC 每年 **$800** 最低特许税（franchise tax），第一年也不免（2021–2023 年曾免首年，已过期）；总收入超 $250,000 还有按档加收的 LLC fee。
  Cost: A California LLC owes **$800**/year minimum franchise tax (the 2021–2023 first-year exemption has expired); gross receipts over $250,000 trigger additional tiered LLC fees.
- 说人话：多方共识：LLC 默认是**穿透征税**（单人 LLC 在税上就是个体户），"注册个 LLC"本身不省一分税，省税靠的是之后再做 S corp 选举（第 20 条）。中文圈"开了 LLC 就能抵税"的说法，错把法律实体的有限责任当成了税务优惠。反过来，加州的 $800 是**注册了就交**，哪怕当年零收入、零开业——很多人注册了 LLC 放着不用，每年白交 $800，关掉还要办解散手续。
  In plain terms: Forum consensus — an LLC defaults to **pass-through taxation** (a single-member LLC is a sole proprietorship for tax purposes). "Forming an LLC" saves zero tax by itself; savings come from a later S-corp election (item 20). The Chinese-language claim that "an LLC lets you deduct everything" mistakes limited liability for a tax benefit. Worse, California's $800 is due **for existing at all** — even with zero revenue. Many people form LLCs, never use them, and pay $800/year for nothing; dissolving takes formal paperwork.
- 收益：别为省税去注册 LLC；真有业务再注册，注册了就用起来，不用就正式解散止损。
  Benefit: Don't form an LLC to save tax; form one when there's a real business, use it, or formally dissolve it to stop the bleed.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：FTB, LLC Tax Information. <https://www.ftb.ca.gov/file/business/types/limited-liability-company/>；IRS, LLC filing as corporation or partnership
- 备注：LLC 的价值是有限责任隔离和商业形象，不是税务。需要税务穿透 + 有限责任之外的东西，找 CPA 按你的业务类型设计。
  Note: An LLC's value is liability separation and business credibility, not taxes. For anything beyond that, have a CPA design around your business type.
