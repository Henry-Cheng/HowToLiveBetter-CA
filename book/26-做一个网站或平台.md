# 26. 做一个网站或平台 / Building a Website or Platform

口径：金钱。/ Scope: Money.

本节口径是金钱，换回的是「别被罚」和「别被告」。在美国做网站或平台，没有"许可证"这一说（收费网站不用办证，见第 2 条），但有几条硬约束：用户掏钱的链路不能经你的手再转（见第 1 条），用户内容要配 DMCA 流程（见第 9 条），加州居民的个人信息要按 CCPA/CPRA 伺候（见第 8 条和第 11 章第 15 条），营销邮件和短信要守 CAN-SPAM 和 TCPA（见第 12、13 条）。本节 13 条，按"先保命、再合规、最后选型"的顺序排，最后两条是 2026 年事实核对时新增。

This section is about money — what buys you "don't get fined" and "don't get sued." In the U.S., there's no "license" for running a website or platform (a paid website needs no permit — see item 2), but several hard constraints apply: money from users must not flow through your hands first (see item 1), user content needs a DMCA process (see item 9), Californians' personal information must be handled per the CCPA/CPRA (see item 8 and Chapter 11, item 15), and marketing emails and texts must comply with CAN-SPAM and the TCPA (see items 12 and 13). These 13 entries are ordered "survive first, comply second, choose infrastructure last" — the last two were added in the October 2026 fact-check.

### 1. 平台不要自己收钱再转给卖家：收款走持牌支付机构，资金不进你账户 / Don't Collect Buyers' Money and Forward It to Sellers Yourself: Route Payments Through a Licensed Processor — Keep Funds Out of Your Account
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：Stripe Connect、PayPal Payouts 这类分账方案，手续费每笔 2%–3% 加几毛钱。花几小时接支付
  Cost: Split-payout solutions like Stripe Connect or PayPal Payouts charge 2%–3% plus a few dozen cents per transaction. A few hours to integrate.
- 收益：自己收买家的钱、再转给卖家，在加州叫"货币传输"（money transmission），要向加州金融保护与创新局（DFPI）拿牌照（Money Transmission Act, Financial Code §§2003、2030），无证经营是重罪（felony）；联邦层面，无牌照货币传输（18 U.S.C. §1960）最高 5 年，还要在 FinCEN 注册为货币服务商（MSB）。正确姿势：用持牌支付机构的分账能力（Stripe Connect、Adyen、PayPal），资金从买家直接到卖家，不进平台自己的账户。平台只收自己的佣金和服务费。
  Benefit: Collecting buyers' money and forwarding it to sellers is "money transmission" in California, requiring a DFPI (Department of Financial Protection and Innovation) license (Money Transmission Act, Fin. Code §§2003, 2030) — operating without one is a felony; federally, unlicensed money transmission (18 U.S.C. §1960) carries up to 5 years, plus FinCEN registration as a money services business (MSB). The right posture: use a licensed processor's split-payout capability (Stripe Connect, Adyen, PayPal) so funds flow from buyer to seller without touching the platform's own accounts. The platform takes only its commission and service fees.
- 说人话：平台自己收钱再转给卖家，无证就是重罪（加州）加联邦最高 5 年。用 Stripe Connect 这类分账：钱不进你账户，你只收佣金。
  In plain terms: A platform collecting money and forwarding it to sellers is a felony unlicensed (California) plus up to 5 years federally. Use split-payout tools like Stripe Connect: funds never touch your account; you take only commission.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Cal. Fin. Code §2030 (money transmission license required; unlicensed activity prohibited). <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?sectionNum=2030.&lawCode=FIN>; 18 U.S.C. §1960 (unlicensed money transmitting business, up to 5 years). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/1960>; FinCEN. Money Services Business (MSB) registration. <https://www.fincen.gov/money-services-business-msb-registration>

### 5. 让用户上来卖东西，平台就要核验卖家身份：INFORM 法案的高销量卖家门槛 / Letting Users Sell: Verify Your Sellers — The INFORM Act's High-Volume Seller Threshold
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：卖家身份核验（KYC）服务每月几十到几百美元，加一次性几小时的工程接入。难在每年重新认证、信息更新，运营上要有人盯
  Cost: Seller identity-verification (KYC) services run tens to hundreds of dollars a month, plus a few hours of one-time engineering integration. The hard part is operational: annual re-certification and information updates need someone watching.
- 收益：联邦 INFORM Consumers Act（2023 年 6 月 27 日生效）：过去 24 个月里有连续 12 个月在平台上完成 200 笔以上交易、且流水 5000 美元以上的第三方卖家，算"高销量卖家"，平台必须收集并核验银行账户、政府签发的身份证件、税号、联系方式，每年重新认证一次，不合规的暂停其销售；年流水 2 万美元以上的卖家，平台还要向消费者披露其联系方式；平台还要设可疑行为举报机制。卖假货的（见第 12 章第 20 条），平台配合执法、提供卖家信息，是免责和减责的关键。
  Benefit: The federal INFORM Consumers Act (effective June 27, 2023): third-party sellers with 200+ transactions and $5,000+ in revenue over 12 continuous months within the prior 24 count as "high-volume sellers" — the platform must collect and verify bank accounts, government-issued ID, tax IDs, and contact information, re-certify annually, and suspend non-compliant sellers; sellers grossing $20,000+ a year must have their contact information disclosed to consumers; platforms must also provide a suspicious-activity reporting mechanism. For counterfeit sellers (see Chapter 12, item 20), cooperating with enforcement and turning over seller information is key to avoiding or reducing liability.
- 说人话：平台上有卖家年卖 200 单以上、流水 5000 美元以上，你必须核验他的银行账户、身份证件、税号，每年复核一次，不合规则暂停。年销 2 万美元以上的，向买家公开联系方式。
  In plain terms: Sellers on your platform doing 200+ orders and $5,000+ a year must have their bank account, ID, and tax number verified by you, re-checked yearly — suspend them if they don't comply. Sellers grossing $20,000+ get their contact info disclosed to buyers.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：INFORM Consumers Act, Pub. L. 117-354 (15 U.S.C. §45f: high-volume third-party sellers — 200+ transactions and $5,000+ revenue; verification, annual certification, suspension). <https://www.congress.gov/117/plaws/publ354/PLAW-117publ354.htm>; FTC. INFORM Consumers Act compliance guidance. <https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-inform-consumers-act>

### 6. 用户发的内容，平台原则上不背锅，但 DMCA 通知来了必须处理 / User Content: The Platform Generally Isn't Liable — But DMCA Notices Must Be Handled
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：DMCA agent 在 copyright.gov 注册 6 美元（每 3 年续展）；配一套通知-删除流程，工程上几小时到几天。难在持续有人盯通知邮箱
  Cost: $6 to register a DMCA agent at copyright.gov (renew every 3 years); a notice-and-takedown workflow takes hours to days of engineering. The hard part is having someone continuously watch the notices inbox.
- 收益：Section 230（47 U.S.C. §230(c)(1)）：平台不被视为用户发布内容的"发布者"，用户发的侵权、诽谤内容，原则上告不到平台头上；(c)(2) 还保护平台善意删除不良内容的决定。美国没有"平台必须主动审查"的法定义务。但版权是例外：DMCA §512 的"避风港"要求平台做到三条——指定代理人并在 copyright.gov 注册公示、收到合格的侵权通知后迅速删除、有"重复侵权者"政策（repeat infringer policy）并执行；做不到就失去避风港，变成共同侵权。另外一条硬义务：发现疑似儿童性虐待内容（CSAM），电子服务商必须向 NCMEC（国家失踪与受虐儿童中心）报告（18 U.S.C. §2258A）。
  Benefit: Section 230 (47 U.S.C. §230(c)(1)): platforms aren't treated as the "publisher" of user-posted content — infringing or defamatory user posts generally can't be pinned on the platform; (c)(2) further protects good-faith removal decisions. The U.S. imposes no general duty to proactively moderate. But copyright is the exception: the DMCA §512 safe harbor requires three things — a designated agent registered and listed at copyright.gov, expeditious removal upon receiving a valid infringement notice, and a repeat-infringer policy that's actually enforced; fail any and you lose the harbor and face contributory liability. One more hard duty: electronic service providers must report apparent child sexual abuse material (CSAM) to NCMEC (the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children) (18 U.S.C. §2258A).
- 说人话：用户发的东西，平台原则上不背锅（Section 230），也没有义务主动审查。但版权通知来了必须迅速删除，删慢了就失去避风港；发现儿童性虐待内容必须向 NCMEC 报告。
  In plain terms: User posts are generally not the platform's liability (Section 230), and there's no duty to proactively review. But copyright notices must trigger swift removal — delay loses the safe harbor; and apparent child sexual abuse material must be reported to NCMEC.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：47 U.S.C. §230(c)(1)–(2) (platform not treated as publisher; Good Samaritan protection for moderation). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/47/230>; 17 U.S.C. §512(c), (i) (safe harbor: designated agent, expeditious takedown, repeat-infringer policy). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/512>; 18 U.S.C. §2258A (duty to report apparent CSAM to NCMEC). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/18/2258A>; U.S. Copyright Office. DMCA Designated Agent Directory ($6 registration, 3-year renewal). <https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/>

### 8. 13 岁以下用户要过 COPPA：先拿家长同意再收集信息 / Users Under 13 Trigger COPPA: Get Parental Consent Before Collecting Anything
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：年龄门（age gate）工程几小时；可验证的家长同意（verifiable parental consent）流程，第三方服务每月几十到几百美元。难在产品设计上要把 13 岁以下用户的数据流单独处理
  Cost: An age gate takes hours of engineering; verifiable parental consent flows run tens to hundreds of dollars a month via third-party services. The hard part is designing a separate data flow for under-13 users.
- 收益：COPPA（Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, 15 U.S.C. §§6501–6506; 16 CFR Part 312）：网站或在线服务"针对"13 岁以下儿童，或者"实际知道"用户不到 13 岁，收集个人信息前必须拿到可验证的家长同意，还要给家长看收集了什么、怎么用，并提供删除渠道。FTC 按每次违规累计罚款，每次最高 53,088 美元（2025 年通胀调整数，2026 年仍有效）；Epic Games 2022 年为《堡垒之夜》儿童隐私问题赔了 2.75 亿美元。常见做法：13 岁以下不让注册（13+ 年龄门），或者单独做儿童版走 COPPA 合规。注意：加州自己的儿童设计法案（AB 2273，加州适龄设计规范）打过官司，效力有变，见备注。
  Benefit: COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, 15 U.S.C. §§6501–6506; 16 CFR Part 312): a website or online service "directed to" children under 13, or with "actual knowledge" a user is under 13, must obtain verifiable parental consent before collecting personal information, show parents what's collected and how it's used, and provide deletion. The FTC fines per violation, up to $53,088 each (the 2025 inflation-adjusted figure, still current in 2026); Epic Games paid $275 million in 2022 over Fortnite children's-privacy issues. Common practice: block under-13 registration (a 13+ age gate) or build a separate kids' version with COPPA compliance. Note: California's own kids-design law (AB 2273, the Age-Appropriate Design Code) has been litigated and its status has shifted — see the note below.
- 说人话：知道用户不到 13 岁还收集信息，先拿家长同意，否则 FTC 按每次违规最高 53,088 美元累计。最省事：13 岁以下不让注册。美国没有"打赏分级、直播禁令"那套，但儿童隐私这条是真罚。
  In plain terms: Collecting information from users you know are under 13 without parental consent draws FTC fines of up to $53,088 per violation, stacked. The simplest path: no registration under 13. The U.S. has no tiered tipping bans or livestream bans, but children's privacy is genuinely enforced.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：15 U.S.C. §§6501–6506 (COPPA: verifiable parental consent under 13). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/6501>; 16 CFR Part 312 (Children's Online Privacy Protection Rule). <https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312>; FTC (2022). Epic Games $275M COPPA penalties. <https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2022/12/fortnite-owner-epic-games-pay-245-million-dollars-refunds-275-million-dollars-penalties>
- 备注：加州 AB 2273（适龄设计规范，CAADCA）：第九巡回上诉法院 2026 年 3 月 12 日（NetChoice v. Bonta）部分解除禁令——年龄推定、高隐私默认设置等条款现可执行；数据使用限制、"dark patterns" 相关条款仍被禁（法院认为模糊）；AB 2246 替代被禁条款，2027 年 1 月 1 日生效。动手前查 oag.ca.gov 最新口径。
  Note: California AB 2273 (the Age-Appropriate Design Code, CAADCA): on March 12, 2026, the Ninth Circuit (NetChoice v. Bonta) partially lifted the injunction — age-assumption and high-privacy-by-default provisions are now enforceable; data-use restrictions and "dark patterns" provisions remain enjoined (as vague); AB 2246 replaces the enjoined provisions effective January 1, 2027. Check oag.ca.gov for the latest before acting.

### 9. 接到侵权通知要及时处理：DMCA 的通知-删除与反通知流程 / Handle Infringement Notices Promptly: DMCA Takedown and Counter-Notice
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：DMCA agent 注册 6 美元（copyright.gov，每 3 年续展），网站上公示代理人联系方式；通知邮箱有人盯，工程上把删除-恢复流程做进后台。花几天搭好
  Cost: $6 DMCA agent registration (copyright.gov, renew every 3 years), agent contact info posted on the site; someone watching the notices inbox; takedown-and-restore workflow built into the backend. A few days to set up.
- 收益：DMCA §512 的标准流程：① 权利人发合格的侵权通知（§512(c)(3) 要求写清作品、侵权位置、联系方式、善意声明、伪证罚则声明、签名，缺一项可不受理）；② 平台"迅速"（expeditiously）删除或屏蔽；③ 用户发反通知（counter-notice, §512(g)(3)），平台转给投诉方，10 到 14 个工作日后恢复内容——除非投诉方在这期间起诉了用户。虚假投诉也有代价：明知故犯发虚假通知，按 §512(f) 赔对方损失。注意 Lenz v. Universal（第九巡回）：发通知前要考虑合理使用（fair use），不考虑就发可能构成 §512(f) 的虚假陈述。
  Benefit: The DMCA §512 standard flow: ① the rights holder sends a valid infringement notice (§512(c)(3) requires identifying the work, the infringing location, contact info, a good-faith statement, a perjury statement, and a signature — a notice missing any element need not be honored); ② the platform removes or disables access "expeditiously"; ③ the user files a counter-notice (§512(g)(3)), the platform forwards it to the complainant, and restores the content after 10 to 14 business days — unless the complainant sues the user in the meantime. False complaints have a price too: knowingly false notices trigger §512(f) liability for the other side's losses. Note Lenz v. Universal (9th Cir.): consider fair use before sending a notice — skipping that step can be §512(f) misrepresentation.
- 说人话：合格的侵权通知来了，迅速删除；用户反通知，转给投诉方，10 到 14 个工作日后恢复（除非对方起诉）。乱发虚假投诉，自己要赔对方损失。
  In plain terms: A valid infringement notice arrives — remove swiftly; the user counter-notices — forward it to the complainant and restore after 10 to 14 business days (unless they sue). Knowingly false complaints make you pay the other side's losses.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：17 U.S.C. §512(c)(3) (valid notice elements), (g) (counter-notice; restore in 10–14 business days). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/512>; §512(f) (liability for knowing material misrepresentation). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/17/512>; Lenz v. Universal Music Corp., 815 F.3d 1145 (9th Cir. 2016) (fair use must be considered before takedown). <https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca9/13-16106/13-16106-2016-03-14.html>; U.S. Copyright Office. DMCA Designated Agent Directory. <https://www.copyright.gov/dmca-directory/>

### 11. 选服务器先看停机能不能忍：大厂云贵但省心，小 VPS 便宜但半夜要自己起床 / Picking Servers: Downtime Tolerance First — Big-Cloud Costs More but Lets You Sleep; Cheap VPS Means 3 A.M. Wake-Up Calls
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少|多 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：AWS/GCP/Azure 按量付费，小应用每月几十美元起，流量大了上千；小 VPS（DigitalOcean、Linode、Hetzner）每月几美元起。难在半夜告警要自己爬起来
  Cost: AWS/GCP/Azure pay-as-you-go starts at tens of dollars a month for small apps, thousands at scale; small VPS providers (DigitalOcean, Linode, Hetzner) start at a few dollars a month. The hard part is dragging yourself up for 3 A.M. alerts.
- 收益：这是工程选型，没有法律强制要求（美国不指定服务器放哪）。原则：用户付费的、停机就丢钱的业务，用大厂云的多可用区（multi-AZ）加自动扩缩，贵但 SLA 和运维工具齐全；个人项目、内部工具，小 VPS 加定时快照够了。数据备份遵守 3-2-1（3 份拷贝、2 种介质、1 份异地）；数据库开 point-in-time 恢复。别把鸡蛋放一个篮子：单台 VPS 无备份，硬盘一坏就是删库跑路的现实版。
  Benefit: This is an engineering choice — no law dictates where your servers live. The rule: for revenue-generating services where downtime loses money, use big-cloud multi-AZ with autoscaling — pricier, but SLAs and ops tooling included; for personal projects and internal tools, a small VPS with scheduled snapshots is enough. Backups follow 3-2-1 (3 copies, 2 media types, 1 offsite); enable point-in-time recovery on databases. Don't put all eggs in one basket: a single VPS with no backup turns a dead disk into a real-world "deleted the database" story.
- 说人话：赚钱的业务用大厂云多可用区，贵但半夜不用起床；个人项目小 VPS 加定时快照就行。备份 3-2-1，数据库开 point-in-time 恢复。
  In plain terms: Revenue services go on big-cloud multi-AZ — expensive but no 3 A.M. wake-ups; personal projects are fine on a small VPS with scheduled snapshots. Backups 3-2-1, point-in-time recovery on databases.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：AWS. Shared Responsibility Model; multi-AZ deployments. <https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/shared-responsibility-model/>
- 备注：本条为工程经验，无法律强制要求，证据等级为 C。涉及加州居民个人信息的备份，仍受 CCPA/CPRA 约束（见第 11 章第 15 条）。
  Note: This entry is engineering experience with no legal mandate — hence grade C. Backups containing Californians' personal information remain subject to the CCPA/CPRA (see Chapter 11, item 15).

### 12. 群发营销邮件要守 CAN-SPAM：每封违规最高罚 53,088 美元 / Marketing Emails Must Comply With CAN-SPAM: Up to $53,088 per Violating Email（新增）
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：邮件服务商（Mailchimp、SendGrid 等）每月几十美元起；退订机制工程上几小时。难在持续维护退订名单、别把退订的人加回去
  Cost: Email providers (Mailchimp, SendGrid, etc.) start at tens of dollars a month; opt-out handling takes hours of engineering. The hard part is maintaining the suppression list and never re-adding people who opted out.
- 收益：联邦 CAN-SPAM Act（15 U.S.C. §§7701–7713）管所有商业邮件：① 不许伪造发件人、标题、路由信息；② 标题不能欺骗；③ 必须说明这是广告；④ 每封邮件必须带真实有效的实体邮寄地址（physical postal address）；⑤ 必须给清晰明显的退订（opt-out）方式，10 个工作日内处理退订，之后不得再发。FTC 按每封违规邮件最高罚 53,088 美元（2025 年通胀调整数）。注意：CAN-SPAM 不要求事先同意（和短信不一样），但退订必须管用；纯交易/关系类邮件（如订单确认、密码重置）要求较松，但夹带商业广告的就按商业邮件算。
  Benefit: The federal CAN-SPAM Act (15 U.S.C. §§7701–7713) covers all commercial email: ① no forged sender, subject, or routing information; ② no deceptive subject lines; ③ must identify the message as an ad; ④ every message must carry a valid physical postal address; ⑤ must offer a clear and conspicuous opt-out mechanism, honor opt-outs within 10 business days, and never email that recipient again. The FTC fines up to $53,088 per violating email (2025 inflation-adjusted figure). Note: CAN-SPAM doesn't require prior consent (unlike texts), but opt-out must actually work; purely transactional/relationship messages (order confirmations, password resets) face lighter requirements — but ones carrying commercial content count as commercial.
- 说人话：群发营销邮件：退订链接必须有、10 个工作日内必须处理、每封邮件必须带实体地址；每封违规最高罚 53,088 美元。买名单群发是最容易被罚的姿势。
  In plain terms: Bulk marketing email: an opt-out link is mandatory, opt-outs must be honored within 10 business days, and every message must include a physical address — up to $53,088 per violating email. Blasting a purchased list is the fastest way to get fined.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：FTC. CAN-SPAM Act: A Compliance Guide for Business (penalties up to $53,088 per email; 10-business-day opt-out). <https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/can-spam-act-compliance-guide-business>; 15 U.S.C. §§7701–7713. <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/7701>
- 备注：本条为 2026 年 10 月事实核对时新增。CAN-SPAM 只管邮件；短信和电话营销另有 TCPA（见第 13 条），要求更严（事先书面同意）。
  Note: This entry was added in the October 2026 fact-check. CAN-SPAM covers email only; text and phone marketing fall under the TCPA (see item 13), which is stricter (prior written consent required).

### 13. 营销短信和自动外呼要守 TCPA：每条最高 1,500 美元，对方可以直接告你 / Marketing Texts and Robocalls Under the TCPA: Up to $1,500 per Message — Recipients Can Sue You Directly（新增）
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=中 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：合规短信平台（Twilio 等）按条收费；同意记录（consent log）工程上几小时。难在每一条营销短信的同意来源都要留证据
  Cost: Compliant SMS platforms (Twilio, etc.) charge per message; consent logging takes hours of engineering. The hard part is keeping proof of the consent source for every marketing text.
- 收益：联邦 TCPA（Telephone Consumer Protection Act, 47 U.S.C. §227）：用自动拨号系统或预录语音打营销电话、发营销短信，必须事先拿到对方的"事先明确书面同意"（prior express written consent）——网站表单勾选、短信回复确认都算，口头不算。每条短信/每通电话违规赔 500 美元，故意或明知违规 1,500 美元（§227(b)(3)），消费者可以直接起诉你（私人诉权），有专门接这类案子的原告律师。必须提供退订方式（回复 STOP）并及时处理。各州还有自己的迷你 TCPA（如佛州、俄克拉荷马），要求更严。
  Benefit: The federal TCPA (Telephone Consumer Protection Act, 47 U.S.C. §227): marketing calls or texts using an autodialer or prerecorded voice require the recipient's "prior express written consent" first — a checked website form or a confirming text reply counts; verbal doesn't. Each violating text or call costs $500, or $1,500 if willful or knowing (§227(b)(3)), and consumers can sue you directly (private right of action) — plaintiff lawyers specialize in these cases. You must offer an opt-out (reply STOP) and honor it promptly. Several states have their own mini-TCPAs (e.g., Florida, Oklahoma) that are stricter.
- 说人话：营销短信必须先拿到用户书面同意，否则每条 500 美元、故意 1,500 美元，对方可以直接告你。买号码群发短信，是美国小公司最常见的"一夜被告"姿势。
  In plain terms: Marketing texts require the recipient's written consent first — otherwise $500 per message, $1,500 if willful, and they can sue you directly. Blasting texts to a purchased number list is the classic way small U.S. companies get sued overnight.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：47 U.S.C. §227(b)(3) (private right of action; $500 per violation, $1,500 if willful/knowing). <https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/47/227>
- 备注：本条为 2026 年 10 月事实核对时新增。短信的同意标准比邮件严得多：邮件是"可退订就行"，短信是"先拿到同意再发"。
  Note: This entry was added in the October 2026 fact-check. Texts face a far stricter consent standard than email: email is "opt-out works," texts are "get consent first."
