# 16. 得了慢性病之后怎么活·加州版

口径：总死亡率与金钱。查出慢性病以后往往还有几十年要过，本节算的是这几十年怎么管。本章的法律与制度内容按加州和美国联邦现行规定写：ACA（平价医疗法案）禁止保险公司因既有病症（pre-existing condition）拒保或加价（42 U.S.C. §300gg-3）；没有雇主医保的人可通过加州医保交易所 Covered California（coveredca.com，电话 1-800-300-1506）参保，开放参保期为每年 11 月 1 日至次年 1 月 31 日，失业、搬家等人生大事触发 60 天特殊参保期，Medi-Cal（加州医疗补助）全年可申请。健康科学类条目的证据、数字和结论沿用原书，只把费用换成美元、把国内特有的流程做了本地化。

*Scope: all-cause mortality and money. A chronic diagnosis usually leaves decades ahead; this section is about how to manage those decades. The legal and institutional content in this chapter follows current California and U.S. federal rules: the ACA (Affordable Care Act) bars insurers from denying coverage or charging more because of a pre-existing condition (42 U.S.C. §300gg-3); people without employer coverage can enroll through California's marketplace, Covered California (coveredca.com, 1-800-300-1506), with open enrollment November 1–January 31 each year, a 60-day special enrollment period after major life events such as job loss or a move, and Medi-Cal (California Medicaid) available year-round. The health-science items keep the original book's evidence, numbers, and conclusions; only costs were converted to dollars and China-specific procedures localized.*

### 1. 药按医嘱吃满，别感觉好了就停 / Take your medication exactly as prescribed — don't stop when you feel better
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，反而省下以后治并发症的钱。每天吃药占不了几分钟，难在一天不落地吃下去。
  Cost: Free — it saves the money you'd spend treating complications later. Taking pills takes only minutes a day; the hard part is never skipping a day.
- 说人话：按医生说的把药吃满的人，同期死亡的可能性大约只有断断续续吃药的人的一半。这个数要打折看。连假药都吃满的人死亡率也低，说明有一部分好处来自「能坚持吃药的人本来就更注意健康」。但停药的害处是确定的，降压药、抗凝药、抗排异药尤其如此。
  In plain terms: People who take their medication exactly as prescribed have roughly half the mortality of those who take it on and off. Take that number with a grain of salt: even people who diligently took the placebo had lower mortality, which means part of the benefit comes from "people who stick with pills tend to look after their health in general." But the harm of stopping is real — especially for blood pressure drugs, blood thinners, and anti-rejection drugs.
- 收益：21 项研究、46847 人合起来算（荟萃分析）：和吃药断断续续的人相比，按医嘱吃满的人同期死亡的可能性约低 44%。原值是比值比 0.56，95% CI 0.50–0.63（可信范围）。只看确有疗效的治疗，约低 45%（0.55，0.49–0.62）
  Benefit: A meta-analysis of 21 studies with 46,847 people: compared with intermittent users, people with good adherence had about 44% lower mortality during the same period — odds ratio 0.56, 95% CI 0.50–0.63. For treatments of proven benefit only, about 45% lower (0.55, 0.49–0.62).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Simpson SH, Eurich DT, Majumdar SR, et al. (2006). A meta-analysis of the association between adherence to drug therapy and mortality. BMJ, 333(7557), 15. <https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.38875.675486.55>
- 备注：争议。这也是这本书里最需要打折看的一个数字。同一项汇总分析里，连安慰剂（假药）都吃满的人死亡率也低（0.56，0.43–0.74）。这说明相当一部分好处来自「能坚持吃药的人本来就更注意健康」。作者把这层混进来的因素叫健康依从者效应。但停药本身的害处是明确的，尤其是降压药、抗凝药和抗排异药。/ Note: A caveat — this is the number in the book that most needs discounting. In the same meta-analysis, even people who diligently took the placebo had lower mortality (0.56, 0.43–0.74), meaning much of the benefit comes from "people who stick with pills are healthier in other ways" — the healthy-adherer effect. Still, the harm of stopping is clear, especially for blood pressure drugs, blood thinners, and anti-rejection drugs.

### 3. 复查按医生给的间隔做，把每次的指标记在同一个本子上 / Keep your follow-up schedule and log every result in one place
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：每次检查几十到几百美元。去一趟医院加上等结果，要占一点时间。难在隔一段就得去一次，容易一拖再拖。
  Cost: Each checkup runs tens to a few hundred dollars. A trip to the clinic plus waiting for results takes some time. The hard part is going back on schedule instead of putting it off.
- 说人话：慢性病看的是一段时间里的变化，不是某一次的数值。把日期、指标、当时吃的药和剂量记在同一个本子上。换医院的时候拿出来，不用把检查重做一遍、重花一遍钱。
  In plain terms: With chronic disease, what matters is the trend over time, not any single reading. Log the date, the numbers, and the drugs and doses you were on — all in one notebook or file. When you switch doctors, bring it along and skip repeating tests and paying for them twice.
- 收益：慢性病要看的是数值一段时间里怎么变，只看某一次不够。记在同一处还有一个好处：换医院时不用重复检查，也不用重复花钱
  Benefit: Chronic disease is about how numbers move over time; one reading isn't enough. Keeping everything in one place has a second payoff: when you change providers, you don't repeat tests or pay for them again.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验，无直接文献 / Author's experience; no direct literature
- 备注：每次至少记四样：日期、指标值、当时在吃的药、药的剂量。用手机拍化验单也行，但都要放在同一处，别东一张西一张。/ Note: Log at least four things each time: the date, the values, the drugs you were taking, and their doses. Phone photos of lab slips work too — just keep them all in one place, not scattered around.

### 4. 别为了试偏方、保健品停掉正规治疗 / Don't drop proven treatment to try folk remedies or supplements
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱，还省下买保健品的钱。不占时间。难在别人反复劝你试的时候能顶住。
  Cost: Free — it saves the money you'd spend on supplements. Takes no time. The hard part is holding your ground when people keep urging you to try something.
- 说人话：停药的坏处是确定的，偏方和保健品的好处是不确定的。这等于拿确定的损失去换不确定的好处。想试补充剂就先告诉主治医生。有些补充剂会影响抗凝药、抗排异药在血里的浓度。
  In plain terms: The harm of stopping is certain; the benefit of folk remedies and supplements is not. That's trading a certain loss for an uncertain gain. If you want to try a supplement, tell your doctor first — some supplements change blood levels of blood thinners and anti-rejection drugs.
- 收益：停药的害处是确定的，偏方的好处是不确定的。常见的无效品类，这本书第 6 节列了一批（原书第 6 节）
  Benefit: The harm of stopping is certain; the benefit of folk remedies is not. The book lists common useless categories in Section 6 (original book, Section 6).
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验，无直接文献；相关证据见第 6 节（原书第 6 节）/ Author's experience; no direct literature; related evidence in Section 6 (original book, Section 6)
- 备注：想试补充剂就先告诉主治医生。有些补充剂会和处方药相互作用，比如影响抗凝药、抗排异药在血里的浓度。/ Note: Tell your doctor before trying any supplement. Some interact with prescription drugs — for example, changing blood levels of blood thinners and anti-rejection drugs.

### 5. 病情稳定的慢性病，让医生开 90 天处方、用邮购药房一次拿齐 / For stable chronic conditions, get a 90-day prescription filled through a mail-order pharmacy
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：不花钱。问医生和保险各一句话的事。
  Cost: Free. One question each for your doctor and your insurer.
- 说人话：美国没有"去社区医院开 12 周长期处方"的制度，但对诊断明确、用药方案稳定的慢性病，可以请医生开 90 天的处方，走保险合作的邮购药房一次寄到家。省掉每月跑一趟药房排队的工夫，很多保险的 90 天邮购自付额按月算还更便宜。出远门或搬家前先把 90 天的量备齐。
  In plain terms: The U.S. has no "12-week prescription at the community clinic" system, but for a stable chronic condition with an unchanged regimen, you can ask your doctor for a 90-day prescription and have it filled through your plan's mail-order pharmacy — delivered to your door. That skips the monthly trip to the pharmacy, and many plans charge a lower copay per month for 90-day mail orders. Stock up before a long trip or a move.
- 收益：不用每月往返药房排队取药。多数商业保险和 Medicare Part D 都支持 90 天邮购，问医生能不能开 90 天、问保险走哪家邮购药房，两句话办完
  Benefit: No more monthly pharmacy trips. Most commercial plans and Medicare Part D support 90-day mail orders — ask your doctor for the 90-day script and your plan which mail-order pharmacy to use, and it's done.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验，无直接文献（原书本条的 A 级依据是中国国务院分级诊疗文件，不适用于加州，证据等级相应调整）/ Author's experience; no direct literature (the original item's grade-A basis was a Chinese State Council document that does not apply in California, hence the adjusted grade)
- 备注：换保险时先确认新保险的药房网络，别等药吃完了才发现邮购药房变了。需要冷藏或管制的药不一定能走邮购，问药房。/ Note: When you switch insurance, confirm the new plan's pharmacy network before your pills run out. Refrigerated or controlled drugs may not be mail-order eligible — ask the pharmacy.

### 6. 签直接付费家庭医生（Direct Primary Care）之前，问清保险报不报 / Before signing up for Direct Primary Care, find out what insurance covers
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。签约前多问一句，一分钟的事。
  Cost: Free. One extra question before signing, a minute of your time.
- 说人话：美国的 Direct Primary Care（DPC，直接付费家庭医生）是按月交会员费（通常每月几十到一百多美元）直接签约家庭医生，这笔会员费一般不走保险。保险报的是按次看的普通门诊。有人推销"会员制医疗"时，先问清楚这是走保险还是纯自费、一年多少钱、急诊和专科转诊还保不保。
  In plain terms: U.S. Direct Primary Care (DPC) means paying a monthly membership (typically tens to a bit over a hundred dollars a month) directly to a primary care doctor — that fee usually isn't billed to insurance. Insurance covers ordinary per-visit appointments. When someone pitches a "membership medical plan," first ask: is this billed to insurance or all out-of-pocket, what's the annual cost, and are ER visits and specialist referrals still covered?
- 收益：签约本身的价值在于有人长期跟着你这个病，不在于打包了多少项目。先问清付费方式，避免交了会员费才发现保险不认、看病还得另掏钱
  Benefit: The value of signing up is having one doctor follow your condition long-term, not how many items are bundled. Clarify the payment model first, so you don't discover after paying that insurance won't recognize it and visits still cost extra.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验，无直接文献（原书本条的 A 级依据是中国国务院分级诊疗文件，不适用于加州，证据等级相应调整）/ Author's experience; no direct literature (the original item's grade-A basis was a Chinese State Council document that does not apply in California, hence the adjusted grade)
- 备注：DPC 适合常看门诊、想随时联系上医生的人；很少看病的人可能按次自付更划算，算一笔账再签。/ Note: DPC suits people who see a doctor often and want direct access; if you rarely go, per-visit payment may be cheaper — do the math before signing.

### 7. 确诊糖尿病就去查一次眼底，之后按医生给的间隔查；每年查一次脚 / After a diabetes diagnosis, get a dilated eye exam once, then on your doctor's schedule; check your feet yearly
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：一次散瞳眼底检查几十到一百多美元，多数保险报。足部检查通常包含在门诊里，不另花钱。难在眼睛和脚都没不舒服的时候也要记着去查。
  Cost: A dilated eye exam runs tens to a bit over a hundred dollars, usually covered by insurance. Foot checks are typically part of a regular visit at no extra charge. The hard part is going when your eyes and feet feel perfectly fine.
- 说人话：糖尿病伤到眼底、伤到脚，在坏到看不见、烂起来之前几乎没有感觉。早查出来还有得治。2 型糖尿病确诊当时就查一次散瞳眼底，1 型在发病 5 年后开始查。查过一两次没事、血糖也达标的，可以放宽到一两年一次。脚每年查一次。
  In plain terms: Diabetes damages the retina and the feet silently — by the time vision fades or a foot ulcer appears, it's late. Caught early, both are treatable. Type 2 diabetes: get a dilated comprehensive eye exam at diagnosis. Type 1: start 5 years after onset. If one or more annual exams show no retinopathy and glucose is on target, screening every 1–2 years is acceptable. Feet: a comprehensive evaluation at least once a year.
- 收益：糖尿病伤到眼底叫视网膜病变，伤到脚叫糖尿病足。这两样在出问题之前几乎没有感觉。早查出来还有得治。等到看不见或者脚烂起来，就晚了
  Benefit: Diabetic retinopathy and diabetic foot cause almost no symptoms before the damage is done. Caught early, both are treatable. Wait until vision is gone or the foot is ulcerated, and it's too late.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：美国糖尿病学会 (2026). 糖尿病诊疗标准 2026·第 12 章 视网膜病变、神经病变与足部护理. Diabetes Care：「People with type 2 diabetes should have an initial dilated and comprehensive eye examination by an ophthalmologist or optometrist at the time of the diabetes diagnosis.」「Adults with type 1 diabetes should have an initial dilated and comprehensive eye examination by an ophthalmologist or optometrist 5 years after the onset of diabetes.」「If there is no evidence of retinopathy from one or more annual eye exams and glycemic indicators are within the goal range, then screening every 1–2 years may be considered.」「Perform a comprehensive foot evaluation at least annually to identify risk factors for ulcers and amputations.」<https://doi.org/10.2337/dc26-S012>
- 备注：2 型糖尿病确诊的时候就要查，1 型是发病 5 年后才开始查。差别在于 2 型往往已经悄悄高了好几年。查过一两次没事、血糖也达标的，可以放宽到 1 到 2 年一次。脚上感觉变迟钝的人，每天自己看一眼脚底，用镜子照也行。/ Note: Type 2 gets checked at diagnosis, type 1 five years after onset — because type 2 has usually been running high silently for years. If a couple of annual exams are clear and glucose is on target, every 1–2 years is fine. If your feet feel numb, look at the soles daily; a mirror works.

### 8. 得过肾结石就把水喝到每天 2.5–3 升，盐降到 6 克以内 / After a kidney stone, drink 2.5–3 liters of water a day and keep salt under 6 grams
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：不花钱。把随时喝水变成习惯。出汗多的日子还要再多喝一些，难在天天记着。
  Cost: Free. Make drinking water a habit; drink more on sweaty days. The hard part is remembering every day.
- 说人话：第一次长了钙结石的人，什么都不做的话，五年里约四分之一会再长。每天把水喝够的人，五年里复发的只有一成多，少了一半多。英国指南给的量是每天喝 2.5 到 3 升水，盐不超过 6 克。
  In plain terms: After a first calcium stone, doing nothing means about one in four people grow another within five years. People who drank enough water had recurrences of just over one in ten — down by more than half. The UK guideline: 2.5–3 liters of water a day, salt under 6 grams.
- 收益：一项为期 5 年、随机分成两组的对照试验，对象是 199 名第一次得钙结石、又查不出原因的患者（也叫特发性钙结石）。一组每天至少喝 2 升水，另一组不做任何干预。5 年里复发的，多喝水那组 12/99（12.1%），不干预那组 27/100（27.0%），P=0.008。平均隔多久复发：38.7±13.2 个月对 25.1±16.4 个月，P=0.016。NICE 指南 NG118 第 1.8.1 条建议，成人每天喝 2.5 到 3 升水，儿童青少年按年龄喝 1 到 2 升。水里加点鲜柠檬汁，别喝碳酸饮料，成人每天盐不超过 6 克
  Benefit: A 5-year randomized trial of 199 first-time idiopathic calcium stone formers: one group drank at least 2 liters of water daily, the other got no intervention. Five-year recurrence: 12/99 (12.1%) vs 27/100 (27.0%), P=0.008. Mean time to recurrence: 38.7±13.2 vs 25.1±16.4 months, P=0.016. NICE guideline NG118 §1.8.1 advises adults to drink 2.5–3 liters of water daily (1–2 liters for children and teens by age), add fresh lemon juice, skip sodas, and keep salt under 6 g/day for adults.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Borghi L, Meschi T, Amato F, Briganti A, Novarini A, Giannini A (1996). Urinary volume, water and recurrences in idiopathic calcium nephrolithiasis: a 5-year randomized prospective study. The Journal of Urology, 155(3), 839-843. <https://doi.org/10.1016/s0022-5347(01)66321-3>；National Institute for Health and Care Excellence (2019). Renal and ureteric stones: assessment and management. NICE guideline NG118，第 1.8.1 条. <https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/ng118/chapter/Recommendations>
- 备注：复发少了大约 55%，又不花钱，按本书的排序规则这是本节性价比最高的一条。要盯的是 24 小时尿量，目标是 2 到 2.5 升以上，不是看喝进去多少。出汗多的人、在高温环境里干活的人，得喝得更多。那项试验做的是第一次发作的特发性钙结石患者。结石成分不止一种，尿酸结石、感染性结石、胱氨酸结石各有各的对策。复发过的人值得做一次结石成分分析和 24 小时尿检，再定要不要加药。别为了防结石把钙戒掉，见第 6 节第 21 条（原书第 6 节）。发作时腰腹绞痛得厉害要去医院，不是靠多喝水冲下去的。不疼的血尿另有要查的东西，见第 1 节第 27 条（原书第 1 节）。/ Note: Recurrence down ~55% for free — the best value in this section by the book's ranking. Track 24-hour urine volume (target 2–2.5+ liters), not just intake. Sweat a lot or work in heat, drink more. The trial covered first-time idiopathic calcium stones; uric acid, infection, and cystine stones each need their own approach. Recurrent stone formers should get a stone analysis and 24-hour urine test before adding drugs. Don't cut calcium to prevent stones — see Section 6, item 21 (original book, Section 6). Severe flank pain during an attack means going to the hospital, not flushing it out with water. Painless bloody urine needs its own workup — see Section 1, item 27 (original book, Section 1).

### 9. 确诊痛风就长期吃降尿酸药，把血尿酸压到 360 µmol/L 以下并一直维持 / With gout, stay on urate-lowering drugs long-term and keep uric acid below 360 µmol/L
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=是 收益=大 口径=死亡率 -->
- 成本：别嘌醇是便宜的老药，每月几美元。刚开始每几周查一次血尿酸来调药量，稳定之后每几个月查一次。头 3 到 6 个月还要同时吃一种预防发作的药。难在不疼的时候也得一直吃下去。
  Cost: Allopurinol is a cheap generic, a few dollars a month. Check uric acid every few weeks while titrating, then every few months once stable. Take an anti-inflammatory flare prophylaxis for the first 3–6 months too. The hard part is staying on it when nothing hurts.
- 说人话：痛风要长期吃降尿酸药，把尿酸压到 360 µmol/L（6 mg/dL）以下，不能只在疼的时候吃止痛药。由护士按化验结果帮着调药量的病人，两年后 95% 达标，照常看病的只有 30%。达标之后也不能停药。靠忌口降不到这条线，戒酒也只低 1.6 mg/dL。**汉族人用别嘌醇之前值得先查 HLA-B\*5801**：带着这个基因的人用别嘌醇可能发生严重超敏反应，查一次终身有效。
  In plain terms: Gout needs long-term urate-lowering drugs to keep uric acid below 360 µmol/L (6 mg/dL) — not just painkillers during flares. In a trial, patients whose doses were nurse-titrated to target hit 95% at two years vs 30% with usual care. Don't stop once you hit target. Diet alone can't get you there; even quitting alcohol lowers uric acid by only 1.6 mg/dL. **People of Han Chinese ancestry should consider HLA-B\*5801 testing before starting allopurinol**: carriers face a risk of severe hypersensitivity; one test lasts a lifetime.
- 收益：一项随机对照试验，517 名过去 12 个月内发作过的成人。一组由护士主导、按指标把尿酸调到达标，另一组照常找全科医生看。2 年时血尿酸 <360 µmol/L（6 mg/dL）的比例，95% 对 30%（RR 3.18，95% CI 2.42–4.18，P<0.0001）。第 2 年的发作频率、痛风石、生活质量也都优于常规组。美国风湿病学会 2020 年痛风指南：皮下痛风石、影像见骨侵蚀、每年发作 ≥2 次的人强烈推荐启动降尿酸治疗，持续监测调量直到 <6 mg/dL，首选别嘌醇（3 期以上慢性肾病也是首选，起始 ≤100 mg/日），启动时同时给抗炎预防药至少 3 到 6 个月
  Benefit: An RCT of 517 adults with a flare in the past 12 months: nurse-led treat-to-target vs usual GP care. At 2 years, uric acid <360 µmol/L (6 mg/dL): 95% vs 30% (RR 3.18, 95% CI 2.42–4.18, P<0.0001). Year-2 flare frequency, tophi, and quality of life also favored treat-to-target. The 2020 ACR gout guideline strongly recommends starting urate-lowering therapy for subcutaneous tophi, imaging-evident bone erosion, or ≥2 flares/year; titrate to <6 mg/dL with ongoing monitoring; allopurinol first-line (including CKD stage 3+, start ≤100 mg/day), with anti-inflammatory prophylaxis for at least 3–6 months at initiation.
