# 25. 人走了以后要办什么 / After Someone Passes: What to Take Care Of

口径：金钱。本节只讲手续怎么办、钱怎么拿：人刚走时先做什么、死亡证明怎么办、殡葬消费怎么不被坑、分散在各处的钱怎么取回来。加州不强制火葬，没有户口注销，也没有殡葬中介备案；对应的是联邦 FTC 的殡葬消费保护规则和加州的 probate（遗产检验）简化程序。老人生前该做的安排，比如遗嘱、医疗预嘱、账户受益人，见第 17 节。
Scope: Money. This section covers only procedures and money: what to do right after a death, how to get the death certificate, how not to get ripped off on funeral costs, and how to collect money scattered across accounts. California doesn't mandate cremation, has no household-registration cancellation, and no funeral-broker registration; the counterparts are the federal FTC's funeral consumer protections and California's simplified probate procedures. Arrangements the elderly should make in advance — wills, advance directives, account beneficiaries — are in Section 17.

### 1. 人在家里或外面没了，先分清正常死亡还是非正常死亡：意外、死因不明、独居才发现的，立刻报警，遗体和现场不要动 / Unexpected Death at Home: Call 911 and Don't Move Anything
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。打一个电话就行。
  Cost: Costs nothing. One phone call.
- 说人话：在家里突然走的、死因不明的、独居很久才被发现的，打 911。警察和 coroner（验尸官）会来，现场不要动。住在 hospice（临终关怀）的预期死亡，打 hospice 护士，他们来宣布死亡，不用报警。
  In plain terms: Sudden death at home, unknown cause, or someone living alone discovered after a long time — call 911. Police and the coroner will come; don't touch the scene. For an expected death under hospice care, call the hospice nurse — they pronounce the death, no police needed.
- 收益：加州 Government Code §27491：暴力、突然、非正常死亡，无人看护的死亡，死前 20 天没看过医生或 hospice 护士的，都归 coroner 调查死因和死亡方式。现场被动过，调查说不清，死亡证明也开不出来。分不清是哪一种，就先打 911，警察到场会决定要不要叫 coroner。
  Benefit: California Government Code §27491: violent, sudden, or unusual deaths, unattended deaths, and deaths where the deceased saw neither a physician nor a hospice nurse in the 20 days before death all fall to the coroner to investigate the circumstances, manner, and cause. A disturbed scene muddies the investigation and can block the death certificate. When in doubt, call 911 first — responding officers decide whether to call the coroner.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Government Code §27491. <https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=GOV&sectionNum=27491>
- 备注：不要自己搬遗体、擦洗换衣服，也不要收走药瓶和字条。coroner 调查完才放行遗体给殡仪馆。
  Note: Don't move the body, wash or change clothes, or remove pill bottles and notes yourself. The coroner releases the body to the funeral home only after the inquiry.

### 2. 死亡证明是后面所有事的钥匙：谁救治谁开，家中正常死亡找社区卫生服务机构，一日内签发 / The Death Certificate Unlocks Everything: Get Plenty of Certified Copies
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=时间 -->
- 成本：认证副本一份几十美元，多买几份。
  Cost: A few dozen dollars per certified copy — buy several at once.
- 说人话：在医院走的，主治医生签；coroner 接手的案子，coroner 签。签完交到县里登记。之后去县 recorder 或 vital records（生命记录）办公室买 certified copy（认证副本）。银行、保险、社保、房产过户，每一件都要它。一次多买几份，十份起步。
  In plain terms: In a hospital death, the attending physician signs; in a coroner's case, the coroner signs. It's then filed with the county. After that, buy certified copies from the county recorder or vital records office. Banks, insurers, Social Security, property transfers — every one of them wants it. Buy plenty at once; start with ten.
- 收益：死亡证明由最后接诊的医生或 coroner 签署，交县卫生部门登记。之后所有事——银行取钱、保险理赔、SSA 遗属补助、房产过户——都要 certified copy。原件只有一份，普通复印件不管用，所以一次买够。加州一般要 2–3 周才能拿到。
  Benefit: The death certificate is signed by the last attending physician or the coroner and registered with the county health department. Everything after — bank withdrawals, insurance claims, SSA survivor benefits, property transfers — requires a certified copy. There's only one original and plain photocopies don't count, so buy enough in one go. In California it typically takes 2–3 weeks to become available.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：加州各县 vital records 办公室实务；常识
- 备注：每份 certified copy 的费用各县不同，待核实（约 20–30 美元）。名字、社保号有错当场改，拖到办继承时才发现，每个窗口重跑一遍。
  Note: The per-copy fee varies by county — to be verified (roughly $20–30). Fix errors in the name or Social Security number on the spot; discovering them during probate means redoing every office visit.

### 3. 遗体接运、存放、火化只能走殡仪馆，医院太平间不办殡仪，存放一般不超过 3 日 / Funeral Homes Are Licensed — but Nothing Requires Embalming or Cremation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。
  Cost: Costs nothing.
- 说人话：加州的殡仪馆（funeral home）要持牌，由 Cemetery and Funeral Bureau（墓园殡葬局）发牌。但和中国不同：加州不强制火葬，土葬火葬都行；法律也不强制防腐（embalming），殡仪馆未经你同意不能收费做。医院太平间只临时停放。
  In plain terms: California funeral homes must be licensed by the Cemetery and Funeral Bureau. But unlike China: California doesn't mandate cremation — burial and cremation are both fine; and the law doesn't require embalming — a funeral home can't charge you for it without your permission. A hospital morgue is only for temporary holding.
- 收益：加州殡葬业由 Department of Consumer Affairs 下属的 Cemetery and Funeral Bureau 监管，殡仪馆和从业人员都要持牌。联邦 FTC Funeral Rule：殡仪馆必须给价目表（见第 6 条），未经家属许可不得做防腐并收费。加州没有"必须火化"的法律，选土葬、火葬、海葬都是合法选项。
  Benefit: California's funeral industry is regulated by the Cemetery and Funeral Bureau under the Department of Consumer Affairs — both funeral homes and practitioners must be licensed. The federal FTC Funeral Rule requires funeral homes to give you a price list (see item 6) and bars embalming for a fee without family permission. California has no mandatory-cremation law — burial, cremation, and sea burial are all legal options.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：FTC Funeral Rule 解读. <https://www.econdolence.com/learning-center/planning-and-coordinating/what-to-do-when-someone-dies/funeral-rule-funeral-pricing-guidelines>
- 备注：在医院去世后凑上来的"一条龙"，多半不是你选的那家殡仪馆。先定殡仪馆，再让医院放行遗体。
  Note: The "full-service" operators who approach you at the hospital are usually not the funeral home you chose. Pick your funeral home first, then have the hospital release the body to them.

### 4. 对死因有异议，48 小时内提出尸检，同时封存病历 / Doubt the Cause of Death? Arrange an Autopsy Before Cremation
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：私人尸检自费，通常几千美元（待核实）。难的是在那个时候还想得起来办。
  Cost: A private autopsy is out of pocket, usually several thousand dollars (to be verified). The hard part is thinking of it at such a moment.
- 说人话：美国没有"48 小时提尸检"的法定期限。但铁律只有一条：火化之后就再也做不了尸检。coroner 只查法律规定他要查的案子；他不查、你想查，自己花钱请法医病理医生做私人尸检（private autopsy），要近亲属签字同意。
  In plain terms: The U.S. has no statutory "48-hour autopsy" deadline. But one rule is ironclad: after cremation, no autopsy is possible ever again. The coroner only investigates cases the law assigns to him; if he doesn't take it and you want answers, hire a forensic pathologist for a private autopsy at your own expense, with next-of-kin consent.
- 收益：coroner 决定是否做官方尸检（见第 1 条的法定情形），家属无权强制。家属自费的私人尸检是合法选项：找法医病理医生（forensic pathologist）。必须在火化或防腐前做，防腐会破坏部分证据。怀疑医疗事故的，同时按 HIPAA 索取完整病历（见第 24 节第 5 条）。
  Benefit: The coroner decides whether to perform an official autopsy (see the statutory cases in item 1); the family can't compel one. A family-funded private autopsy is a lawful option: hire a forensic pathologist. It must happen before cremation or embalming — embalming destroys some evidence. If you suspect medical malpractice, request the complete medical records under HIPAA at the same time (see Section 24, item 5).
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：法医病理实务常识；各县 coroner / medical examiner 办公室
- 备注：私人尸检的费用和预约时限各地不同，待核实。保险一般不报这笔钱。
  Note: Private autopsy fees and scheduling lead times vary — to be verified. Insurance generally doesn't cover it.

### 6. 殡葬服务分基础项目和非基础项目，基础项目有清单、收费依法制定 / Get the General Price List First: Federal Law Requires 16 Itemized Prices
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。出发前花几分钟看一眼价目表。
  Cost: Costs nothing. A few minutes to look at the price list before you go.
- 说人话：联邦 FTC Funeral Rule 规定：殡仪馆必须在谈价格前给你一份价目表（General Price List），16 项服务逐项标价，这份表归你，不用还。你有权只买要的那几项。唯一不能砍的是"基本服务费"（basic services fee）。
  In plain terms: The federal FTC Funeral Rule requires the funeral home to hand you a General Price List before any price discussion — 16 categories of goods and services, each with its price, yours to keep. You may buy only the items you want. The one charge you can't decline is the "basic services fee."
- 收益：Funeral Rule 要求 GPL 逐项列明 16 项：转运遗体、直接火化、直接土葬、基本服务费、防腐、遗体准备、告别仪式场地、葬礼仪式、追思会、墓边仪式、灵车、礼宾车、棺材价格、 outer burial container（外层葬具）价格等。殡仪馆不能强迫你买套餐，不能因为你用外面买的棺材收"手续费"，不能在未经同意的情况下做防腐。电话问价也必须如实回答。
  Benefit: The Funeral Rule requires the GPL to itemize 16 things: forwarding and receiving remains, direct cremation, immediate burial, basic services fee, transfer of remains, embalming, other preparation, use of facilities for viewing/ceremony/memorial service, graveside service equipment, hearse, limousine, and casket and outer burial container prices, among others. The funeral home can't force package deals, can't charge a "handling fee" for a casket you bought elsewhere, and can't embalm without permission. Price questions by phone must be answered truthfully.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Federal Trade Commission, Complying with the Funeral Rule. <https://www.ftc.gov/node/60348>
- 备注：16 项里殡仪馆只列它实际提供的项目。basic services fee 是唯一不可减免的一项，谈价前先看它的金额。
  Note: The GPL lists only the 16 items the provider actually offers. The basic services fee is the only non-declinable charge — check its amount before negotiating anything else.

### 7. 遇到强制搭售、标价外加价、把一项拆成几项收费，记下来投诉 / Tying, Hidden Markups, "Handling Fees": Write It Down and Report It
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是在那个当口还愿意较真。
  Cost: Costs nothing. The hard part is being willing to push back at such a moment.
- 说人话：这几样都是 Funeral Rule 点名的违法行为：不给价目表、标价外加钱、"买这个才能办那个"、自己带棺材收手续费。留好价目表、收据和聊天记录。管殡葬的是加州 Cemetery and Funeral Bureau，联邦层面找 FTC 投诉。
  In plain terms: These are exactly the violations the Funeral Rule names: no price list, charges beyond the listed prices, "you must buy this to get that," handling fees for a casket you brought yourself. Keep the price list, receipts, and messages. Funeral services are overseen by California's Cemetery and Funeral Bureau; at the federal level, complain to the FTC.
- 收益：Funeral Rule 禁止：虚假陈述法律要求、未经许可收费防腐、直接火化强制买棺材、把买某些商品作为提供另一些服务的前提。自己从 Costco、网上买的棺材骨灰盒，殡仪馆必须接受，不得收手续费。投诉渠道：加州 Cemetery and Funeral Bureau（dca.ca.gov）和 FTC。
  Benefit: The Funeral Rule prohibits misrepresenting legal requirements, charging for embalming without permission, requiring a casket for direct cremation, and conditioning some goods or services on buying others. A casket or urn you bought yourself — from Costco, online, anywhere — must be accepted with no handling fee. Complaint channels: California's Cemetery and Funeral Bureau (dca.ca.gov) and the FTC.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Federal Trade Commission, Complying with the Funeral Rule. <https://www.ftc.gov/node/60348>；Funeral Rule 解读. <https://www.econdolence.com/learning-center/planning-and-coordinating/what-to-do-when-someone-dies/funeral-rule-funeral-pricing-guidelines>
- 备注：当场先要一张价目表，收费凭据和聊天记录都留好，事后再投诉也来得及。
  Note: Ask for the price list on the spot, keep receipts and messages — filing the complaint later still works.

### 9. 分散在各处的钱要逐个去取：公积金余额、社保待遇、工伤待遇 / Claim Every Account: Social Security, 401(k), Life Insurance, Bank POD
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=中 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱，但要跑几个窗口。去之前备齐死亡证明认证副本。
  Cost: Costs nothing, but several offices to deal with. Bring certified copies of the death certificate.
- 说人话：人走以后，有四笔钱要主动去要：SSA 的遗属补助（配偶、未成年子女按月领，另有一笔 255 美元的一次性补助）；401(k)/IRA 按受益人指定直接给，不用走 probate；人寿保险找保险公司理赔；银行账户看有没有 POD（payable-on-death）指定。都没指定的小额遗产，加州有简化程序，不用打完整官司。
  In plain terms: After a death, four pots of money need active claiming: SSA survivor benefits (monthly for a spouse and minor children, plus a one-time $255 payment); 401(k)/IRA accounts, which pass directly to the named beneficiary with no probate; life insurance — file the claim with the insurer; bank accounts — check for a POD (payable-on-death) designation. For small estates with no designations, California has simplified procedures — no full court case needed.
- 收益：SSA 遗属福利：去世工人的配偶（60 岁以上，或抚养 16 岁以下孩子）、未成年子女可按月领遗属金；另有 255 美元一次性死亡补助（两年内申请）。401(k)、IRA、人寿保险、银行 POD 账户都按受益人指定直接转移，不进 probate。都没指定的：加州小额遗产宣誓书（Probate Code §§13100–13106），2025 年 4 月 1 日后去世的门槛是 208,850 美元，死后满 40 天、无 probate 在办就能用；小额房产走 §13200（69,625 美元）；自住房 75 万美元以下可走法院简化程序（§§13150–13157）。房子想直接留给某人，生前可办 transfer-on-death deed（Probate Code §§5600–5696，现行有效）。
  Benefit: SSA survivor benefits: a deceased worker's spouse (60 or older, or caring for a child under 16) and minor children can receive monthly survivor payments, plus a $255 lump-sum death payment (apply within two years). 401(k)s, IRAs, life insurance, and bank POD accounts all transfer directly to the named beneficiary, bypassing probate. With no designations: California's small estate affidavit (Probate Code §§13100–13106) — $208,850 for deaths on or after April 1, 2025, usable 40 days after death when no probate is pending; small-value real property via §13200 ($69,625); a primary residence under $750,000 via a simplified court petition (§§13150–13157). To leave a house directly to someone, record a transfer-on-death deed during life (Probate Code §§5600–5696, currently in effect).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California small estate affidavit 解读. <https://www.hoganlegal.com/california-small-estate-affidavit-skip-probate/>；Transfer on death deed 解读. <https://guidewaylegal.com/avoid-probate-in-property-transfer-in-california/>
- 备注：遗嘱、advance health care directive（预立医疗指示，Probate Code §4600 起）、TOD deed 都是生前安排，见第 17 节。TOD deed 日落条款的具体到期日待核实。
  Note: Wills, advance health care directives (Probate Code §4600 et seq.), and TOD deeds are all advance planning — see Section 17. The exact sunset date of the TOD deed statute is to be verified.

### 10. 死者的账号和个人信息，近亲属可以要求查阅、复制、更正、删除 / Digital Accounts After Death: Set Up Legacy Contacts Now
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。平台不配合，才需要走法律程序。
  Cost: Costs nothing. Legal process is only needed if the platform won't cooperate.
- 说人话：加州有《数字资产统一法》（RUFADAA）：人走以后，遗嘱执行人或信托受托人可以依法接管数字资产。但优先级是：平台自带的"身后联系人"设置 > 遗嘱/信托里的安排 > 平台用户协议。所以趁现在就把 Apple 的 Digital Legacy、Google 的 Inactive Account Manager、Facebook 的 legacy contact 设好。
  In plain terms: California has the Revised Uniform Fiduciary Access to Digital Assets Act (RUFADAA): after death, the executor or trustee can lawfully take over digital assets. But priority goes: the platform's built-in "legacy contact" setting first, then arrangements in your will or trust, then the platform's terms of service. So set up Apple's Digital Legacy, Google's Inactive Account Manager, and Facebook's legacy contact now, while you can.
- 收益：加州 2016 年通过 AB 691，把 RUFADAA 写进 Probate Code（第 20 编，自 §870 起），2017 年 1 月 1 日生效。三层优先级：第一是平台的在线工具（Apple Digital Legacy、Google Inactive Account Manager、Facebook legacy contact）；第二是遗嘱、信托、授权书里的书面安排；第三才是平台的用户协议。没做任何安排的，平台按自己的协议办，家属可能拿不到。
  Benefit: In 2016 California enacted AB 691, adding RUFADAA to the Probate Code (Part 20, commencing with §870), effective January 1, 2017. Three tiers of priority: first, the platform's online tool (Apple Digital Legacy, Google Inactive Account Manager, Facebook legacy contact); second, written directions in a will, trust, or power of attorney; third, the platform's terms of service. With no arrangements at all, the platform follows its own terms — and the family may get nothing.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California AB 691 bill text. <https://www.leginfo.ca.gov/pub/15-16/bill/asm/ab_0651-0700/ab_691_bill_20160411_amended_sen_v92.pdf>；California Probate Code §883. <https://law.justia.com/codes/california/code-prob/division-2/part-20/section-883/>
- 备注：通信内容（邮件、私信正文）的披露比普通数字资产更严，遗嘱里要写明授权。所以趁人清醒，把账号去向写进遗嘱，见第 17 节。
  Note: Disclosing the content of communications (email bodies, message contents) faces stricter rules than ordinary digital assets — authorize it expressly in the will. So while everyone's of sound mind, put account directions in the will — see Section 17.
