# 14. 账号与信息安全 / Account and Information Security

口径：金钱与个人信息。账号被别人登进去，丢的先是钱。对方还会用你的账号去骗你通讯录里的人。你的身份也等于被人拿走了。
Scope: money and personal information. If someone gets into your accounts, money goes first. They will also use your accounts to scam people in your contacts. Your identity is taken too.

### 1. 邮箱、支付、社交账号都开二次验证，优先用手机弹窗确认，其次才是短信验证码 / Turn on two-factor authentication for email, payment, and social accounts: approve a push notification on your phone first, SMS codes second
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。每个账号设置一次，两三分钟就设完。
  Cost: Free. Set it up once per account; it takes two or three minutes each.
- 说人话：二次验证是指登录时除了密码，还要再确认一次是不是本人。用手机上弹个窗、你点一下确认的办法，九成以上的钓鱼盗号都被拦住了。靠回答「上次在哪登的」「备用邮箱是什么」这种老办法，钓鱼盗号只能拦下一成左右。
  In plain terms: Two-factor authentication means that besides your password, you confirm once more that it's really you. Approving a push notification on your phone blocks more than nine in ten phishing takeovers. The old way—answering "where did you last sign in" or "what's your backup email"—stops only about one in ten.
- 收益：谷歌统计了 35 万次真实的盗号尝试（也叫账号劫持）。一类是靠设备来验证，比如手机上弹窗让你点确认，或者插一把安全密钥。这一类拦下了「超过 94% 源于钓鱼的劫持尝试和 100% 的自动化劫持尝试」。钓鱼是指骗你在假网站上输密码，自动化是指机器拿泄露的密码批量去试。另一类是靠回答问题来验证，比如问你上次在哪登录、备用邮箱是什么。这一类「只拦下了低至 10% 的钓鱼劫持和 73% 的自动化劫持」
  Benefit: Google analyzed 350,000 real account-takeover attempts. One group used device-based checks, such as approving a push notification on your phone or plugging in a security key. These stopped "over 94% of phishing-based takeover attempts and 100% of automated takeover attempts." Phishing means tricking you into typing your password on a fake site; automated means machines trying leaked passwords in bulk. The other group relied on challenge questions, such as where you last signed in or what your backup email is. These "blocked as few as 10% of phishing takeovers and 73% of automated takeovers."
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Doerfler P, Thomas K, Marincenko M, et al. (2019). Evaluating Login Challenges as a Defense Against Account Takeover. The World Wide Web Conference (WWW '19). <https://doi.org/10.1145/3308558.3313481>
- 备注：同一项研究也发现，这类验证有时会把本人挡在外面。52% 的真用户第一次没登进去，不过最后有 97% 还是进去了。先给邮箱开这个功能，因为别的账号大多能用邮箱找回密码。验证码只自己用，替别人报码、刷脸会背上什么，见第 8 节第 45 条（念验证码）/ Note: The same study found these checks sometimes lock out the real user: 52% of legitimate users failed on the first try, but 97% eventually got in. Turn it on for your email first, because most other accounts can be recovered through your email. Only ever use verification codes yourself; for what happens when you read out codes or scan your face for someone else, see Section 8, Item 45 (reading out verification codes).

### 2. 邮箱密码单独一个，不和任何网站重复 / Give your email its own password; never reuse it on any other site
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。用密码管理器存着就不用自己记。难的是要改掉一个密码到处用的老习惯。
  Cost: Free. Store them in a password manager and you don't have to memorize anything. The hard part is breaking the old habit of using one password everywhere.
- 说人话：别的网站被偷走的密码，会被人拿来直接登你的邮箱。邮箱一旦被人登进去，凡是能用邮箱找回密码的账号，都跟着一起丢。所以邮箱的密码要单独一个，别的地方一律不用。
  In plain terms: Passwords stolen from other websites get tried directly on your email. Once someone is in your email, every account that can be reset through that email goes with it. So your email password should be unique—used nowhere else.
- 收益：把别处泄露的账号和密码挨个拿来试着登，这叫撞库，是最省事的攻击方式，你的邮箱也会被这样试。邮箱一旦被人登进去，所有用它找回密码的账号一起丢。美国网络安全和基础设施安全局的建议是：每个账号用一个不同的强密码，至少 16 位，交给密码管理器存
  Benefit: Trying leaked usernames and passwords site by site is called credential stuffing—the laziest attack there is, and your email will be tried this way too. Once someone is in your email, every account recoverable through it falls at once. The U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency recommends a different strong password for every account, at least 16 characters, stored in a password manager.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：US CISA. Use Strong Passwords. <https://www.cisa.gov/secure-our-world/use-strong-passwords>
- 备注：记不住就用浏览器自带的密码管理器。它替你记住每个网站的密码，比到处用同一个密码强得多。别把密码存在聊天软件的收藏、手机备忘录或者纸条上 / Note: If you can't remember them, use the password manager built into your browser. It remembers each site's password for you, which beats reusing one password everywhere. Don't store passwords in a messaging app's saved notes, your phone's notes app, or on sticky notes.

### 3. 手机设锁屏密码，SIM 卡设 PIN 码 / Lock your phone with a passcode and protect your SIM card with a PIN
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。锁屏密码和 PIN 码各设一次就行。
  Cost: Free. Set the passcode and the PIN once each and you're done.
- 说人话：SIM 卡就是手机里那张小卡片，短信验证码靠它来收。手机丢了，捡到的人会把这张卡拔下来，插进别的手机收验证码，再挨个重置你的账号。给卡设一个 PIN 码，卡换到别的手机上，一开机就要先输这个码，这条路就断了。
  In plain terms: The SIM card is the little chip in your phone, and SMS verification codes arrive through it. If you lose your phone, whoever finds it can pull the card out, put it in another phone to receive your codes, and reset your accounts one by one. Set a PIN on the card, and any other phone will demand that code at startup—closing off that route.
- 收益：手机丢了以后，捡到的人最快的一条路是把 SIM 卡插到别的手机上，收你的短信验证码。有了验证码，他就能一个个重置你的账号。SIM 卡设了 PIN 码，换到别的手机上开机就要先输密码，捡到的人就没法拿它收验证码
  Benefit: After a phone is lost, the fastest move for whoever finds it is to put your SIM card in another phone and collect your SMS codes. With those codes, they can reset your accounts one after another. With a SIM PIN set, any other phone demands the code at startup, so the finder can't use your card to receive codes.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验，无直接文献
- 备注：PIN 码在手机设置里的「SIM 卡锁」那一项里设。出厂时的初始码一般是 1234 或者 0000。连着输错三次，就要用运营商给的 PUK 码才能解开。所以设完先把这个码记在纸上 / Note: Set the PIN under "SIM card lock" in your phone's settings. The factory default is usually 1234 or 0000. Three wrong tries in a row and you'll need the PUK code from your carrier to unlock it. So write the code down on paper right after setting it.

### 4. 手机丢了按这个顺序做：挂失 SIM 卡、远程锁定、改密码、报警、冻结银行卡 / If your phone is lost, do things in this order: suspend the SIM, lock the phone remotely, change passwords, file a police report, freeze bank cards
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=否 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。整套做下来十几分钟。
  Cost: Free. The whole sequence takes about ten minutes.
- 说人话：顺序比手快更重要。第一步挂失 SIM 卡，验证码这条命脉就断了。接着远程锁住手机，再用电脑改邮箱和支付密码，然后报警拿回执，最后看情况冻结银行卡。手机不在身上，借别人的手机拨 611 就能打通自己运营商的客服。
  In plain terms: The order matters more than speed. First suspend the SIM card—that cuts off the lifeline of verification codes. Then lock the phone remotely, change your email and payment passwords from a computer, file a police report and keep the receipt, and freeze bank cards if needed. Without your phone, you can still reach your own carrier's customer service by borrowing someone's phone and dialing 611.
- 收益：顺序比速度更重要。第一步，挂失 SIM 卡，别人就收不到你的验证码了。第二步，远程锁定手机，并清空手机里的内容。第三步，从电脑上改邮箱和支付密码。第四步，报警，拿回执。最后按需要冻结银行卡。美国联邦通信委员会的提示也是：就算觉得只是弄丢了，也要远程锁住。被偷了马上报警，报上型号和 IMEI 串号（手机的身份编号），并马上告诉运营商
  Benefit: Order beats speed. Step one: suspend the SIM so nobody can receive your verification codes. Step two: lock the phone remotely and wipe its contents. Step three: change email and payment passwords from a computer. Step four: file a police report and keep the receipt. Finally, freeze bank cards as needed. The U.S. Federal Communications Commission gives the same advice: lock the device remotely even if you think you merely misplaced it. If it was stolen, report it to the police right away with the model and IMEI number (the phone's identity number), and tell your carrier immediately.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：US FCC. Protect Your Smart Device. <https://www.fcc.gov/consumers/guides/protect-your-mobile-device>；步骤顺序是作者经验；被人冒名开户或贷款，按 IdentityTheft.gov 的恢复计划处理
- 备注：提前查好自己运营商的客服渠道，存在不用手机也能拿到的地方。自己的手机号码记牢，客服会核对身份。IMEI 串号在拨号盘输 *#06# 就能看到，提前抄下来 / Note: Look up your carrier's customer-service channels in advance and save them somewhere reachable without your phone. Know your own phone number by heart—customer service will verify your identity. You can see the IMEI number by dialing *#06#; copy it down ahead of time.

### 5. 卡被盗刷先挂失冻结再报警：信用卡最多承担 50 美元，借记卡按报失快慢分三档 / If your card is skimmed, freeze it first, then call the police: credit-card losses are capped at $50, debit cards have three tiers depending on how fast you report
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=大 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。发现卡上有不对劲的动静，立刻挂失或者冻结。把报警记录、挂失记录、银行发来的交易通知都留着。卡还在自己身上的，就近做一笔小额查询或者存取，留下记录证明出事的时候真卡在你手上。难的是要忍住别先跟客服争论，先挂失。
  Cost: Free. The moment you spot something wrong on your card, suspend or freeze it. Keep the police report, the suspension record, and the bank's transaction alerts. If the physical card is still with you, make a small balance inquiry or transaction nearby to leave a record that the real card was in your hands when it happened. The hard part is resisting the urge to argue with customer service first—freeze the card first.
- 说人话：卡被人盗刷，不用你去证明「这不是我刷的」。信用卡盗刷，联邦法律把你的损失上限卡在 50 美元，而且是发卡行来证明「这是你自己刷的」，不是你自证清白。借记卡看你报失的速度：知道丢卡两天之内报，最多 50 美元；超过两天，最高 500 美元；账单寄出 60 天还没报，后面的损失不封顶。
  In plain terms: If your card is skimmed, you don't have to prove "I didn't make this charge." For credit cards, federal law caps your loss at $50, and it's the card issuer that must prove "you made this charge yourself"—not you proving your innocence. For debit cards, it depends on how fast you report: within two business days of learning the card is gone, at most $50; after two days, up to $500; if you still haven't reported 60 days after the statement was mailed, later losses are uncapped.
- 收益：信用卡适用《公平信用账单法》（Fair Credit Billing Act，15 U.S.C. §1643）。第 (a) 款规定，持卡人对信用卡被冒用的责任不超过 50 美元；第 (b) 款把举证责任放在发卡行：银行要证明这笔是持卡人授权的，或者证明满足了 (a) 款的各项条件，拿不出证据就不能让你承担。多数大银行还自愿把 50 美元也免了，实际是零。借记卡适用《电子资金转账法》及 Reg E（12 CFR 1005.6(b)）：知道卡丢了，两个工作日内通知银行，责任不超过 50 美元；超过两个工作日才通知，不超过 500 美元；账单寄出后 60 天内还没报告那笔冒用转账，60 天之后的转账损失不封顶。信用卡的账单争议要在收到账单 60 天内书面提出（15 U.S.C. §1666），光打电话不够
  Benefit: Credit cards are covered by the Fair Credit Billing Act (15 U.S.C. §1643). Subsection (a) limits a cardholder's liability for unauthorized credit-card use to $50; subsection (b) puts the burden of proof on the card issuer: the bank must show the charge was authorized by the cardholder or that the conditions in (a) were met—if it can't, it can't make you pay. Most big banks voluntarily waive even the $50, making it effectively zero. Debit cards fall under the Electronic Fund Transfer Act and Regulation E (12 CFR 1005.6(b)): notify the bank within two business days of learning the card is lost, and liability is capped at $50; notify after two business days, capped at $500; fail to report an unauthorized transfer shown on a statement within 60 days of mailing, and losses on transfers after those 60 days are uncapped. Credit-card billing disputes must be raised in writing within 60 days of receiving the statement (15 U.S.C. §1666)—a phone call alone is not enough.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Fair Credit Billing Act, 15 U.S.C. §1643(a)（50 美元上限）、§1643(b)（举证责任在发卡行）、§1666（账单争议 60 天内书面提出）；Regulation E, 12 CFR 1005.6(b)（借记卡分档说明见 FDIC 考试手册） <https://www.fdic.gov/consumer-compliance-examination-manual/vi-2-electronic-fund-transfer-act>
- 备注：这套只管「没经你同意的刷卡」。你是被骗着自己把钱转出去的（比如骗子冒充熟人让你转账），这两条都不管，要走另一套：立刻联系银行看能不能撤回或止付，同时报警。身份被盗走的（冒名开户、冒名退税），去 IdentityTheft.gov 按联邦贸易委员会的恢复计划一步步处理。退税被冒领的，去 IRS 官网申请一个身份保护 PIN（Identity Protection PIN），以后报税多一道只有你知道的号码 / Note: These rules only cover charges made without your consent. If you were tricked into sending the money yourself (e.g., a scammer posing as someone you know talked you into a transfer), neither law covers it—use a different playbook: contact your bank immediately to see if the transfer can be reversed or stopped, and call the police. If your identity was stolen (accounts or tax refunds filed in your name), follow the Federal Trade Commission's recovery plan step by step at IdentityTheft.gov. If a tax refund was claimed in your name, get an Identity Protection PIN from the IRS website—future tax filings will require this number that only you know.

### 6. 每隔一段时间看一次账号的登录设备和已授权应用，不用的清掉 / Every so often, review the signed-in devices and authorized apps on your accounts, and remove the ones you don't use
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。每次看几分钟。难的是没人提醒，要自己记得去看。
  Cost: Free. A few minutes each time. The hard part is that nobody reminds you—you have to remember on your own.
- 说人话：「登录设备」是指现在还能用你账号的那些手机和电脑。盗号的人常常先潜伏一阵再动手。列表里冒出你不认识的设备，或者早就不用的软件还连着你的账号，看到就退出全部登录，再改密码。
  In plain terms: "Signed-in devices" are the phones and computers that can use your account right now. Account thieves often lurk quietly for a while before acting. If the list shows a device you don't recognize, or an app you stopped using long ago is still connected to your account, sign out everywhere and change the password.
- 收益：被盗号往往不是当场就动手，对方会先潜伏一阵。账号里的登录设备列表，记着现在还能用这个账号的手机和电脑。已授权应用列表，记着你准许它用这个账号登录的别家软件。列表里的陌生设备，和早就不用的第三方软件，是最容易发现的痕迹
  Benefit: Takeovers often don't strike immediately—the attacker lurks first. The signed-in-devices list records which phones and computers can use the account right now. The authorized-apps list records which third-party services you let sign in with this account. An unfamiliar device on the list, or a long-unused third-party app still connected, is the easiest trace to spot.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：作者经验，无直接文献
- 备注：谷歌账号、苹果账号、邮箱和银行 App 里都有这个入口。发现不认识的设备，就点退出全部登录，再改密码 / Note: Google accounts, Apple accounts, email, and banking apps all have this page. If you spot a device you don't recognize, choose "sign out everywhere," then change the password.

### 7. 装 App 别点「全部同意」：不必要的权限在手机设置里关掉，推销电话去 donotcall.gov 登记 / Don't tap "Allow all" when installing apps: turn off unneeded permissions in your phone's settings, and register with donotcall.gov to cut telemarketing calls
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。难的是要忍住不点「全部同意」，装完再去设置里逐项检查一遍。
  Cost: Free. The hard part is resisting "Allow all" and going back through the permissions one by one after installing.
- 说人话：App 要的权限，只留它正常干活必须的那几项。地图要位置是必须的，手电筒要通讯录就不是。装完先去手机设置里的权限页，把不必要的关掉，等真用到的时候再选「仅这一次允许」。电话推销是另一回事，去 donotcall.gov 把号码登记进全国谢绝来电名单。
  In plain terms: Only keep the permissions an app truly needs to do its job. A map app needs your location; a flashlight app does not need your contacts. After installing, open the permissions page in your phone's settings, turn off what isn't needed, and choose "Allow only this time" when you actually need it. Telemarketing calls are a separate matter—register your number on the National Do Not Call Registry at donotcall.gov.
- 收益：iOS 和安卓都在设置里给了按权限逐项管理的入口，也支持「仅使用期间允许」和「仅这一次允许」。推销电话这头，联邦法律（《电话消费者保护法》，47 U.S.C. §227）限制自动拨号和预录语音的推销电话，联邦贸易委员会维护的全国谢绝来电名单（donotcall.gov，电话 1-888-382-1222 登记）要求电话推销商 31 天内停止给登记号码打推销电话。登记只拦推销电话，政治、慈善和催债电话不在此列
  Benefit: Both iOS and Android let you manage permissions one by one in settings, with options like "Allow only while using the app" and "Allow only this time." For telemarketing, federal law (the Telephone Consumer Protection Act, 47 U.S.C. §227) restricts autodialed and prerecorded sales calls, and the FTC's National Do Not Call Registry (donotcall.gov, or 1-888-382-1222) requires telemarketers to stop calling registered numbers within 31 days. Registration only blocks sales calls—political, charitable, and debt-collection calls are not covered.
- 证据等级：C / Evidence grade: C
- 来源：Telephone Consumer Protection Act, 47 U.S.C. §227；FCC 实施细则 47 CFR 64.1200；全国谢绝来电名单 donotcall.gov（联邦贸易委员会维护）；权限管理是作者经验
- 备注：骚扰和诈骗电话的另一半在运营商那头：AT&T、Verizon、T-Mobile 都有免费的骚扰电话拦截功能，在各自的 App 里打开。已经被骗走钱的，按第 5 条（卡被盗刷）先冻结，再去 IdentityTheft.gov 上报 / Note: Your carrier handles the other half of spam and scam calls: AT&T, Verizon, and T-Mobile all offer free spam-call blocking—turn it on in their apps. If money was already taken, freeze the card first per Item 5 (card skimmed), then report at IdentityTheft.gov.

### 8. 你有权查看、删除和更正自己的个人信息：加州居民用 CCPA 的几项权利 / You have the right to see, delete, and correct your personal information: Californians can use the CCPA's rights
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：不花钱。先走企业网站上的隐私申请通道，对方拖着不办再向加州总检察长或加州隐私保护局投诉。真打官司就是几个月起步，律师费还要自己出，所以先投诉更划算。难的是对方拖着的时候得反复去催。
  Cost: Free. Start with the privacy-request channel on the company's website; if they stall, complain to the California Attorney General or the California Privacy Protection Agency. A real lawsuit takes months at minimum, and you'd pay your own lawyer, so complaining first is the cheaper move. The hard part is following up repeatedly when they drag their feet.
- 说人话：加州居民有权要求企业交出它收集了你的哪些信息（查看权），删掉它们（删除权），改掉错的（更正权），还可以让它别把你的信息卖给或共享给第三方（拒绝出售/共享权），以及限制它对社保号、位置这类敏感信息的使用（限制敏感信息权）。企业要在 45 天内回复。你行使这些权利，企业不许涨价或降质报复你。
  In plain terms: California residents can demand that a business hand over what information it collected about you (right to know), delete it (right to delete), fix what's wrong (right to correct), stop it from selling or sharing your information with third parties (right to opt out of sale/sharing), and limit how it uses sensitive information like your Social Security number or location (right to limit sensitive information). The business must respond within 45 days. It may not raise prices or degrade service to punish you for exercising these rights.
- 收益：加州《消费者隐私法》（CCPA，经《隐私权法案》CPRA 修订，加州民法典 §§1798.100–199）给了加州居民这几项权利：§1798.110 查看权（企业收集了哪些类别和具体信息）、§1798.105 删除权、§1798.106 更正权、§1798.120 拒绝出售或共享权、§1798.121 限制敏感个人信息使用和披露权、§1798.125 不歧视权。§1798.130 要求企业在收到可验证的消费者请求后 45 天内免费答复，必要时可再延长 45 天。网站上常见的「Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information」（不卖或不共享我的个人信息）链接，就是行使 §1798.120 权利的入口（§1798.135）
  Benefit: California's Consumer Privacy Act (the CCPA, as amended by the CPRA, Cal. Civil Code §§1798.100–199) gives California residents these rights: §1798.110 the right to know (what categories and specific pieces of information a business collected), §1798.105 the right to delete, §1798.106 the right to correct, §1798.120 the right to opt out of sale or sharing, §1798.121 the right to limit the use and disclosure of sensitive personal information, and §1798.125 the right to non-discrimination. §1798.130 requires businesses to respond free of charge within 45 days of receiving a verifiable consumer request, with one 45-day extension when reasonably necessary. The "Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information" link you see on websites is the doorway to exercising the §1798.120 right (§1798.135).
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：California Consumer Privacy Act (as amended by CPRA), Cal. Civ. Code §§1798.100, 1798.105, 1798.106, 1798.110, 1798.120, 1798.121, 1798.125, 1798.130, 1798.135. <https://cppa.ca.gov/pdf/20260101_ccpa_statute.pdf>
- 备注：这是加州居民的权利，别的州居民按本州法律。CCPA 只管达到门槛的企业（比如年营收超过 2625 万美元），街角小店不在此列。删账号和删信息是两件事，删完账号还要单独提删除。换手机或卖旧手机之前，先在旧手机上把所有账号退出、解除绑定，再恢复出厂设置。企业就是不办的，向加州总检察长或加州隐私保护局（CPPA）投诉；个人直接起诉只限数据泄露那一类情形（§1798.150），别的一律走行政投诉 / Note: These are California residents' rights; residents of other states follow their own state laws. The CCPA only covers businesses that meet its thresholds (e.g., annual revenue over $26.625 million)—the corner shop is not covered. Deleting an account and deleting your information are two separate things; after deleting the account, request deletion separately. Before switching or selling a phone, sign out of every account and unpair everything on the old phone, then factory-reset it. If a business simply won't comply, complain to the California Attorney General or the California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA); individuals can sue directly only for data-breach cases (§1798.150)—everything else goes through the administrative complaint route.

### 9. 刷脸不是必须答应的：加州没有伊利诺伊州那样的专门法律，但你可以要求别的验证方式 / You don't have to agree to face scans: California has no Illinois-style biometric law, but you can demand another way to verify
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=0 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=金钱 -->
- 成本：不花钱。被要求刷脸时，问一句「有没有别的验证方式」。难的是要当面开口问。
  Cost: Free. When asked to scan your face, ask: "Is there another way to verify?" The hard part is asking out loud in person.
- 说人话：伊利诺伊州有专门的《生物识别信息隐私法》，企业收指纹人脸之前必须书面告知用途和保存期限，并拿到你的书面同意，加州没有这样一部专门法。加州这边，CCPA 把生物识别信息列为敏感个人信息，你有权限制企业对它的使用。人脸和密码最大的区别是泄露之后改不掉，所以它值得比密码更谨慎。
  In plain terms: Illinois has a dedicated Biometric Information Privacy Act—before collecting fingerprints or face scans, a business must tell you in writing the purpose and retention period and get your written consent. California has no equivalent standalone law. On California's side, the CCPA treats biometric information as sensitive personal information, and you can limit how businesses use it. The biggest difference between your face and a password is that you can't change your face after a leak—so it deserves more caution than a password.
- 收益：伊利诺伊州《生物识别信息隐私法》（740 ILCS 14/15）要求：私人机构收集生物识别信息前，必须书面告知收集和存储的事实、具体目的和期限，并取得书面同意；还必须公开数据保留和销毁政策。违反的个人可以起诉，每次过失违规赔 1000 美元、故意或重大过失 5000 美元（740 ILCS 14/20）。加州没有对等的专门法。加州《消费者隐私法》把生物识别信息列入敏感个人信息的类别（加州民法典 §1798.140），消费者有权按 §1798.121 限制企业对敏感信息的使用和披露
  Benefit: Illinois' Biometric Information Privacy Act (740 ILCS 14/15) requires private entities, before collecting biometric information, to inform the person in writing that it is being collected or stored, the specific purpose and length of term, and to obtain a written release; they must also publish a retention and destruction policy. Aggrieved individuals can sue: $1,000 per negligent violation, $5,000 per intentional or reckless one (740 ILCS 14/20). California has no equivalent standalone law. California's Consumer Privacy Act lists biometric information among the categories of sensitive personal information (Cal. Civil Code §1798.140), and consumers may limit businesses' use and disclosure of sensitive information under §1798.121.
- 证据等级：A / Evidence grade: A
- 来源：Illinois Biometric Information Privacy Act, 740 ILCS 14/15（收集前书面告知与书面同意）、14/20（个人诉权与赔偿）；California Consumer Privacy Act, Cal. Civ. Code §1798.121（限制敏感信息使用）、§1798.140（敏感个人信息定义含生物识别信息）
- 备注：最常遇到的是健身房、公寓门禁、雇主考勤要你录人脸。对方说「系统只支持刷脸」的时候，要求它提供刷卡、密码或者身份证件这类别的方式。还是不给的，可以向加州隐私保护局（CPPA）反映。人脸信息一旦泄露改不掉，这是它和密码的本质区别 / Note: You'll most often run into this at gyms, apartment access systems, and employer time clocks asking for your face. When told "the system only supports face scans," demand an alternative like a key card, PIN, or ID check. If they still refuse, you can report it to the California Privacy Protection Agency (CPPA). Once face data leaks, you can't change it—that's the fundamental difference from a password.

### 10. 别把代码、密钥和隐私资料交给来路不明的「AI 中转站」，让 AI 自动执行命令时尤其如此 / Don't hand code, API keys, or private material to shady "AI relay stations"—especially when letting AI run commands on its own
<!-- 成本标签: 钱=少 时间=少 毅力=些 收益=中 口径=自由 -->
- 成本：官方直连通常比中转站贵，充值也没那么方便。难在中转站便宜，一个入口能调好几家模型
  Cost: Official direct access is usually pricier than relay stations, and topping up is less convenient. The temptation is that relays are cheap and one endpoint reaches many models.
- 说人话：中转站夹在你和模型厂商中间，你发出去的内容和收回来的回答，它都看得见、改得了。有研究测了 400 多个中转站，9 个往回答里塞恶意代码，17 个动了研究者故意放进去的云服务密钥。让 AI 自动执行命令的，一条被改过的命令就够让电脑被人控制。
  In plain terms: A relay station sits between you and the model provider—it can see and alter everything you send and everything that comes back. One study tested 400+ relay stations: 9 injected malicious code into responses, and 17 tampered with cloud-service keys the researchers had planted as bait. If you let AI run commands automatically, a single altered command is enough to hand your computer to someone else.
- 收益：2026 年 6 月的一份官方风险提示指出：部分中转站会把用户提交的数据留在服务器上，有的私自截留，倒卖给别的大模型厂商做训练；有的暗藏后门，向用户设备植入恶意代码，窃取账号密钥和云端凭证，甚至装上远程控制程序。加州大学圣塔芭芭拉分校等单位的研究者 2026 年做了一项测量。他们买了 28 个付费中转站，又从公开社区收集了 400 个免费中转站。结果 1 个付费的和 8 个免费的，会往返回的工具调用里注入恶意代码。工具调用就是 AI 让你的电脑去执行的命令，比如安装软件包。还有 2 个会挑时机下手，论文举的例子是只对全自动执行的会话下手。17 个动用过研究者故意放进去的亚马逊云服务密钥。1 个转走了研究者以太坊钱包里的钱，钱包里只放了很少的钱，损失不到 50 美元。论文演示的改法很隐蔽：把安装命令里的 requests 换成拼法相近的 reqeusts，一眼看不出来
  Benefit: A June 2026 official risk advisory noted that some relay stations keep user-submitted data on their servers, some siphon it off privately and resell it to other model vendors for training; some hide backdoors that plant malware on user devices, steal account keys and cloud credentials, or even install remote-control programs. Researchers including UC Santa Barbara measured this in 2026. They bought 28 paid relay stations and collected 400 free ones from public communities. Result: 1 paid and 8 free ones injected malicious code into returned tool calls. A tool call is a command the AI asks your computer to run, such as installing a software package. Two more timed their attacks—for example, only striking fully automated sessions. Seventeen tampered with Amazon cloud-service keys the researchers had planted. One drained the researchers' Ethereum wallet, which held only a small amount—under $50 lost. The paper's example of tampering was subtle: swapping "requests" in an install command for the lookalike "reqeusts," easy to miss at a glance.
- 证据等级：B / Evidence grade: B
- 来源：国家安全部 (2026). 「AI中转站」，风险要防范. <https://www.szzg.gov.cn/2026/xwzx/szkx/202606/t20260608_5331487.htm>（数字中国建设峰会官网转载国家安全部微信公众号）；Liu H, Shou C, Wen H, Chen Y, Fang RJ, Feng Y. (2026). Your Agent Is Mine: Measuring Malicious Intermediary Attacks on the LLM Supply Chain. arXiv:2604.08407. <https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.08407>
- 备注：定 B 级，是因为只有一项研究，而且是预印本。测的中转站多数是免费的，付费的只有 28 个，算不出「中转站有几成有问题」。有媒体把那次被盗写成 50 万美元，论文原文是不到 50 美元。已经用过来路不明的中转站，就到模型厂商那里把用过的密钥作废、重新生成，云服务和代码仓库的密钥一起换。经过中转站时，别让 AI 全自动执行命令，命令先看一眼再放行。受益人是你自己 / Note: Graded B because there is only one study, and it's a preprint. Most tested relays were free; only 28 were paid, so there's no basis for "what share of relays is malicious." Some media reported the theft as $500,000; the paper says under $50. If you've already used a shady relay, go to the model provider and revoke and regenerate any keys you used, and rotate cloud-service and code-repository keys too. When going through a relay, don't let AI run commands fully automatically—glance at each command before approving. The beneficiary is yourself.
